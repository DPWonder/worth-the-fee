Partly. Sapphire Preferred points can pay for flights and for hotels near the Disney parks, either through Chase Travel or by transfer to an airline or hotel program. Park tickets are the weak spot: Chase Travel is reported not to sell tickets to the US Disney parks, so points cover them only as cash back at 1 cent each.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## What points are worth here

| Way to use points | Value per point | Works for |
|---|---|---|
| Statement credit or cash | 1 cent | Anything, including park tickets |
| Chase Travel, no Points Boost | 1 cent | Flights, hotels, rental cars |
| Chase Travel with Points Boost | Up to 1.5 cents on selected hotels and flights | Only the bookings Chase marks |
| Transfer to Hyatt | 4 Chase points become 3 Hyatt points | Hyatt hotels near the parks |
| Transfer to Southwest, United or Marriott | 1:1 | Flights to Orlando or Anaheim, Marriott hotels |

Chase does not publish which bookings get a Points Boost. See [how Points Boost works](/chase-points-boost-explained/).

## How far a balance goes

| Points | As cash (1 cent) | Chase Travel with a 1.5-cent Boost |
|---|---|---|
| 25,000 | $250 | $375 |
| 50,000 | $500 | $750 |
| 75,000 | $750 | $1,125 |
| 100,000 | $1,000 | $1,500 |

A $300 hotel night costs 30,000 points at 1 cent and 20,000 points with a 1.5-cent Boost. A family's park tickets for several days usually cost more than the hotel, and tickets get the 1-cent rate.

## Hotels

**Through Chase Travel.** AwardWallet reports that the Swan, Swan Reserve and Dolphin hotels at Walt Disney World can be booked with points, and that Disney's own resorts appear only occasionally. A booking made this way earns no Marriott stay credit or elite perks, and resort fees still apply.

The card's hotel credit of up to $100 per account year applies to hotel stays bought through Chase Travel, so it can come off one of these bookings.

**By transfer to Hyatt.** AwardWallet names Hyatt Regency Grand Cypress and Hyatt Place Lake Buena Vista near Walt Disney World, and Hyatt House, Hyatt Place and Hyatt Regency Orange County near Disneyland. Hyatt waives resort fees on award nights.

In April 2025 the Grand Cypress cost 12,000 to 18,000 Hyatt points a night. At the Sapphire Preferred's 4:3 rate that would be 16,000 to 24,000 Chase points. Hyatt changed its award chart in May 2026, and the hotel's current price is not confirmed. The [Hyatt transfer change](/chase-to-hyatt-transfer-change-2026/) explains the 4:3 rate.

**By transfer to Marriott.** The Swan and Dolphin are Marriott hotels. Upgraded Points gives two different starting prices in the same article, about 40,000 and about 50,000 Marriott points a night, and Marriott does not waive the resort fee on award nights. Compare the Chase Travel price first.

## Park tickets

Sources disagree on whether Chase Travel sells them:

- Upgraded Points (June 2026) says Chase Travel does not sell tickets to US Disney parks, though it lists overseas parks.
- AwardWallet (April 2025) says Chase Travel sometimes sells Walt Disney World and Disneyland admission, usually at higher prices than Disney or other sellers.

Check Chase Travel before counting on it. Otherwise, pay for tickets with the card and redeem points as a statement credit at 1 cent each.

## What the card earns on Disney spending

| Purchase | Reported rate |
|---|---|
| Tickets bought alone from Disney | 1 point per dollar; they code as entertainment |
| A Disney package with lodging and tickets, booked direct | 2 points; it codes as travel |
| Food at the US parks | 3 points, as dining |
| Hotels or activities bought through Chase Travel | 5 points |

How a ticket reseller codes on this card is not confirmed.

## Insurance on a park trip

The card's trip cancellation cover applies to prepaid travel paid with the card. Standalone theme park tickets that are not part of a travel package are reported as excluded. Details are on the [travel insurance page](/chase-sapphire-preferred-travel-insurance/).

## Who should skip it

- Anyone whose main cost is park tickets. A flat cash back card pays the same or more on tickets.
- Anyone set on a Disney-owned resort. They are rarely bookable with Chase points.
- Anyone who would not otherwise spend enough to build a useful balance.

## Quick answers

**Can I buy Disney park tickets with Chase points?**
Not reliably through Chase Travel for the US parks; sources disagree. You can pay with the card and redeem points as a statement credit at 1 cent each.

**Can I book a Disney hotel with Sapphire Preferred points?**
The Swan, Swan Reserve and Dolphin are reported as bookable in Chase Travel. Disney-owned resorts appear only occasionally.

**Do Disney tickets earn bonus points on the card?**
Tickets bought alone are reported to earn 1 point per dollar. A package with lodging earns 2 points as travel.
