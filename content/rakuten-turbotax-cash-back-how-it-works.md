Yes, but only on paid online federal filings. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's TurboTax page showed 5% cash back on three kinds of online federal filing. State filings, free filings and downloadable or CD software earn nothing, and the return must be filed within 7 days of the click.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## Rates seen on November 25, 2026

| Rate | Product on Rakuten's list |
|---|---|
| 5% | Do It Yourself Deluxe and Premium online federal filings |
| 5% | Experts online federal filings (Assisted and Full Service) |
| 5% | Experts for Business online federal filings |
| 0% | Everything in the exclusions below |

No "was" label appeared, so 5% was not marked as a raised rate. This was read in November, outside the main filing season. The page does not say what the rate will be in early 2027; not confirmed.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for TurboTax list these as earning nothing:

- Free filings
- State filings
- Audit defense
- E-filing directly to the IRS
- Bank products
- The refund transfer fee
- Max benefits
- CDs and downloadable software

The terms add that "rebates are not offered on any other products or services", and that cash back is only for orders completed through the U.S. site.

## The 7-day rule

Three conditions in the special terms set the timing:

- Click through Rakuten before paying.
- After the click, there are 7 days to file to receive cash back.
- Cash back is processed once a federal tax return is filed.

A return started through Rakuten in January and filed in April is outside the 7 days. The terms do not say whether a second click through Rakuten shortly before paying and filing restarts the 7 days. The first condition points that way, but it is not confirmed.

## What it comes to

The federal filing fee is the only part that earns. TurboTax prices change through the season, so the fees below are placeholders for the sums, not quoted prices.

| Part of the bill | Example fee | Cash back |
|---|---|---|
| Online federal filing, paid tier | $80 | $4.00 |
| State filing | $60 | $0 |
| Audit defense add-on | $50 | $0 |
| Fee to pay from the refund | Any | $0 |
| **Total** | **$190** | **$4.00** |

On that example, 5% of the federal fee comes to about 2% of the whole bill.

## Paying from the refund

The exclusions name bank products and the refund transfer fee. Those are tied to paying the filing fees out of the refund. The terms say the fee itself earns nothing. Whether choosing that payment method also affects cash back on the federal filing is not confirmed.

## Coupon codes and other discounts

The TurboTax terms on Rakuten have no rule about coupon codes. On November 25, 2026, the page listed two offers, neither with a code. Whether a discount from a bank, employer or another partner link can be combined with a Rakuten click is not confirmed.

## App, desktop and stores

- **Mobile app.** Rakuten's page describes the TurboTax mobile app. The terms do not mention it. Whether a return filed in the app tracks is not confirmed.
- **Desktop software.** CDs and downloads are excluded, wherever they are bought.
- **In stores.** There is no in-store offer on the page.

## Who should skip it

- Your return qualifies for the free edition. Free filings earn nothing.
- You use the downloaded or CD version.
- You start early and file weeks later. The 7 days will have passed.
- You have a discount through another link that takes more than 5% off the federal fee.
- Most of your bill is state filings and add-ons. Those earn nothing.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at TurboTax?**
5% on November 25, 2026, on paid online federal filings. Rates change daily.

**Does the state return earn cash back?**
No. State filings are excluded, along with free filings and audit defense.

**How long after the click can the return be filed?**
The terms give 7 days from the click. Cash back is processed once the federal return is filed.
