Purchases of products and services made by you or an authorized user count toward the Chase Sapphire spending requirement. The annual fee, interest, balance transfers, cash advances and cash-like transactions such as money orders do not. Chase asks you to allow 6 to 8 weeks after qualifying for the points to post, though reviewers report they often arrive when the next statement closes.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The requirement on November 20, 2026

| Card | Public bonus stated | Purchases needed | Time allowed | Annual fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sapphire Preferred | 75,000 points | $5,000 | First 3 months from account opening | $95 |
| Sapphire Reserve | 100,000 points | $6,000 | First 3 months | $795 |

These are the offers on Chase's pages on that date. Offers change, and the bonus may not be available to you. See [who is eligible](/chase-sapphire-bonus-eligibility-2026/).

On average that is about $1,667 a month for the Preferred and $2,000 a month for the Reserve.

## What counts and what does not

Chase's Sapphire Preferred page defines purchases as when "you, or an authorized user" use the card to buy products and services.

| Transaction | Counts? |
|---|---|
| Everyday purchases of products and services | Yes |
| Purchases by an authorized user on your account | Yes |
| The annual fee and any other fee | No |
| Interest | No |
| Balance transfers | No |
| Cash advances | No |
| Traveler's checks, foreign currency, money orders | No |
| Other cash-like transactions | No |
| Unauthorized or fraudulent charges | No |
| Gift card purchases | Not confirmed |

Chase's list of cash-like transactions is longer than the part read for this page, so read the offer details on the application for the full wording. Whether a gift card bought at a store counts is not confirmed in the terms read. Do not build a plan on it.

Purchases covered by a card credit, such as the Preferred's hotel credit, are a separate question from the bonus. Chase's terms say the first $100 of those hotel purchases earns no points. Whether they still count toward the $5,000 is not confirmed.

## Refunds

The Points Guy states that a refunded amount is typically deducted from progress toward a spending requirement. One Mile at a Time gives the same warning. A return near the end of the three months can leave you short, so a small margin over the target helps.

## When the three months start

Chase's wording is the first 3 months from account opening. That points to the day the account is opened and not the day the card arrives in the mail. No source read spells out the exact day, so ask Chase for your deadline.

## When the points post

| Source | Timing |
|---|---|
| Chase's terms | Allow 6 to 8 weeks after qualifying |
| Upgraded Points (August 2026) | Often when the statement closes after you qualify; one editor's points posted about three weeks after qualifying |
| Same source | Qualifying just before a statement closes can push the bonus to the following statement |

Chase's terms add that the account must be open and not in default when the bonus is paid.

The 6 to 8 weeks matter if you want the points for a specific trip. Count back from the date you need to book: time to reach the spending, plus up to 8 weeks.

## The sum to do first

| Your normal card spending per month | Three-month total | Reaches $5,000 (Preferred)? | Reaches $6,000 (Reserve)? |
|---|---|---|---|
| $1,000 | $3,000 | No, $2,000 short | No, $3,000 short |
| $1,500 | $4,500 | No, $500 short | No, $1,500 short |
| $1,700 | $5,100 | Yes, with little margin | No, $900 short |
| $2,100 | $6,300 | Yes | Yes, with little margin |

Count only spending you would do anyway, or a large purchase you had already planned. Adding an authorized user at no fee on the Preferred lets a household member's purchases count on the same account.

## Who should skip it

- Anyone whose normal spending will not reach the figure in three months without extra purchases.
- Anyone who would carry a balance to get there. Interest does not count, and it can cost more than the points are worth.
- Anyone relying on balance transfers, cash advances or money orders. None of them count.
- Anyone who needs the points sooner than the spending time plus 8 weeks.
- Anyone who has already earned that card's bonus. See [the eligibility rules](/chase-sapphire-bonus-eligibility-2026/).

## Quick answers

**Does the annual fee count toward the Sapphire spending requirement?**
No. Chase's terms exclude fees of any kind, including the annual fee.

**Do authorized user purchases count?**
Yes. Chase defines purchases as those made by you or an authorized user on the account.

**How long until the bonus points show up?**
Chase says to allow 6 to 8 weeks after qualifying. Reviewers report they often post when the next statement closes.
