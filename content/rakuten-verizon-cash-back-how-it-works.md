Yes, but only for certain orders. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Verizon page showed up to $150 cash back, marked "was $75", paid as a flat amount per order for new phone service. Upgrades, bring-your-own-device orders, prepaid service, accessories and orders placed by phone or chat earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## Amounts seen on November 25, 2026

Verizon pays flat dollar amounts on Rakuten, not a percentage.

| Order type | Cash back shown |
|---|---|
| New service (phone plans) | $150 |
| VerizonONE (new mobile plus home activation) | $75 |
| Add-a-line activations | $50 |
| 5G Home | $45 |

The headline carried a "was $75" label, which Rakuten shows when an offer has been raised. The page does not say which lines were raised or when the raised amount ends. Not confirmed.

The terms say cash back amounts are per order. Read plainly, an order that opens several new lines earns the amount once, not once per line. How Verizon counts an order with more than one type of activation is not confirmed.

One oddity in the table: a new phone plan alone is shown at $150, and VerizonONE, which the terms describe as new mobile plus home, is shown at $75. The page does not explain the difference.

## Who qualifies

The special terms say cash back is available to:

- New customers
- Add-a-line orders
- 5G Home
- VerizonONE, described as a new mobile plus home activation

An existing customer can earn on an added line or on 5G Home. An existing customer replacing a phone on the same line cannot; that is a contract renewal, which is excluded.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Verizon list these as earning nothing:

- Business orders made through Verizon Business Markets
- Bring Your Own Device
- Contract renewals (upgrades)
- Prepaid services
- Accessories
- Gaming products, such as controllers, consoles and gaming headsets
- Subscription services
- Orders placed by phone or chat
- Gift cards

Because the amounts are flat and tied to activations, buying a phone case or a pair of earbuds through the link earns nothing, whatever the price.

## Payment must be processed first

The terms add a condition: cash back is earned only after the merchant has confirmed that the order payment has been processed. The page does not say what counts as processed for a phone paid in monthly installments. Not confirmed.

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "within a few weeks", which is slower than the same-day or next-day posting shown for many stores. Payment then follows Rakuten's schedule; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

## Coupon codes, trade-ins and Verizon's own offers

The Verizon terms have no line about coupon codes. Rakuten's general help page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back at any store. On November 25, 2026, the offers on Rakuten's Verizon page were links to Verizon promotions, such as a phone offered with a watch and tablet, and four lines at $25 a line on one plan. No code was listed.

The page also describes Verizon's trade-in program and switching offers. The terms do not say whether a trade-in or a switching credit affects cash back. Not confirmed.

## Pickup, app and stores

- **Pickup.** The page says Verizon offers free in-store pickup. The cash back terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
- **App.** The page describes the Verizon app for managing an account. The terms do not mention it. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
- **Phone or chat.** Excluded by name. An order started online and finished with a Verizon agent by chat would fall under this line.
- **In stores.** The Verizon page showed an online offer only. Verizon did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Who should skip it

- You are upgrading a phone on a line you already have. Upgrades earn nothing.
- You are bringing your own phone to Verizon.
- You want prepaid service.
- You are buying only accessories or gaming gear.
- You expect to finish the order by phone or chat with an agent.
- You are buying for a business through Verizon Business Markets.

## Quick answers

**How much does Rakuten pay at Verizon?**
Up to $150 per order on November 25, 2026, for new phone service. Add-a-line was $50 and 5G Home $45.

**Do phone upgrades earn Rakuten cash back at Verizon?**
No. Contract renewals, which the terms call upgrades, are on the excluded list.

**Do accessories earn cash back at Verizon?**
No. Accessories and gaming products are excluded, and the offer pays flat amounts for activations.
