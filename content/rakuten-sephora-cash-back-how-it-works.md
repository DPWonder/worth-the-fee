Yes. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Sephora page showed 4% cash back, raised from a usual 2%. Gift cards, auto-ship subscription orders and the part of an order paid with rewards are excluded, and a coupon code not listed on Rakuten may void it.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate here and at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/). The other large beauty listing is [Rakuten at Ulta](/rakuten-ulta-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## The rate, and how it moves

| Date checked | Rate shown | Rakuten's "was" label |
|---|---|---|
| November 21, 2026 | 4% | 2% |

"Was" is Rakuten's own label for the store's usual rate. The page does not say when the rate was raised or when it ends. No category table was seen in the part of the page that loaded, so one rate appears to cover all eligible items. Rakuten's terms say rates can vary by member.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Sephora list these as earning nothing:

- Gift cards and e-gift cards
- The portion of an order paid with rewards
- Auto-ship (subscription) orders
- Orders placed with the store's sites for Canada, Hong Kong or Singapore
- Orders the store judges to be for reselling

No brands or product categories are listed as excluded. That is less common. Many department stores on Rakuten exclude a list of brands.

## Coupon codes

The terms say: "Using coupon codes that are not listed on Rakuten may void Cash Back." The page advises using only codes Rakuten provides.

A sale code that is not on Rakuten's page puts the cash back at risk. The sums decide it, using a 20% code as an example:

| Order | A 20% code, no cash back | 4% cash back, no code | 2% cash back, no code |
|---|---|---|---|
| $100 | $20.00 off | $4.00 back | $2.00 back |
| $250 | $50.00 off | $10.00 back | $5.00 back |

If the code is also listed on Rakuten's store page that day, both may apply.

## Pickup, app and loyalty points

- **Pickup.** The terms do not mention in-store pickup. The page describes it only as a shopping option. Not confirmed.
- **App.** The terms do not mention the store's app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension. An order started directly in a store app is not expected to track.
- **Loyalty points.** The terms do not say that Rakuten cash back affects the store's own loyalty points. Paying with rewards is different: that part of the order does not earn.

Taxes and shipping do not count. Returned items lose their cash back.

## When you get the money

Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/) and [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/). Card rewards are separate and come on top; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- You have a sale-event code that is not on Rakuten and it is worth more than 2% to 4%.
- You use auto-ship for regular orders. Those are excluded.
- You pay mostly with rewards or gift cards.
- You order in the store's own app and do not want to start from Rakuten.
- You buy beauty products a few times a year and nothing else through Rakuten. A payment needs about $5 confirmed.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Sephora?**
4% on November 21, 2026, against a usual 2%. Rates change daily.

**Can I use a promo code and still get cash back?**
Only safely with a code listed on Rakuten. The terms say other codes may void it.

**Do gift cards earn cash back?**
No. Gift cards and e-gift cards are excluded.
