No. On November 27, 2026 (Black Friday), Rakuten had no store page for Michaels: the store's address returned Rakuten's page-not-found notice, so there was no rate, no "was" label and no list of exclusions. The one related listing with a rate was Michaels gift cards from a separate seller, at 1%.

This page is information, not financial advice. Store listings change, so check Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rates at stores that do pay, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## What Rakuten showed on November 27, 2026

| Where | What it showed |
|---|---|
| Michaels store address on Rakuten | Page-not-found notice |
| Rakuten's list of store pages (4,258 addresses) | No Michaels entry |
| Michaels Black Friday page | Not found |
| Rakuten search for the store name | Other stores, and Michaels gift cards |
| Michaels gift card page | Cards from CardCash at 1% |

This is a different case from a store that Rakuten lists with coupons only, such as [Costco](/rakuten-costco-cash-back-how-it-works/). There the store has a page and the page says it pays nothing. For Michaels there was no page at all, so there are no terms, no coupon codes and no posting time to report.

Why Michaels is not listed, and whether it will return, is not confirmed.

## It did pay once

An article on Rakuten's own blog about saving at Michaels, published April 23, 2021 and updated May 13, 2021, said shoppers would earn 4% there. The article is still online. It is more than five years old and the store page it points to is gone, so the 4% is history, not a rate to count on. When the listing ended is not confirmed.

The same article lists the store's own discounts as they stood in 2021, such as a coupon for joining the email list and a rewards program. Those belong to Michaels and never depended on Rakuten. Their current terms are not confirmed here.

## Michaels gift cards: the one listing with a rate

Rakuten has a gift card page for Michaels. On November 27, 2026 it listed cards sold by CardCash below face value, with 1% cash back beside each.

| Card | Price shown | Off face value | Cash back shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| $25 | $24.00 | 4% | 1% |
| $50 | $48.00 | 4% | 1% |
| $100 | $96.00 | 4% | 1% |

If the 1% is paid on the price, a $100 card comes to $96.00 less $0.96, or $95.04. Which base the 1% uses is not confirmed. The cash back falls under CardCash's terms on Rakuten, and the card then spends at Michaels like any other.

The seller matters. The same search showed a $25 Michaels gift card with email delivery sold by Staples, marked as earning no cash back. See [gift cards and cash back](/rakuten-gift-cards-cash-back/) for how these sellers differ.

## Stores and the extension

- **In stores.** Michaels did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 27, 2026. The list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).
- **Extension.** What the Rakuten browser extension shows on Michaels.com is not confirmed. With no store listing, there is no rate for it to activate.
- **Cyber Monday.** Cyber Monday is November 30, 2026. Nothing on Rakuten promises a Michaels listing by then.

## Who should skip it

- Anyone looking for a Michaels link on Rakuten for cash back. There is no store page.
- Anyone relying on the 4% in the 2021 article. That listing is gone.
- Shoppers who would buy a gift card only for the 1%. The gain is small, and the seller's terms govern the card.
- Shoppers with a Michaels coupon in hand. That is the store's own saving and is the larger number here.

## Quick answers

**Does Rakuten give cash back at Michaels?**
No. On November 27, 2026, Rakuten had no Michaels store page and no rate.

**Did Rakuten ever pay at Michaels?**
A Rakuten blog article from 2021 said 4%. That listing no longer exists.

**Do Michaels gift cards earn cash back on Rakuten?**
Cards sold by CardCash showed 1% on November 27, 2026. A card sold by Staples showed none.
