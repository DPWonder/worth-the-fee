For someone who flies only Delta, the Delta SkyMiles Reserve is the closer fit: it costs $650, gives 15 Sky Club visits a year against the Platinum's 10, and adds free checked bags and a companion certificate. The $895 Platinum fits a Delta flyer who also flies other airlines, since its Centurion and Priority Pass access does not depend on a Delta ticket.

This page is information, not financial advice. Terms change, so check the issuer's pages before deciding.

## Side by side

| | Amex Platinum | Delta SkyMiles Reserve |
|---|---|---|
| Annual fee | $895 | $650 |
| Additional card | $195 | $175 |
| Delta Sky Club visits | 10 a year, February 1 to January 31 | 15 per Medallion Year, February 1 to January 31 |
| Sky Club guest passes | None | Four one-time passes a year |
| Centurion Lounges | Yes, on any airline | Only on a same-day Delta flight bought with a US Amex card |
| Other lounges | Priority Pass, Escape, Plaza Premium | None listed |
| Earning on Delta flights | 5 points per dollar | 3 miles per dollar |
| Checked bags on Delta | No bag benefit; $200 airline fee credit can cover bag fees | First bag free, and second bag free on domestic flights, for up to nine people on a reservation |
| Companion certificate | No | One round trip a year after renewal |
| Status help | None | 2,500 MQDs a year, plus 1 per $10 spent |
| Main credits | Hotel $600, Resy $400, digital entertainment $300, Uber Cash $200 and more | Delta Stays $200, Resy $240, rideshare $120 |
| Transfer partners | About 16 airlines, Delta included, plus three hotel programs | None; the card earns SkyMiles |

On both cards a Sky Club visit needs a same-day Delta flight, and Delta's basic fares are not admitted. Entries within 24 hours count as one visit. Extra visits cost $50 each, and both cards become unlimited after $75,000 of spend in a calendar year.

## The lounge sum

Count Sky Club visits by airport days, not flights. Six round trips with no connections outside 24 hours is about 12 visits.

| Delta round trips a year | Visits needed | Platinum (10 included) | Reserve (15 included) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 8 | Covered | Covered |
| 6 | 12 | 2 extra visits, $100 | Covered |
| 8 | 16 | 6 extra visits, $300 | 1 extra visit, $50 |

At five round trips or fewer both cards cover the visits.

Guests change the count. The Reserve's four guest passes are worth $200 at the $50 guest price. The Platinum has no Sky Club guest passes, and sources disagree on its guest price: The Points Guy says $50 per guest and Upgraded Points says $29.

## The bag and fee sum

The Reserve costs $245 less. It also waives Delta's first checked bag fee, $45 each way on domestic routes.

- One traveler, six round trips, one bag: 12 bags, $540 saved with the Reserve.
- The Platinum's $200 airline fee credit can pay bag fees if Delta is the selected airline. That covers four bags and a bit.

For a Delta flyer who checks a bag, the Reserve is ahead by the fee gap plus the bag fees. From the second year its companion certificate adds a second ticket on one round trip for the taxes and fees.

## Where the Platinum is ahead

- **Trips on other airlines.** Centurion Lounges, Priority Pass and Escape lounges work with any airline. The Reserve's Centurion access needs a Delta ticket bought with an Amex card.
- **Points that move.** Platinum points transfer to Delta at 1 to 1 for a small fee, and to about 15 other airlines. Reserve miles stay with Delta.
- **Earning on airfare.** 5 points per dollar on flights against 3 miles.
- **Credits outside Delta.** See [what the Platinum credits are worth](/amex-platinum-credits-real-value/).

## Which pattern favors which card

- **Flies Delta six or more round trips, checks a bag, chases Medallion status:** Delta Reserve.
- **Flies Delta and one or two other airlines, carry-on only:** Amex Platinum.
- **Flies Delta four times a year or fewer:** neither lounge allowance is used up; compare on credits, or see the [three Delta cards side by side](/delta-gold-vs-platinum-vs-reserve-amex/).
- **Travels with a partner into Sky Clubs:** Delta Reserve, for the guest passes and the lower additional card fee.

## Who should skip both

- Anyone who buys Delta's basic fares. Those tickets do not get Sky Club entry with either card.
- Anyone who wants only the free bag. The Delta Gold card gives that for $150 after a free first year.
- Anyone who carries a balance month to month.

Welcome offers on both cards vary by applicant and change.

## Quick answers

**Which card gives more Delta Sky Club visits?**
The Delta Reserve, 15 a year against 10 on the Platinum. Both become unlimited after $75,000 of spend in a calendar year.

**Can I use a Centurion Lounge with the Delta Reserve?**
Yes, but only when flying Delta the same day on a ticket bought with a US-issued Amex card. Guests cost $50.

**Does the Platinum give a free checked bag on Delta?**
No. Its $200 airline fee credit can cover bag fees on one selected airline, but there is no bag waiver.
