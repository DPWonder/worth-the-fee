For most travelers the Sapphire Reserve fee is easier to earn back: its $300 travel credit applies to any travel purchase without enrollment, which leaves $495 of the $795 fee to cover. The $895 Platinum fee comes back only through several credits tied to named brands, so it fits people who already spend with those brands or who use Centurion and Delta lounges.

This page is information, not financial advice. Terms change, so check each issuer's pages before deciding.

## Side by side

| | Amex Platinum | Chase Sapphire Reserve |
|---|---|---|
| Annual fee | $895 | $795 |
| Authorized user | $195 each | $195 each |
| Flexible travel credit | None. Up to $200 a year in fees on one chosen airline, not tickets | Up to $300 each account year on any travel |
| Hotel credit | Up to $600 a year, $300 each half-year, prepaid Fine Hotels + Resorts or The Hotel Collection | Up to $500 a year, $250 per prepaid stay of two nights at The Edit hotels |
| Dining credit | Resy, up to $100 a quarter ($400) | Exclusive Tables on OpenTable, up to $150 each half-year ($300) |
| Other credits | Digital entertainment $300, lululemon $300, Uber Cash $200, CLEAR+ $219, Walmart+ $155, Uber One $120, Equinox $300, Oura $200 | StubHub $300, DoorDash promos up to $35 a month, Lyft $120, Peloton $120, Apple TV and Apple Music |
| Flights | 5x booked direct or through Amex Travel | 8x through Chase Travel, 4x direct |
| Hotels | 5x prepaid through Amex Travel, 1x direct | 8x through Chase Travel, 4x direct |
| Dining | 1x | 3x |
| Everything else | 1x | 1x |
| Lounges | Centurion Lounges, 10 Delta Sky Club visits a year, Priority Pass, Escape, Plaza Premium | Sapphire Lounges at eight US airports, 1,300+ Priority Pass lounges |
| Lounge guests | Centurion $50 each; two free at Priority Pass | Two free; $27 each after that |
| Transfer partners | About 16 airlines, Delta included; Hilton 1:2, Marriott, Choice | 16 programs, with United, Southwest and Hyatt at 1:1 |

All credits are "up to" amounts. Most Platinum credits need enrollment, several Reserve credits need activation, and unused amounts do not carry over.

## The Reserve sum

- Fee: $795.
- Travel credit: $300, applied to the first travel charges of the account year.
- Left to cover: $495.

The rest has to come from credits that take a choice. One prepaid two-night stay at an Edit hotel returns $250. Meals at Exclusive Tables restaurants, one in each half of the year, return up to $300. A reader who does both is past the fee: $495 minus $250 minus $300 is minus $55. A reader who does neither pays $495 for lounges, the higher earning rates and the travel cover. The page on [what the Reserve credits are worth](/chase-sapphire-reserve-credits-real-value/) goes through each one.

## The Platinum sum

- Fee: $895.
- No credit works on travel in general.

To reach $895 a reader has to add up credits they would have paid cash for anyway:

| Credit | Yearly amount | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel | $600 | Two prepaid stays through Amex Travel, one in each half of the year |
| Resy | $400 | A meal at a US Resy restaurant each quarter |
| Digital entertainment | $300 | A listed subscription every month |
| Uber Cash | $200 | A US ride or Uber Eats order every month |
| Airline fees | $200 | Bag or seat fees on one airline picked in advance |

The hotel credit plus any two of the others passes $895 on paper. Without the hotel credit it takes four of them. The page on [what the Platinum credits are worth](/amex-platinum-credits-real-value/) has the full list.

## Which spending pattern favors which card

- **Books travel direct and eats out often:** Reserve. On $6,000 of flights and hotels booked direct and $4,000 of dining it earns 24,000 + 12,000 = 36,000 points. The Platinum earns 5x only on the flights; hotels booked direct and dining earn 1x.
- **Spends mostly on airfare:** Platinum, at 5x against 4x.
- **Flies Delta or passes through a Centurion Lounge airport:** Platinum. The Reserve has no Delta lounge access.
- **Travels with two guests:** Reserve. Its lounges admit two guests free. Centurion Lounges charge $50 per adult guest.
- **Stays at Hyatt or flies United or Southwest on points:** Reserve. Amex points do not move to those three programs.
- **Would use Resy, Uber and a streaming subscription every month anyway:** Platinum. Those three credits add up to $900 a year.

For a single Hawaii trip, see the [Hawaii version of this comparison](/amex-platinum-vs-sapphire-reserve-hawaii/). For point values, see [Amex points against Chase points](/amex-points-vs-chase-points-which-are-worth-more/).

## Who should skip both

- Anyone who carries a balance. Interest costs more than either card returns.
- Anyone who travels once or twice a year and does not use lounges. A $95 card costs less; see [Preferred against Reserve for a light traveler](/sapphire-preferred-vs-reserve-light-traveler/).
- For the Platinum: anyone who does not want to track monthly and quarterly credits.
- For the Reserve: anyone who would not use the hotel or dining credits and rarely sees a lounge.

Welcome offers on both cards vary by applicant and change, so they are left out of the sums.

## Quick answers

**Which card has the lower fee?**
The Sapphire Reserve, at $795 against $895. After its $300 travel credit, $495 is left to cover.

**Which card has wider lounge access?**
The Platinum. It adds Centurion Lounges and 10 Delta Sky Club visits a year. The Reserve has Sapphire Lounges and admits two guests free.

**Do both cards' points move to Hyatt?**
No. Reserve points move to Hyatt at 1:1. Amex points go to Hilton, Marriott and Choice, not Hyatt.
