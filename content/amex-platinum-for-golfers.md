Very little directly. The US Amex Platinum has no free rounds, no green fee credit and no tee time priority; what it offers a golfer is travel help around the trip, mainly a hotel credit, airport lounges and some travel protections.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## What the card does not have

The current benefit lists for the US personal Platinum Card do not mention golf.

| Golf benefit | On the US Amex Platinum? |
|---|---|
| Free or discounted rounds | No |
| Statement credit for green fees | No |
| Priority tee times | No |
| Golf club membership or network access | No |
| Help booking a tee time through the concierge | Not listed on Amex's card page. Upgraded Points says the concierge takes golf reservation requests; you pay for the round |
| Packaged golf trips | None found |

Platinum cards in some other countries, such as Singapore, have golf programs. Those do not apply to the US card.

How this compares with Visa Infinite and Mastercard golf programs is on [premium cards, tee times and golf packages](/premium-credit-card-golf-benefits-tee-times-packages/).

The card costs $895 a year. Nobody should pay that for golf benefits, because there are none to pay for.

## What does help on a golf trip

These benefits are not about golf, but a golf trip can use them.

### The hotel credit

The card gives up to $300 back each half-year, up to $600 per calendar year, on prepaid stays booked through Amex Travel at Fine Hotels + Resorts or The Hotel Collection properties, or on prepaid Airbnb Luxe bookings. The Hotel Collection and Airbnb Luxe need a two-night minimum, and from January 1, 2027, Fine Hotels + Resorts does too.

Fine Hotels + Resorts stays also include a property credit of at least $100 per stay, daily breakfast for two and a guaranteed 4 p.m. late checkout. What the property credit can be spent on varies by hotel, so ask the resort whether golf counts before you book. CNBC Select says the credit "can generally be used on hotel property golf courses to cover green fees."

### Airline fee credit

The card gives up to $200 a year for incidental fees, such as checked bags, on one airline you select in advance. If that airline charges a normal checked bag fee for your clubs, this is the kind of fee the credit is described as covering. Whether a separate oversize or sports equipment fee counts is not confirmed.

### Lounges

On travel days the card gets you into Centurion Lounges, Priority Pass lounges after enrollment, and Delta Sky Clubs up to 10 times a year when you fly Delta that day. Centurion guests cost $50 each, which matters if you travel with a group.

### Travel protections

| Protection | What sources report | Golf caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Trip delay | Up to $500 per trip for delays over 6 hours, round-trip travel, 2 claims per 12 months | Covers delay costs, not a missed tee time |
| Rental car | Secondary cover up to $75,000 when the rental is paid with the card and the rental company's waiver is declined | Secondary means your own insurance comes first |
| Baggage | Reported at up to $2,000 per checked bag, with a conflicting footnote in the same source | Whether golf clubs are covered is not confirmed. Read the benefit terms before relying on it |

### Points

Green fees, pro shop purchases and lessons earn 1 point per dollar. The card's 5 points per dollar apply only to flights and to prepaid hotels booked through Amex Travel.

## Is that enough for a golfer?

Add up only what you would use. One golf trip a year with a two-night prepaid stay at an eligible hotel and checked clubs might return $300 from the hotel credit and up to $200 in airline fees. That is $500 against an $895 fee. The rest has to come from credits that have nothing to do with golf.

## Who should not get it

- Golfers who want rewards on green fees and club spending. This card earns 1 point per dollar there.
- Golfers who drive to their courses and rarely fly or stay in hotels.
- Golfers who stay in ordinary rentals or mid-priced hotels. The hotel credit only works at hotels in Amex's two programs and on Airbnb Luxe bookings.
- Anyone expecting course access or tee time help as a card benefit.

## Quick answers

**Does the Amex Platinum give free rounds of golf?**
No. The US card has no free rounds, green fee credits or tee time priority.

**Can I use the Amex hotel credit at a golf resort?**
Yes, if the resort is in Fine Hotels + Resorts or The Hotel Collection and you prepay through Amex Travel. The credit is for the stay, not for golf.

**Does the card's baggage insurance cover golf clubs?**
Not confirmed. Check the benefit terms before relying on it.
