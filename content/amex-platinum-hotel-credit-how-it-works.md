The Platinum Card gives up to $300 back in each half of the year, $600 per calendar year, on prepaid bookings made through American Express Travel at Fine Hotels + Resorts or The Hotel Collection, or on Airbnb Luxe stays. Any other hotel booking earns nothing, and an unused half does not carry over.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The amounts

| Period | Credit | What happens to an unused amount |
|---|---|---|
| January to June | Up to $300 | Lost on July 1 |
| July to December | Up to $300 | Lost on January 1 |
| Calendar year | Up to $600 | Nothing carries over |

If a booking costs less than $300, you get back only what you paid. Several smaller bookings in the same half can add up to the $300, according to One Mile at a Time.

## Which bookings count

Amex's own page lists three kinds of booking. All must be paid with the Platinum Card.

| Booking type | Where to book | Minimum stay |
|---|---|---|
| Fine Hotels + Resorts | Amex Travel site, app or by phone | None in 2026; two nights from January 1, 2027 |
| The Hotel Collection | Amex Travel site, app or by phone | Two nights |
| Airbnb Luxe | Amextravel.com or the Amex Travel app | Two nights |

The booking must be prepaid. Amex Travel shows two ways to pay for these hotels, and only the pay-now choice counts. Bookings paid later at the hotel do not.

Prepaid is not the same as non-refundable. One Mile at a Time notes that many prepaid rates can still be canceled under the hotel's own policy. Check the cancellation terms on the rate before paying.

Airbnb Luxe is on Amex's page, but the guides from The Points Guy and One Mile at a Time do not mention it, so details beyond the two-night minimum are not confirmed.

For how the two hotel programs differ, see [Fine Hotels + Resorts vs. The Hotel Collection](/fine-hotels-resorts-vs-hotel-collection/).

## Which half-year a booking falls in

The date you book and pay decides the half-year, not the date you stay. One Mile at a Time says the timeline is "based on when you make your booking and not when you stay." The Points Guy agrees that a stay does not need to be finished by June 30 to use the first-half credit.

So a booking paid in May for a September stay uses the January to June credit. That leaves the July to December credit free for another booking.

## Small rules that matter

- **Enrollment.** The Points Guy says no registration is needed. One Mile at a Time says in one place that enrollment is required and in another that it is not. Sources disagree, so check the benefits page in your account before booking.
- **Posting time.** Amex says the credit can take up to 90 days. The Points Guy reports about three days on average.
- **Cancellations and changes.** The Points Guy warns that the credit may be reversed if a booking is canceled or changed, and a new credit may not arrive until the next half-year.
- **Additional cardholders.** They can make a booking that earns the credit, but the account gets one $600 a year in total.
- **Taxes and resort fees.** Whether taxes and fees inside the prepaid total count toward the $300 is not confirmed.
- **Points.** Whether a booking paid partly with points earns the credit is not confirmed.

## What it is really worth

The credit is worth $600 only to someone who would have paid for two such stays anyway. Two things reduce it for everyone else.

First, the credit is a part payment. As an example, a $300 credit against a $700 booking still leaves $400 to pay.

Second, AwardWallet notes that Amex Travel prices can differ from the hotel's own price. Compare the two before booking. A $300 credit on a rate that is $120 higher than the direct rate is worth $180.

From 2027 the sums change for short stays. A one-night Fine Hotels + Resorts booking counts today. From January 1, 2027, it will need two nights.

## Who should skip it

Count this credit as $0 if:

- You book hotels with points or stay with one chain to earn status nights.
- You usually pay under $150 a night.
- You do not want to prepay for a hotel.
- You would take a trip only to use the credit.

To see this credit next to the others, see [what the credits are worth](/amex-platinum-credits-real-value/).

## Quick answers

**Is the Amex Platinum hotel credit $600 at once or split?**
Split. It is up to $300 for January to June and up to $300 for July to December.

**Does a one-night stay count for the hotel credit?**
At Fine Hotels + Resorts, yes, until the end of 2026. The Hotel Collection and Airbnb Luxe need two nights, and Fine Hotels + Resorts will too from January 1, 2027.

**Does the stay have to happen in the same half-year as the booking?**
No. Two sources say the half-year is set by when you book and prepay, not when you stay.
