For one Hawaii trip a year, the Amex Platinum mainly gives you up to $300 back on a prepaid stay at certain hotels, plus lounge access at Honolulu and Maui airports. It does little at Kona and Lihue airports, and one trip does not come close to covering the $895 annual fee by itself.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## Lounges at Hawaii airports

There is no Centurion Lounge in Hawaii. What the card opens depends on the island.

| Airport | What a Platinum holder can use | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Honolulu (HNL) | Delta Sky Club, Terminal 2 near gate F1 | Same-day Delta flight only; uses one of your 10 yearly visits; no basic economy tickets |
| Honolulu (HNL) | IASS Hawaii Lounge, Terminal 2, through Priority Pass | Space permitting; Priority Pass needs enrollment first |
| Honolulu (HNL) | Hawaiian Airlines Premier Club, Terminal 1 near gate A18 | Listed by Amex for the US card, up to 3 hours before departure. One travel site names only the IASS lounge for Priority Pass, so check first |
| Kahului, Maui (OGG) | Two Hawaiian Airlines Premier Club lounges | Up to 2 hours before departure, domestic flights only, space permitting |
| Kona (KOA) | None found | The only lounge is the Hawaiian Airlines Premier Club, which is for Hawaiian and Alaska premium passengers and members |
| Lihue, Kauai (LIH) | None found | Same as Kona; Priority Pass is not accepted there |

Two things to know before you plan around these:

- The Hawaiian Plumeria Lounge in Honolulu no longer takes Priority Pass. Some older guides still list it. It sells a $40 day pass at the desk.
- "None found" for Kona and Lihue comes from the state airport site and lounge guides. Amex's lounge finder did not show results for either.

## The hotel credit in Hawaii

The card gives up to $300 back each half-year, up to $600 per calendar year, on prepaid stays booked through Amex Travel at Fine Hotels + Resorts or The Hotel Collection properties.

On November 20, 2026, Amex Travel listed 40 Hawaii properties in these two programs: 25 Fine Hotels + Resorts and 15 in The Hotel Collection. The list changes.

| Program | Where in Hawaii | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Fine Hotels + Resorts | Oahu, Maui, Kauai, Big Island, Lanai | Halekulani, Four Seasons Resort Maui at Wailea, Grand Hyatt Kauai, Mauna Lani |
| The Hotel Collection | Oahu, Maui, Kauai, Big Island | Sheraton Waikiki, Hyatt Regency Maui, Ko'a Kea, Hilton Waikoloa Village |

How it works on one trip:

- The booking must be prepaid, made through Amex Travel, and paid with the Platinum Card.
- One trip in one half of the year earns one $300 credit, not both.
- The Hotel Collection needs a two-night minimum. From January 1, 2027, Fine Hotels + Resorts needs two nights too.
- Fine Hotels + Resorts stays also come with daily breakfast for two, a guaranteed 4 p.m. late checkout, a room upgrade when available, and a property credit of at least $100 per stay.

These are higher-priced hotels. If you would normally rent a condo, paying more to use a $300 credit may leave you worse off.

## Other things that help on the trip

| Benefit | What it does |
|---|---|
| Airline fee credit | Up to $200 a year for fees like checked bags on one airline you pick in advance |
| Hotel status | Hilton Honors Gold and Marriott Bonvoy Gold Elite, after enrollment |
| Trip delay cover | Up to $500 per trip for delays over 6 hours, on round-trip travel |
| Rental car cover | Secondary cover up to $75,000 when you pay for the rental with the card and decline the rental company's waiver |

## What it does not cover

- Lounges at Kona and Lihue, or the Sky Club when you are not flying Delta.
- Hotels outside Amex's two programs, condos and most vacation rentals. Only prepaid Airbnb Luxe bookings through Amex Travel count for the hotel credit.
- Whether the hotel credit applies to resort fees is not confirmed.
- Priority Pass restaurants.

## Who should not get it for this

If one Hawaii trip is your only trip of the year, the card returns about $300 on the hotel plus up to $200 in airline fees against an $895 fee. It makes more sense if you would use the second hotel credit and other credits anyway.

## Quick answers

**Is there an Amex Centurion Lounge in Hawaii?**
No. In Honolulu the card opens the Delta Sky Club on Delta flights and Priority Pass lounges.

**Can I use both $300 hotel credits on one Hawaii trip?**
Not in most cases. One credit is for each half of the year, so a single trip earns one.

**Does the card get me into a lounge in Kona or Lihue?**
No lounge access was found at either airport for Platinum holders.
