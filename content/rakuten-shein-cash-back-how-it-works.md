Yes. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's SHEIN page showed 6% cash back, raised from a usual 2%. Buying or redeeming gift cards, subscriptions and the redemption of SHEIN reward points earn nothing, and a coupon code not listed on Rakuten may void the cash back.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## Where the store page is

Rakuten files SHEIN under an older store name. On November 25, 2026:

| Rakuten address | What it returned |
|---|---|
| rakuten.com/shein.com | "Page Not Found" |
| rakuten.com/shop/shein | A general Rakuten page with no store rate |
| rakuten.com/shop/sheinside | The SHEIN store page, 6% (was 2%) |
| rakuten.com/sheinside.com | The same SHEIN store page |

A missing page at the first two addresses does not mean the store has no offer. Searching for SHEIN on Rakuten leads to the working page.

## The rate, and how it moves

| Date checked | Rate shown | Rakuten's "was" label |
|---|---|---|
| November 25, 2026 | 6% | 2% |

"Was" is Rakuten's own label for the store's base rate. No category table appears, so the one rate covers all eligible items. Rakuten's Black Friday page for the store showed the same 6% on the same day. Black Friday is November 27, 2026. Nothing on the page says how long 6% lasts.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for SHEIN list these as earning nothing:

- The purchase of gift cards
- The redemption of gift cards
- Subscriptions
- The redemption of reward points
- Orders that originate outside a list of 28 countries

The terms do not say which subscriptions they mean. Not confirmed.

## The country rule

This term is unusual among store pages on Rakuten. Cash back is paid only on orders that originate from the United States or one of 27 other listed countries, among them Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom, Australia, France, Germany, Italy and Spain. The terms say orders from anywhere else "will void" cash back. The terms do not say how the origin of an order is judged, such as by shipping address or by the country version of the site. Not confirmed.

## Coupon codes and points

**Coupon codes.** The terms say codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. Rakuten's page adds that SHEIN accepts a single coupon code per order.

**SHEIN points.** Rakuten's page describes the store's loyalty program as 1 point per $1 spent, with 100 points worth $1 off, and points covering up to 70% of an order before tax and shipping. Earning points is not named as a problem. Redeeming them is: the terms say cash back is not available on the redemption of reward points. Whether the rest of an order paid partly with points still earns is not confirmed.

## What it comes to

| Order, before tax and shipping | At the base 2% | At 6% |
|---|---|---|
| $29 | $0.58 | $1.74 |
| $60 | $1.20 | $3.60 |
| $100 | $2.00 | $6.00 |
| Gift card, bought or redeemed | $0 | $0 |

The $29 row is the free standard shipping threshold shown on Rakuten's page.

## App, pickup and returns

- **App.** The terms do not mention the SHEIN app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension. Whether an order finished in the SHEIN app tracks is not confirmed. The gap between the app and the extension is covered in [Rakuten app or browser extension](/rakuten-app-vs-browser-extension/).
- **Pickup and stores.** The page shows an online rate only, with no in-store rate.
- **Returns.** Rakuten's page describes free returns for up to 45 days, with free return shipping on the first return from an order only. No cash back rule for returns is listed for this store. Rakuten's general help page says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back.

## When it shows up

The posting line on the page says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "today". That is the pending entry, not the payment. See [how long until a purchase shows up](/rakuten-how-long-until-purchase-shows-up/) and [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

Temu, a similar marketplace, has different terms; see [Rakuten at Temu](/rakuten-temu-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Who should skip it

- You have a coupon code from another site that saves more than 6%. Only one code applies per order.
- You plan to pay with SHEIN points or a gift card.
- You are ordering from a country outside the list.
- You are buying a gift card.
- The order is small. At $29, the gap between 2% and 6% is $1.16.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at SHEIN?**
6% on November 25, 2026, against a base rate of 2%. Rates change daily.

**Why does Rakuten's SHEIN address show "Page Not Found"?**
The store page sits under the name sheinside. The shein.com address on Rakuten returned an error page on November 25, 2026.

**Do SHEIN points and Rakuten cash back work together?**
Earning points is not excluded. Redeeming points is: the terms pay no cash back on it.
