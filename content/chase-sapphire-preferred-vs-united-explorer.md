It depends on checked bags. A United flyer who checks a bag on two round trips a year saves more in bag fees than the United Explorer's $150 fee. A United flyer who travels with a carry-on gets little from the Explorer and more from the $95 Sapphire Preferred, whose points move to United at 1:1 and to 15 other programs.

This page is information, not financial advice. Terms change, so check Chase's pages before deciding.

## Side by side

| | Chase Sapphire Preferred | United Explorer |
|---|---|---|
| Annual fee | $95 | $0 the first year, then $150 |
| Main credits | Up to $100 each account year on Chase Travel hotel stays | Rideshare up to $60, Instacart up to $120 (through 2027), JSX up to $100, United Hotels up to $100 a year |
| United flights | 2x | 3x from the card, on top of the miles the fare earns |
| Chase Travel bookings | 5x | No bonus |
| Dining | 3x | 2x |
| Hotels booked direct | 2x | 2x |
| Everything else | 1x; 3x on gas, streaming, online grocery | 1x |
| Checked bag on United | No benefit | First bag free for the cardholder and one companion |
| Boarding | No benefit | Group 2 |
| Lounge access | None | Two one-time United Club passes a year |
| Where the points go | 16 transfer partners, United included | United MileagePlus only |
| Trip cancellation cover | Up to $10,000 per traveler | Up to $1,500 per person (one source) |
| Foreign transaction fee | None | None |

Both cards are issued by Chase. Each earns its own kind of point: the Preferred earns Chase points, which can become United miles, and the Explorer earns United miles, which cannot become anything else.

## The bag sum

United charges $45 for a first checked bag paid in advance, or $50 within 24 hours of departure, on tickets bought since April 3, 2026.

- One traveler, one round trip: $90.
- Two travelers on one reservation, one round trip: $180.
- Explorer fee from the second year: $150.

Two solo round trips with a checked bag ($180), or one round trip with a companion ($180), cover the fee. Two rules apply: the ticket has to be bought with the Explorer, and the cardholder's MileagePlus number has to be on the reservation. Only flights operated by United or United Express count.

## The points sum

Take $3,000 a year of United fares and $3,000 of dining.

| | Sapphire Preferred | United Explorer |
|---|---|---|
| United fares, $3,000 | 6,000 points | 9,000 miles |
| Dining, $3,000 | 9,000 points | 6,000 miles |
| Total | 15,000 points | 15,000 miles |

The totals match, but the points differ. Chase points can move to United at 1:1, so 15,000 of them can be 15,000 United miles, or they can go to Hyatt, Southwest or an airline with a lower award price for the same trip. The Points Guy valued Chase points at 2.05 cents and United miles at 1.2 cents in November 2026; One Mile at a Time uses 1.3 cents for United. Explorer cardholders do see lower award prices on some United flights, according to One Mile at a Time.

## Which pattern favors which card

- **Checks a bag on United at least twice a year, or travels with a companion who does:** Explorer. The bag saving alone passes $150.
- **Travels carry-on only:** Preferred. Without bags, the Explorer's fee has to come back from two club passes and credits for rideshare, Instacart and JSX.
- **Flies United and other airlines:** Preferred. Its 2x covers every airline and its trip cover is higher.
- **Chasing United status:** Explorer. It earns 1 Premier Qualifying Point per $20 spent, up to 1,000 a year. The Preferred earns none.
- **Spends $10,000 a year on one card:** the Explorer adds $100 of United TravelBank cash at that level.

Holding both costs $245 a year from the second year. Chase's 5/24 rule applies to both; see [how the 5/24 rule works](/chase-5-24-rule-explained/).

## Who should skip it

- Skip the Explorer if you fly United once a year without a bag. The fee is $150 from year two.
- Skip the Preferred if all you want is cheaper United flights with bags. It has no United benefit beyond points.
- Skip both if you carry a balance. Interest costs more than either card returns.

Welcome offers vary by applicant and change, so they are left out of the sums. Sources disagree on the Explorer's public offer today: Chase's own page showed one figure in its headline and another in its terms.

## Quick answers

**Do Sapphire Preferred points transfer to United?**
Yes, at 1:1 in 1,000-point steps. Transfers cannot be reversed.

**Does the Sapphire Preferred give a free checked bag on United?**
No. Among these two cards only the United Explorer does, for the cardholder and one companion on the same reservation.

**Does the United Explorer include United Club access?**
Only two one-time passes a year, added to the MileagePlus account. It is not a club membership.
