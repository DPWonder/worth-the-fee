Yes. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's IHG Hotels & Resorts page showed 9% cash back, raised from a base of 3%, across 18 listed brands. Rooms paid for with points, corporate, group and friends-and-family rates earn nothing, and a change made to the reservation at the hotel voids the cash back.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate seen on November 25, 2026

The page listed the same 9% for every brand in its rate list, each with a "was 3%" label.

| Rate | Brands listed |
|---|---|
| 9% (was 3%) | Atwell Suites, Candlewood Suites, Crowne Plaza, EVEN Hotels, Garner Hotels, Holiday Inn, Holiday Inn Express, Hotel Indigo, Iberostar Beachfront Resorts, InterContinental, Kimpton, Regent, Ruby, Six Senses, Staybridge Suites, Vignette, avid hotels, voco |

The "About" text on the page also names HUALUXE, Holiday Inn Club Vacations, Holiday Inn Resort and Holiday Inn – the niu. They are not in the rate list. Whether they earn is not confirmed.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for IHG list these as earning nothing:

- Bookings with IHG Army Hotels in the US and Canada
- Six Senses and Kimpton hotels that are not on the page's named lists (see below)
- Specialty negotiated rates, including friends and family, corporate and group rates
- Rooms booked and paid for with points
- The purchase of points
- The Ambassador certificate program, including Ambassador reward nights and the Royal Ambassador rate
- Bookings by travel agents who have their own partnership with the hotel group

Two things void cash back after booking: changes made to the reservation at the hotel, and coupon codes not listed on Rakuten. The wording for both is "will void".

## Six Senses and Kimpton: only named hotels

For these two brands the terms give a list, and only hotels on it earn.

| Brand | Hotels that earn |
|---|---|
| Six Senses | Seven: Crans Montana, Douro Valley, Kyoto, La Sagesse, London, Rome, Zil Pasyon |
| Kimpton | A named list of about 65 properties, from Al Rowda Doha to Vividora Hotel |

A Kimpton or Six Senses hotel that is not named earns nothing, even though both brands show 9% in the rate list. The list is long, so the store page is the place to look up a given hotel.

## What the 9% is paid on

The terms say rewards are based on the room rate alone. No other costs or charges tied to the stay count. Iberostar Beachfront Resorts is the exception: there the base is the all-inclusive rate, meaning the room with its meal plan.

As an example, a $600 room rate at 9% comes to $54.00. At the 3% base it would be $18.00. Rakuten's general help page says taxes are never eligible.

## Points, app and timing

- **Earning hotel points.** The page describes IHG One Rewards. The terms cover stays paid with points, not points earned. Whether a stay booked through Rakuten still earns hotel points is not confirmed.
- **App.** The terms do not mention the hotel group's app. A booking earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
- **Timing.** The page says cash back is added within 3 months after travel has been completed. Payment then follows Rakuten's four dates a year; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

Card rewards are separate and come on top; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/). Other booking options are compared in [Rakuten for hotels and flights](/rakuten-hotels-flights-cash-back/).

## Who should skip it

- You are paying with points or using an Ambassador certificate.
- You book a corporate, group or friends-and-family rate.
- You expect to change dates or room type at the front desk. That voids the cash back.
- Your Kimpton or Six Senses hotel is not on the named list.
- You have a code from outside Rakuten that saves more than the cash back.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at IHG?**
9% on November 25, 2026, with a "was 3%" label. Rates change daily.

**Do award nights earn cash back?**
No. Rooms booked and paid for with points are not eligible.

**Does every Kimpton earn?**
No. Only the Kimpton properties named in Rakuten's terms earn.
