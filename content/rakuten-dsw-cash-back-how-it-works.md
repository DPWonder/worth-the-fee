Yes. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's DSW page showed 2% cash back, with no raised rate two days before Black Friday. The part of an order paid with rewards, gift cards and purchases made with gift cards, and five named luxury brands earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate seen on November 25, 2026

The page showed 2% with no "was" label, so 2% is the plain rate. No category table appears. The Black Friday deals listed on the page that day carried the same 2%.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for DSW list these as earning nothing:

- Orders deemed to be for reselling
- The portion of an order paid for with rewards
- Gift cards
- Purchases made with gift cards
- Products from five brands: Gucci, Bottega Veneta, Ferragamo, Off-White and Versace

The gift card rule is wider than at many stores. It covers both buying a gift card and paying with one.

## What earns and what does not

| Purchase | Earns? |
|---|---|
| Shoes or a handbag from a brand not on the excluded list | Yes, 2% that day |
| A pair from one of the five excluded brands | No |
| The part of an order paid with a VIP reward | No |
| An order paid with a gift card | No |
| A gift card | No |

In dollars: an $80 pair at 2% comes to $1.60, and a $150 pair to $3.00. These are worked examples. Rakuten's general help page says taxes and shipping fees are never eligible.

## VIP rewards and the 2%

The page describes DSW VIP, the store's rewards program: one point per $1, and a $5 reward for every 100 points. It also describes a $5 birthday reward.

Earning points is not mentioned in the cash back terms. Spending a reward is: the portion paid for with rewards earns nothing. On an $80 order with a $5 reward applied, $75 would be the amount that counts, which is $1.50 at 2%. Whether points are still earned on an order that also earns cash back is not confirmed.

## Coupon codes

The terms on this page have no line about coupon codes. Rakuten's general help page says using codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. On November 25, 2026, the page listed sale offers, including 30% off a purchase for Black Friday, with the same 2%.

## Pickup, app and stores

- **Pickup.** The page describes buying online and picking up in a store. The cash back terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
- **Pay over time.** The page says Afterpay can be chosen at checkout. Whether an order paid that way tracks is not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The store page showed an online rate only. The store did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "today". It stays pending until the store confirms it; Rakuten's help page gives 3 to 14 weeks as typical. Payment follows on four dates a year; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). Card rewards are separate and come on top; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- You are paying with a gift card. The purchase earns nothing.
- You are paying most of the order with VIP rewards.
- You want one of the five excluded brands.
- You have a code from outside Rakuten that saves more than 2%.
- You often send shoes back. Rakuten's help page says returned orders do not earn.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at DSW?**
2% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

**Does an order paid with a gift card earn cash back?**
No. The terms exclude gift cards and purchases made with gift cards.

**Which brands are excluded?**
Gucci, Bottega Veneta, Ferragamo, Off-White and Versace products earn nothing.
