Yes, all three are listed. On November 21, 2026 Rakuten showed 2% at Dell, 2% at Lenovo and 1% on HP home laptops (3% on other HP items). Outlet stores, business stores, warranties and orders using an outside coupon code are mostly excluded.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check each store's page on Rakuten on the day you buy.

## Rates seen on November 21, 2026

| Store | Laptop rate | On a $1,000 laptop | Added to account |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dell (listed as Dell Technologies) | 2% | $20 | Same day |
| HP | 1% on home desktops and laptops | $10 | Not read |
| Lenovo | 2% | $20 | Not read |

HP's headline rate is up to 3%. The 3% applies to other non-excluded items, such as monitors and accessories. A home laptop is in the 1% line. Whether an HP business laptop falls under the 3% line is not confirmed; the page names only home desktops and laptops at 1%.

None of the three showed a "was" label, so these looked like ordinary rates. Electronics stores often get raised rates in sale weeks; see [Black Friday on Rakuten](/rakuten-black-friday-cash-back-2026/).

## What each store excludes

| | Dell | HP | Lenovo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outlet or refurbished | Dell Outlet and Dell Refurbished excluded | Not mentioned | Lenovo Outlet excluded |
| Warranties | Excluded | Not mentioned | Warranties and protection plans excluded |
| Store rewards | Orders using Dell Rewards excluded | Portion paid with rewards excluded | Portion paid with Lenovo Rewards excluded |
| Business and member stores | US consumer site only | Bulk or reseller orders excluded | Lenovo Pro, employee, education, government stores excluded |
| Outside coupon codes | Voids cash back | Not mentioned | May void cash back |
| Other | Preorders, gift cards | Ink, toner, gift cards | Legion Go, parts, non-US sites |

A few of these deserve a closer look.

**Dell Outlet.** It has its own Rakuten listing, which showed no cash back on November 21, 2026. A search summary of that listing showed 2% at some earlier point, so it changes. A separate Dell Refurbished listing at 2.5% was seen only in a search summary and is not confirmed.

**Dell Rewards.** Dell's wording excludes orders that use Dell Rewards, which reads as the whole order, not only the part paid with rewards.

**HP returns.** HP's terms say: "Partial cancellations/partial returns will void Cash Back for the entire order." Returning a $30 mouse from a laptop order can cost the laptop's cash back too.

**HP free gift cards.** If an order comes with a free gift card, the eligible amount drops by the card's value.

**HP phone and chat orders.** Orders completed by an HP call center or chat agent are voided unless tracked on the web.

**Lenovo order limits.** Cash back is limited to one order per shopping trip and three orders a month. Five or more of one unit counts as resale.

## Shorter deadlines for missing cash back

Rakuten's general terms allow 90 days to report missing cash back. Two of these stores set shorter windows:

| Store | Report missing cash back within |
|---|---|
| HP | 60 days of the order |
| Lenovo | 45 days of the order date |
| Dell | Not stated on the listing |

Check that the order shows as pending within a few days. The steps are in [what to do about missing cash back](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## Comparing the whole price

Cash back of 1% to 2% is small next to the price gaps between stores. A worked comparison for the same class of laptop:

| Option | Price | Cash back | Net |
|---|---|---|---|
| Store A at $1,000, 2% | $1,000 | $20 | $980 |
| Store B at $950 with an outside coupon, cash back voided | $950 | $0 | $950 |

A coupon or outlet price that beats the regular price by more than 2% is the larger saving, even with no cash back. The card you pay with adds its own rewards either way; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- You are buying from an outlet or refurbished store. Dell Outlet and Lenovo Outlet did not earn on the day checked.
- You buy through an education, employee or business store. Those prices are usually excluded.
- You have a coupon code from elsewhere that saves more than 1% to 2%.
- You plan to order by phone or chat with a sales agent.
- You may return part of an HP order.

## Quick answers

**Which of the three paid the most on a laptop?**
Dell and Lenovo showed 2% and HP showed 1% on home laptops on November 21, 2026. Rates change daily.

**Does Dell Outlet earn Rakuten cash back?**
Not on November 21, 2026. Its listing showed no cash back that day.

**Do warranties earn cash back?**
Not at Dell or Lenovo. Both exclude warranties; HP's terms do not mention them.
