The Hotel Collection usually costs less, so the $300 half-year credit covers a larger share of the bill. Fine Hotels + Resorts gives more at the hotel, mainly daily breakfast for two and a guaranteed 4 p.m. checkout, and until January 1, 2027 it is the only one of the two that works for a one-night stay.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The credit both programs share

The Platinum Card gives up to $300 back each half-year, $600 per calendar year, on a prepaid booking made through Amex Travel and paid with the card. Both programs qualify. A booking must use the prepaid, pay-today rate; a pay-at-the-hotel rate earns no credit. Prepaid bookings also earn 5 points per dollar.

One trip in one half of the year can use one $300 piece, not both.

## The January 2027 change

Today, a single night at a Fine Hotels + Resorts hotel can earn the credit. Amex's hotel credit page states: "Effective January 1, 2027, Fine Hotels + Resorts bookings will require a minimum two-night stay". After that date both programs need two nights.

This matters for the sums. At a hotel that charges $500 a night, one night with a $300 credit costs $200 before taxes. Two nights with the same credit cost $700. From 2027 the one-night use is gone.

The sources describe the two-night rule only for the statement credit. Whether it also applies to the breakfast and $100 hotel credit on a one-night stay is not confirmed.

## Which uses the $300 better

It depends on what you want the credit to do.

**To pay as little as possible.** The Hotel Collection tends to win. Its hotels are generally cheaper, so a two-night stay can land closer to $300. The Points Guy gives an example of a two-night stay that cost $577, or $277 after the credit.

**To get the most at the hotel.** Fine Hotels + Resorts gives breakfast for two each morning and a checkout you can count on. Amex puts the average value of the program's benefits at $550 per stay, based on two-night stays. That is Amex's own figure, and it assumes you would have paid for those things.

**For one night, through December 31, 2026.** Only Fine Hotels + Resorts works.

Before booking, compare the Amex Travel price with the price elsewhere. AwardWallet notes that Amex Travel prices can differ from the hotel's own price, sometimes by a lot. A $300 credit on a rate that is $150 higher is a $150 credit.

## Limits that apply to both

- The hotel must be in one of the two programs. Most ordinary hotels are not.
- Hotel loyalty points and elite perks are not guaranteed on these bookings. A Points Guy writer reports usually receiving them at The Hotel Collection hotels, but that is one person's experience.

## Who should skip it

Leave the hotel credit out of your sums on the $895 fee if:

- You normally book budget or mid-priced hotels. A credit that moves you to a $350-a-night hotel raises your spending.
- Your trips are single nights, from 2027 on.
- You book with hotel points, or need a refundable pay-later rate.
- You take one trip a year. Then the credit is worth $300 to you, not $600.

For a worked example on one destination, see [the Hawaii trip page](/amex-platinum-hawaii-trip/).

## Quick answers

**Does The Hotel Collection include free breakfast?**
No. Breakfast for two is a Fine Hotels + Resorts benefit. The Hotel Collection gives up to $100 to spend at the hotel.

**Can a one-night stay earn the Amex Platinum hotel credit?**
Only at Fine Hotels + Resorts, and only until January 1, 2027. After that both programs need two nights.

**Can you use the whole $600 on one stay?**
No. It comes as up to $300 in each half of the year, so one stay uses one piece.
