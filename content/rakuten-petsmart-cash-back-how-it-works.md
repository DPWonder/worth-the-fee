Yes. On November 24, 2026, Rakuten's PetSmart page showed 10% cash back, raised from a usual 2%. Pharmacy items, veterinary care, adoption services, gift cards and every Autoship order after the first earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate here and at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate, and how it moves

| Date checked | Rate shown | Rakuten's "was" label |
|---|---|---|
| November 24, 2026 | 10% | 2% |

"Was" is Rakuten's own label for the store's usual rate. No category table appears, so the one rate covers all eligible items. Rakuten's Black Friday page for the store showed the same 10% on the same day.

For history only: The Points Guy listed PetSmart at 20% on Rakuten on Black Friday 2025. Black Friday is November 27, 2026. Nothing on the page promises what the rate will be then, or how long 10% lasts.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for PetSmart list these as earning nothing:

- Gift cards
- Veterinary care
- Adoption services
- Pharmacy items
- Items with a quantity over 100
- Donations
- The part of an order paid by redeeming Treats Rewards points
- Autoship orders after the initial order
- Orders placed through a PetSmart site outside the US

The terms do not define "pharmacy items". Whether flea and tick products sold without a prescription count is not confirmed.

## Three terms that stand out

**Services can earn.** The terms say cash back is offered on grooming and PetsHotel stays, after the service is completed. Grooming packages are excluded. Training and Doggie Day Camp are not named in the terms; not confirmed.

**One order per shopping trip.** The terms pay cash back on only one order per click from Rakuten. A second order needs a new click through Rakuten first.

**Coupon codes.** The PetSmart terms say codes not listed on Rakuten "will void" cash back. Rakuten's general wording is that such codes may void it; the store wording is firmer.

## Autoship

Autoship is the store's repeat-delivery program. The first Autoship order earns cash back and later ones do not. Rakuten's page says the first Autoship order typically gets a percentage off and later deliveries get a recurring store discount. It gives no figures, so the size of either discount is not confirmed.

Chewy's terms on Rakuten have the same rule for repeat orders. See [Rakuten at Chewy](/rakuten-chewy-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Treats Rewards and cash back

Treats Rewards is the store's free loyalty program. Rakuten's page describes it as 10 points per $1 spent, with 1,000 points redeemable for $2 off. On those figures the points are worth about 2% of spending.

Earning points and earning cash back are separate. Spending points is different: the part of an order paid with points earns no cash back.

## What it comes to

Prices are those shown on Rakuten's PetSmart page on November 24, 2026.

| Purchase | Price | At 2% | At 10% |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dry dog food, 40 lb bag | $94.99 | $1.90 | $9.50 |
| Clumping cat litter, 38 lb | $26.79 | $0.54 | $2.68 |
| Pharmacy item or vet care | Any | $0 | $0 |
| Repeat Autoship order | Any | $0 | $0 |

Taxes and shipping do not count. On a $94.99 order, 10% is $7.60 more than the usual rate.

## Pickup, app and returns

- **Store pickup.** Rakuten's page says the store offers free in-store pickup for online orders. The cash back terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
- **App.** The terms do not mention the PetSmart app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
- **Returns.** No store rule is listed. Rakuten's general help page says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back.

## In stores

The PetSmart page showed an online rate only. PetSmart did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 24, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## When you get the money

The posting line on the page says cash back is added to the Rakuten account within a few days. For grooming and PetsHotel, that is after the service. Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/) and [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/). Card rewards are separate; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- Your orders are repeat Autoship deliveries. Only the first one earns.
- You are buying pharmacy items or paying for vet care.
- You have a coupon code from another site that saves more than the cash back.
- You pay most of the order with Treats Rewards points.
- You found the same item for less elsewhere by more than the day's rate.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at PetSmart?**
10% on November 24, 2026, against a usual 2%. Rates change daily.

**Do PetSmart Autoship orders earn cash back?**
The first Autoship order does. Later Autoship orders do not.

**Does grooming earn cash back?**
The terms say yes, after the service is completed. Grooming packages are excluded.
