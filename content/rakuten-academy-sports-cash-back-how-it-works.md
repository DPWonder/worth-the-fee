Yes, but only on part of the store. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Academy Sports & Outdoors page showed 2% cash back, with no raised rate. Clearance and sale items, Hot Deals, firearms, ammunition and gift cards earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate seen on November 25, 2026

| Item | What the page showed |
|---|---|
| Headline rate | 2% |
| Rakuten's "was" label | None |
| Rate by category | No category list |

A "was" label appears when a rate has been raised above the store's base rate. Academy's page has none, so 2% is the plain rate. Nothing on the page promises a higher rate on Black Friday (November 27, 2026).

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Academy list these as earning nothing:

- Clearance and sale items
- Hot Deals
- Firearms
- Ammunition
- Gift cards
- Orders Academy deems to be for reselling

The first two lines matter most this week. On November 25, 2026, the offers on Rakuten's own Academy page were nearly all discounts: up to 25% off Nike, 20% off select drinkware, up to 50% off game tables, and doorbusters. Each was shown with a "2% Cash Back" label beside it. The terms say sale items do not earn.

The page does not explain how the two fit together. Whether a marked-down item in one of those offers earns the 2% is not confirmed. The terms are the stricter reading. One member review shown on the page describes placing an order and then finding the exclusions meant no cash back.

The terms do not define "sale items" or "Hot Deals". Whether an item with a lower price for a few days counts is not confirmed.

## What earns and what does not

| Purchase | Earns cash back? |
|---|---|
| Regular-price item, ordered online after a Rakuten click | Yes, 2% |
| Clearance or sale item | No |
| Hot Deals item | No |
| Firearms and ammunition | No |
| Gift card | No |

At 2%, a $100 order of regular-price items earns $2.00. A $100 order of sale items earns $0 under the terms.

## Coupon codes

The Academy terms on Rakuten have no rule about coupon codes. Whether a code from outside Rakuten affects cash back is not confirmed. The page says Academy offers 10% off for military, teachers and first responders with ID; how that discount interacts with the sale-item exclusion is not confirmed.

## Where Academy does not ship

The special terms say Academy does not ship to Alaska, California, Hawaii or U.S. territories such as Puerto Rico, and does not ship outside the United States. The page says shipping is free on orders of $50 or more.

## Who should skip it

- Your cart is mostly clearance, sale or Hot Deals items. The terms say they earn nothing.
- You are buying firearms, ammunition or a gift card.
- You live in Alaska, California, Hawaii or a U.S. territory. Academy does not ship there.
- You are shopping Black Friday doorbusters and expect 2% on them. That is not confirmed.
- You shop in an Academy store. The rate is for online orders.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Academy Sports?**
2% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

**Do sale items earn cash back at Academy?**
The terms say no. Clearance items, sale items and Hot Deals are excluded.

**Do firearms and ammunition earn cash back?**
No. Both are named in the exclusions, along with gift cards.
