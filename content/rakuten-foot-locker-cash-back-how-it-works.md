Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Foot Locker. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Foot Locker page showed 9% cash back, marked "was 3%". Jordan Retro, the New Balance 550 and five named Nike lines earn nothing, and neither do gift cards or orders with five or more of the same item.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate seen on November 25, 2026

| Item | What Rakuten's page showed |
|---|---|
| Rate | 9% |
| "Was" label | 3% |
| Category rates | None listed |
| Posting line | Added to the Rakuten account within one week |

A "was" label means the rate has been raised above the store's usual rate. The page does not say when 9% ends, and nothing on it promises the rate for Black Friday or Cyber Monday.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Foot Locker list these as earning nothing:

| Earns nothing | Detail given in the terms |
|---|---|
| New Balance 550 | Named model |
| Jordan Retro | Named line |
| Nike LeBron, Kyrie, Foamposite, Kobe and PG | Named lines |
| Gift cards | Buying them |
| The part paid with FLX Cash | The store's rewards currency |
| Five or more of the same SKU on one order | The store's definition of a high volume order |
| Orders deemed to be for resale | Decided by the store |

## Orders over $500

The terms add that orders over $500 are subject to review for reseller activity and may be reversed. That is a review, not an exclusion, but cash back on a large order can be taken back after it. What the review looks at is not confirmed.

## Coupon codes

The terms say use of a promotional or coupon code not found on Rakuten may void cash back. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's page listed two codes of its own: an extra 15% off during the Black Friday sale, and $15 off a $150 order.

Other parts of the page say Rakuten cash back can be combined with student and military discounts and promo codes. Those passages do not override the terms. A code from outside Rakuten carries the risk the terms describe.

## FLX Rewards

Rakuten's page describes FLX Rewards, the free program shared by Foot Locker, Kids Foot Locker and Champs Sports: 100 points per $1 spent, redeemed for FLX Cash in $5, $10 or $20 amounts.

- **Earning points.** The page says FLX points and perks work alongside cash back.
- **Spending FLX Cash.** The part of an order paid with FLX Cash earns no cash back.

Whether a click through Rakuten's Foot Locker page covers an order at either of them is not confirmed.

## App, stores and returns

- **App.** The page describes the Foot Locker app and its launch reservation tool. The cash back terms do not mention the app. Whether an order finished in the Foot Locker app tracks is not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The page showed an online rate only. No in-store offer was shown on it. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).
- **Returns.** The page says the store accepts returns within 45 days of shipment. Rakuten's general help page says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back.

## When it shows up

The page says cash back is added within one week, after the order is confirmed and out of pending status. Payment follows every three months; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). If nothing appears, see [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## Who should skip it

- You are buying Jordan Retro, a New Balance 550 or a Nike LeBron, Kyrie, Foamposite, Kobe or PG shoe.
- You have a code from outside Rakuten that is worth more than the cash back.
- You are paying most of the order with FLX Cash.
- You are buying five or more of the same item.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Foot Locker?**
9% on November 25, 2026, shown as raised from 3%. Rates change daily.

**Do Jordan Retro shoes earn cash back?**
No. Jordan Retro is on the excluded list, with the New Balance 550 and five Nike lines.

**Do FLX points and Rakuten cash back stack?**
Earning both is allowed. The part of an order paid with FLX Cash earns no cash back.
