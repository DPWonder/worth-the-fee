A Southwest flyer who checks bags gets more from a Southwest Rapid Rewards card, because every Southwest card waives the first bag fee for up to nine people on a reservation. A Southwest flyer who travels with a carry-on, or who also flies other airlines, gets more from the $95 Sapphire Preferred, whose points move to Southwest at 1:1.

This page is information, not financial advice. Terms change, so check Chase's pages before deciding.

## Side by side

| | Chase Sapphire Preferred | Southwest Rapid Rewards Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Annual fee | $95 | $229 |
| Yearly points or credit | Up to $100 each account year on Chase Travel hotel stays | 7,500 points each account anniversary |
| Southwest flights | 2x | 4x |
| Dining | 3x | 2x |
| Gas stations | 3x | 2x |
| Chase Travel bookings | 5x | No bonus |
| Everything else | 1x | 1x |
| Checked bag on Southwest | No benefit | First bag free for the cardholder and up to 8 others on the reservation |
| Seats | No benefit | Preferred seat at booking; Extra Legroom seat within 48 hours of departure, when available |
| Boarding | No benefit | Group 5 |
| Lounge access | None | None |
| Where the points go | 16 transfer partners, Southwest included | Southwest Rapid Rewards only |
| Foreign transaction fee | None | None |

Chase's page for the Priority card no longer lists the $75 Southwest travel credit or the upgraded boardings that some older reviews still describe.

## The other two Southwest cards

| | Plus | Premier | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual fee | $99 | $149 | $229 |
| Southwest flights | 2x | 3x | 4x |
| Anniversary points | 3,000 | 6,000 | 7,500 |
| Seat selection | Standard seat, within 48 hours | Preferred seat, within 48 hours | Preferred seat at booking; Extra Legroom within 48 hours |
| Free first bag, up to 9 people | Yes | Yes | Yes |

This table comes from a Points Guy comparison dated February 2026. The Plus at $99 costs $4 more than the Preferred and carries the same bag benefit as the Priority.

## The bag sum

Southwest charges $45 for a first checked bag on Basic, Choice and Choice Preferred fares booked since April 9, 2026. Sources disagree here: one February 2026 comparison still prints $35, and two later pages give $45.

- One traveler, one round trip: $90.
- A family of four, one round trip: 4 x $90 = $360.

For the Priority, the 7,500 anniversary points are worth about $94 at The Points Guy's 1.25 cents a point. $229 minus $94 leaves about $135, which is one and a half solo round trips of bag fees. A family of four passes the full $229 on one trip.

For the Plus, 3,000 points are worth about $38. $99 minus $38 leaves about $61, less than one solo round trip with a bag.

## The points sum

Take $2,500 a year of Southwest fares and $3,000 of dining.

| | Sapphire Preferred | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Southwest fares, $2,500 | 5,000 points | 10,000 points |
| Dining, $3,000 | 9,000 points | 6,000 points |
| Anniversary | 0 | 7,500 points |
| Total | 14,000 points | 23,500 points |

The Priority earns more Southwest points. Chase points can do more: they move to Southwest at 1:1 and also to United, Hyatt and 13 other programs. The Points Guy valued Chase points at 2.05 cents and Southwest points at 1.25 cents in November 2026.

## Which pattern favors which card

- **Checks bags on Southwest, alone, twice a year or more:** a Southwest card. The Plus covers its fee on the first round trip.
- **Family or group on one reservation:** a Southwest card. One trip for four saves $360 in bag fees.
- **Wants a seat picked at booking or more legroom:** the Priority. The Plus and Premier open seat selection only within 48 hours of departure.
- **Carry-on only, a few Southwest flights a year:** the Preferred. Without bags the Priority's $229 has to come back from 7,500 points and seat selection.
- **Working toward the Companion Pass:** a Southwest card. Each one adds 10,000 qualifying points a year toward the 135,000 needed, and card spending counts.
- **Flies several airlines or wants trip insurance:** the Preferred, for its [travel insurance](/chase-sapphire-preferred-travel-insurance/) and wider partners.

Whether points moved from Chase to Southwest count toward the Companion Pass is not confirmed in the sources read.

## Who should skip it

- Skip a Southwest card if you fly Southwest once a year with no checked bag.
- Skip the Priority if the bag benefit is all you want. The $99 Plus has the same one.
- Skip the Preferred if nearly all your travel is Southwest with bags. It has no Southwest benefit beyond points.
- Skip both if you carry a balance. Interest costs more than either card returns.

Welcome offers vary by applicant and change, so they are left out of the sums. Chase limits the Southwest card bonus to people who do not hold a personal Southwest card and have not had a Southwest card bonus in 24 months.

## Quick answers

**Do Sapphire Preferred points transfer to Southwest?**
Yes, at 1:1. Southwest award prices follow the cash fare of the flight.

**Do all Southwest cards include a free checked bag?**
Yes, according to The Points Guy's comparison: the first bag for the cardholder and up to eight others on the reservation.

**Does either card include airport lounge access?**
No lounge access is listed for the Sapphire Preferred or for the Southwest Priority on Chase's pages.
