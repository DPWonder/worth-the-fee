After a one-time enrollment, the Platinum Card gives back up to $100 each calendar quarter ($400 a year) when you pay at an eligible US restaurant listed on Resy. You do not need a reservation, so it works anywhere an eligible restaurant exists, but outside larger cities there may be few or none nearby.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The basic rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Amount | Up to $100 per quarter, $400 per calendar year |
| Quarters | January to March, April to June, July to September, October to December |
| Rollover | None. An unused quarter is lost |
| Enrollment | Required, once, in the benefits section of your Amex account |
| Where | Eligible US restaurants on Resy. Restaurants abroad do not count |
| Reservation | Not needed |
| Card | Personal Platinum. Purchases by additional cardholders count, but there is one $400 per account |
| Timing | Amex says up to 8 weeks. Review sites report it usually takes days |

Several smaller bills in a quarter can add up to the $100. You get back only what you spend.

## You do not need to book through Resy

Resy is a reservation service, but the credit is triggered by paying with the card at the restaurant. Upgraded Points puts it this way: "You don't need to book through Resy for the credit to apply." Walk-ins count. One Mile at a Time adds that takeout counts if you pay the restaurant directly, and that you do not need to tell the staff anything.

## The 2026 change: check for the badge

In summer 2026, Amex updated the terms. A restaurant, winery or cafe must now be marked as eligible for the Resy Credit on the Resy website or app at the time you pay. One Mile at a Time reports that the venue's Resy page shows a badge marking it as eligible for the Resy Credit. A search-result copy of Amex's page gave August 1, 2026 as the start date; that date is not confirmed, because the page itself would not load.

From September 15, 2026, some restaurants that take bookings through Tock also count, with more being added over several months. Tock has been merged into Resy.

So the safe habit is: look up the restaurant on Resy on the day, and check for the badge before you rely on the credit.

## What does not count

- Gift cards and merchandise.
- Restaurants outside the US.
- A restaurant that is on Resy but not marked eligible.

Whether an order through a third-party delivery app, or a payment through a phone wallet, triggers the credit is not confirmed.

## Outside big cities

Amex and the review sites say more than 10,000 US restaurants qualify. That sounds large, but Resy is concentrated in cities. No source gives a count by town size, so the only way to know is to check your own area:

1. Open Resy and search your town, and the nearest larger town.
2. Count the places with the eligibility badge where you would eat anyway.
3. Add the cities you already visit each year.

Then score yourself honestly:

| Your situation | Realistic yearly value |
|---|---|
| Several eligible places you already go to, every quarter | Up to $400 |
| One or two nearby, or you travel to a city most quarters | $200 to $300 |
| None nearby, one or two city trips a year | $100 to $200 |
| None nearby, no city trips | $0 |

Timing matters too. Two city trips in the same quarter still earn only $100. One Mile at a Time notes that people in small towns without Resy restaurants may have to go out of their way to use the credit. A dinner you drive an hour for, only because of the credit, is not a saving.

## Who should skip it

Do not count the Resy credit toward the $895 annual fee if:

- Your area has no eligible restaurant and you seldom travel to a city.
- You eat out mostly at chains or casual places that do not use Resy or Tock.
- You dislike tracking four deadlines a year.

The Platinum Card earns 1 point per dollar at restaurants, so the credit is the card's only real dining benefit. ## Quick answers

**Do you need a Resy reservation to get the credit?**
No. Paying with the enrolled card at an eligible US restaurant is enough. Walk-ins count.

**Does an unused quarter carry over?**
No. Each quarter's $100 ends on the last day of that quarter.

**How do you know if a restaurant qualifies?**
Look it up on the Resy website or app. It must be marked as eligible for the Resy Credit when you pay.
