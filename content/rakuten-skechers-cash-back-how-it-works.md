Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Skechers. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Skechers page showed 3% cash back, with no raised rate. Orders greater than $500, orders with five or more of the same item, gift cards and the part of an order paid with loyalty rewards earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate seen on November 25, 2026

| Item | What the page showed |
|---|---|
| Headline rate | 3% |
| Rakuten's "was" label | None |
| Rate by category | No category list |

A "was" label appears when a rate has been raised above the store's base rate. This page has none, so 3% is the plain rate. The page listed the store's Black Friday sale at the same 3%. Nothing on the page promises a higher rate on Black Friday (November 27, 2026).

Rakuten's page gives one more figure: the average cash back earned per Skechers shopper was $11.37 as of the end of 2025. It does not say over what period.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Skechers list these as earning nothing:

- Orders greater than $500
- Orders with 5 or more of the same item
- Orders that do not originate in the U.S.
- Gift cards, bought or redeemed
- The part of an order paid with loyalty rewards
- Orders deemed to be for reselling

No shoe line or product category is excluded.

## The $500 line

The wording is "orders greater than $500", so the whole order loses cash back, not only the amount above $500. The terms do not say whether $500 is measured before or after discounts, tax and shipping; not confirmed.

| Order | Earns cash back? | At 3% |
|---|---|---|
| One pair at $90 | Yes | $2.70 |
| Four pairs, different styles, $360 total | Yes | $10.80 |
| Five pairs of the same shoe | No | $0 |
| A $540 family order | No | $0 |
| Two separate orders of $270 | Not confirmed | Not confirmed |

## Where the page disagrees with itself

The terms say "5 or more of the same item". The page's question-and-answer section describes the exclusion as orders with five or more items. Those are different rules: five different pairs pass the first and fail the second. The terms are the formal text. Which reading is applied is not confirmed.

## Coupon codes

The terms say that use of coupons or promotional codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. The page says Skechers advertises 20% off a next purchase for joining its email list. A code from that email comes from outside Rakuten, so it falls under the "may void" rule. On November 25, 2026, the offers Rakuten listed were sales, with no code shown.

## Skechers Plus

Skechers Plus is the store's free rewards program. Rakuten's page says 1,000 points are worth a $5 reward and that members get free standard shipping and 90-day returns.

- **Earning points.** The page says Skechers Plus rewards and cash back can be earned on the same eligible purchase.
- **Spending rewards.** The part of an order paid with loyalty rewards earns no cash back.

## Pickup, app and stores

- **Pickup.** The page mentions buying online and picking up in store. The cash back terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
- **App.** The page says the Skechers app gives new users bonus points. The terms do not mention the app. Whether an order finished in the app tracks is not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The page showed an online rate only. Skechers did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## When it shows up

The page says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "tomorrow", after the order is confirmed and out of pending status, and is paid every three months. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). For another shoe brand's terms, see [Rakuten at Nike](/rakuten-nike-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Who should skip it

- Your order is over $500. It earns nothing.
- You are buying five or more of the same item, for a team or a workplace.
- You are ordering from outside the U.S.
- You plan to use the 20% email code. It may void the 3%, and 20% is the larger number.
- You are paying mostly with Skechers Plus rewards or a gift card.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Skechers?**
3% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

**Is there an order size limit?**
Yes. Orders greater than $500 and orders with five or more of the same item earn nothing.

**Do Skechers Plus points and Rakuten cash back stack?**
Earning both on one purchase is allowed. The part paid with loyalty rewards earns no cash back.
