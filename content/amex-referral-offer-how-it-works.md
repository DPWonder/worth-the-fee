An Amex referral link is a personal link from an existing cardmember that takes you to a card application. The page says "as high as" because Amex sets a welcome offer for each applicant and shows you yours before you accept the card, so the number in the headline is the top of a range and not a promise.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## What happens when you use a referral link

1. A cardmember shares their personal link.
2. You open it. Amex says a referred friend "will typically be taken to a product page for the same American Express Card associated with the referral link."
3. You apply. Amex shows you the welcome offer you qualify for.
4. You decide whether to accept the card with that offer.
5. If you are approved through the link, the cardmember who referred you can earn a referral bonus.

You have to apply through that person's own link for the referral to count.

## Why it says "as high as"

For the Platinum Card, the offer is worded "As High As 175,000 points. Find Out Your Offer." New cardmembers may be eligible for up to that figure after spending $12,000 in the first six months. Many applicants are shown less.

What reviewers report about the process:

- You apply and see your offer amount, then choose to accept or not.
- One Mile at a Time reports "there won't be a hard pull unless you accept the offer", and that declining has no effect on your credit score.
- Some reader comments there say they saw a lower offer only after a hard credit check. That is not verified, but read each screen with care.

Nobody outside Amex can tell you what your offer will be. The spend requirement matters as much as the points: an offer that needs $12,000 of spending in six months is not useful if you would not normally spend that.

## Who is not eligible for a welcome offer

| Situation | What generally happens |
|---|---|
| You have or had the same card before | Not eligible. Amex generally allows one welcome offer per card in a lifetime |
| You upgrade or switch from another Amex card | Generally no welcome offer |
| You are an authorized user on someone else's card | You can still get the offer on your own account |

Amex may show a pop-up during the application saying you do not qualify for the offer.

## You can pick a different card

A referral link is not locked to one card. Amex says that from the page the link opens, a referred friend can look at other available American Express cards and choose a different one.

The card on the link may not fit you. Amex cards differ a lot:

| Card | Annual fee | Built for |
|---|---|---|
| Platinum Card | $895 | Frequent travelers who will use lounges and many credits |
| Delta SkyMiles Gold | $0 the first year, then $150 | Delta flyers who want a free first checked bag |
| Hilton Honors Surpass | $150 | Hilton guests; includes Hilton Honors Gold status |
| Marriott Bonvoy Brilliant | $650 | Marriott regulars; includes Platinum Elite status |

## Is a referral offer higher than the public one?

Not always. AwardWallet notes a referral offer "may not match other publicly available offers." It can be higher or lower. The Points Guy adds that referral offers "can also vary significantly from person to person, even among people with the same card."

Compare the offer you are shown through a referral link with the one on Amex's public page before you accept either.

## What the person who referred you gets

The cardmember who shared the link earns a referral bonus if you are approved. Three facts about that:

- The size of their bonus depends on their card and their current referral offer, not on which card you pick.
- Amex's page says a cardmember can earn a referral bonus up to five times per card account per calendar year. The Points Guy describes the yearly cap differently, as a points limit that varies by card.
- Referral bonuses are treated as taxable income for the person who receives them.

A person who sends you a referral link has a reason to want you to apply.

## Quick answers

**Will I get the full "as high as" amount?**
Maybe not. Offers vary by applicant, and Amex shows you yours before you accept the card.

**Can I choose a different Amex card from a referral link?**
Yes. Amex says referred friends can look at other available cards from the page the link opens.

**Does the person who referred me get something?**
Yes. If you are approved through their link, they can earn a referral bonus, up to five times a year per card according to Amex's page.
