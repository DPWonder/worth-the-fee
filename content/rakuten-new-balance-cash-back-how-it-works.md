Yes. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's New Balance page showed 9% cash back, with a "was 3%" label. Limited releases and collaborations earn nothing, and neither do gift card purchases, gift card redemptions or orders judged to be for reselling.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate

The page showed 9% with a "was 3%" label and no rate by category. A "was" label means the rate has been raised above the store's usual rate. The page does not say when 9% ends, and nothing on it promises the rate for Black Friday (November 27, 2026) or Cyber Monday (November 30, 2026).

## What earns and what does not

| Purchase | Earns cash back? |
|---|---|
| Shoes, apparel and accessories at regular or sale prices | Yes, unless excluded |
| Limited releases | No |
| Collaborations | No |
| Buying a gift card or e-gift card | No |
| Redeeming a gift card or e-gift card | No |
| An order deemed to be for reselling | No |
| Taxes and shipping | No, under Rakuten's general rules |

The terms do not define "Limited Releases" or "Collaborations", and the page does not list which shoes fall under them. Which products count is not confirmed.

The gift card line covers "purchase or redemption". Whether an order paid partly with a gift card loses all of its cash back or only that part is not confirmed.

## Coupon codes and discounts

The terms say coupons or promotional codes not listed on Rakuten "may void" cash back. On November 25, 2026, the offers on the page were sale links, not codes.

Rakuten's page describes store discounts of this kind:

- 15% off a first purchase for signing up for the store's newsletter. Another section of the page gives this as 10%.
- 25% off for students, teachers, military, first responders and others verified through ID.me. Another section says students get 10% through UNiDAYS.

The page does not agree with itself on the first two figures, and the terms name none of these discounts. Whether an order using one of them still earns cash back is not confirmed.

## What it comes to

Round examples, not prices from the page.

|---|---|---|---|
| Running shoes | $140 | $12.60 | $4.20 |
| Limited release or collaboration | Any | $0 | $0 |
| Gift card | Any | $0 | $0 |

## Pickup, stores and returns

- **Pickup.** The terms do not mention store pickup. Not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The page showed an online rate only. New Balance did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).
- **Returns.** Rakuten's page says New Balance offers free returns within 45 days of purchase. Rakuten's general help page says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back.

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "today". Payment follows on Rakuten's schedule of four dates a year; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). Two other shoe brands: [Rakuten at Nike](/rakuten-nike-cash-back-how-it-works/) and [Rakuten at Adidas](/rakuten-adidas-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Who should skip it

- You are buying a limited release or a collaboration. Those earn nothing.
- You are buying or paying with a gift card.
- You have a verified 25% discount or another code that is not on Rakuten. It may void the cash back, and it is worth more than 9%.
- You buy several pairs to resell.
- You are counting on 9% for a later date. The usual rate shown is 3%.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at New Balance?**
9% on November 25, 2026, with a "was 3%" label. Rates change daily.

**Do limited releases earn cash back?**
No. The terms list Limited Releases and Collaborations as earning nothing.

**Do sale items earn cash back at New Balance?**
The terms do not exclude sale items. The sale offers on the page carried the 9% rate.
