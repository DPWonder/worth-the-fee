Yes. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Samsung page showed 2% cash back, with phones, TVs and appliances all listed at that rate. Cash back is paid only on the amount left after trade-in credit or Samsung Rewards, and Employee Perks orders, memory, Samsung Care+ and gift cards earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate here and at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate, and how it moves

| Date checked | Rate shown | Rakuten's "was" label |
|---|---|---|
| November 21, 2026 | 2% | None shown |

No "was" label appeared, so 2% was the store's usual rate that day, not a raised one. The page lists these categories, all at 2%:

- Mobile phones
- Tablets and laptops
- Wearables (watches and rings)
- Home electronics (TVs)
- Monitors
- Home appliances
- All other non-excluded items

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Samsung list these as earning nothing:

- Business (B2B) purchases
- Employee Perks orders
- Memory
- Samsung Care+ and insurance
- Installation and setup
- Gift cards and gift certificates
- Large-volume orders, defined as 5 or more of the same item, counted across a buyer's orders
- Bulk purchases and orders Samsung judges to be for reselling
- Orders outside Samsung's US site

The terms add that ineligible orders may be canceled. They do not define "memory" further, so which storage products fall under it is not confirmed.

## Phones, trade-ins, carriers and discount programs

- **Phones.** Mobile phones are on the 2% list. Apple's listing on Rakuten works differently: iPhones are excluded there. See [how Rakuten works at Apple](/rakuten-apple-cash-back-how-it-works/).
- **Trade-ins.** The terms say cash back applies only to the portion paid after trade-in credit or Samsung Rewards have been applied. A large trade-in credit shrinks the cash back with it.
- **Carrier activations.** The terms do not mention carriers, activations or installment plans billed by a carrier. Whether a phone bought on Samsung.com with a carrier activation earns is not confirmed.
- **Discount programs.** Employee Perks orders are excluded by name. The exclusion list does not name Samsung's student, educator, military or first responder programs, so whether an order placed in one of those program stores earns is not confirmed.
- **Samsung financing.** The terms do not say whether a financed order earns. Not confirmed.

## App, pickup and coupon codes

- **App.** The terms do not mention the Shop Samsung app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension, so an order started directly in a store app is not expected to track.
- **Pickup.** The terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
- **Coupon codes.** The terms say coupon or promotional codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.
- **Returns.** The Samsung terms on Rakuten do not mention returns. Rakuten's general rule is that a returned item loses its cash back.

## What it comes to

| Purchase | Price | Trade-in credit | Amount that counts | Cash back at 2% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phone, no trade-in | $1,300 | $0 | $1,300 | $26.00 |
| Same phone with trade-in | $1,300 | $500 | $800 | $16.00 |
| TV | $1,000 | $0 | $1,000 | $20.00 |
| Employee Perks order | Any | n/a | $0 | $0 |

The prices and the trade-in credit are examples, not Samsung's figures. Taxes and shipping do not count.

Other stores on Rakuten sell Samsung products at their own rates. Best Buy's listing that day showed 1% on Samsung TVs; see [how Rakuten works at Best Buy](/rakuten-best-buy-cash-back-how-it-works/). A higher rate helps only if the price is the same.

## In stores

Samsung did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 21, 2026. The list was cut off, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## When you get the money

The Samsung page says cash back is added to a Rakuten account within one week. It then waits to be confirmed, which typically takes 3 to 14 weeks, and is paid on the next of four dates a year. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/) and [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/). Card rewards are separate and come on top; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- You buy through Samsung's Employee Perks store. Those orders are excluded.
- Your trade-in credit covers most of the price. Cash back counts only what is left.
- You have a promo code that is not on Rakuten and is worth more than 2%.
- You are buying memory, Samsung Care+ or gift cards.
- You buy for a business or to resell.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Samsung.com?**
2% on November 21, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

**Do Galaxy phones earn cash back?**
Mobile phones are listed at 2%. With a trade-in, cash back counts only the amount paid after the credit.

**Do Samsung discount program orders earn cash back?**
Employee Perks orders do not. Student, educator, military and first responder program orders are not confirmed.
