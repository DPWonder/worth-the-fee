The Platinum Card refunds the cost of a monthly Walmart+ membership, $12.95 plus sales tax, each month you pay for it with the card, which Amex counts as up to $155 a year. Only the monthly plan is refunded, so the membership is free to hold but worth something only to people who order from Walmart or use its streaming choice.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Amount | Up to $12.95 a month plus applicable sales tax |
| Yearly figure | Up to $155 per calendar year |
| Plan | Monthly Walmart+ membership only |
| Payment | Charged to the Platinum Card |
| Enrollment | Required, according to The Points Guy |
| Excluded | Plus Ups (paid add-ons) and the annual plan |
| Per account | One credit; additional cardholders do not get their own |

The membership renews by itself every month. If you close the card or move the payment to another card, the charge continues and the refund stops. Cancel it in your Walmart account if you no longer want it.

## The monthly plan trap

Walmart+ is sold two ways: $12.95 a month or $98 a year. The annual plan is the lower-cost one for most shoppers, but the Amex credit does not cover it.

| Plan | Cost per year | Amex credit | Your cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly, paid with the Platinum Card | $155.40 plus tax | Refunded each month | $0 |
| Annual | $98 | None | $98 |

A cardholder who already pays for the annual plan would need to switch to monthly billing on the Platinum Card to get the credit.

A newer plan has the same limit. One source, Kudos, reports that Walmart launched "Walmart+ with Sam's Club Plus" on October 20, 2026, at $120 a year. It is an annual plan, so moving to it ends the Amex credits. This was found in one source only.

## What the membership includes

These perks are listed by The Points Guy:

- Free shipping on Walmart.com orders with no minimum.
- Free grocery delivery from a local store on orders of $35 or more. This is not offered where no Walmart store is near.
- Up to 10 cents off a gallon at Exxon, Mobil, Murphy, Walmart and Sam's Club fuel stations.
- A choice of Paramount+ Essential or Peacock Premium.
- Free returns from home.
- Burger King offers and Walmart Cash on travel booked through Walmart's travel site.

On the fuel discount, Upgraded Points gives 10 cents in its article and 5 cents in its own FAQ. Check the amount in the Walmart app.

The streaming choice can overlap with another benefit on the card. Paramount+ and Peacock are also on the list for the digital entertainment credit; see [what the digital entertainment credit covers](/amex-platinum-digital-entertainment-credit-what-counts/). Taking one through Walmart+ leaves that $25 a month free for something else.

## Is the membership worth having?

Since the card pays for it, the question is how much use you get, not whether to pay.

| Your situation | Fair value per year |
|---|---|
| You already pay $98 a year for Walmart+ | About $98 |
| You already pay $12.95 a month | About $155 |
| You do not have it, but would use the streaming service and some deliveries | What you would have paid for those elsewhere |
| A Walmart store is near and you fill up at the listed stations | Depends on gallons: 300 gallons a year at 10 cents is $30 |
| No Walmart delivery in your area and no interest in the streaming choice | $0 |

The yearly figure of $155 is the price of the monthly plan, not the saving. A person who was paying $98 saves $98.

Free grocery delivery has a floor. Orders under $35 do not qualify for it, and the card refunds only the membership fee.

## Not confirmed

- Walmart's own terms page for the benefit could not be read.
- How long the credit takes to appear. The Points Guy suggests contacting Amex if it has not posted after a few weeks.
- Current prices of Paramount+ Essential and Peacock Premium when bought alone.

## Who should skip it

Count this credit as $0 if:

- Walmart does not deliver to your address and you do not shop on Walmart.com.
- You already get the same streaming service another way.
- You prefer the Sam's Club bundle or another annual plan.
- You would not cancel the membership after closing the card.

To see this credit next to the others, see [what the credits are worth](/amex-platinum-credits-real-value/).

## Quick answers

**Does the Amex Platinum cover the annual Walmart+ plan?**
No. Only the monthly plan, $12.95 plus tax, is refunded. The $98 annual plan earns no credit.

**Is sales tax on the Walmart+ fee refunded too?**
Yes. The credit covers $12.95 plus applicable sales tax each month.

**Do additional cardholders get their own Walmart+ credit?**
No. There is one Walmart+ credit per card account.
