No. The consumer American Express Gold Card is not on Amex's list of cards with cell phone protection, and its own benefits page does not show the benefit. What it does have is purchase protection, which can cover a phone bought with the card for the first 90 days.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## What Amex's own list says

Amex keeps a page of the cards that carry cell phone protection. On November 23, 2026 it named 16 cards. The Gold Card was not one of them. The page tells holders of any card not listed to call the number on the back of the card to verify their benefits.

| Listed on Amex's page | Cards |
|---|---|
| Personal | Platinum Card, Centurion Card, Delta SkyMiles Platinum, Delta SkyMiles Reserve, Marriott Bonvoy Brilliant, Hilton Honors Aspire |
| Platinum versions | Charles Schwab, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley |
| Business and corporate | Business Platinum, Business Centurion, Business Gold, Delta SkyMiles Platinum Business, Delta SkyMiles Reserve Business, Corporate Platinum, Corporate Centurion |
| Not listed | American Express Gold Card (consumer) |

The name causes confusion. The American Express Business Gold Card is on the list. The consumer Gold Card is a different product and is not.

## The terms on the cards that have it

Every card on Amex's page links to the same Guide to Benefits.

| Term | Detail |
|---|---|
| Maximum per claim | $800, the lesser of repair cost or replacement value |
| Deductible | $50 per claim |
| Claims | Two per card account per 12-month period |
| Covered events | Theft and damage |
| Condition | The monthly wireless bill is charged to the eligible card |
| Order of payment | After any other insurance that applies |

The full rules, exclusions and claim deadlines are on the page about [Amex Platinum cell phone protection](/amex-platinum-cell-phone-protection/). They come from the same guide.

## Where sources differ

An article from The Points Guy published in March 2021, when the benefit began, lists 12 cards and leaves out several that Amex lists now, including Business Gold. Upgraded Points, updated October 22, 2026, lists Business Gold. No source read for this page says the consumer Gold Card has the benefit.

## What the Gold Card offers instead

Amex's Gold Card page lists these protections. None of them works like phone insurance on a phone you already own.

| Protection | What Amex's page says |
|---|---|
| Purchase protection | Up to 90 days from purchase when an item is accidentally damaged, stolen or lost. Up to $10,000 per purchase and $50,000 per calendar year |
| Extended warranty | Up to one extra year on a manufacturer's warranty of five years or less. Up to $10,000 per item and $50,000 per calendar year |
| Trip delay | Up to $300 per trip after a delay of more than 12 hours, two claims per 12 months |
| Baggage insurance | Up to $1,250 for carry-on and up to $500 for checked baggage |
| Car rental loss and damage | Secondary cover when the rental is paid with the card and the waiver is declined |

Amex's page introduces purchase protection with the words "Dropped your phone?" So a phone bought with the Gold Card has cover for accidental damage, theft or loss in its first 90 days. After that, extended warranty only adds time to the manufacturer's warranty, which deals with defects, not drops or theft.

One source disagrees on the shopping protections. A December 2025 guide from The Points Guy does not list the standard Gold Card for purchase protection or extended warranty. Amex's own Gold Card page lists both.

## The sums

The Gold Card costs $325 a year and the Platinum Card costs $895. Cell phone protection alone does not make up that $570 difference. Compare the [whole set of benefits on each card](/amex-platinum-vs-gold-which-fits/).

For a phone already owned, a Gold cardholder's choices are a carrier or manufacturer protection plan, a homeowner's or renter's policy that applies, or paying the wireless bill with a different card that carries the benefit. The Gold Card earns 1 point per dollar on a wireless bill, so moving that bill gives up little.

## Who should skip counting on it

- Gold cardholders who assumed the phone was covered because the bill is on the card. It is not.
- Anyone who reads "Gold" on Amex's list. That entry is the Business Gold Card.
- Anyone relying on purchase protection for a phone bought more than 90 days ago.
- Anyone weighing the Gold Card mainly on insurance. Its strength is points on restaurants and US supermarkets, covered in [whether the Gold Card is worth its fee](/amex-gold-card-worth-the-fee/).

## Quick answers

**Does the Amex Gold Card have cell phone protection?**
No. The consumer Gold Card is not on Amex's list of eligible cards as of November 23, 2026.

**Does the Amex Business Gold Card have it?**
Yes. Amex lists the Business Gold Card, with up to $800 per claim after a $50 deductible, two claims per 12 months.

**Will the Gold Card cover a new phone I buy with it?**
For 90 days, under purchase protection, if it is accidentally damaged, stolen or lost. Limits are $10,000 per purchase and $50,000 per calendar year.
