Yes. On November 24, 2026, Rakuten's Dyson page showed 10% cash back online, raised from a usual 1%, and 1% in Dyson's own stores. Refurbished machines, spare and replacement parts, accessories and gift cards earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate here and at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate, and how it moves

| Date checked | Online rate | Rakuten's "was" label | In-store rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| November 21, 2026 | 10% | 1% | Not checked |
| November 23, 2026 | 10% | 1% | Not checked |
| November 24, 2026 | 10% | 1% | 1% |

"Was" is Rakuten's own label for the store's usual rate. No category table appears, so the one online rate covers all eligible items. The first two rows come from Rakuten's Black Friday and Cyber Monday pages; the third is the Dyson store page.

The gap between 1% and 10% is wide. On a $400 machine it is $4 against $40. How long 10% lasts is not confirmed, and nothing on the page promises a rate for Black Friday (November 27, 2026).

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Dyson list these as earning nothing:

- Camerajet toothbrush
- The Robot
- Chitosan products
- Refurbished items
- Spare parts and replacement parts
- Accessories
- Orders deemed as reselling, defined as more than 10 of the same product
- Gift cards
- Orders placed through a Dyson site outside the US

The product names are given as Rakuten's terms spell them. The terms do not define "accessories". Whether a machine bundled with extra tools is affected is not confirmed.

## Owner Rewards and coupon codes

Dyson Owner Rewards gives registered owners a one-time code for 20% off select products, as described on Rakuten's page. The page's FAQ says purchases made with Owner Rewards can earn cash back.

The same answer says to use only coupon codes listed on Rakuten, because outside codes can void cash back. The Owner Rewards code comes from Dyson, not from Rakuten's list. The page does not settle how the two statements fit together, so treat cash back on an Owner Rewards order as not confirmed. Note too that the 20% code covers spares and accessories, which earn no cash back in any case.

## In Dyson's own stores

The page showed 1% in store. This uses a card linked to the Rakuten account, with the offer added before paying. The in-store terms on the Dyson page say:

- US stores only, not US territories.
- Not valid on gift cards or online purchases.
- A debit card must be run as credit, without a PIN.
- After the first use, the offer covers purchases for up to 1 hour, then must be added again.
- An unused offer expires 45 days after it is added, or when the offer ends if sooner.

Rakuten's general help page says most in-store offers expire after 75 days. The Dyson terms say 45. The store's own terms are the ones shown for this offer. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Buying a Dyson somewhere else

The terms cover Dyson's US site only. A Dyson bought from another retailer earns that retailer's rate. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Best Buy page listed Dyson appliances at 5%; see [Rakuten at Best Buy](/rakuten-best-buy-cash-back-how-it-works/). Rakuten's page says Dyson has a Price Match Promise covering the same model at authorized US retailers within 30 days, with exclusions such as hair care machines. That is the store's policy and can change.

## Returns

Rakuten's page says Dyson accepts returns within 30 days of delivery and that refurbished machines are final sale. Rakuten's general help page says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back.

## When you get the money

The page says cash back is added to the Rakuten account in a few days, as pending. Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/) and [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/). Card rewards are separate; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- You are buying a refurbished machine. It is excluded.
- You need a filter, battery, attachment or other part. Those are excluded.
- Another retailer's price is lower by more than the day's rate.
- You are buying in a Dyson store and the online rate is 10%. In store it is 1%.
- You want the saving at checkout. Cash back arrives months later.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Dyson?**
10% online on November 24, 2026, against a usual 1%. In Dyson stores it was 1%.

**Do refurbished Dyson machines earn cash back?**
No. Refurbished items, spare parts, replacement parts and accessories are excluded.

**Can you use Dyson Owner Rewards with Rakuten?**
Rakuten's page says yes, but also says codes not listed on Rakuten can void cash back. Not confirmed.
