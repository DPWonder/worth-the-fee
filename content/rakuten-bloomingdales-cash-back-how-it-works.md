Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Bloomingdale's. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Bloomingdale's page showed 12% cash back, marked "was 2%". Gift cards, phone orders, orders placed through a style advisor or customer service, and orders with five or more of the same item earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate seen on November 25, 2026

| Item | What Rakuten's page showed |
|---|---|
| Rate | 12% |
| "Was" label | 2% |
| Category rates | None listed |
| Posting line | Added to the Rakuten account within one week |

A "was" label means the rate has been raised above the store's usual rate. Here the usual rate is 2%, so the raised rate is six times that. The page does not say when 12% ends. Nothing on the page promises the rate for Black Friday (November 27, 2026) or Cyber Monday (November 30, 2026).

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Bloomingdale's list these as earning nothing:

| Earns nothing | Note |
|---|---|
| Gift cards | Buying them |
| "Adjustments" | The terms do not define the word |
| Employee orders | Orders by store employees or with an employee discount |
| Phone orders | Also orders through a style advisor or customer service |
| Five or more of the same item | Counted per order |
| Reselling | Orders the store deems to be for resale |

The terms name no excluded brands or departments. Whether every brand sold on the site earns the full rate is not confirmed beyond that.

Rakuten's page says the store offers price adjustments within 10 days of an order. The terms exclude "adjustments" from cash back. Whether a price adjustment lowers the amount that earns, or removes the cash back, is not confirmed.

## Coupon codes

The Bloomingdale's terms have no line about coupon codes. Rakuten's general help page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. Rakuten's page describes a 10% off code for signing up for the store's emails or texts. That code comes from the store, not from Rakuten, so whether it is safe to use with cash back is not confirmed.

## Loyallist points

Rakuten's page describes Loyallist, the store's rewards program: one point per $1 spent, and $25 for every 5,000 points. Members get free shipping with no minimum; others need $150 or more.

The cash back terms do not mention Loyallist. Whether the part of an order paid with a Loyallist reward earns cash back is not confirmed.

## Pickup, outlets and stores

- **Pickup.** The page describes buy online, pick up in store for eligible items. The cash back terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
- **Outlets.** The page mentions Bloomingdale's outlets as a place to earn Loyallist points. It does not say outlet purchases earn cash back. Not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The page showed an online rate only. No in-store offer was shown on it. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).
- **Returns.** Rakuten's general help page says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back.

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account within one week. Payment follows every three months; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). If nothing appears, see [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## Who should skip it

- You order by phone or through a style advisor. Those orders are excluded.
- You are buying gift cards.
- You are buying five or more of the same item in one order.
- You shop with an employee discount.
- You are counting on 12% next week. The usual rate shown is 2%, and no end date is given for the raised one.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Bloomingdale's?**
12% on November 25, 2026, shown as raised from 2%. Rates change daily.

**Do phone orders earn cash back?**
No. Phone orders and orders through a style advisor or customer service are excluded.

**Do Loyallist points and Rakuten cash back stack?**
The terms do not mention Loyallist. Whether an order paid partly with a reward earns is not confirmed.
