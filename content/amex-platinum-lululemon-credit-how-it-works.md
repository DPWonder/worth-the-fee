The Platinum Card gives up to $75 back each calendar quarter, $300 a year, on purchases at US lululemon stores and on lululemon.com, once the card is enrolled. Outlet stores, gift cards and orders shipped outside the US are excluded, and an unused quarter is lost.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The amounts

The credit was added to the card in September 2025. It comes in four pieces.

| Quarter | Credit | Unused amount |
|---|---|---|
| January to March | Up to $75 | Lost on April 1 |
| April to June | Up to $75 | Lost on July 1 |
| July to September | Up to $75 | Lost on October 1 |
| October to December | Up to $75 | Lost on January 1 |

The quarters cannot be pooled. A $300 jacket bought in one visit earns $75, not $300. A $40 purchase earns $40, and the other $35 of that quarter can still be used on a later purchase before the quarter ends.

## How to turn it on

Enrollment is required, and it is done once. The basic cardmember enrolls in the benefits section of the Amex account. After that, a purchase on any card on the account can earn the credit, but the account gets one $300 a year in total. Additional cardholders do not get their own.

Purchases made before enrolling do not count.

## What counts and what does not

| Purchase | Counts? |
|---|---|
| Clothing and gear at a US lululemon retail store | Yes |
| Orders on lululemon.com shipped in the US | Yes |
| Sale items on lululemon.com | No rule against them was found |
| lululemon outlet stores | No |
| Stores outside the US | No |
| Orders shipped outside the US | No |
| Gift cards | No, under the terms |
| lululemon products sold by another retailer | Not listed as eligible |

The terms, as quoted by AwardWallet, say the credit is "Not valid on purchases shipped outside the U.S. or gift card purchases."

### Where sources disagree: gift cards

The terms exclude gift cards. Doctor of Credit reported that physical gift cards bought in a store had still been earning the credit for some people. Its update of October 13, 2026 says gift card purchases no longer work. Electronic gift cards bought online never did. Do not plan on a gift card earning the credit.

### Not confirmed

- Whether purchases in the lululemon app count.
- What happens to the credit when an item is returned.
- Whether the Business Platinum Card has this credit. No source lists it there.

## Timing

Amex's terms allow up to 8 weeks for the credit to appear. AwardWallet says most post within a few days.

Online orders carry one risk near the end of a quarter. Doctor of Credit notes that an online charge may not be completed until the order ships. An order placed on the last day of March could be charged in April and use the next quarter's credit. A purchase in a store is charged the same day.

Apple Pay in a store works, according to the same source.

## What it is really worth

The credit is worth its full amount only on money you would have spent anyway. On an item that costs more than $75, you pay the rest.

| Your habit | Fair value per year |
|---|---|
| You already spend $75 or more a quarter at lululemon | $300 |
| You buy there once or twice a year | $75 to $150 |
| You would buy something small each quarter that you need anyway (socks, a headband) | Less than $300; count what you would have paid elsewhere |
| You never shop there | $0 |

Take the third row as an example. If a $28 item bought with the credit replaces a $10 one you would have bought elsewhere, the credit saved you $10, not $28.

## Who should skip it

Leave this credit out of your sums if:

- No lululemon retail store is near you and you do not want to order clothes online.
- The only store near you is an outlet.
- You live outside the US for most of the year.
- You would not remember to use a credit four times a year.

To see this credit next to the others on the card, see [what the credits are worth](/amex-platinum-credits-real-value/). The Resy credit follows the same quarterly pattern; see [how the Resy credit works](/amex-platinum-resy-credit-how-it-works/).

## Quick answers

**Does the lululemon credit roll over if a quarter is missed?**
No. Each quarter's $75 is lost when the quarter ends.

**Do lululemon outlet stores count?**
No. The credit covers US lululemon retail stores and lululemon.com, and outlets are excluded.

**Do gift cards earn the lululemon credit?**
The terms exclude gift cards. Reports that store-bought gift cards worked have been followed by a report that they no longer do.
