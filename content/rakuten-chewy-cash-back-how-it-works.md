Yes. On November 22, 2026, Rakuten's Chewy page showed 4% cash back, raised from a usual 2%. Pet prescriptions, flea and tick products, Chewy+ subscriptions, gift cards and every Autoship order after the first are excluded.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate here and at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate, and how it moves

| Date checked | Rate shown | Rakuten's "was" label |
|---|---|---|
| November 22, 2026 | 4% | 2% |

"Was" is Rakuten's own label for the store's usual rate. No category table appears, so the one rate covers all eligible items.

Black Friday is November 27, 2026. Rates often move that week, in either direction, and nothing on the page promises what the rate will be.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Chewy list these as earning nothing:

- Pet prescriptions
- Flea and tick products
- Chewy+ subscriptions
- The purchase or redemption of gift cards
- Autoship orders after the initial order

The terms do not define "flea and tick products". Whether that covers only treatments, or also collars, shampoos and sprays, is not confirmed. Whether an excluded item affects the rest of an order is not stated either.

## Autoship is the term that matters

Autoship is Chewy's repeat-delivery program. Rakuten's page is clear on it: the first Autoship order earns cash back, and the page's FAQ says "subsequent Autoship orders do not qualify for Cash Back."

The page says later Autoship orders get the store's own 5% discount. That is a store discount, described on Rakuten's page on November 22, 2026, and it can change.

That leaves a sum for regular buyers. A one-time order placed through Rakuten earns the day's rate. A repeat Autoship order earns no cash back but gets the store's Autoship discount.

| $80 food order | Store discount | Rakuten cash back | Total back |
|---|---|---|---|
| One-time, at 2% | $0 | $1.60 | $1.60 |
| One-time, at 4% | $0 | $3.20 | $3.20 |
| Repeat Autoship, 5% off | $4.00 | $0 | $4.00 |

At these figures the repeat Autoship discount is worth more than either rate, and it arrives as a lower price rather than weeks later. Taxes and shipping do not count toward cash back. Whether canceling Autoship and starting a new one counts as a new "initial order" is not confirmed.

## Other terms

- **Coupon codes.** The Chewy terms list no code rule. Rakuten's general page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.
- **App.** The page's FAQ says to reach Chewy through Rakuten's site or the Rakuten app. The terms do not mention orders placed directly in the Chewy app. Not confirmed; a purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
- **Returns.** No store rule is listed. Rakuten's general help page says returned and canceled items lose their cash back.

## In stores

Chewy sells online. It did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 22, 2026. The list was cut off after 21 stores. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## When you get the money

The page says cash back is added to the Rakuten account the same day, as pending. Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/) and [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/). Card rewards are separate and come on top; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- Your orders are repeat Autoship deliveries. Only the first one earns.
- You mainly buy prescriptions or flea and tick products.
- You are buying or spending gift cards.
- You order directly in the Chewy app and do not want to start from Rakuten.
- You are paying for a Chewy+ subscription.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Chewy?**
4% on November 22, 2026, against a usual 2%. Rates change daily.

**Do Autoship orders earn cash back?**
The first Autoship order does. Later Autoship orders do not.

**Do pet medications earn cash back?**
Pet prescriptions and flea and tick products are excluded.
