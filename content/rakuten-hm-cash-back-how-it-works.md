Yes, on full-price items. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's H&M page showed 2% cash back, with no raised rate two days before Black Friday. The biggest exclusion is sale items, which earn nothing; shipping, gift cards and Rabanne items are excluded too.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate seen on November 25, 2026

The page showed 2% with no "was" label, so 2% is the plain rate. No category table appears.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for H&M list these as earning nothing:

- Sale items
- Shipping
- The purchase or redemption of gift cards
- Rabanne items
- Orders on any site other than the US site
- Orders the store determines are shipping to Mexico, South Korea, Bulgaria, the Czech Republic, Greece, Croatia, Hungary, Poland, Romania, Serbia or Slovakia

## The sale-item rule during a sale week

This matters most in late November. On November 25, 2026, the same Rakuten page listed two store promotions: up to 40% off sitewide, and Black Friday deals of up to 50% off. Both were shown with 2%.

The terms do not define "sale items". Two readings are possible:

- Only items in the store's sale section are excluded.
- Any item with a reduced price, including during a sitewide promotion, is excluded.

Which one the store applies is not confirmed. If the wider reading holds, little in a sitewide promotion would earn. The exclusions on the activation page on the day of purchase are the place to look.

## What earns and what does not

| Purchase | Earns? |
|---|---|
| Full-price clothing or home goods on the US site | Yes, 2% that day |
| Item from the sale section | No |
| Item discounted in a sitewide promotion | Not confirmed |
| Rabanne item | No |
| Gift card, or an order paid with one | No |
| Shipping charge | No |
| Order shipping to one of the listed countries | No |

In dollars: $60 of full-price items at 2% comes to $1.20. This is a worked example.

## Coupon codes and member offers

The terms on this page have no line about coupon codes. Rakuten's general help page says using codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.

The page describes several discounts that come from the store, not from Rakuten: 25% off one item for joining the email list, a birthday coupon for members and a student discount. Whether using one of them affects the 2% is not confirmed. Whether an item bought with such a discount then counts as a sale item is also not confirmed.

## App, pickup and stores

- **App.** The page says the store has an app with occasional app-only discounts. The terms do not mention the app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension; whether an order finished in the store's app tracks is not confirmed.
- **Pickup.** Neither the page nor the terms mention store pickup. Not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The store page showed an online rate only. The store did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## When it shows up

The page says cash back is added to the Rakuten account within one week. Payment follows on Rakuten's four dates a year; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). If an order does not appear, see [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## Who should skip it

- You mostly buy from the sale section. Those items earn nothing.
- You are shopping a sitewide promotion and need certainty. Whether those items earn is not confirmed.
- You are buying Rabanne items or a gift card.
- You shop on a site other than the US one.
- You have a member or student code that saves more than 2%.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at H&M?**
2% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

**Do sale items earn cash back?**
No. Sale items are on the excluded list. The terms do not define the phrase.

**Does shipping count?**
No. Shipping is excluded, as is the purchase or redemption of gift cards.
