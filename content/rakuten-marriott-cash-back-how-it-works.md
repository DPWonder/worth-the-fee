Yes. On November 26, 2026, Rakuten's Marriott.com page showed 3% cash back on bookings, with no "was" label, paid on the room rate of a completed stay and capped at $100 per order. Rooms paid with points, member-only and negotiated rates, and hotels in Asia or Australia earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you book. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate seen on November 26, 2026

| Listing on Rakuten | Rate shown | "Was" label |
|---|---|---|
| Marriott.com, one tier: "Marriott.com Bookings" | 3% | None |
| Marriott Bonvoy – Points (buying points only) | 1.25% | None |

The store page sits at rakuten.com/shop/marriotthotelsandresorts. The address rakuten.com/marriott.com led to Rakuten's "page not found" screen that day. The points listing says in its terms that it does not cover hotel bookings.

## What the 3% is paid on

The terms say cash back is available on the room rate, and not on fees, taxes or VAT. Resort fees are not named one by one; the word used is "fees".

A special term limits the amount: a maximum of $100 may be earned per order. At 3%, that limit is reached at a room rate of $3,333.33.

| Room rate for the stay | 3% works out to | Paid after the cap |
|---|---|---|
| $450 (three nights at $150) | $13.50 | $13.50 |
| $1,200 | $36.00 | $36.00 |
| $3,333.33 | $100.00 | $100.00 |
| $5,000 | $150.00 | $100.00 |

On a bill of $450 in room charges plus $63 in taxes and fees, the base is $450, not $513. Whether a booking of two rooms counts as one order or two is not confirmed.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Marriott.com list these as earning nothing:

- Rooms booked and paid for with Marriott Bonvoy points
- Member Exclusive Rates, Member Flexible Rates, Bonvoy Offer discounts and packages, and Elite Exclusive Offers
- Corporate, government, group, event, employee and friends-and-family rates
- Airfare, car rentals, vacation packages and gift cards
- Properties located in Asia or Australia
- Four brands: Ritz-Carlton Destination Club, Design Hotels, Bulgari and W Hotels
- About two dozen named properties, among them Atlantis, The Cosmopolitan Las Vegas, The Ritz-Carlton London, The Westin Resort Guam, five resorts in Fiji and Four Points by Sheraton Havana

The member-rate lines matter for anyone signed in to a Bonvoy account while booking. Which rate types Marriott's site offers a signed-in member, and how each is labeled at checkout, is not confirmed here. The terms name the rate types; they do not say how Rakuten identifies them.

Rakuten's gift card FAQ also names Marriott among stores where the part of a purchase paid with a gift card earns no cash back.

## Stay rules, points and prepaid rates

- **Completed stays.** Cash back is available only on completed hotel stays. The posting line says it is added within six weeks after travel has been completed.
- **Hotel points and elite credit.** The booking is made on Marriott's own site, and Rakuten's page describes it as booking directly. The Rakuten terms say nothing about earning Bonvoy points or elite night credit on a stay that starts at Rakuten. Marriott's program terms did not load in full on November 26, 2026. Not confirmed.
- **Prepaid or pay at the hotel.** The terms do not separate the two. Not confirmed.
- **Award nights.** Not eligible.
- **Changes and outside codes.** The Marriott.com terms do not mention either. Not confirmed.

Payment follows Rakuten's schedule once the cash back is added; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

## Brand pages with their own terms

Rakuten also keeps separate pages for some Marriott brands. JW Marriott, Sheraton and Westin each showed 3% and the same $100 cap on November 26, 2026, but their exclusion lists differ. The Sheraton and Westin pages add bookings in Macau and properties in the Pacific Islands. The JW Marriott page names Ritz-Carlton and Edition as excluded brands, which the Marriott.com page does not. Which list governs a stay when the pages disagree is not confirmed.

## Amex points instead of cash

Members with an eligible American Express card can take Membership Rewards points in place of cash, at 1 point per cent. The $13.50 above would be 1,350 points, and the $100 cap would work out to 10,000 points. How the cap is applied to points is not confirmed. See [Amex points instead of cash](/rakuten-amex-points-instead-of-cash/). Other booking routes are compared in [Rakuten for hotels and flights](/rakuten-hotels-flights-cash-back/), and another chain's rules are in [Rakuten at IHG](/rakuten-ihg-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Who should skip it

- You are paying with Bonvoy points.
- You book a member-only, corporate, government or group rate.
- Your hotel is in Asia or Australia, or is a W, Bulgari or Design Hotels property.
- Your room rate is far above $3,333. The cash back stops at $100.
- You are booking a package with flights or a car. Those are excluded.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Marriott?**
3% on November 26, 2026, with no "was" label. Rates change daily.

**Is there a limit on Marriott cash back?**
Yes. The terms cap it at $100 per order, reached at a $3,333.33 room rate.

**Do stays booked with Bonvoy points earn cash back?**
No. Rooms booked and paid for with points are not eligible.
