Yes, Rakuten pays cash back on online orders at CVS. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's CVS page showed 3% cash back on all non-excluded items, with no raised rate. Prescriptions, orders placed through the CVS mobile app, gift cards and the part of an order paid with ExtraBucks earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate seen on November 25, 2026

| Rate | Category on Rakuten's list |
|---|---|
| 3% | All CVS non-excluded items |
| 0% | Excluded items and orders (see below) |

No "was" label appeared. That label marks a rate raised above the store's base rate, so 3% is the plain rate. Nothing on the page promises a higher rate on Black Friday (November 27, 2026).

## What earns and what does not

| Purchase | Earns cash back? |
|---|---|
| Non-excluded items ordered on the CVS website after a Rakuten click | Yes, 3% |
| Prescriptions | No |
| CarePass membership signups | No |
| OTC Health Solutions orders | No |
| COVID-19 tests | No |
| Gift cards, bought or redeemed | No |
| The part of an order paid with ExtraBucks | No |
| Orders placed through the CVS mobile app | No |
| Orders CVS deems to be for reselling | No |

## The reselling limits

The terms put numbers on reselling. An order CVS deems to be for reselling does not earn if it is over $200, or if it includes more than 8 of the same item (the terms say "SKU"). The wording ties both limits to orders CVS judges to be for resale. Whether every order over $200 loses cash back is not confirmed.

## Coupon codes and ExtraBucks

The CVS terms say that using coupon codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back, and to keep to codes Rakuten provides.

Rakuten's page describes the ExtraCare card as paying 2% back in ExtraBucks Rewards on qualifying purchases. Spending ExtraBucks is different: the part of an order paid with them earns no cash back. The page's question-and-answer section says CVS allows up to three coupons on an order, plus manufacturer coupons and ExtraBucks offers. That is the store's rule. Whether clipped ExtraCare coupons count as "codes not listed on Rakuten" is not confirmed.

## CVS Photo is listed separately

The terms say CVSPhoto.com purchases earn the rate on the CVSPhoto.com store page, not the CVS rate. That rate was not read for this page.

## What it comes to

| Order | Amount that earns | At 3% |
|---|---|---|
| $40 of vitamins and skin care, website | $40.00 | $1.20 |
| $40 order, $10 paid with ExtraBucks | $30.00 | $0.90 |
| $40 order placed in the CVS app | $0 | $0 |
| Prescription refill | $0 | $0 |

Taxes and shipping are not mentioned in the CVS terms. The page says CVS ships free on orders of $35 or more.

## Pickup, delivery and stores

- **Pickup.** The terms do not mention store pickup of online orders. Not confirmed.
- **Instacart.** Rakuten's page describes one-hour CVS delivery through Instacart. That is an order with a different company; the CVS terms do not cover it. Not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The CVS page showed an online rate only. CVS did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "tomorrow". Payment follows on Rakuten's schedule; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). Card rewards are separate; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- You are filling a prescription. It earns nothing.
- You order in the CVS mobile app. App orders are excluded.
- You shop in a CVS store. The rate is for online orders.
- You are paying mostly with ExtraBucks.
- You are relying on a coupon code that is not on Rakuten.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at CVS?**
3% on November 25, 2026, on all non-excluded items, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

**Do prescriptions earn cash back?**
No. Prescriptions are first on the excluded list, along with COVID-19 tests and CarePass signups.

**Does the CVS app count?**
No. The terms say cash back is not available on orders placed through the CVS mobile app.
