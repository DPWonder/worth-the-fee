Hilton Gold is the useful one: a daily food and beverage credit at many US hotels or a continental breakfast abroad, 80% bonus points and possible room upgrades. Marriott Gold is modest: 25% bonus points, 2 p.m. checkout when available and a few hundred welcome points, with no breakfast or lounge access.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The two side by side

| | Hilton Honors Gold | Marriott Bonvoy Gold Elite |
|---|---|---|
| Bonus points on stays | 80% | 25% |
| Food | Daily food and beverage credit at select US brands; continental breakfast at select brands abroad | None listed |
| Room upgrade | When available, up to Executive Floor rooms | Enhanced room when available; some brands do not take part |
| Late checkout | When available | 2 p.m. on request, when available |
| Welcome gift | Bottled water | 250 or 500 points per stay |
| Award stays | Fifth night free on stays of five nights or more | Not a Gold benefit in the sources read |
| Normally earned with | 25 nights, 15 stays or $6,000 spent in a year | 25 nights in a year |

Sources: The Points Guy, One Mile at a Time and Upgraded Points, all dated 2026.

## How to get the status

It is not automatic. Enroll in the benefits section of your Amex account with your Hilton Honors and Marriott Bonvoy numbers, one enrollment for each program. Status is reported to post in about 72 hours (One Mile at a Time) or 3 to 5 business days (Upgraded Points), so enroll before a trip.

- Status lasts as long as the card is open. No stays are required.
- Authorized users with a Platinum additional card can enroll too. Each card is registered separately.
- The status does not count toward higher tiers. It brings no elite night credits.

## Hilton Gold: the food credit in dollars

At US hotels the breakfast benefit is a daily credit. AwardWallet's June 2026 table:

| Hilton brand (US) | Credit per day | Two guests |
|---|---|---|
| Conrad, LXR, NoMad, Waldorf Astoria | $25 | $50 |
| Canopy, Curio, DoubleTree, Graduate, Hilton Hotels & Resorts, Signia, Tapestry, Tempo | $15, up to $18 in select markets | $30 to $36 |
| Hilton Garden Inn | $10 | $20 |

Embassy Suites, Hampton, Homewood Suites, Home2 Suites, Spark and Tru serve breakfast to every guest, so Gold adds nothing there. Outside the United States, Gold members get a continental breakfast for the member and one registered guest at participating hotels.

**A sum.** Two people, four nights at a US Hilton or DoubleTree: 4 x $30 = $120 in credits. The same four nights at a Hampton: $0 extra, since breakfast is included for everyone.

## Marriott Gold: what is missing

None of the sources read lists breakfast or lounge access for Marriott Gold. Those start at Platinum Elite, which the [Bonvoy Brilliant card](/marriott-bonvoy-brilliant-amex-worth-650/) includes.

## Where sources disagree

- **Hilton lounge access.** One Mile at a Time lists executive lounge access, subject to availability, as a Hilton Gold benefit. The Points Guy describes only possible upgrades to Executive Floor room types. Treat lounge access as not confirmed.
- **Point values.** The Points Guy values Hilton points at 0.4 cents and One Mile at a Time at about 0.5 cents. Either way, the bonus points are a small rebate next to the food credit.

## Things that reduce the value

- Upgrades and late checkout depend on what the hotel has that day. Nothing is guaranteed.
- Other cards give the same Hilton Gold for far less. The [Hilton Surpass](/hilton-surpass-vs-aspire-amex/) includes it at a $150 annual fee.
- Prepaid stays through Amex's own hotel programs bring their own breakfast and credits. See [Fine Hotels + Resorts versus The Hotel Collection](/fine-hotels-resorts-vs-hotel-collection/).

## Who should skip counting it

- Anyone who stays mostly at Hampton, Embassy Suites or other brands where breakfast is already included.
- Anyone who rarely stays at Hilton or Marriott hotels.
- Anyone who already has the same status or higher another way.
- Anyone expecting breakfast or lounge access from Marriott Gold.

For the status alone, the Platinum Card's $895 fee is hard to justify. It is an extra on top of the [credits and lounges](/amex-platinum-credits-real-value/).

## Quick answers

**Does Amex Platinum give free breakfast at Hilton?**
At select US brands Gold gives a daily food and beverage credit of $10 to $25 per person. At select brands abroad it gives a continental breakfast.

**Does Marriott Gold include breakfast or lounge access?**
No source read lists either for Gold Elite. Its main benefits are 25% bonus points and 2 p.m. checkout when available.

**Is the status automatic?**
No. You enroll once for each program in your Amex account, and it stays while the card is open.
