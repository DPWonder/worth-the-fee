No. On November 27, 2026 (Black Friday), Rakuten's GameStop page carried the label Coupons Only and no cash back rate, with no "was" label. Games, consoles, pre-owned items, collectibles and trade-ins earn nothing; the one related listing with a rate was GameStop gift cards from a separate seller, at 1%.

This page is information, not financial advice. Store listings change, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rates at stores that do pay, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

Rakuten's GameStop Black Friday page and Cyber Monday page were read the same day. Neither showed a rate for GameStop, and the Cyber Monday title also says coupons only. Cyber Monday is November 30, 2026; no rate is promised for it.

## What the page says about GameStop's own coupons

The question-and-answer part of Rakuten's page describes the store's rules. They are the store's, not Rakuten's, and can change.

- **One coupon per purchase.** The page says GameStop does not allow coupon stacking.
- **Rewards certificates.** The page says certificates from the store's paid rewards program may stack, depending on their value.
- **Birthday coupon.** The page describes a 20% coupon once a year in the birthday month, usually for pre-owned games only.
- **Trade-ins.** The page says old games, systems, phones, tablets and accessories can be traded for cash or trade credit.

The page calls the paid program PowerUp Rewards Pro. Whether that is still its current name and what it costs are not confirmed. None of this earns Rakuten cash back.

## GameStop gift cards: the one listing with a rate

Rakuten has a gift card page for GameStop. On November 27, 2026 it listed cards sold by CardCash, below face value, with 1% cash back shown beside each.

| Card | Price shown | Cash back shown |
|---|---|---|
| $25 | $24.50 | 1% |
| $50 | $49.00 | 1% |
| $100 | $98.00 | 1% |
| $250 | $245.00 | 1% |

If the 1% is paid on the price, a $100 card comes to $98.00 less $0.98. The cash back is paid under CardCash's terms on Rakuten, not GameStop's. Which base the 1% uses is not confirmed.

A Rakuten search the same day also showed GameStop gift cards sold by Staples, marked as earning no cash back. So the seller matters. More on this in [gift cards and cash back](/rakuten-gift-cards-cash-back/).

- **In stores.** The page showed no in-store offer. GameStop did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 27, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

- **Extension.** What the Rakuten browser extension shows on GameStop's site is not confirmed. With no rate on the store page, there is nothing for it to activate.

## Who should skip it

- Anyone starting a GameStop order from Rakuten for cash back. There is none to earn.
- Anyone who reads a rate on GameStop's Black Friday page as GameStop's. Those rates are for other stores.
- Buyers who would take a discounted gift card only for the 1%. On a $50 card that is about $0.49, and the seller's terms govern the card.
- Console buyers comparing other stores. Read that store's exclusions first.

## Quick answers

**Does Rakuten give cash back at GameStop?**
No. On November 27, 2026, the store's Rakuten page showed coupons only and no rate.

**Do GameStop gift cards earn cash back on Rakuten?**
Cards sold by CardCash showed 1% on November 27, 2026. Cards sold by Staples showed no cash back.

**Do trade-ins or pre-owned games earn cash back?**
No. With no rate for the store, nothing bought or traded at GameStop earns through Rakuten.
