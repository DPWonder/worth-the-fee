Both cards cost $95 a year. The Citi Strata Premier earns more for someone with large supermarket and airfare spending, and the Sapphire Preferred fits someone who wants stronger trip protection or points that move to United, Southwest and Hyatt.

This page is information, not financial advice. Terms change, so check each issuer's pages before deciding.

## Side by side

| | Sapphire Preferred | Citi Strata Premier |
|---|---|---|
| Annual fee | $95 | $95 |
| Portal bookings | 5x on Chase Travel | 10x on hotels, car rentals and attractions in Citi Travel |
| Restaurants | 3x | 3x |
| Supermarkets, in store | 1x | 3x |
| Online grocery | 3x, not Walmart, Target or wholesale clubs | Not listed separately |
| Gas and EV charging | 3x | 3x |
| Flights booked direct | 2x | 3x |
| Hotels booked direct | 2x | 3x |
| Selected streaming | 3x | 1x |
| Everything else | 1x | 1x |
| Yearly hotel benefit | Up to $100 in credits on Chase Travel hotels | $100 off one Citi Travel hotel stay of $500 or more |
| Airport lounges | None | None |
| Foreign transaction fee | None | None |
| Trip cancellation cover | Up to $10,000 per traveler | Up to $5,000 per trip (one source) |
| Rental car cover | Primary | Secondary at home, primary abroad |
| US airline partners | United, Southwest, JetBlue | American, JetBlue |
| Hotel partners | Hyatt (4 points to 3), IHG, Marriott, Wyndham | Wyndham, Choice, Accor and others; no Hyatt |

The Strata Premier's hotel benefit comes off the price at booking, once per calendar year, and needs a $500 stay before taxes and fees. The Preferred's credit has no stated minimum and runs on the account anniversary year.

## The plain sum

The two cards tie on restaurants, gas and everything else. They differ in four places:

- **Supermarkets in store:** Strata Premier earns 2 more points per dollar.
- **Flights and hotels booked direct:** Strata Premier earns 1 more per dollar.
- **Streaming:** Sapphire Preferred earns 2 more per dollar.
- **Online grocery:** Sapphire Preferred earns 3x; Citi's 3x covers supermarkets and the sources read do not say how online orders are treated.

| Yearly spending | Preferred | Strata Premier |
|---|---|---|
| $6,000 supermarkets | 6,000 | 18,000 |
| $3,000 flights booked direct | 6,000 | 9,000 |
| $1,500 hotels booked direct | 3,000 | 4,500 |
| $4,000 restaurants | 12,000 | 12,000 |
| $600 streaming | 1,800 | 600 |
| $10,000 everything else | 10,000 | 10,000 |
| Total | 38,800 | 54,100 |

On that household the Strata Premier earns about 15,000 more points a year, nearly all of it from supermarkets. Take the supermarket line away and the gap falls to about 3,300.

## What a point can do

Points are only worth what they can be used for.

- **Chase:** 1 cent as cash or in Chase Travel, up to 1.5 cents on Points Boost bookings (some sources report 1.75). Partners include United, Southwest and Hyatt, which no Citi card reaches.
- **Citi:** 1 cent toward travel or gift cards. Partners include American Airlines at 1 to 1, which no Chase card reaches, plus Flying Blue, Turkish, Singapore, Virgin Atlantic and others.

A reader who flies American should weigh that partner heavily. A reader who stays at Hyatt or flies United or Southwest has the same reason to look at Chase.

## Trip protection

The Sapphire Preferred's cover is stronger on paper: trip cancellation up to $10,000 per traveler, trip delay up to $500 after 12 hours, and primary rental car cover in the US. Frequent Miler lists the Strata Premier at up to $5,000 per trip for cancellation, $500 for trip delay, and rental cover that is secondary in the US. Those Citi amounts come from one source. The [Sapphire Preferred travel insurance page](/chase-sapphire-preferred-travel-insurance/) has the Chase limits.

## Which pattern favors which card

- **Large in-store supermarket bill, flies American:** Citi Strata Premier.
- **Books airfare direct several times a year:** Citi Strata Premier, 3x against 2x.
- **Rents cars in the US, or books costly nonrefundable trips:** Sapphire Preferred.
- **Wants Hyatt, United or Southwest:** Sapphire Preferred.
- **Books hotel stays under $500 through a card portal:** Sapphire Preferred; Citi's $100 needs a $500 stay.

## Who should skip both

- Anyone who carries a balance month to month.
- Anyone who wants cash back with no points to manage.
- For the Preferred: anyone with five or more new personal cards in 24 months; see [Chase's 5/24 rule](/chase-5-24-rule-explained/).
- For the Strata Premier: anyone counting on a repeat welcome bonus. Sources disagree on Citi's rule. Upgraded Points describes a 48-month wait, while One Mile at a Time says it is now once in a lifetime. Read the offer terms.

Welcome offers on both cards vary and change, so they are not in these sums.

## Quick answers

**Which card earns more on groceries?**
The Strata Premier, 3x at supermarkets. The Sapphire Preferred earns 3x only on online grocery orders and 1x in store.

**Can either card transfer points to American Airlines?**
The Citi Strata Premier can, at 1 to 1. Chase has no American Airlines transfer.

**Is the $100 hotel benefit the same on both?**
No. Chase's is a statement credit on Chase Travel hotels. Citi's is $100 off one Citi Travel stay of $500 or more.
