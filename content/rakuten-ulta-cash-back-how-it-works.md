Yes, Rakuten works at Ulta Beauty: 12% cash back on November 26, 2026, the day before Black Friday, raised from a usual 2%. Dior products, Marketplace items, gift cards and the part of an order paid with Ulta rewards earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check Ulta's page on Rakuten on the day you shop. For this morning's headline rate here and at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate

| Date read | Rate shown on Rakuten's Ulta page |
|---|---|
| November 24, 2026 | 2% |
| November 25, 2026 | 2% |
| November 26, 2026 | 12% (was 2%) |

Ulta showed one rate across the store, with no category rates. "Was" is Rakuten's own label for the usual rate, and the page carried a banner tying the 12% rate to Black Friday. A raised rate can end without notice. How long 12% lasts, and what the rate will be on Black Friday itself or on Cyber Monday, is not confirmed; see [Black Friday on Rakuten](/rakuten-black-friday-cash-back-2026/) and [Cyber Monday on Rakuten](/rakuten-cyber-monday-cash-back-2026/).

| Order | At the usual 2% | At 12% |
|---|---|---|
| $40 | $0.80 | $4.80 |
| $75 | $1.50 | $9.00 |
| $150 | $3.00 | $18.00 |

The other large beauty listing is covered in [Rakuten at Sephora](/rakuten-sephora-cash-back-how-it-works/), and Sephora shops inside another chain in [Rakuten at Kohl's](/rakuten-kohls-cash-back-how-it-works/).

The listing says cash back is added to the Rakuten account in a few days.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Ulta, as read on November 26, 2026:

| Excluded | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Dior products | A Dior item in the cart earns nothing; the other items still count |
| Marketplace items | Items sold through Ulta's Marketplace by other sellers |
| Part paid with Rewards or Rewards Discount | Ulta points used at checkout reduce the amount that earns |
| Gift cards, including e-gift cards | Buying one earns nothing; see [gift cards and Rakuten](/rakuten-gift-cards-cash-back/) |
| Coupon codes not listed on Rakuten | May void the cash back |

The gift card line is firm. Rakuten's terms say: "Purchases of any gift card including e-gift cards are not eligible for Cash Back."

Salon and other services are not mentioned in the terms read. Services are booked and paid in the store, and the page showed no in-store Ulta offer on November 26, so cash back on them is not confirmed. How linked-card offers work at other stores is in [in-store cash back](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Paying with Ulta points

Redeeming Ulta rewards does not cancel the cash back. It shrinks the base.

| $100 order | Paid with Ulta rewards | Earns Rakuten on | At 2% | At 12% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| No rewards used | $0 | $100 | $2.00 | $12.00 |
| $17.50 in rewards used | $17.50 | $82.50 | $1.65 | $9.90 |

Rakuten's page describes Ulta's program as one point for every $1 spent, with Platinum status at $450 and Diamond at $1,200 in a calendar year. Whether an order tracked by Rakuten still earns those points is not stated there. Ulta issues the points itself, so nothing in Rakuten's terms prevents it. It is not confirmed.

## Coupons

Ulta's terms on Rakuten say codes not listed on Rakuten may void it, and Rakuten's advice is to use the codes it provides. The page's FAQ adds that Ulta allows one store coupon per order, and that this one coupon can be combined with cash back, sale prices and redeemed points. The deals listed on November 26 were brand sales, such as 30% off Urban Decay and 50% off select Origins skincare, not codes.

So there is a choice to weigh, and the raised rate changes the sums:

| $60 order | Saving at the usual 2% | Saving at 12% |
|---|---|---|
| Outside coupon for $10 off, cash back at risk | $10.00 | $10.00 |
| No outside coupon, cash back kept | $1.20 | $7.20 |

At 2% the $10 coupon is worth far more. At 12% the gap is smaller, and on a $90 order the cash back, $10.80, passes the coupon.

## How to keep the order eligible

Rakuten's help pages give the same steps for every store, set out in full in [how Rakuten works, step by step](/how-does-rakuten-work-step-by-step/):

- Activate cash back before adding anything to the cart.
- Check out in the same window that opened from Rakuten.
- Turn off ad blockers and other coupon or rewards extensions while you shop.

If nothing shows up after a couple of days, see [what to do about missing cash back](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/). On a phone, the choice between the app and the Safari extension is covered in [app versus extension](/rakuten-app-vs-browser-extension/).

Cash back also comes on top of what your credit card earns; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- You have a coupon from another source that beats the cash back rate that day.
- Your order is mostly Dior or Marketplace items.
- You are paying mostly with Ulta rewards.
- You are buying a gift card.
- You shop at the counter in the store. This listing is for online orders.

## Quick answers

**Does Rakuten work at Ulta?**
Yes. Ulta showed 12% on November 26, 2026, against a usual 2%. Rates change daily.

**Which brands are excluded at Ulta?**
Dior was the only brand named in the terms read. Marketplace items are also excluded.

**Do Ulta gift cards earn cash back?**
No. The terms exclude any gift card, including e-gift cards.
