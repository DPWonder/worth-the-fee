Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Priceline. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Priceline page showed up to 8% cash back, marked "was 4%", with 8% on hotels and a flat $2 on flights. The biggest exclusion is coupon codes: the terms say an order placed with any coupon or promotional code earns nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## Rates seen on November 25, 2026

| What is booked | Rate shown |
|---|---|
| Express Deals hotels | 8% |
| All other hotels | 8% |
| Express Deals rental cars | 4% |
| All other rental cars | 4% |
| Cruises | 2% |
| Flights | $2 flat |

The headline carried a "was 4%" label, which Rakuten shows when a rate has been raised. The page does not say which categories were raised or how long the raised rate lasts. Not confirmed.

Vacation packages have no line of their own. The terms say a package earns on each of its parts (hotel, car, flight), so a flight inside a package is counted as a flight.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Priceline list these as earning nothing:

- Taxes
- Insurance
- Experiences
- Any carrier or resort fees
- Canceled orders
- Orders placed using a coupon code
- Bookings completed by phone, through another travel website or through a search engine

The order must be placed on the priceline.com website.

## Coupon codes: stricter than most stores

Many Rakuten store pages say codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. The Priceline terms go further. They say orders placed using a coupon code are not eligible, and that use of coupon or promotional codes "will void" cash back. No exception is written for codes shown on Rakuten. On November 25, 2026, the offers listed on Rakuten's Priceline page were sale links, not codes.

## The app

Rakuten's page describes app-only deals in the Priceline mobile app. The terms say all orders must be placed on the priceline.com website. They do not name the app. Whether a booking finished in the Priceline app earns is not confirmed.

## When it shows up

This is slower than a store purchase. The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account within 3 months after travel has been completed. Rakuten's general help page says travel cash back is typically confirmed 6 to 17 weeks after travel is complete.

So the clock starts at the end of the trip, not at booking. A stay booked in November for the following July would not show as confirmed until after July. Payment then follows Rakuten's usual schedule; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). For how this compares with other travel sites, see [Rakuten on hotels and flights](/rakuten-hotels-flights-cash-back/) and [Rakuten at Expedia and Booking.com](/rakuten-expedia-booking-com-cash-back/).

## In stores

Priceline is an online booking site, and the page showed an online offer only. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/) for the stores that have one.

## Who should skip it

- You have a Priceline coupon code worth more than the cash back. Using it voids the cash back.
- You are booking only a flight. The return is $2.
- You book by phone, or you reach Priceline from a search engine or another travel site.
- A large share of the bill is taxes and resort fees. They do not count.
- You may cancel. Canceled orders earn nothing.
- You need the money soon. It posts after the trip, not after booking.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Priceline?**
Up to 8% on November 25, 2026: 8% on hotels, 4% on rental cars, 2% on cruises and $2 on flights. Rates change daily.

**Can you use a Priceline coupon code and still get cash back?**
No. The terms say orders placed using a coupon code are not eligible for cash back.

**When does Priceline cash back post?**
Within 3 months after travel is completed, according to the posting line on Rakuten's page.
