Yes, on Temu's website only. On November 24, 2026, Rakuten's Temu page showed 2% cash back, paid on orders up to a maximum cart size of $250. Orders placed in the Temu app, phones, tablets, laptops and any order with items put in the cart before clicking through Rakuten are excluded.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate here and at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate and the limits seen on November 24, 2026

| Point | What Rakuten's Temu page showed |
|---|---|
| Rate | 2% |
| "Was" label (the usual rate) | None shown |
| Category tiers | None |
| Maximum cart size | $250 |
| Maximum cash back on a single purchase | $50 |
| Delivery address | United States only, not U.S. territories |
| Posting | Added to the Rakuten account "today" |

Rakuten's Temu Black Friday page showed the same 2% on the same day. A Rakuten blog post dated March 5, 2026 says the Temu rate varies. What it will be later this week is not confirmed.

## The two caps

The terms say cash back is paid "on orders up to a maximum cart size of $250 USD" with at most $50 on a single purchase.

At 2%, a $250 cart earns $5.00. So at this rate the cart limit is the one that binds, and the $50 limit is out of reach. The $50 limit would only matter at a rate above 20%.

What happens to a cart over $250 is not spelled out. Whether it earns on the first $250 or earns nothing is not confirmed.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Temu list these as earning nothing:

- Phones, tablets and laptops
- Orders placed using the Temu app
- Orders that start from clicking through a promotional email
- Any items in the shopping cart before clicking through Rakuten
- Orders where items are added after checkout
- Purchases made on a mobile device using Cash App Pay
- Items bought using One Click Pay
- The portion of an order paid with store credit
- Price adjusted orders
- Purchases made for resale or wholesale

Several of these are about how the order is placed, not what is in it. The page's FAQ repeats two of them: app orders do not earn, and items cannot be in the cart before the click.

## The order of steps the terms require

1. Start with an empty Temu cart.
2. Click through from Rakuten to Temu's website, not the app.
3. Add items, then check out in the same visit, without One Click Pay.
4. Do not add items to the order after checkout.

A Temu marketing email opened between steps 2 and 3 starts a different visit. The terms exclude orders that originate from a promotional email.

## Coupon codes and new-user offers

The Temu page has its own rule here: codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. On most store pages that warning appears only on Rakuten's general help page. Rakuten's Temu page listed several codes on November 24, 2026, and the page says its listed codes can be combined with cash back.

The terms add that each user may take part in only one new-user promotion, and that the offer cannot be combined with other new-user offers. Which offer "this offer" refers to is not clear from the page. Not confirmed.

## Other terms

- **Store credit.** The part of an order paid with Temu credit does not earn. Rakuten's page notes that Temu gives credit for some late deliveries and as a faster refund choice on returns.
- **Returns.** No store rule is listed. Rakuten's general help page says returned and canceled items lose their cash back.
- **Missing cash back.** No store deadline is given. Rakuten's general terms allow 90 days from the transaction. See [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).
- **Shipping.** Rakuten's page says Temu offers free shipping on orders shipped from Temu.
- **Rakuten app.** The terms exclude the Temu app. Whether a click from the Rakuten app that opens Temu's mobile website counts is not confirmed.

## When you get the money

Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). Card rewards are separate and come on top; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- You shop in the Temu app. App orders do not earn.
- You are buying a phone, tablet or laptop.
- Your cart is well over $250.
- You have a code from outside Rakuten that saves more than 2%.
- You pay mostly with Temu credit, or with Cash App Pay on a phone.
- The order ships to a U.S. territory or outside the United States.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Temu?**
2% on November 24, 2026, on orders up to a $250 cart. Rates change daily.

**Do Temu app orders earn Rakuten cash back?**
No. Rakuten's terms exclude orders placed using the Temu app. The order must be completed on Temu's website.

**Can a Temu coupon code be used with Rakuten?**
Codes listed on Rakuten can. Rakuten's terms say codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.
