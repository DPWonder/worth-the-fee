On the major US airlines a golf bag is charged as an ordinary checked bag: about $45 each way as your first bag and $55 as your second, as of the April 2026 fee increases. The Amex Platinum's airline fee credit can repay checked bag fees on one chosen airline, and airline credit cards waive the first bag.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## Fees and golf bag rules by airline

Bag fees rose in April 2026. Older tables showing $30 or $35, or free bags on Southwest, are out of date. Fees are one-way, for standard economy fares.

| Airline | First / second bag | Golf bag rule | Weight and size | Case rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Delta | $45 / $55 | One golf bag at standard baggage charges: one set of clubs, balls and tees, one pair of shoes | Over 50 lb pays an excess weight fee. Over 115 linear inches or 100 lb is not accepted | Hard-shell case, or soft travel bag after signing a limited release |
| United | $45 / $55 prepaid; $50 / $60 within 24 hours of departure | One set of clubs counts toward the checked bag allowance. No oversize fee on the golf bag | Overweight charges may apply | United says it is not liable for damage unless the clubs are in a hard-sided case |
| American | $45 / $55 prepaid; $50 / $60 at the airport | One golf bag with up to 14 clubs, 12 balls and one pair of shoes, at the standard fee | Standard fee applies up to 50 lb and 126 inches | Not confirmed |
| Southwest | $45 / $55 | Standard and overweight fees apply. Oversize fees are waived | Over 100 lb or 115 inches cannot be checked | Hooded or soft-sided bags travel with a limited release of liability |
| Alaska and Hawaiian | $45 / $55, including mainland to Hawaii. $30 / $40 between the islands | Standard fees apply. Oversize or overweight fee waived for golf clubs | Other items packed in the case can still trigger an overweight fee | Soft or hard case made for the equipment |

Notes on the table:

- American charges $55 and $65 on domestic Basic Economy tickets, less $5 if prepaid.
- Delta's and Southwest's pages were read in full. The United, American and Alaska rules come from search summaries of their pages.
- Overweight fee amounts were not confirmed for any airline.

## What a round trip costs

| How the clubs travel | Each way | Round trip |
|---|---|---|
| Golf bag is your only checked bag | $45 | $90 |
| Suitcase first, golf bag second | $45 + $55 = $100 | $200 |

## Card benefits that cover the fee

| Benefit | What is confirmed | What is not |
|---|---|---|
| Amex Platinum airline fee credit | Up to $200 per calendar year on one airline chosen in advance: Alaska, American, Delta, JetBlue, Southwest or United. Checked bag fees count, and AwardWallet lists oversized baggage charges too | No source names golf bags or overweight fees. Whether a fee on a Hawaiian-operated flight counts when Alaska is the chosen airline is not confirmed |
| Sapphire Reserve travel credit | Up to $300 per account year on any purchase the card categorizes as travel | Chase's page does not name bag fees |
| Sapphire Preferred | No bag fee benefit found | |
| Airline credit cards | First checked bag free on United Explorer, Citi / AAdvantage Platinum Select, Delta SkyMiles Gold, Atmos Rewards Ascent and Southwest Rapid Rewards cards | Rules on companions and paying with the card differ |

With the Platinum credit, the airline can be changed once a year, by January 31. It does not cover tickets or award taxes.

A free first bag helps most when the golf bag is the only checked bag. With a suitcase too, the golf bag becomes the second bag at $55.

Damage and loss are a separate question. See [what card insurance covers on a golf trip](/golf-trip-travel-insurance-credit-card/).

## Who should skip it

- Golfers taking one short trip a year. Compare $90 to $200 in bag fees with renting clubs at the course or shipping them. Neither price was checked for this page.
- Travelers with a heavy staff bag. Over 50 lb, Delta, United, American and Southwest can add an overweight fee, and no card benefit here is confirmed to cover it.

See also [the Amex Platinum for golfers](/amex-platinum-for-golfers/).

## Quick answers

**Do airlines charge extra for golf clubs?**
Not on these five. A golf bag is charged as a standard checked bag if it stays within the weight limit.

**Does Southwest still fly golf bags free?**
No. Since April 9, 2026 the first bag is $45 and the second $55 on most fares.

**Does the Amex Platinum airline credit cover a golf bag fee?**
It covers checked bag fees on your one chosen airline. No source names golf bags, but airlines charge them as checked bags.
