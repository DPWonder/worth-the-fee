The $95 Sapphire Preferred is worth paying for if you will use its $100 hotel credit, spend abroad, or move points to airline and hotel programs. If you want cash back and travel little, the no-fee Freedom Unlimited returns more, because it pays 1.5% on ordinary purchases where the Preferred pays 1 point.

This page is information, not financial advice. Terms change, so check Chase's pages before deciding.

## Side by side

| | Sapphire Preferred | Freedom Unlimited |
|---|---|---|
| Annual fee | $95 | $0 |
| Chase Travel bookings | 5x | 5% |
| Dining | 3x | 3% |
| Drugstores | 1x | 3% |
| Gas, EV charging, selected streaming, online grocery | 3x | 1.5% |
| Travel booked direct | 2x | 1.5% |
| Everything else | 1x | 1.5% |
| Yearly credit | Up to $100 on Chase Travel hotels | None |
| Foreign transaction fee | None | 3% |
| Airport lounges | None | None |
| Transfer partners | 14 airline and hotel programs | None when held alone |
| Value of a point | 1 cent as cash; up to 1.5 cents on Points Boost bookings (some sources report 1.75); more through some partners | 1 cent as cash |
| Trip cancellation cover | Up to $10,000 per traveler | Up to $1,500 per traveler (one source) |
| Rental car cover | Primary | Not confirmed |

The Freedom Unlimited is sold as a cash back card, but it earns the same kind of Chase points. One percent equals one point.

## The plain sum

Take a household that puts $20,000 a year on one card: $4,000 dining, $2,000 travel booked direct, $14,000 everything else.

| | Sapphire Preferred | Freedom Unlimited |
|---|---|---|
| Dining, $4,000 | 12,000 | 12,000 |
| Travel, $2,000 | 4,000 | 3,000 |
| Everything else, $14,000 | 14,000 | 21,000 |
| Total points | 30,000 | 36,000 |
| As cash at 1 cent | $300 | $360 |
| After the annual fee | $205 | $360 |

Taken as cash, the Freedom Unlimited is ahead by $155. For the Preferred to draw level, its 30,000 points have to be worth $455, which is about 1.5 cents each.

Now add the hotel credit. A reader who books one hotel a year through Chase Travel gets up to $100 back, which leaves the Preferred $5 short on fees. Its points then need to be worth about 1.2 cents each to match. Points Boost bookings and transfers to partners can reach that. Cash cannot.

If part of the "everything else" is gas, streaming or online grocery, the Preferred earns 3x there and the gap narrows.

## Three things that tip it toward the Preferred

- **Spending abroad.** The Freedom Unlimited adds 3% to purchases in foreign currency. On $3,200 abroad that is $96, the whole Preferred fee.
- **Transfers.** Only the Preferred, the Reserve or the Ink Business Preferred can send points to United, Southwest, Hyatt and the other partners. Hyatt is now 4 Chase points to 3 from the Preferred; see [the Hyatt transfer change](/chase-to-hyatt-transfer-change-2026/).
- **Trip cover.** The Preferred's trip cancellation, trip delay and primary rental car cover are stronger. The limits are on the [travel insurance page](/chase-sapphire-preferred-travel-insurance/).

## Holding both

One person can hold both cards and move Freedom Unlimited points into the Sapphire Preferred account. Those points then transfer to partners like any others. In the example above, that would mean 1.5 points on the $14,000 of general spending and 2x on travel, for 37,000 points a year that can all go to partners, for one $95 fee.

That only pays if the points are used for travel. A reader who would cash out at 1 cent gains nothing from the second card.

## Which pattern favors which card

- **No trips abroad, wants cash back, pays for travel rarely:** Freedom Unlimited.
- **One or more hotel stays a year and some spending abroad:** Sapphire Preferred.
- **Collects points for flights or Hyatt stays:** Sapphire Preferred, or both cards together.
- **Large drugstore spending:** Freedom Unlimited, at 3%.

## Who should skip the fee

- Anyone who redeems for cash every time. The Preferred's extra value comes from travel use.
- Anyone who would not book a hotel through Chase Travel. Without the $100 credit the fee is a full $95 each year.
- Anyone carrying a balance. Interest outweighs rewards on either card.
- Anyone with five or more new personal cards in 24 months is generally declined for both; see [Chase's 5/24 rule](/chase-5-24-rule-explained/).

Welcome offers on both cards vary and change, so they are left out of the sums. A [first-year plan for the Preferred](/chase-sapphire-preferred-first-year-plan/) covers the early months.

## Quick answers

**Does the Freedom Unlimited earn more than the Sapphire Preferred on ordinary purchases?**
Yes. It earns 1.5 points per dollar where the Preferred earns 1.

**Can Freedom Unlimited points transfer to airlines?**
Not from that card alone. They can after being moved to a Sapphire Preferred, Sapphire Reserve or Ink Business Preferred held by the same person.

**How much spending abroad cancels out the $95 fee?**
About $3,200 a year. The Freedom Unlimited's 3% foreign transaction fee on that amount is $96.
