Yes. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Gap page showed 2% cash back, with no raised rate two days before Black Friday. Collabs, Gap Home, gift cards, items sold and shipped by third-party sellers and the part of an order paid with reward points earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate, and how it moves

| Date checked | Rate shown | Rakuten's "was" label |
|---|---|---|
| November 22, 2026 | 2% | None |
| November 25, 2026 | 2% | None |

A "was" label appears when a rate has been raised above the store's base rate. Gap's page has none, so 2% is the plain rate. No category table appears. Rakuten's Black Friday page for Gap showed the same 2% on November 25, 2026. Nothing on the page promises a higher rate on Black Friday (November 27, 2026) or Cyber Monday (November 30, 2026).

Rakuten's page gives one more figure: the average cash back earned per Gap shopper was $19.88 as of the end of 2025. It does not say over what period.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Gap list these as earning nothing:

- Collabs
- Gap Home
- Gift cards
- Items sold and shipped by third-party sellers
- The part of an order paid with reward points

The terms do not define "collabs". Which product lines count is not confirmed.

## Coupon codes: the firm wording

The Gap terms say that using codes not listed on Rakuten "will void" cash back, and to keep to codes Rakuten provides. Many store pages say "may void"; this one is firmer. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's page listed one Gap code, for an extra 15% off a purchase.

Rakuten's page also says Gap accepts up to five coupon codes on one online order. That is the store's rule. Under the cash back terms, one code from outside Rakuten is enough to lose the 2%.

The page does not fully agree with itself here. Its terms say outside codes will void cash back; its question-and-answer section says they can void it. The terms are the stricter reading.

## Encore points and GapCash

Encore is the free rewards program shared by Gap, Old Navy, Banana Republic and Athleta. Rakuten's page describes it as 5 points per $1 at the Core level, with free shipping on orders of $50 or more.

- **Earning points.** The page says Encore points and cash back can be earned on the same eligible purchase.
- **Spending points.** The part of an order paid with reward points earns no cash back.
- **GapCash.** The page describes GapCash vouchers earned during promotions. The terms name reward points, not GapCash. Whether redeeming GapCash reduces the amount that earns is not confirmed.

One section of Rakuten's page still calls the program "Gap Good Rewards". Both names are on the page.

## Gap Factory is a separate store on Rakuten

Gap Factory has its own Rakuten page, with its own terms. It also showed 2% on November 25, 2026. Its exclusions are shorter: gift cards and the part paid with reward points. A click through Rakuten's Gap page is for gap.com. Whether a Gap Factory order placed after that click earns is not confirmed.

Old Navy is listed separately too; see [Rakuten at Old Navy](/rakuten-old-navy-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Pickup, app and stores

- **Pickup.** Rakuten's page describes curbside and in-store pickup for online orders. The cash back terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
- **App.** The page says the Gap app has had app-only deals. The terms do not mention the app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension. Whether an order finished in the Gap app tracks is not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The Gap page showed an online rate only. Gap did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).
- **Returns.** The page describes online returns within 30 days of delivery. Rakuten's general help page says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back.

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "tomorrow", after the order is confirmed. Payment follows on Rakuten's schedule of four dates a year; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

## Who should skip it

- You have a code from Gap's emails or app that is not on Rakuten. Using it voids the 2%.
- You are buying a collab item, Gap Home or a gift card.
- The item is sold and shipped by a third-party seller.
- You are paying most of the order with Encore points.
- You are waiting for a raised rate. None was shown, and none is promised.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Gap?**
2% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

**Can you use a Gap promo code and still get cash back?**
Only a code listed on Rakuten. The terms say other codes will void cash back.

**Do Encore points and Rakuten cash back stack?**
Earning both on one purchase is allowed. The part paid with points earns no cash back.
