The Sapphire Reserve gives up to $250 back on a prepaid stay of two nights or more at a hotel in Chase's The Edit collection, on up to two separate bookings per calendar year, for $500 in total. The catches are the two-night minimum, the need to prepay, hotel prices that often run $500 a night or more, and the fact that one booking can never earn more than $250.

This page is information, not financial advice. The Reserve's other credits are on [the full credits page](/chase-sapphire-reserve-credits-real-value/); this page covers only The Edit.

## The rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Amount | Up to $250 per booking, up to $500 per calendar year |
| Period | January 1 to December 31; both credits usable at any time since 1/1/2026 |
| Stay | Two nights or more |
| Payment | Prepaid "Pay Now" rate through Chase Travel, on the Reserve |
| Who can book | The cardmember or an authorized user; one $500 cap per account |
| Points | The credited part earns none; the rest earns 8x |

Before 2026 the credit was split by half-year.

## What comes with an Edit stay

The Points Guy lists the on-property benefits as daily breakfast for two, a $100 property credit per booking, a room upgrade and early check-in or late checkout when available, and Wi-Fi where offered. Upgraded Points notes that back-to-back bookings at the same hotel get one set of benefits, not two.

The Points Guy puts the collection at more than 1,000 properties; Thrifty Traveler counted 1,371 in November 2025 and notes the list rotates.

## The catches

**Price.** These are upscale hotels. Thrifty Traveler's examples run from about $500 to over $600 a night. NerdWallet, seen in a search summary, found only about a third of the rates it checked were under $500 a night.

**Prepaying.** The credit needs a Pay Now rate. Cancellation terms vary by hotel and rate and were not read for this page; check them before paying. Chase can reverse the credit if the booking is canceled, returned or changed, or if the account closes within 90 days of the credit.

**Two bookings, not one.** A single $900 stay earns $250, not $500.

**The portal price.** The Points Guy notes prices in Chase Travel may be higher than booking direct. Compare the same room and dates on the hotel's site.

**Hotel loyalty.** Thrifty Traveler says hotel points and elite night credits are earned in most cases. The Points Guy notes exceptions, such as Small Luxury Hotels bookings earning no Hilton points.

**Timing.** The price is not cut at booking. Chase's terms say the credit posts within 3 business days and may take up to 4 weeks. The Points Guy says up to eight weeks. A purchase delayed in processing near December 31 can fall into the next year.

Whether a stay paid with points triggers the credit is not confirmed.

## A sample sum

An illustration, not a typical result:

| | Two nights at $450 a night |
|---|---|
| Prepaid price | $900 |
| Edit credit | $250 |
| Net cost | $650 |
| Points on the $650 at 8x | 5,200 |
| On-property | Breakfast for two, $100 property credit |

If you would have paid $900 for that hotel anyway, the credit is worth the full $250. If you would have stayed somewhere for $300 a night, you spent $50 more than planned to use it.

## Stacking in 2026

Through December 31, 2026, the Reserve also has a one-time credit of up to $250 for prepaid stays of two nights or more at selected brands in Chase Travel: IHG, Montage, Pendry, Omni, Virgin Hotels, Minor Hotels and Pan Pacific. The Points Guy reports both credits can apply to one reservation when a hotel qualifies for each. Upgraded Points describes that second credit as annual; the other sources say it is one-time and ends this year.

## Who should skip it

- Anyone who would not pay $400 or more a night for a hotel. Count this credit as $0 when adding up the card.
- Anyone who takes one-night stays, or needs flexible, pay-at-hotel rates.
- Anyone whose usual destinations have no Edit hotels. Search Chase Travel first.
- Anyone who gets more from booking direct with elite status than from the breakfast and property credit here.

## Quick answers

**Can one stay use the full $500?**
No. Each booking gets up to $250. The full $500 takes two separate prepaid stays of two nights or more.

**Does the credit follow the calendar year or the account year?**
The calendar year, January 1 to December 31. Unused credit does not carry over.

**Do authorized users get their own $500?**
No. Their bookings can trigger the credit, but the account has one $500 cap.
