It covers the cost to repair or replace a stolen or damaged phone, including a cracked screen, up to $800 per claim after a $50 deductible, with two claims per 12 months. The wireless bill must be paid with the card. A lost phone is not covered, and the benefit pays only what other insurance does not.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The terms at a glance

These come from Amex's Guide to Benefits for cell phone protection, which names the Platinum Card as an eligible account.

| Term | Detail |
|---|---|
| Maximum per claim | $800, the lesser of repair cost or replacement value |
| Deductible | $50 per claim |
| Claims | Two per card account per 12-month period |
| Covered events | Theft and damage, including a cracked screen |
| Which phones | The primary line and additional lines on your wireless bill |
| Underwriter | New Hampshire Insurance Company, an AIG company |

The deductible is taken from the repair or replacement cost first, and the $800 cap applies after that. A $300 screen repair pays $250. A $1,200 replacement pays $800.

## How to have cover in place

Charge your monthly wireless bill to the Platinum Card. Cover starts on the first day of the calendar month after that payment.

If a month's bill is paid another way, cover is suspended from the first day of the following month. It resumes the first day of the month after you next pay the bill with the card.

Additional card members count as card members under the guide.

## What it does not cover

- **Lost phones.** A phone that is lost, or that vanishes without evidence of theft, is excluded.
- **Cosmetic damage.** Scratches, scuffs and chips that do not break through the screen or casing and do not affect how the phone works. A cracked screen is not treated as cosmetic.
- **Accessories.** Only the standard battery and antenna from the manufacturer are included.
- **Phones in transit.** A phone in the care of the mail, an airline or a delivery service.
- **Rented, leased, borrowed or prepaid-plan phones.**
- **Phones bought for resale or for commercial use**, on consumer cards.
- **Wear, defects and disasters.** Normal wear and tear, product defects, flood and earthquake.
- **Warranty losses.** Anything a manufacturer or seller warranty covers.

## It pays after other insurance

The guide calls the cover "excess of any other applicable insurance or indemnity." If you have phone insurance through your carrier, or a homeowner's or renter's policy that applies, the card benefit pays only what those do not. Claim papers can include a copy of the claim you made to that insurer, or the policy's declarations page if the loss is under its deductible.

## Filing a claim

| Step | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Report the loss by phone or on the claims website | Within 90 days of the loss |
| Send written proof | Within 120 days of the loss |
| Police report, for theft | Filed within 48 hours of the theft |

Papers include the card statement showing the phone bill payment, the wireless bill, proof of the phone model, and a repair estimate with photos.

## The sums

If the benefit lets you drop a paid protection plan, the saving is that plan's monthly fee times 12, per line. Weigh three things against it:

- The benefit excludes loss, which paid plans often include.
- The $800 cap is below the price of many flagship phones.
- Upgraded Points notes the phone bill generally earns 1 point per dollar on premium Amex cards. A card that pays more on phone bills, or a carrier discount for paying another way, is what you give up.

This benefit alone does not carry an $895 annual fee. It is one line in the [full count of what the card returns](/amex-platinum-credits-real-value/).

The benefit does not come with every Amex card. The consumer Gold Card is not on the list; see [whether the Amex Gold has cell phone protection](/amex-gold-cell-phone-protection/).

## Who should skip relying on it

- Anyone mainly worried about losing a phone. Loss is excluded.
- Anyone with a phone worth well over $800 who wants full replacement.
- Anyone on a prepaid plan or with a leased phone.
- Anyone who will not keep the wireless bill on this card every month.

## Quick answers

**What is the deductible on Amex Platinum cell phone protection?**
$50 per claim. It is applied to the repair or replacement cost before the $800 cap.

**Does it cover a cracked screen?**
Yes. The guide lists a cracked screen as covered damage and says it is not cosmetic damage.

**Does it cover a lost phone?**
No. Lost phones and unexplained disappearance are excluded. Theft is covered with a police report filed within 48 hours.
