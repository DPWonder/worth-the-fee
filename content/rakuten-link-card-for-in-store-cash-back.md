In your Rakuten account, open Linked Cards, select Link a Card and enter the card details. Rakuten says most Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards and most American Express cards can be linked; Discover, Apple Card and Wells Fargo Mastercards cannot.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## How to link a card

1. Sign in and go to Linked Cards in your account.
2. Select Link a Card.
3. Enter the card details.

To remove a card later, open Linked Cards, select Edit next to the card, then Remove.

Rakuten's terms say there can be a lag of about 24 hours before a newly enrolled card's purchases are reported. For the whole program, see [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Which cards can be linked

| Card | Can it be linked? |
|---|---|
| Most Visa credit and debit cards | Yes |
| Most Mastercard credit and debit cards | Yes |
| Most American Express cards | Yes |
| Discover and Diners Club | No |
| Wells Fargo Mastercards | No |
| American Express debit cards, Macy's American Express | No |
| Apple Card and other virtual cards with a different number each time | No |
| Corporate and purchasing cards | No |
| HSA and FSA cards | No |
| Government prepaid cards (including EBT), insurance prepaid cards, Visa Buxx | No |

Rakuten's in-store page names credit cards only. Its two help articles add Visa and Mastercard debit cards.

## How many cards

Rakuten's help article says "you can link more than one card to your account." It gives no maximum for in-store offers, so a limit is not confirmed. The program terms set one only for dining: up to 12 cards in the restaurant program, and a card may be enrolled in only one dining program at a time.

Whether one card can be linked to two Rakuten accounts is not confirmed.

## Add the offer before you pay

A linked card does not earn at a store until that store's offer is added.

| Rule | In-store offers | Dining offers |
|---|---|---|
| Added how | By you, on the site or in the app's In-Store tab | Automatically, once a card is linked |
| When | Before the purchase | No step needed |
| Expires | Most after 75 days, or once used | Never, per Rakuten |
| After a purchase | Add the offer again | Nothing to do |

## Paying with a phone wallet

Rakuten's pages do not agree.

| Rakuten page | What it says about wallets |
|---|---|
| In-store FAQ | Recommends the physical card. Apple Pay, Google Wallet and Samsung Pay may not always track. |
| Program terms | Payments through third-party wallets or payment apps other than Apple Pay and Samsung Wallet may not be eligible. |

Paying with the physical linked card avoids the question.

## Debit cards and PINs

A linked debit card has to be run as credit, with a signature if the terminal asks. The help article says debit purchases processed with a PIN may not qualify. The terms are firmer: do not use a PIN if you want the purchase to count.

## What Rakuten says about card data

From the help article on card linking:

- Card details are stored in a secure, encrypted environment.
- Rakuten does not sell card information.
- Rakuten will not charge the card and will not ask for the CVV.

Its in-store page adds that the storage environment is certified as compliant with the Payment Card Industry (PCI) standard.

The program terms describe what is shared. By enrolling a card you agree that the card network or processor may monitor that card's transactions at participating stores and send Rakuten the merchant name, location, date and amount. Rakuten may pass transaction data to offer networks, vendors and participating stores. After a card is removed, Rakuten keeps the past data.

## If the purchase does not show

In-store cash back usually shows as pending within a few days. Rakuten's listed causes of a miss are an offer not added first, a card not linked, and a debit card not run as credit. Next steps are in [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## Who should skip it

- Your everyday card is Discover, Apple Card, a Wells Fargo Mastercard or a store card without a network logo.
- You do not want a card network reporting your purchases at participating stores to Rakuten.
- You pay by phone tap or debit PIN and prefer not to change.
- You will not add the offer before each visit. Without it, the linked card earns nothing at that store.

## Quick answers

**Which cards can you link to Rakuten?**
Most Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards and most American Express cards. Not Discover or Apple Card.

**How many cards can you link?**
More than one. Rakuten gives no maximum for in-store offers; its terms cap dining at 12 cards.

**Does Apple Pay work with a linked card?**
Rakuten's terms say Apple Pay is eligible. Its FAQ says phone wallets may not always track.
