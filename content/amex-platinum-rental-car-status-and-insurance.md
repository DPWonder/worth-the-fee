The Platinum Card gives mid-level status with Hertz, Avis and National once you enroll, and secondary cover for theft or damage to a rental car up to $75,000. Premium Car Rental Protection is a separate paid add-on, about $12 to $25 per rental, that makes the damage cover primary; neither one covers liability.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The three statuses

None of these is automatic. You enroll for each one through the benefits section of your Amex account. Additional Platinum cardholders can enroll too.

| Program | Status | Normally earned with | Main perks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hertz Gold Plus Rewards | President's Circle | 15 rentals or $3,000 a year | One-class upgrade at participating locations, choice of car from the President's Circle section, spouse or domestic partner drives free in the US, 50% bonus points |
| Avis Preferred | Preferred Plus | 12 rentals or $5,000 a year | Skip the counter at most locations, upgrade when available, extra points |
| National Emerald Club | Executive | 12 paid rentals or 40 days a year | Book a midsize car and pick any car, full-size or above, from the Executive area (US and Canada); no second-driver fee; a free day after six rentals |

The earning figures and perks are from One Mile at a Time. Upgrades depend on what cars are on the lot.

Upgraded Points says the Amex discount code with Hertz adds a four-hour grace period for returns in the US.

### Hertz deadline: December 31, 2026

Amex told cardholders on July 13, 2026 that they must enroll again for Hertz President's Circle by December 31, 2026, according to Upgraded Points. Those who do not could lose the status and the Amex discount with Hertz. It is done under Car Rental Privileges in the Amex account and can take up to 72 hours.

Whether Avis and National need the same step is not confirmed. The notice named only Hertz.

## The built-in insurance

The card includes Car Rental Loss and Damage Insurance at no extra cost.

| Feature | Built-in cover |
|---|---|
| Type | Secondary: your own auto insurance pays first |
| Theft or damage to the rental car | Up to $75,000 |
| Rental length | Up to 30 consecutive days |
| Liability (damage to other cars, injury to other people) | Not covered |
| Not valid in | Australia, Italy, New Zealand |
| Vehicles excluded | Motorcycles, RVs, certain vans and trucks, peer-to-peer rentals |

To use it, pay for the whole rental with the card and decline the rental company's collision damage waiver.

Secondary means a claim goes to your personal auto policy or other insurance first. The card then covers what that insurance does not, up to its limit.

One source disagrees. One Mile at a Time's article on rental status says the card does not include a collision damage waiver as a standard benefit. Upgraded Points, Your Mileage May Vary and AutoSlash all describe the secondary cover above. Confirm in your card's benefit guide before relying on it.

## What Premium Car Rental Protection adds

This is an optional Amex program, open to most Amex cards. You enroll the card once at no charge. After that, a flat fee is charged each time you pay for a rental with that card, per rental and not per day.

| | Built-in | Premium Basic | Premium Plus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price per rental (most states) | $0 | $19.95 | $24.95 |
| California residents | $0 | $15.95 | $17.95 |
| Florida residents | $0 | $12.25 | $15.25 |
| Damage and theft | Secondary, up to $75,000 | Primary, up to $75,000 | Primary, up to $100,000 |
| Medical expenses | Up to $5,000 (secondary) | Up to $7,500 per person (secondary) | Up to $15,000 per person (secondary) |
| Personal property | Up to $1,000 per person | Up to $2,500 per person | Up to $5,000 per person |
| Longest rental | 30 days | 42 days (30 for Washington residents) | 42 days (30 for Washington residents) |
| Liability | No | No | No |

Prices and limits are from Your Mileage May Vary (October 2026); Amex's own terms page could not be read. AutoSlash gives the lowest price as $12.95, not $12.25.

Premium does not apply to rentals in Australia, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Jamaica or New Zealand, and it is not offered to New Hampshire residents, according to AwardWallet and Your Mileage May Vary.

Primary means the claim does not go through your own auto policy first. On a week-long rental, $24.95 is about $3.56 a day. Once a card is enrolled, every rental paid with it is charged the fee.

Some other cards include primary cover with no per-rental fee; see [Sapphire Preferred rental car insurance](/chase-sapphire-preferred-rental-car-insurance/).

## Who should skip it

The status is of little use if you rent less than once a year or rent from other companies.

Skip Premium Car Rental Protection if:

- Another card you hold already gives primary cover.
- You rent mainly in the six excluded countries.
- You need liability cover. Neither Amex product provides it.

## Quick answers

**Is Amex Platinum rental car insurance primary or secondary?**
Secondary, up to $75,000 for theft or damage. Your own auto insurance pays first.

**How much does Amex Premium Car Rental Protection cost?**
In most states, $19.95 or $24.95 per rental, not per day. California and Florida residents pay less.

**Is rental car status automatic with the Amex Platinum?**
No. You enroll for Hertz, Avis and National separately, and Hertz needs a new enrollment by December 31, 2026.
