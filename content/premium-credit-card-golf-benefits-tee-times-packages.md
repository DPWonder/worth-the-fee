Mostly tee-time help, not packages. The Amex Platinum and Chase Sapphire Reserve offer a concierge that can request tee times and hotel programs that include some golf resorts, but neither lists packaged golf trips as a standing benefit. The clearest packaged trips found were on Mastercard's Priceless site, sold by a tour operator and paid for in full by the cardholder.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## What each card or network offers

| Card or network | Tee-time help | Golf program | Packaged golf trips |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amex Platinum ($895) | Concierge takes golf requests | None on the US card | One 2026 ballot for a St Andrews package |
| Chase Sapphire Reserve ($795) | Visa concierge; golf not named | Visa Infinite rate at Troon Privé clubs | None listed |
| Capital One Venture X ($395) | Visa Infinite concierge; golf not named | Same Visa Infinite rate | None listed |
| World Elite Mastercard | Concierge books TPC tee times | TPC benefit through December 31, 2026 | Listed on Priceless, sold by a tour operator |

## Amex Platinum

The US Platinum Card has no free rounds, green fee credit or tee time priority. See [what the Platinum does for golfers](/amex-platinum-for-golfers/).

Upgraded Points, updated November 11, 2026, says the Platinum Concierge takes "requests for reservations, including restaurants, golf, and spas." The service is free, and the cardholder pays for whatever is booked.

The nearest thing to a package was a single event. In August 2026 Amex and St Andrews Links Trust announced a ballot for Platinum and Centurion members in select countries. Those drawn could buy a golf package for two at St Andrews courses in 2027. The window closed on September 9, 2026, and no price was published. Sources disagree on the countries: the release names none, one trade outlet names only the UK, and one travel site says 15 including the US. A repeat is not confirmed.

For lodging, [Fine Hotels + Resorts and The Hotel Collection](/fine-hotels-resorts-vs-hotel-collection/) include some golf resorts. Golf is paid for separately.

## Chase Sapphire Reserve

Chase's page for the card does not mention golf. The Points Guy describes a Visa concierge for dining, travel arrangements and event access. Golf is not named, so tee-time help is a request, not a listed benefit.

At the 2025 PGA Championship, Reserve cardmembers could buy daily grounds ticket packages through Chase and reserve a free viewing space. Those were tickets to watch, not trips to play. A 2026 version is not confirmed.

For lodging, [The Edit](/chase-sapphire-reserve-the-edit-credit-how-it-works/) gives a $100 property credit, breakfast and an upgrade when available at its hotels. Whether a resort lets that credit be spent on golf varies by hotel.

## The Visa Infinite golf rate

Visa still has a golf benefit with Troon, now run under the name Access. Its terms say US-issued Visa Infinite cardholders can request tee times at more than 20 participating Troon Privé private clubs for $99 plus tax per player per round. Up to three guests pay the same rate, and each club can be played up to two times a year.

The terms show no end date and say the offer can change without notice. The Sapphire Reserve and Venture X are Visa Infinite cards, but neither issuer names this benefit on the pages read, so confirm it with the program first.

## Mastercard: the one place packages appear

Mastercard's Priceless Golf page listed two kinds of offer on November 23, 2026.

**TPC courses.** World Elite Mastercard holders can have a concierge book a round at TPC courses, with 20% off green fees at select courses. The listing runs until December 31, 2026, with one visit per course per year. Whether it continues in 2027 is not confirmed.

**Packaged trips.** The page listed golf trips in Scotland, England and Northern Ireland sold by a golf tour operator, priced from about $3,261 to $6,522 per person. One example, in Troon, includes four hotel nights, three rounds and private transfers. Airfare is not included. It is for four golfers, is non-refundable unless noted, and names no card tier.

These trips are sold to cardholders at full price. No annual fee pays for them.

## The sums

None of these benefits pays for golf. The concierge is free and the round is not. The Troon Privé rate is $99 a player. The Mastercard packages cost thousands of dollars a person before flights. What a premium card returns on a golf trip is mostly hotel credits, lounges and insurance. See [airline fees for golf clubs](/flying-with-golf-clubs-airline-fees/) and [card insurance on a golf trip](/golf-trip-travel-insurance-credit-card/).

## Who should skip it

- Anyone choosing a card with a fee of $395 to $895 mainly for golf. The golf content is thin on all of them.
- Golfers who want a ready-made trip. A golf tour operator sells that without a premium card.
- Golfers who play public courses near home.

## Quick answers

**Does any premium card include a golf trip in its annual fee?**
No. None of the cards covered here does. Packages listed for cardholders are sold at full price.

**Can the Amex Platinum concierge book a tee time?**
It takes golf reservation requests, according to Upgraded Points. The cardholder pays the green fee, and a booking is not guaranteed.

**Is there still a Visa Infinite golf benefit?**
Yes. Troon's terms give US-issued Visa Infinite cardholders a $99 rate at participating Troon Privé clubs, up to twice a year per club.
