You get whichever bonus was on the page you joined from, and never both. On November 24, 2026, Rakuten's own store pages advertised a $10 welcome bonus, while a member's referral link showed $50 after $50 of purchases, so the referral bonus was the larger one that day.

This page is information, not financial advice. Both amounts change, and the page you land on shows the amount that applies to you.

## The two bonuses side by side

| | Standard welcome bonus | Referral sign-up bonus |
|---|---|---|
| How you join | From Rakuten's own site or app | Through a member's link, or by entering the member's email on the join form |
| Amount seen November 24, 2026 | $10 | $50 |
| Other amounts in Rakuten's page text | $20; an extra 10% cash back in place of a fixed bonus | $30 |
| Purchases needed | At least $25 | $50 for the $50 offer ($30 for the $30 version) |
| Time limit | 90 days from signing up | 90 days from signing up |
| What counts | Purchases that earn cash back or points through Rakuten | Purchases that earn cash back through Rakuten |
| Amazon.com orders | Do not count | Do not count |
| Paid | With the next cash back payment or points transfer | With the next cash back payment |

## What each one requires

The welcome bonus terms set the same test whatever the amount: join as a new member from a page where the welcome bonus was offered, then make qualifying purchases of at least $25 within 90 days. You also need to choose a payment method and have a mailing address on file.

The referral bonus follows the offer shown on the referral page. The current one runs from October 1 to December 31, 2026 and asks for qualifying purchases of at least $50 within 90 days. The new member must pay for the purchase and must live in the 50 states or D.C. A purchase that is returned, canceled or exchanged does not count. The full list of ways it fails is on [how the referral bonus works](/rakuten-50-bonus-how-it-works/).

Neither bonus is instant. The store must confirm the purchase first, and the bonus then goes out on a quarterly payment date. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

## Why the amounts differ from page to page

Rakuten's pages did not all show the same welcome offer on November 24, 2026. Store pages such as Best Buy's said $10. The home page description offered an extra 10% cash back for new members and named no fixed dollar bonus. Text built into the same pages also carried a line about a $20 welcome bonus after $25 of purchases. Rakuten's separate boost bonus terms describe the 10% version: it applies to purchases in the first seven days and has a cap. The cap amount is not confirmed.

Referral amounts move too. The referral page showed $50 after $50, and its sharing text also carried a $30 after $30 message. Rakuten's referral FAQ says the new member gets "the bonus that was available on day of sign up."

## Can you get both?

No. The welcome bonus terms say it is not combinable with other offers, and they name the referral sign-up bonus. The boost terms say the same. Each is limited to one per new member.

## If you already have an account

Both bonuses are for new members only, and Rakuten's terms limit each person to one account. An existing member cannot earn either one by joining again.

Whether a member who joined from Rakuten's own site can add a friend's email afterward is not confirmed. Rakuten's join article describes entering it only on the join form.

## Who should skip it

- You would be buying $50 of things you do not need to reach the larger bonus. The welcome bonus asks for $25.
- Your first purchases will be on Amazon.com. They count toward neither bonus.
- You already have a Rakuten account. Neither bonus applies.
- You live outside the 50 states and D.C.

## Quick answers

**Which Rakuten bonus is bigger?**
On November 24, 2026 the referral bonus was: $50 against $10. Both change, so compare the pages on the day you join.

**Can you get the welcome bonus and the referral bonus?**
No. Rakuten's terms say the two cannot be combined, and each is one per new member.

**Does an existing Rakuten member get either bonus?**
No. Both are for new members, and the terms allow one account per person.
