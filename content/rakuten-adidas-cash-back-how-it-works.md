Yes. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Adidas page showed up to 6% cash back, raised from a usual 2%. Samba, Gazelle, Spezial and EVO SL shoes earn 2%, and named collaborations, gift cards and purchases in the Confirmed app earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy.

## The rate, and how it moves

| Date checked | Rate shown | Rakuten's "was" label |
|---|---|---|
| November 21, 2026 | Up to 6% | 2% |

"Was" is Rakuten's own label for the store's usual rate. "Up to" matters here, because the page splits the rate in two:

| What you buy | Rate on November 21, 2026 |
|---|---|
| Samba, Gazelle, Spezial, EVO SL | 2% |
| All other non-excluded items | 6% |

So the raised rate did not reach those four shoe lines that day.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Adidas list these as earning nothing:

- Collaborations "such as" Edison Chen, Y-3, Fear of God, Bad Bunny, Wales Bonner, Pharrell, Song for the Mute and Willy Chavarria
- Gift cards
- Purchases made in the Confirmed app
- Orders Adidas judges to be for reselling
- High-volume orders, defined as 10 or more of the same eligible item
- Orders from outside the US

The words "such as" mean the list of collaborations is examples, not the full list. Whether a collaboration that is not named earns cash back is not confirmed.

## Terms worth knowing before you order

- **Orders over $1,500.** These may be subject to review and reversal. Nike's terms set that line at $500.
- **Coupon codes.** The terms say codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.
- **Returns.** The Adidas terms on Rakuten say nothing about returns. Rakuten's general rule is that a returned item loses its cash back. Nike's terms are stricter on this point; see [how Rakuten works at Nike](/rakuten-nike-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## App, pickup and member discounts

- **App.** Only the Confirmed app is named as excluded. The terms do not mention the main Adidas app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension, so an order started directly in a store app is not expected to track.
- **Pickup.** The terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
- **adiClub.** Whether a member discount, an adiClub voucher or adiClub points affect cash back is not confirmed.
- **Student and hero discounts.** The page mentions a 15% student discount and discounts for verified groups such as first responders and military. These usually arrive as a code. A code not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.

## What it comes to

| Purchase | Price | Rate that day | Cash back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Samba shoes | $100 | 2% | $2.00 |
| Running shoes, not a 2% line | $130 | 6% | $7.80 |
| Apparel order | $200 | 6% | $12.00 |
| Named collaboration or gift card | Any | 0% | $0 |

Taxes and shipping do not count.

Other retailers on Rakuten sell Adidas shoes, each with its own rate and exclusions. On the same page that day, Foot Locker showed 6% (was 3%), JD Sports 8% (was 3%) and Finish Line 2%. Whether a Samba earns the full rate at those stores is not confirmed. A higher rate helps only if the price is the same.

## In stores

Adidas did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 21, 2026. The list was cut off, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## When you get the money

The Adidas page says cash back is added to a Rakuten account the next day. It then waits to be confirmed, which typically takes 3 to 14 weeks, and is paid on the next of four dates a year. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/) and [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/). Card rewards are separate and come on top; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- You buy collaborations. The named ones are excluded.
- You buy through the Confirmed app.
- You have a student or hero discount code worth more than the cash back. On a $100 order, 15% off is $15 and 6% back is $6.
- You pay with or buy Adidas gift cards.
- You buy 10 or more of one item, or buy to resell.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Adidas?**
Up to 6% on November 21, 2026, against a usual 2%. Rates change daily.

**Do Sambas earn cash back?**
Yes, at 2% on that date. Samba, Gazelle, Spezial and EVO SL were held at 2% while other items earned 6%.

**Can I use an Adidas promo code and still get cash back?**
The terms say codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. Codes shown on Rakuten are the safe ones.
