Yes, Rakuten does Black Friday. It has a Black Friday page for November 27, 2026, and on November 25 many stores already showed raised cash back rates, such as 10% at Macy's against a usual 2%. Earning works the same as on any other day: activate first, then buy.

This page is information, not financial advice. For this morning's rate at 70 stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/). Not a member yet? Start with [is Rakuten free](/is-rakuten-free/) and [welcome bonus or referral bonus](/rakuten-welcome-bonus-vs-referral-bonus/).

## The dates

| Day | Date in 2026 |
|---|---|
| Black Friday | Friday, November 27 |
| Cyber Monday | Monday, November 30 |
| Cyber Week | The days after Cyber Monday; length varies by store |

Rakuten's Black Friday page says earning that day "works exactly the same as it does every other day." It says start dates vary by retailer and that many launch their sales in the week of Thanksgiving. On November 25 Rakuten's home page advertised Black Friday to Cyber Monday shopping with hundreds of brands at up to 15%. Its Cyber Monday page says some stores extend offers through the week and some deals last one day only.

- Rates fall as well as rise. Walgreens went from 10% back to 2% in two days. Dermstore showed 20% on November 20 and 10% on November 25.
- LEGO's 15% is for NINJAGO items. Its page shows 2.5% on other items that are not excluded and 1.5% on exclusives and hard-to-find items.
- Not every store is raised. [Apple](/rakuten-apple-cash-back-how-it-works/), [Ulta](/rakuten-ulta-cash-back-how-it-works/) and [Kohl's](/rakuten-kohls-cash-back-how-it-works/) each showed 2% with no "was" label on November 25.
- Rakuten has not published store rates for November 27 in advance; none were found.
- "Up to" means only some product categories earn the top figure.
- Rakuten's home page says cash back may vary. The number in your account is the one that counts.

Earlier this month Rakuten ran ten one-day offers, one store a day from November 1 to 10, at rates from 12% to 30%, according to Upgraded Points.

**Not confirmed:** which rate applies if a store's rate changes between your click and your order, and whether Rakuten adjusts an order when the rate rises later. Rakuten's terms do not say.

## Exclusions

Each store sets its own. Common ones in Rakuten's help center:

- Gift cards, at most stores.
- Taxes and shipping.
- Certain items, categories or brands, or a lower rate for them.
- Orders placed by phone, chat or customer service.
- Orders using a coupon code not listed on Rakuten.

A store's list is behind "See all Terms & Exclusions" on its Rakuten page. Rakuten's Black Friday page features Apple, but the Apple listing excludes new iPhones, Macs, iPads and Apple Watches.

Coupons are the frequent problem. Rakuten's Black Friday page says many codes can be used together with cash back, while its help center says codes not listed on Rakuten may void it. See [stacking with coupons and card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Tracking on a busy day

Rakuten lists these reasons an order fails to track:

- Activating after items are already in the cart.
- An ad blocker, VPN or cookie blocker, or declined cookies.
- Another rewards or coupon site or extension taking over the click.
- A long idle session, or checking out in a different tab.

For a Black Friday order that means: empty the cart, activate, add the items, and check out in one sitting. Rakuten's blog says a second order needs a new shopping trip.

Check your Rakuten account a day or two later. Rakuten's terms give 90 days from the purchase to report missing cash back. For a November 27 order that is February 25, 2027. A store can set a shorter limit; Best Buy's listing says 30 days from the order date. See [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

Shopping at the mall instead? Black Friday cash back in a physical store needs a linked card and an offer added first; see [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Returns

Rakuten's help center says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back, and a partial return is adjusted. Cash back stays "pending" while the store's return period runs, so a gift that goes back in January reduces the February payment.

## When the money arrives

Rakuten's payment page says online orders typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed; its status page says 3 to 17 weeks. It pays on fixed dates.

| Order on November 27, 2026 | Confirmed about | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed in 3 weeks | December 18, 2026 | February 15, 2027 |
| Confirmed in 14 weeks | March 5, 2027 | May 15, 2027 |

These dates are worked out from Rakuten's typical range. More in [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). The Monday after has its own page: [Rakuten on Cyber Monday 2026](/rakuten-cyber-monday-cash-back-2026/).

## Who should skip it

- The store's sale price is lower somewhere that Rakuten does not cover. A lower price today is worth more than a rebate in February.
- You have a coupon from another site that is worth more than the cash back.
- You would buy more because the rate looks high. Fifteen percent back still leaves 85% spent.

## Quick answers

**Does Rakuten do Black Friday deals?**
Yes. Rakuten has a Black Friday page, and many stores showed raised cash back rates on November 25, 2026.

**Are Rakuten rates higher on Black Friday?**
Many stores show raised rates in late November, but no rate is set in advance and each can change daily.

**When will Black Friday cash back be paid?**
On February 15 or May 15, 2027, depending on when the store confirms the order.
