Yes. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Hotels.com page showed up to 3% cash back: 3% on hotel bookings and 1% on cars, raised from a base of 1%. The biggest limit is coupon codes: the terms say cash back is not available when a coupon code is used, and it is credited only for completed hotel stays.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## Rates seen on November 25, 2026

| Booking type | Rate shown |
|---|---|
| Hotel bookings | 3% |
| Cars | 1% |
| Headline | Up to 3%, "was 1%" |

The "was 1%" label means the rate was raised above the store's base rate that day. Nothing on the page says how long the raised rate lasts.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Hotels.com list these limits:

- **Coupon codes.** Cash back is not available when a coupon code is used in a purchase. The terms make no exception for codes listed on Rakuten.
- **Airfare.** Flights earn nothing.
- **Bookings made through a travel agent.**
- **Gift cards.** Neither buying one nor paying with one earns.
- **Location.** Only customers located in the United States or Canada are eligible.
- **Stays that do not happen.** Cash back is credited only for completed, eligible hotel stays.

The rate list names hotel bookings and cars. Whether vacation rentals booked on the site count as hotel bookings is not confirmed.

## What earns and what does not

| Purchase | Earns? |
|---|---|
| Hotel booking, stay completed, no coupon code | Yes, 3% that day |
| Car booking | Yes, 1% that day |
| Hotel booking with a coupon code | No |
| Airfare | No |
| Canceled booking or no-show | No; the stay must be completed |
| Gift card purchase or redemption | No |

As an example, a completed $400 stay at 3% comes to $12.00. Rakuten's general help page says taxes are never eligible.

## One Key, the app and pay-later bookings

- **One Key.** Rakuten's page describes One Key, the rewards program shared with Expedia and Vrbo. The terms do not mention it. Whether paying part of a stay with OneKeyCash reduces the cash back is not confirmed.
- **The page is not consistent.** One section describes One Key; the question-and-answer section still describes collecting 10 stamps for a free night.
- **App.** The page says the Hotels.com app can show mobile-only prices. The terms do not mention the app. A booking earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension; whether a booking finished in the Hotels.com app tracks is not confirmed.
- **Pay now or pay at the hotel.** The terms do not separate the two. Not confirmed.

## When it shows up

The page says cash back is added to the Rakuten account within 3 months after travel has been completed. That is longer than for shopping. A stay booked now for next summer would not be credited until after that stay. Payment then follows Rakuten's four dates a year; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). How this store compares with other booking sites is in [Rakuten for hotels and flights](/rakuten-hotels-flights-cash-back/) and [Rakuten at Expedia and Booking.com](/rakuten-expedia-booking-com-cash-back/).

## Who should skip it

- You have a coupon code that saves more than 3%. Using it ends the cash back.
- You are booking a flight.
- You may cancel. A canceled stay earns nothing.
- You are located outside the United States and Canada.
- You need the money soon. It can take up to 3 months after the stay to be added.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Hotels.com?**
Up to 3% on November 25, 2026: 3% on hotel bookings and 1% on cars. Rates change daily.

**Can you use a coupon code and still get cash back?**
No. The terms say cash back is not available when a coupon code is used.

**When is the cash back added?**
Within 3 months after travel is completed, according to the store page.
