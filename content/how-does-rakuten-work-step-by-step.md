Rakuten works in six steps: you create a free account, click through Rakuten to a store, and buy as usual; the store pays Rakuten a commission, Rakuten passes part of it to you as cash back, and confirmed cash back is paid four times a year once you have about $5. Every step below was re-read on Rakuten's help pages on November 27, 2026.

This page is information, not financial advice. Still deciding? See [is Rakuten free](/is-rakuten-free/) and [is Rakuten legit, and how it makes money](/is-rakuten-legit-how-it-makes-money/). What each bonus asks you to spend is on [how the sign-up and referral bonuses work](/rakuten-50-bonus-how-it-works/).

## Step 1: Create an account

Rakuten says joining is free. You sign up with an email and password, or with a Facebook, Google or Apple account. Its terms say members must be at least 18 and live in the 50 states or Washington D.C., and each person may have one account. No card is needed to join. What the form asks for is on [how to sign up for Rakuten](/how-to-sign-up-for-rakuten/), and the two kinds of bonus are compared on [welcome bonus or referral bonus](/rakuten-welcome-bonus-vs-referral-bonus/).

## Step 2: Start a shopping trip

A "shopping trip" is Rakuten's name for the click that takes you from Rakuten to a store. Each trip gets a unique number, which Rakuten uses to verify the purchase with the store. Rakuten's help page says purchases earn cash back "only if you start a Shopping Trip with Rakuten." A trip stays in your account history for up to three months, even if you buy nothing.

There are five ways to earn; the first three start a trip with a click, and the last two use a linked card.

| Way to shop | How the trip starts | Where it works |
|---|---|---|
| Rakuten.com | Pick the store on the site and click through | Computer or phone browser |
| Browser extension | Click the activate button when it appears on a store's site | Desktop Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari; Safari on iPhone |
| Mobile app | Tap the store in the app | iOS and Android |
| In a physical store | Link a payment card, add the store's offer, pay with that card | Participating stores |
| At a restaurant | Link a card once and pay with it | 22,000+ restaurants, at 5%, per Rakuten |

A pop-up confirms that cash back is active. You need a new trip for every purchase, and the browser window you buy in must be the one that opened from Rakuten. Rakuten's pages differ on how many stores take part: its main help page says 3,500+, its extension page over 3,700. Rates differ by store and change daily; this morning's are on [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

For how the two main tools differ, see [the app and the extension compared](/rakuten-app-vs-browser-extension/). Card linking has its own rules; see [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/) and [which cards can be linked](/rakuten-link-card-for-in-store-cash-back/).

## Step 3: Buy at the store

You check out on the store's own website and pay the store, not Rakuten. Rakuten's example: spend $100 at a store offering 5% and you earn $5. Taxes and shipping do not earn cash back, most gift cards are excluded, and each store lists its excluded items. Orders finished by phone or chat do not qualify.

Rakuten's checklist for an order that tracks:

- Activate before adding anything to the cart.
- Check out in the tab that opened from Rakuten.
- Use only coupon codes listed on Rakuten.
- Accept the store's cookies, and turn off ad blockers, VPNs and cookie blockers.
- Keep the order. Returned or canceled orders do not earn.

## Step 4: The store pays Rakuten, and Rakuten shares it

The store reports the order to Rakuten and pays it a commission for sending the shopper. Rakuten keeps a portion and gives the rest to you as cash back. What share Rakuten keeps is not confirmed; its page says only that it shares part. The business side is covered in [how Rakuten makes money](/is-rakuten-legit-how-it-makes-money/).

## Step 5: Processing, pending, confirmed

| Status | What it means | Typical time, per Rakuten |
|---|---|---|
| Processing | Rakuten is waiting for the store to send the order details | 24 to 48 hours |
| Pending | The store has reported the order; its return period is running | 3 to 17 weeks overall; 3 to 14 weeks for online and in-store shopping |
| Confirmed | The amount is in your confirmed balance | Included in the next payment |
| Ineligible | The order did not qualify | Returns, exclusions, coupon codes not found on Rakuten |

Other kinds of purchase run on their own clocks. Rakuten's payment page says dining is confirmed within 3 to 5 days, and travel typically within 45 to 120 days after the booking is complete. Its terms say travel rewards typically are not confirmed until after the travel itself. See [how long a purchase takes to show up](/rakuten-how-long-until-purchase-shows-up/), and if nothing appears, [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/). The terms give 90 days from the purchase to report it.

## Step 6: Get paid

Rakuten pays on February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15. Each payment covers the cash back confirmed before that date. Bonuses are confirmed together with the purchase that earned them.

The minimum is about $5. Rakuten's help page says $5 or more; its terms say $5.01. A smaller balance carries over to the next date.

Rakuten's payment page lists PayPal, check, Bilt points and Samsung Cash. Its home page lists PayPal, Bilt points and check. Eligible American Express card members can take Membership Rewards points instead. A check or PayPal payment needs a mailing address on the account. The payment page also offers a faster route: a confirmed balance of at least $5 can be redeemed for a gift card, within limits. Worked dates are in [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

## Who should skip it

- You want the saving at checkout. Rakuten pays months later; a lower price or a coupon is money today.
- You do not want a company recording where you shop. See [what the extension collects](/rakuten-browser-extension-safe-privacy/).
- You always use coupon codes from other sites. Codes not listed on Rakuten can void the cash back.
- You shop online once or twice a year. The balance may sit under the minimum for a long time.

## Quick answers

**Do you pay Rakuten anything?**
No. Rakuten says joining is free. Its income is the commission stores pay for each sale.

**Do you have to click through Rakuten every time?**
Yes. Each purchase needs its own shopping trip, started from the site, app or extension.

**How often does Rakuten pay?**
Four times a year: February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15, with about $5 confirmed.
