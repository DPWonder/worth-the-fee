# Fact sheet: Chase daily batch 2026-11-21 (purchase protection, extended warranty, Reserve travel credit)

Checked 2026-11-21. Adds to the other chase fact sheets. UNCONFIRMED means no source read settled it. DISAGREE means sources conflict. "(search)" means seen only in a search summary.

## 1. Sapphire Preferred purchase protection

- 120 days from purchase, theft or accidental damage, $500 per claim, $50,000 per account; at least part of the price on the card or its points; administrator repairs, replaces or reimburses, capped at the lesser of amount charged or the limit (updated 2026-10-06). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/chase-price-and-purchase-protection-benefits/
- 120 days, up to $500 per item, second source; says lost, damaged or stolen; excludes animals, antiques, anything with an engine, perishables, used items (2026-06-23). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-preferred-lesser-known-benefits-2026/
- Exclusions: antiques and collectibles, vehicles, software, resale or commercial items, items that mysteriously disappear, items in a common carrier's custody, jewelry or watches in baggage, nondelivery or voluntary parting, medical equipment, perishables, cash, gift cards, tickets, used items. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/chase-price-and-purchase-protection-benefits/
- Claim: call 888-320-9656 within 90 days of the theft or damage; return the form within 120 days; police report within 48 hours for theft; papers are the claim form, card receipt, itemized store receipt, proof of other payment, police, fire or insurance report; damaged item may have to be shipped at own cost. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/chase-price-and-purchase-protection-benefits/
- DISAGREE: The Points Guy says lost items are covered; Upgraded Points says theft and accidental damage and excludes mysterious disappearance; Chase's wording for the Reserve is damage or theft. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-preferred-lesser-known-benefits-2026/
- Chase's Preferred card page text that loaded shows only a "Travel & purchase coverage" heading, no limits; $95 fee. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/preferred
- A search summary said one Chase card page lists a 90-day window; not seen on any page read. (search) https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/preferred
- UNCONFIRMED: whether gift recipients are covered; whether the benefit is secondary to other insurance; any per-year cap; the Guide to Benefits itself was not read. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/chase-price-and-purchase-protection-benefits/

## 2. Extended warranty (Sapphire Preferred and Reserve)

- Adds one year to a US manufacturer's warranty of three years or less, up to four years from purchase; store warranties or bought service contracts of three years or less count; item bought with the card or its points. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/extended-warranty-protection-with-chase-sapphire
- Pays the lesser of repair cost, replacement cost or the amount charged (less shipping and handling), up to $10,000 per item and $50,000 per account. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/extended-warranty-protection-with-chase-sapphire
- Exclusions: motorized vehicles and their parts, costs outside the original warranty, resale, professional or commercial items, rented or leased items, software, medical equipment, used or pre-owned items. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/extended-warranty-protection-with-chase-sapphire
- Claim: contact the administrator within 90 days of the failure, documents within 120 days; card statement, other payment records, itemized receipt, original warranty and any store warranty, repair estimate or paid repair bill; chasecardbenefits.com can store receipts. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/extended-warranty-protection-with-chase-sapphire
- DISAGREE: Upgraded Points says the original warranty must be under 3 years; Chase says three years or less. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/chase-price-and-purchase-protection-benefits/

## 3. Sapphire Reserve purchase and return protection

- Purchase protection: 120 days, damage or theft, up to $10,000 per item. Return protection: 90 days, $500 per item, $1,000 per 12-month period. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/extended-warranty-protection-with-chase-sapphire
- $10,000 per claim and $50,000 per account, second source; return protection only on the Reserve of the two cards, claim line 888-880-5844. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/chase-price-and-purchase-protection-benefits/

## 4. Sapphire Reserve $300 travel credit

- "Up to $300 in statement credits for travel purchases each account anniversary year"; applies to "purchases made in the travel category"; annual means the account open date through the first statement date after the anniversary, then each 12 monthly billing cycles; any portion that qualifies earns no points; Chase does not determine whether merchants code correctly; fee $795, authorized user $195; 8x Chase Travel, 4x flights and hotels direct. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve
- Travel category includes airlines, hotels, motels, timeshares, car rental agencies, cruise lines, travel agencies, discount travel sites, campgrounds, passenger trains, buses, taxis, limousines, ferries, toll bridges and highways, parking lots and garages. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/rewards-category-faq
- Travel category excludes real estate agents, educational merchants arranging travel, in-flight goods and services, on-board cruise goods and services, sightseeing, excursions, tourist attractions, RV and boat rentals, merchants within hotels and airports, public campgrounds, vehicle rentals for hauling, gift card merchants and merchants selling points or miles. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/rewards-category-faq
- No activation and no specific booking site; examples: $270 airfare plus $180 hotel gives $270 and $30; a $500 car rental gives $300; credit may appear within a few days, up to 1 to 2 billing cycles on the statement; renews on the account anniversary. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/rewards-benefits/chase-sapphire-reserve-travel-credit-how-it-works
- Accounts opened before May 21, 2017 get the credit by calendar year, after the December statement closes; not counted: theme park tickets bought from the park, ski lift tickets, Points.com, some gift cards; credits usually post within a couple of days (published 2025-07-08, "regularly updated"). One source. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/chase-sapphire-reserve-annual-travel-credit/
- Rideshare and subways count; $1,000 spent with $300 credited earns points on $700; a refund should reverse the credit, though some readers report it does not; track under "More", "Card benefits" (2026-06-02). https://onemileatatime.com/guides/chase-sapphire-reserve-travel-credit/
- DISAGREE on posting time: Chase guide says a few days, up to 1 to 2 billing cycles; The Points Guy says a couple of days; Chase's terms for other Sapphire credits say 3 business days, up to 4 weeks (chase-more.md). https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/rewards-benefits/chase-sapphire-reserve-travel-credit-how-it-works
- UNCONFIRMED: Airbnb and vacation rentals toward the credit (search summary only); whether authorized-user purchases draw on the same $300; order of use against The Edit and other hotel credits on a Chase Travel booking; the pre-May 2017 calendar-year rule on a Chase page. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/rewards-category-faq
