# Fact sheet: Rakuten (US), batch `rakuten-more`

Checked 2026-11-20. Adds to `chase-rakuten.md` section 7 and `rakuten-deep.md`; nothing here repeats them unless a new source changes it. Every line ends with the page it came from. "(search)" means the fact was seen in a search-result summary of that page, not in a full read. UNCONFIRMED means no source settled it. DISAGREE means sources conflict. Rates are "seen on 2026-11-20" and change daily.

## 1. Capital One Shopping

- Free; no Capital One bank account or card needed. Linking one is used to confirm redemption eligibility and may be requested for verification. https://capitaloneshopping.com/help
- Shopping Rewards are "not equivalent to money and do not have a cash value"; they are separate from any credit card rewards. https://capitaloneshopping.com/help
- Earned by clicking "Activate" in the extension or app and completing an eligible purchase; counted on the subtotal, not tax, shipping or fees. Common exclusions: canceled orders, gift cards, in-store or phone orders. https://capitaloneshopping.com/help
- Redeemed for e-gift cards from select retailers; delivered within 24 to 72 business hours; nonrefundable, no exchanges. No PayPal or bank payout is mentioned. Minimum to redeem and expiry: not stated on the help page. https://capitaloneshopping.com/help
- Travel rewards post 60 to 90 days after travel is completed. https://capitaloneshopping.com/help
- Help page says to disable other shopping extensions and avoid incognito mode, VPNs and other ad blockers; if another program interferes it cannot confirm the purchase or adjust rewards afterward. https://capitaloneshopping.com/help
- The extension monitors products viewed and bought on shopping sites and promo codes that worked, and shares price updates and working codes with other users. https://capitaloneshopping.com/help
- TPG (dated 2026-11-03): website, extension and app; coupon code search, price comparison, price-drop alerts; rewards typically post within 30 to 90 days; redeemed for electronic gift cards, not cash; card rewards can generally be earned on the same purchase; another portal or an unlisted coupon code may interfere with tracking. https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/capital-one-shopping/
- AwardWallet (dated 2026-06-21): gift cards are the only redemption; credits take 30 to 90 days to post and do not expire while the account is open and in good standing; offers are personalized (author saw up to 1% at one store logged out and up to 8% in a logged-in promotion). https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/capital-one-rewards/capital-one-shopping/
- Minimum gift-card redemption amount: UNCONFIRMED (not on the help page or in either article).
- Rakuten on competing extensions: clicking a link from another coupon or rewards site after activating Rakuten "will disable Rakuten Cash Back"; Rakuten says it notifies you so you can re-activate, and asks members to disable or uninstall competing extensions. No extension is named. https://rakuten.com/help/article/using-the-cash-back-button-with-other-similar-browser-extensions-360001446948
- That only one service is credited per purchase (the last click before checkout) is the general affiliate pattern; the exact behavior between these two extensions is UNCONFIRMED. Source is a competing cash back company. https://discover.shopback.com/compare/capital-one-shopping-alternatives (search)

## 2. TopCashback (US)

- Paid by transfer to a bank, Venmo or PayPal account, or as gift cards (Amazon, Starbucks, Target, Sephora named). Once cash back is "available" it can be cashed out at any time. https://www.topcashback.com/help/receive-cash-back/
- Cash-out steps: Cash Out page, pick a method, pick an amount, answer a security question, confirm; an email arrives when the payment is available. No processing time stated. https://www.topcashback.com/help/cash-out-help/
- Each retailer sets its own period before cash back becomes available; the Earnings page shows an estimate per purchase; travel becomes available only after the stay is complete. https://www.topcashback.com/help/cash-back-not-payable/
- Income: retailers pay TopCashback for sending shoppers, plus advertising on its site; it says advertising lets it run "without taking a cut from your Cash Back". The page does not say what share of retailer payments goes to members. https://www.topcashback.com/help/how-we-make-money/
- Highest Cash Back Guarantee: buy through TopCashback, file a claim once the cash back has tracked, and TopCashback says it will beat the other offer after approval. Full terms sit behind sign-in. https://www.topcashback.com/help/price-matching
- TPG (dated 2025-11-24): 7,000+ retailers; no minimum payout; PayPal, ACH and gift cards, some gift cards with minimum amounts; usually up to a 5% bonus when paid as merchant credit or a Visa or American Express prepaid card; pending within 7 days, payable usually within a few weeks, up to 90 days. https://thepointsguy.com/guide/topcashback/
- TPG on the guarantee: covers higher rates from eight named competitors including Rakuten and Capital One Shopping; the higher rate must be from the same day as the purchase; claim within 7 days; it matches cash rates only, not points or miles. https://thepointsguy.com/guide/topcashback/
- DISAGREE (minor): payout methods. TopCashback's page lists bank, Venmo, PayPal and gift cards; TPG lists PayPal, ACH and gift cards; one blog adds a check option, which TopCashback's page does not list. https://www.moneysmylife.com/topcashback-vs-rakuten-which-pays-more/
- DISAGREE (minor): minimums. TPG says none; other summaries say some gift cards carry minimums of a few dollars. Not stated on TopCashback's help pages read. https://thepointsguy.com/guide/topcashback/
- Which pays more: one blog's own test over 15 stores in 2025 found TopCashback higher at 10, Rakuten at 4, equal at 1 (article dated 2026-01-30; single unverified test). https://www.moneysmylife.com/topcashback-vs-rakuten-which-pays-more/
- NerdWallet's comparison says TopCashback's rates are often higher than Rakuten's. https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/travel/topcashback-vs-rakuten (search; page text would not load)
- How long a TopCashback payout takes after the request: UNCONFIRMED on its own pages (third parties say 1 to 7 business days by method).

## 3. Rakuten browser extension

- Chrome Web Store listing: "Rakuten: Get Cash Back For Shopping", offered by Ebates Performance Marketing Inc., d/b/a Rakuten Rewards; 3,000,000 users; rated 4.7 out of 5 from 43.6K ratings; version 26.22.1; updated November 17, 2026. https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/rakuten-get-cash-back-for/chhjbpecpncaggjpdakmflnfcopglcmi
- The developer's Chrome privacy declaration lists web history, user activity and website content; it declares data is not sold to third parties outside approved use cases, not used for purposes unrelated to the item's core functionality, and not used for creditworthiness or lending. https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/rakuten-get-cash-back-for/chhjbpecpncaggjpdakmflnfcopglcmi
- Extension privacy notice (updated March 13, 2026): the extension automatically collects information on the pages you visit, including URLs and general page information, "whether you are interacting with it or not". https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/rakuten-cash-back-button-privacy-notice-360052819794
- Also collected: product searches and results, items added to cart, products, coupons and offers viewed or clicked, date and time of use, browser type and version, device type. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/rakuten-cash-back-button-privacy-notice-360052819794
- On order confirmation pages it collects order contents and order total for "Instant Cash Back", from certain merchants, not all. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/rakuten-cash-back-button-privacy-notice-360052819794
- On certain web-based generative AI apps it detects store names or domains in the AI's output; the notice says Rakuten does not collect or store chat history. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/rakuten-cash-back-button-privacy-notice-360052819794
- Uses: personalized offers (including by email), internal testing, faster confirmation of cash back, cart reminders and coupon offers at checkout. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/rakuten-cash-back-button-privacy-notice-360052819794
- A shopping session sets a cookie through the affiliate network; accepting an offer can overwrite other companies' affiliate cookies (also Terms 6.2(b)). https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/rakuten-cash-back-button-privacy-notice-360052819794 and https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/terms-conditions
- Settings inside the extension can turn off order confirmations and offers shown in generative AI output. Uninstalling stops all collection by the extension. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/rakuten-cash-back-button-privacy-notice-360052819794
- Firefox add-on permissions: access browser tabs, access browser activity during navigation, access your data for all websites; notifications optional. https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/ebates/ (search)
- An independent security audit of the extension: UNCONFIRMED (none found).
- Retention periods for extension data: not stated in the notice. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/rakuten-cash-back-button-privacy-notice-360052819794

## 4. Check versus PayPal

- Checks are issued to the name and address in Account Settings. PayPal is updated in Account Settings; in the app: Account, then Choose How You Get Paid. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/choosing-how-you-get-paid-360002100828
- Terms 10.1: to be paid by PayPal you must provide a valid PayPal email address. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/terms-conditions
- A Big Fat Check is valid for 90 days plus a 90-day grace period, 180 days from the issue date. If one is missing, lost, destroyed or expired, Rakuten will issue a replacement; contact Member Services. No reissue fee is mentioned. A "Track My Payment" tool is linked. https://rakuten.com/help/article/missing-lost-or-expired-checks-360002101268
- Terms 10.2: if a check expires or is returned uncashed, the amount goes back to the account, where it may be subject to maintenance charges or state abandoned property law. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/terms-conditions
- Mobile deposit: many banks' apps accept the check; detach the top part and endorse it, then photograph front and back. https://rakuten.com/help/article/mobile-deposit-of-your-big-fat-check-360048175773
- Deadline to change the payment method before a payment date: UNCONFIRMED (not on the page). https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/choosing-how-you-get-paid-360002100828
- PayPal fees or how fast a PayPal payment lands: UNCONFIRMED (not on Rakuten's pages).
- A search summary of Rakuten's help center mentioned a 45-day window between the end of a payment period and delivery; not seen in the pages read. UNCONFIRMED. https://rakuten.com/help/category/getting-paid-26579048142483 (search)
- Samsung Cash: needs an eligible Samsung device; appears in Samsung Wallet, redeemable any time or sent every 3 months. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/choosing-how-you-get-paid-360002100828

## 5. Stacking

- Portal rewards come on top of the rewards the credit card earns; Amex Offers, Chase Offers and Citi Merchant Offers can generally be combined with portal earnings (article dated 2026-08-23). https://thepointsguy.com/guide/maximizing-online-shopping-portals/
- A promo code not found on the portal may make the purchase ineligible; many stores do not pay on gift card purchases. https://thepointsguy.com/guide/maximizing-online-shopping-portals/
- Rakuten's Black Friday page on stacking codes: "Often, yes. Many coupons and promo codes can be used together with Rakuten Cash Back." It advises checking each program's terms. https://www.rakuten.com/black-friday
- Terms 6.1(a): if you visit other sites before completing the purchase or use coupons not provided by Rakuten, the purchase might be associated with another service and might not earn rewards. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/terms-conditions
- Rakuten's Cyber Monday page says cash back can be layered on sales, store promotions, loyalty rewards and coupon or promo codes. https://www.rakuten.com/cyber-monday
- Stacking Rakuten with a second shopping portal on one purchase: Rakuten says a click from another rewards site disables its cash back. https://rakuten.com/help/article/using-the-cash-back-button-with-other-similar-browser-extensions-360001446948

## 7. Switching to Amex points and back

- Rakuten's steps: Account Settings; update payment method to Membership Rewards points; connect by entering the Membership Rewards username and password. Relink under Account Settings, "Edit" below Membership Rewards points. https://rakuten.com/help/article/american-express-membership-rewards-points-faq-360017609253
- Points terms: "Cash Back accrued but not disbursed prior to linking accounts will be converted into Membership Rewards points"; the card account must stay open, active and in good standing until points are transferred. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/american-express-membership-rewards-points-at-rakuten-terms-360017609913
- Neither Rakuten page says whether you can switch back to cash. https://rakuten.com/help/article/american-express-membership-rewards-points-faq-360017609253
- Miles to Memories (2025-05-13): Account Settings, "How You're Getting Paid" section, pick the option, confirm an "Are You Sure?" pop-up, add a mailing address for a check, enter a six-digit emailed code if asked; the change shows at once. The author reports switching back and forth works. It quotes Rakuten's on-screen warning that points earned before switching to cash back are converted to cash back and sent by check on the next payment date. https://milestomemories.com/?p=152585
- TPG (byline 2025-08-22): select PayPal or check first, then "Switch to Membership Rewards points" in the American Express section; says the selection is irreversible and that an existing cash balance stays cash. https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/earn-amex-points-with-rakuten/
- Doctor of Credit (updated 2026-05-16), writing about Bilt: members could change to Bilt and change back to Membership Rewards after a payout; on linking Bilt, pending rewards pay out as Bilt points. https://www.doctorofcredit.com/rakuten-cashback-can-now-be-earned-as-bilt-points-temporarily-11-conversion/
- DISAGREE: switching back. TPG says irreversible; Miles to Memories and Doctor of Credit report switching back works; Rakuten's help pages are silent. An older Doctor of Credit post said a switch from points to cash could not be reversed. https://www.doctorofcredit.com/ebates-you-can-now-switch-your-account-from-cash-earn-to-membership-rewards-points-earn/amp (search)
- DISAGREE: existing balance on switching to points. Rakuten's FAQ and terms say it converts to points; TPG says it stays cash. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/american-express-membership-rewards-points-at-rakuten-terms-360017609913 and https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/earn-amex-points-with-rakuten/
- TPG says the sign-up bonus stays as cash but a referral bonus can pay as points (3,000 points in its 2025 example). Current amount as points: UNCONFIRMED. https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/earn-amex-points-with-rakuten/

## 8. Tax

- Terms 10.5: "You may be taxed on your receipt of bonuses and other consideration" for referrals or promotions, and the member is solely responsible for any tax liability from them. Tax forms, 1099, W-9 and taxpayer ID are not mentioned in the Terms. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/terms-conditions
- IRS Publication 525 (2025), "Cash rebates": a cash rebate from a dealer or manufacturer of an item you buy is not income, but you reduce your basis in the item by the rebate. https://www.irs.gov/publications/p525
- IRS private letter ruling 201027015 (released 2010): a rebate received by a buyer is an adjustment to the purchase price and not gross income. Read through TPG's description; the PDF text did not extract cleanly. https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-wd/1027015.pdf and https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/complete-guide-to-paying-taxes-on-credit-card-rewards/
- TPG (2026-02-06): rewards tied to spending are generally treated as rebates and not taxed; referral bonuses and bonuses that need no spending are considered taxable income; income is reportable even if no 1099 arrives; reported on Schedule 1 (Form 1040), line 8z. The article is about credit cards and does not mention shopping portals. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/complete-guide-to-paying-taxes-on-credit-card-rewards/
- IRS instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC: for tax years beginning after 2025 the minimum reporting amount for certain payments rose to $2,000 (box 3 "other income of $2,000 or more"); it may be adjusted for inflation from 2027. https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099mec
- The earlier threshold was $600 (law change in OBBBA section 70433). https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/17/2026-07519/increase-in-threshold-for-requiring-information-reporting-with-respect-to-certain-payees-extension (search)
- A member reported getting a Form 1099-NEC from Rakuten for a referral bonus (thread dated 2026-02-06; no amount or year given). https://community.freetaxusa.com/discussion/2900/question-about-reporting-referral-bonus-on-1099-nec
- Another member reported no 1099 from Rakuten for $120 of referral bonuses and cash back. https://ttlc.intuit.com/community/business-taxes/discussion/rakuten-referral-bonus/00/2925711 (search)
- Rakuten's own policy on when it issues a 1099 or asks for a W-9: UNCONFIRMED (no Rakuten page found).
- DISAGREE: where a referral bonus goes on a return. A tax-software agent says a 1099-NEC amount generally goes on Schedule C; TPG says rewards income goes on Schedule 1, line 8z. https://community.freetaxusa.com/discussion/2900/question-about-reporting-referral-bonus-on-1099-nec and https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/complete-guide-to-paying-taxes-on-credit-card-rewards/
- Tax treatment of the new member's sign-up bonus (it requires purchases) and of rewards taken as Amex points: UNCONFIRMED for Rakuten specifically; no IRS guidance naming shopping portals was found.
