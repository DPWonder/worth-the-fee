- Free first checked bag for the primary cardmember and one companion on the same reservation; the ticket must be bought with the card and carry the cardmember's MileagePlus number; United and United Express flights only. https://creditcards.chase.com/travel-credit-cards/united/united-explorer
- Two one-time United Club passes after account opening and every 12 months after. https://creditcards.chase.com/travel-credit-cards/united/united-explorer
- Group 2 boarding for the cardmember and companions on the reservation; 25% back on United inflight food, drinks and Wi-Fi; no foreign transaction fee. https://creditcards.chase.com/travel-credit-cards/united/united-explorer
- Credits: rideshare up to $5 a month ($60 a calendar year, yearly opt-in); Instacart up to $10 a month ($120 a calendar year) through 2027-12-31; JSX up to $100 per anniversary year; United Hotels $50 on each of the first two prepaid stays per anniversary year; Avis or Budget $25 TravelBank cash on each of the first two rentals booked through United's site; Global Entry, TSA PreCheck or NEXUS up to $120 every 4 years. https://creditcards.chase.com/travel-credit-cards/united/united-explorer

- 1 Premier Qualifying Point per $20 of purchases, up to 1,000 a calendar year. https://creditcards.chase.com/travel-credit-cards/united/united-explorer
- Cardholders see lower award prices on United; One Mile at a Time values a United mile at 1.3 cents and each club pass at $59. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/chase/united-explorer-card/

One source: UNCONFIRMED on Chase's page. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/chase/united-explorer-card/

## 8. Own arithmetic used on the pages

- Platinum fee less Reserve fee: $895 - $795 = $100. Reserve after its travel credit: $795 - $300 = $495. Platinum airline-fee credit only: $895 - $200 = $695. (amex.md, chase-rakuten.md)
- United bag saving: $45 prepaid each way, so $90 a round trip for one traveler and $180 for two. $150 / $90 = 1.67, so two round trips alone, or one with a companion, cover the fee. (section 1; trips.md)
- Explorer versus Preferred on $3,000 of United fares: 3 x 3,000 = 9,000 miles against 2 x 3,000 = 6,000 points.
- Southwest: 7,500 points x 1.25 cents = $93.75; $229 - $93.75 = $135.25; $135.25 / $90 a round trip = 1.5 round trips of bag fees. A family of four on one round trip: 4 x $90 = $360. (section 2)
- Aspire with only the flight credit and one resort half used: $550 - $200 - $200 = $150. Hilton spend of $5,000: Aspire 14 x 5,000 = 70,000 Hilton points; Platinum 1 x 5,000 = 5,000 Amex points, 10,000 Hilton points at 1:2. (section 3; amex.md)
- Green versus Preferred: fee gap $150 - $95 = $55. Green with CLEAR+ used: $150 - $219 = minus $69. Preferred with the hotel credit used: $95 - $100 = minus $5. (section 4; chase-rakuten.md)
- Bilt, rent $2,000 a month ($24,000 a year). Housing-only at 75% ($1,500 a month of other spending): 2,000 points a month, 24,000 a year. At 100%: 2,500 a month, 30,000 a year. At 25% ($500): 1,000 a month, 12,000 a year. Bilt Cash option: $30 per 1,000 points, so $60 a month needs $1,500 of spending at 4%. Rent paid through Bilt with a Sapphire Preferred: no housing points from a card reward (1x at most, less any card fee the landlord or portal charges; not researched). (section 5)
- Autograph Journey versus Preferred on $4,000 airfare, $3,000 hotels booked direct, $4,000 dining: Journey 16,000 + 15,000 + 12,000 = 43,000; Preferred 8,000 + 6,000 + 12,000 = 26,000. (section 6; chase-rakuten.md)
- Strata Elite: $595 - $300 hotel = $295; less $200 Splurge = $95; less $200 Blacklane = minus $105. With Citigold: $595 - $145 = $450. Reserve: $795 - $300 = $495; less $500 The Edit = minus $5; less $300 dining = minus $305. Fee gap $795 - $595 = $200. (section 7; chase-rakuten.md)
- Bilt against Preferred, rent $2,000 a month, other spending $1,500 a month of which $500 dining (75% of rent, so 1x on rent): Preferred 0 + 18,000 + 12,000 = 30,000; Blue 24,000 + 6,000 + 12,000 = 42,000; Obsidian (dining chosen) 24,000 + 18,000 + 12,000 = 54,000. At $400 a month of other spending (20%): 250 points a month, 3,000 a year on rent. (section 5; chase-rakuten.md)
- Explorer against Preferred, $3,000 United fares and $3,000 dining: Preferred 6,000 + 9,000 = 15,000; Explorer 9,000 + 6,000 = 15,000. Both fees: $95 + $150 = $245. (section 1)
- Southwest, $2,500 fares and $3,000 dining: Preferred 5,000 + 9,000 = 14,000; Priority 10,000 + 6,000 + 7,500 = 23,500. Plus: 3,000 x 1.25 cents = $37.50; $99 - $37.50 = $61.50. (section 2)
- Green against Preferred, $5,000 direct travel and $4,000 dining: Preferred 10,000 + 12,000 = 22,000; Green 15,000 + 12,000 = 27,000; the same travel through Chase Travel earns the Preferred 25,000. (section 4)
- Strata Elite authorized users: 2 x $75 = $150; Reserve 2 x $195 = $390. Direct travel $8,000: Reserve 32,000, Strata Elite 12,000. Other spending $20,000: Strata Elite 30,000, Reserve 20,000. Reserve with every hotel and dining credit: $795 - $300 - $500 - $300 = minus $305. (section 7)
- Platinum and Aspire together: $895 + $550 = $1,445. Point values per dollar: 14 Hilton points x 0.4 to 0.5 cents = 5.6 to 7 cents; 5 Amex points x 1.7 to 2.0 cents = 8.5 to 10 cents. (section 3; amex-cards.md)
- Reserve against Platinum, $6,000 direct flights and hotels and $4,000 dining: Reserve 24,000 + 12,000 = 36,000. Platinum Resy + Uber Cash + digital entertainment: 400 + 200 + 300 = $900. (amex.md; chase-rakuten.md)
