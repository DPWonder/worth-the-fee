## 1. Sign-up bonus as Amex points

- Rakuten's American Express page offers new members a welcome bonus in points: on 2026-11-23 it read 5,000 points after joining and spending $25 on qualifying purchases; fine print: new member, at least $25 before taxes on purchases eligible for points within 90 days of becoming a member. https://www.rakuten.com/american-express
- Same page: new users sign up and enter their Membership Rewards username and password when prompted; existing members go to Account Settings and select Membership Rewards points under How You Get Paid. https://www.rakuten.com/american-express
- Same page: "10% Cash Back = 10X Membership Rewards points"; card must be enrolled in US Membership Rewards, not canceled or past due; Gold, Green and Platinum named as popular eligible cards; corporate cards and the Rakuten American Express Card not eligible. https://www.rakuten.com/american-express
- Same page: confirmed cash back earned before switching to points is converted to points and sent by the next transfer date; 501 confirmed points needed before a transfer date, 500 or fewer carries over. https://www.rakuten.com/american-express
- Welcome bonus terms: bonus "will be added to your next Cash Back payment or points transfer"; qualifying purchase is one that "earns Cash Back or points"; $25 within 90 days; new members only; one per member; not combinable with other offers including the Referral Sign Up Bonus; may be modified or canceled anytime. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/welcome-bonus-terms
- Membership Rewards FAQ: confirmed points earned "on purchases, bonuses and other rewards" are transferred; transfer dates February 15, May 15, August 15, November 15. The FAQ does not name the welcome or sign-up bonus. https://rakuten.com/help/article/american-express-membership-rewards-points-faq-360017609253
- Membership Rewards terms: no mention of welcome, referral or sign-up bonuses and no stated ratio; cash back accrued but not disbursed before linking is converted to points; never both cash back and points for the same purchase; corporate cards not eligible; other issuers' promotions unavailable while set to points. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/american-express-membership-rewards-points-at-rakuten-terms-360017609913
- Welcome bonus FAQ for new members: three steps (sign up, $25 qualifying purchase within 90 days, choose a payout method); mailing address needed; no mention of points. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/welcome-bonus-new-member-faqs
- Referral page (read 2026-11-23): referrer $50; friend needs qualifying purchases of at least $50 within 90 days; offer period ends 11:59 PM PT 2026-12-31; "Bonus amounts are subject to change from time to time"; no mention of points or Membership Rewards; friend's amount not stated. https://www.rakuten.com/referral
- Referral program terms and refer-a-friend FAQ: neither says whether the friend's sign-up bonus can be paid as points. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/referral-program-terms-360002101348 and https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/referafriend-program-faq-360002117087

- Rakuten's Cyber Monday FAQ: Black Friday was historically tied to in-store doorbusters, Cyber Monday began as an online-only day, and the two now overlap. https://www.rakuten.com/cyber-monday

## 3. Target: Circle Card, Circle deals, Circle 360

- Rakuten's Target page seen 2026-11-23: 1% cash back, no "was" rate; terms unchanged from `rakuten-stores-a.md` (allow list of categories; Prepaid and Gift Cards, Target Circle Earnings, Target Circle 360 and Drive Up items/orders excluded; codes not listed on Rakuten void cash back; free gift card reduces the eligible amount). https://www.rakuten.com/target.com
- The terms do not mention the Target Circle Card, Order Pickup, same-day delivery, the Target app, or paying with a Target gift card. https://www.rakuten.com/target.com
- Rakuten's own text on that page describes combining Target Circle offers, credit card rewards and cash back by starting at Rakuten, using Target's offers and paying with a rewards card. Its shopping tips say the card discount (under its old name, REDcard) still applies on top of coupons. https://www.rakuten.com/target.com
- Target: Circle Card gives 5% off at Target stores and Target.com; "Get 5% off on top of your Target Circle deals"; available as credit, Mastercard and debit. https://www.target.com/circlecard
- Target help, Circle Card benefits: the 5% applies to eligible purchases minus any other discounts and minus the value of promotional Target gift cards received in the transaction; with split payment it applies only to the amount put on the Circle Card. https://www.target.com/help/article/000062733
- Same article, no 5% on: prescriptions and pharmacy-counter OTC items, Target Optical eye exams and protection plans, Target GiftCards and prepaid cards, previous purchases, Shipt and Target Circle 360 fees, gift wrapping, taxes, shipping and fees, among others. It does not address orders that start from a third-party site. https://www.target.com/help/article/000062733
- Target help, Circle membership: online, Circle deals need you to be signed in at checkout; per item, one item-level and one category-level deal apply; storewide deals and bonuses can combine with those; Circle Bonuses must be activated; Target Circle Rewards are earned on eligible purchases and cannot be applied in part. https://www.target.com/help/article/000211153
- Target help: most Target Circle deals apply automatically at checkout. https://www.target.com/help/article/000071294
- Target Circle 360: $99 a year or $10.99 a month; $49 a year or $4.99 a month with a Circle Card saved to the account; includes shipping benefits, same-day delivery on orders over $35, monthly offers, 30 extra days to return. https://www.target.com/l/target-circle-360/-/N-2rguk
- UNCONFIRMED: whether Rakuten's 1% is counted before or after the 5% Circle Card discount; whether an automatic Circle deal counts as a "coupon code" under Rakuten's Target terms; what "Target Circle Earnings" excludes (Target's name is Target Circle Rewards); whether the part of an order paid with a Target gift card earns; whether Order Pickup and Circle 360 same-day delivery orders earn; Target's limit on promo codes per online order (only coupon sites state one). https://www.rakuten.com/target.com
