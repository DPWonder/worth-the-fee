## 1. How Rakuten works, step by step

- Rakuten's five steps: sign up for free with an email address at Rakuten.com or in the app; choose a store; activate cash back by clicking through from the site or app, or in the browser extension, "to start a shopping trip"; complete checkout at the store's website; cash back is added to the account and paid quarterly once you earn $5 or more. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-does-rakuten-work-360002117047
- Example on the same page: $100 at a store offering 5% earns $5. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-does-rakuten-work-360002117047
- Joining is free; an account is made with an email and password, or with a Facebook, Google or Apple account. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-to-join-rakuten-360002101108
- Terms 2.1: members must be at least 18; the service is not available to residents outside the 50 states or Washington D.C.; one account per person. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/terms-conditions
- Ways to shop: Rakuten.com on a computer or mobile browser; the app for iOS and Android (app-only deals, dining, in-store); the browser extension on desktop Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari; a Safari mobile extension for iPhone that comes with the app. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-does-rakuten-work-360002117047
- In-store and dining: link a card to the account and earn at participating in-store and dining locations. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-does-rakuten-work-360002117047
- A shopping trip is created when you click a Rakuten link (site, app or Cash Back Button); it gets a unique number used to verify the purchase with the store. Purchases earn only if a shopping trip was started. A new trip is needed for every purchase, and the browser window used to shop must have come from a Rakuten link. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/what-is-a-rakuten-shopping-trip-360002100588
- A shopping trip with no purchase stays recorded in the account for up to three months. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/what-is-a-rakuten-shopping-trip-360002100588
- Sign of activation: a confirmation pop-up saying cash back at that store is now active. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-can-i-tell-if-im-earning-cash-back-360002100668
- Tracking uses a cookie placed in the browser when you click through; it links the purchase to the account. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/why-didnt-i-earn-cash-back-360036367393
- DISAGREE (minor): store count. The "How does Rakuten work" page says 3,500+ stores; the "All the ways to shop" page says the extension finds cash back at over 3,700 stores. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-does-rakuten-work-360002117047 and https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/all-the-ways-to-shop-with-rakuten-360002100548
- Statuses re-read today: processing typically 24 to 48 hours; pending typically 3 to 17 weeks; confirmed; ineligible. Status is under Cash Back Activity in the account. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/tracking-your-cash-back-360002117107
- Share of the store's commission that Rakuten passes on: UNCONFIRMED (the page says only "part of that commission"). https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-does-rakuten-work-360002117047

## 2. Linking a card for in-store cash back

- How to link: go to Linked Cards in the account, select Link a Card, enter the card details. To remove: Linked Cards, Edit next to the card, Remove. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/linking-a-card-for-instore-and-dining-cash-back-360002100528
- Eligible: most Visa credit and debit cards, Mastercard credit and debit cards and American Express cards. Ineligible list is the same as in the in-store FAQ (Wells Fargo Mastercards, American Express debit cards, virtual cards with a different number each transaction such as Apple Card, Discover, Macy's American Express, corporate and purchasing, HSA, FSA, government prepaid including EBT, insurance prepaid, Visa Buxx, Diners Club). https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/linking-a-card-for-instore-and-dining-cash-back-360002100528
- "Yes, you can link more than one card to your account." No maximum is given for in-store offers. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/linking-a-card-for-instore-and-dining-cash-back-360002100528
- The card-linked terms set a limit only for dining: up to twelve (12) cards in the restaurant program, and one card may be in only one dining program. A maximum for in-store offers: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-terms-conditions-115013738007
- In-store offers must be added before the purchase. Dining offers are added to a linked card automatically and never expire. Most in-store offers expire in 75 days or once used and must be re-added. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/linking-a-card-for-instore-and-dining-cash-back-360002100528
- Debit: run as credit and sign if prompted; "Debit transactions processed as PIN may not qualify." https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/linking-a-card-for-instore-and-dining-cash-back-360002100528
- Terms on PIN: do not use a PIN if you want the transaction to qualify; PIN-based purchases may not be eligible. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-terms-conditions-115013738007
- DISAGREE: mobile wallets (re-read today, unchanged). FAQ: physical cards recommended; Apple Pay, Google Wallet and Samsung Pay "may not always track properly". Terms: payments through third-party digital wallets or payment apps "other than Apple Pay and Samsung Wallet" may not be eligible. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-faq-15935028227987 and https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-terms-conditions-115013738007
- DISAGREE (minor, re-read today): the /in-store page FAQ names American Express, Mastercard and Visa credit cards only; the help articles add Visa and Mastercard debit cards. https://www.rakuten.com/in-store
- Card data, help article: card details are stored in a secure, encrypted environment; Rakuten does not sell card information; it will never charge the card and never ask for the CVV; payment network partners confirm eligible purchases. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/linking-a-card-for-instore-and-dining-cash-back-360002100528
- Card data, FAQ: payment networks confirm eligible transactions "without sharing your full card details". https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-faq-15935028227987
- Card data, /in-store page: card data is stored in an encrypted environment certified as compliant with the Payment Card Industry (PCI) standard. https://www.rakuten.com/in-store
- Card data, terms: the card network or processor may monitor transactions on the enrolled card at participating merchants and share merchant name, location, date and amount; Rakuten provides card information to the networks for that purpose; transaction data may go to offer networks, vendors and participating merchants, and into performance reports; after a card is removed, past transaction data is kept. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-terms-conditions-115013738007
- A newly enrolled card may have a lag of about 24 hours before a merchant's processor can report its transactions. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-terms-conditions-115013738007
- Common reasons in-store cash back is not earned: offer not added before the purchase; card not linked; debit card not run as credit. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-faq-15935028227987
- Whether an authorized user's card or a joint card can be linked, and whether one card can be linked to two Rakuten accounts: UNCONFIRMED (not stated on the pages read). https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/linking-a-card-for-instore-and-dining-cash-back-360002100528

## 3. How long until a purchase shows up

- Processing "typically takes 24–48 hours"; the transaction then appears in the activity "within a day or two". https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/tracking-your-cash-back-360002117107
- DISAGREE (minor): a second Rakuten page says it takes "from a few hours to several days", depending on the store, for the store to notify Rakuten; Rakuten then adds the cash back and notifies the member. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-can-i-tell-if-im-earning-cash-back-360002100668
- In-store: pending "within a few days". https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-faq-15935028227987
- Dining: if rewards have not registered within 10 days of the dine, the terms send the member to rakuten.com/help/missing-cash-back. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-terms-conditions-115013738007
- Travel: a pending amount may not show until travel is complete; Expedia shows pending within 5 days of the reservation. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-cash-back-on-travel-works-360002117527
- Store pages give their own posting times (from earlier fact sheets): Booking.com, Priceline and IHG "within 3 months after travel"; Expedia within a week after travel; TaylorMade and Rock Bottom Golf the next day. https://www.rakuten.com/booking.com and https://www.rakuten.com/priceline.com
- The shopping trip itself is recorded when you click; the help pages do not give a time for a trip to appear in the account. UNCONFIRMED. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/what-is-a-rakuten-shopping-trip-360002100588
- Deadline re-read today, Terms 10.4: the member "must contact Rakuten Member Services within ninety (90) days of the transaction" if rewards were not correctly credited. It is the member's responsibility to check the account regularly. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/terms-conditions
- How soon after an order the Missing Cash Back form accepts a claim: UNCONFIRMED (the form sits behind sign-in; the public address shows only the help center). https://www.rakuten.com/help/missing-cash-back
- The help article on missing cash back gives no number of days to wait; it says to fill out the form after checking its list of causes. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/why-didnt-i-earn-cash-back-360036367393
