## 6. Expiry and closing a card

- Chase: points "do not expire" while the account is open; "Inactivity won't cause your Chase points to disappear from your account."; if you close the card before redeeming or transferring "you'll no longer have those points"; if Chase closes the account for missed payments, misuse or other agreement reasons you "will forfeit your points"; points can be moved to another open Chase card first. No 30-day window is mentioned. https://chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/rewards-benefits/do-credit-card-points-expire
- AwardWallet (2026-02-15): 30 days to use or transfer Chase points after closing; all points lost if the minimum payment is 60 days late; points can go to another of your cards, a household member's card, or a business co-owner's. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/do-chase-points-expire/
- TPG (2024-02-16): Chase points forfeited at closure, at least 30 days to redeem if in good standing; can move to a spouse or domestic partner. Amex points forfeited at once on canceling the only Membership Rewards card; 30 days if another Amex card that does not earn Membership Rewards stays open. https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/debunking-credit-card-myths-do-you-keep-your-points-and-miles-when-you-cancel-a-credit-card/
- DISAGREE: grace period after closing a Chase card. Chase's guide states none; AwardWallet and TPG say 30 days. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/do-chase-points-expire/
- Amex points do not expire from inactivity; 30 days to use points after canceling the earning card; late payment may forfeit that billing period's points, $35 to reinstate after paying; other triggers: returns, program abuse, negative balance. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-membership-rewards/do-amex-points-expire/
- Amex change (article 2026-10-15): a newly opened US card gets its own rewards account ("When you open a new Product, a new Rewards Account will be created and linked to that Product."); balances combine only once linked through Manage Connections or by phone; a rewards account's balance is forfeited when its last linked product is canceled; New York billing address: 90 days; last linked Corporate Card: up to 30 days. https://viewfromthewing.com/?p=258466
- Same change, second source (2026-10-15); applies to new cards, not existing ones; the article does not state what happens on cancellation. https://onemileatatime.com/news/amex-stops-automatically-pooling-membership-rewards-points-new-cards/
- DISAGREE: grace period after closing an Amex card. AwardWallet 30 days; TPG none for the last Membership Rewards card; View from the Wing forfeiture on the last linked card with the New York and Corporate exceptions. UNCONFIRMED on Amex's terms (PDF unreadable). https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/rewards/membership-rewards/mr-terms-conditions-september-2026.pdf
