# Daily fact sheet 2026-11-21: Amex Platinum travel insurance and shopping protections

Checked 2026-11-21. Access notes: americanexpress.com HTML policy pages failed to load ("header overflow"). Six Amex benefit-guide PDFs were fetched and their text extracted locally. None of the Amex guides names the cards it applies to ("Eligible Card"); the match to the Platinum Card rests on the third-party pages below. nerdwallet.com pages loaded without article text.

## Trip delay

- Amex Trip Delay guide ($500 / 6 hours version): reimburses "reasonable additional expenses (including but not limited to meals, lodging, toiletries, medication, and other personal use items)" when a Covered Trip is delayed more than 6 hours by a Covered Loss. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/TD500_846bff_a11y.pdf
- Same guide: limits are $500 per Covered Trip and 2 claims per Eligible Card per 12 consecutive months; secondary to and in excess of other insurance and carrier benefits. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/TD500_846bff_a11y.pdf
- Same guide: four covered causes: inclement weather; terrorist action or hijacking; a common carrier's equipment failure as documented by the carrier; lost or stolen passports or travel documents. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/TD500_846bff_a11y.pdf
- Same guide: Covered Trip is round-trip travel by common carrier, up to 365 days, ending back at the city of departure; may be made of round-trip and one-way tickets; full fare charged to the card, or the card combined with its points or frequent flyer awards, vouchers or certificates. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/TD500_846bff_a11y.pdf
- Same guide: Eligible Traveler is the card member, Family Members (spouse, domestic partner, unmarried dependent child up to 19, or under 26 if a full-time student) and Traveling Companions who purchase a Covered Trip to the card. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/TD500_846bff_a11y.pdf
- Same guide: not covered: losses made public or known before departure, expenses paid before the trip, intentional acts. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/TD500_846bff_a11y.pdf
- Same guide: notify within 60 days of the loss at 1-844-933-0648 or travelguard.claimnotify.com/americanexpress; proof of loss within 180 days; underwriter New Hampshire Insurance Company, an AIG Company. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/TD500_846bff_a11y.pdf
- Platinum Card has the $500 / more than 6 hours level, annual fee $895 (page updated 2026-11-11). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/travel-insurance-benefits/
- Second source for Platinum: $500 per trip, 6 hours, causes weather, equipment failure, lost or stolen travel documents; two claims per 12 months; fee $895. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-membership-rewards/amex-platinum/
- DISAGREE: One Mile at a Time (modified 2026-01-22) says delays for any reason qualify; the guide lists four causes. It also says miles tickets qualify if taxes and fees are on the card, and that its author's claim took about six weeks. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-trip-delay-insurance/
- DISAGREE (wording): Upgraded Points says "two per covered trip" in one place and two per card per 12 months in another; the guide says 2 per card per 12 months. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/travel-insurance-benefits/

## Trip cancellation and interruption

- Amex guide (10.09.23): to get coverage, charge the full amount to the card or the card combined with its points or frequent flyer awards; same Covered Trip, Family Member and Traveling Companion definitions as trip delay. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/Trip_Cancellation_and_Interruption_Insurance_10.09.23.-final.pdf
- Same guide: covered reasons also include change in military orders of the traveler or spouse, terrorist action or hijacking, a subpoena that cannot be postponed, and sickness or injury of a traveling companion or a family member of either. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/Trip_Cancellation_and_Interruption_Insurance_10.09.23.-final.pdf
- Same guide: interruption also pays extra transport to rejoin the trip or return home, capped at an economy fare by the most direct route. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/Trip_Cancellation_and_Interruption_Insurance_10.09.23.-final.pdf
- Same guide: Pre-Existing Condition lookback is 60 days before the purchase date; stable conditions on maintenance medication are not counted. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/Trip_Cancellation_and_Interruption_Insurance_10.09.23.-final.pdf
- Same guide: exclusions include pre-existing conditions, one-way travel with no return destination, financial insolvency of a travel agency, tour operator or travel supplier, war, mental or emotional disorders unless hospitalized, paid sporting activity, intoxication, illegal acts. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/Trip_Cancellation_and_Interruption_Insurance_10.09.23.-final.pdf
- Same guide: notify within 60 days at 1-844-933-0648; proof of loss within 180 days; papers include tickets, card statement, proof of the cause, the supplier's cancellation policy. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/Trip_Cancellation_and_Interruption_Insurance_10.09.23.-final.pdf
- Platinum Card: $10,000 per trip, $20,000 per card per 12 months (updated 2026-11-11). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/travel-insurance-benefits/
- DISAGREE: NerdWallet (search summary) calls Membership Rewards bookings an exception for trip insurance; the Amex guide allows the card combined with "accumulated points on your Eligible Card". NerdWallet's page text did not load. https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/travel/amex-platinum-travel-insurance-guide

## Baggage, medical, hotline

- DISAGREE: Upgraded Points' table shows carry-on at $2,000; the Amex guide in trips.md and AwardWallet show $3,000 carry-on with a $3,000 combined cap. Upgraded Points agrees on $2,000 checked, $3,000 combined, $1,000 high-value items, and New York limits of $2,000 per bag and $10,000 per trip. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/travel-insurance-benefits/
- Delayed baggage is not covered on the Platinum Card; baggage claims within 30 days at 800-228-6855; underwriter AMEX Assurance Company. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/travel-insurance-benefits/
- Emergency medical treatment is not covered; the Premium Global Assist Hotline may provide emergency medical transport at no cost when it approves and coordinates it, on trips more than 100 miles from home. One source for the standard Platinum Card: UNCONFIRMED against Amex's own terms. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/travel-insurance-benefits/
- TPG describes Premium Global Assist the same way but lists it for the Schwab, Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley Platinum cards (article dated 2025-12-24). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/american-express-cards-travel-protections/

## Purchase protection

- Amex Purchase Protection guide 316-404 (Rev. 4/19/2024): up to $10,000 per Covered Purchase, $50,000 per Eligible Card per calendar year, $500 per natural disaster event, up to 90 days from purchase; a covered event is "damage to, losing or theft of" the item; gifts and business use count. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/PP-Benefit-Guide_316-404_EDT_10.20_REV_4.24_a11y.pdf
- Same guide: Membership Rewards / Pay with Points purchases qualify; other loyalty programs do not; pays replacement cost (repair, replace or the amount charged, whichever is less); shipping, handling and installation excluded; pays only amounts not covered by other insurance or agreement. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/PP-Benefit-Guide_316-404_EDT_10.20_REV_4.24_a11y.pdf
- Same guide: exclusions include items in the care of a third party including a common carrier, items not reasonably safeguarded, wear and defects, damage to sports and recreational equipment (drones excepted), used and one-of-a-kind items, consumables, gift cards and tickets, rented items, motorized devices, software, items for resale, medical or dental equipment, items for professional or commercial use. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/PP-Benefit-Guide_316-404_EDT_10.20_REV_4.24_a11y.pdf
- Same guide: claim at americanexpress.com/onlineclaim or 1-800-228-6855; notice within 30 days of the loss; proof of loss within 60 days; theft needs a report filed before the claim; payable claims paid within 30 days of proof; underwriter AMEX Assurance Company. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/PP-Benefit-Guide_316-404_EDT_10.20_REV_4.24_a11y.pdf
- A second Amex guide, 316-402, is the lower level: $1,000 per purchase, same $50,000 and $500, damage or theft only, lost items excluded. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/PP-Benefit-Guide_316-402_EDT_10.20_REV_4.24_a11y.pdf
- Platinum Card is at the $10,000 / $50,000 / $500 level, loss included; can pay another policy's deductible (updated 2026-11-11). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/purchase-protection/
- Second source: $10,000 per incident, $50,000 per year, $500 natural disaster for the consumer Platinum Card (article dated 2024-12-25). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/amex-purchase-protection/
- DISAGREE: TPG's shopping-protections guide (2025-12-25) lists the "American Express Platinum Credit Card" at $1,000 per occurrence and the Schwab, Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley Platinum cards at $10,000. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/american-express-shopping-protections
- DISAGREE inside one source: AwardWallet's main text says $10,000 per incident; a disclosure on the same page says $1,000 per occurrence. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-membership-rewards/amex-platinum/

## Return protection

- Amex Return Protection program description (Rev 10-20, file dated 10.09.23): 90 days from purchase; up to $300 per item excluding shipping and handling; $1,000 per Card Member account per calendar year by purchase date; purchases in the 50 states, DC and US territories, charged in full to the card; item in original condition and working; merchant must have refused the return. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/RP_Benefit_Guide_Rev_10-20_10.09.23-final.pdf
- Same document: not eligible if the merchant's own return program is equal or better and covers the claim; 28 excluded categories including jewelry, watches, books, software, tickets, formal wear, seasonal items, used or custom items, personal hygiene items, medical equipment, perishables, firearms, gift cards, motorized vehicles. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/RP_Benefit_Guide_Rev_10-20_10.09.23-final.pdf
- Same document: call 1-800-228-6855 within 90 days of purchase; receipt and card record of charge due within 30 days of the call; Amex may ask for the item within 30 days and repays that shipping cost. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/RP_Benefit_Guide_Rev_10-20_10.09.23-final.pdf
- Platinum Card has return protection at $300 / $1,000 / 90 days (updated 2026-11-11). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/return-protection/
- DISAGREE: TPG's shopping-protections guide lists return protection and extended warranty only for the Schwab, Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley Platinum cards, not the standard card. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/american-express-shopping-protections
- AwardWallet's Platinum review does not mention return protection. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-membership-rewards/amex-platinum/

## Extended warranty

- Amex Extended Warranty guide 314-400 (Rev. 4/19/2024, purchases on or after 10/21/2021): up to 1 additional year on original manufacturer's warranties of 5 years or less; up to $10,000 per Covered Purchase and $50,000 per Eligible Card per calendar year; warranty must be valid in the US or its territories. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/EW-Benefit-Guide_314-400_EDT_10.21_REV_4.24_a11y.pdf
- Same guide: cover starts after the longer of the manufacturer's warranty or a purchased service contract ends; pays replacement cost; excludes product recalls, repairs at facilities the warranty does not accept, motorized devices, software, used items; notice within 30 days of the loss, proof within 60 days. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/EW-Benefit-Guide_314-400_EDT_10.21_REV_4.24_a11y.pdf
- Platinum Card: one extra year, warranties of five years or less, $10,000 per item, $50,000 per year, fee $895. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-membership-rewards/amex-platinum/
