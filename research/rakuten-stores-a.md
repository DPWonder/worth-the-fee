## 9. General

- Reasons a purchase earns nothing (re-read today): excluded items or categories, a different category rate, codes not listed on Rakuten, ad blockers/VPNs, other reward or coupon sites, items in the cart before activation, most gift cards and some purchases paid with them, taxes and shipping, phone/chat orders, returns and cancellations, idle sessions or tab switching, declined cookies. Retailer apps and store pickup are not mentioned. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/why-didnt-i-earn-cash-back-360036367393
- A purchase earns only if a Shopping Trip starts from a Rakuten link on the site, in the Rakuten app or with the Cash Back Button; a later second purchase needs a new trip. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/what-is-a-rakuten-shopping-trip-360002100588 (search)
