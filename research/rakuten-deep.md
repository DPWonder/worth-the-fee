## 1. Who Rakuten is and how it makes money

- "Stores pay Rakuten a commission for referring shoppers to them." Rakuten keeps part and shares the rest with the member as cash back. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-does-rakuten-work-360002117047
- Rakuten describes itself as a free shopping platform; sign-up is free. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-does-rakuten-work-360002117047
- Cash back at 3,500+ stores; "Since 1999" members have earned more than $4.6 billion in cash back. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-does-rakuten-work-360002117047
- Terms 6.1: Rakuten generally receives compensation from brands and other commercial partners, and that compensation may play a part in whether retailers and products appear on the site. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/terms-conditions
- Legal entity: Ebates Performance Marketing, Inc. dba Rakuten Rewards, 800 Concar Drive, Suite 175, San Mateo, CA 94402. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/terms-conditions
- Founded as Ebates in Menlo Park, California; bought by Rakuten in September 2014 for US$1 billion; Ebates name phased out in 2019. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rakuten_Rewards
- DISAGREE (minor): founding year. Wikipedia's infobox says 1998 and its history section says the site launched 3 May 1999; Rakuten says "Since 1999". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rakuten_Rewards and https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-does-rakuten-work-360002117047
- Rakuten's own history post: Ebates partnered with Rakuten in 2014; Rakuten began in Japan in 1997 as an e-commerce site; post dated 2019-03-06 says the Rakuten logo would replace Ebates "this year". https://www.rakuten.com/blog/history-of-rakuten/
- Parent: Rakuten Group, Inc., Tokyo, founded 1997-02-07, listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange (ticker 4755; First Section since 2013-12-03). https://global.rakuten.com/corp/about/ (search) and https://global.rakuten.com/corp/investors/faqs (search)
- BBB: Rakuten's careers page displays an A+ BBB rating. UNCONFIRMED on bbb.org (not read). https://rakuten.com/careers (search)
- Terms 10.4: Rakuten may deduct rewards for returns and cancellations, and may cancel rewards for a transaction if the store that funds them does not pay Rakuten. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/terms-conditions
- Terms 11.2: an account is active if you shopped or started a shopping trip in the past 12 months. After 12 months of inactivity Rakuten may charge a maintenance fee of $5.00 a month, taken from the balance; it cannot make the balance negative. Rakuten may close an account inactive for more than 36 months with a zero balance. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/terms-conditions

## 2. Privacy policy (updated October 23, 2026)

- Collected: name, postal and email address, phone number; IP address, device and session IDs; payment account name and number, card expiration date, billing address; searches, clicks, cart items, merchant visits, purchases, reward balances; URLs visited, click stream and timestamps. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/privacy-policy
- The browser extension and app collect data about online activity on that device even without interaction; uninstalling them is how you withdraw consent to that collection. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/privacy-policy
- Precise location only if enabled on the device. Fingerprint/face sign-in stays with the phone: "Rakuten does not receive, collect, or store your biometric data." https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/privacy-policy
- Shared with: affiliate networks (click data and a tracking ID), merchants (hashed email, order IDs, purchase data), referral, loyalty and redemption partners, financial partners, digital advertising providers, and Rakuten Group companies. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/privacy-policy
- The policy says certain disclosures "may be considered" selling, sharing or targeted advertising under US state laws. Members opt out in the Privacy Preference Center; non-members use an opt-out link; Global Privacy Control signals are honored. Rights to access, correct and delete. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/privacy-policy
- Offers and cash back rates "may be personalized based on your account history". https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/privacy-policy

## 3. Payment, minimum, timing

- Paid every three months on February 15, May 15, August 15, November 15; needs "$5 or more in confirmed Cash Back"; under that carries over. Methods: PayPal, Bilt Points, Samsung Cash, check, or Amex Membership Rewards points. Check and PayPal need a mailing address; Membership Rewards and Bilt do not. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/when-will-i-get-paid-360002117667
- DISAGREE: minimum. Help page says $5 or more; Terms 10.2 say "The minimum payout amount for Rewards is $5.01." Terms also say Rakuten may modify the payment schedule at any time. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/when-will-i-get-paid-360002117667 and https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/terms-conditions
- Gift card: a confirmed balance of at least $5 can be redeemed for a gift card that arrives within minutes, with limits. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/when-will-i-get-paid-360002117667
- Statuses: processing 24 to 48 hours; pending typically 3 to 17 weeks; then confirmed and included in the next payment. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/tracking-your-cash-back-360002117107
- Online and in-store: typically 3 to 14 weeks to confirm, to allow for returns, exchanges, exclusions or other order changes. Dining: 3 to 5 days. Travel: typically 6 to 17 weeks after the trip is complete; Expedia within one week after the trip. https://rakuten.com/help/article/how-long-does-it-take-for-pending-cash-back-to-be-confirmed-34494180297875
- DISAGREE: travel confirmation. The "When will I get paid" page says travel is typically confirmed 45 to 120 days after booking; the travel article and the confirmation article say 6 to 17 weeks after the trip is complete. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/when-will-i-get-paid-360002117667 and https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-cash-back-on-travel-works-360002117527
- How long a check or PayPal payment takes to arrive after the payment date: UNCONFIRMED (not on the page). https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/when-will-i-get-paid-360002117667

## 6. Missing cash back

- Claim window: Terms 10.4 say you "must contact Rakuten Member Services within ninety (90) days of the transaction" about missing rewards. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/terms-conditions
- DISAGREE: a third-party site says 45 days. Rakuten's help articles give no deadline; its Terms say 90. https://joingerald.com/learn/financial-wellness/rakuten-missing-cash-back (search)
- Two cases: a missing shopping trip (Rakuten has no record of the click) versus missing cash back (the trip was recorded but shows $0 or ineligible). Form: rakuten.com/help/missing-cash-back. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/why-didnt-i-earn-cash-back-360036367393
- Each store's exclusions are behind "See all Terms & Exclusions" on the activation page. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/why-didnt-i-earn-cash-back-360036367393
- What the form asks for, how long a review takes, and whether some stores take no claims: UNCONFIRMED (form sits behind sign-in). https://www.rakuten.com/help/missing-cash-back
- Rakuten may ask for a receipt or other documents to verify a transaction. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-terms-conditions-115013738007

## 7. In-store cash back

- Steps: link an eligible card; add the store's offer on the site or in the app (In-Store tab) before shopping; pay in the store with the linked card. Tracked automatically; no receipt upload; keep the receipt in case of a problem. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-faq-15935028227987
- Cards: most Visa credit and debit, Mastercard credit and debit, and American Express cards. More than one card can be linked. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-faq-15935028227987
- Not eligible: Wells Fargo Mastercards, American Express debit cards, virtual cards with a new number per transaction (such as Apple Card), Discover, Macy's American Express, corporate and purchasing cards, HSA and FSA cards, government prepaid cards including EBT, insurance prepaid cards, Visa Buxx, Diners Club. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-faq-15935028227987
- Debit: run as credit and sign if asked; "Debit transactions processed as PIN may not qualify." https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-faq-15935028227987
- Offers: most expire 75 days after being added or once used; re-add after each purchase or expiry. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-faq-15935028227987
- Timing: pending usually within a few days; confirmed typically in 3 to 14 weeks. Online and phone orders do not count. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-faq-15935028227987
- The Rakuten American Express Card earns an extra 4% on in-store purchases. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-faq-15935028227987
- DISAGREE: mobile wallets. The FAQ recommends the physical card and says Apple Pay, Google Wallet and Samsung Pay "may not always track properly". The terms say Apple Pay and Samsung Wallet are eligible and other third-party wallets are not. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-faq-15935028227987 and https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-terms-conditions-115013738007
- DISAGREE (minor): debit. The /in-store page FAQ lists American Express, Mastercard and Visa credit cards only; the help FAQ adds Visa and Mastercard debit. https://www.rakuten.com/in-store
- Terms: by enrolling you agree the card network or processor may monitor transactions on the enrolled card at participating merchants and share merchant name, location, date and amount. Matching is run by Rakuten Card Linked Offer Network, Inc. A newly linked card can take about 24 hours to start reporting. Remove a card any time in account settings; past data is kept. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-terms-conditions-115013738007
- In-store rates seen 2026-11-20: Mattress Firm 10% (was 2%); Carhartt 10% (was 3%); ASICS 10% (was 3%); CVS 2%; Finish Line 2%; Cole Haan 2%; UGG 2%; Famous Footwear 2%; At Home 1%; Sportsman's Warehouse 2%. Some merchants do not pay on gift-card purchases; physical stores only. https://www.rakuten.com/in-store
- Whether an in-store offer can be combined with an online click-through on one purchase: UNCONFIRMED (in-store offers are for purchases in physical stores; online orders do not count). https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/in-store-cash-back-faq-15935028227987

## 8. What an Amex point is worth, by use

- Transfers to 20 airline and hotel programs: "possibly 2 cents per point", can be more or less; TPG's April 2026 valuation is 2 cents. https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/amex-points-value/
- Amex Travel flights: 1 cent. Most Amex Travel hotels, cars and vacation rentals: about 0.7 cents. Fine Hotels + Resorts: 1 cent (Platinum). Cruises 0.5 to 0.7 cents. https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/amex-points-value/
- Gift cards: 0.5 to 1 cent depending on merchant. Amazon, Grubhub, Best Buy at checkout: 0.7 cents. Covering card charges: 0.6 cents. https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/amex-points-value/
- Amex's own guide gives the same 0.6 cents for covering charges, up to 1 cent for flights and gift cards, 0.7 cents for prepaid hotels. Seen in a search summary only; the page would not load. https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/credit-cards/credit-intel/american-express-points-value/ (search)
- Business Platinum: 35% of points back on eligible airfare booked with points through Amex Travel, up to 1 million points a calendar year. By arithmetic that is about 1.54 cents per point (1 / 0.65). https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/amex-points-value/
- Schwab Platinum "Invest with Rewards": 1.1 cents per point on the first 1 million points a year, then 0.8 cents (page updated 2026-10-22). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card-for-schwab/
- DISAGREE: value of a point through transfers. One Mile at a Time 1.7 cents (in chase-rakuten.md); TPG 2 cents (April 2026); Upgraded Points about 2.2 cents (search). https://upgradedpoints.com/news/amex-schwab-platinum-card-invest-with-rewards-devaluation/ (search)
