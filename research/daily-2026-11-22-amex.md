## Foreign use
- Platinum Card agreement, fees table: "Foreign Transaction: None." https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- Foreign charges are converted to US dollars by AE Exposure Management Ltd. on the processing date, at a rate no more than the highest official government rate or the highest interbank rate on the conversion date or the prior business day. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- Cash advance fee: 5% of an ATM cash advance (including any ATM operator fee) or other cash advance, minimum $10; added to the Cash Advance balance. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- Cash advance balance may not exceed $8,000; there may also be limits on the amount and number of ATM withdrawals in a period. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- Interest on cash advances runs from the transaction date; it cannot be avoided. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- A cash advance includes travelers cheques, foreign currency, money orders, digital currency and gaming chips. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- NOTE: this copy of the agreement (dated 09-18-2025) shows an Annual Fee of $695 in its fees table as extracted, not $895; do not cite it for the $895 figure. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- Upgraded Points (updated 2026-11-11): no foreign transaction fee; annual fee $895; typical foreign fee on other cards 3% (citing Amex); additional Platinum Cards have the same no-fee terms. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/international-fees/
- Upgraded Points, citing Amex: accepted at more than 170 million locations worldwide; Amex lacks the international footprint of Visa or Mastercard; not accepted in Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, Crimea, and the Donetsk and Luhansk regions of Ukraine; suggests a Visa or Mastercard backup. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/international-fees/

- Fine Hotels + Resorts has over 1,900 hotels; The Hotel Collection over 1,400. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/international-fees/
- The Points Guy (2021, older): Amex accepted in more than 100 countries; easier in Australia and Canada; varies by merchant in Europe and Asia; suggests carrying a Visa or Mastercard. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/amex-now-accepted-in-as-many-places-as-visa-mastercard/

## Annual fee timing and refund
- Agreement, closing your account: Amex will refund the Annual Fee if you notify it you are voluntarily closing the Account "within 30 days after the Closing Date of the billing statement on which that fee appears." "For cancellations after this 30-day period, the Annual Fee is non-refundable." https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- The policy does not apply if the billing address is in Massachusetts at closing; what applies instead is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- You may close the Account by calling or writing to Amex. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- Additional cards: each Additional Platinum Card $195 a year, each Companion Platinum Card $0; the same refund policy applies to an additional card's fee when that card is canceled. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- UNCONFIRMED: when the $195 additional card fee posts (the agreement does not say; The Points Guy's authorized user article does not say). https://thepointsguy.com/guide/why-add-authorized-users-amex-platinum/
- Amex annual fee timing: year 1 on the statement closing date after approval; later years on every twelfth statement from the first (13th, 25th); can drift by a day or so. One source; not on a US Amex page. https://www.doctorofcredit.com/issuers-charge-annual-fees-guide/
- Doctor of Credit (2020): full refund if closed within 30 days after the statement on which the fee posts; no prorated refund on closing since September 2016; product changes still get a prorated refund outside the window. https://www.doctorofcredit.com/annual-fee-refund-rules-for-each-card-issuer/
- DISAGREE (wording): One Mile at a Time (2026-09-22) says cancel or downgrade "within 30 days of the annual fee being billed"; no prorated refund on closing after that; a downgrade can often get a prorated refund. https://onemileatatime.com/?p=325172
- The Points Guy (2020): 30-day period after the statement in which the fee posts; cancel or downgrade inside it and the fee is refunded; a product change outside it is prorated. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/keep-new-amex-account-open-at-least-one-year/
- First-year wording quoted from Amex offer terms: "if you cancel or downgrade your account within 12 months after acquiring it", Amex may take away points and may cancel this and other card accounts. Quoted in 2020; said to be on nearly every Amex card. Presence in the current (Nov 2026) Platinum offer terms UNCONFIRMED. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/keep-new-amex-account-open-at-least-one-year/
- Same wording quoted from Delta Gold Amex terms in 2017. https://onemileatatime.com/amex-adds-strong-language-to-counter-bonus-abuse/
