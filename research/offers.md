## 1. Amex Platinum: public offer, referral offer, how to check

## 2. Amex welcome offer: what counts toward the spending requirement

- Amex offer terms as quoted by a January 2026 guide: eligible purchases do not include fees or interest charges, purchases of traveler's checks, purchases or reloading of prepaid cards, purchases of gift cards, person-to-person payments, or purchases of other cash equivalents. https://www.mypointslife.com/amex-welcome-offer-eligible-purchases/
- UNCONFIRMED on an Amex page: the application terms would not load. A 2022 Amex PDF (non-US path) carries similar wording (search). https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-idc/benefits/2022-Offers/Platinum/Plat_FF_2022.pdf (search)
- Also listed as not counting by that guide: the annual fee, cash advances, balance transfers, late fees, Amex Send & Split payments. https://www.mypointslife.com/amex-welcome-offer-eligible-purchases/
- Upgraded Points (updated 2026-04-13): annual fees, balance transfers, gift cards, prepaid gift cards, person-to-person payments and cash-like transactions such as traveler's checks do not count; a welcome offer tracker shows progress and deadline online (above the Rewards section) and in the app (Membership Rewards tab), can lag a few days and may not appear for every card; Membership Rewards bonuses "tend to post within a few days" of meeting the requirement. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/track-amex-credit-card-bonus-progress/
- Annual fee does not count (another source, last modified 2025-04-04). https://onemileatatime.com/do-annual-fees-count-towards-credit-card-minimum-spend/
- Refunds: "the refunded amount is typically deducted from your progress" (TPG, updated 2026-11-18). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/refunds-hurting-sign-up-bonus
- Refunds, Amex: an Amex representative told TPG a bonus can be taken back if a refund puts you under the requirement and you do not make it up in time (search). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/refunds-hurting-sign-up-bonus (search)
- Statement credits from card benefits (such as the airline fee credit) are treated as payments, not returns, per one guide; some readers report being told otherwise. UNCONFIRMED. https://www.mypointslife.com/amex-welcome-offer-eligible-purchases/
- Tax payments through a card processor and utility bills count, per the same guide; processors charge about 1.8% to 2%. One Mile at a Time mentions paying quarterly taxes "if eligible". https://www.mypointslife.com/amex-welcome-offer-eligible-purchases/ and https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-card-bonus/
- Additional cardholders' spending counts toward the primary's offer (already in amex-deep.md). https://thepointsguy.com/guide/why-add-authorized-users-amex-platinum/
- Timing: Gold Card terms say points are credited within 8 to 12 weeks after the threshold is met, and the window runs from the date the account is opened (search); reports say Membership Rewards bonuses often post within days. The Platinum terms themselves: UNCONFIRMED. https://americanexpress.com/us/credit-cards/card-application/apply/prospect/terms/gold-card/26129-10-0 (search) and https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/how-fast-banks-issue-rewards/ (search)
- $12,000 in six months is $2,000 a month on average. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-card-bonus/

## 6. Credit score effect of an application

- FICO: one more inquiry takes "less than five points" off for most people; more for people with few accounts or a short history. https://www.myfico.com/credit-education/credit-reports/credit-checks-and-inquiries
- Hard inquiries stay on a credit report up to two years; FICO scores count only the last 12 months. https://www.myfico.com/credit-education/credit-reports/credit-checks-and-inquiries
- VantageScore: typical drop five to 10 points; can count inquiries for 24 months; on both models the effect "usually lasts just a few months" (Experian, 2025-12-12). https://www.experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/how-long-do-hard-inquiries-stay-on-your-credit-report/
- DISAGREE (minor): a credit expert quoted by CNBC Select (2025-08-05) says both FICO and VantageScore ignore inquiries after one year; Experian says VantageScore can use 24 months. https://www.cnbc.com/select/how-applying-for-new-credit-impacts-credit-score/
- Credit card inquiries are not grouped the way mortgage, auto and student loan inquiries are; each card application counts by itself. https://www.experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/how-long-do-hard-inquiries-stay-on-your-credit-report/
- FICO score weights: payment history 35%, amounts owed 30%, length of history 15%, new credit 10%, credit mix 10%. Length of history uses the average age of all accounts, so a new account lowers it. https://www.myfico.com/credit-education/whats-in-your-credit-score
- Chase: hard inquiries "typically stay on your credit report for two years"; a soft inquiry does not affect the score. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/credit-score/how-hard-soft-inquiries-affect-credit-score
- Experian (2019 article) suggests waiting six months to a year between applications. https://www.experian.com/blogs/ask-experian/is-there-a-hard-pull-on-credit-cards-if-i-apply/
- Exact point loss from the lower average account age: UNCONFIRMED (no source gives a figure).

## 7. Tax (US)

- IRS Form 1099-MISC instructions (Rev. 12/2026): box 3 is for other income of $2,000 or more; "For tax years beginning after 2025" the threshold rose from $600 to $2,000 and may be adjusted for inflation from 2027. https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099mec
- Rewards earned by spending, welcome bonuses with a spending requirement included, are generally treated as a rebate on purchases and not as income (TPG, 2026-02-06; cites a 2010 IRS memorandum for cash back). https://thepointsguy.com/guide/complete-guide-to-paying-taxes-on-credit-card-rewards
- Same view, second source (2026-02-06): a bonus tied to a spending requirement is "a rebate on spending rather than income". https://thriftytraveler.com/guides/points/are-credit-card-rewards-taxable
- Bonuses with no spending requirement (referral bonuses, bank account bonuses) are treated as taxable income; the person referred owes nothing on a welcome bonus earned by spending. Report under other income, Schedule 1 line 8z, even with no form. https://thepointsguy.com/guide/complete-guide-to-paying-taxes-on-credit-card-rewards
- TPG's guide (February 2026, written for 2025 returns) still gives $600 as the level at which to expect a 1099; the $2,000 level applies to payments in 2026, reported in early 2027. https://thepointsguy.com/guide/complete-guide-to-paying-taxes-on-credit-card-rewards and https://www.doctorofcredit.com/which-banks-send-out-tax-form-1099-discussion-post-for-2025-tax-forms/
- Amex: sends Form 1099-MISC for referral bonuses and counts a Membership Rewards point as 1 cent. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-refer-a-friend/ and https://thriftytraveler.com/guides/points/are-credit-card-rewards-taxable
- Chase: referral terms say the bonus "may be taxable income to you" (search); Chase has sent 1099s for referral points, valued at 1 cent each, to people at $600 or more (article dated 2020-02-02). https://onemileatatime.com/chase-referral-1099/
- Whether Chase and Amex will send forms only at $2,000 for 2026: UNCONFIRMED (no issuer statement read). Whether Chase sends a form under the threshold: open question in a February 2026 reader roundup. https://www.doctorofcredit.com/which-banks-send-out-tax-form-1099-discussion-post-for-2025-tax-forms/
- Anikeev v. Commissioner (US Tax Court, February 2021): rewards on money orders and prepaid debit card reloads were held taxable because those are cash equivalents; rewards on gift card purchases were not. TPG says ordinary purchases are not affected. https://thepointsguy.com/news/tax-on-rewards-ruling
- State tax treatment: not researched. UNCONFIRMED.
