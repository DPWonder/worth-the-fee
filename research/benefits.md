# Fact sheet: card benefits (batch benefits)

Checked 2026-11-20. Adds to `chase-rakuten.md`, `chase-deep.md`, `amex.md`, `amex-deep.md` and `amex-cards.md`; facts already confirmed there are not repeated unless a line adds detail. Every line ends with the page it came from. "(search)" means the fact was seen in a search-result summary of that page, not in a full read. UNCONFIRMED means no source settled it. DISAGREE means sources conflict.

## 1. Sapphire Preferred rental car cover

- Chase's own guide: primary cover for theft and damage, valid loss-of-use charges, administrative fees and reasonable towing; reimbursement is capped at the vehicle's actual cash value. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/chase-sapphire-rental-car-insurance-guide
- The cardmember must be the primary driver on the rental agreement; charge the full rental to the card (Chase points may be used toward it); decline the rental company's collision damage waiver, because accepting it cancels the benefit. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/chase-sapphire-rental-car-insurance-guide
- Rentals must be fewer than 31 consecutive days. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/chase-sapphire-rental-car-insurance-guide
- Exclusions on Chase's guide: expensive and exotic vehicles, vehicles more than 10 years old, motorcycles, electric scooters, mopeds, vans with more than nine passengers, rented moving trucks and vans, peer-to-peer rentals, hourly rentals, valuables left in the car, off-road driving. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/chase-sapphire-rental-car-insurance-guide
- Claim papers named by Chase: rental agreement, card statement showing the rental paid in full, police report for vandalism or carjacking, repair estimate, accident report form, photographs. No deadline is on that page. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/chase-sapphire-rental-car-insurance-guide
- DISAGREE on van size: Chase's guide says vans with more than nine passengers are excluded; Upgraded Points says passenger vans seating more than 12 (already in `chase-deep.md`). https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/chase-sapphire-rental-car-insurance-guide
- DISAGREE on countries: Chase's guide says cover applies in the United States and some countries abroad and tells travelers to confirm the destination before leaving; third-party guides say most countries, and one reports an administrator saying all countries. https://www.cnbc.com/select/chase-sapphire-rental-car-insurance/ (search)
- High-value examples named as excluded in one guide: Corvette, Ferrari, Jaguar, Porsche. https://www.cnbc.com/select/chase-sapphire-rental-car-insurance/ (search)
- Renting through Chase Travel with points keeps the card's rental car cover. https://awardwallet.com/blog/how-to-use-ultimate-rewards-points-for-a-disney-world-resort
- UNCONFIRMED: the claim deadlines for the rental benefit specifically (the 20-day and 90-day figures in `chase-deep.md` are from a general insurance guide); NerdWallet's rental guide returned no article text.

## 2. Sapphire Preferred abroad

- No foreign transaction fee on purchases outside the US (article checked 2026-09-29). https://wise.com/us/blog/chase-sapphire-foreign-transaction-fee
- Dynamic currency conversion: when a terminal offers to charge in dollars, the merchant sets the rate and it can be inflated; choosing the local currency is typically the lower-cost choice. https://wise.com/us/blog/chase-sapphire-foreign-transaction-fee
- Cash advance at an ATM: $10 or 5% of each transaction, whichever is greater, plus any ATM operator fee. Buying foreign currency or traveler's checks is cash-like and earns no points. https://wise.com/us/blog/chase-sapphire-foreign-transaction-fee
- The card is a Visa Signature card; no travel notice to Chase is needed; no airport lounge access (article updated 2026-11-06). https://financebuzz.com/using-chase-sapphire-preferred-internationally
- Exchange rate: Visa converts the purchase to dollars before it reaches Chase, using a wholesale market rate or a government-mandated rate. https://financebuzz.com/using-chase-sapphire-preferred-internationally
- Luggage sub-limits: up to $500 for jewelry and watches and up to $500 for cameras and electronics inside the $3,000 lost luggage limit. Travel and emergency assistance is a referral service; the cardholder pays for services used. https://financebuzz.com/using-chase-sapphire-preferred-internationally
- UNCONFIRMED: whether the card supports chip-and-PIN at unattended machines. Search summaries describe it as chip-and-signature; no source read settles it. https://financebuzz.com/using-chase-sapphire-preferred-internationally
- UNCONFIRMED: no source read states in so many words that 3x dining applies at restaurants outside the US on the Preferred; Chase's summary names no US-only limit. https://financebuzz.com/using-chase-sapphire-preferred-internationally

## 3. Sapphire Preferred and a Disney trip

- Chase Travel does not sell tickets to the US Disney theme parks, but does list them for overseas parks (updated 2026-06-29). https://upgradedpoints.com/news/chase-sapphire-preferred-card-theme-park-tickets
- DISAGREE: AwardWallet (2025-04-06) says Chase Travel sometimes sells Walt Disney World and Disneyland admission, usually at higher prices than Disney or third-party sellers. https://awardwallet.com/blog/how-to-use-ultimate-rewards-points-for-a-disney-world-resort
- Most theme park tickets code as entertainment, not travel; activities bought through Chase Travel earn 5x. https://upgradedpoints.com/news/chase-sapphire-preferred-card-theme-park-tickets
- Hotels and hotel-and-ticket packages booked directly with Disney or an authorized Disney travel planner code as travel; ticket-only purchases from Disney do not (article dated 2026-02-23, written about the Sapphire Reserve). https://thepointsguy.com/guide/best-credit-cards-disney
- On the Preferred: 3x on food at the US parks and 2x on Disney packages that include lodging and tickets. https://awardwallet.com/blog/how-to-use-ultimate-rewards-points-for-a-disney-world-resort
- The Swan, Swan Reserve and Dolphin hotels at Walt Disney World can be booked with points in Chase Travel; Disney's own resorts appear only occasionally. https://awardwallet.com/blog/how-to-use-ultimate-rewards-points-for-a-disney-world-resort
- Second source: several Disney World and Disneyland hotels are listed in Chase Travel, including the Walt Disney World Swan Reserve. https://thepointsguy.com/guide/best-credit-cards-disney
- A Chase Travel hotel booking earns no Marriott stay credit or elite perks. Marriott does not waive the resort fee on award nights at the Swan; one April 2025 example was 66,000 Marriott points plus a $56 fee. https://awardwallet.com/blog/how-to-use-ultimate-rewards-points-for-a-disney-world-resort
- Hyatt near Walt Disney World: Hyatt Regency Grand Cypress and Hyatt Place Lake Buena Vista. Near Disneyland: Hyatt House, Hyatt Place and Hyatt Regency Orange County. Hyatt waives resort fees on award stays. https://awardwallet.com/blog/how-to-use-ultimate-rewards-points-for-a-disney-world-resort
- Hyatt Regency Grand Cypress cost 12,000, 15,000 or 18,000 Hyatt points a night in April 2025. UNCONFIRMED: its price under the May 2026 five-level chart. https://awardwallet.com/blog/how-to-use-ultimate-rewards-points-for-a-disney-world-resort
- Marriott's Swan and Dolphin: "as few as 40,000" points a night in one place and "around 50,000" in another, inside the same article (updated 2026-11-18); Swan Reserve from about 60,000. DISAGREE within the source. https://upgradedpoints.com/travel/disney-world-for-cheap-with-points/
- Chase points cash out at 1 cent each. https://awardwallet.com/blog/how-to-use-ultimate-rewards-points-for-a-disney-world-resort
- UNCONFIRMED: current Disney ticket prices (none read); how ticket resellers code on the Preferred.

## 4. Sapphire Preferred as a first travel card

- Chase publishes no minimum credit score. TPG recommends a FICO score of at least 700 (article dated 2026-07-11). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/credit-score-needed-for-chase-sapphire-preferred/
- TPG: very limited credit history makes approval harder; build credit with a starter card first if new to credit; issuers often look for an average account age over one year; keeping balances below about 20% of limits can help. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/credit-score-needed-for-chase-sapphire-preferred/
- TPG: if denied, read the notice and call Chase's reconsideration line; success is not guaranteed. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/credit-score-needed-for-chase-sapphire-preferred/
- Upgraded Points (updated 2025-07-16): wait for a score of at least 670; people have been approved in the 600s and denied in the 800s; minimum credit limit for new cardholders is $5,000; it suggests income of at least $30,000 and at least 2 years of good credit history. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/chase-sapphire-preferred-card/credit-score-requirements
- DISAGREE on the score: 670 (Upgraded Points), 690 (NerdWallet, search), 700 (TPG). https://www.nerdwallet.com/travel/learn/things-you-should-know-before-getting-the-chase-sapphire-preferred (search)
- UNCONFIRMED: an "average approved score of 741" seen in a search summary only. https://www.cardrates.com/advice/chase-sapphire-preferred-credit-score/ (search)

## 5. Amex Platinum: Global Entry, TSA PreCheck and CLEAR+

- Credit of up to $120, once every 4 years, for a Global Entry or TSA PreCheck application fee charged to the card; no registration; each authorized user with a Platinum card gets their own; the fee can be for someone else's application; NEXUS is not covered (updated 2026-06-25). https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-global-entry-tsa-precheck-credit/
- Global Entry includes TSA PreCheck and lasts five years; application through a Trusted Traveler Programs account, conditional approval, then an interview. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-global-entry-tsa-precheck-credit/
- Amex's terms: $120 for Global Entry or up to $85 for TSA PreCheck through an official enrollment provider; one credit per 4 years for Global Entry or per 4.5 years for TSA PreCheck; credit goes to the first program paid for; allow up to 8 weeks; NEXUS, SENTRI and Privium are not eligible. https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/benefits/travel/expedite-your-travel/ (search; the page itself would not load)
- DISAGREE (minor): One Mile at a Time says once every 4 years for either program; Amex's terms give 4.5 years for TSA PreCheck. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-global-entry-tsa-precheck-credit/
- CLEAR+ credit: up to $219 per calendar year; CLEAR+ costs $219 a year from 2026-07-01; card enrollment required; membership auto-renews; first sign-up is finished in person at a CLEAR airport location (article dated 2026-05-25 with a July price note). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/guide-to-using-the-amex-platinum-clear-credit/
- CLEAR+ family: adults 18 and over $125 each, up to three; children under 18 go with a member free; the credit can pay for someone else's membership and can be used in parts up to the cap. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/guide-to-using-the-amex-platinum-clear-credit/
- CLEAR+ discounted prices: $209 for general Delta and United members and Alaska members; $169 with an eligible Delta or United credit card; $179 for most Delta Medallion and United Premier tiers; $129 for United 1K; free for Delta Diamond and United Global Services. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/guide-to-using-the-amex-platinum-clear-credit/
- CLEAR is at more than 55 US airports; credit posts within two to four weeks per Amex, often in a day or two; CLEAR gift cards from a third party do not trigger it. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/guide-to-using-the-amex-platinum-clear-credit/
- What each does: CLEAR confirms identity by eye or fingerprint scan and takes you to the front of the ID check; PreCheck lets you keep shoes, belt and jacket on and laptops and liquids in the bag; Global Entry speeds US immigration. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/guide-to-using-the-amex-platinum-clear-credit/
- DISAGREE on the CLEAR+ amount: TPG's body text and the Amex card page (in `amex.md`) say $219; TPG's own FAQ and a search summary of an Amex travel page still say $209; older pages say $189. https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/account/get-started/platinum/travel-benefits (search; the page itself would not load)

## 6. Amex Platinum: cell phone protection

- Amex's Guide to Benefits (read directly; file dated 2026): Platinum is an eligible account. Reimbursement is the lesser of repair cost or replacement value, up to $800 per claim after a $50 deductible; two claims per eligible card account per 12-month period. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/CPP_GTB_3f1bed_a11y.pdf
- Covered: a stolen or damaged phone, including a cracked screen. Theft needs a police report filed within 48 hours. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/CPP_GTB_3f1bed_a11y.pdf
- Eligible phones: the primary line and additional lines on the wireless bill for the billing cycle before the month of the loss. Basic and additional card members count. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/CPP_GTB_3f1bed_a11y.pdf
- Start and suspension: cover starts the first day of the calendar month after the wireless bill is paid with the card; missing a month suspends it from the first day of the next month until the month after a later payment. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/CPP_GTB_3f1bed_a11y.pdf
- Excess cover: it pays only what other insurance or indemnity does not. Claim papers can include a copy of the claim to a homeowner's, renter's, auto or phone insurer. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/CPP_GTB_3f1bed_a11y.pdf
- Not covered: lost phones and mysterious disappearance; cosmetic damage; accessories other than the standard battery and antenna; phones in the care of a common carrier (mail, airline, delivery service); rented, leased, borrowed or prepaid-plan phones; phones bought for resale or commercial use; abuse, intentional acts, normal wear, flood, earthquake, product defects; replacement phones not bought from a carrier's store; taxes and delivery charges; losses under a warranty. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/CPP_GTB_3f1bed_a11y.pdf
- Claims: report within 90 days of the loss (1-833-784-1467 or the online portal); written proof within 120 days. Underwriter: New Hampshire Insurance Company, an AIG company. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/us/credit-cards/features-benefits/CPP_GTB_3f1bed_a11y.pdf
- Second source for limits, exclusions and deadlines (updated 2026-10-22); also says the phone bill generally earns 1 point per dollar on premium Amex cards. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/amex-cell-phone-protection/
- UNCONFIRMED: the guide's effective date (the file's dates conflict).

## 7. Amex Platinum: Marriott Gold and Hilton Gold

- Enrollment: in the Amex account's benefits section with the hotel loyalty number; status posts in about 72 hours (One Mile at a Time) or 3 to 5 business days (Upgraded Points); lasts while the card is open; authorized users with a Platinum card can enroll; does not count toward higher tiers (updated 2026-11-11). https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-hotel-elite-status/
- Hilton Gold: 80% bonus points; daily food and beverage credit at select US brands, continental breakfast at select brands outside the US for the member and one guest; space-available upgrades up to Executive Floor rooms; fifth night free on reward stays of five nights or more (article dated 2026-07-21). https://thepointsguy.com/guide/amex-platinum-hotel-elite-status/
- Hilton Gold also: late checkout when available, bottled water, no resort fees on award stays; normally earned with 25 nights, 15 stays or $6,000 of Hilton spending in a year (updated 2026-11-11). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/hilton-honors-elite-status-benefits/
- Hilton US food and beverage credit per day, doubled for two guests: $25 at Conrad, LXR, NoMad, Waldorf Astoria; $15 (up to $18 in select markets) at Canopy, Curio, DoubleTree, Graduate, Hilton Hotels & Resorts, Signia, Tapestry, Tempo; $10 at Hilton Garden Inn. Embassy Suites, Hampton, Homewood Suites, Home2 Suites, Spark and Tru give breakfast to all guests (article dated 2026-06-12). https://awardwallet.com/hotels/hilton-honors/hilton-food-beverage-credit
- DISAGREE on Hilton Gold lounge access: One Mile at a Time lists executive lounge access subject to availability; TPG describes only upgrades up to Executive Floor room types; reader comments say lounge access is not a stated Gold benefit. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-hotel-elite-status/
- Marriott Gold Elite: 25% bonus points; 2 p.m. late checkout on request when available; enhanced room upgrade when available (some brands do not offer it); 250 or 500 welcome points per stay; enhanced internet. No source lists breakfast or lounge access for Gold. https://thepointsguy.com/guide/amex-platinum-hotel-elite-status/
- Marriott Gold normally takes 25 nights in a calendar year; each card must be registered separately (updated 2026-10-22). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/marriott-bonvoy-elite-status-benefits/
- One Mile at a Time's view: Hilton Gold is worth much more than Marriott Gold because of the breakfast or credit. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-hotel-elite-status/

## 8. Amex Platinum: downgrade, retention, cancel

- Downgrade targets: Gold ($325) or Classic Green ($150). No downgrade to a no-annual-fee card. The Green Card closed to new applicants on 2026-07-23 but is still available as a downgrade. The account must be open at least 12 months (updated 2026-10-22). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/upgrade-downgrade-process/
- Downgrading by phone or chat; no change is recorded on the credit report; ask for a retention offer first; a prorated fee refund on downgrade "may" be available. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/upgrade-downgrade-process/
- Earning the Platinum welcome offer likely makes you ineligible for the Gold welcome offer, so a downgrade to Gold gives up little; one retention example was 50,000 points for keeping the Platinum a year (article dated 2025-10-09). https://thepointsguy.com/news/amex-platinum-downgrade-amex-gold
- Fee refund: full refund if the card is closed within 30 days after the closing date of the statement showing the fee; no prorated refund after that (since September 2016); a product change still gets a prorated refund (post dated 2020-06-10). https://doctorofcredit.com/annual-fee-refund-rules-for-each-card-issuer
- Second source for the 30-day window and the prorated refund on downgrade; retention offers reported from 30,000 to 55,000 points, some with a spending condition, or statement credits; some people get none; Membership Rewards points are pooled under the primary cardholder; no-fee cards named for keeping points are the EveryDay and the Blue Business Plus (article dated 2022-04-05, flagged by the site as partly out of date). https://frequentmiler.com/dont-want-to-pay-that-695-annual-fee-heres-the-strategy-before-you-cancel/
- Points on closing: closing your last Membership Rewards card forfeits the points. https://doctorofcredit.com/annual-fee-refund-rules-for-each-card-issuer
- DISAGREE on timing: AwardWallet (2026-04-03) says you have 30 days after canceling a Membership Rewards card to use the points; other sources describe forfeiture at closing. New York cardmembers may have 90 days after notice (search). https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-membership-rewards/do-amex-points-expire/
- One report says newer Amex cards may not be pooled into one points balance automatically, so a second card may not protect the balance unless the accounts are linked. https://viewfromthewing.com/?p=258466 (search)
- UNCONFIRMED: whether the no-fee EveryDay card is open to new applicants today; whether Amex takes back a welcome offer when a card is closed or downgraded inside 12 months (search summaries say its terms allow it; no page read confirms); the 30-day refund window on a current Amex page; typical retention offers in 2026 (figures seen were 30,000 to 55,000 points or $300 to $550, search summaries only).
