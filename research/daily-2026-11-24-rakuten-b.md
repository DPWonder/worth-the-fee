## 4. Forgot to activate

- "Your purchases will earn Cash Back only if you start a Shopping Trip with Rakuten." Without a click on a Rakuten link, Rakuten has no way of showing the store that you are a member. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/what-is-a-rakuten-shopping-trip-360002100588
- A trip with no purchase stays recorded for up to three months; a new trip is needed for every purchase; with multiple windows, the shopping window must have come from a Rakuten link. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/what-is-a-rakuten-shopping-trip-360002100588
- Extension: activate cash back before you start shopping or before checkout; if no Activate Cash Back button shows, the store may not be available for the extension. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/why-didnt-i-earn-cash-back-360036367393
- Activate before adding anything to the cart: some stores do not allow Rakuten to link a purchase if items were in the cart before activation. First checklist question: "Did you activate Cash Back through Rakuten before adding items to your cart?" https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/why-didnt-i-earn-cash-back-360036367393
- Missing shopping trip = no record of the click-through; missing cash back = trip recorded but $0 or ineligible. The Missing Cash Back form is offered to members who checked the list and believe they should have earned. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/why-didnt-i-earn-cash-back-360036367393
- Terms 10.4: contact Member Services within ninety (90) days of the transaction; whether a purchase qualifies is at Rakuten's sole discretion. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/terms-conditions
- Exchanges: to keep cash back, return the item and place an entirely new order, starting with Rakuten. Returned or canceled orders may void cash back. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/returns-cancellations-exchanges-360002100688
- Activation shows as a confirmation pop-up; the store can take a few hours to several days to report an order. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-can-i-tell-if-im-earning-cash-back-360002100668
- The extension alerts shoppers when to activate; no need to visit Rakuten.com first. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/what-is-the-rakuten-browser-extension-360002116947
- UNCONFIRMED: whether the Missing Cash Back form accepts an order with no shopping trip, or whether Rakuten ever credits one as a courtesy (the form redirects to sign-in; no help page says so). https://www.rakuten.com/help/missing-cash-back
- UNCONFIRMED: any Rakuten page that tells shoppers to cancel and reorder after forgetting to activate (only the exchange and travel pages describe a new order through Rakuten). https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/returns-cancellations-exchanges-360002100688
