## 2. Delta SkyMiles cards (Gold, Platinum, Reserve; personal)

- Annual fees: Gold $0 the first year then $150; Platinum $350; Reserve $650. https://www.delta.com/us/en/skymiles/airline-credit-cards/american-express-personal-cards
- Same three fees, second source (dated 2026-09-16). https://onemileatatime.com/?p=373039
- First checked bag free on Delta flights for all three; second checked bag free on domestic Delta flights. https://www.delta.com/us/en/skymiles/airline-credit-cards/american-express-personal-cards
- The free second bag on domestic Delta-operated flights started 2026-06-04 for Gold, Platinum and Reserve. https://news.delta.com/more-travel-value-added-delta-skymiles-cards-no-increase-annual-fees.md
- The bag benefit covers the cardmember and up to eight companions on the same reservation. The flight does not have to be paid with the card, but the cardmember's SkyMiles number must be on the reservation. (published 2026-11-10) https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/delta-skymiles-gold-amex-card/
- Up to eight companions, second source (dated 2026-08-30). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/reviews/platinum-delta-skymiles-credit-card-review/
- Delta bag fees: first bag $45 (was $35), second $55 (was $45), third $200 (was $150), for tickets booked on or after 2026-04-08. Long-haul international fees did not change. Delta Amex cardholders keep the free bag. https://thepointsguy.com/news/delta-air-lines-hikes-checked-bag-fees/
- Bag fee $45 and $55, second source. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/delta-skymiles-gold-amex-card/
- UNCONFIRMED: whether the free bag applies on Delta's lowest ("Basic") fares. No source read states it either way. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/delta-skymiles-gold-amex-card/
- Gold: 2 miles per dollar on Delta, restaurants and US supermarkets; $200 Delta flight credit after $10,000 of purchases in a calendar year (issued as an eCredit that expires one year after issue); up to $100 a year Delta Stays credit; 15% off award flights on Delta; Zone 5 boarding; 20% back on inflight purchases; no foreign transaction fee; no lounge access; no companion certificate. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/delta-skymiles-gold-amex-card/
- Gold rideshare credit: up to $10 a month ($120 a year) on US rideshare with select providers, enrollment required, available only after the card renews. https://news.delta.com/more-travel-value-added-delta-skymiles-cards-no-increase-annual-fees.md
- Platinum: 3 miles per dollar on Delta and on hotels booked direct; 2 miles at restaurants and US supermarkets; companion certificate each year after renewal; up to $150 a year Delta Stays; up to $10 a month Resy ($120); up to $10 a month rideshare ($120); 2,500 MQDs a year plus 1 MQD per $20 spent; Global Entry ($120) or TSA PreCheck (up to $85) credit every four years; no lounge access. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/reviews/platinum-delta-skymiles-credit-card-review/
- Platinum and Reserve credits, issuer-side confirmation: Delta Stays $150 / $200; Resy $10 / $20 a month; rideshare $10 a month; MQD boost $1 per $20 / $1 per $10; $2,500 MQD Headstart. https://www.delta.com/us/en/skymiles/airline-credit-cards/american-express-personal-cards

- Companion certificate: one round trip a year after renewal; Platinum in Delta Main; Reserve in Delta First, Premium Select, Comfort or Main; taxes and fees of $22 to $250 apply; Basic fares excluded; since June 2026 it can also be used on the flight part of a Delta Vacations package. https://news.delta.com/more-travel-value-added-delta-skymiles-cards-no-increase-annual-fees.md

- Platinum certificate books only into fare classes L, T, U, V or X. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/reviews/platinum-delta-skymiles-credit-card-review/
- Reserve: 3 miles per dollar on Delta; 15 Delta Sky Club visits per Medallion Year when flying Delta, unlimited after $75,000 of purchases in a calendar year; Centurion and Escape Lounge access when the Delta flight is booked with the card; up to $20 a month Resy ($240); up to $10 a month rideshare ($120). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/delta-skymiles-reserve-american-express-card/
- TPG values a SkyMile at 1.2 cents (November 2026). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/reviews/platinum-delta-skymiles-credit-card-review/

## 5. Blue Cash Preferred and Blue Cash Everyday

- Preferred: 6% at US supermarkets on up to $6,000 a year then 1%; 6% on select US streaming; 3% at US gas stations and on transit; 1% elsewhere. https://www.cnbc.com/select/amex-blue-cash-everyday-vs-preferred/
- Everyday: 3% at US supermarkets, US gas stations and US online retail, each on up to $6,000 a year, then 1%; 1% elsewhere. https://www.cnbc.com/select/amex-blue-cash-everyday-vs-preferred/

- Disney streaming credit (enrollment required; DisneyPlus.com, Hulu.com, Stream.ESPN.com): Preferred up to $10 a month ($120 a year); Everyday up to $7 a month ($84 a year). https://www.cnbc.com/select/amex-blue-cash-everyday-vs-preferred/
- Both charge a 2.7% foreign transaction fee. Cash back comes as Reward Dollars, redeemable as a statement credit. https://www.cnbc.com/select/amex-blue-cash-everyday-vs-preferred/
- US supermarkets do not include superstores (Walmart, Target), warehouse clubs (Costco, Sam's Club) or convenience stores. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/blue-cash-preferred-card/

## 6. The "once per lifetime" welcome offer rule

- Current wording on the Platinum Card: "You may not be eligible to receive a welcome offer if you have or have had this Card". The terms also say Amex will tell you before processing the application if you are not eligible, so you can withdraw. (dated 2026-09-30) https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express-bonus-lifetime-restrictions/
- Older wording was "not available to applicants who have or have had this product"; the change to "may not be eligible" was reported 2025-10-22. Whether Amex loosened the rule is unclear. https://awardwallet.com/news/amex-membership-rewards/amex-once-per-lifetime-rule-changes/
- The rule is per card. It covers renamed versions of a card (Premier Rewards Gold counts for the Gold Card). Personal and business versions are separate cards. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express-bonus-lifetime-restrictions/
- A card you got by upgrading counts as having had it. Upgrade offers are not welcome offers. (dated 2026-05-26) https://onemileatatime.com/guides/amex-lifetime-rule/
- Pop-up: during an application Amex can show a notice that you are not eligible for the welcome offer; you can withdraw or go on without the offer. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express-bonus-lifetime-restrictions/
- Pop-up appears before a hard credit inquiry; message given as "There is no welcome offer available for you at this time." (updated 2026-11-10) https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-gold-card/welcome-offer-eligibility-changes/
- Amex also has a welcome offer eligibility tool and can show a "no offer" message while you browse. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express-bonus-lifetime-restrictions/
- Family rule, Membership Rewards cards: having or having had any Platinum Card (Schwab and Morgan Stanley versions included) can make you ineligible for the Gold Card offer. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-gold-card/welcome-offer-eligibility-changes/
- Family rule, Green Card (2024): Green, Gold and Platinum cards and earlier versions can block the Green offer. The Classic Green Card is now closed to new applicants per Upgraded Points. https://awardwallet.com/news/credit-cards/amex-adds-family-restrictions-green-card/
- Family rule, Delta: see section 2. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/delta-amex-current-offers/
- UNCONFIRMED: family rules for Hilton, Marriott and Blue Cash cards. No source read covers them. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express-bonus-lifetime-restrictions/
- Other factors Amex names: balance transfer history, history as a cardmember, number of cards opened and closed. https://awardwallet.com/news/credit-cards/amex-adds-family-restrictions-green-card/
- "Lifetime" resetting after about seven years is a common belief; Amex has not confirmed it. UNCONFIRMED. https://onemileatatime.com/guides/amex-lifetime-rule/
- Targeted offers sometimes carry no lifetime wording. https://onemileatatime.com/guides/amex-lifetime-rule/
- Reported, not official: most cardmembers are limited to five Amex credit cards. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express-bonus-lifetime-restrictions/
