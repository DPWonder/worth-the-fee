## 1. Uber Cash and Uber One (Platinum)

- Uber Cash: up to $200 a year, as $15 each month plus a $20 bonus in December ($35 that month). https://www.uber.com/us/en/u/amex/
- Unused Amex Uber Cash "expires at 11:59 PM Hawaii Standard Time on the last day of each calendar month"; no rollover. https://www.uber.com/us/en/u/amex/
- US only: "The Amex Benefit may only be used in the United States." Works on rides and orders where Uber Cash is accepted. https://www.uber.com/us/en/u/amex/
- To redeem, Uber Cash must be toggled on and "an American Express Card must be selected as the payment method for your transaction"; if the Uber Cash is short, that Amex card is charged the difference. https://www.uber.com/us/en/u/amex/
- Eligible: Basic Card Members on a consumer Platinum account (additional Platinum cardholders do not get their own Uber Cash). A card can give the benefit to only one Uber account. Cards added through Apple Pay or PayPal are not eligible. https://www.uber.com/us/en/u/amex/
- Cannot be used when paying with a family profile. https://www.uber.com/us/en/u/amex/
- Uber Cash can take up to 48 hours to appear after the card is added. https://www.uber.com/us/en/u/amex/
- Second source for $15 monthly, $35 in December, month-end expiry, rides and Uber Eats (and Uber grocery orders) in the US. https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/amex-platinum-uber-cash-credit/
- Authorized users do not receive their own Uber Cash (second source). https://thepointsguy.com/guide/why-add-authorized-users-amex-platinum/
- Whether Uber Eats pickup orders (no delivery) take the Amex Uber Cash: UNCONFIRMED; Uber's terms say "eligible rides and orders where Uber Cash is accepted" and no source read names pickup. https://www.uber.com/us/en/u/amex/
- Whether Uber Cash covers tips: UNCONFIRMED (not addressed in the pages read). https://www.uber.com/us/en/u/amex/
- Uber One credit: "up to $120 in statement credits per calendar year on Uber One membership fees charged to an eligible Card"; one $120 per card account; charges by additional cardholders count toward it. https://www.uber.com/us/en/u/amex-uberone
- Uber One credit covers monthly and annual memberships; the Platinum card must be the payment method, Uber Cash must be toggled off for the membership fee, and no credit is given on any part paid with Uber Cash, on free trials or on discounted portions. https://www.uber.com/us/en/u/amex-uberone
- Uber One auto-renews; removing the card does not cancel it; credits can take up to 8 weeks. https://www.uber.com/us/en/u/amex-uberone
- Uber One price: $9.99 a month or $96 a year (US). https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/amex-platinum-uber-cash-credit/
- Uber One price, second source ($9.99 a month, $96 a year; seen in search results from several 2026 pages, not opened). https://dealnews.com/features/uber-one
- Uber One enrollment: TPG and Upgraded Points say enrollment required; Uber's page describes only setting the card as the payment method. Treated as DISAGREE (carried over from amex.md). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/benefits/
- Uber One member perks listed by TPG: $0 delivery fee on eligible Uber Eats orders, lower service fees, 6% back on rides as Uber One credits. https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/amex-platinum-uber-cash-credit/

## 2. Resy credit (Platinum)

- Up to $100 per calendar quarter, $400 a year, at US Resy restaurants; quarters are Jan-Mar, Apr-Jun, Jul-Sep, Oct-Dec; no rollover; several purchases can add up to the $100. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/resy-dining-credit/
- Enrollment required, once (Rewards & Benefits tab of the Amex account, or by phone). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/resy-dining-credit/
- No reservation needed: "You don't need to book through Resy for the credit to apply." Walk-ins count. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/resy-dining-credit/
- Second source: no Resy booking needed, no need to tell the restaurant; takeout counts if you pay the restaurant directly; personal Platinum only (not Business Platinum). Article modified 2026-11-11. https://onemileatatime.com/guides/amex-platinum-card-resy-dining-credit/
- "Well over 10,000" US restaurants (OMAAT); "more than 10,000" (Upgraded Points). https://onemileatatime.com/guides/amex-platinum-card-resy-dining-credit/
- Purchases by additional cardholders count, but there is one $400 per account. https://onemileatatime.com/guides/amex-platinum-card-resy-dining-credit/
- Gift cards and merchandise are excluded. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/resy-dining-credit/

OMAAT says the venue page shows a "Resy Credit eligible" badge. https://onemileatatime.com/news/amex-resy-credits-tock-restaurants/

## 4. Airline fee credit (Platinum)

- Up to $200 per calendar year (Jan 1 to Dec 31), one selected airline, enrollment/selection required; you get back only what you spend on eligible fees. Article dated 2026-05-06. https://awardwallet.com/blog/amex-platinum-airline-fee-credit
- Qualifying airlines per AwardWallet (May 2026): Alaska, American, Delta, JetBlue, Southwest, United. Spirit removed after it stopped flying in May 2026; Hawaiian removed after its merger with Alaska. https://awardwallet.com/blog/amex-platinum-airline-fee-credit
- DISAGREE: TPG (2026-07-24) lists Alaska, American, Delta, Hawaiian, JetBlue, Southwest, United (Hawaiian still on, Spirit off). OMAAT's guide (modified 2026-01-26) still lists both Hawaiian and Spirit and looks out of date. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/choosing-your-amex-platinum-200-airline-fee-credit/
- Spirit ceased operations (TPG news, 2026-05-04; seen in search results). https://thepointsguy.com/news/amex-airline-benefit-spirit/
- The airline can be changed once a year, in January (by January 31); last year's pick carries over. TPG says Amex has sometimes allowed a later change by phone or chat if none of the credit is used; not guaranteed. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/choosing-your-amex-platinum-200-airline-fee-credit/
- Generally counts when charged separately from the ticket: checked and overweight bag fees, seat assignment fees, change fees, in-flight food and drinks, in-flight entertainment charged by the airline, pet fees, phone booking fees, lounge day passes and memberships. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/choosing-your-amex-platinum-200-airline-fee-credit/
- Does not count: airline tickets, award tickets, upgrades, mileage purchases and transfer fees, gift cards, duty-free, Wi-Fi billed by a third party, fees on partner airlines, fees charged before the airline was selected, seat fees paid in the same charge as the ticket. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/choosing-your-amex-platinum-200-airline-fee-credit/
- Same exclusion list (tickets, upgrades, miles, gift cards, duty-free, award tickets), second source. https://onemileatatime.com/guides/amex-airline-fee-credits/
- DISAGREE on award ticket fees: OMAAT lists "award ticket fees" as often reimbursed; AwardWallet lists award taxes and fees as not counting; TPG calls award taxes off-label. https://onemileatatime.com/guides/amex-airline-fee-credits/
- The credit depends on how the airline codes the charge; TPG says United lounge passes may code as airfare. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/choosing-your-amex-platinum-200-airline-fee-credit/
- Charges by additional cardholders on the account count toward the one $200. https://awardwallet.com/blog/amex-platinum-airline-fee-credit
- Credits post within 8 weeks per the terms; often faster. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/choosing-your-amex-platinum-200-airline-fee-credit/
- Workarounds closed in early 2026: United TravelBank loads and Delta gift-card splits no longer trigger the credit (Upgraded Points, updated 2026-11-19). One source plus a reader comment at AwardWallet: UNCONFIRMED by a second article. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/benefits/

## 6. Fine Hotels + Resorts and The Hotel Collection

- Fine Hotels + Resorts (FHR) benefits: daily breakfast for two, $100 credit toward eligible charges (varies by property), Wi-Fi, guaranteed 4 p.m. checkout, noon check-in when available, upgrade when available. Article dated 2026-08-13. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-membership-rewards/amex-fine-hotels-and-resorts
- The Hotel Collection (THC) benefits: up to $100 credit toward eligible charges (varies by property), room upgrade when available, noon check-in when available, 4 p.m. checkout when available. No breakfast. Two-night minimum. Article dated 2026-07-20. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express-the-hotel-collection-guide/
- FHR late checkout is guaranteed; THC late checkout is "when available". https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express-the-hotel-collection-guide/
- FHR has over 1,900 hotels; the two programs together have more than 3,400 hotels in 116 countries. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-membership-rewards/amex-fine-hotels-and-resorts
- THC has more than 1,300 hotels and is generally cheaper than FHR. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express-the-hotel-collection-guide/
- Back-to-back THC stays at the same hotel within 24 hours count as one stay (one $100 credit). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express-the-hotel-collection-guide/
- FHR is for Platinum and Centurion cardholders; THC is also open to Gold cardholders. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express-the-hotel-collection-guide/
- The $600 hotel credit needs a prepaid ("Pay Today" / "Book and Pay Today") rate; prepaid bookings also earn 5 points per dollar. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-membership-rewards/amex-fine-hotels-and-resorts
- From January 1, 2027, an FHR booking must be at least two nights to earn the hotel credit (second and third sources for the Amex line in amex.md). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express-the-hotel-collection-guide/
- Amex values FHR benefits at an average of $550 per stay, based on two-night stays (Amex's figure, not checked). https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-membership-rewards/amex-fine-hotels-and-resorts
- Amex Travel prices can differ from the hotel's direct price, sometimes widely. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-membership-rewards/amex-fine-hotels-and-resorts
- Hotel loyalty points and elite perks on these bookings: not guaranteed; a TPG writer reports usually getting them on THC stays. UNCONFIRMED as a rule. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express-the-hotel-collection-guide/
- Whether the 2027 two-night rule also applies to FHR's on-property benefits (breakfast, $100 credit) or only to the statement credit: sources describe it only for the credit. UNCONFIRMED for the on-property benefits. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-membership-rewards/amex-fine-hotels-and-resorts
