## 2. Ibotta

- Ibotta's "how" page: a free app; find offers and save them to Your list; shop in store or by online grocery and delivery; submit a receipt or link a store loyalty account; "Once your earnings hit $20 you can withdraw to your bank account, PayPal, or a gift card."; online offers are activated by clicking through from Ibotta; a browser extension shows cash back, compares prices and sets price drop alerts. https://home.ibotta.com/how/
- Three ways to withdraw: bank (only banks that have opted in; some banks do not allow third-party transfers), PayPal, or a digital gift card (some gift cards have a minimum purchase of $25). Qualifications: at least $20 in earnings, at least one redeemed offer, and at least 7 days after redeeming the first offer or purchasing a gift card. Funds take 0 to 5 days depending on the method. https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/223788308-What-are-the-different-ways-to-withdraw-my-earnings
- PayPal: after the $20 threshold; payment within 2 hours, or 1 to 3 business days in high-traffic periods; PayPal withdrawal is supported only in the app, not on Ibotta.com; a phone number may need to be verified. https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004907048-How-do-I-withdraw-my-earnings-to-PayPal
- Gift card: balance of at least $20.00 and all qualifications met; the e-gift card link is emailed within the hour. https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000105328-How-do-I-withdraw-my-earnings-to-a-gift-card
- Bank: at $20.00 in current earnings a bank account can be linked (through Plaid) and withdrawn to from the app or Ibotta.com; a phone number must be verified first; each bank account can be linked to only one Ibotta account. https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/360013893000-How-do-I-get-cash-back-straight-to-my-bank-account
- DISAGREE (minor, Ibotta's own pages): gift card delivery is "within the hour" in the help center and within 24 hours in a 2018 Ibotta blog post, which also lists only PayPal and gift cards. https://home.ibotta.com/blog/tips-tricks/how-to-withdraw-your-ibotta-earnings
- Account maintenance fee: Ibotta is free to use; an account with no activity for 180 days is inactive, and $3.99 is deducted from its earnings for every 30 days it stays inactive; taken only from the Ibotta balance, never from a personal bank account; activity is a completed offer redemption or a credited referral bonus; Ibotta tries to email before the first fee. https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/225528367-What-are-Account-Maintenance-Fees
- Earnings can stay in the account as long as the member likes, subject to that fee. https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000491267-How-long-can-I-keep-earnings-in-my-Ibotta-account
- How it works: add offers before shopping; three submission options: linked loyalty account (no receipt), manual paper receipt (in-store only), or online shopping. https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/360008385000-How-does-Ibotta-work-A-Step-By-Step-Guide-for-the-App
- Receipts: a paper register receipt with store name, date and time and an itemized list, submitted within 7 days of purchase; digital receipts, e-receipts, screenshots and home prints are not valid for in-store offers. https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/225450727-What-are-receipt-qualifications-for-in-store-offers
- Online shopping: started from the Online shopping category in the app, website or browser extension; grocery pickup and delivery purchases do not count toward Online shopping offers; at qualifying retailers cash back arrives 7 days after the transaction date, other retailers have their own pending periods, and travel pending periods start after the stay. https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/115006516068-What-are-some-frequent-Online-Shopping-questions
- Pending periods let Ibotta verify the purchase with the retailer and follow its return period; each offer's Details shows the length. https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000428307-Help-My-Online-Shopping-offer-is-still-pending
- "Ibotta works in over 2,000 retail chains, restaurants, movie theaters, convenience stores, home improvement centers, pet stores, and pharmacies nationwide in-store and online." https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/223834187-Where-Does-Ibotta-Work
- Ibotta's minimum age and residency rules: UNCONFIRMED (ibotta.com/terms returned a script shell with no text; home.ibotta.com/terms returned 404). https://ibotta.com/terms
- Any fee Ibotta charges on a withdrawal: UNCONFIRMED (none is mentioned on the withdrawal articles read). https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/223788308-What-are-the-different-ways-to-withdraw-my-earnings
- Whether a Rakuten card-linked in-store offer and an Ibotta receipt offer can both pay on one purchase: UNCONFIRMED (neither company's pages address it).

## 3. Sapphire Reserve Exclusive Tables

- Chase terms, read today: through 06/30/2030 the benefit is available on U.S.-issued Chase Sapphire Reserve Visa Infinite, JPMorgan Reserve Visa Infinite and Chase Sapphire Reserve for Business cards. Steps: visit OpenTable.com or open the app; sign in or sign up for a free account; complete the eligibility check; add the eligible card to the OpenTable account. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- Same terms: the benefit is "exclusive access to primetime reservations and special events" at participating restaurants in select cities through OpenTable's website, app and benefit page; inventory not booked before the reservation day and time may be released to general OpenTable inventory; where a restaurant requires card information or prepayment, the eligible Chase card stored in the OpenTable account must be used; if the card is no longer stored, another eligibility check may be required; OpenTable checks eligibility at verification and then once every 24 hours when the user visits; an ineligible card may be stored and granted access later; new cardmembers allow up to 4 business days; OpenTable and Chase have no further responsibility for the dining experience. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- Chase guide, read today: the program gives access to reservations and special events; The Infatuation, Chase and OpenTable selected the restaurants; eligible cardmembers "and any authorized users on their accounts" can make a reservation as a complimentary perk; link the card through the OpenTable Chase Dining page; restaurants are in "several cities across the U.S."; no count is given. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/guide-to-chase-sapphire-reserve-exclusive-tables
- Chase dining credit article, read today: cardmembers access the program by enrolling the card on OpenTable; to receive the dining credit they pay with the Sapphire Reserve at an eligible Exclusive Tables restaurant; up to $150 January through June and $150 July through December. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/how-to-use-dining-credit-chase-sapphire-reserve-exclusive-tables
- The Points Guy, read today: the Sapphire Reserve, J.P. Morgan Reserve and Sapphire Reserve for Business have access; the business card has no dining statement credit; reported 362 to 397 locations; about 50 US cities and metro areas in OpenTable's FAQ; a "Sapphire Reserve Exclusive Tables" banner on OpenTable's home page after a card is connected; a linked card does not need to be reactivated; the writer has three nearby restaurants in a midsize city. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-reserve-exclusive-tables-guide/
