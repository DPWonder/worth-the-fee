# Fact sheet: Rakuten (US), batch `rakuten-stores-b`

Checked 2026-11-21. Adds to `chase-rakuten.md`, `rakuten-deep.md` and `rakuten-more.md`; nothing here repeats them unless a new read changes it. Every line ends with the page it came from. "(search)" means the fact was seen in a search-result summary of that page, not in a full read. UNCONFIRMED means no source settled it. DISAGREE means sources conflict. Rates are "seen on 2026-11-21" and change daily. Store pages were read through a page fetch that summarizes; short phrases in quotes are as returned.

## 7. App and browser extension

- Extension: desktop Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, Opera and Samsung Internet; on phones only Safari on iOS. It notifies you when cash back is available, but you activate it; activate on every visit, because a second purchase at the same store needs its own activation. https://rakuten.com/help/article/how-to-use-the-rakuten-browser-extension-360002100788
- Toolbar icon: purple means the store has no cash back; red means available but not activated; blue means activated. https://rakuten.com/help/article/how-to-use-the-rakuten-browser-extension-360002100788
- DISAGREE: Rakuten's blog (2026-01-30) says the extension activates cash back automatically; the help article says it notifies you and you activate. https://www.rakuten.com/blog/rakuten-explained-how-it-works-every-way-you-can-shop/ and https://rakuten.com/help/article/how-to-use-the-rakuten-browser-extension-360002100788
- Rakuten's blog calls the extension on desktop plus the app on the phone the "most reliable setup". https://www.rakuten.com/blog/rakuten-explained-how-it-works-every-way-you-can-shop/
- The Safari mobile extension is bundled with the Rakuten iOS app; it needs the latest app and the latest iOS Safari, is turned on under Manage Extensions, and needs "Always Allow" on every website. Not available on Android; Android users use the app. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/safari-mobile-extension-faq-24111151607059
- Safari (Mac, iPhone, iPad) can block tracking at some stores because of Intelligent Tracking Prevention 2.0; the store's page on Rakuten says if tracking is blocked in your browser. Advice: on mobile shop through the Rakuten app; on desktop switch to Chrome. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/browsers-preventing-cash-back-360002117007
- Things that can stop activation: cookie-blocking security software, firewalls with ad blocking, other rewards or coupon extensions, private or incognito browsing, VPNs, network-level blockers, ad blockers, mobile content blockers. For mobile content blockers: shop in the Rakuten app or turn them off while shopping. https://rakuten.com/help/article/software-or-settings-preventing-cash-back-360002100568
- Also: declining the store's cookie prompt, adding items to the cart before activating, checking out in a different tab or after a long idle period can break tracking. If the extension shows no activate button, the store may not be available through the extension. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/why-didnt-i-earn-cash-back-360036367393
- A shopping trip starts with a click on a Rakuten link on the site, in the app or with the extension; each has a number used to verify the purchase; the window you buy in must have come from a Rakuten link; trips without a purchase stay in the account for up to three months. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/what-is-an-rakuten-shopping-trip-360002100588
- App: restaurant cash back (5%, including tips) is only in the app; in-store offers are added and cards linked in the app; price-drop alerts need notifications on. https://rakuten.com/help/article/all-the-ways-to-shop-with-rakuten-360002100548
- Checking the extension: confirm it is enabled on the browser's extensions page and pinned to the toolbar; a browser update can disable it; fix by updating the browser or reinstalling. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/how-to-check-if-the-rakuten-browser-extension-is-working-360002117067
- Any measured tracking success rate for the app versus the extension: UNCONFIRMED (none found from Rakuten or a third party).
- What happens when a link from the Rakuten app opens the store's own app instead of a browser page: UNCONFIRMED on Rakuten's pages (not mentioned in the articles read).
