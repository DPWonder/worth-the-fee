# Fact sheet: more Chase Sapphire pages (batch chase-more)

Checked 2026-11-21. Adds to `chase-rakuten.md`, `chase-deep.md`, `benefits.md`, `compare.md` and `points.md`; facts already confirmed there are not repeated unless a line adds detail. Every line ends with the page it came from. "(search)" means the fact was seen in a search-result summary of that page, not in a full read. UNCONFIRMED means no source settled it. DISAGREE means sources conflict.

## 1. Sapphire Preferred hotel credit (now $100, was $50)

- Chase's June 15, 2026 release: "$100 Chase Travel Hotel Credit each account anniversary, doubled from $50"; no activation; the FAQ says it requires a prepaid hotel booking through Chase Travel; new and existing cardmembers have the new benefits from June 15, 2026. https://media.chase.com/news/Meet-the-New-Chase-Sapphire-Preferred
- Chase's terms: statement credit applied automatically to hotel accommodation purchases made through Chase Travel, up to $100 a year; "annual" is the year from the account open date through the first statement date after the account anniversary, then each 12 billing cycles; the first $100 of those purchases each year earns no points; credit posts within 3 business days but may take up to 4 weeks; it may be reversed if the purchase is returned, canceled or modified, or if the account is closed within 90 days of the credit. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- Chase's guide: the credit resets on the cardmember anniversary, does not carry over, does not reduce the price at booking, and does not apply to other travel or to hotels booked outside Chase Travel. https://chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/rewards-benefits/how-to-use-the-sapphire-preferred-hotel-credit
- No minimum stay and no set hotel list; a stay under $100 uses part of the credit and the rest carries to a later booking in the same anniversary year (updated 2026-10-12). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/chase-sapphire-preferred-card/hotel-credit/
- Choose to pay with the card, not with points, or the credit does not apply; the rest of the booking earns 5x; Chase Travel is a third-party channel, so stays at big chains generally earn no hotel points or elite benefits; Chase Travel rates can be higher than booking direct (article dated 2026-07-03). https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/chase/chase-sapphire-preferred-hotel-credit/
- TPG (2026-06-27): applies to prepaid hotel stays through Chase Travel; some bookings show a "loyalty program eligible" tag but hotel points are not guaranteed. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/chase-sapphire-preferred-hotel-credit/
- Existing cardmembers who had already used $50 got another $50 from June 15, 2026. https://thepointsguy.com/news/chase-sapphire-preferred-refresh-2026/
- DISAGREE on posting time: Chase's terms say 3 business days, up to 4 weeks; One Mile at a Time says within two days of the purchase posting; Upgraded Points says 1 to 2 billing cycles. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- DISAGREE (minor) on prepayment: Chase's release FAQ and TPG say a prepaid booking is required; Chase's terms page and Upgraded Points do not mention it. https://media.chase.com/news/Meet-the-New-Chase-Sapphire-Preferred
- UNCONFIRMED: whether a booking made with an authorized user's card triggers the credit (stated for The Edit credit on the Reserve, not for this one); whether vacation rentals in Chase Travel count; whether a booking paid partly with points gets a partial credit.

## 2. Sapphire Preferred DoorDash and DashPass

- Chase's terms: DashPass for at least 12 months when activated by 12/31/2029; membership runs from activation until 12/31/2029 or for 12 months, whichever is longer; primary cardmember and authorized users each add the card on DoorDash or Caviar and activate; all users on the account share one membership period that starts when the first one activates. https://chase.com/digital/resources/terms-of-use/sapphire-doordash
- Monthly promo: $10 off one non-restaurant order (grocery, convenience and similar) per calendar month; subtotal only, not fees, taxes or tip; no rollover; must pay with the enrolled card; gift cards excluded; alcohol orders may be restricted. https://chase.com/digital/resources/terms-of-use/sapphire-doordash
- The card itself must be used at checkout; digital wallets do not qualify. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- DoorDash's help page: Preferred DashPass is free through 12/31/29 or a minimum of one year; DashPass gives a $0 delivery fee and reduced service fees on eligible orders that meet the minimum subtotal shown in the app; Chase promos have no separate minimum but DoorDash's own minimums apply; an existing paid DashPass is replaced on activation and billing stops (no refund of past charges mentioned). https://help.doordash.com/consumers/s/article/Chase-Partnership
- Chase states the DashPass value as $120 a year. https://media.chase.com/news/Meet-the-New-Chase-Sapphire-Preferred
- DashPass renews at the then-current monthly rate after the free period, "currently $9.99/month". https://help.doordash.com/consumers/s/article/Chase-Partnership (search)
- DashPass applies to eligible orders over $12, one source. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-doordash-partnership/ (search). UNCONFIRMED on a DoorDash page read in full.
- DISAGREE on the activation deadline: Chase's terms, Chase's Preferred update page and DoorDash say 12/31/2029; a search summary of an older Chase page and announcement says 12/31/2027. https://www.chase.com/travel/guide/tips/preferred-update
- Reserve for comparison: the $15 monthly promo started 2026-10-01 and replaced a $5 restaurant promo; only one $10 promo per order. https://help.doordash.com/consumers/s/article/Chase-Partnership

## 3. Sapphire Preferred 10% anniversary points bonus (ended)

- Chase: "The 10% Anniversary Bonus will be discontinued, effective immediately for cardmembers who apply on or after June 15, 2026"; for earlier applicants, eligible purchases through October 1, 2026 still earn it; all bonuses earned are awarded by January 31, 2027. https://media.chase.com/news/Meet-the-New-Chase-Sapphire-Preferred
- How it worked: each account anniversary Chase added points equal to 10% of the dollars spent on purchases in the prior year; $20,000 of spending gave 2,000 points, $50,000 gave 5,000 (published 2026-05-08, modified 2026-06-24). https://thriftytraveler.com/news/credit-card/chase-sapphire-preferred-anniversary-bonus-ending/
- TPG: it "essentially added 0.1 points per dollar spent on the card"; final bonus deposited by January 2027. https://thepointsguy.com/news/chase-sapphire-preferred-refresh-2026/
- One Mile at a Time: $100,000 of spending earned 10,000 bonus points; negative changes hit existing cardmembers on October 1, 2026. https://onemileatatime.com/news/chase-sapphire-preferred-changes
- AwardWallet (2026-06-10): the last bonus is "posted in early 2027". https://awardwallet.com/news/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-preferred-card-changes/
- The same refresh added 3x on gas and EV charging, 3x on vacation homes at brands such as Airbnb and Vrbo, the $100 hotel credit, a Global Entry / TSA PreCheck / NEXUS credit of up to $120 every four years, and emergency evacuation cover up to $100,000. https://media.chase.com/news/Meet-the-New-Chase-Sapphire-Preferred
- Chase's current terms page for the Sapphire cards does not mention an anniversary bonus. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- UNCONFIRMED: whether the final bonus posts on the cardholder's anniversary or as one deposit for everyone (sources give only "by January 31, 2027" or "early 2027").

## 4. Sapphire Reserve credit for The Edit

- Chase's terms: up to $250 per credit, maximum $500 per calendar year from 1/1/2026; prepaid "Pay Now" stays of two nights or more made by the cardmember or an authorized user; the qualifying part earns no points; posts within 3 business days, up to 4 weeks; purchases delayed by processing can fall into the next period; may be reversed on a return, cancellation or modification, or if the account closes within 90 days of the credit. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- Each $250 credit must be used on a separate booking; either can be combined with the 2026 select-hotel credit on one reservation; credits do not reduce the price at booking; prices in Chase Travel may be higher than booking direct (article dated 2026-06-12). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/stacking-chase-sapphire-reserve-hotel-credits-the-edit/
- On-property benefits: daily breakfast for two, $100 property credit per booking, room upgrade and early check-in or late checkout when available, Wi-Fi where offered, welcome amenity subject to availability. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/stacking-chase-sapphire-reserve-hotel-credits-the-edit/
- 2026 select-hotel credit: one-time, up to $250, prepaid stays of at least two nights through Chase Travel at IHG, Montage, Pendry, Omni, Virgin Hotels, Minor Hotels and Pan Pacific; expires 2026-12-31. This settles the booking-deadline line marked UNCONFIRMED in `chase-deep.md` as far as third-party sources go. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/stacking-chase-sapphire-reserve-hotel-credits-the-edit/
- Chase's own example of stacking: a two-night $480 stay, The Edit credit takes $250, the select-hotel credit covers the remaining $230. https://account.chase.com/sapphire/benefits (search)
- Size of the collection: "more than 1,000 properties" (TPG, 2026-05-21); 1,371 as of November 2025 and the list rotates (Thrifty Traveler, modified 2026-10-16). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-reserve-the-edit-credit/ and https://thriftytraveler.com/guides/credit-card/the-edit-by-chase-travel/
- TPG example: a two-night stay of $847 came to $597 after the credit; the first $250 of each booking earns no points; Edit bookings can earn hotel loyalty points at chains such as Marriott, but Small Luxury Hotels bookings earn no Hilton points. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-reserve-the-edit-credit/
- Thrifty Traveler: hotel points and elite night credits are earned "in most cases"; example prices just over $600 a night (Fairmont Chateau Lake Louise, off-peak) and from about $500 a night (Four Seasons Minneapolis); few or no Hilton properties. https://thriftytraveler.com/guides/credit-card/the-edit-by-chase-travel/
- NerdWallet: only about one third of the rates checked were under $500 a night. https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/travel/chase-sapphire-reserve-the-edit-hotel-credit (search)
- Back-to-back bookings at the same hotel get one set of on-property benefits (updated 2026-01-01). https://upgradedpoints.com/news/chase-sapphire-reserve-hotel-credit-updates-2026/
- DISAGREE on posting time: Chase's terms say 3 business days, up to 4 weeks; TPG says up to eight weeks; Thrifty Traveler says a few days, with Chase quoting six to eight weeks. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- DISAGREE (minor): Upgraded Points calls the select-hotel credit annual; TPG and Chase's page for the business card call it one-time and ending 2026-12-31. https://upgradedpoints.com/news/chase-sapphire-reserve-hotel-credit-updates-2026/
- UNCONFIRMED: whether a stay paid with points triggers the credit; cancellation terms of Pay Now rates (they vary by hotel and were not read).

## 5. Sapphire Reserve dining credit (Exclusive Tables)

- Chase's terms: up to $150 from January 1 to June 30 and up to $150 from July 1 to December 31; personal Sapphire Reserve and J.P. Morgan Reserve; excluded are merchandise, gift cards and purchases through third parties such as payment processors or delivery platforms; the purchase must be identifiable as made directly with the restaurant; posts within 3 business days, up to 4 weeks; the restaurant list is on OpenTable and can change. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- Exclusive Tables itself (reservation access) runs through 06/30/2030; cardholders add the card to an OpenTable account and pass an eligibility check that runs every 24 hours; new cardmembers may wait up to 4 business days; the Sapphire Reserve for Business has the access but not the credit. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- Chase's guide: pay with the Reserve at an eligible restaurant; the credit can be split across several restaurants in a half-year ($80 in February and $70 in May fill the first $150); list at opentable.com/sapphire-reserve-exclusive-tables. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/how-to-use-dining-credit-chase-sapphire-reserve-exclusive-tables
- TPG (2026-07-06): booking through Exclusive Tables is not required; Chase has not published a count, reported figures range from 362 to 397 restaurants; OpenTable lists about 50 US cities and metro areas; 3x points are earned on the full bill including the credited part; charges near the end of a period can post into the next one; some readers report restaurant gift cards triggering the credit. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-reserve-exclusive-tables-guide/
- Thrifty Traveler (modified 2026-08-24): 51 locations listed; three participating restaurants in the Twin Cities; restaurants in OpenTable's Visa Dining Collection without the Sapphire Reserve badge do not count; takeout unclear; some reports of credits posting with Apple Pay or Google Pay. https://thriftytraveler.com/guides/credit-card/sapphire-reserve-dining-credit/
- Chase may reverse a credit if the purchase is refunded or changed, or the account closes within 90 days. https://thriftytraveler.com/guides/credit-card/sapphire-reserve-dining-credit/
- Chase has city guides for New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, San Francisco, Philadelphia, Houston, Las Vegas and San Diego. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/guide-to-chase-sapphire-reserve-exclusive-tables (search)
- DISAGREE on posting time: Chase 3 business days to 4 weeks; TPG one or two billing cycles; Thrifty Traveler six to eight weeks. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- DISAGREE on gift cards: Chase's terms exclude them; TPG reports cases where they triggered the credit. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-reserve-exclusive-tables-guide/
- DISAGREE (minor) on the program's end date: Thrifty Traveler says tables and events run through June 30, 2026; Chase's terms now say 06/30/2030. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- UNCONFIRMED: whether the card must be linked on OpenTable for the credit itself (Chase says linking gives access to the program and that paying with the card earns the credit); a "major update" to the collection in July 2026 reported by NerdWallet, whose article would not load. https://www.nerdwallet.com/travel/learn/chase-dining-credit (search)

## 6. Authorized users on the Sapphire cards

- Chase's terms: authorized users have the same charging privileges but are not financially responsible; the primary cardmember repays all balances and confirms the person's consent when adding them. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- Reserve: Priority Pass Select is activated automatically for the primary cardmember and authorized users; each may bring two guests free, $27 per extra guest; Air Canada lounges for the cardholder plus one guest, extra guests $59 at US lounges. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- Reserve authorized user (article dated 2026-10-18): $195 each a year; no hard limit on the number; points go to the primary cardmember; they get Priority Pass with two guests, Sapphire Lounge access, select Air Canada lounges, the same rental car cover and travel protections, DashPass and the monthly DoorDash promos, access to The Edit; they get no welcome bonus and no second set of the travel, dining, The Edit or Global Entry credits, though their spending can use up the account's credits; typically no credit pull; the card usually shows on the authorized user's credit report and counts toward 5/24; the card is mailed to the primary cardmember in five to seven business days. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/chase/chase-sapphire-reserve-authorized-user/
- Preferred authorized users are free, earn at the same rates and get travel and rental car cover but no lounge access. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/chase/chase-sapphire-reserve-authorized-user/
- Preferred: DoorDash lets the primary cardmember and authorized users each activate DashPass. https://chase.com/digital/resources/terms-of-use/sapphire-doordash
- Apple TV on the Preferred is for primary cardholders (article dated 2026-11-10). https://thepointsguy.com/guide/reasons-why-college-student-authorized-user-chase-sapphire-preferred/
- Reserve for Business authorized users (employees) do not get Air Canada lounge access. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- DISAGREE: an older One Mile at a Time snippet says Preferred authorized users cost $195 and get Priority Pass; Chase and the same site's current article say free and no lounges. https://onemileatatime.com/chase-sapphire-reserve-authorized-user-benefits (search)
- UNCONFIRMED: Chase's minimum age for an authorized user; whether the $195 is refunded or prorated when an authorized user is removed; whether the Reserve's StubHub, Lyft and Peloton credits can be triggered by an authorized user's card.

## 7. Product changes (upgrade and downgrade)

- Chase's own page: a Preferred can be switched to a Reserve online (24 to 48 hours), by phone or at a branch; the Preferred must have been held at least a year; the Reserve needs a credit limit of at least $10,000; people who switch are not eligible for the Reserve's new-cardmember offer; since the 2025 relaunch a person can hold both cards. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/chase-cards/upgrading-to-sapphire-reserve-from-sapphire-preferred
- TPG (2026-08-09): the Preferred's downgrade options are the Freedom Flex and Freedom Unlimited; Chase typically wants 12 months with the card; ask for a "product change" by phone; generally no credit check; the card number typically stays, the expiration date and security code may change; credit line and due date usually stay; no welcome bonus on the new product; transfer or redeem points before moving to a card that cannot reach partners unless another transfer-capable card is kept. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/downgrade-chase-credit-card/
- Fee: TPG (2026-08-09) says Chase may prorate or refund part of a fee if the downgrade comes shortly after it posts. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/downgrade-chase-credit-card/
- DISAGREE on the fee: TPG (2025-06-17) says Chase typically refunds the fee only on canceling within 30 days of it posting and not to expect a prorated refund on a downgrade. https://thepointsguy.com/news/cancel-chase-sapphire-reserve-card
- Doctor of Credit: Chase gives 30 days after the fee is billed to cancel for a refund (one reader reported 41 days); nothing on downgrades (posted 2020, Chase data to 2022). https://doctorofcredit.com/annual-fee-refund-rules-for-each-card-issuer
- Reserve terms: after a downgrade and a later upgrade back, earlier spending does not count toward that calendar year's $75,000 threshold. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- Freedom Flex is open to new applicants: $0 fee; 5% on up to $1,500 of combined purchases each quarter in rotating categories that must be activated; 5% on Chase Travel; 3% on dining and drugstores; 1% elsewhere. https://creditcards.chase.com/cash-back-credit-cards/freedom/flex
- UNCONFIRMED: a Chase page that says in so many words that points stay on the account through a product change (the guides read imply it); which cards a Reserve can be downgraded to (the names did not load in TPG's guide); whether Chase takes back a welcome bonus after an early downgrade.

## 8. Moving points between Chase cards

- Chase's own guide: points move between a person's own Chase cards that earn Ultimate Rewards and to one person who shares the same address; "you can only designate one person to share points with"; no minimum, no fee, no points lost; a transfer "cannot be undone". https://chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/rewards-benefits/how-to-combine-chase-credit-card-points-in-household
- AwardWallet (2026-09-07): only the primary cardholder can combine; any Sapphire, Freedom or Ink card, in either direction; a household member is linked the first time by phone; steps are Rewards details, then Combine points; there is no undo but points can be moved back between a person's own cards; move points off a card before closing it; the redemption value depends on the card holding the points. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/how-to-combine-chase-points/
- TPG (2026-07-02): transfers between a person's own cards are instant; Ink Business Premier points cannot be combined; Chase may suspend or close accounts for misuse such as selling points or sending them to ineligible people. https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/transfer-ultimate-rewards-points-between-accounts/
- What changed in 2025: points earned on, or combined to, a Sapphire card from 2025-10-26 get only the base 1 cent or a Points Boost in Chase Travel; 2025-10-25 was the last day to combine points onto a Reserve opened before 2025-06-23 and keep the 1.5-cent floor until 2027-10-26 (article dated 2025-10-25). https://frequentmiler.com/reminder-last-day-for-grandfathered-chase-sapphire-reserve-cardholders-to-preserve-value-by-combining-points/
- Your Mileage May Vary (2026-07-16): moving points to a Sapphire no longer raises their value on every Chase Travel booking; it gives Sapphire Preferred Boosts as up to 1.5 cents. https://yourmileagemayvary.com/2026/07/16/why-combine-chase-ultimate-rewards-points/
- Some people have found the online combine option or a destination card missing since late 2025; a phone agent can move the points (one source, undated, offers marked 2026-11-21). https://cloud9club.net/combine-chase-points/
- DISAGREE: Chase says a transfer cannot be undone; AwardWallet says points can be moved straight back between your own cards. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/how-to-combine-chase-points/
- DISAGREE: top Points Boost on the Preferred is 1.75 cents in AwardWallet's and TPG's 2026 guides and 1.5 cents in Your Mileage May Vary (same split as in `chase-deep.md`). https://yourmileagemayvary.com/2026/07/16/why-combine-chase-ultimate-rewards-points/
- UNCONFIRMED: Chase's own wording for the October 2025 rule (not on the terms page read); whether Freedom Flex charges a foreign transaction fee (the card page text read today said none, which conflicts with the 3% long reported for the Freedom cards and in `compare.md` for the Freedom Unlimited). https://creditcards.chase.com/cash-back-credit-cards/freedom/flex
