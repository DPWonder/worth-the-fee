## 2. PayPal Honey

- Honey is "a free browser extension that searches for deals"; it "tests coupon codes at checkout on 30,000+ popular sites" and applies a working code; sign-up by Google, PayPal or Apple; browsers named: Chrome, Safari, Opera, Edge (page updated November 4, 2026). https://help.joinhoney.com/article/39-what-is-the-honey-extension-and-how-do-i-get-it
- US users redeem points at PayPal checkout by connecting Honey and PayPal accounts, or redeem 1,000 points for a $10 gift card from participating stores. https://help.joinhoney.com/article/39-what-is-the-honey-extension-and-how-do-i-get-it
- PayPal Rewards is free; points are given on the subtotal of eligible items, not tax or shipping; not offered at every store Honey supports; turned on with the "Activate Rewards" button; post as pending within 2 to 14 days (page updated October 26, 2026). https://help.joinhoney.com/article/34-what-are-paypal-rewards
- "Using another rewards program will likely interfere with Honey"; Honey recommends disabling other rewards programs and ad blockers. https://help.joinhoney.com/article/34-what-are-paypal-rewards
- Points stay pending for approximately 60 to 90 days until the merchant confirms, sometimes more; pending points cannot be redeemed; returns reduce points; travel points are confirmed after the stay is completed (page updated April 9, 2026). https://help.joinhoney.com/article/13-pending-paypal-rewards
- Redeeming to PayPal: US only; points can be used "anywhere you use PayPal checkout or to donate to a charity"; needs a valid U.S. personal PayPal account in good standing whose primary or additional email matches the Honey email; a redemption cannot be canceled or changed (page updated April 10, 2026). https://help.joinhoney.com/article/347-redeeming-honey-gold-to-paypal
- Gift cards: e-gift card emailed within 24 hours; cannot be canceled or exchanged (page updated November 22, 2024). https://help.joinhoney.com/article/85-redeem-paypal-rewards-for-gift-cards
- Two rewards programs at once: "the credit can only be attributed to one of us", and Honey has no way of knowing which; a purchase credited to Honey cannot be moved to another program; transactions older than 90 days and not listed cannot be investigated (page updated April 10, 2026). https://help.joinhoney.com/article/35-missing-paypal-rewards
- Expiry: points expire unless at least 10 points are earned in each 365-day period, or the Honey account is linked to PayPal and 3 eligible PayPal transactions are completed in each 365-day period (page updated September 30, 2024). https://help.joinhoney.com/article/208-do-paypal-rewards-expire
- How Honey earns: commissions from merchant partners when a member uses Honey to find savings or activate PayPal Rewards; part is passed to members as points (page updated November 25, 2024). https://help.joinhoney.com/article/30-how-does-honey-make-money
- Name: "We're now PayPal Honey"; a PayPal account in good standing is needed for most redemption options (page updated October 26, 2026). https://help.joinhoney.com/article/374-what-is-paypal-honey
- PayPal help: no cost and no separate enrollment for PayPal account holders; points pending within 1 to 14 days and pending "for up to 90 days"; redeem "when checking out with PayPal at the same 100 points = $1 rate, for gift cards through PayPal Honey's gift card offering, or donate them"; points have no value until redeemed and may expire. https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/what-is-paypal-rewards-and-how-does-it-work-help958
- PayPal Rewards terms (last updated March 17, 2025): US residents aged 18 or over; redemption rate one cent per point unless stated; "There may be a minimum redemption threshold that will be disclosed to you at the time of redemption"; at checkout points can be redeemed in any whole number unless PayPal says otherwise; describes redemption for cash back to a PayPal account (up to 24 hours) and into PayPal Savings; for points earned through Honey, the Honey account must be linked to PayPal to redeem for cash back; linked accounts cannot be unlinked. https://www.paypal.com/us/legalhub/paypal/pp-rewards-program-tnc
- DISAGREE: PayPal's terms (March 17, 2025) describe cash back to a PayPal account; PayPal's help page read today lists only PayPal checkout, Honey gift cards and donations, and Honey's redemption page lists PayPal checkout and donation. https://www.paypal.com/us/legalhub/paypal/pp-rewards-program-tnc and https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/what-is-paypal-rewards-and-how-does-it-work-help958
- UNCONFIRMED: a search summary of a PayPal notice says redeeming points for cash to a PayPal balance, bank or debit card was to end at 11:59 PM Pacific on August 1, 2026; the page returned no article text today. https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/updates-to-us-paypal-rewards-program-help1355 (search)

Appearing as pending: Honey 2 to 14 days, PayPal 1 to 14 days. https://help.joinhoney.com/article/13-pending-paypal-rewards and https://www.paypal.com/us/cshelp/article/what-is-paypal-rewards-and-how-does-it-work-help958
