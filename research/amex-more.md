## 1. Hotel credit ($600)

- Amex's page: up to $300 semi-annually, up to $600 per calendar year, on prepaid Fine Hotels + Resorts and The Hotel Collection bookings through American Express Travel, and prepaid Airbnb Luxe bookings made through Amextravel.com or the Amex Travel app. Two-night minimum for The Hotel Collection and Airbnb Luxe. From 2027-01-01 Fine Hotels + Resorts also needs two nights. https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/get-inspired/Platinum-Card-600-Dollar-Hotel-Credit
- The half-years are January to June and July to December (article dated 2026-05-27). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-hotel-credit/
- Same half-year dates, second source (modified 2026-05-20). https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-card-hotel-credit/
- The half-year is set by the booking date, not the stay date: "based on when you make your booking and not when you stay". https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-card-hotel-credit/
- TPG: the stay need not be finished by June 30; it can be later in the year or the next year because the booking is prepaid. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-hotel-credit/
- Prepaid does not have to mean non-refundable; many prepaid rates can be canceled under the hotel's policy. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-card-hotel-credit/
- Pay Later and Pay at Hotel bookings do not count; booking is by AmexTravel.com, the app or the number on the card. https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/benefits/how-to-use-hotel-credit/ (search)
- No registration needed for the hotel credit (TPG). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-hotel-credit/
- DISAGREE inside One Mile at a Time: its introduction says enrollment is required, a later section says no registration is required. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-card-hotel-credit/
- Spending under $300 returns only what was spent; several bookings in one half can add up. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-card-hotel-credit/
- Additional cardholders can book and trigger the credit, but there is one $600 per account. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-hotel-credit/
- Posting: Amex says up to 90 days; TPG says about three days on average. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-hotel-credit/
- The credit "may be reversed if the booking is canceled or modified"; a new credit may not come until the next half-year. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-hotel-credit/
- The Business Platinum has the same credit. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-card-hotel-credit/
- Airbnb Luxe: on Amex's page; TPG's and One Mile at a Time's guides do not mention it. Treated as confirmed by the issuer page only. https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/travel/get-inspired/Platinum-Card-600-Dollar-Hotel-Credit
- UNCONFIRMED: whether taxes and resort fees inside the prepaid total count toward the $300; whether a booking paid partly with points counts. A reader comment says some bookings prepay only 50% to 75% of the stay (not verified). https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/american-express/amex-platinum-card-hotel-credit/

## 2. lululemon credit ($300)

- Up to $75 per quarter, $300 per calendar year; quarters are Jan-Mar, Apr-Jun, Jul-Sep, Oct-Dec; no rollover; enrollment required; US lululemon retail stores (excluding outlets) and lululemon.com; launched September 2025 (updated 2026-11-11). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/lululemon-credit/
- Terms as quoted by AwardWallet: "Not valid on purchases shipped outside the U.S. or gift card purchases." Enrollment is by the basic cardmember; purchases can be on any card on the enrolled account; credits can take up to 8 weeks, most post in days (article dated 2025-10-17). https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-membership-rewards/amex-platinum-lululemon-credit/
- Doctor of Credit (post 2026-09-25, update 2026-10-13): only regular physical goods trigger the credit; outlets and stores outside the US do not; Apple Pay in store works; online e-gift cards never worked. https://www.doctorofcredit.com/maximizing-the-lululmen-300-credit-on-american-express-platinum/
- DISAGREE on gift cards: the terms exclude them; Doctor of Credit's body text says physical gift cards bought in store had been triggering the credit, and its 2026-10-13 update says gift card purchases no longer work. https://www.doctorofcredit.com/maximizing-the-lululmen-300-credit-on-american-express-platinum/
- Sale items ("We Made Too Much") bought at lululemon.com are suggested as a use; no rule against sale items was found. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/amex-membership-rewards/amex-platinum-lululemon-credit/
- One lululemon credit per account, shared with additional cardholders (already in `amex-deep.md`). https://thepointsguy.com/guide/why-add-authorized-users-amex-platinum/
- UNCONFIRMED: whether the lululemon app counts; what happens to the credit when an item is returned; whether the Business Platinum has it (no source lists it there).

## 5. Approval, credit score, Apply With Confidence

- "American Express does not disclose a minimum credit score requirement." Upgraded Points suggests a FICO score of 670 or higher and says income of $50,000 or more may improve the odds; neither is an Amex rule (updated 2026-07-01). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/credit-score-requirements/
- Amex can pull from all three bureaus; applicants most often report Experian. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/credit-score-requirements/
- CNBC Select (updated 2026-11-12): "good to excellent" credit, no number; no published income minimum; Amex also weighs income and number of open cards; a pop-up tells you if you do not qualify for the welcome offer; a declined applicant can call Amex's reconsideration line. https://www.cnbc.com/select/amex-platinum-credit-score/

## 6. Pay Over Time (Card Member Agreement read directly)

All lines in this section are from the Platinum Card Member Agreement, rates table "As of: 09/18/2025", unless marked. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf

- Annual fee $895. APR for Pay Over Time: Prime Rate + 12.74% to Prime Rate + 21.74%, variable. Cash advance APR: Prime Rate + 21.99%. Penalty APR: Prime Rate + 26.74%, for at least 6 months, on new Pay Over Time transactions after a late or returned payment. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- Late payment fee up to $40; returned payment fee up to $40; cash advance fee $10 or 5%; no foreign transaction fee. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- "Pay Over Time has two settings: active and inactive." It can be changed online or by phone. When inactive, purchases and the annual fee go to the Pay In Full balance, due in full each month. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- When active, eligible charges (and an eligible annual fee) are added automatically to the Pay Over Time balance up to the Pay Over Time Limit; a charge that would exceed the limit goes to Pay In Full. Each month you can pay the full new balance, the minimum, or anything between. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- Amount due each month = Pay In Full new balance + Pay Over Time minimum due + any plan payment. The minimum due on Pay Over Time is the highest of $40, 2% of the balance, or a tiered formula. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- No interest on charges added automatically if the Account Total New Balance is paid by the due date each month; the due date is at least 25 days after the billing period closes. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- Not eligible for Pay Over Time: cash advances; Amex may change eligibility and may suspend the feature based on creditworthiness or account status. Charges subject to a foreign transaction fee are not moved at closing. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- Plan It: a fixed monthly plan fee of up to 1.33% of each purchase placed in a plan; a qualifying purchase is "at least a specified dollar amount" (the amount is not in the agreement). https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- Spending: Amex decides whether to approve each charge from spending and payment history, credit history and known resources; an account has a preset limit only if page 1 shows one. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- Credit reporting: Amex gives information about the account to credit reporting agencies and will report a failure to comply with the agreement. https://www.americanexpress.com/content/dam/amex/en-us/company/legal/cardmember-agreements/public-site-adhoc-2025-09-18/platinum-card-09-18-2025.pdf
- Amex wording seen in search: no preset spending limit "does not mean unlimited spending"; charges above the Pay Over Time Limit are due in full. https://www.americanexpress.com/en-us/benefits/payment-flexibility/ (search)
- Upgraded Points (updated 2026-08-20): the Platinum has no preset spending limit but a flexible one; Amex's spending power tool says whether a given purchase would be approved; a charge card traditionally requires payment in full each month. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/amex-no-preset-limit/
- TPG (2025-03-21): the Pay Over Time limit is not reported to the credit bureaus; the feature can be switched off online or by phone, effective at once; Plan It on personal cards is for purchases over $100; the limit can be raised on request. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/american-express/your-ultimate-guide-to-amex-pay-over-time/
- UNCONFIRMED: whether Pay Over Time is set to active by default on a new Platinum account; a minimum charge size for Pay Over Time itself (none is in the agreement); whether a newer agreement than 09/18/2025 changes the rates; today's Prime Rate was not looked up.

## 7. Delta Sky Club (Delta's page read directly)

- 10 Visits per Medallion Year (February 1 to January 31) for US Platinum and Business Platinum cardmembers, basic and additional each; Visits do not roll over, are not transferable and cannot be used for guests; Companion Platinum Cards are not eligible; rules in effect since 2025-02-01. https://www.delta.com/us/en/delta-sky-club/access
- One Visit = entries at one or more airports for up to 24 hours from the first Club entry or Grab and Go use, covering departure, connecting and arrival airports. https://www.delta.com/us/en/delta-sky-club/access
- Flights: same-day Delta-operated flight (Delta or Delta Connection) or a Delta-marketed flight operated by WestJet (ticket number starting 006), departing from or arriving at that airport; Delta Main Basic (basic economy) fares get no access; partner lounges not included. https://www.delta.com/us/en/delta-sky-club/access
- Departing passengers may enter only within 3 hours of scheduled departure, or any time during a layover. https://www.delta.com/us/en/delta-sky-club/access
- Guests: up to two guests or immediate family at $50 per person per Visit, paid with the Platinum Card; children under 2 free. Extra Visits after the 10: $50 per person. Grab and Go: $25 per person. https://www.delta.com/us/en/delta-sky-club/access
- Unlimited Visits after $75,000 in eligible purchases in a calendar year; additional cardmembers' purchases count; takes about a week and up to 12 weeks to start; returns that drop spend below $75,000 can revoke it. https://www.delta.com/us/en/delta-sky-club/access
- DISAGREE on how long unlimited access lasts: Delta's page (as read) says the rest of that calendar year and through January 31 of the next calendar year; TPG (2026-08-18) says through the end of the next program year. https://thepointsguy.com/guide/american-express-platinum-lounge-access/
- Second source for 10 visits, $50 guests and extra visits, $25 Grab and Go, immediate family = spouse or domestic partner and children under 21. https://thepointsguy.com/guide/american-express-platinum-lounge-access/
- After a Grab and Go use, no other Grab and Go or Sky Club entry at the same airport for three hours (article dated 2026-06-29). https://thepointsguy.com/loyalty-programs/ultimate-guide-delta-sky-club-access/
- Arrival: entry after landing on a Delta flight works on domestic arrivals; international arrivals exit through customs (updated 2026-06-09). https://upgradedpoints.com/news/delta-sky-clubs-on-arrival-amex-platinum/
- The earlier DISAGREE in `amex.md` on the guest price ($29 at Upgraded Points) is settled: Upgraded Points (updated 2026-11-11) now says $50, matching Delta. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/american-express-platinum-card/airport-lounge-access/
