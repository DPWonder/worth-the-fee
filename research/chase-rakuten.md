## 3. Chase eligibility rules

- 5/24: unofficial (Chase has never published it). Chase generally declines most of its cards if you opened five or more personal cards, from any bank, in the past 24 months. Both Sapphire cards are subject to it. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/ultimate-guide-chase-5-24-rule/
- What counts: personal cards from any bank, even if since closed; authorised-user cards on your report; Discover, TD and most Capital One business cards. Most other business cards and denied applications do not count. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/ultimate-guide-chase-5-24-rule/
- Sapphire bonus rule now: one welcome bonus per Sapphire card, once in a lifetime, only if you never earned the bonus on that exact card. Holding the other Sapphire card does not block it. In force since 2026-01-22. https://thepointsguy.com/news/chase-sapphire-bonus-eligibility-changes-2026
- History: before June 2025 a new bonus was possible 48 months after the last one; that is gone. From June 2025 to January 2026, holding one Sapphire blocked a first bonus on the other; reversed in January 2026. https://thepointsguy.com/news/chase-sapphire-bonus-eligibility-changes-2026
- DISAGREE (minor): the start date of the January 2026 change is given as 22 January by TPG and 25 January in another summary. https://www.cnbc.com/select/chase-sapphire-welcome-bonus-rules-what-to-know/ (search)
- Chase's Reserve page: the card is not available if you already have it open, and the bonus may not be available if you held the card or got its bonus before. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve
- Chase shows a pop-up before the credit check telling an applicant if they will not get the bonus. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-preferred-lifetime-bonus-popup/ (search)

## 7. Rakuten (US)

### How it works and when it pays
- You click through Rakuten (site, app or browser extension) to a store, buy, and the store reports the order. Cash back shows as processing for 24 to 48 hours, then pending for typically 3 to 17 weeks, then confirmed. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/tracking-your-cash-back-360002117107
- Confirmation times: online and in-store 3 to 14 weeks; dining 3 to 5 days; travel 6 to 17 weeks after the trip ends. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/why-didnt-i-earn-cash-back-360036367393
- Payment dates: February 15, May 15, August 15, November 15. Only cash back confirmed before the date is included. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/when-will-i-get-paid-360002117667
- Payment methods: PayPal, cheque ("Big Fat Check"), Bilt points, Samsung Cash, or Amex Membership Rewards points. PayPal and cheque need a mailing address on file. Gift cards are another option from $5 confirmed. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/when-will-i-get-paid-360002117667
- Minimum: DISAGREE between Rakuten's own pages. One says at least $5; another says at least $5.01 (a balance of $5.00 or less carries over). Treat it as "more than $5". https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/when-will-i-get-paid-360002117667 and https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/earning-rewards-with-the-rakuten-card-41497337630611 (search)
- Quarter cut-offs (January to March paid May 15, April to June paid August 15, July to September paid November 15, October to December paid February 15) come from a third party; Rakuten's page only says "confirmed before the payment date". https://onemileatatime.com/guides/amex-points-rakuten/

### Amex Membership Rewards instead of cash
- Rate: 1 point per cent of cash back ($1 = 100 points; a 10% offer = 10 points per dollar). https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/american-express-membership-rewards-points-faq-360017609253 and https://onemileatatime.com/guides/amex-points-rakuten/
- Who qualifies: a US Amex card enrolled in Membership Rewards, open and not past due. Named examples: Gold, Green, Platinum. Not eligible: corporate cards and the Rakuten American Express Card. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/american-express-membership-rewards-points-faq-360017609253
- You do not need to pay with the Amex card; any card works at checkout. https://onemileatatime.com/guides/amex-points-rakuten/
- How to switch: Account Settings, change payment method to Membership Rewards points, then sign in to the Membership Rewards account to link it. New members can pick it at sign-up. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/american-express-membership-rewards-points-faq-360017609253
- Transfer dates are the same four: February 15, May 15, August 15, November 15. Minimum 501 confirmed points; 500 or fewer carry over. Points show in the Amex account as "Rakuten Additional MR Points". https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/american-express-membership-rewards-points-faq-360017609253
- If you replace or close the linked card you must relink. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/american-express-membership-rewards-points-faq-360017609253
- Offers from card issuers other than Amex may not be available once you pick points. You cannot get both cash and points on one purchase. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/american-express-membership-rewards-points-at-rakuten-terms-360017609913
- DISAGREE: existing balance. Rakuten says confirmed cash back from before the switch is converted to points; One Mile at a Time says existing rewards are not converted. Rakuten's own page is the better source. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/american-express-membership-rewards-points-faq-360017609253 and https://onemileatatime.com/guides/amex-points-rakuten/
- DISAGREE / UNCONFIRMED: switching back to cash. TPG (August 2025) says the choice is irreversible; the same page also says you can always change your preference. Rakuten's two help pages say nothing either way. https://thepointsguy.com/news/earn-amex-points-with-rakuten
- Value of a point: One Mile at a Time uses 1.7 cents; TPG about 2 cents (August 2025). Both assume transfers to airline partners. https://onemileatatime.com/guides/amex-points-rakuten/ and https://thepointsguy.com/news/earn-amex-points-with-rakuten

### New-member bonus through a friend's link
- Rakuten's referral page: friend joins and makes qualifying purchases totalling at least $50 within 90 days of signing up; the referrer gets $50; "the purchase requirement and reward amount can vary"; offer period ends 11:59 PM PT 2026-12-31 and can be changed or cancelled. https://www.rakuten.com/referral
- Friend's amount: $50 according to a July 2026 third-party guide. The Rakuten referral page text I read did not state the friend's figure. UNCONFIRMED on Rakuten's page. https://onemileatatime.com/guides/amex-points-rakuten/
- A friend gets the bonus that was on offer the day they signed up. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/referafriend-program-faq-360002117087
- Qualifying purchase: one that earns cash back through rakuten.com, the app or the extension. Amazon.com purchases do not count (except music.amazon.com). https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/referral-program-terms-360002101348
- The friend must be new to Rakuten, a real person in the 50 states, DC or Canada, must sign up through the referrer's link, and must be the one who pays. Self-referral is banned; accounts sharing a name, address or email can be flagged. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/referral-program-terms-360002101348
- The referral fails if the purchase is cancelled, returned or exchanged, or earns no cash back. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/referral-program-terms-360002101348 (search)
- Bonus is confirmed only after the purchase's cash back is confirmed (typically 3 to 14 weeks), then paid on the next payment date. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/referafriend-program-faq-360002117087
- To be paid you also need a payment method chosen and a mailing address on file. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/welcome-bonus-new-member-faqs
- The ordinary (non-referral) welcome bonus is separate: $25 of qualifying purchases within 90 days, one per new member, and it cannot be combined with the referral sign-up bonus. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/welcome-bonus-terms
- DISAGREE: spend needed. $50 on the referral page; $25 in the ordinary welcome-bonus terms; $30 in an older TPG article. They are different offers; the referral page governs a friend's link. https://www.rakuten.com/referral and https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/welcome-bonus-terms and https://thepointsguy.com/news/earn-amex-points-with-rakuten
- DISAGREE: bonus as points. Rakuten's welcome terms say the bonus is added to "your next Cash Back payment or points transfer"; TPG (2025) says the sign-up bonus stays as cash. Whether a $50 bonus becomes 5,000 points: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/welcome-bonus-terms and https://thepointsguy.com/news/earn-amex-points-with-rakuten
- Gift-card purchases toward the $50: UNCONFIRMED in the referral terms, but Rakuten says most gift-card purchases earn no cash back, and a purchase must earn cash back to qualify. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/why-didnt-i-earn-cash-back-360036367393

### Why cash back fails to track
- Excluded items or categories; a lower rate for that category; coupon codes not from Rakuten; ad blockers, VPNs or cookie blockers; declined cookies; another rewards extension or coupon site taking over the click; a long idle session or checking out in a different tab; activating after items are already in the cart; a store the extension does not support; returns, exchanges, cancellations; most gift-card purchases; taxes and shipping; orders by phone or chat. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/why-didnt-i-earn-cash-back-360036367393
- Fix: wait out the normal confirmation window, then file the Missing Cash Back form at rakuten.com/help/missing-cash-back. Filing deadline: UNCONFIRMED (not on the help page). https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/why-didnt-i-earn-cash-back-360036367393
