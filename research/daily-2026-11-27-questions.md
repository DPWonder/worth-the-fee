## 2. Rakuten: two people in one household, and referring each other

- The words household, spouse and roommate do not appear in the terms, the referral program terms or the referral FAQ; "same device" and IP address do not appear in the terms or the referral program terms (text searched today). Whether a referral between two people at one address is paid: NOT CONFIRMED. https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/referral-program-terms-360002101348
- Referral program terms (updated 10/8/2024): the program is offered to members "referring friends and/or family". A Qualified Referral must be a natural person, a new member, sign up by the referrer's link or other supplied method, make purchases that earn Cash Back within 90 days of sign-up (Amazon.com excluded other than music.amazon.com), comply with the terms, and "Be the individual who pays for the Qualifying Purchase." https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/referral-program-terms-360002101348

- Referral program terms, section 7 (not permitted, non-exhaustive): self-referral; making purchases on behalf of another or under a Rakuten account that is not yours; creating fake accounts; "Multiple accounts created with the same name, address, email address or other identifying feature may be flagged as fraudulent referrals." https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/referral-program-terms-360002101348

## 3. Chase Sapphire rental cover and Turo

- Sapphire Preferred Guide to Benefits, form BGC11387, "in effect as of 10/01/24" (PDF read directly today): Auto Rental Coverage reimburses theft or collision damage up to $60,000; primary inside and outside the United States; for New York residents inside the United States it is primary unless the cardholder has personal auto insurance, then excess; rental periods up to thirty-one (31) consecutive days. https://static.chasecdn.com/content/services/structured-document/document.en.pdf/card/benefits-center/product-benefits-guide-pdf/BGC11387_v2.pdf
- Same guide, exclusions: "Vehicles that are not rented from a Rental Agency" and "The Rental Vehicle is rented through a car sharing company that allows individuals to rent out their own vehicle". https://static.chasecdn.com/content/services/structured-document/document.en.pdf/card/benefits-center/product-benefits-guide-pdf/BGC11387_v2.pdf
- Same guide, definition: "Rental Agency - a commercial rental company licensed under the laws of the applicable jurisdiction and whose primary business is renting automobiles". https://static.chasecdn.com/content/services/structured-document/document.en.pdf/card/benefits-center/product-benefits-guide-pdf/BGC11387_v2.pdf
- Same guide, vehicles not covered: exotic vehicles or any with MSRP over $125,000; antique cars (over 20 years old or not manufactured for 10 years or more); cargo vans; trucks other than pick-ups; motorcycles, mopeds, motorbikes; limousines and vehicles rented with a driver; recreational vehicles; passenger vans seating more than 12 including the driver. Also excluded: leases and mini leases, personal liability, injury to anyone, loss of personal belongings. https://static.chasecdn.com/content/services/structured-document/document.en.pdf/card/benefits-center/product-benefits-guide-pdf/BGC11387_v2.pdf
- Same guide, claims: report at chasecardbenefits.com or 1-800-350-1362 as soon as reasonably possible and no later than 100 days from the incident; documents within 365 days; the insurer is Virginia Surety Company, Inc., an Assurant company. https://static.chasecdn.com/content/services/structured-document/document.en.pdf/card/benefits-center/product-benefits-guide-pdf/BGC11387_v2.pdf
- DISAGREE with an earlier fact sheet: `chase-deep.md` and the rental page carry 20 days to give notice and 90 days for documents (from a general guide); the Guide to Benefits read today says 100 days and 365 days for Auto Rental Coverage. https://static.chasecdn.com/content/services/structured-document/document.en.pdf/card/benefits-center/product-benefits-guide-pdf/BGC11387_v2.pdf
- DISAGREE (Chase's own pages): the web guide says rentals of less than 31 consecutive days, vans with more than nine passengers and vehicles more than 10 years old are excluded; the Guide to Benefits says up to 31 days, vans seating more than 12, and antique cars over 20 years old. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/chase-sapphire-rental-car-insurance-guide
- Whether BGC11387 (10/01/24) is the newest Sapphire Preferred guide: NOT CONFIRMED (no later version was found; other guessed file names returned 404). https://static.chasecdn.com/content/services/structured-document/document.en.pdf/card/benefits-center/product-benefits-guide-pdf/BGC11387_v2.pdf
- Chase web guide, re-read today (covers Preferred and Reserve): "Vans with more than nine passengers—as well as peer-to-peer and hourly car rentals—are not covered."; Sapphire Reserve covers up to $75,000 and does not exclude expensive or exotic vehicles; Turo is not named. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/chase-sapphire-rental-car-insurance-guide
- Sapphire Reserve Guide to Benefits: NOT CONFIRMED directly (PDF not found). The Points Guy quotes it as excluding "vehicles that are not rented from a rental agency" with the same Rental Agency definition. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/turo-car-rental-insurance/
- The Points Guy (published June 17, 2021): its writer called the number on a Sapphire Preferred and reports a benefits specialist said Turo rentals are excluded unless the booking is with a commercial car rental agency and the contract names the commercial company; American Express excludes vehicle sharing or peer-to-peer arrangements. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/turo-car-rental-insurance/

- Turo help (updated May 4, 2026): it is very unlikely a credit card covers damage to a car booked through Turo, and less likely it covers liability; Turo is a peer-to-peer car sharing platform, not a rental car company; Turo cannot advise on a card's terms. https://help.turo.com/en_us/insurance-or-coverage-via-a-credit-card-rklgH4g45.md
- Turo protection plans for US guests (updated October 21, 2026): maximum out of pocket to Turo for physical damage is $0 on Premier, $500 on Standard, $3,000 on Minimum; mechanical and interior damage are not limited on Standard and Minimum; Premier is not available to drivers under 21, on some vehicles over $25,000 or any over $60,000; all plans carry state-minimum third-party liability; optional supplemental liability up to $300,000 for US residents in some states; declining protection leaves the guest owing all physical, mechanical and interior damage plus administrative claims fees and appraisal costs, with state-minimum liability secondary to personal insurance; plans are contracts with Turo, not insurance. https://help.turo.com/en_us/summary-and-cost-of-protection-plans-or-us-guests-BJSgBNgVq.md
- Turo help (updated May 4, 2026): third-party liability insurance is issued to Turo by Travelers Excess and Surplus Lines Company; a guest's personal insurance, if it applies, is primary to protection plan benefits. https://help.turo.com/en_us/understanding-insurance-and-your-physical-damage-contract-or-us-guests-BkXlH4xN9.md
