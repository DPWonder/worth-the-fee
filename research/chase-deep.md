## 1. Sapphire Reserve credits (cadence and limits)

- $300 travel credit: applied automatically to travel purchases; runs on the account anniversary year (account open date through the first statement after the anniversary); purchases covered by it earn no points. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve
- The Edit credit: up to $250 per prepaid "Pay Now" booking, up to $500 per calendar year, two-night minimum; from 1/1/2026 the two $250 credits can be used at any time in the year (no longer one per half-year); purchases covered earn no points. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve and https://thepointsguy.com/news/chase-sapphire-bonus-eligibility-changes-2026
- Dining credit: up to $150 January to June and $150 July to December at restaurants in Sapphire Reserve Exclusive Tables on OpenTable; third-party delivery and payment-processor purchases do not qualify. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve
- StubHub / viagogo: up to $150 per half-year, through 12/31/2027; one-time activation; gift cards and stubhub.co.uk excluded. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve
- DoorDash: DashPass (Chase states a $120 value for 12 months), activate by 12/31/2029; monthly promos of $15 off one restaurant order and two $10 off non-restaurant orders, through 12/31/2029; promos apply to the subtotal only and expire at month end. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve
- Lyft: up to $10 a month in ride credits through 9/30/2027; not for fees, taxes, tips or gift cards. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve
- Peloton: up to $10 a month on eligible memberships through 12/31/2027; activation required. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve
- Apple TV and Apple Music: at least 12 months each, Chase's stated value $324 a year combined; activate by 6/22/2027. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve
- Global Entry / TSA PreCheck / NEXUS: one credit up to $120 every four years; third-party application services do not qualify. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve
- One-time 2026 hotel credit: up to $250 for a prepaid stay of two nights or more at selected Chase Travel hotel brands (reported: IHG, Omni, Montage, Pendry, Virgin Hotels, Minor Hotels, Pan Pacific), 2026 only. https://frequentmiler.com/new-sapphire-reserve-benefit-for-2026-a-250-credit-for-select-chase-travel%E2%84%A0-hotels/ (search) and https://thepointsguy.com/news/chase-sapphire-bonus-eligibility-changes-2026
- UNCONFIRMED: that 2026 hotel credit is not on Chase's public card page read today; a search summary gave a booking deadline of 2026-12-01, not confirmed on a Chase page. https://www.doctorofcredit.com/chase-sapphire-reserve-enhances-500-the-edit-credit-adds-one-time-250-hotel-credit/ (search)
- Existing Reserve cardholders got the June 2025 refresh benefits in October 2025. https://thepointsguy.com/news/chase-sapphire-bonus-eligibility-changes-2026
- Sum of recurring face values (my arithmetic from the lines above): 300 + 500 + 300 + 300 + 420 (DoorDash promos, $35 x 12) + 120 + 120 + 324 = $2,384 a year, before DashPass and the Global Entry credit.

## 2. Points Boost

- Points Boost began showing in Chase Travel on 2025-06-23. It gives more than 1 cent per point on a rotating set of flights and hotels; Chase publishes no fixed list. https://upgradedpoints.com/news/chase-travel-points-boost/
- Base value in Chase Travel without a Boost: 1 cent per point. Activities and cruises: 1 cent, no Boost. https://upgradedpoints.com/news/chase-travel-points-boost/ and https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-points-boost
- Sapphire Reserve, Sapphire Reserve for Business, J.P. Morgan Reserve: up to 2 cents per point (Chase: 100 points could be worth up to $2.00). https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve
- Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred: up to 1.5 cents on selected hotels and flights, up to 1.75 cents on premium-cabin fares with selected airlines. https://upgradedpoints.com/news/chase-travel-points-boost/ and https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-points-boost
- DISAGREE: top Boost on Sapphire Preferred. TPG (2026-09-21), Upgraded Points and NerdWallet say up to 1.75 cents on premium-cabin flights. Chase's Reserve page describes Boosts as "up to 1.5x or 2x depending on your card", and AwardWallet (2026-03-24) says the Preferred maximum "drops to 1.5 cents" from December 2025 while also listing 1.75. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve and https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/points-boost/
- Freedom Unlimited, Freedom Flex, Freedom: up to 1.1 cents on selected hotels, not on The Edit hotels; flights not currently available. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-points-boost
- Eligible fares: economy, premium economy, business and first; basic economy excluded. Boosts on domestic economy are uncommon; they show more on long-haul premium cabins and on The Edit hotels. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/points-boost/ and https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-points-boost
- TPG says 2-cent Boosts are harder to find than at launch. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-points-boost
- Old fixed rates: Sapphire Reserve 1.5 cents; Sapphire Preferred, Ink Business Preferred and Ink Plus 1.25 cents; other cards 1 cent. https://upgradedpoints.com/news/chase-travel-points-boost/
- Legacy rule, Reserve: approved before 2025-06-23, points earned before 2025-10-26 get the higher of the Boost or 1.5 cents, until 2027-10-26. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/points-boost/
- Legacy rule, Preferred: points earned before 2025-10-26 get the higher of the Boost or 1.25 cents, until 2027-10-26. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/points-boost/
- DISAGREE (minor): Upgraded Points applies the "account opened before 2025-06-23" condition to all cards; AwardWallet states it only for the Reserve. Doctor of Credit gives the end date as 2027-10-25, others 2027-10-26. https://upgradedpoints.com/news/chase-travel-points-boost/ and https://www.doctorofcredit.com/chase-sapphire-reserve-points-boost/ (search)
- UNCONFIRMED: the legacy-rate wording was not found on a Chase page read today.
- TPG valuation of Chase points, September 2026: 2.05 cents (assumes transfers to partners). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-points-boost

## 3. Chase to Hyatt transfers

- 4:3 cards: Sapphire Preferred, Ink Business Preferred, Ink Plus. 1:1 cards: Sapphire Reserve, Sapphire Reserve for Business, J.P. Morgan Reserve. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/chase-to-hyatt-transfer/
- Existing Sapphire Preferred holders (opened before 2026-06-15) moved from 1:1 to 4:3 on 2026-10-01. https://thepointsguy.com/news/chase-hyatt-transfer-ratio-change-october-1/
- Cardholders approved on or after 2026-06-15 got 4:3 from the start. https://www.nerdwallet.com/travel/news/chase-sapphire-preferred-hyatt-transfer-changes (search) and https://awardwallet.com/news/chase-ultimate-rewards/hyatt-transfer-rate-devaluation/ (search)
- The same number of Hyatt points costs 33% more Chase points at 4:3: 60,000 Hyatt points take 80,000 Chase points. https://thepointsguy.com/news/chase-hyatt-transfer-ratio-change-october-1/
- Only Hyatt changed; other partners stay 1:1 from the Preferred. https://www.nerdwallet.com/travel/news/chase-sapphire-preferred-hyatt-transfer-changes (search)
- Combining: points moved from a 4:3 card to your own Reserve account transfer to Hyatt at 1:1. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/chase-to-hyatt-transfer/ and https://thepointsguy.com/news/chase-hyatt-transfer-ratio-change-october-1/
- Transfers are instant in most cases and cannot be reversed; Hyatt points expire after 24 months with no qualifying activity. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/chase-to-hyatt-transfer/ and https://thepointsguy.com/news/chase-hyatt-transfer-ratio-change-october-1/
- UNCONFIRMED: minimum transfer size and increments (not stated in the sources read); the Hyatt ratio is not shown on Chase's public Preferred card page.

## 4. 5/24 detail

- TPG guide dated 2026-08-24. Cards it lists as subject to 5/24 include Sapphire Preferred, Sapphire Reserve, Sapphire Reserve for Business, the Freedom cards, the Ink cards, and the Chase airline and hotel cards. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/ultimate-guide-chase-5-24-rule/
- Capital One business cards that do not count: Venture X Business and Spark Cash Plus. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/ultimate-guide-chase-5-24-rule/
- Store cards on a national payment network count; some reports say single-store cards count too. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/ultimate-guide-chase-5-24-rule/
- Do not count: denied applications and inquiries; most business cards (Chase's own included); auto loans, student loans, mortgages. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/ultimate-guide-chase-5-24-rule/
- Chase business cards do not add to the count, but you generally must be under 5/24 to be approved for one. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/ultimate-guide-chase-5-24-rule/
- Product changes usually are not reported as new accounts; a hard pull or a new account number may mean it is treated as new. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/ultimate-guide-chase-5-24-rule/
- Timing: you are under 5/24 from the first day of the 25th month after the fifth account was opened (fifth card opened October 2024: apply from 2026-11-01). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/ultimate-guide-chase-5-24-rule/
- How to check: a free Experian credit report, sorted by date opened; count cards opened in the past 24 months, closed ones included. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/ultimate-guide-chase-5-24-rule/
- Authorized-user cards: Chase's reconsideration line may disregard them if asked; this is manual and may not work. Alternative: be removed as an authorized user at least a month before applying. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/ultimate-guide-chase-5-24-rule/
- Other reported patterns: no more than two new Chase accounts in 30 days; targeted "Just for you" offers have sometimes bypassed 5/24. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/ultimate-guide-chase-5-24-rule/
- UNCONFIRMED: whether charge cards count (not addressed); whether Chase Credit Journey shows enough to count (not mentioned).

## 5. Sapphire bonus eligibility

- Chase's wording on both card pages: "The new cardmember bonus may not be available to you if you previously held this card"; Chase may also consider the number of cards you have opened and closed. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/preferred and https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve
- Pop-up: appears as the application is submitted; says you may be approved but will not receive the bonus; you can cancel (TPG: no effect on your credit score) or continue without the bonus. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-preferred-lifetime-bonus-popup/
- A past bonus still counts if the card was later downgraded or closed. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-preferred-lifetime-bonus-popup/
- DISAGREE: TPG's January 2026 news says the test is whether you ever earned the bonus on that card. Chase's wording and TPG's July 2026 article say people who only held the card before may also be ineligible, even with no bonus. https://thepointsguy.com/news/chase-sapphire-bonus-eligibility-changes-2026 and https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-preferred-lifetime-bonus-popup/
- Product changes within the Chase points family may be possible after a year with the card and carry no welcome bonus unless targeted. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/ultimate-guide-chase-5-24-rule/
- UNCONFIRMED: how the rule treats Sapphire Reserve for Business; whether the pop-up shows before the credit check (the article says before proceeding and that cancelling does not affect the score).

## 6. Sapphire Preferred travel protections (detail and exclusions)

- Trip cancellation/interruption also has a cap of $40,000 per 12 months. Some or all of the trip must be charged to the card or paid with Chase points. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/chase-sapphire-preferred-card/travel-insurance-benefits and https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/chase-sapphire-travel-insurance/
- Covered people: cardholder and immediate family (broadly defined); traveling companions for some losses. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/chase-sapphire-travel-insurance/
- Covered reasons include injury, sickness or death of you, a companion or immediate family; severe weather and named storms; jury duty or subpoena; military orders; uninhabitable home or lodging; quarantine ordered by a physician; organized strikes; certain terrorist incidents. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/chase-sapphire-travel-insurance/
- Not covered: pre-existing conditions (advice, diagnosis, care or treatment in the 60 days before the first trip payment); trips over 60 days; travel after 26 weeks of pregnancy; trips for medical treatment; change of plans, finances or work obligations; missing passport, visa or vaccine papers; reluctance to travel, border closures; travel supplier insolvency; standalone event tickets not part of a package. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/chase-sapphire-travel-insurance/
- Trip delay: covered causes are equipment failure, weather, strike, hijacking; pays meals, lodging, toiletries, medication; Reserve trigger is 6 hours versus 12 hours or overnight on the Preferred. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/chase-sapphire-travel-insurance/
- Claims: notify within 20 days; documents within 90 days; chasecardbenefits.com. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/chase-sapphire-travel-insurance/
- Rental car: primary; up to $60,000; vehicles with MSRP of $125,000 or less; for New York residents cover inside the US is secondary to personal auto insurance. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/preferred
- Rental car conditions: pay with the card and decline the rental company's collision waiver; rental of 31 consecutive days or fewer; covers listed additional drivers, loss-of-use and towing. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/chase-sapphire-preferred-card/travel-insurance-benefits
- Rental car exclusions: antique cars, cargo vans, trucks other than pickups, motorcycles, mopeds, limousines, RVs, passenger vans seating more than 12, car-sharing such as Turo; liability to other people or property; personal belongings; off-road use; driving under the influence. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/chase-sapphire-preferred-card/travel-insurance-benefits
- Lost luggage is excess of what the carrier pays; New York residents: $2,000 per bag, $10,000 per trip. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/preferred and https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/chase-sapphire-preferred-card/travel-insurance-benefits
- Travel accident insurance: up to $500,000 common carrier, up to $100,000 24-hour. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/chase-sapphire-preferred-card/travel-insurance-benefits
- No emergency medical expense benefit is listed for the Preferred. Emergency evacuation up to $100,000 needs a trip of 5 to 60 days, more than 100 miles from home, arranged in advance by the benefit administrator; repatriation of remains up to $15,000. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/chase-sapphire-preferred-card/travel-insurance-benefits
- Guide to Benefits reported as effective 06/14/26 for the Preferred; not read directly. https://www.nerdwallet.com/travel/learn/the-chase-sapphire-preferred-card-travel-insurance-benefits-explained (search)
