# Fact sheet: daily batch 2026-11-22, Chase (StubHub credit; Sapphire Preferred credit score)

Checked 2026-11-22. Each line ends with its source URL.

## 1. Sapphire Reserve StubHub / viagogo credit

- Re-confirmed: up to $150 in statement credits January 1 to June 30 and up to $150 July 1 to December 31, maximum $300 a year, through 12/31/27; one-time activation on chase.com or the Chase app. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve
- Terms: purchases (excluding gift cards) made directly with StubHub and viagogo through their websites or apps, by "you or an authorized user of the account"; no minimum purchase required. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/reserve
- Terms: events in the UK are redirected to stubhub.co.uk (StubHub International, not affiliated) and do not qualify. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- Terms: periods end 11:59 PM ET on the last day; merchant processing delays can move a purchase into the next period; credits post within 3 business days but may take up to 4 weeks; credits may be reversed if the purchase is returned, canceled or modified or the account is closed within 90 days of a credit; account must be open and not in default. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- Terms cover the personal Sapphire Reserve and J.P. Morgan Reserve; the Sapphire Reserve for Business is not named. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- Chase's terms do not say whether taxes and fees count toward the credit. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire
- Chase's guide: activate first; purchases made before activation do not qualify; authorized users' purchases count; credit "typically" appears within 1 to 2 billing cycles; names sports, concerts and theater; parking not addressed. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/rewards-benefits/how-to-use-the-$300-stubhub-credit-with-chase-sapphire-reserve
- SOURCES DISAGREE on posting time: Chase terms say 3 business days up to 4 weeks; Chase's guide says 1 to 2 billing cycles; Doctor of Credit says the terms say 6 to 8 weeks and that most post within a few days; TPG reports about a day. https://doctorofcredit.com/maximizing-the-stubhub-300-credit-on-chase-sapphire-reserve-card
- TPG (2026-07-21): enrollment through the benefits hub; viagogo.com and app qualify; the $150 need not be spent in one purchase; no rollover; credit typically posts within a day in its data points; a June 30 purchase posted July 1 and counted in the second half; gift cards excluded. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-reserve-stubhub-credit-examples/
- Doctor of Credit (reposted 2026-06-22): taxes and fees are credited (example: $130 ticket, $151 with fees, full $150 credit); gift cards do not trigger the credit; reader reports that PayPal payments triggered it and that activating after purchase sometimes worked (conflicts with Chase's guide); sellers pay 10 to 15% fees. https://doctorofcredit.com/maximizing-the-stubhub-300-credit-on-chase-sapphire-reserve-card
- AwardWallet (2026-10-25): activation path is Benefits and travel > Benefits > "$300 StubHub Credit" tile; no rollover; examples use order totals with fees. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/chase-ultimate-rewards/sapphire-reserve-stubhub-credit/
- Thrifty Traveler (modified 2026-08-18): StubHub purchases earn the card's usual 1x; Paze at StubHub earned 10x through the end of 2026. https://thriftytraveler.com/guides/credit-card/chase-sapphire-reserve-stubhub-credit/
- Upgraded Points (updated 2026-01-08): no minimum purchase; activation needed; stubhub.co.uk and other international sites do not qualify (it does not mention viagogo.com as an exception). https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/chase-sapphire-reserve/stubhub-credit/
- TPG credits guide (2026-05-26): one-time activation; viagogo under the same cap and end date 12/31/2027. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase-sapphire-reserve-credits/
- UNCONFIRMED: whether parking passes and other non-ticket items on StubHub count; whether fees and taxes count in Chase's own words; whether the Sapphire Reserve for Business has this credit. https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/offerdetails/chasesapphire

## 2. Sapphire Preferred: credit score and approval

- Chase's card page states no credit score, no minimum credit line and no income figure; "Subject to credit approval"; annual fee $95; links to a "Check for Offers" preapproval tool. https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/preferred
- Chase's preapproved-offers page: asks for basic information such as name and address; "Filling out this form will not impact your credit score, but applying for a credit card will impact your score"; does not say an offer guarantees approval. https://creditcards.chase.com/check-for-preapproved-offers
- TPG (2026-07-11): "Chase doesn't publish a minimum credit score requirement"; recommends FICO about 700 or higher; no disclosed income minimum; 5/24 applies and agents generally cannot override it; Chase may limit total credit across a customer's cards, and shifting credit from an existing card can help in reconsideration. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/chase/credit-score-needed-for-chase-sapphire-preferred/
- CNBC Select (updated 2026-10-09): at least 670, above 700 improves odds; factors are income, length of credit history, recent applications, the unofficial 5/24 rule; Chase's preapproval tool does not affect the score; a rejection can be contested by calling Chase. https://www.cnbc.com/select/chase-sapphire-preferred-card-credit-score/
- CAUTION: the same CNBC Select page still describes a 48-month Sapphire bonus rule, which conflicts with the once-per-lifetime rule on this site's eligibility page; not used. https://www.cnbc.com/select/chase-sapphire-preferred-card-credit-score/
- Upgraded Points (updated 2025-07-16): wait for at least 670; most cardholders 720 or better; approvals in the 600s and denials in the 800s; minimum limit for new cardholders $5,000; suggests income of $30,000 and 2 years of history; Chase may pull any of the three bureaus. https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/reviews/chase-sapphire-preferred-card/credit-score-requirements
- Motley Fool Money (published 2025-08-12): Chase publishes no cutoff; most approved applicants above 700; 680 to 699 approved less often; below 670 unlikely; low debt-to-income helps; two or more years of history suggested. https://www.fool.com/money/credit-cards/chase/chase-sapphire-preferred-card-review/chase-sapphire-preferred-credit-score
- FinanceBuzz (updated 2026-11-18): "good to excellent" credit, good starting at 670; minimum credit limit of at least $5,000, attributed to Visa Signature; reports of limits over $10,000 and $20,000. https://financebuzz.com/chase-sapphire-preferred-common-questions
- AwardWallet (2026-07-17): Chase personal reconsideration line 888-270-2127; approval not guaranteed; Chase approved a card after lowering credit on the caller's other cards; issuers typically do not override rules such as 5/24. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/reconsideration-call/
- SOURCES DISAGREE on the reconsideration number: a search summary showed LendingTree listing 888-609-7805 (page not read). https://www.lendingtree.com/credit-cards/articles/credit-card-reconsideration
- UNCONFIRMED: the $5,000 Visa Signature minimum line in Chase's own terms (only FinanceBuzz and Upgraded Points state it); any Chase limit of two new cards in 30 days (not in the sources read today); NerdWallet's listed score range (page returned 403). https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/preferred
