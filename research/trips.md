## 6. Checked bag and golf bag rules, US airlines

- Delta: $45 first bag, $55 second, $200 third on domestic and some short-haul international routes, from 2026-04-08. https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/travel/story/american-airlines-alaska-airlines-join-carriers-raising-bag-131890702
- Delta golf: one golf bag per passenger as a standard checked item; "Standard baggage charges apply"; contents are one set of clubs, balls and tees, one pair of shoes; over 50 lb pays the excess weight fee; over 115 linear inches or 100 lb is not accepted; hard-shell case, or a soft-sided travel bag after signing a limited release. https://www.delta.com/us/en/baggage/special-items/sporting-equipment
- Delta overweight fee $100 for 51 to 70 lb. UNCONFIRMED (golf blog, search only). https://bagboy.com/blogs/dynamic-brands-blog/flying-with-golf-clubs-airline-policies-fees-and-what-happens-if-they-get-damaged (search)

- United golf: one set of clubs in a hard-sided case counts toward the checked bag allowance; no oversize fee on the golf bag; overweight charges may apply; United is not liable for damage to golf equipment not in a hard-sided case. https://united.com/en/gb/fly/baggage/sports-equipment.html (search)
- American: tickets bought on or after 2026-04-09: first bag $45 prepaid or $50 at the airport; second $55 or $60; third $200 at the airport. Hooded or soft-sided cases travel only with a limited release of liability. https://support.southwest.com/helpcenter/article/flying-with-golf-clubs
