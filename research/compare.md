- $300 credit, second source: flights, hotels or rental cars booked through Capital One Travel (2026-10-03). https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/capital-one/capital-one-venture-x-card/
- UNCONFIRMED: whether the $300 credit resets on the account anniversary or the calendar year. Neither review states it and the issuer text did not load. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/capital-one/capital-one-venture-x-card/
- 10,000 bonus miles on each account anniversary, starting at the first anniversary; worth at least $100 at 1 cent a mile. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/capital-one/capital-one-venture-x-card/

One source for each: UNCONFIRMED by a second. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/capital-one/capital-one-venture-x-card/

## 2. Capital One Venture

- Annual fee $95. https://www.capitalone.com/credit-cards/venture/
- Earning: 2 miles per dollar on every purchase; 5 on hotels, vacation rentals and rental cars booked through Capital One Travel. https://www.capitalone.com/credit-cards/venture/
- Up to $120 credit for Global Entry or TSA PreCheck; Hertz Five Star status; transfers to "15+ travel loyalty programs". https://www.capitalone.com/credit-cards/venture/
- Miles can cover travel purchases made with the card in the past 90 days. https://www.capitalone.com/credit-cards/venture/
- That redemption is worth 1 cent a mile (article dated 2026-09-09). https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/capital-one-venture-card-review
- No airport lounge access; no foreign transaction fee. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/capital-one-venture-card-review
- No lounge access, second source; cash redemption is 0.5 cents a mile; travel accident insurance up to $250,000, lost luggage up to $3,000, extended warranty. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/capital-one-rewards/venture-card
- UNCONFIRMED: whether the Venture's rental car cover is primary or secondary (AwardWallet describes the cover but not the type); no trip cancellation or trip delay cover was listed in either review. https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/capital-one-rewards/venture-card
- Public offer on Capital One's page today: 75,000 miles after $4,000 in 3 months plus $300 for Capital One Travel stays in the first year. Not used as a promise. https://www.capitalone.com/credit-cards/venture/
- A bonus on a Venture or Venture X in the last 48 months can make an applicant ineligible. https://thepointsguy.com/credit-cards/capital-one-venture-card-review

## 4. Chase Freedom Unlimited

- Annual fee $0. Earning: 5% on Chase Travel, 3% on dining (takeout and eligible delivery included) and drugstores, 1.5% on everything else. https://creditcards.chase.com/cash-back-credit-cards/freedom/unlimited
- Rewards are points; cash back has no minimum and does not expire while the account is open. https://creditcards.chase.com/cash-back-credit-cards/freedom/unlimited
- 2% on Lyft through 2027-09-30; six months of DashPass if activated by 2029-12-31, then $10 off one non-restaurant order a quarter. https://creditcards.chase.com/cash-back-credit-cards/freedom/unlimited
- Foreign transaction fee 3%; points are worth 1 cent each as cash (article dated 2026-10-16). https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/chase/chase-freedom-unlimited/
- Travel booked outside Chase Travel earns 1.5%. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/chase/chase-freedom-unlimited/
- On its own the card cannot transfer to airline or hotel partners; points moved to a Sapphire Preferred, Sapphire Reserve or Ink Business Preferred held by the same person can. Hyatt is 4:3 from the Preferred. https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/chase/chase-freedom-unlimited/
- Protections: trip cancellation and interruption up to $1,500 per traveler and $6,000 per trip; purchase protection 120 days, $500 per item; extended warranty of one year. One source: UNCONFIRMED on Chase's page (not shown in the text that loaded). https://onemileatatime.com/reviews/credit-cards/chase/chase-freedom-unlimited/
- DISAGREE (minor): DashPass enrollment deadline is 12/31/2029 on Chase's page and 12/31/2027 in One Mile at a Time. https://creditcards.chase.com/cash-back-credit-cards/freedom/unlimited
- Public offer today: $200 after $500 in 3 months; not available to someone who has the card or got its bonus in the past 24 months. Not used as a promise. https://creditcards.chase.com/cash-back-credit-cards/freedom/unlimited

## 8. Own arithmetic used on the pages

- Venture X: $395 fee less $300 travel credit less 10,000 miles at 1 cent ($100) = minus $5. (sections 1)
- Sapphire Reserve less its $300 travel credit: $795 - $300 = $495. (chase-rakuten.md section 2)
- Preferred versus Venture points: 3D + 2T + O against 2(D + T + O); the Preferred is ahead only when 3x-category spend D is larger than 1x spend O. (sections 2; chase-rakuten.md section 1)
- Business Platinum credits with no spending threshold: 150 + 360 + 300 + 200 + 120 = $1,130. Shared credits: 600 + 200 + 219 = $1,019. Personal-only credits: 400 + 300 + 300 + 300 + 200 + 200 + 155 + 120 = $1,975. (section 6; amex.md section 2)
