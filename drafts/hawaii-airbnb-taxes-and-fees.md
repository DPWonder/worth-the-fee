A Hawaii host owes three taxes on short stays: an 11% state lodging tax, a county lodging tax of up to 3%, and 4.5% general excise tax. That is about 18.5% of the rent, and Airbnb's own fee, about 15.5% for most hosts, comes on top.

This page is general information, not legal or tax advice. Rates were checked on November 20, 2026. Confirm them with the Hawaii Department of Taxation and your county before you set a price.

## The table

| Charge | Rate | Who gets it | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transient Accommodations Tax (TAT) | 11% | State of Hawaii | Up from 10.25% on January 1, 2026. Set to run through December 31, 2030. |
| County TAT | 3% | Your county | 3% is the most state law allows. Confirmed on county pages for Kauai, Maui and Honolulu. For Hawaii County, check with the county. |
| General Excise Tax (GET) | 4.5% | State and county | 4% state plus a 0.5% county surcharge in all four counties. |
| Airbnb host fee | About 15.5% | Airbnb | Most hosts pay 15.5%. Others typically pay 14% to 16%. |

## Why the lodging tax went up in 2026

A 2025 state law, often called the green fee, raised the state TAT from 10.25% to 11% from January 1, 2026. The part of that law taxing cruise ships was blocked in court. The state tax department says the 11% rate on lodging is still in force.

TAT applies to stays of less than 180 consecutive days, whether or not you pass the tax on to your guest.

## Does Airbnb collect these for you?

No. Airbnb publishes a list of places where it collects and sends in lodging taxes. Hawaii is not on it. The host is responsible.

You need two state tax numbers before your first guest: a general excise tax licence and a TAT certificate of registration. You file and pay the state, and pay your county its TAT. Kauai, for example, wants payment by the 20th of the following month, plus a yearly reconciliation.

A 2026 state bill would have made booking platforms collect these taxes from 2027. One report says it failed. This was not confirmed on the legislature's site.

Honolulu has started mailing assessment notices for unpaid 2023 county TAT.

## Numbers that must be on your listing

State law requires any ad for a transient accommodation, including an online one, to show the TAT registration number or a link to it. A first citation can carry a $5,000 fine.

Counties add their own rules. On Oahu, an ad for stays under 30 days must also show the city registration or certificate number and the tax map key. Maui listings carry the tax map key and TAT number.

## The Airbnb fee

Airbnb is moving all home hosts to a single fee that comes out of the host's payout. Most hosts pay 15.5%. The rest typically pay 14% to 16%.

The older split fee, where most hosts paid 3% and the guest paid 14.1% to 16.5%, is being phased out. Airbnb gives no end date. Check your account to see which one you are on.

## Worked example: a $300 night

Assume one night at $300, no cleaning fee, a 3% county TAT and the single 15.5% Airbnb fee. The taxes are worked out on the $300 of rent.

| Line | Sum | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| State TAT | 11% of $300 | $33.00 |
| County TAT | 3% of $300 | $9.00 |
| GET | 4.5% of $300 | $13.50 |
| Total tax | 18.5% of $300 | $55.50 |
| Airbnb host fee | 15.5% of $300 | $46.50 |

If you take the taxes out of the $300 yourself, you keep $300 minus $55.50 minus $46.50, which is $198.00. That is 66% of the price.

If you add the taxes to the price instead, you keep about $253.50, and the guest pays more than $300. Two cautions. The most GET you may pass on is 4.712%, which is $14.14 on $300. And it is not confirmed whether Airbnb's fee also applies to tax a host adds to the price.

Both figures are before your other costs.

## Other costs to plan for

- **Cleaning.** No reliable statewide average was found. One Wailea condo programme lists $264 per stay for a one-bedroom and $314 for a two-bedroom, plus tax.
- **Management.** One Hawaii brokerage says resort front-desk programmes on Maui typically take 35% to 60% of gross rental revenue. One Maui manager publishes 20% for full service.
- **Property tax.** Maui County's short-term rental rate for fiscal 2026-2027 starts at $13.00 per $1,000 of assessed value.
- **County registration.** Hawaii County charges $250 hosted, $500 unhosted.

## Quick answers

**What is the total tax on a Hawaii Airbnb stay?**
About 18.5% of the rent: 11% state TAT, 3% county TAT and 4.5% general excise tax.

**Does Airbnb pay Hawaii taxes for hosts?**
No. Hawaii is not on Airbnb's list of places where it collects and remits tax. The host registers, files and pays.

**How much does Airbnb take from a Hawaii host?**
Most hosts on the single fee pay 15.5% of the booking. Some pay between 14% and 16%.
