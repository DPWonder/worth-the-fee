Sometimes. A Hawaii condo can be rented by the night only if its county allows short stays on that exact parcel, and each of the four counties draws that line differently. On Maui, many condos that are legal today lose that right in 2029 or 2031.

This page is general information, not legal or tax advice. The rules below were checked on November 20, 2026. They change often, so confirm your own unit with the county before you list or buy.

## The short version by county

| County | Where short stays are allowed | What you need | What is unsettled |
|---|---|---|---|
| Honolulu (Oahu) | Resort-zoned areas, and per one city page certain apartment-zoned areas. Elsewhere, stays must be 30 days or longer. | City registration, renewed each year, or an older nonconforming use certificate. | Two city pages describe the allowed zones differently. A 90-day minimum passed in 2022 was blocked in court. |
| Maui County | Hotel and resort zones. Apartment-zoned condos on the "Minatoya list" may continue only until the phase-out dates. | Listing must show the parcel number and state tax number. | Two lawsuits challenge the phase-out. Building-by-building rezoning is in progress. |
| Kauai | Inside a Visitor Destination Area. | Outside that area, only a property with an existing non-conforming use certificate. | Nothing major found. |
| Hawaii County (Big Island) | Resort and some commercial zones, and resort areas in the General Plan. | County registration, new in 2026: $250 hosted, $500 unhosted. | A second bill that would change the zones is still pending. |

## Oahu

Honolulu treats any stay under 30 consecutive days as a short-term rental. One city page allows these in resort-zoned areas and "certain apartment-zoned areas." The city's FAQ says resort-zoned areas only. Use the city's eligibility map, or pay $300 for a written zoning verification.

A legal unit must be registered with the city, one year at a time. Some older units instead hold a nonconforming use certificate from before October 22, 1986. No new certificates are issued.

An ad for stays under 30 days must show that number and the tax map key. An unregistered unit's ad must say it may not be rented for less than 30 consecutive days.

A 2022 law raised the minimum to 90 days outside resort areas. A federal judge blocked that part, and reports say the ruling protects rentals already operating. Whether it covers a new 30-day rental is not clear, so ask the city.

## Maui

Maui County passed Bill 9 in December 2025. It ends vacation rentals in apartment-zoned condos, about 7,000 units known as the Minatoya list, on January 1, 2029 in West Maui and January 1, 2031 elsewhere.

Until those dates, a Minatoya-list condo can still be rented. Condos in hotel and resort zones, permitted bed and breakfasts and timeshares are not affected.

Two things could change this:

- **Lawsuits.** Owners filed two cases in state court in December 2025. A real-estate site reported in early November 2026 that no court had paused the law. No later court news was found.
- **Rezoning.** In June 2026 the county created two hotel districts, H-3 and H-4. A building moved into one could keep renting, but each needs its own vote. The planning commission has recommended only 5 of the first 48 properties.

## Kauai

Kauai uses Visitor Destination Areas. Inside one, the county's 180-day limit does not apply. Outside one, renting a home or a room for less than 180 days is not permitted, and you cannot apply for permission.

The only exceptions are properties that already hold a non-conforming use certificate, and approved homestays. The county lists them by parcel number. The certificate is renewed every year, and a late renewal ends it for good.

Do not rely on a town name. Check the parcel with the county.

## Big Island

Hawaii County allows unhosted vacation rentals in resort and certain commercial zones, and in resort areas of its General Plan. Elsewhere, a rental needs an existing nonconforming use certificate.

Ordinance 25-50 adds registration for every rental of 180 days or less. It opened September 1, 2026, and runs through the end of 2026. The fee is $250 hosted and $500 unhosted, and the fine for not registering can reach $10,000. Registering does not change the zoning.

## What to check first

1. **Zoning.** Find the unit's tax map key and ask the county whether short stays are allowed on that parcel.
2. **Building rules.** Read your condo association's declaration, bylaws and house rules for a minimum-stay rule.
3. **Numbers.** Confirm the registration, permit or certificate number is current. On Oahu it goes on the listing.
4. **Dates.** On Maui, ask which phase-out date applies.

If the answer to step 1 is no, do not list the unit for short stays.

## Quick answers

**Can I rent my Oahu condo for a week?**
Only if it is in an eligible zone and registered with the city, or it holds an older nonconforming use certificate.

**Is my Maui condo still legal to rent?**
If it is on the Minatoya list, yes for now. That ends January 1, 2029 in West Maui and January 1, 2031 elsewhere, unless a court or a rezoning changes it.

**Does being on Airbnb mean a listing is legal?**
No. The county decides. Check the parcel with the county yourself.
