# Rakuten Ray-Ban cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-ray-ban-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Ray-Ban page showed up to 12% cash back, with a "was 4%" label: 12% on most items and 4% on Meta glasses. The one exclusion in the terms is gift cards, which earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## Rates seen on November 25, 2026

| Category on Rakuten's list | Rate |
|---|---|
| Meta glasses | 4% |
| All other non-excluded items | 12% |

The headline says "up to" because of the two tiers. The "was 4%" label means the top rate has been raised above the store's usual rate. The page does not say when 12% ends, and nothing on it promises the rate for Black Friday (November 27, 2026) or Cyber Monday (November 30, 2026).

The page does not show a "was" figure for each tier. What the Meta glasses rate is when the headline rate is back at 4% is not confirmed.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Ray-Ban are short. Listed as earning nothing:

- Gift cards

That is the full list. Sunglasses, eyeglasses and prescription lenses are not named, so they fall under "all other non-excluded items" at 12%. Rakuten's page says Ray-Ban accepts most vision insurance plans at checkout. The terms do not address orders paid in part with insurance. Whether the insured part earns is not confirmed.

## Coupon codes

The terms say coupon or promotional codes not listed on Rakuten "may void" cash back. On November 25, 2026, the page listed several offers with codes, including an extra 20% off selected styles for Black Friday weekend and Cyber Week offers of up to 50% off.

Rakuten's page also describes a refer-a-friend offer of $10 off a first purchase. The terms do not name it. Whether an order using it earns cash back is not confirmed.

## What it comes to

The $284 figure is the starting price for AI glasses in an offer on Rakuten's page on November 25, 2026. The other rows are round examples.

| Purchase | Price | Rate seen | Cash back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sunglasses | $180 | 12% | $21.60 |
| Eyeglasses with prescription lenses | $300 | 12% | $36.00 |
| Meta glasses | $284 | 4% | $11.36 |
| Gift card | Any | Excluded | $0 |

At the usual headline rate of 4%, the $180 sunglasses would earn $7.20. Taxes and shipping do not count, under Rakuten's general rules.


## Pickup, stores and returns

- **Pickup.** Rakuten's page says an order can be picked up free at a Ray-Ban retail store. The cash back terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The page showed an online rate only. Ray-Ban did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).
- **Returns.** Rakuten's general help page says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back.

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "tomorrow". Payment follows on Rakuten's schedule of four dates a year; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

## Who should skip it

- You are buying Meta glasses and expected the headline rate. They earn 4%.
- You are buying a gift card.
- You have a referral or email code that is not on Rakuten and it saves more than the cash back.
- Most of the order is paid by vision insurance. How that part is treated is not confirmed.
- You are counting on 12% for a later date. The usual rate shown is 4%.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Ray-Ban?**
Up to 12% on November 25, 2026, with a "was 4%" label. Rates change daily.

**Do Ray-Ban Meta glasses earn the top rate?**
No. Meta glasses were listed at 4%. All other non-excluded items were at 12%.

**Do prescription lenses earn cash back?**
The terms exclude only gift cards. Prescription lenses are not named as excluded.
