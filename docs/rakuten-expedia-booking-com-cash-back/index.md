# Does Rakuten give cash back on Expedia and Booking.com, and when does it pay out?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-expedia-booking-com-cash-back/
Facts checked: 2026-11-21
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, both are listed. On November 21, 2026 Rakuten showed Expedia at up to 3% and Booking.com at up to 8%, with flights at a flat $1 to $1.25. The cash back is added only after the trip is over: within a week at Expedia, within three months at Booking.com.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check each store's page on Rakuten on the day you book.

For the wider picture across hotel chains and other booking sites, see [Rakuten on hotels and flights](/rakuten-hotels-flights-cash-back/). This page goes deeper on these two.

## Rates seen on November 21, 2026

| What you book | Expedia | Booking.com |
|---|---|---|
| Hotels and other stays | 3% | 8% (Rakuten lists the usual rate as 4%) |
| Flights | $1.25 flat | $1 flat |
| Packages | 2% | Stays or flights in a package are eligible |
| Rental cars | 1% | 3% prepaid, 2% pay at pickup |
| Activities | 3% | 2% (attractions) |
| Airport taxis | Not listed | 2% |
| Cruises | Excluded | Not listed |

## How fast the rate moves

Expedia is a clear example. One day earlier, on November 20, 2026, the same Rakuten page showed Expedia hotels at 10%. On November 21 it showed 3%.

| $800 hotel stay | Cash back |
|---|---|
| At 10% (Expedia, November 20) | $80 |
| At 8% (Booking.com, November 21) | $64 |
| At 3% (Expedia, November 21) | $24 |

The rate that matters is the one shown when you start the booking. A rate seen last week says little about today.

## When it pays out

| Stage | Expedia | Booking.com |
|---|---|---|
| Shows as pending | Within 5 days of reserving | May not show until travel is complete |
| Added to the account | Within a week after travel | Within 3 months after travel |
| Paid to you | Next quarterly date | Next quarterly date |

Rakuten pays on February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15. A worked example for a stay that ends on March 20, 2027:

- **Expedia:** added by about March 27, which points to the May 15 payment.
- **Booking.com:** added by about June 20 at the latest. If it lands after the cut-off for May, it is paid August 15.

The exact cut-off before each payment date is not confirmed here. Rakuten's general travel guidance is wider still, at 6 to 17 weeks after the trip. The full schedule is in [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

## Rules specific to each site

**Expedia**

- Only orders completed through Expedia.com count. Bookings through the travel agent portal do not.
- Event tickets and cruises are excluded.
- Coupon codes: the page is not consistent. One line excludes any booking made with a coupon code. Another says only codes not listed on Rakuten void cash back. The cautious reading is to use no code.
- The page says Rakuten cash back can be combined with One Key member prices and OneKeyCash on eligible bookings.

**Booking.com**

- Only bookings on the U.S. Booking.com site count.
- Booking.com for Business and bookings with Partner Offers are excluded.
- The terms say: "Cash Back will be paid out only for completed bookings." A canceled stay or a no-show earns nothing.

**Both**

Rakuten's travel rules say any change to a reservation voids cash back, including dates, room type and flight times. To change plans, cancel and rebook through Rakuten.

## Not confirmed

- Whether a booking finished in the Expedia or Booking.com phone app counts. Neither Rakuten page says. Expedia's terms refer to orders completed through Expedia.com.
- Whether pay-at-the-property stays are treated differently from prepaid ones.
- Whether a Booking.com Genius discount affects eligibility.

## Who should skip it

- Your plans may change. An edited booking loses the cash back.
- You want hotel points or elite credit from a chain. Third-party bookings usually earn neither.
- You are booking only a flight. $1 to $1.25 is the whole reward.
- You have a coupon code for Expedia that you would rather use.
- You need the money soon. A stay months away can mean a wait of half a year or more.

## Quick answers

**Does Rakuten work with Expedia?**
Yes. On November 21, 2026 it showed 3% on hotels and homes, 2% on packages, 1% on cars and $1.25 on flights.

**Does Rakuten work with Booking.com?**
Yes. On November 21, 2026 it showed 8% on accommodations and $1 on flights, on the U.S. site only.

**When is travel cash back paid?**
After the trip. Expedia adds it within a week of travel, Booking.com within three months, then it goes out on the next quarterly payment date.
