Privacy

Short version: we do not ask for your name, we do not run ads, and we do not sell data.

What this site collects

  • Visit statistics. We count page views and clicks on referral buttons so we know which pages are useful. If an analytics service is in use, it is named below.
  • Email you send us. If you write to hello@itsworththefee.com, we keep your message so we can reply.

We have no accounts, no comment forms and no newsletter.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits and button clicks. Google sets cookies and processes data under its own privacy policy. You can block it with a browser setting or extension and the site works the same.

When you click a referral link

You leave this site and go to the company's own website. That company's privacy policy applies from then on. The company can tell that you arrived through our link; we are not told who you are, only (in some programs) that a referral succeeded.

Other services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its typeface from Google Fonts. Both receive your IP address as part of delivering the page.
