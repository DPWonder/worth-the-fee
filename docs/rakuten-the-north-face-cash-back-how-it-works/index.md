# Rakuten The North Face cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-the-north-face-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at The North Face, but several of the brand's most recognized products are left out. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's page showed 2% cash back with no raised rate. "Icon & Core" products such as the Nuptse, Denali and Basecamp Duffel earn nothing, and neither does any order that uses a coupon code.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate seen on November 25, 2026

| Item | What Rakuten's page showed |
|---|---|
| Rate | 2% |
| "Was" label | None |
| Category rates | None listed |
| Posting line | Added to the Rakuten account "tomorrow" |

A "was" label appears when a rate has been raised. This page had none, two days before Black Friday. The Black Friday deals listed on the page (30% off select styles) carried the same 2%. Nothing on the page promises a higher rate later in the week.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for The North Face list these:

| Earns nothing | Detail given in the terms |
|---|---|
| Icon & Core products | Examples named: Nuptse, Denali, Basecamp Duffel |
| Collaboration products | Examples named: TNF x Gucci, TNF x OC |
| Gift cards | No further detail |
| Orders over $2,000 | No further detail |
| The North Face Renewed | Purchases made through the resale program |
| Orders combined with coupon codes | See below |
| Orders outside the US site | Orders placed through other countries void cash back |

The terms give the Icon & Core names as examples. They do not give a full list, so which other products fall in that group is not confirmed. The activation page on the day of purchase is the place to look.

## Coupon codes: stricter than most

Many store pages on Rakuten say that codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. The North Face terms go further: cash back is not available when combined with coupon codes. No exception for codes listed on Rakuten is given.

On November 25, 2026, the same page listed offers at 2% cash back that look like discounts: 10% off for students, 10% off for teachers, and 30% off a $250 order for XPLR Pass members. Whether those discounts count as coupon codes under the terms is not confirmed.

## XPLR Pass

Rakuten's page describes XPLR Pass, the brand's free rewards program: one point per $1 spent, and a $10 reward for every 100 points. The cash back terms do not mention it. Whether the part of an order paid with an XPLR Pass reward earns cash back is not confirmed.

## App, pickup and stores

- **App.** The page says the brand has a mobile app. The terms do not mention it. Rakuten's help page says a purchase is tracked from a click through Rakuten. Whether an order finished in the brand's app tracks is not confirmed.
- **Pickup.** Not mentioned on the page. Not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The page showed an online rate only. No in-store offer was shown on it. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).
- **Returns.** Rakuten's general help page says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back.

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added "tomorrow". Payment follows every three months; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). If nothing appears, see [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## Who should skip it

- You are buying a Nuptse, a Denali or a Basecamp Duffel. They are named as excluded.
- You have a coupon code worth more than 2% of the order. The two do not combine.
- You are buying a collaboration product or shopping The North Face Renewed.
- Your order is over $2,000.
- You are ordering from outside the US site.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at The North Face?**
2% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

**Does a Nuptse jacket earn cash back?**
No. The terms name Nuptse, Denali and Basecamp Duffel as excluded Icon & Core products.

**Can you use a coupon code and still get cash back?**
The terms say cash back is not available when combined with coupon codes.
