Can you stack Rakuten cash back with credit card points and store coupons?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

Yes. Rakuten cash back and your credit card's rewards come from two different companies, so one purchase can earn both. Store coupons often work as well, but Rakuten says a code it did not list can void the cash back, and a second cash back site cannot be added on top.

Why the layers are separate

Rakuten is paid by the store for sending you there, and shares part of that with you. Your card issuer pays rewards for the charge on the card. Rakuten does not require a particular card for online orders; any card works at checkout.

A worked example

Step Amount
Order after the store's sale price and a coupon listed on Rakuten $200.00
Rakuten at an assumed 5% $10.00
Credit card at an assumed 2% $4.00
Total back $14.00, or 7%

Rakuten does not pay on tax or shipping, and its part arrives months later, on one of four payment dates; see when Rakuten pays.

The coupon catch

This is the part most likely to go wrong. Rakuten's terms say that if you use coupons it did not provide, the purchase might be associated with another service, and you might not earn. Many store pages on Rakuten carry a line that codes not found on Rakuten may void cash back.

Each store's rules are behind "See all Terms & Exclusions" on its Rakuten page.

Card offers

Amex Offers, Chase Offers and similar deals are added to a card in the issuer's app. They trigger on the charge itself, so they do not interfere with Rakuten's click tracking. The Points Guy reports that these can generally be combined with portal earnings.

One limit from Rakuten's side: if you choose Amex Membership Rewards points as your Rakuten payout, its terms say promotions tied to other card issuers will not be available to you. See Amex points instead of cash.

Steps that keep tracking intact

  1. Start at Rakuten, or activate in the extension, before adding items to the cart.
  2. Use only codes shown on Rakuten for that store.
  3. Do not open another coupon or rewards site in between.
  4. Check out in the same tab, with any card.
  5. Look for the order in your Rakuten account within a couple of days. If it is missing, see what to do when cash back did not track.

Who should skip it

  • You have a coupon from elsewhere that is worth more than the cash back. Take the larger one; trying for both can lose the smaller.
  • You are paying with a store gift card at a store that excludes gift card purchases.
  • You would buy something only because the stack looks large. A percentage back on a purchase you did not need is still a cost.

Quick answers

Do I need a special credit card to use Rakuten online? No. Any card works at checkout, and it earns its usual rewards.

Will a coupon code cancel my Rakuten cash back? It can, if the code is not one listed on Rakuten for that store.

Can I use Rakuten and another cash back site on one order? No. Rakuten says a click from another rewards site disables its cash back.
