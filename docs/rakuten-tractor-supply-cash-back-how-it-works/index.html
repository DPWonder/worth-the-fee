The rate seen on November 25, 2026

The page showed 1% with no "was" label, so 1% is the plain rate. No category table appears. The Black Friday deals and products listed on the page that day carried the same 1%.

What is excluded

The terms for this store are among the shortest on Rakuten:

  • Exclusion. Cash back is not available on the redemption of gift cards.
  • Special term. Using coupon codes that are not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.

The terms name redeeming a gift card. They do not name buying one. Whether a gift card purchase earns is not confirmed. Rakuten's general help page says most gift cards are excluded.

No product category or brand is listed as excluded. Rakuten's general help page says taxes and shipping fees are never eligible.

Coupon codes

The page says the store accepts coupons and promo codes online and in stores, with a "Promo Code & Rewards" field in the cart. Under the cash back terms, a code from outside Rakuten may void the 1%. On November 25, 2026, the offers listed on Rakuten's page were sales, such as 25% off Christmas decor; no code was needed for them.

Neighbor's Club, pickup and stores

  • Neighbor's Club. The page describes the store's free rewards program, with points on purchases and rewards to redeem. The cash back terms do not mention it. Whether an order paid partly with a reward earns on the full amount is not confirmed.
  • Pickup. The page describes buying online and picking up in a store, and free shipping to stores. The terms do not exclude pickup, and they do not mention it. Not confirmed.
  • Sister companies. The page says club points are earned at the store's sister companies. Whether an order at a sister company's site earns cash back from this page is not confirmed.
  • In stores. The store page showed an online rate only. The store did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.

When it shows up

The page says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "in a few days". Payment follows on Rakuten's four dates a year; see when Rakuten pays. Card rewards are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • You buy at the register in a local store. The rate shown is for online orders.
  • You are paying with a gift card.
  • You have a promo code from the store's emails that is not on Rakuten. On a $100 order the cash back at stake is $1.00.
  • Your orders are small. A $20 bag of feed earns $0.20.
  • You are waiting for a raised rate. None was shown, and none is promised.
