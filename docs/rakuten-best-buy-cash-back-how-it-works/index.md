# Does Rakuten give cash back at Best Buy, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-best-buy-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-21
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Best Buy page showed up to 8% cash back, set by category and brand, with most general categories at 1%. Apple products, video game consoles, gift cards, services and warranties are excluded.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy.

The same kind of product can sit in different tiers by brand. TVs ran from 1% to 7% that day.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms say cash back is only on select categories. Listed as earning nothing:

- Apple products and Beats headphones
- Video game consoles, including the PS5 Pro, and digital download games
- Gift cards and prepaid cards
- Services, installation, delivery, warranties and Geek Squad Protection
- Premium and luxury appliances, appliance parts and accessories
- Wi-Fi and networking, security, tools, musical instruments, electric car chargers
- The store's business program and employee-discount orders
- Six or more of the same item, counted within 30 days
- Items shipped from or through a Texas distribution center (how a shopper can tell is not confirmed)
- From marketplace sellers: Apple, flat screen TVs, point and shoot cameras, game consoles

The US site only. Phone orders do not qualify under Rakuten's general rules.

## Where the page disagrees with itself

The exclusions list names laptops and desktops. The rate list gives rates for several laptop lines, from 1% to 3%. Which rule wins for a given laptop is not confirmed. The activation page on the day of purchase is the place to look.

## Pickup, app and coupon codes

- **Store pickup.** The terms do not exclude it. They say an order must be picked up within five days of the ready-for-pickup email or it is canceled and refunded, which ends the cash back.
- **App.** The terms say nothing about the store's app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
- **Coupon codes.** No store-specific rule appears. Rakuten's general page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.

## A shorter claim window

Rakuten's general terms allow 90 days to report missing rewards. The terms for this store say missing cash back must be reported within 30 days of the order date. See [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## When you get the money

Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/) and [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/). Card rewards are separate and come on top; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- You are buying Apple products, a game console or gift cards. They earn nothing.
- You buy six or more of one item in a month.
- A price match or member discount at another store beats 1% on the item you want.
- You tend to return electronics. Returned items lose their cash back.
- You order by phone or in a store. In-store offers for this retailer were not seen in the part of Rakuten's in-store list that loaded.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at this store?**
Up to 8% on November 21, 2026, but most general categories were at 1%. Rates change daily.

**Do Apple products earn cash back?**
No. Apple products are on the excluded list, as are Beats headphones.

**Does store pickup count?**
The terms do not exclude it. The order must be picked up within five days or it is canceled.
