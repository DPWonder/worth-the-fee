What is excluded

Rakuten's terms say cash back is only on select categories. Listed as earning nothing:

  • Apple products and Beats headphones (what earns at Apple's own store is in Rakuten at Apple)
  • Video game consoles, including the PS5 Pro, and digital download games
  • Gift cards and prepaid cards
  • Services, installation, delivery, warranties and Geek Squad Protection
  • Premium and luxury appliances, appliance parts and accessories
  • Magnolia Home Theater
  • Wi-Fi and networking, security, tools, digital music, musical instruments, electric car chargers
  • The store's business program and employee-discount orders
  • Six or more of the same item, counted within 30 days
  • A fourth order in one day; the terms set a limit of three orders a day
  • Items shipped from or through a Texas distribution center (how a shopper can tell is not confirmed)
  • From marketplace sellers: Apple, flat screen TVs, "Blue Label" laptops, point and shoot cameras, game consoles

The US site only. Phone orders do not qualify under Rakuten's general rules.

Where the page disagrees with itself

The exclusions list names laptops and desktops. The rate list gives rates for several laptop lines, from 1% to 5%, and for Chromebooks at 4%. Which rule wins for a given laptop is not confirmed. The activation page on the day of purchase is the place to look. Rates at the makers' own stores are in laptop cash back at Dell, HP and Lenovo.

Pickup, stores, app and coupon codes

  • Store pickup. The terms do not exclude it. Rakuten's page says an order must be picked up within five days of the ready-for-pickup email or it is canceled and refunded, which ends the cash back.
  • In stores. The page showed an online rate only on November 26, with no in-store offer for this retailer. How linked-card offers work elsewhere is in in-store cash back.
  • App. The terms say nothing about the store's app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
  • Coupon codes. No store-specific rule appears. Rakuten's general page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. The deals listed on November 26 were sale links, such as a Thanksgiving 24-hour flash sale and 15% off Meta Quest 3S headsets, not codes.

A shorter claim window

Rakuten's general terms allow 90 days to report missing rewards. The terms for this store say missing cash back must be reported within 30 days of the order date. See what to do when cash back did not track.

When you get the money

The listing says cash back is added to the Rakuten account the same day. Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See when Rakuten pays. Card rewards are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • You are buying Apple products, a game console or gift cards. They earn nothing.
  • You buy six or more of one item in a month, or place more than three orders in a day.
  • A price match or member discount at another store beats 1% on the item you want.
  • You tend to return electronics. Returned items lose their cash back.
  • You order by phone or pay at a store register.

Quick answers

What is the Rakuten Best Buy cash back rate today? Up to 6% on November 26, 2026, with most general categories at 1%. Rates change daily.

Do Apple products earn cash back? No. Apple products are on the excluded list, as are Beats headphones.

Does store pickup count? The terms do not exclude it. The order must be picked up within five days or it is canceled.
