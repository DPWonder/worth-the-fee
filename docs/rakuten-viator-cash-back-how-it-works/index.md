# Rakuten Viator cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-viator-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Viator page showed 11% cash back, with a "was 2%" label. Cash back is paid on completed tours only, and theme park purchases, virtual tours, gift vouchers and bookings made with a discount code or card-linked offer earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate

The page showed 11% with a "was 2%" label and no rate by category. A "was" label means the rate has been raised above the store's usual rate. The page does not say how long 11% lasts, and nothing on it promises this rate on Black Friday (November 27, 2026) or after.

## What earns and what does not

| Booking | Earns cash back? |
|---|---|
| A tour booked on viator.com and completed | Yes |
| A tour that is canceled or refunded | No |
| Theme park purchases | No |
| Virtual tours | No |
| Gift vouchers, or a booking paid with one | No |
| A booking with a discount code, or a staff or student discount code | No |
| A booking with a card-linked offer | No |
| A booking through the Viator Travel Agent Program | No |
| An app-to-app sale | No |

The terms do not define "theme park purchases". Which tickets count is not confirmed.

## Special conditions

- **The tour must happen.** Cash back is paid on completed tours only.
- **One session.** The purchase must be completed in one session.
- **The website, not the app.** Purchases must be made through viator.com. App-to-app sales are not eligible.
- **The rebooking rule.** Cash back is not available if the customer canceled another booking for the same tour, tour option, start time, travel date and number of travelers within 14 days before or after the booking was made.

## Coupon codes: the page says two things

The exclusions say cash back is not available if discount codes are used. The special terms say codes not listed on Rakuten "may void" cash back, which reads as if a code listed on Rakuten is allowed. Whether a code shown on Rakuten's Viator page keeps the cash back is not confirmed. On November 25, 2026, the offers on the page were sale links, not codes.

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account within a couple of weeks after travel has been completed. Payment then follows Rakuten's schedule of four dates a year; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

## What it comes to

Round examples, not prices from the page.

| Booking | Price | At 11% | At 2% |
|---|---|---|---|
| City walking tour for two | $120 | $13.20 | $2.40 |
| Full-day excursion for four | $600 | $66.00 | $12.00 |
| Theme park purchase | Any | $0 | $0 |

Rakuten's general help page says taxes and shipping fees never earn cash back. How taxes and fees inside a tour price are treated is not confirmed.


## In stores

Viator sells online. The page showed an online rate only. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/), and for hotel and flight sites, [Rakuten on hotels and flights](/rakuten-hotels-flights-cash-back/).

## Who should skip it

- You book in the Viator app. App-to-app sales are not eligible.
- You have a discount code, a student or staff code, or a card-linked offer that saves more than the cash back.
- You are buying theme park tickets, a virtual tour or a gift voucher.
- You tend to cancel and rebook the same tour. A canceled tour earns nothing, and the rebooking rule can void the new booking too.
- You want the money soon. Nothing posts until after the travel date.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Viator?**
11% on November 25, 2026, with a "was 2%" label. Rates change daily.

**When does Viator cash back post?**
Within a couple of weeks after the travel is completed, not after booking.

**Do bookings in the Viator app earn cash back?**
The terms say purchases must be made through viator.com and that app-to-app sales are not eligible.
