Forgot to activate Rakuten before buying: can you still get the cash back?

Facts checked November 24, 20265 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

Usually not. Rakuten's help page says purchases earn cash back "only if you start a Shopping Trip with Rakuten", and no Rakuten help page describes adding cash back to an order placed without one. What can earn is a new order that starts from a Rakuten link.

Why the order cannot be matched afterward

A shopping trip is Rakuten's name for the click from Rakuten to a store, made on the site, in the app or with the extension's button. Each trip gets a unique number, and a tracking cookie in the browser links the purchase to the account.

Rakuten's help page explains what happens without that click: Rakuten has no way of showing the store that the buyer is a Rakuten member. The store pays Rakuten a commission only for shoppers it can see Rakuten sent. With no click, the store has no record to pay on. The steps are on how Rakuten works, step by step.

Not paid yet: you still have time

Rakuten's help page says that with the extension, cash back can be activated before shopping starts or before checkout. So activating late is allowed, up to the point of paying.

There is a catch. The same page says to activate before adding anything to the cart, because some stores do not let Rakuten link a purchase when items were already in the cart. Which stores those are is not listed. Emptying the cart, activating, and adding the items again covers both cases. Then check out in the tab that opened from Rakuten, and look for the pop-up saying cash back is active.

Already paid: check for a trip first

People sometimes click through and forget they did, or the extension was activated earlier in the session. Rakuten keeps a shopping trip in the account for up to three months, even when nothing was bought. If a trip to that store shows on the order date, this is a tracking question, not a forgotten activation, and the claim steps on what to do when cash back did not track apply. Give the order 48 hours to appear first.

What the Missing Cash Back form covers

Rakuten separates two cases. A missing shopping trip means it has no record of the click. Missing cash back means the trip was recorded but the order shows $0 or ineligible.

The help page points to the form for members who have gone through its checklist and still believe they should have earned. The first question on that checklist is whether cash back was activated before items went in the cart. Rakuten's terms set the deadline: contact Member Services within 90 days of the transaction. The terms also say that whether a purchase qualifies is at Rakuten's sole discretion.

The form sits behind sign-in. Whether it accepts an order with no shopping trip, and whether Rakuten ever credits one as a courtesy, is not confirmed. Nothing in the help pages says it does.

Canceling and ordering again

No Rakuten help page covers this for a forgotten activation. Two pages cover close cases:

  • For exchanges, Rakuten's page says a shopper can return the item and place an entirely new order, starting with Rakuten.
  • For travel, Rakuten says to cancel and rebook through Rakuten when plans change.

Both rest on the same rule: a new order that starts from a Rakuten link is a new shopping trip. The canceled order earns nothing, and the new one is treated like any other.

Whether you can cancel is the store's decision, not Rakuten's. Before doing it, check four things at the store:

  • Its cancellation or return policy, and any return shipping or restocking cost.
  • That the item is still in stock.
  • That the price and any coupon you used are still available.
  • The store's exclusions on Rakuten, so the new order is one that earns.

Not forgetting next time

Rakuten's extension shows an alert at stores with an offer, so there is no need to visit Rakuten first; it still has to be clicked. On a phone, the trip starts from the Rakuten app. The two are compared on the app and the extension. A new trip is needed for every purchase, including a second order at the same store.

Who should skip it

  • The cash back at stake is a dollar or two. Canceling and reordering is not worth the risk.
  • The item was a limited release or is low in stock.
  • The first order used a sale price or code that has ended.
  • The item is excluded at that store. A new order would earn nothing as well.

Quick answers

Can Rakuten add cash back after a purchase made without activating? Rakuten's help pages say a purchase earns only if it started from a Rakuten link. No retroactive credit is described.

Can you activate Rakuten after adding items to the cart? Yes, before checkout. Some stores cannot link the purchase, so empty the cart and add the items again.

How long do you have to report missing cash back? Rakuten's terms say within 90 days of the transaction. That applies where a shopping trip was started.
