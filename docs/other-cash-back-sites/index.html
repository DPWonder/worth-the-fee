Is TopCashback legit and free, and how does it make money?

TopCashback is a real cash back site that has run since 2005 and charges no fee to join or to be paid. It says it passes the whole store commission to the member and earns its own money from bonuses stores pay for sending many shoppers, plus advertising on its site.

TopCashback Highest Cash Back Guarantee: how does the 110% match work?

TopCashback says that if another cash back site shows a higher rate on the day you buy, it will pay 110% of that higher rate on a purchase made through TopCashback. The match is not automatic: it covers seven named sites, and you have to file a claim with proof within seven days of the cash back tracking.

How does TopCashback pay you, and when can you cash out?

TopCashback pays on request, not on fixed dates: once cash back is marked "available" you can send it to a bank account, Venmo or PayPal, or take a gift card. How long a purchase takes to become available is set by each store, and travel is not available until the stay is finished.
