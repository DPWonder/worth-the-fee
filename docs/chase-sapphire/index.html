Chase Sapphire: every short answer on one page

Each answer links to its full page, with sources and the date it was checked. This is information, not financial advice.

What is Chase's 5/24 rule, and how do you count your cards?

The 5/24 rule is an unofficial Chase practice: if you have opened five or more personal credit cards, from any bank, in the past 24 months, Chase will generally decline you for most of its cards. To count, list every card on your credit report by its opening date and count the ones from the last 24 months, closed cards included.

What is Chase Points Boost, and how much are Chase points worth now?

Points Boost is Chase's system for giving more than 1 cent per point on selected flights and hotels booked through Chase Travel. Without a Boost, a point is worth 1 cent there; with one, it is worth up to 2 cents on a Sapphire Reserve and up to 1.5 or 1.75 cents on a Sapphire Preferred.

What does an authorized user cost on the Sapphire Preferred and the Reserve, and what do they get?

An authorized user costs nothing on the Sapphire Preferred and $195 a year each on the Sapphire Reserve. On both cards they earn points at the card's rates into the primary cardholder's balance; on the Reserve they also get their own airport lounge access, but not a second set of the card's credits.

Can you get a Sapphire welcome bonus if you had a Sapphire card before? The 2026 rules

It depends on which card. Under the rules in force since January 2026, you can earn the welcome bonus on each Sapphire card once in your lifetime, so a past Sapphire Preferred does not block a first Sapphire Reserve bonus, and the reverse. A second bonus on the same card is not available, no matter how long ago the first one was.

How does the Sapphire Preferred hotel credit work, and what bookings count?

The Sapphire Preferred's $50 hotel credit no longer exists in that form: Chase doubled it to $100 on June 15, 2026. It is a statement credit of up to $100 each account anniversary year for hotel stays booked and paid for through Chase Travel with the card, and it does not apply to hotels booked anywhere else.

How does the Sapphire Preferred 10% anniversary points bonus work?

The Sapphire Preferred's 10% anniversary points bonus has ended: people who applied on or after June 15, 2026 never get it, and for earlier cardholders it stopped counting purchases after October 1, 2026. Chase says the last bonuses will be awarded by January 31, 2027, and in its place the card gained new 3x categories and a larger hotel credit.

What credit score do you need for the Chase Sapphire Preferred?

Chase does not publish a minimum credit score for the Sapphire Preferred. Sources that track approvals report a FICO score of about 670 as the low end and 700 or higher as the usual range, and Chase also weighs income, recent card openings and the credit it has already given you.

What DoorDash and DashPass benefits come with the Sapphire Preferred, and what are they worth?

The Sapphire Preferred comes with a free DashPass membership, which runs until December 31, 2029 if you activate it before then, plus $10 off one non-restaurant DoorDash order each month. At face value that is up to $240 a year, but it is worth only what you already spend on DoorDash delivery fees and grocery or convenience orders.

Is the Chase Sapphire Preferred a sensible first travel card, and what credit do you need?

It can be, for someone who already has good credit, at least a year or two of credit history, and one or more trips a year. Chase publishes no minimum score; guides suggest 670 to 700 or higher. It is a poor first credit card of any kind, because approval is harder with a thin credit file.

What should you do in the first year with a Sapphire Preferred to get your $95 back?

One step covers the fee: book a hotel stay of $100 or more through Chase Travel, and the card's yearly hotel credit returns up to $100 against the $95 annual fee. Everything else in the first year, such as the DoorDash promos, the Apple TV offer and the points you earn, is on top of that.

Can Chase Sapphire Preferred points pay for a Disney trip, and how far do they go?

Partly. Sapphire Preferred points can pay for flights and for hotels near the Disney parks, either through Chase Travel or by transfer to an airline or hotel program. Park tickets are the weak spot: Chase Travel is reported not to sell tickets to the US Disney parks, so points cover them only as cash back at 1 cent each.

Is the Chase Sapphire Preferred a good card to use abroad?

For purchases, yes. The Sapphire Preferred charges no foreign transaction fee, runs on the Visa network, and carries trip and rental car protections that apply outside the United States. It is a poor way to get cash, it has no lounge access, and it does not pay medical bills abroad.

Is the Chase Sapphire Preferred worth it if you take one big trip a year?

For most people who take one real trip a year, yes. The card costs $95 a year, and a single hotel booking through Chase Travel returns up to $100, so the fee can be covered by one stay. If you would never book a hotel through Chase's site, the answer gets weaker.

How do purchase protection and extended warranty work on the Chase Sapphire Preferred?

Purchase protection on the Sapphire Preferred covers a new item against theft or damage for 120 days from purchase, up to $500 per item and $50,000 per account. Extended warranty adds one year to a manufacturer's warranty of three years or less, up to $10,000 per item and $50,000 per account.

How does a Chase Sapphire refer-a-friend link work for the person applying?

A Chase Sapphire refer-a-friend link is a personal link from a cardholder that opens a standard Chase application showing a stated welcome bonus. You get that bonus if you are approved, are eligible for it and meet its spending requirement, and the cardholder who shared the link earns points from Chase.

Does the Chase Sapphire Preferred cover a rental car, and what does primary coverage leave out?

Yes. The Sapphire Preferred gives primary cover for theft of, or damage to, the rental car itself, up to $60,000, when you pay for the whole rental with the card and decline the rental company's collision damage waiver. It leaves out liability for other people and property, injuries, your belongings and several kinds of vehicle.

What travel insurance comes with the Sapphire Preferred, and what does it not cover?

The Sapphire Preferred includes trip cancellation and interruption cover, trip delay, baggage delay, lost luggage and primary rental car damage cover when you pay for the trip with the card. It does not pay medical bills abroad, it does not cover pre-existing conditions, and it does not pay out if you simply decide not to go.

Does pairing a Sapphire Preferred with Freedom cards make sense, and how do you combine the points?

Pairing a Sapphire Preferred with a no-fee Freedom card makes sense for people who transfer points to airlines or hotels, because Freedom points can be moved to the Preferred for free and sent to partners from there. Since October 2025 the move no longer raises what the points are worth on ordinary Chase Travel bookings, so for someone who takes points as cash the pairing adds little.

Is the Chase Sapphire referral offer the same as the public or in-branch offer?

Often, but not always. On November 20, 2026 the Sapphire Preferred referral page and Chase's public page showed the same bonus, 75,000 points after $5,000 of purchases in three months. The two have differed for days or weeks in the past, and no current in-branch offer could be confirmed.

What counts toward the Chase Sapphire Reserve's $300 travel credit, and when does it reset?

Any purchase that Chase codes as travel counts, including airfare, hotels, car rentals, trains, taxis, tolls and parking, with no sign-up and no booking site required. The credit resets each account anniversary year, not on January 1, and the part of a purchase it covers earns no points.

What are the Sapphire Reserve credits really worth if you only count the ones you would use anyway?

At face value the Sapphire Reserve's recurring credits add up to about $2,384 a year against a $795 fee, before counting the DashPass membership and the Global Entry credit. Count only what you would have paid for anyway and most people land far lower, often between $300 and $900, so the card pays for itself only for those who already spend in several of its categories.

How does the Sapphire Reserve dining credit (Exclusive Tables) work, and which restaurants count?

The Sapphire Reserve dining credit pays back up to $150 from January to June and up to $150 from July to December when you pay with the card at a restaurant in the Sapphire Reserve Exclusive Tables list on OpenTable. Only those restaurants count, reported at roughly 360 to 400 in about 50 US cities and metro areas, so the credit is worth nothing in a city without one.

Which airport lounges does the Sapphire Reserve get you into, and what are the guest rules?

The Sapphire Reserve gets you into Chase's own Sapphire Lounges at eight US airports, a partner lounge at Washington Dulles, more than 1,300 Priority Pass lounges worldwide, and some Air Canada lounges. You can bring two guests free to Sapphire and Priority Pass lounges; each extra guest is $27 a visit. Priority Pass airport restaurants are not included.

How does the Chase Sapphire Reserve StubHub credit work, and what counts?

The Sapphire Reserve pays back up to $150 from January to June and up to $150 from July to December on purchases made directly with StubHub or viagogo, through December 31, 2027. You activate it once in your Chase account before buying, and whatever you do not use in a half-year is gone.

How does the Sapphire Reserve credit for The Edit hotels work, and what are the catches?

The Sapphire Reserve gives up to $250 back on a prepaid stay of two nights or more at a hotel in Chase's The Edit collection, on up to two separate bookings per calendar year, for $500 in total. The catches are the two-night minimum, the need to prepay, hotel prices that often run $500 a night or more, and the fact that one booking can never earn more than $250.

Can you upgrade a Sapphire Preferred to a Reserve or downgrade to a Freedom card, and what happens to points and the bonus?

Yes: after a year with the card, Chase lets a Sapphire Preferred holder switch to a Sapphire Reserve, or down to a Freedom Flex or Freedom Unlimited, without a new application. A product change pays no welcome bonus on the new card, and after a downgrade to a Freedom card your points can no longer move to airline and hotel partners unless you hold another card that allows it.

What counts toward the Chase Sapphire welcome-bonus spending requirement, and when do the points post?

Purchases of products and services made by you or an authorized user count toward the Chase Sapphire spending requirement. The annual fee, interest, balance transfers, cash advances and cash-like transactions such as money orders do not. Chase asks you to allow 6 to 8 weeks after qualifying for the points to post, though reviewers report they often arrive when the next statement closes.

Did Chase change the Hyatt transfer rate, and which Sapphire card still transfers 1:1?

Yes. From a Sapphire Preferred, 4 Chase points now become 3 World of Hyatt points, where it used to be 1 for 1. The Sapphire Reserve still transfers to Hyatt at 1:1.

How much does applying for a travel card affect your credit score, and for how long?

For most people, one credit card application lowers a FICO score by fewer than five points, according to FICO. The hard inquiry counts in FICO scores for 12 months and stays on your credit report for two years, and the new account also lowers the average age of your accounts.

Sapphire Preferred or Sapphire Reserve for someone who travels two or three times a year?

For two or three trips a year, the Sapphire Preferred is the safer fit for most people. The Sapphire Reserve costs $700 more each year, and a light traveler has to use several of its credits, not only the travel one, to get that back.
