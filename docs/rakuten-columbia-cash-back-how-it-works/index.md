# Rakuten Columbia cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-columbia-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Columbia Sportswear page showed 2% cash back, with no raised rate two days before Black Friday. Gift cards, the part of an order paid with a gift card, pro, partner and employee discount orders and the Star Wars Collection earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate

The page showed 2% with no "was" label and no rate by category. A "was" label appears when a rate has been raised above the store's usual rate, so 2% is the plain rate. Every Black Friday offer on the page carried the same 2%. Nothing on the page promises a higher rate on Black Friday (November 27, 2026) or Cyber Monday (November 30, 2026). Rakuten lists the store as "Columbia Sportswear".

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Columbia list these as earning nothing:

- Gift cards
- The part of an order paid for with gift cards
- Prodeal and Pro Purchase orders
- Partner, employee and friends-and-family discounted portal purchases
- The Star Wars Collection
- Orders deemed to be placed by resellers

The terms do not say how an order is judged to be from a reseller. That is not confirmed.


## Coupon codes and discounts

The terms say coupons or promotional codes not listed on Rakuten "may void" cash back. On November 25, 2026, the offers on the page were sale links, not codes.

Rakuten's page describes a 10% teacher discount verified through ID.me. The terms do not name it. Whether an order with that discount earns the 2% is not confirmed.

## Greater Rewards points

Greater Rewards is Columbia's free member program. Rakuten's page says members earn points on purchases and get free standard shipping when logged in. It also says Greater Rewards points and Rakuten cash back can be earned on the same eligible purchase.

The terms do not say what happens when a reward is spent on an order. Whether the part paid with a reward earns cash back is not confirmed.

## What it comes to

Prices are those shown on Rakuten's Columbia page on November 25, 2026.

| Item | Price shown | At 2% |
|---|---|---|

| Men's Cascadian Peaks Insulated Jacket | $72.00 | $1.44 |
| Men's Alpine Action II Jacket | $90.00 | $1.80 |
| Star Wars Collection item or gift card | Any | $0 |

Taxes and shipping do not count, under Rakuten's general rules.

## Pickup, app and stores

- **Pickup.** The terms do not mention store pickup. Not confirmed.
- **App.** The terms do not mention a Columbia app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
- **In stores.** The page showed an online rate only. Columbia did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).
- **Returns.** Rakuten's general help page says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back.

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "tomorrow". Payment follows on Rakuten's schedule of four dates a year; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

## Who should skip it

- You buy through a pro, partner, employee or friends-and-family portal. Those orders earn nothing.
- You are buying from the Star Wars Collection or buying a gift card.
- You are paying most of the order with a gift card.
- You have a code that is not on Rakuten and it saves more than 2%.
- You are waiting for a raised rate. None was shown, and none is promised.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Columbia?**
2% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

**Do sale items at Columbia earn cash back?**
The terms do not exclude sale items. Every sale offer on the page carried the 2% rate.

**Do Greater Rewards points and Rakuten cash back stack?**
Rakuten's page says both can be earned on the same eligible purchase.
