Does Rakuten give cash back at Nordstrom and Nordstrom Rack, and what is excluded?

Facts checked November 21, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Not at Nordstrom on the day checked, and yes at Nordstrom Rack. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Nordstrom page showed "No Cash Back", while its Nordstrom Rack page showed 2%. At the Rack, gift cards and five brands are excluded.

"Was" is Rakuten's own label for a store's usual rate. Neither page showed one. Two Rakuten addresses for each store showed the same thing.

Nordstrom: no cash back on the day checked

The Nordstrom page's title describes it as coupons, promo codes and no cash back. It lists the store's own sales, which are not Rakuten cash back. The page gives no reason and no return date.

Sources disagree here. A web search on the same day returned summaries of Rakuten pages for Nordstrom that showed 2%, with exclusions for gift cards, some baby gear and several brands. Those summaries carried no date and may be older copies. This page goes by what was read directly. Whether Nordstrom paid 2% earlier in 2026, and whether it will again, is not confirmed.

While the page shows no cash back, gift cards, pickup orders, app orders and sale items all earn nothing.

Nordstrom Rack: what is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Nordstrom Rack list these as earning nothing:

  • Gift cards
  • Free People and Free People Movement
  • Coach
  • Rainbow
  • Chanel
  • Birkenstock

The terms say cash back is added to the Rakuten account the same day.

What the Rack terms do not say

  • Returns. The terms have no rule for returns. Rakuten's general help page says returns and cancellations lose their cash back.
  • Coupon codes. No code rule is listed for the Rack. Rakuten's general page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.
  • App. The terms do not mention the Nordstrom Rack app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
  • Pickup. Not mentioned. Not confirmed.
  • A deadline for missing cash back. None is listed for the Rack. Rakuten's general terms allow 90 days.

In stores

Neither store appeared in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 21, 2026. The list was cut off, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.

When you get the money

Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See when Rakuten pays and what to do when cash back did not track. Card rewards are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • You are shopping at Nordstrom itself while its page shows no cash back. The Rakuten step earns nothing.
  • You are a new member counting on a Nordstrom order for the sign-up bonus. A purchase has to earn cash back to qualify.
  • You are buying Coach, Chanel, Birkenstock, Rainbow or Free People at the Rack.
  • You are buying gift cards.

Quick answers

Does Rakuten give cash back at Nordstrom? Not on November 21, 2026. Rakuten's Nordstrom page showed no cash back that day.

What rate does Rakuten pay at Nordstrom Rack? 2% on November 21, 2026, with no "was" rate shown. Rates change daily.

Which brands are excluded at Nordstrom Rack? Free People, Free People Movement, Coach, Rainbow, Chanel and Birkenstock. Gift cards are excluded too.
