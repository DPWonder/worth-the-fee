TopCashback browser extension: how does it work and what does it see?

Facts checked November 25, 20264 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

The TopCashback browser extension is a free desktop add-on that shows a pop-up when the store you are on pays cash back, so you can turn cash back on with one click without going to TopCashback first. Its privacy notice says it reads the addresses of the sites you visit to check for stores, and does not record or store your searches or browsing history.

What it does, step by step

TopCashback's help page gives three steps once the extension is installed:

  1. Go to the store's own website.
  2. Click to activate cash back in the pop-up.
  3. Use any coupon the pop-up lists, then check out as usual.

You have to be a TopCashback member and logged in for a purchase to earn. If you are logged out, the pop-up still appears; clicking it sends you to the login page and then back to the store.

The help pages also say that after a Google search the extension shows which stores in the results pay cash back and at what rate.

Why a store may show no pop-up

The extension does not cover every store on the site. The help page says stores have to "opt-in" before they can be featured. When no pop-up appears, the store may still pay cash back if you start from TopCashback's website instead.

What it sees

These points come from the extension's privacy notice:

  • It uses a unique identifier to learn how you interact with the extension.
  • It identifies the address of each website you visit, to check whether that site is a TopCashback store.
  • On search engines and some AI chat sites, it reads store addresses shown on the page so it can show cash back offers. The notice says it does not have access to, record or store your search queries, prompts or conversations.
  • It keeps a list of the stores you clicked through that are eligible for cash back.
  • It uses cookies. When you activate cash back, it places a tracking cookie through its affiliate network. The notice says this replaces any existing tracking from other affiliate companies, so the purchase is credited to TopCashback.
  • "On occasion" it may capture an order number and transaction amount from a store's site to help credit your cash back.

A separate help page says the extension installs no spyware, malware or adware. These are the company's statements about its own product; no outside audit was found for this page.

The cookie point matters if you use more than one cash back tool. Only one of them is credited for a purchase, and the last one activated is normally the one that counts. The same is true of Rakuten's extension.

Removing it

For Chrome, Firefox and Edge, the help page says to open the extension's page in that browser's web store and click "Remove." The privacy notice says you can remove it in your browser at any time.

Who should skip it

  • You shop mostly on a phone. The extension is desktop only; TopCashback has a separate app.
  • You do not want an add-on that checks the address of every site you open.
  • You already run another cash back extension and do not want two pop-ups competing on the same store.
  • You are happy to start each purchase from TopCashback's website. The extension is a reminder, not a requirement.

Quick answers

Is the TopCashback browser extension free? Yes. TopCashback's page says it is free to download and free to use.

Does the TopCashback extension work on a phone? No. The help page says it is designed for desktop use only and is not compatible with mobile devices.

Does the TopCashback extension record my searches? Its privacy notice says no. It reads website addresses to find stores, and does not record or store search queries or browsing history.
