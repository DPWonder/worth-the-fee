Rakuten Saks Fifth Avenue cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

The short answer

Yes. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Saks Fifth Avenue page showed 12% cash back, with a "was 2%" label. Jewelry is the main exclusion: it earns nothing, and neither do gift cards or orders placed with a Saks associate discount.

The rate

The page showed 12% with a "was 2%" label and no rate by category. A "was" label means the rate has been raised above the store's usual rate. The page does not say when 12% ends, and nothing on it promises the rate for Black Friday (November 27, 2026) or Cyber Monday (November 30, 2026).

What is excluded

The terms on the page are one sentence long. Listed as earning nothing:

  • Jewelry
  • Gift cards
  • Orders placed using a Saks associate discount

The terms do not define "jewelry". Whether watches or items sold in other departments count is not confirmed.

Coupon codes

The Saks terms on Rakuten carry no line about coupon codes. Many store pages have one; this page does not. Rakuten's general help page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. On November 25, 2026, the page listed one offer with a code: spend $150 and earn a $75 gift card. The other offers were sale links.

A gift card earned in a store promotion is not the same as buying one. Whether an order paid partly with a promotional gift card earns on the full amount is not confirmed. The terms exclude the purchase of gift cards and say nothing about paying with them.

Taxes and shipping do not count, under Rakuten's general rules. Returned or canceled orders do not earn.

Shipping, pickup, app and stores

  • Shipping. The page says Saks offers free shipping on orders of $300 or more.
  • Pickup. Rakuten's page describes buying online and picking up at a Saks store at no extra charge. The cash back terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
  • App. The terms do not mention the Saks app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension. Whether an order finished in the Saks app tracks is not confirmed.
  • In stores. The page showed an online rate only. Saks did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.

When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "in a few days". Payment follows on Rakuten's schedule of four dates a year; see when Rakuten pays. Two other department stores: Rakuten at Nordstrom and Rakuten at Macy's.

Who should skip it

  • You are buying jewelry or a gift card. Both earn nothing.
  • You shop with a Saks associate discount.
  • You have a code from the store's emails that is not on Rakuten and it saves more than the cash back.
  • You are counting on 12% for a later date. The page shows today's rate and promises nothing.
  • You often send orders back. Returned items lose their cash back.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Saks Fifth Avenue? 12% on November 25, 2026, with a "was 2%" label. Rates change daily.

Does jewelry earn cash back at Saks? No. The terms list jewelry, gift cards and associate-discount orders as earning nothing.

Can you use a Saks promo code with Rakuten? The Saks terms say nothing on codes. Rakuten's general rule is that unlisted codes may void cash back.
