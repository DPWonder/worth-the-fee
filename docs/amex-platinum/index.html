Amex Platinum: every short answer on one page

Each answer links to its full page, with sources and the date it was checked. This is information, not financial advice.

What does the Amex Platinum $200 airline fee credit actually cover?

It covers incidental fees, such as checked bags, seat selection and in-flight food, on one airline you choose in advance, up to $200 per calendar year. It does not cover airline tickets, award tickets, upgrades, miles or gift cards.

What credit score and income do you need for the Amex Platinum?

American Express publishes no minimum credit score and no minimum income for the Platinum Card. Guides describe the usual applicant as having good to excellent credit, and Amex's "Apply With Confidence" process can tell a new applicant whether they are approved before any hard inquiry is placed on their credit report.

Is adding an authorized user to the Amex Platinum worth $195?

It is worth $195 a year mainly for someone who will use airport lounges, either with you about four or more times a year or alone. The additional cardholder gets lounge access and hotel and car rental status, but not a second set of the statement credits.

What does Amex Platinum cell phone protection cover, and what is the deductible?

It covers the cost to repair or replace a stolen or damaged phone, including a cracked screen, up to $800 per claim after a $50 deductible, with two claims per 12 months. The wireless bill must be paid with the card. A lost phone is not covered, and the benefit pays only what other insurance does not.

Can you bring guests into a Centurion Lounge with the Amex Platinum, and what does it cost?

Yes. A Platinum cardholder can bring up to two guests into a US Centurion Lounge, but each adult guest costs $50 and each child costs $30. Guests are free only after you put $75,000 of eligible purchases on the card in a calendar year.

What are the Amex Platinum credits really worth if you only count the ones you would use anyway?

The 11 main yearly credits on the Amex Platinum add up to $2,994 on paper, against an annual fee of $895. What they are worth to you is smaller: only the credits for things you would have bought anyway count, and for many people that is a few hundred dollars to about $1,300.

How many Delta Sky Club visits does the Amex Platinum give, and what are the rules?

The Platinum Card gives 10 Delta Sky Club visits per year, counted from February 1 to January 31, and only when you fly Delta that day on a ticket that is not basic economy. Guests cost $50 each, and so does each visit after the tenth.

Which subscriptions count for the Amex Platinum digital entertainment credit?

The credit covers Disney+ (and Disney+ bundles), Hulu, ESPN streaming, Paramount+, standalone Peacock, YouTube Premium, YouTube TV, The New York Times and The Wall Street Journal, when you pay the provider directly with an enrolled Platinum Card. Netflix, Spotify, Apple TV and Amazon Prime Video are not on the list.

If the Amex Platinum fee stops being worth it, what are the options and what happens to the points?

There are three options: ask Amex for a retention offer and keep the card, downgrade to the Gold Card ($325) or the Green Card ($150), or cancel. Your Membership Rewards points stay if you keep any card that earns them. If the Platinum is your only one and you close it, the points are forfeited.

Does the Amex Platinum do anything for golfers?

Very little directly. The US Amex Platinum has no free rounds, no green fee credit and no tee time priority; what it offers a golfer is travel help around the trip, mainly a hotel credit, airport lounges and some travel protections.

How do the Amex Platinum Global Entry, TSA PreCheck and CLEAR credits work, and what are they worth?

Charge the application fee to the Platinum Card and Amex credits it back: $120 for Global Entry or up to $85 for TSA PreCheck, once about every four years. A separate credit covers up to $219 a year of a CLEAR+ membership. Together they are worth about $249 a year at face value, and only if you would use the programs.

What does the Amex Platinum actually do for one Hawaii trip a year?

For one Hawaii trip a year, the Amex Platinum mainly gives you up to $300 back on a prepaid stay at certain hotels, plus lounge access at Honolulu and Maui airports. It does little at Kona and Lihue airports, and one trip does not come close to covering the $895 annual fee by itself.

How does the Amex Platinum hotel credit work?

The Platinum Card gives up to $300 back in each half of the year, $600 per calendar year, on prepaid bookings made through American Express Travel at Fine Hotels + Resorts or The Hotel Collection, or on Airbnb Luxe stays. Any other hotel booking earns nothing, and an unused half does not carry over.

What do the Marriott Gold and Hilton Gold status from Amex Platinum actually get you?

Hilton Gold is the useful one: a daily food and beverage credit at many US hotels or a continental breakfast abroad, 80% bonus points and possible room upgrades. Marriott Gold is modest: 25% bonus points, 2 p.m. checkout when available and a few hundred welcome points, with no breakfast or lounge access.

How does the Amex Platinum Lululemon credit work, and what counts?

The Platinum Card gives up to $75 back each calendar quarter, $300 a year, on purchases at US lululemon stores and on lululemon.com, once the card is enrolled. Outlet stores, gift cards and orders shipped outside the US are excluded, and an unused quarter is lost.

What are the Amex Platinum Oura and Equinox credits worth if you would not buy them anyway?

To someone who would not buy an Oura Ring or an Equinox membership anyway, the two credits are worth close to $0. The $200 Oura credit is a discount on a ring that starts at $349, and the $300 Equinox credit pays for a fitness app or a small part of a gym membership.

Is the Amex Platinum a charge card or a credit card, and how does Pay Over Time work?

The Platinum Card works like a charge card: it has no preset spending limit, and charges are due in full each month unless a feature called Pay Over Time is active. With Pay Over Time active, eligible charges can be carried as a balance with interest, up to a limit Amex sets, which makes that part of the account behave like a credit card.

How do Amex Platinum purchase protection, return protection and extended warranty work?

Purchase protection covers a new item that is stolen, damaged or lost within 90 days, up to $10,000 per item and $50,000 per year. Return protection refunds up to $300 per item, $1,000 per year, when a store refuses a return within 90 days. Extended warranty adds up to one year to a manufacturer's warranty of five years or less.

Is the Amex Platinum referral offer higher or lower than the public offer?

Neither route is reliably higher. In November 2026 the public page and referral links both lead to the same kind of offer, worded as high as 175,000 points, and Amex shows each applicant their own number before they accept. The only way to compare is to look at the number each route gives you.

What rental car status and insurance come with the Amex Platinum?

The Platinum Card gives mid-level status with Hertz, Avis and National once you enroll, and secondary cover for theft or damage to a rental car up to $75,000. Premium Car Rental Protection is a separate paid add-on, about $12 to $25 per rental, that makes the damage cover primary; neither one covers liability.

How does the Amex Platinum Resy credit work, and can you use it without living in a big city?

After a one-time enrollment, the Platinum Card gives back up to $100 each calendar quarter ($400 a year) when you pay at an eligible US restaurant listed on Resy. You do not need a reservation, so it works anywhere an eligible restaurant exists, but outside larger cities there may be few or none nearby.

What travel insurance does the Amex Platinum include, and what does it leave out?

The Amex Platinum includes trip delay cover up to $500 per trip after more than six hours, trip cancellation and interruption cover up to $10,000 per trip, and baggage cover up to $3,000 per person. It does not pay medical bills abroad or for delayed bags, and each benefit pays only after other insurance and refunds.

Is the Amex Platinum Uber credit worth anything if you rarely take Uber?

It can be, because the monthly Uber Cash also works on Uber Eats orders in the US, not only on rides. But it comes as $15 a month that expires at the end of each month, so someone who uses neither Uber nor Uber Eats most months should count it as close to $0, not $200.

Amex Platinum or Amex Gold: which fits someone who travels a few times a year but eats out every week?

For someone who eats out every week and flies a few times a year, the Gold Card is the closer fit: it earns 4 points per dollar at restaurants and costs $325 a year. The Platinum Card earns 1 point per dollar at restaurants and costs $895; it suits that person only if they would use its lounges and its travel credits.

Amex Platinum or Chase Sapphire Reserve for a once-a-year Hawaii trip?

For one Hawaii trip a year, the Chase Sapphire Reserve is the closer fit of the two. It costs $100 less, its $300 travel credit works on any travel, and its points move to Hyatt 1:1. Neither card is an easy case for a single trip: both fees are high and lounge access in Hawaii is thin.

How does the Amex Platinum Walmart+ credit work, and is the membership worth having?

The Platinum Card refunds the cost of a monthly Walmart+ membership, $12.95 plus sales tax, each month you pay for it with the card, which Amex counts as up to $155 a year. Only the monthly plan is refunded, so the membership is free to hold but worth something only to people who order from Walmart or use its streaming choice.

What counts toward the Amex Platinum welcome-offer spending requirement, and what does not?

Ordinary purchases of goods and services count toward the Amex Platinum spending requirement, which is currently $12,000 in the first six months. The $895 annual fee, interest, gift cards, prepaid cards, person-to-person payments and other cash equivalents do not count, and refunds are taken back off your total.

Is the Amex Platinum worth its annual fee if you only fly 4 to 6 times a year?

For most people who fly 4 to 6 times a year, the Amex Platinum is worth its $895 annual fee only if they would already spend money on the things its credits pay for. The lounges and travel perks alone do not cover the fee at that number of flights.

How do Amex referral offers work, and why does the page say "as high as" a number?

An Amex referral link is a personal link from an existing cardmember that takes you to a card application. The page says "as high as" because Amex sets a welcome offer for each applicant and shows you yours before you accept the card, so the number in the headline is the top of a range and not a promise.

Are credit card welcome bonuses and referral bonuses taxable in the US?

Generally, a welcome bonus you earn by meeting a spending requirement is treated as a rebate on your purchases and not as taxable income. A referral bonus, which you get without spending, is generally treated as taxable income, and the card issuer may report it to the IRS on Form 1099-MISC. This page states what sources say and is not tax advice.

Fine Hotels + Resorts or The Hotel Collection: which uses the Amex Platinum hotel credit better?

The Hotel Collection usually costs less, so the $300 half-year credit covers a larger share of the bill. Fine Hotels + Resorts gives more at the hotel, mainly daily breakfast for two and a guaranteed 4 p.m. checkout, and until January 1, 2027 it is the only one of the two that works for a one-night stay.

How do you check which Amex Platinum welcome offer you are shown, and why do people see different numbers?

You see your own Amex Platinum welcome offer by starting an application: Amex shows the points figure before you accept the card, and reviewers report no hard credit check up to that point. People see different numbers because Amex sets the offer for each applicant, so the headline of as high as 175,000 points is a ceiling and not a promise.
