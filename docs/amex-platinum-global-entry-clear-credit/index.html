How do the Amex Platinum Global Entry, TSA PreCheck and CLEAR credits work, and what are they worth?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Charge the application fee to the Platinum Card and Amex credits it back: $120 for Global Entry or up to $85 for TSA PreCheck, once about every four years. A separate credit covers up to $219 a year of a CLEAR+ membership. Together they are worth about $249 a year at face value, and only if you would use the programs.

Global Entry or TSA PreCheck

What each program does. TSA PreCheck is a faster security lane at US airports. Global Entry speeds up US immigration on the way home, includes TSA PreCheck and lasts five years.

How the credit works. Pay the application fee with the Platinum Card. The credit posts automatically. Amex's terms, as shown in a search summary of its benefit page, say to allow up to 8 weeks. The credit goes to the first of the two programs you pay for in the period, so you get one, not both.

Whose application. One Mile at a Time reports that the fee can be for someone else's application, as long as it is charged to your card. Each authorized user holding a Platinum additional card gets a separate credit.

What is not covered. NEXUS, SENTRI and Privium are not eligible, per Amex's terms. That differs from the Chase Sapphire cards, whose credit includes NEXUS.

Sources differ slightly on timing. One Mile at a Time says once every 4 years for either program. Amex's terms give 4 years for Global Entry and 4.5 years for TSA PreCheck.

CLEAR+

What it does. CLEAR+ confirms your identity with an eye or fingerprint scan and takes you to the front of the ID check. The Points Guy counts more than 55 US airports.

How the credit works. Pay for the membership with the card and the charge is credited, up to $219 per calendar year. The Points Guy reports that the card must be enrolled first, that Amex quotes two to four weeks for the credit, and that it often posts within days. First-time members finish sign-up in person at a CLEAR airport location. The membership renews automatically.

The price. CLEAR+ costs $219 a year since July 1, 2026, so the credit covers one standard membership in full.

The credit can be used in parts and can pay for someone else's membership. An added adult family member costs $125, and at the standard price no credit is left for it.

Sources disagree on the amount. The Points Guy's main text and Amex's card page say $219. The same Points Guy article's FAQ, and a search summary of an Amex travel page, still say $209.

The CLEAR+ credit is worth $219 only if you would pay for CLEAR+ yourself. Many people would not. Amex Platinum's annual fee is $895, so these two credits cover a little over a quarter of it at face value. See all the credits.

Who should skip counting them

  • Anyone whose home airport has no CLEAR lanes.
  • Anyone who already gets Global Entry or PreCheck paid by another card. A second credit adds nothing unless it pays for someone else.
  • Anyone who flies once or twice a year. The time saved is small.
  • Anyone who needs NEXUS.

Quick answers

Can I get both the Global Entry and the TSA PreCheck credit? No. The credit applies to the first program you pay for in the period. Global Entry includes TSA PreCheck.

Do authorized users get their own credits? Each Platinum additional card gets its own Global Entry or PreCheck credit. The CLEAR+ credit is one per account. See whether an authorized user is worth $195.

Does the CLEAR+ credit cover a family member? It can pay for anyone's membership, but it is capped at $219 a year, which is the price of one standard membership.
