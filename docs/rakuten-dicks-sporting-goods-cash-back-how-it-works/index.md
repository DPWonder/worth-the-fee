# Rakuten Dick's Sporting Goods cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-dicks-sporting-goods-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-22
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes. On November 22, 2026, Rakuten's Dick's Sporting Goods page showed 9% cash back, raised from a usual 2%. Gift cards, extended warranties, services, three named brands, the part of an order paid with rewards and bulk orders are excluded.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate here and at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate, and how it moves

| Date checked | Rate shown | Rakuten's "was" label |
|---|---|---|
| November 22, 2026 | 9% | 2% |

"Was" is Rakuten's own label for the store's usual rate. The page shows one row, "Dick's Sporting Goods Purchase", so the one rate covers all eligible items. A check of the same page earlier in November 2026 showed 2%, so the 9% is a recent raise. The page does not say how long it lasts.

Black Friday is November 27, 2026. Rates often move that week, in either direction, and nothing on the page promises what the rate will be.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Dick's Sporting Goods list these as earning nothing:

- Huckberry, Yeezy and Rainbow Sandals
- Extended warranties
- Services
- Reproductions and rain checks
- The portion of an order paid for with rewards
- The purchase or redemption of gift cards
- 15 or more of the same item (the same SKU)
- Orders the store judges to be for reselling
- Orders the store judges to come from outside the United States

One flat amount is listed too: a ScoreCard+ membership enrollment earns $20.00 rather than a percentage.

No sport or category is named as excluded. Golf clubs, fitness equipment and team gear are not on the list. For golf purchases across several stores, see [cash back on golf gear](/rakuten-golf-gear-cash-back/).

## Coupon codes and ScoreCard

- **Coupon codes.** The terms say codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back, and limit an order to one coupon.
- **ScoreCard points.** The page's FAQ says ScoreCard points and Rakuten cash back can be earned on the same eligible purchase. The part of an order paid with ScoreCard rewards earns nothing.
- **Returns.** The terms have no store rule. Rakuten's general help page says returned and canceled items lose their cash back.
- **App and pickup.** The terms do not mention the Dick's app or store pickup. Not confirmed. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
- **Golf Galaxy and other sister stores.** The terms cover Dick's Sporting Goods only. Whether orders on sister sites earn under this offer is not confirmed.

Taxes and shipping do not count. Paying $50 of a $250 order with rewards leaves $200 that earns.

## In stores

Dick's Sporting Goods did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 22, 2026. The list was cut off after 21 stores, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## When you get the money

The page says cash back is added to the Rakuten account the same day, as pending. Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/) and [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/). Card rewards are separate and come on top; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- You pay mostly with ScoreCard rewards or gift cards. That part earns nothing.
- You are buying gift cards, a warranty or a service.
- You order 15 or more of one item for a team or league.
- You have a coupon code from somewhere other than Rakuten that is worth more than the cash back.
- You buy in the store rather than online.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Dick's Sporting Goods?**
9% on November 22, 2026, against a usual 2%. Rates change daily.

**Do ScoreCard points still count?**
The page says points and cash back can be earned together. The portion paid with rewards earns no cash back.

**Can I use a coupon code?**
One coupon per order, and codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.
