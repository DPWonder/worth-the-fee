# How many Amex or Chase points does a round trip to Europe cost?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/how-many-points-for-a-flight-to-europe/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Through airline partners, a round trip from the US to Europe starts at about 12,000 to 55,000 Amex or Chase points in economy and about 81,000 to 120,000 in business class, at each program's lowest published price. Taxes and surcharges run from about $100 to more than $1,000 on top, and the low prices are not offered on every date.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## Lowest published prices, round trip

One-way prices from the sources are doubled here. "Both" means Amex and Chase points transfer to the program.

## What the table leaves out

**Fare types.** Since September 8, 2026, Flying Blue sells Light, Standard and Flex awards. The 25,000 and 60,000 one-way prices are Light fares. Light economy has no checked bag, no changes and no refunds. Standard starts at 30,000 in economy and 75,000 in business.

**Surcharges.** A Virgin Atlantic business round trip at 58,000 points can carry about $1,400 in surcharges, by The Points Guy's one-way figure. Frequent Miler notes that Virgin Atlantic awards on Delta flights can add $1,000 or more each way.

**Delta.** Amex points move to Delta SkyMiles, where prices follow the cash fare. Thrifty Traveler (April 2026) reports sale fares from 26,000 miles round trip and basic economy above 100,000 on other dates. AwardWallet found Los Angeles to Paris at 68,000.

**Transfer time.** ANA transfers from Amex take two to three days. The seat can go in that time.

## Booking through the card's travel site instead

Any seat for sale can be bought with points at a fixed rate:

- Amex Travel: 1 cent per point on flights.
- Chase Travel: 1 cent per point, or up to 2 cents on a Sapphire Reserve when a booking carries [a Points Boost](/chase-points-boost-explained/).

As an illustration only: a $700 economy fare costs 70,000 points at 1 cent. A $3,500 business fare costs 350,000 points at 1 cent and 175,000 at 2 cents. These fares are made-up round numbers, not quotes.

So for economy, a partner award helps most when cash fares are high. For business class, partner awards are usually far below the travel-site price in points, if a seat is open. To compare a given seat, use [the cents-per-point check](/points-or-cash-when-to-pay-with-points/).

## Where sources disagree

- **Iberia business class.** The Points Guy and a June 2026 AwardWallet page give 40,500 Avios one way off-peak from New York and Boston. An older AwardWallet page still prints 34,000. Frequent Miler gives 50,500, which matches The Points Guy's price from Chicago and Miami.
- **Aeroplan.** Thrifty Traveler (April 2026) gives 70,000 round trip in economy and 70,000 one way in business. Air Canada's chart changed on June 1, 2026; the table uses the newer figures. The business price on trips under 4,000 miles after that change was not confirmed.
- **Flying Blue fees.** Frequent Miler says about $300 to $350 each way; Flying Blue's own business example is $608.

## Who should skip a partner award

- Travelers with fixed dates in July or August. The lowest prices are scarce then.
- Anyone who would pay $1,400 in surcharges to save points on a seat that costs little more in cash.
- People unwilling to search for the seat before transferring. Transfers cannot be reversed.

Which programs take which points: [Amex partners](/amex-transfer-partners-which-are-worth-it/) and [Chase partners](/chase-transfer-partners-which-are-worth-it/).

## Quick answers

**What is the lowest points price to Europe in economy?**
Virgin Atlantic to London, from 6,000 points each way from the East Coast, plus taxes near $160 out and more coming back.

**How many points for business class to Europe?**
From about 81,000 round trip on Iberia off-peak to 120,000 on Flying Blue, plus fees. Most other programs charge more.

**Can Chase points book ANA awards?**
Not through ANA Mileage Club, which takes Amex points only. Chase points reach Europe through Flying Blue, Virgin Atlantic, Avios programs, Aeroplan and United.
