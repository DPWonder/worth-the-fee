# Rakuten or TopCashback: which pays more, and how do payouts differ?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-vs-topcashback/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Neither site pays more at every store; rates on both change often, so the higher one depends on the store and the day. The clearer difference is the payout: Rakuten pays on four fixed dates once you have about $5 confirmed, while TopCashback lets you cash out on request once cash back is marked available.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## Payouts side by side

| | Rakuten | TopCashback |
|---|---|---|
| Cost to join | Free | Free |
| When you are paid | February 15, May 15, August 15, November 15 | Any time, once cash back is "available" |
| Minimum | About $5 confirmed ($5 on one Rakuten page, $5.01 in its terms) | None, per The Points Guy; some gift cards may have one |
| Cash methods | Check, PayPal | Bank transfer, Venmo, PayPal |
| Other methods | Amex Membership Rewards points, Bilt points, Samsung Cash, gift cards | Gift cards; prepaid cards |
| Wait before money is yours | Typically 3 to 14 weeks to confirm, then the next date | Set by each store; up to 90 days, per The Points Guy |

TopCashback's own help page says each store has a different period before cash back becomes available. Travel becomes available only after the stay is complete.

How many days a TopCashback transfer takes after you request it is not confirmed; its help pages do not give a figure.

For Rakuten's schedule in detail, see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

## Which pays more?

There is no fixed answer. Three things are known:

- NerdWallet's comparison says TopCashback's rates are often higher than Rakuten's.
- One personal finance blog tracked 15 stores during 2025. It found TopCashback higher at 10, Rakuten higher at 4, and one tie. That is one writer's unverified test.
- Rakuten often raises a store's rate for a short time. In early November 2026 it ran ten one-day offers at rates from 12% to 30%, according to Upgraded Points.

Rakuten's terms also say rates are personalized and may differ between members. The practical step is to look at both sites on the day you buy.

## TopCashback's rate guarantee

TopCashback has a Highest Cash Back Guarantee. Its help page describes the steps: buy through TopCashback, file a claim once the cash back has tracked, and it will beat the other offer after approving the claim.

The Points Guy lists the conditions, as of its November 2025 article:

- It covers higher rates from eight named competitors, Rakuten among them.
- The higher rate must have been on offer the same day as your purchase.
- The claim must be filed within seven days.
- It matches cash rates only, not points or miles.

The full terms sit behind a sign-in and were not read for this page.

## Where Rakuten differs

Rakuten can pay in American Express Membership Rewards points at 1 point per cent, if you hold an eligible card. A reader who gets more than 1 cent of value from a point comes out ahead of the same rate paid in cash. TopCashback's guarantee does not match points. See [whether Amex points are worth more than cash](/rakuten-amex-points-worth-more-than-cash/).

## Where TopCashback differs

- No fixed payment dates.
- Bank transfer and Venmo are payout choices.
- The Points Guy says some payouts taken as store credit or a prepaid card usually come with a bonus of up to 5%, at certain merchants.

## Can you use both?

You can hold both accounts. One order can be tracked by only one of them. Rakuten's help center says a click from another rewards site disables its cash back, and it asks members to turn off competing extensions. Pick one before you add items to the cart.

## Where sources disagree

- **Payout methods.** TopCashback's help page lists bank, Venmo, PayPal and gift cards. One blog also lists a paper check, which TopCashback's page does not mention.
- **Minimums.** The Points Guy says TopCashback has none. Other summaries say some gift card options start at a few dollars.

## Who should skip it

- Skip Rakuten for a given order if TopCashback's rate is clearly higher that day and you want cash.
- Skip TopCashback if you collect Amex points; it pays cash and gift cards.

## Quick answers

**Does TopCashback have a minimum payout?**
The Points Guy says no. Some gift card options may have their own minimum.

**How often does Rakuten pay?**
Four times a year: February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15.

**Can I get cash back from both on one order?**
No. Only one site can track a purchase. Choose one before you shop.
