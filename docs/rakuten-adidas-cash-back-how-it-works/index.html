What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Adidas list these as earning nothing:

  • Collaborations "such as" Edison Chen, Y-3, Fear of God, Bad Bunny, Wales Bonner, Pharrell, Song for the Mute and Willy Chavarria
  • Gift cards
  • Purchases made in the Confirmed app
  • Orders Adidas judges to be for reselling
  • High-volume orders, defined as 10 or more of the same eligible item
  • Orders from outside the US

The words "such as" mean the list of collaborations is examples, not the full list. Whether a collaboration that is not named earns cash back is not confirmed.

Terms worth knowing before you order

  • Orders over $1,500. These may be subject to review and reversal. Nike's terms set that line at $500.
  • Coupon codes. The terms say codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.
  • Returns. The Adidas terms on Rakuten say nothing about returns. Rakuten's general rule is that a returned item loses its cash back. Nike's terms are stricter on this point; see how Rakuten works at Nike.

App, pickup and member discounts

  • App. Only the Confirmed app is named as excluded. The terms do not mention the main Adidas app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension, so an order started directly in a store app is not expected to track.
  • Pickup. The terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
  • adiClub. Whether a member discount, an adiClub voucher or adiClub points affect cash back is not confirmed.
  • Student and hero discounts. The page mentions a 15% student discount and discounts for verified groups such as first responders and military. These usually arrive as a code. A code not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.

In stores

Adidas did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 21, 2026. The list was cut off, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.

When you get the money

The Adidas page says cash back is added to a Rakuten account the next day. It then waits to be confirmed, which typically takes 3 to 14 weeks, and is paid on the next of four dates a year. See when Rakuten pays and what to do when cash back did not track. Card rewards are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • You buy collaborations. The named ones are excluded.
  • You buy through the Confirmed app.
  • You have a student or hero discount code worth more than the cash back. On a $100 order, 15% off is $15 and 6% back is $6.
  • You pay with or buy Adidas gift cards.
  • You buy 10 or more of one item, or buy to resell.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Adidas? Up to 6% on November 21, 2026, against a usual 2%. Rates change daily.

Do Sambas earn cash back? Yes, at 2% on that date. Samba, Gazelle, Spezial and EVO SL were held at 2% while other items earned 6%.

Can I use an Adidas promo code and still get cash back? The terms say codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. Codes shown on Rakuten are the safe ones.
