Rakuten: every short answer on one page

Each answer links to its full page, with sources and the date it was checked. This is information, not financial advice.

Does Rakuten give cash back on Amazon?

Not in the usual way. On November 21, 2026 Rakuten listed Amazon with no percentage rate, only a flat $5 on each of the first three eligible Amazon orders for members in their first 60 days. Longer-standing members saw no ongoing Amazon rate on that page.

How does Rakuten work, step by step?

You create a free account, click through Rakuten to a store, and buy as usual. The store pays Rakuten a commission for the sale, Rakuten passes part of it to you as cash back, and confirmed cash back is paid four times a year once you have about $5.

Is Rakuten cash back taxable, and what about the referral bonus?

Cash back on things you buy is generally treated as a rebate that lowers the price you paid, not as income. A referral bonus is different: you did not buy anything to earn it, Rakuten's terms say you may be taxed on it, and it can be reported to the IRS.

Is Rakuten legit, and how does it make money if it pays you?

Yes, Rakuten is a real company that has paid cash back since 1999. Stores pay it a commission for each shopper it sends, and it passes part of that commission to you; in return it collects data about what you browse and buy.

Rakuten sign-up bonus: how the bonus from a friend's link works and what makes it fail

You join Rakuten through a friend's link, then make purchases that earn cash back totalling at least $50 within 90 days of signing up. The bonus fails most often because the purchase did not track, was returned, or was made at a store or on an item that earns no cash back.

Rakuten Adidas cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Adidas page showed up to 6% cash back, raised from a usual 2%. Samba, Gazelle, Spezial and EVO SL shoes earn 2%, and named collaborations, gift cards and purchases in the Confirmed app earn nothing.

Can you get Amex points from Rakuten instead of cash, and is it worth switching?

Yes. If you hold a US American Express card that earns Membership Rewards, Rakuten will pay you 1 point for every cent of cash back in place of the cash. It is worth switching only if you reliably get more than 1 cent of value from each point, which in practice means moving points to airline or hotel programs.

Are Amex points from Rakuten worth more than the cash?

Only when you redeem each point for more than 1 cent. Rakuten gives 1 Membership Rewards point per cent of cash back, so $100 becomes 10,000 points; those points beat $100 through airline transfers and a few card-specific options, and fall short as statement credits, shopping or most gift cards.

Rakuten app or browser extension: which tracks purchases more reliably?

No source gives tracking figures for one against the other, so a measured answer is not confirmed. Rakuten's own guidance is to use the extension on a computer and the app on a phone, and each one fails in different, known situations.

Rakuten Apple cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes, but on a narrow list. Apple was listed on Rakuten at 2% on November 21, 2026, and only for accessories, Apple Refurbished products, AppleCare+ and Beats. A new iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch or AirPods earns nothing.

Rakuten Best Buy cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Best Buy page showed up to 8% cash back, set by category and brand, with most general categories at 1%. Apple products, video game consoles, gift cards, services and warranties are excluded.

Rakuten payout: check or PayPal, and what are the dates and minimum?

A check and a PayPal payment are for the same amount, on the same four dates (February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15), with the same minimum of about $5 confirmed. The difference is handling: a paper check comes by mail and is good for 180 days, while PayPal needs a valid PayPal email address in your Rakuten account.

Rakuten on Black Friday 2026: how cash back works and which rates are raised

Rakuten cash back works on Black Friday (November 27, 2026) and Cyber Monday (November 30) the same way it does on any other day: activate first, then buy. What changes is that many stores' rates are raised for short periods, so the catches are rates that move, items a store excludes, and orders that fail to track.

Is the Rakuten browser extension safe, and what data does it collect?

The extension is published by Rakuten itself and listed in the official Chrome and Firefox stores. It collects the addresses of pages you visit, whether or not you are using it at that moment, plus shopping details such as searches, cart items and order totals. Uninstalling it stops that collection.

Rakuten Chewy cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes. On November 22, 2026, Rakuten's Chewy page showed 4% cash back, raised from a usual 2%. Pet prescriptions, flea and tick products, Chewy+ subscriptions, gift cards and every Autoship order after the first are excluded.

What does Rakuten do for Cyber Monday 2026, and how is it different from Black Friday?

For Cyber Monday (November 30, 2026) Rakuten runs a Cyber Monday page listing stores with raised cash back rates, but as of November 23 it had not published the rates that will apply on the day. Rakuten describes the difference from Black Friday as one of origin: Black Friday grew from in-store sales, Cyber Monday from online ones, and the two now overlap.

Rakuten Dick's Sporting Goods cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes. On November 22, 2026, Rakuten's Dick's Sporting Goods page showed 9% cash back, raised from a usual 2%. Gift cards, extended warranties, services, three named brands, the part of an order paid with rewards and bulk orders are excluded.

Rakuten eBay cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes, in select departments. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's eBay page showed 1% cash back, capped at $50 on any single purchase. Electronics, phones, computers, video games, gift cards and purchases made by sending the seller an offer are excluded.

Does Rakuten give cash back on Expedia and Booking.com, and when does it pay out?

Yes, both are listed. On November 21, 2026 Rakuten showed Expedia at up to 3% and Booking.com at up to 8%, with flights at a flat $1 to $1.25. The cash back is added only after the trip is over: within a week at Expedia, within three months at Booking.com.

Do gift card purchases earn Rakuten cash back?

Mostly no. Rakuten says most gift cards are excluded, and every store listing read for this page excluded them. The exception is a gift card seller with its own Rakuten listing: Giftcards.com showed 3% and CardCash 1% on November 21, 2026.

Can you get Rakuten cash back on golf clubs and gear?

Yes. Several golf retailers and club makers are on Rakuten, and most paid 2% to 3% cash back when checked on November 20, 2026. One retailer, PGA TOUR Superstore, showed a raised rate of 10% that day against a usual 2%.

Rakuten Home Depot cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

No. The Home Depot has a page on Rakuten, but on November 21, 2026 that page showed "No Cash Back". Shopping there through Rakuten earned nothing that day, on any category.

Does Rakuten give cash back on hotels and flights?

Yes for hotels: booking sites such as Expedia, Booking.com and Priceline paid up to 8% to 10% through Rakuten when checked on November 20, 2026. Flights earn very little, usually a flat $1 to $2 per booking, and travel cash back is confirmed only after the trip is over.

How long does a Rakuten purchase take to show up in your account?

Rakuten says a new order is typically "processing" for 24 to 48 hours and then shows as pending cash back in your account. Travel bookings can take until after the trip, and Rakuten's terms give you 90 days from the purchase to report cash back that never appeared.

How do you switch Rakuten from cash back to Amex points, and can you switch back?

You switch in Rakuten's Account Settings: change the payment method to Membership Rewards points, then sign in to your American Express Membership Rewards account to link it. Whether you can switch back to cash is not settled; Rakuten's help pages do not say, one large travel site says no, and two others report that it works.

How does Rakuten in-store cash back work, and which cards can you link?

You link a payment card to your Rakuten account, add a store's in-store offer before you shop, and pay at the register with that card. Rakuten says you can link most Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards and most American Express cards.

Rakuten Kohl's cash back: does it work, and does it stack with Kohl's Cash?

Yes. Kohl's was listed on Rakuten at 2% on November 21, 2026. The part of an order you pay for with Kohl's Cash earns no Rakuten cash back, and whether a Rakuten-tracked order still earns new Kohl's Cash is not confirmed by either company.

Does Rakuten give cash back on a laptop from Dell, HP or Lenovo?

Yes, all three are listed. On November 21, 2026 Rakuten showed 2% at Dell, 2% at Lenovo and 1% on HP home laptops (3% on other HP items). Outlet stores, business stores, warranties and orders using an outside coupon code are mostly excluded.

How do you link a credit card to Rakuten for in-store cash back, and which cards work?

In your Rakuten account, open Linked Cards, select Link a Card and enter the card details. Rakuten says most Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards and most American Express cards can be linked; Discover, Apple Card and Wells Fargo Mastercards cannot.

Rakuten Lowe's cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Lowe's page showed up to 2% cash back, raised from a usual 1%. Appliances earn 1%, and lumber, decking, installation orders and gift cards earn nothing.

Rakuten Macy's cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Macy's page showed 13% cash back, raised from a usual 2%. Furniture, mattresses, electronics and marketplace seller items earn 1.5%, and gift cards and a list of brands earn nothing.

Rakuten order not showing up or cash back missing: what causes it and how do you claim it?

Cash back usually fails to track because the click from Rakuten to the store was lost, or because the item or order was excluded. If the store's terms say you should have earned it, file Rakuten's Missing Cash Back form; Rakuten's terms say to do so within 90 days of the purchase.

Rakuten Nike cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Nike page showed 4% cash back, raised from a usual 2%. New releases, select Jordans, purchases in the SNKRS app, gift cards and orders paid with gift cards are excluded.

Rakuten Nordstrom and Nordstrom Rack cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Not at Nordstrom on the day checked, and yes at Nordstrom Rack. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Nordstrom page showed "No Cash Back", while its Nordstrom Rack page showed 2%. At the Rack, gift cards and five brands are excluded.

Rakuten Old Navy cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes. On November 22, 2026, Rakuten's Old Navy page showed 2% cash back, with no raised rate. Gift cards, e-gift cards, Joseph Altuzarra items and the part of an order paid for with reward points are excluded.

Rakuten Samsung cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Samsung page showed 2% cash back, with phones, TVs and appliances all listed at that rate. Cash back is paid only on the amount left after trade-in credit or Samsung Rewards, and Employee Perks orders, memory, Samsung Care+ and gift cards earn nothing.

Rakuten Sephora cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Sephora page showed 4% cash back, raised from a usual 2%. Gift cards, auto-ship subscription orders and the part of an order paid with rewards are excluded, and a coupon code not listed on Rakuten may void it.

Can you take the Rakuten sign-up bonus as Amex points instead of cash?

Rakuten's own pages say yes for its standard welcome bonus: it is added to your next cash back payment or points transfer, and Rakuten's American Express page states the bonus in points. For the bonus that comes with a friend's referral link, Rakuten's pages are silent and travel sites disagree, so that part is not confirmed.

Can you stack Rakuten cash back with credit card points and store coupons?

Yes. Rakuten cash back and your credit card's rewards come from two different companies, so one purchase can earn both. Store coupons often work as well, but Rakuten says a code it did not list can void the cash back, and a second cash back site cannot be added on top.

Rakuten Target cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes, but only on some departments. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Target page showed 1% cash back, limited to categories such as apparel, home, furniture, kitchen and beauty. Grocery, electronics, toys, gift cards and Drive Up orders are excluded.

Can you stack Rakuten cash back with Target Circle offers and the Target Circle Card 5%?

Yes, in most cases. Rakuten's terms for Target do not exclude paying with a Target Circle Card, and Rakuten's own Target page describes combining Target Circle offers, a rewards card and cash back on one order. The limits are that only some categories earn the 1% seen on November 23, 2026, a coupon code not listed on Rakuten voids the cash back, and Drive Up orders earn nothing.

Rakuten Ulta cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes. Ulta Beauty was listed on Rakuten at 2% on November 21, 2026. Dior products, Marketplace items, gift cards and the part of an order paid with Ulta rewards earn nothing.

Rakuten or Capital One Shopping: how do they differ, and can you use both?

Rakuten pays cash back by check or PayPal (or as points) four times a year, while Capital One Shopping pays Shopping Rewards that you redeem for e-gift cards. You can hold both accounts, but only one of them can track a given purchase, and both companies say to turn off other rewards extensions.

Rakuten or TopCashback: which pays more, and how do payouts differ?

Neither site pays more at every store; rates on both change often, so the higher one depends on the store and the day. The clearer difference is the payout: Rakuten pays on four fixed dates once you have about $5 confirmed, while TopCashback lets you cash out on request once cash back is marked available.

Rakuten Walmart cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Yes, in a limited form. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Walmart page showed a flat $5 cash back on each of up to three eligible orders of $25 or more, not a percentage of the order. Groceries, pharmacy items and orders placed in the Walmart app are excluded.

Rakuten Wayfair cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Not on the day checked. On November 22, 2026, Rakuten's Wayfair page showed "No Cash Back", with no rate, no "was" rate and no terms or exclusions. Wayfair has a page on Rakuten, but a purchase started from it that day would earn nothing.

When does Rakuten pay you, and when is the next payout?

Rakuten pays four times a year, on February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15, as long as you have about $5 in confirmed cash back. The next payout after November 15, 2026 is February 15, 2027. A purchase can take months to be paid because the store must confirm it first, and only then does it wait for the next date.
