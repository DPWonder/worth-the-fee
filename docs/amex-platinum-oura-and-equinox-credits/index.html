What are the Amex Platinum Oura and Equinox credits worth if you would not buy them anyway?

Facts checked November 21, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

To someone who would not buy an Oura Ring or an Equinox membership anyway, the two credits are worth close to $0. The $200 Oura credit is a discount on a ring that starts at $349, and the $300 Equinox credit pays for a fitness app or a small part of a gym membership.

Both are charged in full to the Platinum Card first. The money comes back as a statement credit. Upgraded Points reports the Oura credit usually appears within 48 hours. Amex's terms allow up to 8 weeks.

The Oura sums

The credit covers hardware only. The ring also has a membership fee that the credit does not touch.

Item Cost
Oura Ring 4, lowest-priced finishes $349 plus tax
Amex credit Minus $200
You pay for the ring $149 plus tax
Oura membership $5.99 a month or $69.99 a year
First-year cost with annual membership About $219 plus tax

Other finishes cost $399 or $499, and the ceramic model starts at $499, according to Upgraded Points. Upgraded Points also says the ring works with limited features without a membership.

So the credit does not make a ring free. For a person who was about to buy one, it is worth $200. For a person who was not, it is an offer to spend $149 or more.

The credit returns each year, but a ring is not a yearly purchase for most people. Count $200 in the years you would buy one, not $200 every year.

The Equinox sums

There are two ways to use this credit.

The app. Equinox+ is a workout app. It normally costs $39.99 a month. Upgraded Points reports that Platinum cardmembers are offered an annual price of $300, which the credit covers in full. The Points Guy also says the credit can cover a full year of the app. The app does not include entry to Equinox clubs.

A club membership. Sources give different prices because rates vary by city:

Source Club price per month
The Points Guy $205 to $415
Upgraded Points $200 to $500, before initiation fees
AwardWallet (2025) $325 for all-access

At $300 a month, a year costs $3,600. The credit pays for one month of it. Equinox has a little over 100 clubs, in large cities in the US, Canada and the UK, so most people do not live near one.

The membership must be bought through the Amex enrollment page. Subscribing in the Equinox app or directly on the Equinox site does not earn the credit, according to Upgraded Points and AwardWallet. Memberships renew by themselves.

A related credit

The card also lists up to $300 back on a SoulCycle at-home bike, which costs $2,500 plus tax and needs an Equinox+ membership. It is a discount on a large purchase, not a credit most cardholders will use.

Not confirmed

  • Whether additional cardholders share the one Oura credit. The Equinox credit is one per account.
  • Whether the Oura credit resets on January 1 or on another date. AwardWallet describes it as once per calendar year; Upgraded Points calls it an annual credit and gives no reset date.

Who should skip it

Leave both credits out of your sums if:

  • You do not track sleep or fitness and do not plan to start.
  • You already own a fitness tracker you are happy with.
  • You do not live near an Equinox club and have a workout routine you like.
  • You would buy either one mainly because a credit exists.

Together these two credits make up $500 of the card's advertised yearly total. To see how that changes the sums, see what the credits are worth.

Quick answers

Does the Amex Platinum Oura credit cover the Oura membership? No. It covers the ring only, bought at Ouraring.com. The membership is $5.99 a month or $69.99 a year.

Can the Equinox credit be used without joining a gym? Yes. It can pay for the Equinox+ app, which does not include club entry.

Is an Oura Ring free with the Amex Platinum? No. Rings start at $349 plus tax, so at least $149 is left to pay after the $200 credit.
