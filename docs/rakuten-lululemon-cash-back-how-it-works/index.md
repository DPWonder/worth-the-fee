# Rakuten Lululemon cash back: does it work, and what works instead?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-lululemon-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-24
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Not today. On November 24, 2026, Rakuten's lululemon page read "No Cash Back", so a purchase at lululemon.com started from Rakuten earned nothing. The same day, Rakuten showed 3% on lululemon eGift cards sold by Giftcards.com.

This page is information, not financial advice. Store pages change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## What the page showed on November 24, 2026

| Rakuten page | What it showed |
|---|---|
| lululemon store page | "No Cash Back" |
| lululemon Cyber Monday page | "No Cash Back" in the page title |
| lululemon gift cards page | 3% on eGift cards sold by Giftcards.com (was 1%) |

Two Rakuten addresses for the store led to the same page. It had no "Shop Now" button, no "was" rate and no Terms & Exclusions section. Coupons, reviews and shopping tips were still listed, so the store has a page on Rakuten but no cash back offer.

## It has paid before

The Points Guy listed lululemon at 15% on Rakuten on Black Friday 2025 (November 28, 2025). So the store has had a rate in the past. Whether a rate returns for Black Friday (November 27, 2026) or Cyber Monday (November 30, 2026) is not confirmed, and nothing on Rakuten's page promises one. Why the offer is off is not confirmed either.

## What works instead

**A lululemon eGift card bought through Rakuten.** Rakuten's own lululemon gift cards page listed eGift cards of $50, $100 and $250 sold by Giftcards.com, at 3% cash back against a usual 1%. The purchase is made at Giftcards.com, not at lululemon, and Rakuten's terms for Giftcards.com apply:

- Purchases over $2,000 earn nothing.
- The part of an order paid with rewards earns nothing.
- Coupon codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.
- Cash back is added to the Rakuten account the next day, as pending.

| eGift card | At 3% | At the usual 1% |
|---|---|---|
| $50 | $1.50 | $0.50 |
| $100 | $3.00 | $1.00 |
| $250 | $7.50 | $2.50 |

A gift card is prepaid money. Check lululemon's own gift card terms before buying one for an order you might return. How a return is refunded on a gift card order is not confirmed here. More on this route is on [Rakuten and gift cards](/rakuten-gift-cards-cash-back/).

**The Platinum Card credit.** The Platinum Card from American Express has a lululemon credit of up to $75 each calendar quarter after enrollment, at US lululemon stores and lululemon.com. It needs no shopping portal. Gift cards and outlet stores are excluded from it, so it does not combine with the gift card route on the same dollars. Details are on [how the lululemon credit works](/amex-platinum-lululemon-credit-how-it-works/).

**Card rewards.** The points or cash back from the card you pay with do not depend on Rakuten. See [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

**The store's own programs.** Rakuten's page describes a free lululemon membership with early access to product drops, and a sale section called We Made Too Much. Those are the store's, not Rakuten's, and can change.

Rates at other cash back sites were not confirmed for this page.

Taxes and shipping are left out. The gift card row assumes the card is used in full.

## If the extension shows nothing

Rakuten's help page says that when the extension shows no Activate Cash Back button, the store may not be available for it. With "No Cash Back" on the store page there is no rate to activate, and no rate to claim afterward. For orders at stores that do have a rate, see [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## In stores

Lululemon did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 24, 2026. The list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Who should skip it

- You are buying at lululemon.com today and expect Rakuten cash back. There is none.
- You may return the order. A gift card ties the money to the store.
- You hold the Platinum Card and have this quarter's credit unused. That is worth more than 3%.
- You are waiting on a rate to return. Nothing confirms that it will.

## Quick answers

**Does Rakuten give cash back at lululemon?**
Not on November 24, 2026. The store page read "No Cash Back". Check the page on the day you buy.

**Has Rakuten ever paid cash back at lululemon?**
Yes. The Points Guy listed 15% on Black Friday 2025. No rate is promised for 2026.

**Can you earn cash back on a lululemon gift card?**
Rakuten showed 3% on lululemon eGift cards sold by Giftcards.com on November 24, 2026, against a usual 1%.
