Rakuten JCPenney cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Facts checked November 26, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

No, not as of November 26, 2026. Rakuten still has a JCPenney page, but it is labeled "Coupons Only" and states that JCPenney does not offer cash back on Rakuten. A JCPenney order started from Rakuten earns $0; what does work is cash back at other department stores and the rewards on the card you pay with.

Because there is no cash back, there is also no exclusion list. Nothing on the page says which JCPenney products would or would not earn, since none do.

Where the page disagrees with itself

The header says JCPenney does not offer cash back. The description further down the same page still talks about cash back through Rakuten and says members have earned more than $11 million in cash back at JCPenney to date. That figure points to a time when the store did pay. When it stopped, and whether it will pay again, is not confirmed. The header is the part Rakuten uses to tell you what a purchase earns today.

Rakuten also keeps a JCPenney Black Friday page. The rates shown on it sit under other stores' names and do not apply to JCPenney.

These sums only help if the other store sells the same item at the same price, and if the item is not on that store's exclusion list. A lower shelf price at JCPenney can outweigh 12% back somewhere else.

What still works at JCPenney

  • Card rewards. A card pays its usual rewards on a JCPenney purchase with or without Rakuten. A card that pays 2% on everything returns $1.50 on a $75 order. How card rewards and shopping sites fit together is covered in stacking Rakuten with card rewards.
  • JCPenney's own offers. Rakuten's page says JCPenney lets different types of coupons be combined, such as a free shipping coupon with a percent-off coupon, but not two of the same type. It also describes a price match that leaves out marketplaces and third-party sellers. These are Rakuten's descriptions; JCPenney's own site has the current rules.
  • In stores. JCPenney did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 26, 2026. The list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.

Who should skip it

  • You are buying at JCPenney and hoping for a rebate from Rakuten. There is none to activate.
  • You planned to make a JCPenney order your qualifying purchase for a Rakuten joining bonus. A purchase has to earn cash back to count, and this one earns none.
  • You would switch stores only for the rate. The item, its price and the other store's exclusions decide whether that pays.

Quick answers

Does Rakuten pay cash back at JCPenney? No. On November 26, 2026, the page was labeled "Coupons Only" and showed no rate.

Did JCPenney ever pay cash back on Rakuten? The page says members earned more than $11 million there. When it stopped is not confirmed.

Which department stores did pay that day? Kohl's showed 2%, Macy's 12% online and Belk 13%. Rates change daily.
