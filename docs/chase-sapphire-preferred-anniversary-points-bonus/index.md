# How does the Sapphire Preferred 10% anniversary points bonus work?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/chase-sapphire-preferred-anniversary-points-bonus/
Facts checked: 2026-11-21
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

The Sapphire Preferred's 10% anniversary points bonus has ended: people who applied on or after June 15, 2026 never get it, and for earlier cardholders it stopped counting purchases after October 1, 2026. Chase says the last bonuses will be awarded by January 31, 2027, and in its place the card gained new 3x categories and a larger hotel credit.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## How the bonus worked

Each account anniversary, Chase added points equal to 10% of the dollars you spent on purchases in the year before. It was based on spending, not on the points earned in bonus categories. The Points Guy describes it as adding 0.1 points per dollar.

| Yearly spending | Anniversary bonus | Value at 1 cent a point |
|---|---|---|
| $10,000 | 1,000 points | $10 |
| $20,000 | 2,000 points | $20 |
| $50,000 | 5,000 points | $50 |

The $20,000 and $50,000 rows match the examples given by Thrifty Traveler. The other figures follow the same rule.

## The end dates

| Who | What happens |
|---|---|
| Applied on or after June 15, 2026 | No anniversary bonus at all |
| Applied before June 15, 2026 | Purchases through October 1, 2026 still count |
| Final payout | Chase: all bonuses earned are awarded by January 31, 2027 |

Chase's wording, in its June 15 announcement: "The 10% Anniversary Bonus will be discontinued."

Whether the final bonus arrives on your own anniversary or as one deposit for everyone is not confirmed. AwardWallet says it is posted in early 2027. If you had the card before June 15, 2026 and spent on it up to October 1, look for a line in your points activity by the end of January 2027.

## What replaced it

Chase did not swap the bonus for one thing. The same June 2026 refresh made these changes, with the fee still $95:

| Change | Detail |
|---|---|
| Gas and EV charging | 3x, up from 1x |
| Vacation homes at brands such as Airbnb and Vrbo | 3x |
| Hotel credit | Up to $100 a year on Chase Travel hotel stays, up from $50 |
| Global Entry, TSA PreCheck or NEXUS | Credit up to $120 every four years |
| Hyatt transfers | Now 4 Chase points to 3 Hyatt points, down from 1:1 |

The hotel credit has its own page: [how the $100 hotel credit works](/chase-sapphire-preferred-50-hotel-credit-how-it-works/). The Hyatt change is on the [Hyatt transfer page](/chase-to-hyatt-transfer-change-2026/).

## Did you come out ahead or behind?

Compare what the bonus paid you with what the new terms pay. Two illustrations, not typical results:

| | Reader A | Reader B |
|---|---|---|
| Yearly spending on the card | $15,000 | $40,000 |
| Old bonus lost | 1,500 points | 4,000 points |
| Gas spending | $2,000 | $0 |
| Extra points from 3x gas (2 more per dollar) | 4,000 points | 0 |
| Extra hotel credit, if used | $50 | $0 |
| Net | Ahead by 2,500 points and $50 | Behind by 4,000 points |

A reader who buys gas on the card and books one hotel a year through Chase Travel is ahead. A reader with high spending in categories that earn 1x, who does not use the hotel credit, is behind.

The sum leaves out the Hyatt change. Someone who moved many points to Hyatt each year loses more from the 4:3 ratio than from the bonus.

## What to do about it

Nothing needs to be done to receive the final bonus. Three things are worth checking:

1. Your points activity in early 2027, if you held the card before June 15, 2026.
2. Whether gas or EV charging should move to this card now that it earns 3x.
3. Whether everyday 1x spending would earn more on a no-fee card. See [pairing the Preferred with Freedom cards](/chase-sapphire-preferred-with-freedom-cards-combine-points/).

## Who should skip it

This is no longer a reason to choose or keep the card.

- Anyone comparing cards from an old review that lists the 10% bonus should strike it from the sum.
- Anyone who kept the card mainly for the bonus on heavy 1x spending should rerun the numbers; a 1.5% card may return more on that spending.
- New applicants get nothing from it and should not count it.

## Quick answers

**Does the Sapphire Preferred still have the 10% anniversary bonus?**
No. Chase ended it in 2026. Purchases after October 1, 2026 earn no anniversary bonus.

**When is the last anniversary bonus paid?**
Chase says all bonuses earned are awarded by January 31, 2027. The exact posting date per account is not confirmed.

**Was the bonus 10% of points or 10% of spending?**
It was 10% of the dollars spent on purchases, so $20,000 of spending gave 2,000 points.
