# Rakuten on Black Friday 2026: how cash back works and which rates are raised

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-black-friday-cash-back-2026/
Facts checked: 2026-11-23
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Rakuten cash back works on Black Friday (November 27, 2026) and Cyber Monday (November 30) the same way it does on any other day: activate first, then buy. What changes is that many stores' rates are raised for short periods, so the catches are rates that move, items a store excludes, and orders that fail to track.

This page is information, not financial advice. For this morning's rate at 70 stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The dates

| Day | Date in 2026 |
|---|---|
| Black Friday | Friday, November 27 |
| Cyber Monday | Monday, November 30 |
| Cyber Week | The days after Cyber Monday; length varies by store |

Rakuten's Cyber Monday page says earning that day "works exactly the same as it does every other day." It also says some stores extend offers through the week and some deals last one day only.

## Rates move, sometimes daily

Rakuten shows a raised rate next to the usual one with the label "was". These were on Rakuten's store pages on November 23, 2026, read while signed out.

| Store | Rate seen November 23 | Rate seen November 21 | Rakuten's "was" rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Macy's | 10% | 13% | 2% |
| Dermstore | 10% | 15% | 3% |
| Walgreens | 10% | 2% | 2% |
| LEGO | Up to 15% | Up to 15% | 2.5% |

- Three of the four moved in two days. On November 20 Dermstore showed 20% and Walgreens showed 15%.
- LEGO's 15% is for NINJAGO items. Its page shows 2.5% on other items that are not excluded and 1.5% on exclusives.
- A rate can rise or fall before Black Friday. Rakuten has not published store rates for the day in advance; none were found.
- "Up to" means only some product categories earn the top figure.
- Rakuten's terms say rates are personalized and may differ between members. The number in your account is the one that counts.

Earlier this month Rakuten ran ten one-day offers, one store a day, at rates from 12% to 30%, according to Upgraded Points.

**Not confirmed:** which rate applies if a store's rate changes between your click and your order, and whether Rakuten adjusts an order when the rate rises later. Rakuten's terms do not say.

## Exclusions

Each store sets its own. Common ones in Rakuten's help center:

- Gift cards, at most stores.
- Taxes and shipping.
- Certain product categories, or a lower rate for them.
- Orders placed by phone or chat.
- Orders using a coupon code not listed on Rakuten.

A store's list is behind "See all Terms & Exclusions" on its Rakuten page.

Coupons are the frequent problem. Rakuten says many codes can be combined with cash back, but codes from other sites can void it. See [stacking with coupons and card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Tracking on a busy day

Rakuten lists these reasons an order fails to track:

- Activating after items are already in the cart.
- An ad blocker, VPN or cookie blocker.
- Another rewards or coupon extension taking over the click.
- A long idle session, or checking out in a different tab.

For a Black Friday order that means: empty the cart, activate, add the items, and check out in one sitting.

Check your Rakuten account a day or two later. Rakuten's terms give 90 days from the purchase to report missing cash back. For a November 27 order that is February 25, 2027. See [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## Returns

Rakuten deducts the cash back on returned, exchanged or canceled items. Cash back stays "pending" while the store's return period runs, so a gift that goes back in January reduces the February payment.

## When the money arrives

Rakuten says shopping orders typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed. It pays on fixed dates.

| Order on November 27, 2026 | Confirmed about | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed in 3 weeks | December 18, 2026 | February 15, 2027 |
| Confirmed in 14 weeks | March 5, 2027 | May 15, 2027 |

These dates are worked out from Rakuten's typical range. More in [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). The Monday after has its own page: [Rakuten on Cyber Monday 2026](/rakuten-cyber-monday-cash-back-2026/).

## Who should skip it

- The store's sale price is lower somewhere that Rakuten does not cover. A lower price today is worth more than a rebate in February.
- You have a coupon from another site that is worth more than the cash back.
- You would buy more because the rate looks high. Fifteen percent back still leaves 85% spent.

## Quick answers

**Are Rakuten rates higher on Black Friday?**
Many stores show raised rates in late November, but no rate is set in advance and each can change daily.

**When will Black Friday cash back be paid?**
On February 15 or May 15, 2027, depending on when the store confirms the order.

**Can I add items to my cart before Black Friday?**
Rakuten says activating after items are in the cart can stop tracking. Activate first, then add them.
