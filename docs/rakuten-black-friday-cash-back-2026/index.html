Exclusions

Each store sets its own. Common ones in Rakuten's help center:

  • Gift cards, at most stores.
  • Taxes and shipping.
  • Certain items, categories or brands, or a lower rate for them.
  • Orders placed by phone, chat or customer service.
  • Orders using a coupon code not listed on Rakuten.

A store's list is under "Terms & Exclusions" on its Rakuten page. Rakuten's Black Friday page features Apple, but the Apple listing pays only on accessories, refurbished products, AppleCare+ and Beats, not on iPhones, Macs or iPads. Macy's pays 1.5% on furniture and mattresses and excludes brands such as Apple and Birkenstock.

Rakuten's Black Friday page says many coupon codes can be used together with cash back, while its help center says codes not listed on Rakuten may void it. See stacking with coupons and card rewards.

Tracking on a busy day

Rakuten lists these reasons an order fails to track:

  • Activating after items are already in the cart.
  • An ad blocker, VPN or cookie blocker, or declined cookies.
  • Another rewards or coupon site or extension taking over the click.
  • A long idle session, or checking out in a different tab.

For a Black Friday order that means: empty the cart, activate, add the items, and check out in one sitting.

Rakuten says an order typically shows in the account within 24 to 48 hours. Its terms give 90 days from the purchase to report missing cash back. For a November 27 order that is February 25, 2027. A store can set a shorter limit; Best Buy's listing says 30 days. See what to do when cash back did not track.

Shopping at the mall instead? Thirteen stores showed raised in-store rates on November 26, and each needs a linked card and an offer added first; see how in-store cash back works.

Returns

Rakuten's help center says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back, and a partial return is adjusted.

When the money arrives

Rakuten's payment page says online orders typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed; its status page says 3 to 17 weeks. It pays on fixed dates.

Who should skip it

  • The store's sale price is lower somewhere that Rakuten does not cover. A lower price today is worth more than a rebate in February.
  • You have a coupon from another site that is worth more than the cash back.
  • You would buy more because the rate looks high. Twelve percent back still leaves 88% spent.

Quick answers

Will Rakuten have Black Friday deals in 2026? Yes. On November 26, 2026 its Black Friday page showed 12% cash back at more than 600 stores.

Are Rakuten rates higher on Black Friday? Many are raised this week, to 12% or more, but no rate is set in advance and each can change daily.

When will Black Friday cash back be paid? On February 15 or May 15, 2027, depending on when the store confirms the order.
