What changed since Thanksgiving, counted from this site's morning readings of 77 store pages:

  • 14 stores rose from November 26, none fell, and 63 were unchanged.
  • The rises include Macy's (12% to 15%), Walgreens (12% to 15%), Petco (20% to 25%), PetSmart (15% to 20%), Nike (6% to 10%), Dick's Sporting Goods (2% to 12%) and Sephora (2% to 8%).
  • 26 of the 77 showed 12% or up to 12%, 10 showed 15%, one 20% and two 25%.
  • Not every store is raised. Apple and Kohl's showed 2% and Target 1%, the same as on November 24. Best Buy showed up to 6%. Walmart showed no cash back.
  • Rates can move within the day. Chewy's page read 8% in the morning and 12% later on November 27.
  • LEGO's top rate, 16%, is for NINJAGO items. Its page shows 12% on other items that are not excluded and 3% on exclusives and hard-to-find items.
  • Rakuten's home page, read signed out, shows higher figures such as 22%. Its note says those include an extra 10% welcome reward for new members, capped at $50.

Not confirmed: whether today's rates hold through the weekend or Cyber Monday, which rate applies if a store's rate changes between your click and your order, and whether Rakuten adjusts an order when the rate rises later. Rakuten's pages and terms do not say.

Exclusions

Each store sets its own. Common ones in Rakuten's help center:

  • Gift cards, at most stores.
  • Taxes and shipping.
  • Certain items, categories or brands, or a lower rate for them.
  • Orders placed by phone, chat or customer service.
  • Orders using a coupon code not listed on Rakuten.

A store's list is under "Terms & Exclusions" on its Rakuten page. The Apple listing pays only on accessories, refurbished products, AppleCare+ and Beats, not on iPhones, Macs or iPads. Macy's pays 1.5% on furniture and mattresses and excludes brands such as Apple and Birkenstock.

Rakuten's Black Friday page says many coupon codes can be used together with cash back, while its help center says codes not listed on Rakuten may void it. See stacking with coupons and card rewards.

Tracking on a busy day

Rakuten lists these reasons an order fails to track:

  • Activating after items are already in the cart.
  • An ad blocker, VPN or cookie blocker, or declined cookies.
  • Another rewards or coupon site or extension taking over the click.
  • A long idle session, or checking out in a different tab.

For a Black Friday order that means: empty the cart, activate, add the items, and check out in one sitting.

Rakuten says an order typically shows in the account within 24 to 48 hours. Its terms give 90 days from the purchase to report missing cash back. For a November 27 order that is February 25, 2027. A store can set a shorter limit; Best Buy's listing says 30 days. See what to do when cash back did not track.

In-store rates need a linked card and an offer added first; see how in-store cash back works. Macy's in-store offer on November 27 was a flat $10 on a purchase of $100 or more, valid through December 13, 2026.

Returns

Rakuten's help center says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back, and a partial return is adjusted.

When the money arrives

Rakuten's payment page says online orders typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed; its status page says 3 to 17 weeks. It pays on fixed dates.

Who should skip it

  • The store's sale price is lower somewhere that Rakuten does not cover. A lower price today is worth more than a rebate in February.
  • You have a coupon from another site that is worth more than the cash back.
  • You would buy more because the rate looks high. Twelve percent back still leaves 88% spent.

Quick answers

Will Rakuten have Black Friday deals in 2026? Yes. On Black Friday, November 27, 2026, its Black Friday page showed 12% cash back at more than 600 stores.

Are Rakuten rates higher on Black Friday? On November 27, 14 of 77 tracked stores rose from the day before and none fell. Rates can change daily.

When will Black Friday cash back be paid? On February 15 or May 15, 2027, depending on when the store confirms the order.
