The short answer

No. On November 27, 2026 (Black Friday), Rakuten had no store page for Anthropologie: the store's address returned Rakuten's page-not-found notice, so there was no rate, no "was" label and no list of exclusions. Clothing, home goods, furniture and gift cards from the store all earn nothing through Rakuten.

With no store page there are no terms to read: no category rates, no coupon-code rule, no posting time and no in-store offer.

One trace of an older listing remains. Rakuten's list of seasonal pages still names a 4th of July sale page for Anthropologie, and that address returned the same not-found notice. That suggests the store was listed at some point. When, at what rate and why it ended are not confirmed.

Two more addresses were tried the same day, for Free People and Urban Outfitters. Both returned the not-found notice as well.

The gift card page is a near miss

Rakuten has a page titled for Anthropologie gift cards. On November 27, 2026 it showed no Anthropologie card. What it listed were cards for other brands sold by CardCash at 1%, and gift card deals marked 3% with a "was 1%" label; the page text did not name the seller of those deals. A rate seen on that page is for those sellers and those cards.

That differs from stores such as Michaels, where the matching gift card page did list the store's own cards; see Rakuten at Michaels. Background on how gift card sellers work on Rakuten is in gift cards and cash back.

Among the product results, items carrying the Anthropologie name were listed by Etsy sellers, at Etsy's 1%. Whether they are new or second-hand is not confirmed. One example on the page was a blouse at $38.00, which at 1% comes to $0.38. Those are purchases from an Etsy seller under Etsy's terms, not from the store.

Loyalty program, stores and the extension

  • Loyalty program. Rakuten has no page for the store, so it says nothing about the store's own loyalty program. That program belongs to the store and does not depend on a Rakuten click.
  • In stores. Anthropologie did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 27, 2026. The list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed.
  • Extension. What the Rakuten browser extension shows on the store's site is not confirmed. With no listing, there is no rate for it to activate.
  • Cyber Monday. Cyber Monday is November 30, 2026. Nothing on Rakuten promises a listing by then.

Who should skip it

  • Anyone looking for an Anthropologie link on Rakuten for cash back. There is no store page.
  • Anyone who sees 1% or 3% on the gift card page and takes it for Anthropologie cards. None were listed.
  • Shoppers who want a specific Anthropologie item. A raised rate at another store does not help if the item is not sold there.
  • Anyone choosing an Etsy listing for the 1% alone. On $38.00 that is $0.38.

Quick answers

Does Rakuten give cash back at Anthropologie? No. On November 27, 2026, Rakuten had no Anthropologie store page and no rate.

Do Anthropologie gift cards earn cash back on Rakuten? None were listed. The page with that title showed other brands' cards only.

Was Anthropologie ever on Rakuten? An old seasonal page address remains in Rakuten's lists, which suggests so. Dates and rates are not confirmed.
