How long does a Rakuten purchase take to show up in your account?

Facts checked November 23, 20264 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

Rakuten says a new order is typically "processing" for 24 to 48 hours and then shows as pending cash back in your account. Travel bookings can take until after the trip, and Rakuten's terms give you 90 days from the purchase to report cash back that never appeared.

Rakuten's two pages on online orders do not use the same figure. One says 24 to 48 hours. The other says the store's notice can take "from a few hours to several days." The speed depends on the store.

Two different things can be missing

Your account keeps two records, and they appear at different times.

The shopping trip. This is the click from Rakuten to the store. Rakuten records it when you click and gives it a number. A trip with no purchase stays in your account for up to three months. How quickly a new trip appears in the list is not confirmed; the help pages give no time.

The cash back. This appears only after the store reports the order. It is the part that takes 24 to 48 hours or longer.

So a trip with no cash back yet, a day after the order, is normal. No trip at all means Rakuten has no record of the click. The causes of each are set out in what to do when cash back did not track.

To look, sign in at Rakuten.com and open Cash Back Activity.

Stores that take longer

Travel is the main case. For travel outside Expedia, Rakuten says you may not see a pending amount until after your travel is complete. Some store pages on Rakuten give their own timing.

Store Timing on its Rakuten page
Expedia Within a week after travel
Booking.com Within 3 months after travel
Priceline Within 3 months after travel
IHG Hotels & Resorts Within 3 months after travel

These were read on November 20, 2026 and can change. More in cash back on hotels and flights and Expedia and Booking.com.

Showing up is not the same as being paid

Once the order shows, it is pending, not payable. Rakuten says pending cash back is typically confirmed in 3 to 14 weeks for online and in-store shopping and 6 to 17 weeks after a trip ends for travel. Confirmed cash back is then paid on the next of four dates a year. See when Rakuten pays.

When to file a missing cash back request

Rakuten's help article gives no set number of days to wait. It says to run through its list of causes and then fill out the Missing Cash Back form. A practical reading of Rakuten's own figures:

  • Online order: after the 24 to 48 hours have passed with nothing showing.
  • In-store purchase: after several days. Restaurant: after 10 days.
  • Expedia booking: after 5 days.
  • Other travel: before 90 days from the booking, even if the trip has not happened.

The last line matters because of the deadline. Rakuten's terms say you must contact Member Services within 90 days of the transaction if cash back was not credited correctly. A trip booked four months ahead can pass that mark before the pending amount would normally appear.

Rakuten's help articles state no deadline; the 90 days is in its terms. How soon the form will accept a request is not confirmed, because it sits behind sign-in. The steps are in the missing cash back guide.

Who should skip it

  • The order was placed in the last two days. Rakuten's stated processing time has not passed.
  • The item is listed under the store's exclusions, or the order was returned or canceled. A request will not change that.
  • The trip has not happened and the booking is recent. Waiting is the expected course, as long as the 90 days are not close.

Quick answers

How long before a Rakuten order appears? Typically 24 to 48 hours, Rakuten says. Another of its pages says a few hours to several days.

Why is my hotel booking not showing? Outside Expedia, Rakuten says travel may not show as pending until the trip is complete.

What is the deadline to report missing cash back? Rakuten's terms say within 90 days of the transaction.
