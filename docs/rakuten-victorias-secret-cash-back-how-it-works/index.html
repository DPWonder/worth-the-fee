Rakuten Victoria's Secret cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

The short answer

Yes. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Victoria's Secret page showed 2% cash back, with no raised rate two days before Black Friday. Gift cards, products sold by third parties, orders judged to be for reselling and orders placed outside the US site earn nothing.

Rate read from Rakuten on November 26, 2026: 2%. It can change within a day. Today's rates at every store we track.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see Rakuten rates today, by store.

The rate

The page showed 2% with no "was" label and no rate by category. With no "was" label, 2% is the plain rate. The Black Friday offers on the page, for Victoria's Secret and for Pink, carried the same 2%. Nothing on the page promises a higher rate on Black Friday (November 27, 2026) or Cyber Monday (November 30, 2026).

What earns and what does not

Purchase Earns cash back?
Items sold by Victoria's Secret on its US site Yes, unless excluded
Gift cards No
Products sold by third parties No
An order the store deems to be for reselling No
An order placed through another country's site No; the terms say it "will void" cash back
Taxes and shipping No, under Rakuten's general rules

The terms do not say how a third-party product is marked on the store's site, or how an order is judged to be for reselling. Both are not confirmed.

Coupon codes

The terms say coupon codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back, and to stick to codes Rakuten provides. On November 25, 2026, the offers listed on the page were sales, such as 40% off everything for Black Friday. No code was shown on the page itself.

The app

Rakuten's page says many of the store's deals are available only in the Victoria's Secret app. The cash back terms do not mention the app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension. Whether an order finished in the store's app tracks is not confirmed.

Rewards program and store card

Rakuten's page describes the free VS and Pink Rewards program: points on each order, free shipping on orders over $50 and a $10 birthday reward. The cash back terms do not name it. Whether an order paid partly with a reward earns on the full amount is not confirmed.

Pickup, stores and returns

  • Pickup. The terms do not mention store pickup. Not confirmed.
  • In stores. The page showed an online rate only. Victoria's Secret did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.
  • Returns. Rakuten's general help page says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back.

When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "today". Payment follows on Rakuten's schedule of four dates a year; see when Rakuten pays.

Who should skip it

  • You have an app-only deal or an emailed code that is not on Rakuten and it saves more than 2%.
  • You are buying gift cards.
  • You are ordering from outside the US site.
  • The item is sold by a third party.
  • You are waiting for a raised rate. None was shown, and none is promised.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Victoria's Secret? 2% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

Do Pink items earn cash back? The Pink offers on the page carried the 2% rate. The terms do not exclude Pink.

Do gift cards earn cash back at Victoria's Secret? No. The terms list gift cards as earning nothing.
