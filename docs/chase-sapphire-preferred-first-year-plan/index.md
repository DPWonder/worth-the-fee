# What should you do in the first year with a Sapphire Preferred to get your $95 back?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/chase-sapphire-preferred-first-year-plan/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

One step covers the fee: book a hotel stay of $100 or more through Chase Travel, and the card's yearly hotel credit returns up to $100 against the $95 annual fee. Everything else in the first year, such as the DoorDash promos, the Apple TV offer and the points you earn, is on top of that.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The first-year checklist

| Step | What you get back | Deadline or limit |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Book one hotel stay through Chase Travel | Up to $100 statement credit | Once each account anniversary year; posts automatically |
| 2. Meet the spending requirement with normal spending | The welcome offer shown when you applied | Public offer today asks for $5,000 in the first 3 months |
| 3. Activate DashPass | DashPass for 12 months (Chase states a $120 value) | Activate by 12/31/2029 |
| 4. Use the DoorDash monthly promo, if you already order there | $10 off one non-restaurant order a month | Expires each month; non-restaurant orders only |
| 5. Activate Apple TV | At least 12 months (Chase states a $180 value) | Activate by 12/31/2026 |
| 6. Pay a Global Entry, TSA PreCheck or NEXUS fee with the card | Up to $120 credit | Once every four years |
| 7. Decide where the points go | 1 cent each or more | No deadline while the account is open |

## Step 1: the hotel credit

This is the step that settles the question. Notes on using it:

- The booking must be made through Chase Travel and paid with the card.
- The first $100 of hotel spending that the credit covers earns no points.
- Compare the Chase Travel price with the hotel's own price for the same room. If Chase Travel is $40 more, the credit is really worth $60 to you.

The credit was $50 until a June 2026 refresh raised it to $100, so older articles may still show $50.

## Step 2: the spending requirement

Welcome offers change and vary by applicant, so go by the terms shown on your own application. The public offer today is 75,000 points after $5,000 in purchases in the first three months, with points posting six to eight weeks after you qualify.

$5,000 in three months is about $1,667 a month. If that is more than you normally put on a card, the offer is not a reason to spend more.

If you held a Sapphire Preferred before, check [the bonus eligibility rules](/chase-sapphire-bonus-eligibility-2026/) first.

## Steps 3 to 6: the partner perks

Count these only if you would pay for the thing anyway.

- **DashPass** lowers delivery fees. It has value only if you already order from DoorDash.
- **The $10 monthly promo** is for non-restaurant orders, such as grocery or convenience. Twelve uses is $120 a year, but an unused month is lost.
- **Apple TV** needs activation by December 31, 2026. Note when the free period ends.
- **Global Entry:** pay the application fee with the card and the credit posts.

The 10% anniversary points bonus that older guides mention was removed in June 2026.

## Step 7: where the points go

| Use | Value per point |
|---|---|
| Cash or statement credit | 1 cent or less |
| Chase Travel, ordinary booking | 1 cent |
| Chase Travel with [Points Boost](/chase-points-boost-explained/) | Up to 1.5 cents; some sources say up to 1.75 cents on certain premium-cabin fares |
| Transfer to most airline and hotel partners | 1 point to 1 mile or point; value depends on the booking |
| Transfer to Hyatt | [4 Chase points to 3 Hyatt points](/chase-to-hyatt-transfer-change-2026/) since 2026 |

Day to day, the card earns 5x on Chase Travel, 3x on dining, gas stations, EV charging, selected streaming and online grocery, 2x on other travel and 1x elsewhere.

Transfers cannot be undone.

## The sum

The $95 fee less the $100 hotel credit leaves you $5 ahead. If you were going to pay for Global Entry anyway, add $120. Points and any welcome offer come on top.

## Who should skip it

- Anyone who would not book a hotel through Chase Travel at least once a year. Without the credit, the card costs $95.
- Anyone who carries a balance month to month.
- Anyone who has opened five or more personal cards in 24 months. Chase generally declines them under [the 5/24 rule](/chase-5-24-rule-explained/).
- Anyone who wants lounge access. That comes with the Sapphire Reserve only.

## Quick answers

**Is the $95 fee waived in the first year?**
No. It is charged in year one.

**Does the hotel credit work if I book directly with the hotel?**
No. The stay must be booked through Chase Travel.

**Do I need to use every perk to come out ahead?**
No. The hotel credit alone covers the fee if you would have booked that stay anyway.
