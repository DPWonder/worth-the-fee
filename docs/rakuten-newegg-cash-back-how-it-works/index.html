Rakuten Newegg cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

The short answer

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Newegg. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Newegg page showed 1% cash back, with no raised rate. Gift cards earn nothing, and orders using coupon codes that begin with any of seven named prefixes, such as EMC, earn nothing.

A "was" label appears when a rate has been raised above the store's base rate. Newegg's page has none, so 1% is the plain rate. The page listed Newegg's Black Friday sale at the same 1%. Nothing on the page promises a higher rate on Black Friday (November 27, 2026).

What is excluded

The exclusions for Newegg are short. Cash back is not available on:

  • Gift cards
  • Coupon codes that begin with these prefixes: EMC, EMP, NAF, DYN, MBLN, B2BEMC, BTE

No product category is excluded in the terms. Computer parts, laptops, monitors and TVs are not named as earning nothing. That is different from some other electronics stores on Rakuten; see Rakuten at Best Buy for a longer list.

Coupon codes: check the first letters

The terms treat codes in two ways.

Code used at checkout What the terms say
A code listed on Rakuten Allowed
A code beginning EMC, EMP, NAF, DYN, MBLN, B2BEMC or BTE Cash back is not available
Any other code not listed on Rakuten May void cash back

The terms do not say where the prefixed codes come from. The page does say that signing up for Newegg's emails brings email-only coupons. Whether those are the EMC codes is not confirmed. The safe reading is to look at the first letters of any code before using it.

On November 25, 2026, the Newegg offers shown on Rakuten were sales and free shipping, with no code listed.

Marketplace sellers

Rakuten's page says third-party sellers offer items through Newegg Marketplace, and that some of those items may be used. The cash back terms do not mention Marketplace sellers. Whether an item sold by a third party earns the 1% is not confirmed.

Taxes and shipping are not mentioned in the Newegg terms. The page's question-and-answer section says orders over $25 ship free.

At 1%, a discount code can be worth more than the cash back. As an example of the sums, a code worth 5% on a $600 part takes off $30; the cash back on the same part is $6. If the code starts with one of the listed prefixes, the two cannot be combined.

Pickup, app and stores

  • App. The terms do not mention the Newegg app. Not confirmed.
  • Pickup and stores. The page showed an online rate only and the terms do not mention pickup. Newegg did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.

When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "in a few days". Payment follows on Rakuten's schedule; see when Rakuten pays. Card rewards are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards. For laptops bought from the makers, see laptop cash back at Dell, HP and Lenovo.

Who should skip it

  • You have a Newegg code that starts with EMC, EMP, NAF, DYN, MBLN, B2BEMC or BTE and it saves more than 1%.
  • You are buying a gift card.
  • Your order is small. On $50, the cash back is $0.50.
  • You are buying from a Marketplace seller and need certainty. That case is not confirmed.
  • You are waiting for a raised rate. None was shown, and none is promised.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Newegg? 1% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

Which Newegg promo codes cancel cash back? Codes that begin with EMC, EMP, NAF, DYN, MBLN, B2BEMC or BTE. Other outside codes may void it.

Do computer parts earn cash back at Newegg? The terms exclude no product category. Only gift cards are named.
