# Can you stack Rakuten cash back with Target Circle offers and the Target Circle Card 5%?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-target-circle-card-stack/
Facts checked: 2026-11-23
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, in most cases. Rakuten's terms for Target do not exclude paying with a Target Circle Card, and Rakuten's own Target page describes combining Target Circle offers, a rewards card and cash back on one order. The limits are that only some categories earn the 1% seen on November 23, 2026, a coupon code not listed on Rakuten voids the cash back, and Drive Up orders earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily. Which categories earn is covered in [Rakuten cash back at Target](/rakuten-target-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## What stacks and what does not

| Piece | Stacks with Rakuten cash back? | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Circle Card 5% discount | Yes; not excluded | Rakuten's Target terms and page text; Target's Circle Card page |
| Automatic Target Circle deals | Described by Rakuten as combinable | Rakuten's Target page; not addressed in its terms |
| Coupon or promo code not listed on Rakuten | No; voids the cash back | Rakuten's Target terms |
| Target Circle 360 membership fee | Earns nothing | Rakuten's Target terms |
| Buying gift cards | Earns nothing | Rakuten's Target terms |
| Paying with a Target gift card | Not confirmed | Terms are silent |
| Drive Up | Earns nothing | Rakuten's Target terms |
| Order Pickup inside the store | Not confirmed | Terms are silent |

## The Circle Card 5%

Target says the card takes 5% off at checkout in stores and on Target.com, and that it comes "on top of your Target Circle deals" (Target's Circle Card page). Target's help page adds three rules:

- The 5% is worked out after other discounts are taken off.
- If you split payment, it applies only to the amount put on the Circle Card.
- It does not apply to Target gift cards, prescriptions, Circle 360 fees, taxes or shipping.

Rakuten's terms for Target do not mention the card. Rakuten's page text says paying with a rewards card can be combined with cash back, and its shopping tips say the card discount still applies alongside coupons.

**Not confirmed:** whether Rakuten counts its 1% on the price before or after the 5% comes off.

## Target Circle deals

Target's help pages say most Circle deals apply automatically at checkout when you are signed in. Each item can take one item-level deal and one category-level deal. Circle Bonuses are different: they must be activated first.

Rakuten's rule is about coupon codes: one not listed on Rakuten voids the cash back. An automatic Circle deal is not typed in as a code, and Rakuten's page describes using Target's offers together with cash back. Its terms do not say this in so many words, so treat it as likely rather than guaranteed.

"Target Circle Earnings" is on Rakuten's excluded list. Target's name for what members earn is Target Circle Rewards. Whether the exclusion means the part of an order paid with those rewards is not confirmed.

## Circle 360

Circle 360 is Target's paid membership: $99 a year, or $49 a year with a Circle Card saved to the account. Rakuten's terms list it as excluded, so the fee earns no cash back. Target excludes the fee from the 5% as well. Whether an order sent by Circle 360 same-day delivery earns is not confirmed; the terms name Drive Up only.

## Gift cards as payment

- Buying a Target gift card through Rakuten earns nothing.
- If an order comes with a free Target gift card, Rakuten reduces the amount that earns by the card's value.
- Rakuten's terms for some other stores exclude purchases paid with gift cards. The Target terms do not say either way.
- The Circle Card 5% covers only the part charged to the card, so a part paid by gift card gets no 5%.

More in [gift cards and Rakuten](/rakuten-gift-cards-cash-back/).

## Pickup, Drive Up and the app

Drive Up items and orders are excluded. Order Pickup at the counter is not mentioned. The Target app is not mentioned either; Rakuten's general rule is that an order earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, its app or its extension. See [app or browser extension](/rakuten-app-vs-browser-extension/).

## What it comes to

A $200 order of furniture or apparel, no coupon code, paid on a Circle Card:

| Piece | Amount |
|---|---|
| Circle Card 5% | $10.00 off |
| Rakuten at 1% | $1.90 to $2.00, paid later |
| Total | About $12 |

The range reflects the open question of which price Rakuten counts. The same $200 spent on groceries or electronics gets the $10 and no cash back. A coupon code worth more than 1% saves more than the cash back it voids. Bank card rewards do not come on top here, because the 5% needs the Circle Card as payment; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- You buy mostly groceries, household goods, toys or electronics at Target. Those earn nothing through Rakuten.
- You rely on Drive Up. Those orders are excluded.
- You have a promo code not listed on Rakuten that is worth more than 1%.
- You mainly pay with Target gift cards. Whether that part earns is not confirmed, and it gets no 5%.

## Quick answers

**Does the Circle Card 5% cancel Rakuten cash back?**
No. Rakuten's Target terms do not exclude it, and Rakuten's page describes paying by card alongside cash back.

**Do Target Circle deals void Rakuten cash back?**
Rakuten's terms void cash back for coupon codes not listed on Rakuten. Automatic Circle deals are not named.

**Does a Circle 360 membership earn cash back?**
No. Target Circle 360 is on Rakuten's excluded list for Target.
