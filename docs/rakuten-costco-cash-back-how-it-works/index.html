In stores and with the extension

Costco did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 24, 2026. The list did not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.

What the Rakuten browser extension shows on Costco.com is not confirmed. With no rate on the store page, there is no cash back for it to activate.

What works instead: Costco's own 2% Reward

Costco's Executive Membership pays an annual 2% Reward on qualified purchases at Costco warehouses, on Costco.com and with Costco Travel. These are the terms on Costco's page on November 24, 2026:

Term What Costco's page says
Reward About 2% of pre-tax purchases, less refunds
Cap $1,250 in any 12-month period
Cost of upgrading $65 a year on top of a Gold Star or Business membership
Total at renewal $130 a year
When it is issued About three months before the renewal date, with the renewal statement
Not counted Tobacco, gasoline, Costco Shop Cards, postage stamps, alcohol in certain states, and other items the terms list

Costco states that the Reward may come to less than the upgrade fee. The sum is simple: $65 divided by 2% is $3,250. Below $3,250 a year in qualified purchases, the Reward is less than the extra fee. Purchases made before upgrading do not count.

What works instead: card rewards

Card rewards do not depend on Rakuten, so they are earned at Costco either way. See stacking with card rewards for how the two normally combine.

Costco's own card is the Costco Anywhere Visa Card by Citi. Citi's page on November 24, 2026 listed:

Purchase Rate
Gas at Costco 5%
Other eligible gas and EV charging 4%
Restaurants and eligible travel, including Costco Travel 3%
Other Costco and Costco.com purchases 2%
Everything else 1%

The 5% and 4% rates apply to the first $7,000 a year in combined gas and EV charging, then 1%. There is no annual fee with a paid Costco membership. The cash back comes once a year as a certificate after the February statement closes, to be redeemed at a U.S. Costco warehouse by December 31. Card offers vary by applicant and change.

The other warehouse club

Rakuten lists Sam's Club with cash back, with its own tiers and a long exclusion list. See Rakuten at Sam's Club. Walmart is a third case: see Rakuten at Walmart.

Who should skip it

  • Anyone starting a Costco order from Rakuten for cash back. There is none to earn.
  • Members who spend under $3,250 a year on qualified purchases, if the 2% Reward is the only reason to upgrade.
  • Members who mostly buy gasoline, tobacco or Shop Cards. Those do not count toward the 2% Reward.
  • Anyone who would carry a balance on a card. Interest costs more than 2%.

Quick answers

Does Rakuten give cash back at Costco? No. On November 24, 2026, Rakuten's Costco page showed "Coupons Only" and no cash back rate.

Why does a rate appear on Rakuten's Costco Black Friday page? The rates there are for other stores listed as similar to Costco. The Costco entry itself showed coupons only.

What pays cash back at Costco instead? Costco's annual 2% Reward for Executive Members, up to $1,250 a year, and the rewards on the card used to pay.
