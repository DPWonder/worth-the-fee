Is adding an authorized user to the Amex Platinum worth $195?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

It is worth $195 a year mainly for someone who will use airport lounges, either with you about four or more times a year or alone. The additional cardholder gets lounge access and hotel and car rental status, but not a second set of the statement credits.

What it costs

Each additional Platinum card costs $195 a year, on top of the primary card's $895 fee. Until September 2025 the price was $175 for up to three cards together, so adding several people is now much more expensive than it was.

For the shared credits, a purchase on the additional card can use up the account's credit. It does not create a new one. The additional card's spending earns points into the primary cardholder's account and counts toward the primary's welcome offer spending.

The sum for a couple

Without an additional card, a partner enters a Centurion Lounge as your guest at $50 a visit, and must be on your flight. The Delta Sky Club guest fee is also charged per visit; sources put it at $50 or $29.

Centurion visits together per year Guest fees Additional card
2 $100 $195
3 $150 $195
4 $200 $195

Below four visits a year, paying the guest fee costs less.

Three things can move the sum:

  • Traveling alone. A guest cannot enter without you. If your partner flies alone, only their own card gets them in. For them the comparison is $195 against whatever they would pay for lounges otherwise.
  • Priority Pass. Your own Priority Pass already allows two free guests where the lounge takes guests. If those are the lounges you use together, the additional card saves nothing there.
  • The one-time credit. In a year when the additional cardholder pays for Global Entry, up to $120 comes back, which leaves $75 for that year.

The no-fee Companion Card

Amex also offers a Companion Platinum Card with no annual fee. The Points Guy describes its benefits as very limited, and it does not include lounge access. It suits someone who only needs a card on the account for spending. The benefits in the table above belong to the $195 card only.

Who can be added

The Points Guy gives a minimum age of 13. The maximum number of additional Platinum cards on one account is not confirmed.

An additional cardholder who later wants their own Platinum account can still be eligible for a welcome offer on it, according to One Mile at a Time. Offers vary by applicant and change.

Who should skip it

Adding a $195 card is hard to justify if:

  • The person flies once or twice a year. Guest fees for those trips cost less.
  • You expected a second set of credits. There is none.
  • Your airports have Priority Pass lounges but no Centurion Lounge or Delta Sky Club.
  • You are adding children mainly for lounges. Compare with the child guest fee of $30 a visit first; see the guest rules page.

If the primary card itself is a close call, start with what the credits are worth.

Quick answers

How much is an authorized user on the Amex Platinum? $195 a year for each additional Platinum card. A Companion Platinum Card has no fee but very limited benefits.

Do authorized users get their own Uber Cash and statement credits? No. Uber Cash goes to the primary cardholder only, and the other credits are one per account.

Do authorized users get Centurion Lounge access? Yes, with the $195 additional Platinum card. The no-fee Companion card does not include lounge access.
