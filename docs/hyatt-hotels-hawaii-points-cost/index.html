How many points does a Hyatt hotel in Hawaii cost per night?

Facts checked November 20, 20265 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

A standard room runs from about 12,000 World of Hyatt points a night at Hyatt Place Waikiki Beach to 35,000 to 75,000 a night at Andaz Maui and Grand Hyatt Kauai, depending on the date. Guides disagree on the category of several Hawaii hotels, so treat the table as a starting point and confirm on Hyatt's site.

How Hyatt prices a night now

Since May 20, 2026, each Hyatt category has five price levels instead of three: Lowest, Low, Moderate, Upper and Top. The hotel's category sets the range. The date sets the level.

Category Lowest Low Moderate Upper Top
4 12,000 15,000 20,000 22,500 25,000
5 15,000 20,000 25,000 30,000 35,000
6 20,000 25,000 30,000 35,000 40,000

8 35,000 45,000 55,000 65,000 75,000

These figures come from AwardWallet's chart dated May 9, 2026. A Hawaii travel blog from February 2026 shows higher top prices for Category 6 (50,000) and Category 7 (60,000). Category 5 and Category 8 match in both.

Where the sources conflict most

  • Prices above the chart. One article cites 66,000 to 90,000 points a night at Grand Hyatt Kauai in July and August 2026. Upgraded Points saw 70,000 shown on a night labeled "Lowest". Both are out of line with a standard-room chart that tops out at 75,000. The likely cause is that only larger room types were left, but that is not confirmed.
  • Old ranges on current pages. One guide updated this month still shows three-level prices that predate May 2026.
  • Resort fees. One article says Grand Hyatt Kauai's resort fee, given as $55 a night, is waived on award nights but not on Points + Cash stays. Another guide says Hyatt hotels in Hawaii have no resort fees and then describes one at the same hotel. Ask the hotel.

What that means in Chase points

Chase points move to Hyatt at 1:1 from a Sapphire Reserve and at 4:3 from a Sapphire Preferred. At 4:3, multiply the Hyatt price by four and divide by three.

Category 4, Lowest 60,000 60,000 80,000
Category 8, Lowest 175,000 175,000 233,334
Category 8, Top 375,000 375,000 500,000

To judge a booking, divide the cash rate with taxes and fees by the points needed. The Points Guy values a Hyatt point at 1.65 cents as of November 2026. See also Chase points for a Hawaii trip.

Who should skip it

  • Sapphire Preferred holders with under 100,000 points who want a resort. That balance covers about two nights at Category 8, Lowest level.
  • Travelers going to the Big Island. No Hyatt was listed there.
  • Anyone traveling at Christmas or in midsummer who cannot move dates. Top-level nights cost about twice the Lowest level.

Quick answers

What is the cheapest Hyatt in Hawaii on points? Hyatt Place Waikiki Beach, listed as Category 4, from 12,000 points a night at the Lowest level.

How many points is Andaz Maui per night? Most recent guides list it as Category 8, which is 35,000 to 75,000 points for a standard room. One older blog says Category 7.

Do Chase points still move to Hyatt 1:1? From a Sapphire Reserve, yes. From a Sapphire Preferred the rate is 4:3.
