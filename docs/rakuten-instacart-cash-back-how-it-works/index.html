Rakuten Instacart cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

The short answer

Yes, but not in the Instacart app. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Instacart page showed 1% cash back, with no raised rate. The biggest exclusion is how the order is placed: purchases made directly through the Instacart mobile app earn nothing, and alcohol, gift cards, taxes, delivery fees and tips are excluded too.

The rate seen on November 25, 2026

The page showed 1% with no "was" label, so 1% is the plain rate. No category table appears. Nothing in the terms limits the offer to new customers.

Where the order must be placed

This is the rule most likely to cost a shopper the cash back. The terms say cash back is only available on orders placed:

  • on Instacart.com, or
  • through the Rakuten app, when it sends the shopper to Instacart's mobile web site.

Two kinds of order are named as not eligible:

  • Purchases made directly through the Instacart mobile app.
  • Orders placed through stores that are powered by Instacart. That means a grocer's own site or app that runs on Instacart.

The same Rakuten page describes the Instacart service as built for its mobile app. Under the cash back terms, an order placed there earns nothing.

Coupon codes, pickup and EBT

  • Coupon codes. The terms say codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back, and to keep to codes Rakuten provides. The page describes first-order promotions such as $0 delivery fees on the first three orders; those were listed on Rakuten on November 25, 2026.
  • Pickup. The page mentions pickup orders in its description of Instacart+. The cash back terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
  • EBT SNAP. The page says participating stores may accept it. Whether the part of an order paid with EBT SNAP earns is not confirmed.
  • In stores. There is no in-store offer; this is an online service.

A 90-day limit on claims

The terms say missing cash back inquiries must be made within 90 days of the order, and that cash back can only be researched for up to 90 days from the order date. See what to do when cash back did not track.

When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "tomorrow". Payment follows on Rakuten's four dates a year, and a balance under $5 carries over to the next date; see when Rakuten pays. Card rewards on groceries are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • You order in the Instacart mobile app and do not want to switch to the website.
  • You order through a grocer's own site or app that is powered by Instacart.
  • Most of the order is alcohol.
  • You are buying an Instacart+ membership or a gift card.
  • You have a code from outside Rakuten that saves more than 1% of the order.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Instacart? 1% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

Does an order in the Instacart app earn cash back? No. Purchases made directly through the Instacart mobile app are excluded.

Do delivery fees and tips count? No. Taxes, delivery fees and tips are on the excluded list.
