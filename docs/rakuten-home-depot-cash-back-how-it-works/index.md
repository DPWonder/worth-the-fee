# Rakuten Home Depot cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-home-depot-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-26
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

No. The Home Depot has a page on Rakuten, but on November 26, 2026 that page showed "No Cash Back", the same as on November 21, 24 and 25. Shopping there through Rakuten earned nothing on those days, on any category.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate here and at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## What Rakuten's page shows

| Point | Seen on November 21, 2026 |
|---|---|
| Is there a Rakuten page for the store? | Yes |
| Cash back rate | No cash back |
| "Was" rate (Rakuten's label for a usual rate) | None shown |
| Category rates | None |
| Terms and exclusions | None listed |
| Page last edited | November 18, 2026 |

Two addresses for the store on Rakuten were read, and both showed the same thing. The page title describes it as coupons, promo codes and no cash back.

The page gives no reason. Rakuten's help pages say stores pay Rakuten a commission and Rakuten shares part of it as cash back. A store that shows no cash back is one where that arrangement is not in place on the day. Whether this store paid cash back through Rakuten in the past, and whether it will again, is not confirmed.

## What is on the page instead

The page lists the store's own deals and codes. Its FAQ covers rotating discounts, price matching, promo codes sent by text, and a small coupon for creating a store account. None of that is Rakuten cash back.

It also mentions the store's rebate center. Those are manufacturer rebates run by the store, separate from Rakuten.

## Since nothing is paid, nothing is excluded

The usual questions have a short answer here.

| Question | Answer on November 21, 2026 |
|---|---|
| Gift cards | No cash back |
| Pickup orders | No cash back |
| Orders in the store's app | No cash back |
| Orders with coupon codes | No cash back |
| Appliances, tools, lumber | No cash back |
| In-store purchases with a linked card | Not seen in the part of Rakuten's in-store list that loaded; not confirmed |

The Rakuten extension may still appear on the store's site to show coupons. That is not the same as activating cash back.

An up-to rate means some categories earn less. A rate at another store is worth something only if that store's price is as low. On a $1,000 appliance, 2% is $20. A price difference of more than $20 wipes it out.

## Other ways to earn on the purchase

- **Card rewards.** These are separate from Rakuten and apply wherever the card is used. See [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).
- **Other shopping portals.** Another portal may list this store with a rate. One competing portal's page showed up to 6% on some categories in a search summary. That figure was not checked on the portal's own page and is not confirmed. See [Rakuten compared with TopCashback](/rakuten-vs-topcashback/) and [with Capital One Shopping](/rakuten-vs-capital-one-shopping/).
- **The store's own offers.** Price matching and manufacturer rebates are described on the store's site.

## How this could change

Rates on Rakuten change daily, and stores join and leave. The store page on Rakuten is the place to check on the day. If it shows a percentage, the terms beside it will list what is excluded.

## Who should skip it

Skip the Rakuten step for this store while the page shows no cash back. It adds a click and earns nothing. That includes:

- Shoppers planning a large appliance or tool order who expected a percentage back.
- New Rakuten members hoping an order here would count toward [the sign-up bonus](/rakuten-50-bonus-how-it-works/). A purchase has to earn cash back to qualify, and this one does not.

## Quick answers

**Is The Home Depot on Rakuten?**
It has a page, but the page showed no cash back on November 21, 24, 25 and 26, 2026.

**Does any category earn, such as appliances?**
No. No category rates were listed that day.

**Does Lowe's earn Rakuten cash back?**
Rakuten showed Lowe's at up to 2% (was 1%) on November 21, 2026 and up to 8% on November 26, 2026. Rates change daily; see [Rakuten at Lowe's](/rakuten-lowes-cash-back-how-it-works/).
