Are Amex points from Rakuten worth more than the cash?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Only when you redeem each point for more than 1 cent. Rakuten gives 1 Membership Rewards point per cent of cash back, so $100 becomes 10,000 points; those points beat $100 through airline transfers and a few card-specific options, and fall short as statement credits, shopping or most gift cards.

The break-even is 1 cent

Every $1 of Rakuten cash back can be $1 or 100 points. So 1 cent per point is the line. Above it, points win. Below it, cash wins.

  • The Schwab figure applies only to holders of the Platinum Card for Schwab, on the first 1 million points a year. After that it drops to 0.8 cents.
  • The Business Platinum figure is arithmetic: 35% of the points come back on eligible airfare booked with points, so each point nets about 1.54 cents.
  • The transfer figures are estimates, not fixed prices. One Mile at a Time uses 1.7 cents and The Points Guy uses 2 cents. A third site puts it near 2.2.

Amex's own guide could not be opened to check these numbers directly. Amex can change redemption values, so confirm them in your Membership Rewards account before relying on a figure.

When the switch pays

  • You already move Amex points to airline programs and book award flights. Each $100 of Rakuten cash back adds 10,000 points to a balance you would use anyway.
  • You hold the Schwab or Business Platinum card. The gain is smaller but fixed.

When it does not

  • You would spend the points on statement credits, Amazon or most gift cards. At 0.6 to 0.7 cents, $100 of cash back turns into $60 to $70.
  • You book flights through Amex Travel with points. That is 1 cent: no gain over cash.
  • You have no planned use. A point is worth nothing until redeemed, and programs can raise award prices.

The higher rows are worth that much only if you would have paid cash for those flights. An award seat you would never have bought at full price is not a saving of its full price.

Costs the table leaves out

  • Taxes and fees on awards. These reduce the cents-per-point figure.
  • The card. You need an Amex card in the Membership Rewards program. Rakuten points alone do not cover an annual fee. See what the Platinum credits are really worth.
  • Reversing. Whether you can switch back to cash later is not confirmed. Sources disagree, and Rakuten's help pages do not say.
  • Other offers. Rakuten's terms say offers tied to other card issuers may not be available once you choose points.

Who should skip it

  • You have never transferred points to an airline and do not plan to learn.
  • You redeem points for statement credits or shopping.
  • You do not hold a Membership Rewards card. The comparison does not apply.

Quick answers

What is the break-even value? 1 cent per point. Rakuten gives 100 points per $1 of cash back.

Are the points worth more as a statement credit? No. At about 0.6 cents per point, $100 of cash back becomes about $60.

How much more are transfers worth? By two travel sites' estimates, 1.7 to 2 cents per point, if you find and book award seats.
