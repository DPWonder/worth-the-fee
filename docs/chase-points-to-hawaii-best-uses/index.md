# What is the most practical way to use Chase points for a Hawaii trip?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/chase-points-to-hawaii-best-uses/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

For most travelers the practical use is the flight: move Chase points to United or Southwest at 1:1 when the award price is low, or book through Chase Travel when the cash fare is low. Hyatt hotels still work, but from a Sapphire Preferred each Hyatt point now costs one third more Chase points.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The four options side by side

Award prices move daily. Each figure below is an example from the dated source, not a promise.

| Use | Rate from Chase | Example, with date | Main limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| United MileagePlus | 1:1 | Los Angeles to Honolulu 13,000 miles one-way; Denver 22,000; Chicago 25,000; each plus $5.60 (The Points Guy, June 16, 2026) | No fixed chart; checked bags cost extra |
| Southwest Rapid Rewards | 1:1 | Oakland to Honolulu from 16,500 points plus $5.60 one-way (The Points Guy, April 14, 2026) | Price follows the cash fare; bags now cost $45 and $55 |
| Chase Travel | 1 cent per point; up to 1.5 cents with Points Boost on a Sapphire Preferred (some sources report 1.75 on premium-cabin fares), up to 2 cents on a Sapphire Reserve | A $500 fare costs 50,000 points at 1 cent | Chase publishes no fixed list of boosted bookings |
| World of Hyatt | 4:3 from Sapphire Preferred; 1:1 from Sapphire Reserve | A 35,000-point night costs 46,667 Chase points from a Preferred | Few Hyatt hotels in Hawaii, mostly high categories |

## Flights: transfer or book through Chase Travel?

Do one sum before moving any points. Take the cash fare in cents and divide it by the points the award costs. That gives cents per point.

Two made-up fares show the range (they are illustrations, not quotes):

- If the cash fare were $400 and the award 13,000 United miles, that is about 3 cents per point.
- If the cash fare were $180 and the award 16,500 Southwest points, that is about 1.1 cents per point.

Then check the same flight in Chase Travel. Without a Points Boost, points are worth 1 cent each there. With a Boost, a Sapphire Preferred gets up to 1.5 cents on selected flights and hotels and, by some sources, up to 1.75 cents on premium-cabin fares. If the transfer gives fewer cents per point than Chase Travel does, book through Chase Travel.

Transfers go one way. Sources repeat the same advice: find the award seat first, then move the points.

## Hyatt: the 4:3 rate changes the sum

Since October 1, 2026, every Sapphire Preferred moves points to Hyatt at 4:3. Four Chase points become three Hyatt points. The Sapphire Reserve stays at 1:1.

| Hyatt price per night | Chase points from a Preferred | Chase points from a Reserve |
|---|---|---|
| 15,000 | 20,000 | 15,000 |
| 25,000 | 33,334 | 25,000 |
| 35,000 | 46,667 | 35,000 |
| 75,000 | 100,000 | 75,000 |

The two big Hawaii resorts, Andaz Maui and Grand Hyatt Kauai, are listed by most recent guides as Category 8, at 35,000 to 75,000 Hyatt points a night. Five nights at the lowest level would take 233,334 Chase points from a Preferred. See [what each Hyatt hotel in Hawaii costs in points](/hyatt-hotels-hawaii-points-cost/).

Hyatt can still beat 1 cent per point when the cash rate is high. One guide puts Grand Hyatt Kauai cash rates at $800 to $1,300 a night. But at 4:3 each Chase point buys 25% fewer Hyatt points than it did at 1:1.

## Other partners

British Airways Avios can book American and Alaska flights to Hawaii. Sources disagree on the price. Older guides give about 13,000 Avios one-way from the West Coast. AwardWallet's current chart shows 20,000 to 22,000. Check the live price before relying on it.

Alaska and Hawaiian's own program, Atmos Rewards, is not a Chase partner.

## Who should skip it

- Travelers with a small balance. Two round trips from the Midwest can take 100,000 United miles at the example prices above.
- Sapphire Preferred holders aiming at a Hyatt resort for a week. The 4:3 rate makes this costly.
- Anyone who finds a low cash fare. Paying cash and keeping the points can be the better sum.

A [Sapphire Preferred for one trip a year](/chase-sapphire-preferred-one-trip-a-year/) is covered separately.

## Quick answers

**Do Chase points transfer to Hawaiian Airlines?**
No. Hawaiian's program, Atmos Rewards, is not on Chase's partner list. United and Southwest are.

**Is the Chase to Hyatt rate still 1:1?**
Only from a Sapphire Reserve. From a Sapphire Preferred it has been 4:3 since October 1, 2026.

**Can a transfer be undone?**
No. Find the award seat first, then move the points.
