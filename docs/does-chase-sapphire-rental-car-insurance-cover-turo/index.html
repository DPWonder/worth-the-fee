Does Chase Sapphire rental car insurance cover Turo?

Facts checked November 27, 20265 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

No. The Sapphire Preferred Guide to Benefits excludes a vehicle rented through a car sharing company and any vehicle not rented from a commercial rental agency, and Chase's own guide for both Sapphire cards says peer-to-peer rentals are not covered. Turo's help center says the same from its side.

What Chase's wording says

Chase's Guide to Benefits for the Sapphire Preferred (form BGC11387, describing the benefit in effect as of October 1, 2024) was read directly for this page. Its Auto Rental Coverage section lists two exclusions that matter here:

  • Vehicles that are not rented from a Rental Agency.
  • A rental vehicle rented through "a car sharing company that allows individuals to rent out their own vehicle."

The guide defines a Rental Agency as a commercial rental company, licensed under the laws of the place it operates, whose primary business is renting automobiles. A private owner listing a car on an app does not fit that definition.

Chase's web guide to Sapphire rental coverage, which covers both the Preferred and the Reserve, says peer-to-peer and hourly car rentals are not covered. It does not name Turo. Whether Chase has issued a newer Preferred guide than the one read is not confirmed, and the Sapphire Reserve Guide to Benefits was not read directly.

What Turo says

Turo's help article on credit card coverage, updated May 4, 2026, says it is very unlikely that a card covers damage to a car booked through Turo, because Turo is a peer-to-peer car sharing platform and not a rental car company. It tells guests to read their card's terms and ask the issuer.

Where sources disagree

  • Commercial hosts. The Points Guy, in an article published June 17, 2021, reported that a Chase benefits specialist said a Turo booking might not be excluded when the car comes from a commercial rental company and the rental contract names that company. The written guide read for this page has no such carve-out: it excludes a car rented through a car sharing company without regard to who the host is. Upgraded Points, updated September 16, 2026, lists car sharing rentals such as Turo as excluded.
  • Rental length. Chase's web guide says fewer than 31 consecutive days; the Guide to Benefits says up to 31.
  • Old vehicles. The web guide excludes vehicles more than 10 years old; the Guide to Benefits excludes antique cars, defined as over 20 years old or out of production for 10 years.

A phone answer is not a written term. The guide lists 1-800-350-1362 for coverage questions before a trip.

The sums

Because the card pays nothing on Turo, the choice is between a Turo protection plan, your own auto policy if it extends to car sharing, or carrying the risk. With protection declined, Turo says you owe all physical, mechanical and interior damage costs plus fees. The card's benefit has no value in this sum, so compare the Turo price plus a plan against a rental agency's price with the agency's waiver declined.

More on the benefit is on Sapphire Preferred rental car insurance, Sapphire Preferred travel insurance and, for another issuer's rule, Amex Platinum rental car cover.

Who should skip relying on it

  • Anyone booking Turo and planning to decline Turo's protection because of a Sapphire card.
  • Anyone renting by the hour from any service.
  • Anyone counting on the card for liability. It covers neither liability nor injuries, on Turo or anywhere else.
  • Anyone whose own auto policy excludes car sharing. Ask the insurer before the trip.

Quick answers

Does the Chase Sapphire Preferred cover Turo? No. Its Guide to Benefits excludes vehicles rented through a car sharing company where individuals rent out their own cars.

Does the Chase Sapphire Reserve cover Turo? Chase's web guide for both Sapphire cards says peer-to-peer rentals are not covered. The Reserve's own benefits guide was not read.

Should I buy Turo's protection plan if I have a Sapphire card? The card will not pay for damage to a Turo car. Without a plan, Turo says you owe all damage costs.
