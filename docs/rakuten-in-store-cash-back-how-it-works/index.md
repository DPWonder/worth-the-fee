# Rakuten in store: can you use Rakuten in store, and how does in-store cash back work?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, you can use Rakuten in store; it is not only for online shopping. You link a payment card to your Rakuten account, add a store's in-store offer before you shop, and pay at the register with that card. Rakuten says you can link most Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards and most American Express cards.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the offer in the Rakuten app on the day you shop. New to Rakuten? [How Rakuten works, step by step](/how-does-rakuten-work-step-by-step/) covers the online side.

## The steps

1. In your Rakuten account, link an eligible card.
2. On the Rakuten site or in the app, open the In-Store tab and add the offer for the store you plan to visit.
3. Pay in the physical store with the linked card.
4. Keep the receipt until the cash back is confirmed.

Rakuten's FAQ says the purchase is tracked automatically: "you don't need to upload receipts." There is nothing to show or scan at the register. A newly linked card can take about 24 hours to start reporting, according to the program terms.

## Which cards you can link

| Card | Can it be linked? |
|---|---|
| Most Visa credit and debit cards | Yes |
| Most Mastercard credit and debit cards | Yes |
| Most American Express cards | Yes |
| Discover, Diners Club, Wells Fargo Mastercards | No |
| American Express debit cards, Macy's American Express | No |
| Apple Card and other virtual cards with a new number each time | No |
| Corporate, purchasing, HSA, FSA and prepaid government or insurance cards | No |
| Store cards without a Visa, Mastercard or Amex logo | No |

The linking steps, screen by screen, are on [how to link a card for in-store cash back](/rakuten-link-card-for-in-store-cash-back/).

You can link more than one card. Rakuten's in-store page mentions credit cards only, while its help FAQ adds Visa and Mastercard debit cards.

**Debit cards.** Run the card as credit and sign if asked. Rakuten says debit purchases made with a PIN may not qualify.

**Phone wallets.** Rakuten's pages do not agree. The FAQ recommends the physical card and says Apple Pay, Google Wallet and Samsung Pay may not always track. The program terms say Apple Pay and Samsung Wallet are eligible. Rakuten's blog says phone wallets may work at some stores when the linked card is the card behind them. The physical card avoids the question.

## Rules for offers

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| When to add the offer | Before you pay. An offer added after the purchase does not count. |
| How long it lasts | Most expire 75 days after you add them, or once used |
| Using it again | Add the offer again after each purchase |
| Where it works | Physical stores only, at a store register. Online and phone orders do not qualify. |
| Exclusions | Some stores do not pay on gift cards or on departments such as pharmacy or automotive |

## When the cash back shows up

Pending cash back usually appears within a few days and is typically confirmed in 3 to 14 weeks. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

If nothing shows, Rakuten's FAQ gives three usual causes: the offer was not added before the purchase, the card was not linked, or a debit card was not run as credit. See [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

The card's own rewards are separate. Rakuten's home page says cash back comes on top of credit card rewards; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## What you agree to when you link a card

The program terms say that by enrolling a card, you agree the card network or processor may monitor transactions on that card at participating stores. Merchant name, location, date and amount are shared so the purchase can be matched. You can remove a card at any time in account settings. Rakuten keeps the past transaction data.

## Who should skip it

- You do not want a card network reporting your purchases at participating stores to Rakuten.
- Your main card is on the "No" list: Discover, Apple Card, a Wells Fargo Mastercard, or a store card.
- You pay by phone tap or debit PIN and do not want to change.
- You will not add the offer before each visit. Without that step nothing is earned.

## Quick answers

**Can you use Rakuten in store?**
Yes. Link an eligible card, add the store's in-store offer, then pay in the physical store with that card.

**Is Rakuten only for online shopping?**
No. It also pays on in-store purchases at participating stores and at restaurants, through a linked card.

**Do you need to scan a receipt?**
No. Tracking is automatic. Keep the receipt in case of a problem.
