# How does Rakuten in-store cash back work, and which cards can you link?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-23
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

You link a payment card to your Rakuten account, add a store's in-store offer before you shop, and pay at the register with that card. Rakuten says you can link most Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards and most American Express cards.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the offer in the Rakuten app on the day you shop.

## The steps

1. In your Rakuten account, link an eligible card.
2. On the Rakuten site or in the app, open the In-Store tab and add the offer for the store you plan to visit.
3. Pay in the physical store with the linked card.
4. Keep the receipt until the cash back is confirmed.

Rakuten's FAQ says the purchase is tracked automatically: "you don't need to upload receipts." A newly linked card can take about 24 hours to start reporting, according to the program terms.

## Which cards you can link

| Card | Can it be linked? |
|---|---|
| Most Visa credit and debit cards | Yes |
| Most Mastercard credit and debit cards | Yes |
| Most American Express cards | Yes |
| Discover, Diners Club, Wells Fargo Mastercards | No |
| American Express debit cards, Macy's American Express | No |
| Apple Card and other virtual cards with a new number each time | No |
| Corporate, HSA, FSA and government prepaid cards | No |
| Store cards without a Visa, Mastercard or Amex logo | No |

The linking steps, screen by screen, are on [how to link a card for in-store cash back](/rakuten-link-card-for-in-store-cash-back/).

You can link more than one card. Rakuten's in-store page mentions credit cards only, while its help FAQ adds Visa and Mastercard debit cards.

**Debit cards.** Run the card as credit and sign if asked. Rakuten says debit purchases made with a PIN may not qualify.

**Phone wallets.** Rakuten's pages do not agree. The FAQ recommends the physical card and says Apple Pay, Google Wallet and Samsung Pay may not always track. The program terms say Apple Pay and Samsung Wallet are eligible. The physical card avoids the question.

## Rules for offers

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| When to add the offer | Before you pay. An offer added after the purchase does not count. |
| How long it lasts | Most expire 75 days after you add them, or once used |
| Using it again | Add the offer again after each purchase |
| Where it works | Physical stores only. Online and phone orders do not qualify. |
| Gift cards | Some stores do not pay on gift-card purchases |

## When the cash back shows up

Pending cash back usually appears within a few days and is typically confirmed in 3 to 14 weeks. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

If nothing shows, the usual causes are an offer not added first, a card not linked, or a debit card run with a PIN. See [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## What you agree to when you link a card

The program terms say that by enrolling a card, you agree the card network or processor may monitor transactions on that card at participating stores. Merchant name, location, date and amount are shared so the purchase can be matched. You can remove a card at any time in account settings; past data is kept.

## Who should skip it

- You do not want a card network reporting your purchases at participating stores to Rakuten.
- Your main card is on the "No" list: Discover, Apple Card, a Wells Fargo Mastercard, or a store card.
- You pay by phone tap or debit PIN and do not want to change.
- You will not add the offer before each visit. Without that step nothing is earned.

## Quick answers

**Which cards work with Rakuten in-store cash back?**
Most Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards and most American Express cards. Not Discover or Apple Card.

**Do you need to scan a receipt?**
No. Tracking is automatic. Keep the receipt in case of a problem.

**Can you use an in-store offer more than once?**
Only if you add it again. Most offers end once used or after 75 days.
