# Is Rakuten cash back taxable, and what about the referral bonus?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/is-rakuten-cash-back-taxable/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Cash back on things you buy is generally treated as a rebate that lowers the price you paid, not as income. A referral bonus is different: you did not buy anything to earn it, Rakuten's terms say you may be taxed on it, and it can be reported to the IRS.

This page is information, not financial advice. It is not tax advice either; a tax professional can apply the rules to your return.

## The short version

| What you received | How sources describe it | Could a tax form arrive? |
|---|---|---|
| Cash back on your own personal purchases | A rebate; generally not income | Not expected |
| Bonus for referring a friend | Generally taxable income | Possibly; see below |
| New-member bonus that requires purchases | Not confirmed for Rakuten | Not confirmed |
| Rewards taken as Amex points | Not confirmed for Rakuten | Not confirmed |

No IRS guidance that names shopping portals was found. The first two rows rest on general IRS rules about rebates and on how tax writers apply them to rewards.

## Why purchase cash back is treated as a rebate

IRS Publication 525 has a short entry on cash rebates. It says a cash rebate you receive from a dealer or manufacturer of an item you buy is not income, and that you reduce your basis in the item by the rebate.

A 2010 IRS letter ruling about credit card rebates took the same line: a rebate to a buyer is an adjustment to the purchase price. A letter ruling applies only to the taxpayer who asked for it, but it shows the reasoning. The Points Guy's tax guide, dated February 2026, sums up the general view that rewards tied to spending are treated as rebates and are generally not taxed.

Rakuten cash back comes through Rakuten, not straight from the store, so it is not word for word the case in Publication 525. The common reading is that it is still a rebate on your purchase.

## Why a referral bonus is different

A referral bonus is paid for bringing in a new member. You bought nothing to get it, so it is not a discount on anything.

- Rakuten's terms, section 10.5, say: "You may be taxed on your receipt of bonuses and other consideration."
- The same section says you are solely responsible for any tax owed on them.
- The Points Guy's guide says referral bonuses are considered taxable income because no spending is needed to earn them.

How the referral bonus works is covered in [the Rakuten bonus page](/rakuten-50-bonus-how-it-works/).

## Will Rakuten send a 1099?

Rakuten's terms and help pages do not say when it issues a tax form or asks for a taxpayer ID. That is not confirmed.

What is known:

- A member wrote in a tax software forum in February 2026 that Rakuten had sent a Form 1099-NEC for a referral bonus.
- Another member reported receiving no form for about $120 of bonuses and cash back.
- The IRS instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC say that for tax years beginning after 2025, the minimum amount for reporting certain payments rose to $2,000. It was $600 before. The figure may be adjusted for inflation from 2027.

A form and a tax duty are separate things. The Points Guy's guide notes that taxable income is reportable whether or not a 1099 arrives.

## Where it goes on a return

Sources differ; this is a point for a tax professional.

- The Points Guy says rewards income is reported as other income on Schedule 1 of Form 1040, line 8z.
- A tax software support agent, answering the member with the 1099-NEC, said the IRS generally expects amounts on that form to be reported as business income on Schedule C.

## Records worth keeping

- Rakuten's payment history, which shows each payment date and amount.
- A note of how much of each payment was referral bonus and how much was cash back on purchases.

## Who should skip the referral program

- You do not want any income that may need reporting. Purchase cash back alone does not raise that question in the usual case.
- Your tax situation is complex, for example you buy through Rakuten for a business. Ask a professional first.

## Quick answers

**Do I pay tax on Rakuten cash back from shopping?**
Generally no. Sources treat it as a rebate that lowers the price you paid.

**Is the Rakuten referral bonus taxable?**
Rakuten's terms say you may be taxed on bonuses. Tax writers treat referral bonuses as income.

**Does Rakuten send a 1099?**
Not confirmed. One member reported a 1099-NEC for a referral bonus; another reported no form.
