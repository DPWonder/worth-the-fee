Is the Chase Sapphire Preferred worth it if you take one big trip a year?

Facts checked November 20, 2026

This page has a referral link at the end. The person who runs this site earns points or cash if you sign up through it.

For most people who take one real trip a year, yes. The card costs $95 a year, and a single hotel booking through Chase Travel returns up to $100, so the fee can be covered by one stay. If you would never book a hotel through Chase's site, the answer gets weaker.

This page is information, not financial advice. Card terms change, so check the issuer's page before you decide.

The yearly sums

Item Amount
Annual fee $95, charged in year one too
Hotel credit Up to $100 each account year, on hotel stays bought through Chase Travel
Fee left over if you use the whole credit $0
Fee left over if you never use it $95

The credit posts by itself. The first $100 of those hotel purchases earns no points.

One catch. A hotel booked through Chase Travel is a third-party booking. Your trip has to include at least one hotel night you are happy to book that way. If your one trip is a week in a rented house or with family, the credit may go unused.

What you earn the rest of the year

Points come from ordinary spending, not only from the trip.

Spending Points per dollar
Chase Travel bookings 5
Dining, gas stations, EV charging, selected vacation homes, selected streaming, online grocery 3
Other travel 2
Everything else 1

Online grocery leaves out Walmart, Target and wholesale clubs. There is no foreign transaction fee, which matters if the trip is abroad.

What the points are worth

Through Chase Travel a point is worth 1 cent as the base. On selected bookings Chase marks up the value, which it calls Points Boost. On this card that can reach 1.5 cents on some hotels and flights and 1.75 cents on premium-cabin airfare. Chase does not publish a fixed list, so you cannot plan around it.

You can also move points to 14 airline and hotel programs. United and Southwest transfer 1:1. Hyatt changed in 2026: from a Sapphire Preferred, 4 Chase points now become 3 Hyatt points. Older articles that call Hyatt a 1:1 partner for this card are out of date.

The welcome offer

The public offer on Chase's page today is 75,000 points after $5,000 in purchases in the first 3 months. At the 1-cent base that is $750 of travel through Chase Travel. Moved to Hyatt at 4:3 it becomes 56,250 Hyatt points.

Welcome offers change and can differ by person and by link. Treat the number as today's, not a promise.

$5,000 in 3 months is a real hurdle. If that is more than you normally spend, the bonus is not a reason to get the card.

The protection a single big trip needs

This is the quiet reason the card suits a once-a-year traveler. When the trip is paid with the card, Chase lists:

  • Trip cancellation or interruption: up to $10,000 per traveler, $20,000 per trip.
  • Trip delay: up to $500 per traveler after more than 12 hours or an overnight stay.
  • Rental car: primary cover up to $60,000 for theft and collision.
  • Delayed bags: up to $100 a day for 5 days. Lost bags: up to $3,000 per traveler.

Restrictions apply, and the full terms are in Chase's benefits guide.

Who should skip it

  • People who carry a balance. Interest will cost more than any points return.
  • People who have opened five or more personal cards in the past 24 months. Chase generally declines them under its unofficial "5/24" practice.
  • People who already earned this card's bonus. Chase now gives the bonus once per person per Sapphire card.
  • People who want lounge access. This card has none.
  • People who want plain cash back and will not book through Chase Travel or learn transfers.

Quick answers

Does one trip a year cover the $95 fee? It can. One Chase Travel hotel stay of $100 or more returns up to $100 in statement credit each account year.

Can I still move points to Hyatt at 1:1? Not from this card. Since 2026 the Sapphire Preferred rate is 4:3. The Sapphire Reserve keeps 1:1.

Can I get the bonus if I had the card years ago? Only if you never earned the bonus on it. The old 48-month wait no longer exists. Chase shows a notice before the credit check if you will not get it.
