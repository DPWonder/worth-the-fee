The short answer

So there are no Bose.com terms to report: no rule on refurbished items, coupon codes or posting time.

Best Buy: "up to 6%" is not the Bose rate

Most products on the brand page were Best Buy listings, each tagged with Best Buy's "up to 6%". Best Buy's own Rakuten page the same day named no Bose tier. Its 6% tier covered other items, such as Sonos headphones, and its 1% tier included Audio. On November 26, Bose headphones had been named in a 4% tier; on November 27 they were not named. Which tier a Bose item falls in now is not confirmed, and 1% is the cautious reading.

Stores, the extension and stacking

  • In stores. Bose did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 27, 2026. The list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed.
  • Extension. What the Rakuten browser extension shows on Bose.com is not confirmed. With no store page loading, no rate was on show for it to activate.
  • Card rewards. The rewards on the card you pay with are separate and are earned either way; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • Anyone starting a Bose.com order from Rakuten for cash back on November 27, 2026. No rate was shown.
  • Anyone reading Best Buy's "up to 6%" as the rate on Bose headphones. That is not confirmed.
  • Buyers who want a model or color sold only at Bose.com. Another store's rate does not help there.
  • Anyone for whom the lowest shelf price is at a store with a low rate. The price gap can be larger than the cash back.

Quick answers

Does Rakuten give cash back at Bose.com? Not as read on November 27, 2026. Rakuten's Bose store page did not load and showed no rate.

Can Bose products earn cash back at other stores? Yes. Rakuten's Bose brand page listed nine stores, with headline rates from 1% to 15% that day.

What does Best Buy pay on Bose headphones? Not confirmed. Best Buy's page named no Bose tier on November 27, 2026; its Audio tier was 1%.
