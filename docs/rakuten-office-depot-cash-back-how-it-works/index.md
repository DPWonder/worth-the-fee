# Rakuten Office Depot cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-office-depot-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Office Depot and OfficeMax, which share one store page. On November 25, 2026, that page showed 2% cash back with no raised rate. Gift cards, postage stamps and Business Services Division orders earn nothing, and the store's cookie consent must be accepted on its site.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## Rates seen on November 25, 2026

The page has a category list. Every line was at the same rate that day.

| Category on Rakuten's list | Rate shown |
|---|---|
| Accessories | 2% |
| Computers | 2% |
| Electronics | 2% |
| Furniture | 2% |
| All other non-excluded items | 2% |

No "was" label was shown, so 2% is the plain rate. The page's Black Friday product list carried the same 2%. Nothing on the page promises a higher rate later in the week.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for the store list three things that earn nothing:

| Earns nothing | Note |
|---|---|
| Gift card purchases | Buying gift cards |
| Postage stamps | No further detail |
| Business Services Division orders | The terms do not define which accounts this covers |

Print, copy and shipping services are not named in the exclusions or in the category list. Whether they earn under "all other non-excluded items" is not confirmed.

## The cookie consent rule

The terms carry a special condition: cash back is not available unless the store's cookie consent policy is accepted on its site. Rakuten's general help page says cookies are how it knows where a member is shopping. A shopper who declines the cookie banner on the store's site should expect no cash back on that order.

## Coupon codes and free gift offers

The terms say that using coupon codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's page listed several codes of its own, each at 2% cash back. Examples: a free $25 gift card with a $150 purchase, and a free $20 gas card with a $100 purchase.

Those are codes Rakuten lists, so they fit the rule. Whether a free gift card received with a qualifying order changes the amount that earns is not confirmed.

## The store's own rewards

Rakuten's page describes the store's rewards program in two places, and the two do not match. One passage says members get 10% back in points on ink, toner, paper and copy, print and ship services. Another says 2% back in rewards on supplies, furniture and tech. Which is current is not confirmed; the store's own site is the place to look.

The cash back terms do not mention the rewards program. Whether the part of an order paid with a rewards certificate earns cash back is not confirmed.

## Pickup, stores and returns

- **Pickup.** The page says the store offers free in-store and curbside pickup. The cash back terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The page says cash back is for online purchases at OfficeDepot.com or OfficeMax.com. No in-store offer was shown on it. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).
- **Returns.** The page says unopened products can be returned within 90 days. Rakuten's help page says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back.

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "tomorrow". Payment follows every three months; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). If nothing appears, see [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## Who should skip it

- You buy through a Business Services Division account. Those orders are excluded.
- You are buying gift cards or postage stamps.
- You decline cookie banners. This store's terms require the consent.
- You have a code from the store's emails or flyers that is not on Rakuten and is worth more than 2%.
- You buy in a store. The rate shown is for online orders.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Office Depot?**
2% on November 25, 2026, in every listed category. Rates change daily.

**Do gift cards bought there earn cash back?**
No. Gift card purchases and postage stamps are on the excluded list.

**Does OfficeMax count too?**
Yes. Rakuten lists Office Depot and OfficeMax as one store with one set of terms.
