# Rakuten Staples cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-staples-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Staples. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Staples page showed up to 6% cash back, marked "was 2%". Ink, toner and tech earn 2%, and computers, laptops, desktops, tablets, HP printers, e-readers and gift cards earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## Rates seen on November 25, 2026

| Rate | Category on Rakuten's list |
|---|---|
| 6% | All other non-excluded items |
| 2% | Ink and toner |
| 2% | Tech |
| 0% | Excluded items (see below) |

The headline carried a "was 2%" label. That label appears when a rate has been raised above the store's base rate, so 6% is a raised rate and can drop back. The page gives no end date. Which products Staples files under "Tech" is not defined on the page; not confirmed.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Staples list these as earning nothing:

- Gift cards
- Computers, laptops and desktops
- HP printers
- Tablets and iPads
- E-readers
- Furniture services
- Staples Business Advantage orders
- The part of a purchase paid with a gift card or Staples Rewards
- Orders Staples deems to be for reselling

Other printer brands are not named in the exclusions. Whether they earn 2% as tech or 6% as "all other" is not confirmed.

## Two rules that are easy to miss

- **Empty cart first.** The terms say cash back is not available on items placed in the Staples cart before clicking through from Rakuten. Items added earlier and left in the cart do not earn.
- **One order per trip.** Cash back is only available on one order per shopping trip. A second order after the same click does not earn. The terms do not say what starts a new trip; not confirmed.

## Coupon codes

The Staples terms on Rakuten have no rule about coupon codes. Rakuten's page listed several Staples codes on November 25, 2026, including offers on chairs, stamps and signs. Whether a code from outside Rakuten affects cash back at this store is not confirmed.

## What it comes to

| Purchase | Price | Rate seen | Cash back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copy paper and office supplies | $100 | 6% | $6.00 |
| Ink or toner | $100 | 2% | $2.00 |
| Laptop | $700 | Excluded | $0 |
| Supplies paid half with Staples Rewards | $100 | 6% on $50 | $3.00 |

Taxes and shipping are not mentioned in the Staples terms.

## Pickup, app and stores

- **Pickup.** The terms do not mention store pickup. Not confirmed.
- **App.** The terms do not mention the Staples app. Not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The Staples page showed an online rate only. Staples did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

Rakuten's page describes Staples Rewards as paying 5% back in rewards on in-store purchases. That is the store's own program. Spending those rewards cuts the amount that earns cash back.

## When it shows up

If an order does not appear, see [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/). For computers and tablets, other stores' rules are in [Rakuten at Best Buy](/rakuten-best-buy-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Who should skip it

- You are buying a computer, tablet, e-reader, HP printer or gift card. They earn nothing.
- Your items were already in the Staples cart before the click from Rakuten.
- You order through Staples Business Advantage.
- You are paying most of the order with Staples Rewards or a gift card.
- You are buying only ink or toner and expect 6%. Those earn 2%.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Staples?**
Up to 6% on November 25, 2026, raised from 2%. Ink, toner and tech earn 2%. Rates change daily.

**Do laptops and tablets earn cash back at Staples?**
No. Computers, laptops, desktops, tablets, iPads, e-readers and HP printers are on the excluded list.

**Does it matter when items go in the cart?**
Yes. The terms say items placed in the Staples cart before clicking through from Rakuten do not earn.
