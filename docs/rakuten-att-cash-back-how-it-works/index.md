# Rakuten AT&T cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-att-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, but only for home internet and only for new subscribers. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's AT&T Internet page showed up to $150 cash back, raised from $50, as a flat dollar amount and not a percentage. It is limited to one per member and is earned only after the service has been active for at least 60 days.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## Amounts seen on November 25, 2026

| Service, as listed | Cash back shown |
|---|---|
| AT&T Fiber Internet | $150 |
| "AIA" | $150 |
| AT&T Copper Internet | $5 |
| Headline | Up to $150, "was $50" |

The rate list does not spell out "AIA". Elsewhere the page mentions a 5G home internet service called Internet Air. Whether "AIA" means that service is not confirmed.

The "was $50" label means the amount was raised above the base that day. Nothing on the page says how long it lasts.

## What is excluded

The page at this address is for AT&T Internet. Its terms say:

- AT&T Voice earns nothing.
- AT&T Wireless, AT&T Business and DIRECTV plans earn nothing here. The terms say those orders must be placed through their own store pages on Rakuten.
- Cash back is only for new subscribers.
- One per member.
- Free trials earn nothing.
- The cash back cannot be more than the amount actually paid for the subscription.

The terms have no store-specific rule on coupon codes. Rakuten's general help page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.

## The 60-day condition

The terms say a member is eligible only after the merchant confirms that internet service has been activated for at least 60 days and payment has been processed.

The page does not fully agree with itself here. Its posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account within one week. The special terms require 60 days of active service first. The terms are the stricter reading.

| Situation | Earns? |
|---|---|
| New fiber internet subscriber, service active 60 days, bill paid | Yes, $150 that day |
| New copper internet subscriber | Yes, $5 that day |
| Existing internet subscriber changing plans | No; new subscribers only |
| Second order by the same member | No; one per member |
| Service canceled before 60 days | No |
| Wireless phone plan ordered from this page | No |
| Voice | No |

## What the page does not settle

- **Who counts as new.** The terms do not define "new subscribers". Whether a former customer, or a wireless customer adding home internet, counts is not confirmed.
- **Orders by phone or in a store.** Rakuten's general help page says orders completed by chat, phone or customer service do not qualify. No in-store offer was shown on the page.
- **Bundles.** One deal on the page describes pairing fiber with wireless. Whether the wireless part of a bundle earns anything is not confirmed; the terms point wireless orders to a separate Rakuten page.
- **Reward cards.** The page listed a deal for a $100 reward card from the provider. Whether that offer and the cash back can both be had on one order is not confirmed.

## When you get the money

After the 60 days and the confirmed payment, the cash back becomes payable on the next of Rakuten's four dates a year; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). If nothing appears, see [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## Who should skip it

- You already have this provider's home internet. The offer is for new subscribers.
- You want a wireless plan, voice or DIRECTV. This page's offer does not cover them.
- You have already earned this offer once.
- You may cancel within 60 days.
- You plan to order by phone or in a store.
- Only copper service reaches your address. The amount shown was $5.

## Quick answers

**How much does Rakuten pay for AT&T Internet?**
Up to $150 on November 25, 2026, for fiber; $5 for copper. Amounts change.

**Do existing customers qualify?**
No. The terms say cash back is only available for new subscribers.

**When is it earned?**
After the service has been active at least 60 days and payment is processed.
