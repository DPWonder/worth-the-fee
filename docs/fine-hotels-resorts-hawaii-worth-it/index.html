Is booking a Hawaii resort through Amex Fine Hotels + Resorts worth it?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

It is usually worth it for a short stay at a resort you were going to book anyway, provided the Amex Travel rate is close to the hotel's own rate. The fixed perks, a $100 property credit and up to $300 back from the Platinum Card, do not grow with the length of the stay, so they matter less on a week.

What a Fine Hotels + Resorts booking includes

Amex lists these benefits for Platinum Card members who book through Amex Travel:

Benefit Amex's terms Certain or not
Daily breakfast for two Every morning of the stay Certain
Property credit $100 per stay toward eligible charges, which vary by hotel Certain, but sometimes food and drink only
Late checkout Guaranteed 4 p.m. Certain
Early check-in Noon, when available Not guaranteed
Room upgrade On arrival, when available; some room types excluded Not guaranteed

On November 20, 2026, Amex Travel listed 25 Fine Hotels + Resorts properties in Hawaii, on Oahu, Maui, Kauai, the Big Island and Lanai. Examples are Halekulani, Andaz Maui, Grand Hyatt Kauai and Mauna Lani.

The Platinum hotel credit on top

The Platinum Card gives up to $300 back each half-year, up to $600 per calendar year, on prepaid Fine Hotels + Resorts bookings made through Amex Travel and paid with the card.

  • One stay in one half of the year uses one $300 credit.
  • From January 1, 2027, a Fine Hotels + Resorts booking needs at least two nights to earn the credit. Until then one night qualifies.
  • Only "Book and Pay Today" earns the credit and 5 points per dollar. "Book and Pay at Check-in" does not.

What it adds up to on a short stay

The fixed part is simple: $100 at the hotel plus up to $300 back from the card is up to $400 per stay. Breakfast for two is extra value, but only what you would have paid for breakfast anyway.

Stay length Fixed value ($100 + $300) Fixed value per night
1 night (through 2026) $400 $400
2 nights $400 $200
5 nights $400 $80

Set that against Hawaii prices. One guide puts Grand Hyatt Kauai cash rates at $800 to $1,300 a night. At $800, the fixed $400 is a quarter of a two-night bill and under a tenth of a seven-night bill.

When a direct or other booking is cheaper

Check these before paying:

  • The rate itself. AwardWallet notes that Amex Travel prices "can vary widely" from booking direct. Compare the total with taxes and fees for the same room and dates.
  • Member, resident or package rates. A hotel's member, resident or package rates may not appear on Amex Travel. Whether the rates match is not confirmed for any Hawaii hotel.
  • Resort fees. No source read says whether Fine Hotels + Resorts rates include Hawaii resort fees, or whether the $300 credit applies to them. Not confirmed.
  • Points. Paying with Membership Rewards points through Amex Travel gives about 0.7 cents per point on prepaid hotels, per The Points Guy.
  • Hyatt points. Andaz Maui and Grand Hyatt Kauai can also be booked with World of Hyatt points. See what Hyatt hotels in Hawaii cost in points.

A simple test: take the Amex Travel total, subtract $300 if the credit is unused, subtract $100 only if you would spend it at the hotel anyway, and subtract what breakfast would have cost you. If the result is below the cheapest direct total, the booking wins.

Splitting a longer stay

Two separate bookings in the same half-year still earn one $300 credit, because the cap is per half-year. Two bookings charged in different half-years can each earn up to $300. The exact dates of each half-year were not confirmed on Amex's page.

Who should skip it

  • Travelers who normally rent a condo or pick mid-priced hotels. Paying several hundred dollars more per night to collect $400 leaves you worse off.
  • Anyone who finds a much lower direct or package rate.
  • Anyone counting on the upgrade. It is given only when available.

Quick answers

Does Fine Hotels + Resorts include breakfast in Hawaii? Yes. Amex lists daily breakfast for two on every Fine Hotels + Resorts stay.

Can one Hawaii stay earn both $300 hotel credits? Not as one booking. The credit is up to $300 per half-year.

Does the $100 property credit cover golf or spa? It depends on the hotel. Eligible charges vary by property, and some limit the credit to food and drink.
