# Rakuten Ace Hardware cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-ace-hardware-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Ace Hardware page showed up to 6% cash back, raised from a base of 2%: 6% on most items and 3% on snow equipment. Delivery and assembly charges and the purchase or redemption of gift cards earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## Rates seen on November 25, 2026

| Category, as listed | Rate shown |
|---|---|
| All other items | 6% |
| Snow equipment | 3% |
| Headline | Up to 6%, "was 2%" |

Here the headline rate is the general one, and the named category is lower. The page does not define "snow equipment". Whether shovels, ice melt or only powered machines fall under it is not confirmed. The activation page on the day of purchase is the place to look.

The "was 2%" label means the rate was raised above the store's base that day. Nothing on the page says how long it lasts.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Ace Hardware are short. Listed as earning nothing:

- Delivery and assembly
- The purchase of gift cards
- The redemption of gift cards

One special term: using coupon codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. The page adds that the store accepts only one coupon or promo code per order.

## What it comes to

| Purchase | Price | Rate seen | Cash back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hand tools and paint | $100 | 6% | $6.00 |
| Grill | $500 | 6% | $30.00 |
| Snow equipment | $500 | 3% | $15.00 |
| Assembly or delivery charge | Any | Excluded | $0 |
| Gift card | Any | Excluded | $0 |

These are worked examples, not prices from the page. Rakuten's general help page says taxes and shipping fees are never eligible. At the 2% base, the $100 order would earn $2.00.

## Pickup, rewards and local stores

- **Store pickup.** The page describes free store pickup for orders placed on the website, and free shipping to stores. The terms do not exclude pickup, and they do not mention it either. Not confirmed.
- **Ace Rewards.** The page describes the free rewards program: 10 points per $1 and a $5 reward for every 2,500 points. It says a reward and a coupon can be used on the same order. Whether the part of an order paid with a reward earns cash back is not confirmed; the terms name gift cards, not rewards.
- **App.** The page mentions the store's mobile app. The terms do not. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension; whether an order finished in the store's app tracks is not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The store page showed an online rate only. The store did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## When it shows up

The page says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "in a few days". Payment follows on Rakuten's four dates a year; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). Rates at two larger chains are in [Rakuten at Home Depot](/rakuten-home-depot-cash-back-how-it-works/) and [Rakuten at Lowe's](/rakuten-lowes-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Who should skip it

- You walk into a local store and pay at the register. The rate shown is for online orders.
- You are buying a gift card or paying with one.
- A large share of the bill is delivery or assembly.
- You have a coupon from the store's emails that is not on Rakuten and saves more than the cash back.
- You are waiting for the 6% to hold. It is a raised rate, and none is promised.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Ace Hardware?**
Up to 6% on November 25, 2026; snow equipment was at 3%. Rates change daily.

**Do delivery and assembly earn cash back?**
No. Both are on the excluded list, along with gift cards.

**Does store pickup count?**
The terms do not exclude it and do not mention it. Not confirmed.
