Does the Amex Platinum charge foreign transaction fees, and how well does it work abroad?

Facts checked November 22, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

The Amex Platinum charges no foreign transaction fee on purchases, and its lounge and hotel benefits work around the world. American Express is accepted at fewer merchants abroad than Visa or Mastercard, cash from an ATM is expensive, and several of the card's statement credits apply only in the United States.

The fee

Amex's cardmember agreement for the Platinum Card lists the foreign transaction fee as "None." Upgraded Points (November 2026) reports the same, and says additional Platinum Cards follow the same terms.

Upgraded Points, citing Amex, puts a typical foreign transaction fee on other cards at 3%.

Spending in foreign currency Card with a 3% fee Amex Platinum
$1,000 $30 $0
$4,000 $120 $0

Many cards with no annual fee also charge no foreign fee. This saving alone does not cover an $895 annual fee.

The exchange rate. Under the agreement, an Amex company converts each foreign charge to US dollars on the day it is processed. The rate is no more than the highest official government rate or the highest interbank rate it identifies for that day or the prior business day.

Where the card is accepted

Sources describe acceptance abroad this way:

  • Upgraded Points, citing Amex, gives more than 170 million accepting locations worldwide. It also says Amex does not have the international footprint of Visa or Mastercard, and that acceptance varies by merchant.
  • The Points Guy (2021) says Amex is easier to use in countries such as Australia and Canada, and that in Europe and Asia it varies from merchant to merchant.
  • Upgraded Points lists places where Amex is not accepted at all: Belarus, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, and Crimea and the Donetsk and Luhansk regions of Ukraine.

Both sources suggest carrying a Visa or Mastercard as a second card. No current merchant count that compares the three networks outside the United States was found, so that comparison is not confirmed in numbers.

Paying in dollars at the terminal

Some foreign terminals and ATMs offer to charge you in US dollars. This is dynamic currency conversion. The merchant or ATM operator sets the rate, not Amex. Upgraded Points, citing a European consumer group, says the markup generally runs from 2.6% to 12%. Choosing the local currency leaves the conversion to Amex.

Cash and ATMs

The agreement treats ATM withdrawals and buying foreign currency as cash advances.

Who should skip it

  • Anyone who wants one card for a whole trip. Both sources above suggest a backup.
  • Anyone who mainly needs cash abroad. A debit card with low ATM fees costs less.
  • Anyone who wants bonus points on meals abroad. This card pays 1 point per dollar there.
  • Anyone living abroad for most of the year, since the Uber, Resy and lululemon credits need US spending.

Quick answers

Does the Amex Platinum charge a foreign transaction fee? No. Amex's cardmember agreement lists the foreign transaction fee as none, for the main card and additional cards.

Is the Amex Platinum accepted everywhere abroad? No. Acceptance varies by country and merchant, and sources suggest carrying a Visa or Mastercard as well.

Can I use the Amex Platinum at an ATM abroad? It counts as a cash advance. The fee is 5% with a $10 minimum, and interest starts the same day.
