Rakuten Lowe's cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

The short answer

The page header read "Up to 2% Cash Back" with a "was 1%" label. "Was" is Rakuten's own label for the store's usual rate. Two Rakuten addresses for the store showed the same rates, and Rakuten's Black Friday page showed up to 2% on November 20, 2026. How long the raised rate lasts is not confirmed.

What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Lowe's list these as earning nothing:

  • Lumber
  • Decking
  • My Project and installation purchases, including items picked up and delivered by an installer
  • Installation services
  • Gift cards, both buying them and paying with them

The gift card rule runs both ways. A separate Rakuten help page names Lowe's among stores that do not pay cash back on the part of an order paid with a gift card.

The terms do not define "lumber". Whether plywood, trim or fence pickets count as lumber is not confirmed.

One term that is stricter than usual

Missing cash back must be reported within 60 days of the order date. Rakuten's general terms allow 90 days. The terms say cash back is added to the Rakuten account the next day.

Pickup, app, coupons and discounts

  • Pickup. The cash back terms do not mention pickup orders. Not confirmed.
  • App. The terms do not mention the Lowe's app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension, so an order started directly in the store's app is not expected to track.
  • Coupon codes. No code rule is listed for Lowe's. Rakuten's general page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.
  • Military discount. Whether using the store's military discount affects cash back is not confirmed.
  • Pro accounts. The terms do not mention MyLowe's Pro Rewards or business accounts. Not confirmed.
  • Returns. The terms have no rule for returns. Rakuten's general help page says returns and cancellations lose their cash back.

When you get the money

Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See when Rakuten pays and what to do when cash back did not track. Card rewards are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • You are buying lumber or decking. Those are excluded.
  • You are ordering an appliance, flooring or anything else with installation through the store.
  • You pay with Lowe's gift cards, or you are buying them.
  • You order by phone or at the store's pro desk. Rakuten's general page says phone orders do not earn.
  • You found a lower price elsewhere by more than 1% or 2%.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Lowe's? Up to 2% on November 21, 2026, against a usual 1%. Appliances earn 1%. Rates change daily.

Does lumber earn cash back? No. Lumber, decking, installation purchases and gift cards are excluded.

How long do I have to report missing cash back? 60 days from the order date, under Rakuten's terms for Lowe's. The general limit is 90 days.
