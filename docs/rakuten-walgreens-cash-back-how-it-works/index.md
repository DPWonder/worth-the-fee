# Rakuten Walgreens cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-walgreens-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Walgreens. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Walgreens page showed 2% cash back, with no raised rate two days before Black Friday. Pharmacy, prescription drugs, gift cards and the part of an order paid with Walgreens rewards earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate

| Date checked | Rate shown | Rakuten's "was" label |
|---|---|---|
| November 25, 2026 | 2% | None |

A "was" label appears when a rate has been raised above the store's base rate. The Walgreens page has none, so 2% is the plain rate. No category table appears; every product shown on the page carried the same 2%.

## What earns and what does not

| Purchase | Earns cash back? |
|---|---|
| Household, beauty and grocery items sold by Walgreens online | Yes, 2% on the day checked |
| Photo prints and photo gifts | Not listed as excluded; not confirmed |
| Contact lenses | Not listed as excluded; a contact lens code was listed with the 2% rate |
| Pharmacy | No |
| Prescription drugs | No |
| Gift cards | No |
| The part of an order paid with Walgreens rewards | No |

The terms name "Pharmacy" and "Prescription Drugs" as two separate lines. They do not say where the pharmacy line ends. Whether over-the-counter medicine counts as pharmacy is not confirmed.

## myWalgreens rewards

Rakuten's page describes myWalgreens as a free program that turns points into dollars off future purchases, with rewards of $5 to $50.

- **Earning rewards.** The terms do not stop an order from earning both myWalgreens points and cash back.
- **Spending rewards.** The part of an order paid with Walgreens rewards earns no cash back. On a $40 order with $10 of rewards applied, 2% would be figured on $30.

## Coupon codes

The Walgreens terms say that using coupon codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back, and that the safe course is to keep to codes Rakuten provides. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's page listed three Walgreens codes:

- 20% off $50 of eligible items sitewide
- An extra 20% off $60 or more
- 25% off contact lenses with free shipping

The page also says Walgreens has digital coupons in its own app. The terms do not say whether a digital coupon clipped in the Walgreens app counts as a code not listed on Rakuten. Not confirmed.

## Pickup, app and stores

- **Pickup.** The page describes same-day pickup for photo orders. The cash back terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
- **App.** The terms do not mention the Walgreens app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension. Whether an order finished in the Walgreens app tracks is not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The Walgreens page showed an online rate only. Walgreens did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).
- **Shipping.** The page says Walgreens ships product orders over $35 free, except to Alaska and Hawaii. That is the store's rule, not a cash back rule.

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "tomorrow". Rakuten's help page says online cash back typically takes 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed. Payment follows on Rakuten's schedule; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). If a purchase never appears, see [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## Who should skip it

- You are filling a prescription or buying from the pharmacy. Those earn nothing.
- You are buying gift cards.
- You are paying most of the order with Walgreens rewards.
- You have a coupon from outside Rakuten that takes more off than 2%.
- You shop in the store, not online. No in-store offer was seen.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Walgreens?**
2% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

**Do prescriptions earn Rakuten cash back at Walgreens?**
No. Pharmacy and prescription drugs are both on the excluded list.

**Can you use a Walgreens coupon code and still get cash back?**
A code listed on Rakuten is the safe one. The terms say other codes may void cash back.
