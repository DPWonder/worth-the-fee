# Rakuten Banana Republic cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-banana-republic-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Banana Republic. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Banana Republic page showed 2% cash back, with no raised rate. Gift cards, the Peter Do Collection, Banana Republic Home and the part of an order paid with reward points earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate seen on November 25, 2026

| Item | What the page showed |
|---|---|
| Headline rate | 2% |
| Rakuten's "was" label | None |
| Rate by category | No category list |

A "was" label appears when a rate has been raised above the store's base rate. This page has none, so 2% is the plain rate. The page listed a Black Friday offer from the store, 40% off regular-price items, at the same 2%. Nothing on the page promises a higher rate on Black Friday (November 27, 2026).

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Banana Republic list these as earning nothing:

- Gift cards, bought or redeemed
- The part of an order paid with reward points
- Items from the Peter Do Collection
- Banana Republic Home

## What earns and what does not

| Purchase | Earns cash back? |
|---|---|
| Clothing and accessories, ordered online after a Rakuten click | Yes, 2% |
| Sale styles | Not excluded in the terms |
| Peter Do Collection item | No |
| Banana Republic Home item | No |
| Gift card | No |
| Part of the order paid with reward points | No |

At 2%, a $150 order of eligible items earns $3.00. If $50 of that order is paid with reward points, $100 earns, for $2.00.

## Coupon codes: the firm wording

The terms say that using coupon codes not listed on Rakuten "will void" cash back, and to keep to codes Rakuten provides. Many store pages say "may void"; this one is firmer.

That matters here because the page itself describes other codes. It says signing up for the store's emails brings an instant code for a first order. Such a code comes from outside Rakuten. Under the terms, using it voids the 2%. On November 25, 2026, the offers Rakuten listed for this store were sales and free shipping, with no code shown.

## Rewards points

Rakuten's page says the rewards program is shared with Gap, Old Navy and Athleta, that points are earned on every purchase across those brands, and that members get free shipping on orders of $50 or more.

- **Earning points.** The terms do not exclude it. The page does not say outright that points and cash back are earned together at this store; not confirmed.
- **Spending points.** The part of an order paid with reward points earns no cash back.

The sister brands have their own Rakuten pages and their own terms; see [Rakuten at Gap](/rakuten-gap-cash-back-how-it-works/) and [Rakuten at Old Navy](/rakuten-old-navy-cash-back-how-it-works/). Banana Republic Factory is not mentioned on this page. Whether a Factory order earns after a click to Banana Republic is not confirmed.

## Pickup, app and stores

- **Pickup.** Rakuten's page describes buying online and picking up in store. The cash back terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
- **App.** The terms do not mention a store app. Not confirmed.
- **In stores.** The page showed an online rate only. Banana Republic did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

Taxes and shipping are not mentioned in the terms for this store.

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "tomorrow". Payment follows on Rakuten's schedule; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

## Who should skip it

- You have a first-order code from the store's emails. Using it voids the 2%.
- You are buying from the Peter Do Collection or Banana Republic Home.
- You are buying a gift card or paying with one.
- You are paying most of the order with reward points.
- You are waiting for a raised rate. None was shown, and none is promised.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Banana Republic?**
2% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

**Can you use a Banana Republic promo code and still get cash back?**
Only a code listed on Rakuten. The terms say other codes will void cash back.

**Does Banana Republic Home earn cash back?**
No. Banana Republic Home and the Peter Do Collection are both excluded.
