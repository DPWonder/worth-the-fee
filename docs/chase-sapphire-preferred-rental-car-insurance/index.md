# Does the Chase Sapphire Preferred cover a rental car, and what does primary coverage leave out?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/chase-sapphire-preferred-rental-car-insurance/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes. The Sapphire Preferred gives primary cover for theft of, or damage to, the rental car itself, up to $60,000, when you pay for the whole rental with the card and decline the rental company's collision damage waiver. It leaves out liability for other people and property, injuries, your belongings and several kinds of vehicle.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## What "primary" means

Primary cover pays first. You do not have to claim on your own auto policy before the card's benefit pays, so a claim need not touch your insurer. Secondary cover pays only what your own insurer does not.

One exception: for New York residents renting inside the United States, the cover is secondary to personal auto insurance.

## What it pays for

Chase's own guide lists these costs, capped at the car's actual cash value:

- Theft of the rental car
- Damage to the rental car
- Valid loss-of-use charges from the rental company
- Administrative fees
- Reasonable towing after a covered theft or damage

## The conditions

| Condition | Detail |
|---|---|
| Payment | The full rental is charged to the card. Chase points can be used toward it. |
| Waiver | Decline the rental company's collision damage waiver. Accepting it cancels the card benefit. |
| Driver | The cardmember is the primary driver on the rental agreement. |
| Length | Fewer than 31 consecutive days. |
| Vehicle value | List price of $125,000 or less, per Chase's card page. |
| Place | The United States and some countries abroad. |

A rental booked through Chase Travel with points keeps the cover, according to AwardWallet.

## What primary coverage leaves out

**Liability.** The benefit covers the car you rented. It does not pay for damage to another car or to property, and it does not pay for anyone's injuries. Upgraded Points lists liability as an exclusion. Check whether your own auto policy extends liability to a rental, especially abroad.

**Your belongings.** Valuables left in the car and lost or damaged are excluded.

**Some vehicles and rentals.** Chase's guide excludes:

- Expensive and exotic vehicles
- Vehicles more than 10 years old
- Motorcycles, mopeds and electric scooters
- Rented moving trucks and vans
- Large passenger vans
- Peer-to-peer rentals such as Turo
- Hourly rentals

**Some driving.** Driving off-road can void the cover.

## Where sources disagree

- **Van size.** Chase's guide excludes vans with more than nine passengers. Upgraded Points, summarizing the Guide to Benefits, says passenger vans that seat more than 12. If you plan to rent a van for a group, confirm with the benefit administrator first.
- **Countries.** Chase says the cover applies in the United States and "some countries abroad" and tells travelers to confirm the destination before leaving. Third-party guides say most countries. The list of countries is not confirmed on any page read for this article.

Chase's Guide to Benefits governs. It was not read directly for this page.

## If something happens

Chase's guide names these papers for a claim:

- A copy of the rental agreement
- A card statement showing the rental was paid in full with the card or points
- A repair estimate, an accident report form and photographs
- A police report for vandalism or carjacking

The claim deadline for this benefit is not confirmed. AwardWallet reports 20 days to give notice and 90 days to send documents for Chase's travel benefits in general, so report a loss right away.

## The sums

If you would otherwise buy the rental company's waiver, the saving is that daily price times your rental days, set against the card's $95 annual fee. The saving is zero if you would decline the waiver anyway and rely on your own policy, though primary cover still keeps a claim off that policy.

The other trip protections on the card are on the [travel insurance page](/chase-sapphire-preferred-travel-insurance/).

## Who should skip relying on it

- Anyone with no liability cover for the trip. The card does not supply it.
- Anyone renting for 31 days or more.
- Anyone renting through a car-sharing service, by the hour, or renting a moving truck.
- Anyone renting a luxury or exotic car, or a large van.
- New York residents with personal auto insurance who want primary cover inside the United States.
- Anyone who cannot put the whole rental on the card.

## Quick answers

**Does the Sapphire Preferred cover liability in a rental car?**
No. It covers theft of and damage to the rental car itself. Damage to other cars or property and injuries are outside the benefit.

**Do I need to decline the rental company's insurance?**
You need to decline its collision damage waiver, because accepting it cancels the card's cover. Liability insurance is a separate product and a separate decision.

**Does it cover Turo?**
No. Chase's guide excludes peer-to-peer rentals, and Upgraded Points names Turo as excluded.
