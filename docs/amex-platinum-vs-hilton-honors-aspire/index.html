Amex Platinum or Hilton Honors Aspire for someone who stays at Hilton?

Facts checked November 21, 20265 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

For someone whose hotel nights are mostly at Hilton, the Hilton Honors Aspire gives more for less: a $550 fee, top-tier Diamond status, 14 points per dollar at Hilton and a free night each year. The $895 Platinum Card gives Hilton Gold status and only 1 point per dollar on most Hilton bills; its strengths are airport lounges and points that move to airlines.

A Hilton point and an Amex point are not worth the same. The Points Guy values a Hilton point at 0.4 cents and One Mile at a Time at about 0.5 cents; both put an Amex point near 1.7 to 2 cents. So 14 Hilton points are worth about 5.6 to 7 cents, and 5 Amex points about 8.5 to 10 cents.

Gold against Diamond

Both statuses come with the fifth night free on award stays and space-available upgrades. The main differences:

  • Bonus points on paid stays: 80% with Gold, 100% with Diamond.
  • Food: Gold and Diamond both get a daily food and beverage credit at US hotels and breakfast at many hotels abroad. Diamond adds executive lounge access where a hotel has one. Sources disagree on whether Gold includes lounge access.
  • Upgrades: both are subject to availability.

The page on Platinum hotel status covers what Gold gives in more detail.

The sum for a Hilton stayer

Take $5,000 a year paid at Hilton hotels.

Amex Platinum Hilton Aspire
Points earned 5,000 Amex points 70,000 Hilton points
As Hilton points 10,000 (transfer at 1:2) 70,000

On fees, start with the credits a Hilton stayer uses without changing habits.

  • Aspire: $550, less $200 of flight credit used across four quarters, less $200 from one resort stay, leaves $150. The yearly free night then covers that if the room would have cost more than $150.
  • Platinum: $895. No credit applies to a Hilton stay booked with Hilton. The $600 hotel credit needs a prepaid Fine Hotels + Resorts or Hotel Collection booking through Amex Travel.

The Aspire resort credit works only at Hilton properties on its resort list, not at every Hilton. A reader who never stays at a resort should leave that $400 out. The page on Surpass against Aspire compares the Aspire with the $150 Hilton card.

Which pattern favors which card

  • Ten or more Hilton nights a year, some at resorts: Aspire. Status, earning and credits all apply to those stays.
  • Flies often and wants lounges: Platinum. No airport lounge access is listed for the Aspire in the review read.
  • Wants points for flights: Platinum. Its points move to about 16 airline programs. Aspire points stay in Hilton Honors.
  • Splits hotel nights across Hilton, Marriott and independent luxury hotels: Platinum, for Gold status at two chains and the $600 hotel credit.
  • Both: the two fees total $1,445 a year. That fits only a reader who would use the lounges and the Hilton benefits fully.

Who should skip it

  • Skip the Aspire if you stay at Hilton fewer than a handful of nights a year. The $150 Surpass or no Hilton card costs less.
  • Skip the Aspire if you would not use the quarterly flight credit. It is $50 at a time and unused amounts are lost.
  • Skip the Platinum if Hilton stays are your main reason. It earns 1x there and gives the lower status.
  • Skip both if you carry a balance. Interest costs more than either card returns.

Welcome offers on both cards vary by applicant and change, so they are left out of the sums. Amex generally allows one welcome offer per card in a lifetime; see the once-per-lifetime rule.

Quick answers

Does the Amex Platinum give Hilton Diamond status? No. It gives Hilton Gold on enrollment. The Hilton Honors Aspire gives Diamond for as long as the card is held.

Can Amex Platinum points become Hilton points? Yes, at 1:2. One thousand Amex points become 2,000 Hilton points.

Does the Hilton Aspire include airport lounge access? None is listed in the September 2026 review read. A reader comment there says Priority Pass was removed; that is not confirmed.
