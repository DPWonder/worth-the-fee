When is the Amex Platinum annual fee charged, and can you get it refunded?

Facts checked November 22, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

The $895 annual fee is reported to post on your first billing statement and then once every 12 statements. Amex's cardmember agreement says the fee is refunded if you close the account within 30 days after the closing date of the statement that shows it. After that, the fee is non-refundable.

When the fee posts

Amex's agreement does not give a posting date. Doctor of Credit's guide to issuer fee timing reports this pattern for Amex:

Year When the fee posts
First year The first statement closing date after approval
Later years Every twelfth statement after that: the 13th, the 25th and so on

This timing comes from one source and was not confirmed on a US Amex page.

The fee is due with that statement's balance. It does not count toward a welcome offer's spending requirement, because a fee is not a purchase. See what counts toward the spending requirement.

The refund rule, in Amex's words

The Platinum Card agreement says Amex will refund the annual fee if you tell it you are voluntarily closing the account within 30 days after the closing date of the billing statement on which the fee appears. It goes on: "For cancellations after this 30-day period, the Annual Fee is non-refundable."

The clock starts at the statement closing date, not the payment due date.

Example Date
Statement showing the fee closes January 10
Last day to close for a full refund February 9
Closing on February 10 or later No refund

Two details from the agreement:

  • The rule covers closing by your own choice, by calling or writing to Amex.
  • The agreement says this policy does not apply if your billing address is in Massachusetts when you close. What applies there is not confirmed.

Where sources differ. One Mile at a Time (September 2026) describes the window as 30 days from the fee being billed. The Points Guy and Doctor of Credit count 30 days after the statement. This page uses the agreement's wording.

Downgrading instead of closing

The agreement's refund rule covers closing only. One Mile at a Time, The Points Guy and Doctor of Credit report that a downgrade after the 30 days often brings a prorated refund of the unused fee. That is not confirmed in an Amex document. The choices, their fees and what happens to your points are on the downgrade and cancel page.

Additional card fees

Card Yearly fee
Each Additional Platinum Card $195
Each Companion Platinum Card $0

The agreement applies the same refund policy to an additional card's fee: the 30 days run from the closing date of the statement showing that fee, and the policy applies when you cancel that additional card. When the $195 posts, and whether it follows the main card's date or the date the additional card was added, is not confirmed. See whether an additional card is worth $195.

Closing in the first year

The Points Guy (2020) and One Mile at a Time (2017) quote Amex welcome offer terms that name canceling or downgrading within 12 months of opening as a reason Amex may withhold or take back welcome points, and may close your other Amex accounts. The current Platinum Card offer terms could not be read for this page, so whether today's offer carries that wording is not confirmed.

A new cardholder who closes inside the first 30-day window gets the fee back under the agreement but falls inside the 12-month wording. Welcome offers vary by applicant and change; see the once-per-lifetime rule.

The sums at renewal

If you Fee outcome
Close within 30 days after the renewal statement closes $895 refunded
Close after that $0 refunded
Downgrade after that Prorated refund reported, not confirmed by Amex

Before the window ends, count what the credits returned over the past year.

Who should skip relying on a refund

  • Anyone past the 30 days. The agreement says the fee stays paid.
  • Anyone in the first 12 months who cares about the welcome points.
  • Anyone whose only Membership Rewards card is the Platinum and who still holds points.
  • Anyone counting on a prorated refund for a downgrade without hearing the amount from Amex.

Quick answers

When does Amex charge the Platinum annual fee? Doctor of Credit reports it posts on the first statement after approval, then on every twelfth statement after that.

How long do I have to cancel and get the annual fee back? Thirty days after the closing date of the statement that shows the fee, under Amex's cardmember agreement.

Does the annual fee count toward the welcome offer spending? No. Fees are not purchases, so the $895 does not count.
