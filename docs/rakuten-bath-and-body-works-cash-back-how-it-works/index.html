Rakuten's page says it in one line: "Bath and Body Works doesn't offer Cash Back on Rakuten." The store's web address on Rakuten leads to the same page.

Because the page has no terms section, there is no list of excluded items to report. The whole store is outside cash back for now.

Black Friday and Cyber Monday pages

Rakuten keeps a Black Friday page and a Cyber Monday page for the store. Both were read on November 27, 2026. The Cyber Monday page title also says coupons only. Neither page listed a deal or a code for Bath & Body Works itself.

The rates printed on those pages sit under a heading for similar stores. They belong to other stores, not to Bath & Body Works. Cyber Monday is November 30, 2026; nothing on either page says the store will pay cash back that day.

The reviews on the page mention cash back

The page shows a member rating of 4.8 from 92 reviews. Some of those reviews speak of earning cash back on Bath & Body Works orders. Rakuten's page gives no dates for the reviews. That fits a time when the store did pay, but when it paid, at what rate and when it stopped are not confirmed.

Rewards program, stores and the extension

  • Store rewards. Rakuten's page says nothing about the store's own rewards program. It belongs to the store and does not depend on a Rakuten click.
  • In stores. The page showed no in-store offer. Bath & Body Works did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 27, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.
  • Extension. What the Rakuten browser extension shows on the store's site is not confirmed. With no rate on the store page, there is no cash back for it to activate.
  • Gift cards. Rakuten had no gift card page for the store at the address its other gift card pages use. Whether a gift card seller on Rakuten carries the store's cards is not confirmed.

Who should skip it

  • Anyone starting a Bath & Body Works order from Rakuten for cash back. There is none to earn.
  • Anyone reading a rate on the store's Black Friday page as the store's own. Those rates are for other stores.
  • Anyone waiting for Cyber Monday for a rate here. None is promised.
  • Shoppers who only want this store's own scents. Another store's rate does not help if it does not sell them.

Quick answers

Does Rakuten give cash back at Bath & Body Works? No. On November 27, 2026, the store's Rakuten page showed coupons only and no rate.

Is there a Black Friday or Cyber Monday rate for Bath & Body Works? No. Both Rakuten pages for the store showed no cash back. The rates on them belong to other stores.

Are there Bath & Body Works coupon codes on Rakuten? None appeared on the page as loaded on November 27, 2026, despite the coupons label.
