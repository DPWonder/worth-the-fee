How many Delta flights does it take for the SkyMiles Gold Amex checked-bag benefit to cover the annual fee?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Four one-way Delta flights with one checked bag, which is two round trips, cover the Delta SkyMiles Gold Amex's $150 annual fee for a solo traveler. Two people checking a bag each on the same reservation cover it in one round trip.

The numbers behind the answer

  • The card's annual fee is $0 in the first year, then $150.
  • Delta's first checked bag costs $45 each way on domestic and short-haul international routes, for tickets bought on or after April 8, 2026. Before that it was $35.
  • The card makes the first checked bag free on Delta flights.

$150 divided by $45 is 3.3. So the fourth one-way flight with a checked bag is the one that puts you past the fee.

One-way flights with a bag One traveler Two travelers Family of four
1 $45 $90 $180
2 (one round trip) $90 $180 $360
4 (two round trips) $180 $360 $720
6 (three round trips) $270 $540 $1,080
8 (four round trips) $360 $720 $1,440

Figures in the table are bag fees avoided, before the $150 fee. In the first year there is no fee, so any checked bag is a saving.

At the old $35 bag fee the same sum came to five one-way flights. The April 2026 price rise shortened it to four.

Who is covered

The benefit covers the cardmember and up to eight companions traveling on the same reservation. Two details decide whether it works on a given trip:

  • The cardmember's SkyMiles number has to be on the reservation.
  • The ticket does not have to be bought with the card, according to One Mile at a Time.

Companions booked on a separate reservation are not covered. A family that books in two parts may get the free bag on only one of them.

The second bag

Since June 4, 2026, the card also gives a free second checked bag on domestic Delta-operated flights. Delta charges $55 for a second bag, so a traveler who checks two bags on a domestic flight avoids $100 each way. For that traveler, one round trip ($200) is more than the annual fee.

The free second bag is for domestic flights only. The free first bag applies on Delta flights worldwide.

What can shrink the saving

  • You may already get free bags. Medallion members and first class passengers do not pay for the first bag, so the card adds nothing for bags on those trips.
  • Long-haul international trips. Delta did not change bag fees on long-haul international flights in April 2026. Check the fee for your own route before counting on $45.
  • Basic fares. Whether the free bag applies on Delta's lowest fares is not confirmed in the sources read for this page. Check Delta's terms before you book one.
  • Carry-on only. If you travel with a carry-on, the bag benefit is worth nothing to you.
  • Other airlines. The benefit is described for Delta flights. Whether a flight operated by a partner airline counts is not confirmed.

What else the $150 buys

The bag is the main reason to hold this card, but it is not the only feature:

  • 2 miles per dollar on Delta purchases, at restaurants and at US supermarkets; 1 mile elsewhere.
  • 15% off award flights on Delta booked on delta.com or in the Fly Delta app.
  • Up to $100 a year back on prepaid Delta Stays bookings.
  • Up to $10 a month back on US rideshare with select providers, after the card's first renewal. Enrollment is required.
  • A $200 Delta flight credit after $10,000 of purchases in a calendar year.
  • Zone 5 boarding and 20% back on inflight purchases.

The card has no lounge access and no companion certificate. For those, see how Gold, Platinum and Reserve compare.

Who should skip it

  • People who fly Delta with a checked bag fewer than four one-way flights a year, alone.
  • People who pack carry-on only.
  • People with Medallion status, who already check a bag for free.
  • People who fly several airlines and would rarely be on Delta.

Welcome offers on this card vary by applicant and change, and are not part of the sums above.

Quick answers

How many round trips cover the $150 fee? Two round trips with one checked bag each way for one traveler, or one round trip for two travelers on the same reservation.

Do I have to pay for the flight with the card? One Mile at a Time reports that you do not, but your SkyMiles number must be on the reservation.

Is a second checked bag free too? Yes on domestic Delta-operated flights, since June 4, 2026. On other routes only the first bag is free.
