Rakuten sign-up bonus and referral bonus: how each works, what to spend, and what makes it fail

The short answer

A new Rakuten member gets one bonus, not two: the referral bonus for joining through a member's link, or the welcome bonus for joining from Rakuten's own pages. As read on November 25, 2026, the referral offer asks for purchases that earn cash back totaling at least $50 within 90 days of signing up, and the welcome bonus asks for $25. Amounts and terms change.

Rakuten's referral FAQ says the new member gets "the bonus that was available on day of sign up." The amount on the page you join from is the one that applies to you.

The 10% version is a separate offer called the Boost Bonus. Its terms say it adds to the rate only at stores where a boost is advertised, for the period named in the offer, up to $50.00 in total.

The steps

  1. Open the member's referral link and create a new account from it. Rakuten's join article gives a second way: choose "Did someone refer you?" on the join form and enter the email the member uses for Rakuten.
  2. Within 90 days, start at Rakuten (site, app or browser extension) each time, click through to a store, and buy. Qualifying purchases must total at least $50, in one order or several; see how Rakuten works, step by step.
  3. Choose how you want to be paid in Account Settings. A check or PayPal needs a mailing address on file; Rakuten says Membership Rewards points and Bilt Points do not.
  4. Wait for the store to confirm the purchase.
  5. The bonus is paid with your next cash back payment.

Adding a referrer after an account already exists is not confirmed.

When the money really arrives

Neither bonus is instant. The store confirms your purchase, the bonus is confirmed with it, and it goes out on the next payment date after that. A late November purchase confirmed in three weeks is paid on February 15, 2027; one confirmed in 14 weeks is paid on May 15, 2027. More in when Rakuten pays.

The welcome bonus FAQ says that bonus is sent once its three steps are complete; the welcome bonus terms say it is added to the next cash back payment or points transfer.

What counts as a qualifying purchase

A purchase qualifies only if it earns cash back through Rakuten.com, the app or the extension. These do not count:

  • Purchases on Amazon.com (music.amazon.com is the one exception in the terms).
  • Stores listed with no cash back. Walmart showed "No Cash Back" on November 25, 2026.
  • Most gift-card purchases.
  • Tax and shipping.
  • Items or categories the store excludes.
  • Orders placed by phone, chat or customer service.

What makes it fail

Problems with the account

  • You already had a Rakuten account. Both bonuses are for new members, and the terms allow one account per person.
  • You joined without the member's link or email.
  • You referred yourself, or the two accounts share a name, address or email. The program terms say such accounts may be flagged.
  • You live outside the 50 states or D.C. The program terms also list Canada; the current offer terms name only the 50 states and D.C.
  • Someone else paid for the purchase.

Problems with the purchase

  • You returned, exchanged or canceled it.
  • You spent under the total, or bought after the 90 days.
  • You used a coupon code not listed on Rakuten.
  • An ad blocker, VPN, declined cookies or another coupon extension stopped the tracking.
  • You added items to the cart first and activated Rakuten afterwards.
  • You left the tab idle for a long time, or checked out in a different tab.

Problems with payment

  • No payment method chosen, or no mailing address on file for a check or PayPal.

If a purchase does not show up after 48 hours, see what to do about missing cash back.

For the member who refers

The referral page's terms give the referring member a $50 Referral Bonus for each new member who joins between October 1 and December 31, 2026 and meets the purchase test, with no limit on referrals. It is added to the next cash back payment. The referral page says generally within 60 days of the friend's qualifying purchase; the help FAQ says when the friend's cash back is confirmed.

Who should skip it

  • You would be buying $50 of something you do not need.
  • You will not turn off an ad blocker or VPN for one purchase. The order may not track.
  • You plan to make the purchases at Amazon.com or on gift cards.
  • You already have a Rakuten account.

Quick answers

How much do I need to spend for the Rakuten referral bonus? At least $50 in purchases that earn cash back, within 90 days of joining, under the offer read on November 25, 2026.

Can I get the welcome bonus and the referral bonus? No. The welcome bonus terms say it cannot be combined with the Referral Sign Up Bonus.

When is the Rakuten bonus paid? After the store confirms your purchase, with the next payment: February 15, May 15, August 15 or November 15.
