How does the Rakuten sign-up bonus through a friend's link work, and what makes it fail?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page ends with a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

You join Rakuten through a friend's link, then make purchases that earn cash back totalling at least $50 within 90 days of signing up. The bonus fails most often because the purchase did not track, was returned, or was made at a store or on an item that earns no cash back.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rakuten says the purchase requirement and reward amount can vary, and the current offer period runs to December 31, 2026.

The steps

  1. Open your friend's referral link and create a new Rakuten account from it.
  2. Within 90 days, start at Rakuten (site, app or browser extension) each time, click through to a store, and buy. Your qualifying purchases must total at least $50.
  3. Choose how you want to be paid in Account Settings, and add a mailing address.
  4. Wait for the store to confirm the purchase.
  5. The bonus is paid on Rakuten's next payment date.

Your friend gets $50 once your purchase is confirmed. A recent guide puts the new member's bonus at $50 as well. The amount shown on the day you sign up is the one that applies to you.

When the money really arrives

The bonus is not instant. The store has to confirm your purchase first, which typically takes 3 to 14 weeks. Only then does the bonus go out, on the next of the four payment dates. A December purchase may miss February 15 and be paid on May 15. A travel booking takes longer, because the clock starts after the trip ends.

What counts as a qualifying purchase

A purchase qualifies only if it earns cash back through Rakuten. So anything that earns nothing does not move you toward the $50:

  • Purchases on Amazon.com do not count (music.amazon.com is the one exception).
  • Most gift-card purchases earn no cash back.
  • Tax and shipping never earn cash back.
  • Items or categories the store excludes. Each store lists these under its terms on Rakuten.
  • Orders placed by phone or chat.

What makes it fail

Problems with the account

  • You already had a Rakuten account. The bonus is for new members only.
  • You signed up without using the friend's link, so the accounts are not connected.
  • You referred yourself, or the two accounts share a name, address or email. Rakuten can treat that as abuse.
  • You live outside the 50 states, DC or Canada.
  • Someone else paid for the purchase. The new member has to be the one who pays.

Problems with the purchase

  • You returned, exchanged or cancelled it.
  • You spent under $50 in total, or bought after the 90 days.
  • You used a coupon code that did not come from Rakuten. That can void the cash back.
  • An ad blocker, VPN or cookie blocker stopped the tracking, or you declined cookies.
  • Another shopping or coupon extension took over the click.
  • You added items to the cart first and activated Rakuten afterwards.
  • You left the tab idle for a long time, or checked out in a different tab or window.

Problems with payment

  • No payment method chosen, or no mailing address on file.
  • The store has not confirmed the purchase yet.

If a purchase does not show up, wait out the normal confirmation window, then use Rakuten's Missing Cash Back form in its help center.

Who should not bother

If you would be buying $50 of something you do not need, or will not turn off an ad blocker for one purchase, skip it. Rakuten also has an ordinary welcome bonus outside referral links, which asks for $25 of purchases in 90 days. The two cannot be combined, so compare the amounts shown before picking one.

Quick answers

How much do I need to spend? At least $50 in purchases that earn cash back, within 90 days of joining.

Does an Amazon order count? No. Rakuten's terms leave out Amazon.com purchases.

When is the bonus paid? After the store confirms your purchase, on the next payment date: February 15, May 15, August 15 or November 15.
