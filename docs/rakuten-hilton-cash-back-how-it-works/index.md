# Rakuten Hilton cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-hilton-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-26
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

No (as of November 26, 2026), and here is what works instead. Rakuten's Hilton page read "No Cash Back" that day, with no rate list, no terms and no posting time. A separate listing pays 1.25% on buying Hilton points, and the Marriott and IHG pages on Rakuten paid cash back on room bookings the same day.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you book. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## What the Hilton page showed on November 26, 2026

| Item | What the page showed |
|---|---|
| Headline | "No Cash Back" |
| "Was" label | None |
| Rate list by brand | None |
| Terms and exclusions | None |
| Posting time | None |
| Member reviews | 149, average 4.0 |

The page still exists and still describes the hotel group, so the address works. It simply carries no offer. Some of the member reviews on it mention earning at Hilton in the past. When the rate was removed, and whether it will return, is not confirmed.

Because there are no terms, the usual hotel questions have no answer here. Completed stays, prepaid or pay-at-hotel rates, award nights, taxes and resort fees: none of them earn, because nothing does. A stay booked on Hilton's site still follows Hilton's own rules for Hilton Honors points and elite credit. Starting at Rakuten adds nothing and, as far as the page shows, takes nothing away. Hilton's terms page refused the fetch on November 26, 2026, so its wording on points is not confirmed.

## The one Hilton listing that pays

Rakuten has a second page named "Hilton – Points". On November 26, 2026, it showed 1.25% cash back with no "was" label. Its terms are two sentences: cash back is only available on the purchase of points, and it is not available on hotel bookings. The posting line says cash back is added "tomorrow".

| Points purchase | At 1.25% |
|---|---|
| $100 | $1.25 |
| $400 | $5.00 |
| $1,000 | $12.50 |

These are sums on round figures, not Hilton's prices for points. The price per point is set on Hilton's side and was not read for this page. Hilton Grand Vacations has its own Rakuten page as well, and it also read "No Cash Back".

## What paid on other hotel listings the same day

The Hilton page links to other travel stores. These are the headline rates shown next to each on November 26, 2026.

| Listing | Rate shown | "Was" label |
|---|---|---|
| Marriott.com | 3% | None |
| IHG Hotels & Resorts | 12% | Was 3% |
| Hotels.com | Up to 12% | Was 1% |
| Expedia | Up to 12% | Was 3% |
| Booking.com | Up to 6% | Was 4% |
| Priceline | Up to 4% | None |

"Up to" means the top figure applies to some bookings only. Whether a Hilton hotel booked at one of the travel agencies gets the top figure is not confirmed.

Take a $600 room rate. Booked on Hilton's site from Rakuten, it earned $0 on the day checked. The same $600 at a listing paying 3% comes to $18.00, and at 12% to $72.00. That second sum only matters if the hotel is not a Hilton, or if the booking moves to a travel agency. A booking at a travel agency is a third-party booking, and hotel chains usually treat those differently for points and status. [Rakuten for hotels and flights](/rakuten-hotels-flights-cash-back/) lays out that trade, and the pages on [Marriott](/rakuten-marriott-cash-back-how-it-works/) and [IHG](/rakuten-ihg-cash-back-how-it-works/) give each chain's exclusions.

## Amex points instead of cash

Rakuten members with an eligible American Express card can take Membership Rewards points in place of cash; see [Amex points instead of cash](/rakuten-amex-points-instead-of-cash/). That choice changes the form of the payout, not the rate. On a Hilton room booking, 0% in cash is 0 points. On a $400 points purchase, $5.00 becomes 500 points at 1 point per cent.

## Who should skip it

- You are booking a Hilton room and expect cash back from Rakuten. On November 26, 2026, there was none.
- You were only buying Hilton points for the cash back. At 1.25%, a $400 purchase returns $5.00.
- You want Hilton Honors points and elite credit and are thinking of a travel agency listing for the higher rate. Read the chain's rules on third-party bookings first.
- You are counting on the rate coming back by a certain date. Nothing on the page says it will.

## Quick answers

**Does Rakuten pay cash back at Hilton?**
Not on November 26, 2026. The Hilton page read "No Cash Back" and showed no terms.

**Does buying Hilton points earn cash back?**
Yes. The "Hilton – Points" page showed 1.25% that day, on points purchases only.

**Do Hilton award nights earn through Rakuten?**
No. No Hilton room booking earned on the day checked, paid or award.
