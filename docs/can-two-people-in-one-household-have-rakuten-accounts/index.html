Can two people in the same household each have a Rakuten account, and can one refer the other?

Facts checked November 27, 20264 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

Rakuten's terms limit each person to one account; they set no limit per household, so two adults at one address can each have their own. The referral terms describe referring friends and family and do not name household members as barred, but they ban self-referral and say accounts that share a name, address or email may be flagged.

What the terms say about accounts

  • One per person. The terms say each person is limited to one account. The limit is on the person, not the address.
  • Each member must qualify alone. At least 18, living in the 50 United States or Washington D.C., with an email address and password of their own.
  • PayPal cannot be shared. One PayPal email address cannot be connected to more than one Rakuten account. Two people who both want PayPal need two PayPal accounts.
  • Checks go to a name. A check is issued to the name and address in that member's Account Settings.

What the referral terms say

Rakuten's program terms describe the program as a bonus for referring friends and family, and its FAQ says members can invite friends, family members and coworkers. The person referred must:

  1. Be a real person and new to Rakuten.
  2. Join through the referrer's link, or enter the referrer's Rakuten email on the join form.
  3. Make purchases that earn cash back within 90 days of signing up. Amazon.com purchases do not count, other than music.amazon.com.
  4. Be the individual who pays for those purchases.

The offer on Rakuten's referral page, as read on November 27, 2026, asks for qualifying purchases totaling at least $50 within 90 days, for sign-ups from October 1 to December 31, 2026. Amounts and requirements vary and change; the details are on how the sign-up and referral bonuses work.

Where a shared home can cause trouble

The program terms list what is not allowed, including self-referral, making purchases on behalf of another person, buying under an account that is not yours, and creating fake accounts. They then say several accounts with matching details "may be flagged as fraudulent referrals."

Flagged is not the same as banned. Two real people at one address share an address by definition, and the terms do not say that alone disqualifies a referral. What Rakuten does after a flag is at its discretion: the terms let it investigate referral activity, withhold or cancel a bonus, delay payment, take back rewards and close an account, and they say its decisions are final.

The practical reading: a referral inside a home is closest to what the terms allow when the new member is a different person, has their own email, pays for their own qualifying purchases with their own payment method, and is paid to their own name or PayPal account.

What does not work

  • A second account for yourself. That breaks the one-account rule and is self-referral.
  • Referring someone who already has an account, including an old Ebates one. The referred person must be new.
  • Buying on the new member's behalf. The terms require the new member to be the one who pays.
  • Taking both joining bonuses. A new member gets one; see welcome bonus or referral bonus.

Where Rakuten's pages disagree

The program terms and FAQ say the referral program is open to residents of the 50 states, D.C. and Canada. The current offer terms on the referral page, and the main terms, say the 50 states and D.C. only.

Who should skip it

  • A couple who shop from one login and one card. One account already earns the cash back on those orders; a second adds little beyond a one-time bonus that may be withheld.
  • Anyone who would open the second account in another person's name but run it themselves.
  • Anyone who would spend $50 they had not planned in order to reach a bonus.

If a second person does want their own account, the joining step is on how to sign up for Rakuten.

Quick answers

Can a husband and wife both have Rakuten accounts? Yes, under the terms as written. The limit is one account per person, and each needs their own email.

Can I refer my spouse to Rakuten? The terms do not bar it by name. Accounts sharing an address or payment method may be flagged, so payment is not confirmed.

Can two Rakuten accounts use the same PayPal? No. The terms say one PayPal email address cannot be connected to more than one account.
