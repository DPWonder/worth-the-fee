Rakuten Vrbo cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

The short answer

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back on Vrbo bookings. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Vrbo page showed 3% cash back, marked "was 1%". Bookings below $20, listing renewals and refer-a-friend coupon codes that start with "RAF" are excluded, and the cash back is added only after the trip has been completed.

A "was" label means the rate has been raised above the store's usual rate. The page does not say how long 3% will last. One of the listed deals is for Black Friday, but nothing on the page promises a different rate on that day.

What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Vrbo are short. They list three things that earn nothing:

Earns nothing Note
Bookings below $20 A minimum booking size
Listing renewals This is a fee owners pay, not a guest booking
Refer-a-friend coupon codes that start with "RAF" A booking made with one of these codes

The terms do not mention gift cards, taxes or the Vrbo app. Rakuten's general help page says taxes and shipping fees never earn cash back. Whether cleaning fees, service fees or a damage deposit count toward the 3% is not confirmed.

The stay has to be completed first

The posting line on the page says cash back will be added to the Rakuten account within a month after travel has been completed. Other store pages read the same day gave a posting time of a day or a week.

In practice this means:

  • A stay booked in November for July shows nothing as earned until after the July trip.
  • Rakuten's general help page says canceled orders do not earn cash back. A canceled booking would be treated the same way.
  • Whether a pending amount shows in the account between booking and travel is not confirmed.

Payment then follows Rakuten's normal schedule, every three months; see when Rakuten pays.

One Key and OneKeyCash

Rakuten's page describes One Key, the rewards program shared by Vrbo, Expedia and Hotels.com, where members earn and redeem OneKeyCash on eligible bookings. The cash back terms do not mention One Key. Whether the part of a booking paid with OneKeyCash earns cash back is not confirmed.

For the two other brands in that program, see Rakuten at Expedia and Booking.com and cash back on hotels and flights.

Coupon codes and the app

  • Coupon codes. The only code rule in the Vrbo terms is the "RAF" one. Rakuten's general help page says coupon codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.
  • App. The terms do not mention the Vrbo app. Rakuten's help page says a purchase is tracked from a click through Rakuten and should be finished in the tab that opened. Whether a booking finished in the Vrbo app tracks is not confirmed.
  • Other reward sites. The help page says visiting other reward or coupon sites during the session can void cash back.

Who should skip it

  • You are booking with a refer-a-friend code that starts with "RAF". That booking earns nothing.
  • You are a property owner paying for a listing renewal.
  • You need the money soon. Nothing is added until after the stay, and payment comes after that.
  • Your plans may change. A canceled booking does not earn.
  • You plan to book inside the Vrbo app. Tracking there is not confirmed.

If cash back did not appear a month after the trip, see what to do when cash back did not track.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Vrbo? 3% on November 25, 2026, shown as raised from 1%. Rates change daily.

When does Vrbo cash back show up? The page says within a month after travel has been completed, not at booking.

Does a Vrbo refer-a-friend code earn cash back? No. Codes that start with "RAF" are on the excluded list.
