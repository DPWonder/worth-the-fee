Rakuten DoorDash cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Facts checked November 26, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

No, not as of November 26, 2026. Rakuten's DoorDash page carries the label "No Cash Back", with no rate, no terms and no button to start a shopping trip. Food and grocery orders on DoorDash earn $0 through Rakuten; what does work is Rakuten's listings for Grubhub and Instacart, and the DoorDash benefits attached to some credit cards.

With no cash back there is no exclusion list either. The page does not separate restaurant, grocery, alcohol or DashPass purchases, because none of them earn.

Where the page disagrees with itself

The header says "No Cash Back". Further down, the page's shopping tips still say a DoorDash order through Rakuten earns cash back on top of a coupon. Member reviews on the page describe earning on DoorDash orders in the past, and one says only the driver listing is left. The reviews carry no dates. When orders stopped earning is not confirmed.

The one DoorDash listing that pays

Rakuten has a separate page called DoorDash Driver. It is for people signing up to deliver, not for people ordering.

Rakuten Dining is a different program. A card is linked to the Rakuten account and cash back is earned when that card pays at a participating restaurant, with the amount added within 3 to 5 days. Whether a delivery order placed through DoorDash at one of those restaurants counts is not confirmed.

The Instacart sum assumes the full $60 is eligible groceries; fees, tax and tip earn nothing. Menu prices and fees differ between apps, and a $1.20 rebate is smaller than many delivery fees.

What still works on DoorDash

  • Card benefits. The Chase Sapphire Preferred includes a DashPass membership if activated by December 31, 2029, plus $10 off one non-restaurant DoorDash order each month, according to Chase's terms. See the Sapphire Preferred DoorDash benefit.
  • Card rewards. A card pays its usual dining or everyday rate on a DoorDash charge with or without Rakuten. More in stacking Rakuten with card rewards.
  • DashPass. Rakuten's page gives the price as $9.99 a month, which is $119.88 over 12 months, and says it waives delivery fees on orders of at least $15 from eligible restaurants. It mentions a 30-day free trial for new subscribers. DoorDash's own app has the current price and minimum.
  • Pickup. The page says pickup orders placed through DoorDash carry no fees.

Who should skip it

  • You are opening Rakuten before a DoorDash order. There is nothing to activate, so the step adds nothing.
  • You would move an order to Grubhub only for 4%. That rate is for a first purchase; an existing customer gets 1%.
  • You order groceries in the Instacart app. Rakuten's Instacart terms exclude app orders.
  • You are not planning to deliver for DoorDash. The $15 listing is for new drivers only.

Quick answers

Does Rakuten pay cash back on DoorDash orders? No. On November 26, 2026, Rakuten's DoorDash page showed "No Cash Back" and no terms.

What is the $15 DoorDash offer on Rakuten? It is on the DoorDash Driver page. It pays after a new driver account completes its first delivery.

Which delivery services did pay that day? Grubhub showed 4% for new customers and 1% for existing ones. Instacart showed 1%.
