Does Rakuten give cash back at Old Navy, and what is excluded?

Facts checked November 22, 20264 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

Yes. On November 22, 2026, Rakuten's Old Navy page showed 2% cash back, with no raised rate. Gift cards, e-gift cards, Joseph Altuzarra items and the part of an order paid for with reward points are excluded.

The rate, and how it moves

Store Rate shown on November 22, 2026 Rakuten's "was" label
Old Navy 2% None shown
Gap 2% None shown

"Was" is Rakuten's own label for a store's usual rate, shown when the rate has been raised. Neither page showed one, so 2% reads as the usual rate at both. No category table appears on the Old Navy page, so the one rate covers all eligible items.

Old Navy and Gap are listed as separate stores on Rakuten, each with its own terms. Black Friday is November 27, 2026. Rates often move that week, and nothing on the page promises a change.

What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Old Navy list these as earning nothing:

  • Joseph Altuzarra items
  • Gift cards and e-gift cards
  • The portion of an order paid for with reward points

The page disagrees with itself in one place. Its FAQ gives a different list: Banana Republic Factory orders, Gap Factory orders, gift cards, e-gift cards and the portion paid with reward points. The cautious reading is to treat everything on both lists as excluded.

A Rakuten help page on gift cards also names Old Navy among stores that do not pay cash back on the part of an order bought with a gift card.

Coupon codes, Super Cash and pickup

  • Coupon codes. The Old Navy terms list no code rule. The page's FAQ says Old Navy promo codes can be used while still earning 2%. Rakuten's general page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. Gap's terms, by contrast, say such codes will void it.
  • Super Cash. The page describes Super Cash as the store's own coupon program, $10 for every $25 spent during earning periods. The terms do not say whether redeeming Super Cash reduces the amount that earns. Not confirmed.
  • Pickup. The page describes free in-store pickup for online orders but ties no cash back rule to it. Not confirmed.
  • App. The terms do not mention the Old Navy app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
  • Returns. No store rule is listed. Rakuten's general help page says returned and canceled items lose their cash back.

Taxes and shipping do not count. At 2%, a store sale or code moves the total far more than the cash back does.

In stores

Old Navy did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 22, 2026. The list was cut off after 21 stores, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.

When you get the money

The page says cash back is added to the Rakuten account the next day, as pending. Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See when Rakuten pays and what to do when cash back did not track. Card rewards are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • You pay mostly with reward points or gift cards. That part earns nothing.
  • You are buying gift cards.
  • You shop in the store rather than online.
  • You have a code from outside Rakuten and do not want to risk the cash back on it.
  • Your orders are small. On a $25 order, 2% is 50 cents.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Old Navy? 2% on November 22, 2026, with no "was" rate shown. Rates change daily.

Is Gap's rate the same? On November 22, 2026, yes: Gap's own page also showed 2%. The two stores have separate terms.

Do orders paid with reward points earn cash back? No. The terms exclude the portion of an order paid for with reward points.
