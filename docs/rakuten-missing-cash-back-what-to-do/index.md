# Rakuten missing cash back or cash back not showing up: causes and how to submit a claim

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Rakuten cash back usually goes missing because the click from Rakuten to the store was lost, or because the item or order was excluded. If it is still not showing up after 48 hours and the store's terms say you should have earned it, submit Rakuten's Missing Cash Back form; Rakuten's terms say to report it within 90 days of the purchase.

This page is information, not financial advice. Every figure here was re-read on Rakuten's pages on November 25, 2026.

## First, give it two days

Rakuten says a new order shows as "processing" while it waits for the store, which typically takes 24 to 48 hours. Travel is slower: Rakuten says a booking may not show as pending until the trip is over, except at Expedia, where it shows within 5 days of the reservation. The usual waits by type of purchase are on [how long a purchase takes to show up](/rakuten-how-long-until-purchase-shows-up/).

| Status in your account | What Rakuten says it means |
|---|---|
| Processing | Waiting for the store to send the order details; typically 24 to 48 hours |
| Pending | The store reported the order; a wait for returns and changes follows |
| Confirmed | Added to your confirmed balance and included in the next payment |
| Ineligible | The order does not qualify: a return, an exclusion, a coupon not found on Rakuten |

Rakuten's pages give two figures for the pending wait: 3 to 14 weeks on the payment page and 3 to 17 weeks on the status page.

## Which problem do you have?

Rakuten separates two cases.

| What you see in your account | What it means | Usual cause |
|---|---|---|
| No shopping trip at all | Rakuten has no record that you clicked through | The tracking cookie was blocked or never placed |
| A shopping trip, but $0 or "ineligible" | The click was recorded; the order did not qualify | An exclusion, a return or a cancellation |

## The causes Rakuten lists

**The click was lost**

- An ad blocker, VPN or cookie blocker was on, or cookies were declined.
- Another rewards or coupon site or extension took over the click.
- You checked out in a different tab, or after the session sat idle too long.
- You activated cash back after items were already in the cart. This is covered in [forgot to activate Rakuten](/rakuten-forgot-to-activate-cash-back/).
- The order was placed by phone, chat or customer service.
- You paid with a button such as PayPal, Apple Pay, Shop Pay, Google Pay or Samsung Pay. Rakuten's blog says these open a new window or app that may interrupt tracking.
- You placed a second order without starting a new shopping trip. The blog says each order needs its own.

**The order did not qualify**

- The item, category or brand is excluded at that store.
- You used a coupon code that Rakuten did not list.
- The order was returned or canceled. A partial return is adjusted.
- It was a gift card purchase. Most are excluded, and some stores exclude orders paid with a gift card.
- For travel, the booking was changed after it was made. Rakuten says any change voids the cash back.

A preorder earns when the item ships, not when it is ordered, and an order that ships in parts may show as separate amounts, according to Rakuten's blog.

Taxes and shipping never earn cash back, and a category may pay less than the headline "up to" rate, so a smaller amount is not always an error.

## How to submit a claim, step by step

1. Wait 48 hours after the order. For travel, wait until after the trip unless you booked at Expedia.
2. Open the store's page on Rakuten and read "See all Terms & Exclusions". If your item or payment type is excluded there, there is nothing to claim.
3. Check your Rakuten account for a shopping trip on that date. In the iOS and Android apps the page is called Shopping Trips, and Rakuten says it lists every place you activated cash back, whether or not you bought.
4. Gather the order confirmation email. Rakuten's blog lists what to keep: retailer name, order number, date, items purchased and total amount, plus the full itinerary for travel.
5. Submit the Missing Cash Back form at rakuten.com/help/missing-cash-back, signed in to your account.

What the form asks for and how long a review takes are not confirmed; the form sits behind sign-in. For a purchase in a physical store, Rakuten's in-store FAQ points to its contact page instead and says Member Services uses the receipt.

## The claim window

| Source | Deadline to report missing cash back |
|---|---|
| Rakuten's terms and conditions (section 10.4) | Within 90 days of the transaction |
| Rakuten's help articles | No deadline stated |
| Best Buy's listing on Rakuten | Within 30 days from the order date |

A store can set a shorter window than the general one, so read the store's special terms. Submit soon after the 48 hours, and treat 90 days as the outer limit.

This matters for travel. A stay booked four months ahead may not show as pending until after checkout, when 90 days from the booking have passed. Ask Rakuten early.

## What a claim can and cannot do

Rakuten is paid by the store. Its terms say that if a store does not pay Rakuten for a transaction, Rakuten may cancel the cash back for it, and that Rakuten decides whether a purchase qualifies. A claim asks Rakuten to check the order with the store; it is not a guarantee. Approved cash back is still paid on the usual dates; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

To prevent a repeat: start at Rakuten with an empty cart, stay in the same tab, turn off other coupon extensions, and use only codes shown on Rakuten. Rakuten also offers a filter list for people who keep an ad blocker on. These steps matter most on busy days; see [Rakuten on Black Friday](/rakuten-black-friday-cash-back-2026/).

For purchases in a physical store the causes differ. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Who should skip it

- The amount is a few cents or the item was clearly excluded. A claim will not change that.
- A coupon code from another site saved you more than the cash back would have.
- You will not turn off a VPN or ad blocker to shop. Tracking will keep failing.

## Quick answers

**How long do you have to submit missing Rakuten cash back?**
Rakuten's terms say within 90 days of the transaction. Its help pages give no deadline, and a store can set less.

**Where do you submit missing cash back on Rakuten?**
On the Missing Cash Back form at rakuten.com/help/missing-cash-back, after you sign in.

**Why is my Rakuten cash back not showing up?**
In the first 48 hours it may still be processing. After that, the click was lost or the order did not qualify.
