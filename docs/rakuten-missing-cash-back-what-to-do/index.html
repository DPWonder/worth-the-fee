Rakuten order not showing up or cash back missing: what causes it and how do you claim it?

The short answer

Cash back usually fails to track because the click from Rakuten to the store was lost, or because the item or order was excluded. If the store's terms say you should have earned it, file Rakuten's Missing Cash Back form; Rakuten's terms say to do so within 90 days of the purchase.

First, give it two days

Rakuten says a new order shows as "processing" for 24 to 48 hours while it waits for the store. Travel is slower: outside Expedia, a booking may not show as pending until the trip is over. The usual waits by type of purchase are on how long a purchase takes to show up.

Which problem do you have?

Rakuten separates two cases.

What you see in your account What it means Usual cause
No shopping trip at all Rakuten has no record that you clicked through Tracking was blocked or the click was replaced
A shopping trip, but $0 or "ineligible" The click was recorded; the order did not qualify An exclusion, a return or a cancellation

The causes Rakuten lists

The click was lost

  • An ad blocker, VPN or cookie blocker was on, or cookies were declined.
  • Another rewards or coupon extension took over the click.
  • You checked out in a different tab, or after the session sat idle too long.
  • You activated cash back after items were already in the cart.
  • The order was placed by phone or chat.
  • You paid with a button such as PayPal, Apple Pay, Shop Pay, Google Pay or Samsung Pay. Rakuten's blog says these open a new window or app that may interrupt tracking.

The order did not qualify

  • The item, category or brand is excluded at that store.
  • You used a coupon code that Rakuten did not list.
  • The order was returned, exchanged or canceled.
  • It was a gift card purchase. Most are excluded, and some stores exclude orders paid with a gift card.
  • For travel, the booking was changed after it was made.

A preorder or an order that ships in parts may not show until the item ships, according to Rakuten's blog.

Taxes and shipping never earn cash back, and a category may pay less than the headline "up to" rate, so a smaller amount is not always an error.

How to claim, step by step

  1. Wait 48 hours after the order. For travel, wait until after the trip unless you booked at Expedia.
  2. Open the store's page on Rakuten and read "See all Terms & Exclusions". If your item or payment type is excluded there, there is nothing to claim.
  3. Check your Rakuten account for a shopping trip on that date. In the iOS and Android apps the page is called Shopping Trips, and Rakuten says it lists every store where you activated cash back, whether or not you bought.
  4. Gather the order confirmation email, with the order number, date and amount before tax and shipping. Rakuten's terms say it may ask for a receipt or other documents.
  5. File the Missing Cash Back form at rakuten.com/help/missing-cash-back, signed in to your account.

What the form asks for and how long a review takes are not confirmed; the form sits behind sign-in.

The claim window

Source Deadline to report missing cash back
Rakuten's terms and conditions (section 10.4) Within 90 days of the transaction
Rakuten's help articles No deadline stated
One third-party site 45 days

Sources disagree. File soon after the 48 hours, and treat 90 days as the outer limit.

This matters for travel. A stay booked four months ahead may not show as pending until after checkout, when 90 days from the booking have passed. Ask Rakuten early.

What a claim can and cannot do

Rakuten is paid by the store. Its terms say that if a store does not pay Rakuten for a transaction, Rakuten may cancel the cash back for it. A claim asks Rakuten to check the order with the store; it is not a guarantee. Approved cash back is still paid on the usual dates; see when Rakuten pays.

To prevent a repeat: start at Rakuten with an empty cart, stay in the same tab, turn off ad blockers and other coupon extensions, and use only codes shown on Rakuten.

For purchases in a physical store the causes differ. See how in-store cash back works.

Who should skip it

  • The amount is a few cents or the item was clearly excluded. A claim will not change that.
  • A coupon code from another site saved you more than the cash back would have.
  • You will not turn off a VPN or ad blocker to shop. Tracking will keep failing.

Quick answers

How long do you have to claim missing Rakuten cash back? Rakuten's terms say within 90 days of the transaction. Its help pages give no deadline.

Where is the Missing Cash Back form? At rakuten.com/help/missing-cash-back, after you sign in.

Why does my order show $0 cash back? The click was recorded but the order did not qualify, usually an exclusion, return or cancellation.
