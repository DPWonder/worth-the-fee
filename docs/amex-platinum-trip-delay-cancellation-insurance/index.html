What travel insurance does the Amex Platinum include, and what does it leave out?

Facts checked November 21, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

The Amex Platinum includes trip delay cover up to $500 per trip after more than six hours, trip cancellation and interruption cover up to $10,000 per trip, and baggage cover up to $3,000 per person. It does not pay medical bills abroad or for delayed bags, and each benefit pays only after other insurance and refunds.

The limits at a glance

These figures come from Amex's Guides to Benefits. The guides do not name each card, so the match to the Platinum Card rests on Upgraded Points (updated November 11, 2026, with the $895 fee) and AwardWallet.

Benefit Limit Main condition
Trip delay $500 per trip; 2 claims per card per 12 months Delay of more than 6 hours for a listed reason
Trip cancellation or interruption $10,000 per trip; $20,000 per card per 12 months Canceled or cut short for a listed reason
Checked baggage $2,000 per person Lost, damaged or stolen on a common carrier
Carry-on baggage $3,000 per person Same
All baggage, one trip $3,000 per person; $1,000 for high-risk items Entire fare on the card

New York residents have different baggage limits.

What counts as a covered trip

For delay, cancellation and interruption, the trip must be round-trip travel by common carrier of no more than 365 days. One-way tickets that bring you back to the city of departure count. The full fare must be charged to the card, or to the card together with its points or frequent flyer awards.

Cover extends to the card member, a spouse or domestic partner, dependent children, and traveling companions whose trip is charged to the card.

Trip delay

The guide lists four causes: severe weather, terrorist action or hijacking, the carrier's documented equipment failure, and lost or stolen passports or travel documents. It pays "reasonable additional expenses" such as meals, lodging, toiletries and medication. A delay known before you left is excluded.

Trip cancellation and interruption

Covered reasons:

  • Injury, death or sickness of the traveler, a companion, or a family member of either
  • Severe weather
  • A change in military orders
  • Terrorist action or hijacking
  • Jury duty or a subpoena that cannot be postponed
  • A home made uninhabitable
  • Quarantine ordered by a physician

The benefit repays nonrefundable amounts paid to a travel supplier with the card. For an interruption, it also pays extra transport home, up to an economy fare.

Baggage

The plan pays after the carrier settles its own claim. High-risk items include jewelry, sporting equipment and electronics. Tickets paid with Membership Rewards points qualify. Tickets paid with airline miles do not. There is more on sports gear in card insurance for a golf trip.

What is not covered

  • Medical bills. Upgraded Points says treatment costs stay with the card member. The Premium Global Assist Hotline may arrange emergency medical transport at no cost when it approves and coordinates it. That is one source, not confirmed against Amex's own terms.
  • Delayed bags. The baggage guide has no baggage delay benefit.
  • Pre-existing conditions. A condition that showed symptoms or needed treatment in the 60 days before purchase.
  • Changing your mind. Only the listed reasons count.
  • Supplier insolvency.
  • One-way travel with no return.

Rental cars are a separate benefit. See rental car cover on the card.

Where sources disagree

  • One Mile at a Time says delays for any reason qualify. The guide lists four causes.
  • Upgraded Points shows $2,000 for carry-on bags in a table. The guide and AwardWallet say $3,000.
  • NerdWallet has said bookings paid with Membership Rewards points are an exception for trip insurance. The guide allows the card combined with its points.

Filing a claim

Benefit Give notice within Contact
Trip delay, cancellation, interruption 60 days; proof of loss within 180 days 844-933-0648
Baggage 30 days, per Upgraded Points 800-228-6855

Papers include tickets, the card statement, receipts, and proof of the cause.

Who should skip relying on it

  • Anyone who needs medical cover abroad.
  • Anyone booking one-way trips, or paying part of the fare with another card.
  • Anyone with prepaid costs above $10,000 per trip.
  • Anyone who wants to cancel for reasons not on the list.

This cover alone does not carry an $895 fee. It is one line in the full count of what the card returns.

Quick answers

Does the Amex Platinum cover medical bills abroad? No. Treatment costs are yours. The hotline's transport help is not confirmed against Amex's own terms.

How long must a delay be before trip delay cover applies? More than six hours, for one of four listed causes. The limit is $500 per trip.

Do award tickets qualify? For trip delay and cancellation, the guide allows frequent flyer awards combined with the card. For baggage, tickets paid with airline miles do not qualify.
