Is Ebates now Rakuten?

Facts checked November 25, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Yes. Ebates and Rakuten are the same cash back service: Rakuten bought Ebates in 2014 and the Ebates name was replaced by Rakuten in 2019. Rakuten's own pages say accounts and cash back balances were unchanged, and the old ebates.com address now forwards to rakuten.com.

What happened, by year

Year What happened Source
1999 Ebates founded Rakuten's 2014 press release
2014 Rakuten announced on September 9 that it would buy all of Ebates for $1 billion in cash; closing was planned for October Rakuten press release; Rakuten's company history lists it under October 2014
February 2019 Members with an eligible American Express card could first take Membership Rewards points in place of cash The Points Guy; Doctor of Credit
2019 The Ebates name and logo were replaced by Rakuten Rakuten's blog post of March 6, 2019; Rakuten help center

The exact day the US site switched is not confirmed. Rakuten's help center says members were moved to the new site "one group at a time", so there was no single day for everyone.

The legal name still shows the history. Rakuten's terms name the company as Ebates Performance Marketing, Inc., doing business as Rakuten Rewards.

What happened to old Ebates accounts and balances

Rakuten's announcement page says cash back and account details were unchanged, and that the full balance appears at the top of the new site. Its help center says the name change has no effect on how members use the service.

So an Ebates account did not need to be reopened under a new name. Whether an Ebates login that has not been used for years still works today is not confirmed. Two rules in Rakuten's current terms matter for a long-idle account:

  • After more than 12 months with no shopping trip, Rakuten may take a $5 monthly maintenance fee from the balance. The fee cannot take the balance below zero.
  • Rakuten may close an account that has been inactive for more than 36 months and has a zero balance.

The terms allow one account per person.

Where the old store pages are now

People still search for "Ebates" with a store name. Those pages now live on rakuten.com. In a check on November 25, 2026, old ebates.com addresses for Macy's, Walmart and eBay each forwarded to that store's page on rakuten.com. The old Hotels.com address led to a page-not-found screen.

Guides on this site for the stores people most often pair with the old name:

Who should skip it

  • You want payment soon after each purchase. Rakuten pays every three months.
  • You shop online once or twice a year. A small balance can sit below the payment minimum, and the inactivity fee can reduce it.
  • You do not want a company recording your shopping activity. That is how cash back is tracked, under either name.
  • You expect Ebates-era rates. No source confirms that rates today match those years.

Quick answers

Is Ebates the same company as Rakuten? Yes. Rakuten's help center says Ebates has been a Rakuten company since 2014. The name changed in 2019.

Did Ebates members lose their cash back in the change? No. Rakuten's announcement said cash back and account details were unchanged.

Does ebates.com still work? It forwards to rakuten.com. Rakuten suggests updating old bookmarks to the new address.
