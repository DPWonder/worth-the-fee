Rakuten Nike cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

The short answer

Yes. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's Nike page showed 4% cash back, raised from a usual 2%. New releases, select Jordans, purchases in the SNKRS app, gift cards and orders paid with gift cards are excluded.

"Was" is Rakuten's own label for the store's usual rate. The page's FAQ says rates may change daily. No category table appears, so the one rate covers all eligible items.

What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Nike list these as earning nothing:

  • New releases
  • Select Jordans
  • Purchases made in the SNKRS app, and items that nike.com/launch shows as SNKRS-only
  • Refurbished shoes
  • HyperIce products
  • Gift cards, and purchases paid for with gift cards
  • Nike Team orders and purchases on the employee site
  • Orders Nike judges to be for reselling
  • After Dark marathon tickets
  • Orders from outside the US

The terms do not define "new releases" or say which Jordans are excluded. How long a shoe counts as a new release is not confirmed.

Three terms that are stricter than usual

  • Returns void the whole order. The terms say cash back for the entire order is void if any part is returned, exchanged or canceled. At most stores only the returned item loses its cash back. Ordering two sizes and sending one back can leave the order with nothing.
  • 60 days to raise a problem. Cash back can only be researched for up to 60 days from the order date. Rakuten's general terms allow 90 days.
  • Orders over $500. These may be subject to review and reversal.

App, pickup and coupon codes

  • App. Only the SNKRS app is named. The terms do not mention the main Nike app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension, so an order started directly in a store app is not expected to track.
  • Pickup. The terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
  • Coupon codes. The terms list no code rule for Nike. Rakuten's general page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.
  • Member pricing. Whether Nike member discounts affect cash back is not confirmed.

The same shoe is often sold by other retailers on Rakuten, each with its own rate and exclusions. Foot Locker showed 6% (was 3%) on the same page that day. A higher rate helps only if the price is the same.

In stores

Nike did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 21, 2026. The list was cut off, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.

When you get the money

Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See when Rakuten pays and what to do when cash back did not track. Card rewards are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • You buy launch-day shoes or limited Jordans. Those are excluded.
  • You buy through the SNKRS app.
  • You order several sizes and return what does not fit. One return voids the whole order.
  • You pay with Nike gift cards.
  • You buy for a team or to resell.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Nike? 4% on November 21, 2026, against a usual 2%. Rates change daily.

Do new releases and Jordans earn cash back? New releases and select Jordans are excluded. The terms do not list which ones.

What happens if I return one item? The terms say cash back for the entire order is void if any part is returned, exchanged or canceled.
