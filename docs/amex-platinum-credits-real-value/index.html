What are the Amex Platinum credits really worth if you only count the ones you would use anyway?

Facts checked November 22, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

The 11 main yearly credits on the Amex Platinum add up to $2,994 on paper, against an annual fee of $895. What they are worth to you is smaller: only the credits for things you would have bought anyway count, and for many people that is a few hundred dollars to about $1,300.

Three notes on this list:

  • The Saks Fifth Avenue credit ended. Its last day was June 30, 2026.
  • From January 1, 2027, Fine Hotels + Resorts bookings need a two-night minimum to earn the hotel credit. The Hotel Collection and Airbnb Luxe already need two nights.

Review sites quote different totals, from about $3,324 to $3,574, because they count different credits. The first 11 rows above sum to $2,994.

Why the paper value overstates it

A credit is only worth its face value if you would buy the thing anyway, in the pieces the credit comes in, and you remember to enroll and use each piece. $100 a quarter at Resy restaurants is four separate dinners, not one $400 dinner. A $219 CLEAR+ credit is worth $0 to someone who would never pay for CLEAR+.

A simple way to add up your own number

Go down the table and give each credit one of three scores:

Your answer Count it as
I already pay for this, in these amounts Full value
I would use it sometimes, or pay less for it on my own Half value
I would not buy this without the credit $0

Add the column. Then subtract $895. Above zero, the credits alone cover the fee. Below zero, the gap is what you pay for lounges, status and travel protections.

Who should not count on the credits

  • People who dislike tracking. Six of these credits come in monthly, quarterly or half-year pieces.
  • People who rarely stay in higher-end hotels. The hotel credit only works at hotels in Amex's two programs.
  • People who would shop at lululemon, join Equinox or buy an Oura ring only because of the credit.
  • Authorized users expecting their own credits. An additional Platinum card costs $195 a year and has no second set of credits.

Quick answers

What is the total value of the Amex Platinum credits? The 11 main yearly credits total $2,994 on paper. Sites that add the SoulCycle and Global Entry credits quote totals up to about $3,574.

Do unused credits roll over? No. A monthly, quarterly or half-year piece you do not use is gone.

Is the Saks credit still on the card? No. It ended after June 30, 2026.
