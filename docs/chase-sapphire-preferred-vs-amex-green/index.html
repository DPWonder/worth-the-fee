Chase Sapphire Preferred or Amex Green: which mid-fee travel card fits?

Facts checked November 21, 20265 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

For a new applicant the choice is made already: Amex stopped taking applications for the Green Card in July 2026, and the $95 Sapphire Preferred is open. The Green Card, now named the Classic Green Card, still exists at $150 for current holders and as a downgrade from an Amex Gold or Platinum card, and it earns more on travel booked direct.

What happened to the Green Card

  • Amex told cardholders in June 2026 that the card would be renamed the Classic Green Card on August 20, 2026, according to a U.S. News report.
  • The application page came off Amex's site in late July 2026. An earlier fact check on this site recorded July 23, from Upgraded Points.
  • Upgraded Points, updated October 28, 2026, says the card is closed to new applicants and can be reached only by product change from a Gold or Platinum card.
  • Amex has not said what replaces it. Reports of a new Green card with a fee of $195 to $250 are rumor and not confirmed. No launch was found as of November 21, 2026.

The fee sum

  • Preferred: $95. One hotel stay of $100 or more booked through Chase Travel returns $100, which puts the card at minus $5 for the year.
  • Classic Green: $150. CLEAR+ costs $219 a year, and the credit covers it. For a reader who would pay for CLEAR+ anyway, $150 minus $219 is minus $69. For a reader who would not, the credit is worth nothing and the fee stays $150.

The gap between the fees is $55.

The points sum

Take $5,000 a year of flights and hotels booked direct, and $4,000 of dining.

Sapphire Preferred Classic Green
Travel booked direct, $5,000 10,000 points 15,000 points
Dining, $4,000 12,000 points 12,000 points
Total 22,000 points 27,000 points

The Green earns 5,000 more points on that pattern. If the same travel were booked through Chase Travel, the Preferred would earn 25,000 on it and come out ahead. Add $3,000 of gas and streaming, and the Preferred earns 9,000 points on spending where no Green bonus is listed.

The Points Guy valued an Amex point at 2.0 cents and a Chase point at 2.05 cents in November 2026, so the two are close; the page on Amex points against Chase points explains where they differ.

Which pattern favors which card

  • New to both issuers: Preferred. It is the one that can be applied for.
  • Holds an Amex Platinum or Gold and wants a lower fee: Classic Green is one way down. It keeps Membership Rewards points alive at $150 a year. See Platinum downgrade options.
  • Pays for CLEAR+ and books travel direct: Classic Green. The credit covers the fee and travel earns 3x.
  • Rents cars or wants trip cancellation cover: Preferred. Its rental car insurance is primary.
  • Wants Hyatt, United or Southwest: Preferred. Amex points do not move to those programs.
  • Wants Delta: Classic Green. Chase points do not move to Delta.

A reader weighing an Amex card against the Preferred who cannot get the Green may want the Amex Gold comparison instead.

Who should skip it

  • Skip the Classic Green if you do not use CLEAR+. Without that credit it costs $55 more than the Preferred and has thinner travel cover.
  • Skip waiting for a new Green card as a plan. Its terms and date are not confirmed.
  • Skip the Preferred if most of your travel spending is booked direct and you want Amex partners such as Delta.
  • Skip both if you carry a balance. Interest costs more than either card returns.

Welcome offers vary by applicant and change. A product change to the Classic Green generally does not come with a welcome offer.

Quick answers

Can you still apply for the Amex Green Card? No. Applications closed in July 2026. It is available only by product change from an Amex Gold or Platinum card, according to Upgraded Points.

What is the Amex Green Card's annual fee now? $150, under the name Classic Green Card. A higher fee on a future replacement is rumor only.

Which card earns more on travel? The Classic Green earns 3x on travel booked direct against the Preferred's 2x. The Preferred earns 5x through Chase Travel.
