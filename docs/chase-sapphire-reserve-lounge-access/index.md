# Which airport lounges does the Sapphire Reserve get you into, and what are the guest rules?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/chase-sapphire-reserve-lounge-access/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

The Sapphire Reserve gets you into Chase's own Sapphire Lounges at eight US airports, a partner lounge at Washington Dulles, more than 1,300 Priority Pass lounges worldwide, and some Air Canada lounges. You can bring two guests free to Sapphire and Priority Pass lounges; each extra guest is $27 a visit. Priority Pass airport restaurants are not included.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The other lounges

- **Priority Pass:** more than 1,300 lounges worldwide. Since October 25, 2025, cardmembers are enrolled automatically. The digital card is in the Chase Mobile app under More, then Airport lounges.
- **Air Canada Maple Leaf Lounges and Air Canada Cafés:** with the Reserve card and a same-day boarding pass on a Star Alliance airline. Some lounges are excluded.

## Guest and entry rules

| Rule | Sapphire Lounges | Priority Pass lounges | Air Canada lounges |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free guests | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| Extra guests | $27 each per visit | $27 each per visit | $59 each per visit |
| What to show | Physical Reserve card and boarding pass | Digital or physical Priority Pass card and boarding pass | Reserve card and Star Alliance boarding pass |
| Authorized users | Own access | Own membership, with two guests each | Included |

Other rules on Chase's pages:

- Entry is within three hours of your scheduled departure. Several sites report that this is five hours for a connecting flight since August 2026; that detail was not confirmed on Chase's page.
- Lounges can refuse entry when full.

## What is not included

**Priority Pass restaurants.** Since July 1, 2024, Priority Pass memberships that come from Chase cards no longer include the meal credit at airport restaurants, cafés and markets. AwardWallet's September 2026 guide confirms this is still the case. Some other non-lounge experiences remain, such as certain spas, gaming lounges and the first hour at Minute Suites.

**The Sapphire Preferred.** The $95 card has no lounge access at all.

## A recent change that affects crowding

On August 15, 2026, the Sapphire Lounges left the Priority Pass and LoungeKey networks. People whose Priority Pass comes from another bank's card can no longer use them. Reserve cardholders keep full access with their card. Guides written before August still describe the old rule of one free visit a year.

## What it is worth

Count the airport visits you make in a year where a lounge in this network is in your terminal, and multiply by what you would otherwise spend there on food and drinks. Six visits at $25 each is $150. Set that beside [the credits you would really use](/chase-sapphire-reserve-credits-real-value/) and the $795 fee.

## Who should skip it

- Anyone who flies fewer than four or five times a year. See [Preferred or Reserve for a light traveler](/sapphire-preferred-vs-reserve-light-traveler/).
- Anyone whose home airport has no lounge in the network, or only one in the wrong terminal.
- Anyone who mainly wanted the Priority Pass restaurant credit. It is gone on Chase cards.
- Families of five or more. The third guest onward costs $27 each, every visit.

## Quick answers

**How many guests can I bring with a Sapphire Reserve?**
Two free at Sapphire Lounges and Priority Pass lounges. Each extra guest is $27 per visit.

**Does the Sapphire Reserve Priority Pass work at airport restaurants?**
No. Chase removed that on July 1, 2024.

**Do authorized users get lounge access?**
Yes on the personal Reserve. Each authorized user gets their own access and can bring two guests. The card costs $195 a year per authorized user.
