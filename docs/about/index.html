How this site works

Worth the Fee answers narrow questions about travel cards and travel programmes: the kind where the honest answer depends on how you travel, and is sometimes "no, skip it".

Who runs it

One person who holds the American Express Platinum Card and a Chase Sapphire card, and uses Rakuten. It is not run by a bank, a card issuer or a comparison company.

How it earns

Some pages end with a referral link. These are the ordinary refer-a-friend links that the companies give their own customers. If you sign up through one, this happens:

LinkWhat the site's owner gets
American Express Platinum CardIf you are approved, the person who runs this site earns Membership Rewards points from Amex.
Chase Sapphire cardsIf you are approved, the person who runs this site earns Chase points.
RakutenIf you qualify, the person who runs this site also gets a $50 bonus from Rakuten.

You pay nothing extra, and you can always go to the company's own site instead. A referral link never changes what a page says: several pages tell most readers not to sign up.

How facts are checked

Every number comes from the provider's own page or from a named source listed at the bottom of the page, with the date it was checked. Card offers, fees and local rules change often. Before you decide anything, read the provider's current terms.

What this site is not

It is information, not financial, tax or legal advice. It does not know your finances. Welcome offers vary from person to person, and the issuer decides who is approved.
