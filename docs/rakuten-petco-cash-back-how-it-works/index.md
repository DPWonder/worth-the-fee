# Rakuten and Petco: how the cash back works and what is excluded

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-petco-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Petco page showed 2% cash back online and 2% in stores. Grooming, training, pharmacy items, Vital Care, gift cards, donations and every repeat delivery after the first earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate, and how it moves

| Date checked | Where seen | Rate shown |
|---|---|---|
| November 21, 2026 | Rakuten's Black Friday page | 10% (was 2%) |
| November 25, 2026 | Petco store page | 2% online, 2% in-store |
| November 25, 2026 | Petco Black Friday page | 2% |

The raised rate seen on November 21 was not on the page four days later. No category table appears, so the one rate covers all eligible items. Black Friday is November 27, 2026. Nothing on the page says whether a raised rate returns.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Petco list these as earning nothing:

- Grooming services
- Training
- Pharmacy items
- Petco Vital Care
- Gift cards
- Donations
- The part of an order paid with Vital Care Rewards
- Repeat delivery subscriptions after the first delivery

Veterinary services are not named in the terms. Not confirmed. A separate Rakuten help page names Petco among stores that pay no cash back on the part of an order paid with a gift card.

The services rule differs by store. PetSmart's terms on Rakuten pay cash back on grooming; Petco's exclude it. See [Rakuten at PetSmart](/rakuten-petsmart-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Repeat Delivery

Repeat Delivery is the store's subscription for regular orders. The first delivery earns cash back and later ones do not. Rakuten's page says the first qualifying Repeat Delivery order gets 30% off; that is the store's discount and can change. [Chewy's terms](/rakuten-chewy-cash-back-how-it-works/) have a similar rule for repeat orders.

## Vital Care

Rakuten's page describes the Vital Care Core level as earning 5 Reward Dollars for every $100 spent. Two rules touch it:

- Buying Petco Vital Care itself earns no cash back.
- The part of an order paid with Vital Care Rewards earns no cash back.

Earning rewards on an order is not named as a problem.

## In stores: a linked card, not a click

Petco is one of the stores with an in-store rate on its Rakuten page. It works through a card linked to the Rakuten account and an offer added before paying. The in-store terms on the page say:

- US stores only, not locations in US territories.
- Gift card purchases do not count.
- A debit card must be run as credit, without a PIN.
- Once the offer is added and first used, further purchases count for up to 1 hour. After that it must be added again.
- An added offer that is not used expires 45 days after it is added, or when the offer ends if sooner.
- If the same store is added on more than one rewards site, only the most recently added offer pays.
- In-store offers are not valid for online purchases and cannot be combined with other offers.

Whether the online exclusions, such as grooming and training, also apply to a linked-card payment in a store is not confirmed; the in-store terms name gift cards only. Setup is covered in [how to link a card for in-store cash back](/rakuten-link-card-for-in-store-cash-back/).

## What it comes to

Prices are those shown on Rakuten's Petco page on November 25, 2026.

| Purchase | Price | At 2% |
|---|---|---|
| Purina Pro Plan dog food, 40 lb | $97.99 | $1.96 |
| Arm & Hammer cat litter, 38 lb | $30.99 | $0.62 |
| Grooming or training | Any | $0 |
| Second and later repeat deliveries | Any | $0 |

Taxes and shipping do not count. Rakuten's page says Petco ships free on orders of $49 or more, with exclusions.

## Pickup, app and returns

- **Pickup.** Rakuten's page listed a store deal for 10% off pickup orders of $50 or more, shown with the 2% rate beside it. The terms themselves do not mention pickup. Because in-store offers are "not valid for online purchases", an order placed online for pickup falls under the online offer, if it earns. Not confirmed.
- **App.** The terms do not mention the Petco app. An online purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
- **Returns.** No store rule is listed. Rakuten's general help page says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back.

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "tomorrow". That is the pending entry. Payment follows on Rakuten's four dates a year; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

## Who should skip it

- Your orders are repeat deliveries. Only the first one earns.
- You are paying for grooming, training or pharmacy items.
- You pay with Vital Care Rewards or a gift card.
- You shop at a Petco in a US territory and planned on the in-store rate.
- You have a discount elsewhere worth more than 2% on the same item.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Petco?**
2% online and 2% in stores on November 25, 2026. Rates change daily.

**Do Petco Repeat Delivery orders earn cash back?**
The first delivery does. Later deliveries do not.

**Does Petco grooming earn cash back?**
No. The terms exclude grooming services and training.
