# How does a Chase Sapphire refer-a-friend link work for the person applying?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/chase-sapphire-preferred-referral-bonus-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

A Chase Sapphire refer-a-friend link is a personal link from a cardholder that opens a standard Chase application showing a stated welcome bonus. You get that bonus if you are approved, are eligible for it and meet its spending requirement, and the cardholder who shared the link earns points from Chase.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## What happens, step by step

1. A Sapphire cardholder gets a personal link from Chase and shares it.
2. You open it. The page states the welcome bonus and its spending requirement.
3. You apply. Chase runs its normal credit check.
4. If approved, you earn the bonus by meeting the spending requirement in the stated time.
5. The cardholder who referred you earns points from Chase once you are approved.

Since May 2026 a cardholder with either Sapphire card can refer a friend to the Sapphire Preferred or the Sapphire Reserve, according to Upgraded Points. Before that, a link covered only the referrer's own card.

## What the offer was on November 20, 2026

| Route | Sapphire Preferred bonus stated | Spending requirement | Annual fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Referral page | 75,000 points | $5,000 in the first 3 months | $95 |
| Chase public page | 75,000 points | $5,000 in the first 3 months | $95 |

The two matched on that date. That is a snapshot and not a promise: Chase's own page says "Offers may vary depending on where you apply," for example online or in a branch, and can change over time. Read the figure on the page you apply from. More on the differences: [referral against public and in-branch offers](/chase-sapphire-referral-vs-public-offer/).

Unlike Amex, Chase states one figure on the page for every applicant.

## Rules that still apply to you

A referral link changes where you start the application. No source read says it changes Chase's rules, and Chase's eligibility wording is general.

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| Already have the card | Chase says the card is unavailable if you currently have it open |
| Had the card or its bonus before | The bonus may not be available. Each Sapphire card's bonus is once per lifetime |
| 5/24 | Five or more personal cards opened in 24 months, at any bank, generally means a denial |
| Credit check | Applying adds a hard inquiry whether or not you are approved |
| Spending requirement | No bonus unless you meet it in time |

Details: [Sapphire bonus eligibility](/chase-sapphire-bonus-eligibility-2026/), [the 5/24 rule](/chase-5-24-rule-explained/), [what counts toward the spending requirement](/chase-sapphire-welcome-bonus-spending-requirement/) and [what an application does to your credit score](/does-applying-for-a-travel-card-hurt-credit-score/).

Chase now shows a notice during the application if you would be approved without the bonus. The Points Guy reports you can cancel at that point.

## What the person who referred you gets

The cardholder earns bonus points from Chase for each friend approved through their link, up to a yearly cap. Sources differ on the per-friend figure, and Chase's own referral page sits behind a sign-in, so the current amount is not confirmed here.

The person sharing the link has a reason to want you to use it. You pay nothing extra, and your bonus is not reduced by theirs.

Referral points are generally treated as taxable income for the person who receives them. See [whether card bonuses are taxable](/are-credit-card-welcome-bonuses-taxable/).

## What a referral link does not do

- It does not raise your approval odds.
- It does not waive the $95 annual fee.
- It does not always carry the highest offer available that day. In mid-2026 referral links showed a higher bonus than the public page for about a week; in 2021 they showed a lower one for several weeks, as reported by Doctor of Credit and Frequent Miler.

## Who should skip it

- Anyone who has earned the bonus on the same Sapphire card before.
- Anyone at or over 5/24.
- Anyone who would need to spend more than usual to reach $5,000 in three months, or who would carry a balance to do it.
- Anyone about to apply for a mortgage or car loan, where a new inquiry and account can matter.

For whether the card itself fits, see [Sapphire Preferred on one trip a year](/chase-sapphire-preferred-one-trip-a-year/).

## Quick answers

**Is the bonus through a Sapphire referral link the same as on Chase's website?**
On November 20, 2026 both showed 75,000 points after $5,000 in three months. They have differed at times, so compare on the day.

**Does using a referral link cost me anything?**
No. The cardholder's points come from Chase, not from your bonus.

**Can I use a referral link if I had a Sapphire card before?**
You can apply, but the bonus may not be available if you held that card or earned its bonus before.
