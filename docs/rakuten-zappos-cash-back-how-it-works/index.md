# Rakuten Zappos cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-zappos-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Zappos. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Zappos page showed 2% cash back, with no raised rate. Birkenstock and VRSNL purchases, gift cards, orders where a gift card is used to pay and Final Sale items more than 50% off earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate

| Date checked | Rate shown | Rakuten's "was" label |
|---|---|---|
| November 25, 2026 | 2% | None |

A "was" label appears when a rate has been raised above the store's base rate. The Zappos page has none, so 2% is the plain rate. No category table appears.

## What earns and what does not

| Purchase | Earns cash back? |
|---|---|
| Shoes, clothing and bags not on the excluded list | Yes, 2% on the day checked |
| Sale items that are not Final Sale | Not listed as excluded |
| Final Sale items more than 50% off | No |
| Birkenstock | No |
| VRSNL | No |
| Gift cards | No |
| Orders where a gift card is used to pay | No |
| Orders deemed for reselling purposes | No |
| Orders using codes related to "ZAW" or "EMPLOYEE" | No |

Some notes on that list:

- **Gift card payments.** The wording covers the order, not only the part paid by gift card. Read plainly, a small gift card balance applied to a large order would cost the cash back on all of it. Whether Zappos applies it that way is not confirmed.
- **Final Sale.** The exclusion is for Final Sale items discounted by more than 50%. A Final Sale item at a smaller discount is not named.
- **Reselling.** The terms do not say how an order is deemed to be for reselling. Not confirmed.
- **ZAW.** The terms do not explain the term. Not confirmed.

## Discounts and VIP points lower the base

The terms say that if Zappos VIP points or other discounts are applied, the subtotal after the discount is used to figure cash back. Rakuten's page describes Zappos VIP as a free program paying one point per dollar, with a $1 coupon for every 100 points.

| Order | Discount applied | Amount that counts | At 2% |
|---|---|---|---|
| $160 pair of boots | None | $160.00 | $3.20 |
| $160 pair of boots | 10% status discount | $144.00 | $2.88 |
| $160 pair of boots | $10 in VIP rewards | $150.00 | $3.00 |

The 10% line uses the student, teacher and military discount described on Rakuten's page, which is verified through SheerID. The terms treat discounts as lowering the base, and do not say that this discount voids cash back.

## Coupon codes

The terms say using coupon codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. Rakuten's page also says Zappos does not offer general promo codes, only individual ones, and no code was listed on November 25, 2026. The offers shown were sale links, including up to 50% off selected brands.

## Pickup, app and stores

- **App.** Rakuten's page says the Zappos app offers free expedited shipping. The cash back terms do not mention the app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension. Whether an order finished in the Zappos app tracks is not confirmed.
- **Pickup.** Not mentioned on the page.
- **In stores.** Zappos is listed as an online store, and the page showed an online rate only. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/) for stores that have an offer.
- **Shipping.** The page says Zappos ships all orders free by standard shipping. Whether taxes count toward cash back is not stated in the Zappos terms.

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "tomorrow". Rakuten's help page says online cash back typically takes 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, and that a full or partial return can void it. Payment follows on Rakuten's schedule; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). If a purchase never appears, see [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## Who should skip it

- You are buying Birkenstock or VRSNL. Those brands earn nothing.
- You plan to pay with a Zappos gift card, even in part.
- The item is Final Sale at more than 50% off.
- You order many pairs to try and send most back. Returned items lose their cash back.
- You are buying in quantity to resell.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Zappos?**
2% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

**Do Birkenstock sandals earn cash back at Zappos?**
No. Birkenstock purchases are on the excluded list, along with VRSNL.

**Does paying with a Zappos gift card affect cash back?**
Yes. The terms exclude orders where a customer uses a gift card to make a purchase.
