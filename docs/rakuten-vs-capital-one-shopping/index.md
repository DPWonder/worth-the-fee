# Rakuten or Capital One Shopping: how do they differ, and can you use both?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-vs-capital-one-shopping/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Rakuten pays cash back by check or PayPal (or as points) four times a year, while Capital One Shopping pays Shopping Rewards that you redeem for e-gift cards. You can hold both accounts, but only one of them can track a given purchase, and both companies say to turn off other rewards extensions.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The main differences

| | Rakuten | Capital One Shopping |
|---|---|---|
| Cost to join | Free | Free |
| Card or bank account needed | No | No; a Capital One account may be asked for to verify a redemption |
| What you earn | Cash back, or Amex or Bilt points | Shopping Rewards, which have no cash value |
| How you are paid | Check, PayPal or points on four set dates | E-gift cards from select stores, on request |
| Minimum | About $5 confirmed | Not confirmed |
| Typical wait | 3 to 14 weeks to confirm, then the next payment date | 30 to 90 days to post, by two travel sites' accounts |
| Extra tools | Coupons, in-store offers | Coupon search, price comparison, price-drop alerts |

Capital One Shopping's rewards are separate from any Capital One card rewards.

## How each one pays

Rakuten sends confirmed cash back on February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). Members with an eligible American Express card can take Membership Rewards points in place of cash; see [Amex points instead of cash](/rakuten-amex-points-instead-of-cash/).

Capital One Shopping's help page describes its rewards this way: they are "not equivalent to money and do not have a cash value." You spend them on e-gift cards on its redemption page. The cards arrive by email within 24 to 72 business hours and cannot be refunded or exchanged. The help page lists no PayPal or bank payout. AwardWallet reports that the rewards do not expire while the account is open and in good standing.

So the question is partly what form of payment is useful to you. A gift card to a store you already use is close to cash. A gift card to a store you do not use is not.

## Which pays more?

Neither does at every store. Rates at both change often, and both personalize them. Rakuten's terms say the rate one member sees may differ from another's. AwardWallet's writer saw one store listed at up to 1% on Capital One Shopping when logged out and up to 8% in a logged-in promotion.

The only reliable method is to compare the two on the day you buy, for the exact store.

## Can you use both?

You can have both accounts. You cannot be credited by both for one order.

- Rakuten's help center says that clicking a link from another coupon or rewards site after you activate Rakuten will disable its cash back. It asks members to disable or uninstall competing extensions.
- Capital One Shopping's help page says to disable other shopping extensions before activating. It adds that if another program interferes, it cannot confirm the purchase or adjust rewards afterward.

In affiliate tracking a store generally credits one referrer per order. Exactly which of these two extensions wins when both are active is not confirmed, so running both at checkout risks earning from neither.

A practical way to hold both:

1. Compare the two rates before you shop.
2. Activate only the one you chose, before adding items to the cart.
3. Leave the other switched off in your browser until the order is placed.

## Data

Both extensions watch your shopping activity. Capital One Shopping says its extension monitors products you view and buy and promo codes that worked, and shares price updates and working codes with other users. For Rakuten, see [what its extension collects](/rakuten-browser-extension-safe-privacy/).

## Who should skip it

- Skip Capital One Shopping if you want money in a bank account. Its rewards are redeemed as gift cards.
- Skip Rakuten if you want to redeem a small balance right away. It pays on four fixed dates.
- Skip running both extensions together. One order, one tracker.
- Skip both if you do not want a browser extension recording which products you look at.

## Quick answers

**Does Capital One Shopping pay cash?**
No. Its help page says rewards have no cash value and are redeemed for e-gift cards.

**Do you need a Capital One card to use Capital One Shopping?**
No. Its help page says no Capital One bank account or card is needed.

**Can one purchase earn from both Rakuten and Capital One Shopping?**
No. Both companies say another rewards extension can stop their tracking.
