# Rakuten Neiman Marcus cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-neiman-marcus-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Neiman Marcus. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Neiman Marcus page showed 15% cash back, marked "was 2%". Jewelry, watches, Prada, gift cards, phone orders and orders placed through the Neiman Marcus app earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate seen on November 25, 2026

| Rate | Category on Rakuten's list |
|---|---|
| 15% | Beauty |
| 15% | All other non-excluded items |
| 0% | Excluded items and orders (see below) |

The "was 2%" label means 2% is the base rate and 15% is a raised rate. The page gives no end date for it, so it can drop back at any time.

Rakuten's page gives one more figure: the average cash back earned per Neiman Marcus shopper was $126.19 as of the end of 2025. It does not say over what period.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Neiman Marcus list these as earning nothing:

- Gift cards, bought or redeemed
- Jewelry
- Watches
- Prada, the one brand named
- Phone orders
- Employee orders
- Orders placed by a style adviser or customer service
- Orders placed through the Neiman Marcus app

The terms do not define "jewelry". Whether fashion jewelry is treated differently from fine jewelry is not confirmed.

## Coupon codes: the firm wording

The terms say that using coupon or promo codes not listed on Rakuten "will void" cash back. Many store pages say "may void"; this one is firmer.

The page does not fully agree with itself. Its summary section says Neiman Marcus coupons can be combined with cash back. Its question-and-answer section says outside codes can void it. The terms are the stricter reading: one code from outside Rakuten is enough to lose the 15%.

## InCircle points

InCircle is the store's rewards program. Rakuten's page describes it as 2 base points per eligible dollar, and says InCircle points and cash back can be earned on the same eligible purchase. The terms name gift card redemption as excluded. Whether paying with an InCircle Point Card counts as gift card redemption is not confirmed.

## Online and in-store do not combine

The terms say online offers are not valid in store or in combination with In-Store Cash Back. The 15% is for orders on the website after a Rakuten click. Neiman Marcus did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so whether an in-store offer exists is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

| Purchase | Price | At 15% | At the 2% base |

| Beauty order | $200 | $30.00 | $4.00 |

Taxes and shipping are not mentioned in the Neiman Marcus terms.

## Returns, and when it shows up

Rakuten's page says most items can be returned within 30 days of receipt, and that final sale and personalized items cannot be returned. What happens to cash back on a returned item is not stated on this page.

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "in a few days", after the order is confirmed and out of pending status. The page says payment is every three months; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/). For another department store's terms, see [Rakuten at Nordstrom](/rakuten-nordstrom-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Who should skip it

- You are buying jewelry, a watch, Prada or a gift card. They earn nothing.
- You order by phone, through a style adviser or in the Neiman Marcus app.
- You have a promo code from the store's emails that is not on Rakuten. Using it voids cash back.
- You shop in a Neiman Marcus store. The online rate does not apply there.
- You are counting on 15% next week. The base rate is 2%, and the raised rate has no stated end date.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Neiman Marcus?**
15% on November 25, 2026, raised from a 2% base. Rates change daily.

**Does jewelry earn cash back at Neiman Marcus?**
No. Jewelry, watches, gift cards and Prada are excluded in the terms.

**Can you use a Neiman Marcus promo code and still get cash back?**
Only a code listed on Rakuten. The terms say other codes will void cash back.
