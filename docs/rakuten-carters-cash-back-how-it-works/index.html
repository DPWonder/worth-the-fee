The short answer

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Carter's. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Carter's page showed 8% cash back, marked "was 2%". Buying or redeeming gift cards earns nothing, the offer covers the Carter's US site only, and the terms say a coupon code not listed on Rakuten will void cash back.

Coupon codes: the firm wording

Many store pages say outside codes "may void" cash back. The Carter's terms say using codes not listed on Rakuten "will void" it.

That matters here, because the same page describes several Carter's coupons that come from the store, not from Rakuten:

  • 20% off a first purchase for joining the email list
  • 25% off for referring a friend
  • A Friends and Family sale at 25% off

The page does not say whether these arrive as codes typed at checkout. If they do, the terms count them as codes not listed on Rakuten. On November 25, 2026, the offers on Rakuten's Carter's page were sale links, with no code shown: a Black Friday sale of 60% to 70% off almost everything, pajamas from $6 and free shipping on all orders.

Rewarding Moments

Rakuten's page describes the store's loyalty program: one point per dollar, and $10 in rewards at 100 points. The cash back terms do not mention it. Whether the part of an order paid with those rewards earns is not confirmed.

Pickup, app and stores

  • Pickup. Not mentioned in the terms. Not confirmed.
  • App. The page says to check the Carter's mobile app for coupons. The terms do not mention the app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension, and a coupon from the store's app may be a code not listed on Rakuten.
  • In stores. The Carter's page showed an online rate only. Carter's did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.
  • Shipping. The page gives the store's free shipping threshold as $50 in one place and $35 in another. Whether shipping and taxes count toward cash back is not stated in the Carter's terms.
  • Price adjustments. The page says Carter's refunds the difference if an item goes on sale within 14 days. How a price adjustment changes cash back is not confirmed.

When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "today". Rakuten's help page says online cash back typically takes 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, and that a full or partial return can void it. Payment follows on Rakuten's schedule; see when Rakuten pays. If a purchase never appears, see what to do when cash back did not track.

Who should skip it

  • You have a Carter's coupon code from an email, the app or a referral that takes more off than the cash back gives. Using it voids the cash back.
  • You are buying or paying with a gift card.
  • You are ordering from outside the US site.
  • You shop in a Carter's store. No in-store offer was seen.
  • You are counting on 8% later. The page marks 2% as the earlier rate and promises nothing.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Carter's? 8% on November 25, 2026, marked "was 2%". Rates change daily.

Can you use a Carter's coupon code and still get cash back? Only a code listed on Rakuten. The terms say other codes will void cash back.

Do Carter's gift cards earn cash back? No. The terms exclude both buying and redeeming gift cards.
