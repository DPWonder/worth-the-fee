# Can you upgrade a Sapphire Preferred to a Reserve or downgrade to a Freedom card, and what happens to points and the bonus?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/chase-sapphire-upgrade-downgrade-product-change/
Facts checked: 2026-11-21
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes: after a year with the card, Chase lets a Sapphire Preferred holder switch to a Sapphire Reserve, or down to a Freedom Flex or Freedom Unlimited, without a new application. A product change pays no welcome bonus on the new card, and after a downgrade to a Freedom card your points can no longer move to airline and hotel partners unless you hold another card that allows it.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The two directions

| | Upgrade to Reserve | Downgrade to a Freedom card |
|---|---|---|
| Wait | At least a year with the Preferred (Chase) | Typically 12 months (The Points Guy) |
| Other condition | Credit limit of at least $10,000 | Options vary by account |
| How | Online (24 to 48 hours), phone or branch | Phone; ask for a "product change" |
| Annual fee after | $795 | $0 |
| Welcome bonus on the new card | No | No |
| Credit check | Not stated by Chase | Generally none |
| Transfer partners | Kept; Hyatt becomes 1:1 | Lost unless another eligible card is held |

The Points Guy adds that the card number, credit line and due date typically stay the same. A product change is usually not reported as a new account, so it does not add to your [5/24 count](/chase-5-24-rule-explained/).

## Upgrading: the bonus you give up

Chase's own page says people who switch from the Preferred are not eligible for the Reserve's new-cardmember offer. That offer goes only to new accounts.

This matters more since two rule changes:

- Since the 2025 relaunch, Chase lets one person hold both Sapphire cards.
- Since January 2026, holding a Preferred does not block a first bonus on the Reserve.

So there are two routes to a Reserve. Offers vary by applicant and change.

| | Product change | New application |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome bonus | None | Possible, if you never had the Reserve's bonus |
| Hard credit check | Generally none | Yes |
| Counts toward 5/24 | Usually not | Yes |
| Preferred account | Becomes the Reserve | Stays open, $95 a year, unless changed later |

The rules on who can get a bonus are on the [Sapphire bonus eligibility page](/chase-sapphire-bonus-eligibility-2026/). Chase's wording says a bonus may not be available to someone who previously held the card, and sources differ on whether a Reserve held through an upgrade would count against a later Reserve bonus.

## Downgrading: what happens to points

The guides read treat the points as staying with the account through a product change. A Chase page saying so was not found, so that is not confirmed.

What changes is what the points can do. The Freedom cards earn the same kind of Chase points, but alone they cannot send them to airline or hotel programs. The Points Guy advises transferring or redeeming before a downgrade, unless you keep another card that can transfer.

Three ways people handle it:

1. Transfer to a partner first. Transfers cannot be reversed.
2. Move the points to another transfer-capable card of yours or a household member's. See [how combining points works](/chase-sapphire-preferred-with-freedom-cards-combine-points/).
3. Accept 1 cent a point as cash back on the Freedom card.

In Chase Travel, the Freedom cards get Points Boost of up to 1.1 cents on selected hotels, against up to 1.5 cents (some sources say 1.75) on the Preferred.

## The annual fee

Sources disagree on refunds.

- The Points Guy's August 2026 guide says Chase may prorate or refund part of the fee if you downgrade shortly after it posts.
- The Points Guy's June 2025 article says not to expect a prorated refund on a downgrade, and that Chase typically refunds the fee only if you cancel within 30 days of it posting.
- Doctor of Credit also reports the 30-day window for cancellations.

Ask the agent what will be refunded before you agree.

## Other things that change

- **The earned bonus.** A welcome bonus you already earned on the Preferred still counts as earned after a downgrade, so the card's once-per-lifetime bonus is used. Whether Chase takes back a bonus after a very early downgrade is not confirmed.

Which cards a Reserve can be downgraded to is not confirmed.

## Who should skip it

- Anyone eligible for a Reserve welcome bonus who is under 5/24. An upgrade gives that bonus up.
- Anyone with a points balance meant for Hyatt or airline transfers who is about to downgrade with no other transfer-capable card.

## Quick answers

**Do you get the Reserve welcome bonus when upgrading from the Preferred?**
No. Chase says cardmembers who switch are not eligible for the new-cardmember offer.

**Do points disappear after a downgrade to a Freedom card?**
The guides say they stay with the account, but they can no longer be transferred to partners from that card.

**How long must you hold the Preferred before changing it?**
Chase says at least a year before an upgrade. Downgrades typically need 12 months too.
