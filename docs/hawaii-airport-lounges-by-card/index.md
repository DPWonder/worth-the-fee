# Which Hawaii airport lounges can you get into with the Amex Platinum or Sapphire Reserve?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/hawaii-airport-lounges-by-card/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Both cards get you into lounges at Honolulu and Maui, mostly through Priority Pass, and neither opens a lounge at Kona or Lihue. The Amex Platinum adds the Delta Sky Club in Honolulu when you fly Delta that day.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## One table: airport by card

Lounge lists change. Each row shows what the card issuer's own pages listed on November 20, 2026, unless noted.

Neither issuer has its own lounge in Hawaii. Amex's lounge finder shows no Centurion Lounge at Honolulu or Maui, and Chase's Honolulu and Maui guides list only Priority Pass lounges.

## Honolulu in detail

**IASS Hawaii Lounge.** Terminal 2, Garden Court. This is the one lounge both cards clearly open. Upgraded Points gives Priority Pass hours of 7:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. and 2 p.m. to 6 p.m. Chase's guide says it has no food. Amex's page says "Access is not guaranteed and is subject to space availability."

**Delta Sky Club.** Platinum only, and only with a same-day Delta flight. Basic economy tickets do not qualify. Each visit uses one of the card's 10 Sky Club visits per year.

**Hawaiian Airlines Premier Club, Terminal 1.** Here the sources disagree. Amex's US lounge finder lists it for Platinum holders, up to 3 hours before departure, space permitting. A lounge directory says it rejoined Priority Pass on November 24, 2025. But Chase's Honolulu guide lists only the IASS lounge, and Upgraded Points names IASS as the one Priority Pass lounge at the airport. For Sapphire Reserve holders, access is not confirmed. Check the Priority Pass app on the day.

**Hawaiian Plumeria Lounge.** Not open to either card. It left Priority Pass on April 1, 2025. Some older guides, including one Chase page, still list it. A $40 day pass is sold at the desk.

## Maui in detail

Both cards open the two Hawaiian Airlines Premier Club lounges at Kahului through Priority Pass. One is across from gate 17 and one is opposite gate 15.

- Entry starts 2 hours before departure.
- Amex lists the South lounge for domestic flights only.
- Amex also lists an adults-only rule for the South lounge, with an exception for children under 2.
- Chase describes snacks and drinks, not meals.
- Either lounge can turn people away when full.

## Kona and Lihue

Each airport has one lounge, a Hawaiian Airlines Premier Club. A lounge site that last checked Lihue on October 28, 2026 says entry there is for Hawaiian and Alaska premium-cabin passengers and certain Atmos Rewards members, with no day pass, and that Priority Pass is not accepted.

"None found" is the honest wording. Amex's lounge finder would not load results for either airport, and no Chase page for them was found.

## Rules that apply to both cards

| Rule | Amex Platinum | Sapphire Reserve |
|---|---|---|
| Priority Pass setup | Must enroll first | Physical card or Priority Pass digital card needed |
| Guests | Two free where the lounge takes guests | Two free; more at $27 each |
| Priority Pass restaurants | Not included | Not stated in the pages read |
| When full | Entry can be refused | Entry can be refused |

## Who should skip it

Lounge access in Hawaii is thin. A traveler flying home from Kona or Lihue gets nothing from either card at the airport. A traveler flying Hawaiian from Honolulu's Terminal 1 may get nothing from the Sapphire Reserve. If lounges are the main reason for paying $795 or $895 a year, a Hawaii trip does not support it. See [how the two cards compare for Hawaii](/amex-platinum-vs-sapphire-reserve-hawaii/).

## Quick answers

**Is there a Centurion Lounge or Chase Sapphire Lounge in Hawaii?**
No Centurion Lounge appears in Amex's lounge finder for Hawaii, and Chase's Hawaii guides list only Priority Pass lounges.

**Which lounge in Honolulu takes Priority Pass?**
The IASS Hawaii Lounge in Terminal 2. Whether the Hawaiian Airlines Premier Club in Terminal 1 also does is disputed between sources.

**Can either card get me into a lounge at Kona or Lihue?**
No access was found for either card at either airport.
