Sapphire Preferred or Sapphire Reserve for someone who travels two or three times a year?

Facts checked November 20, 2026

This page has a referral link at the end. The person who runs this site earns points or cash if you sign up through it.

For two or three trips a year, the Sapphire Preferred is the safer fit for most people. The Sapphire Reserve costs $700 more each year, and a light traveler has to use several of its credits, not only the travel one, to get that back.

The Reserve's sums for a light traveler

Start with the fee gap: $795 minus $95 is $700.

The $300 travel credit is easy. It applies to travel purchases in general, and two or three trips will use it. That leaves $400 to find.

The rest of the Reserve's credits are narrower:

  • The Edit hotels: up to $250 per prepaid stay, $500 a calendar year. It needs two stays of two nights or more at hotels in Chase's own collection.
  • Dining: up to $150 in each half of the year, only at restaurants in the Sapphire Reserve Exclusive Tables list on OpenTable.
  • StubHub: up to $150 in each half of the year, through the end of 2027.
  • Lyft: $10 a month. Peloton: $10 a month. DoorDash: DashPass and monthly promos. Apple TV and Apple Music: free with activation.

If you would pay for these things anyway, they are worth close to face value. If you would be changing where you eat or stay to use them, they are worth much less. Be strict when you count.

A simple test: write down only the credits you would have spent cash on last year. If the total, on top of the $300 travel credit, does not clear $400, the Preferred costs you less.

Lounges on two or three trips

Lounge access is the Reserve benefit people picture most. Two or three round trips is roughly four to six airport visits a year. The Reserve gives you and two guests entry, and extra guests are $27 each per visit. Lounges can refuse entry when full.

On so few visits that rarely fills a $400 gap. Check that your home airport has a lounge in the network before you count it.

When the Reserve does make sense at this travel level

  • Your two or three trips are expensive ones, with hotels booked direct, so 4x matters.
  • You stay at Hyatt on points. The Reserve keeps the 1:1 Hyatt rate; the Preferred gives 3 Hyatt points for every 4.
  • You already use Lyft, DoorDash, StubHub and Apple's services every month.
  • You travel with a partner or family and would use the lounge guest allowance.

Who should get neither

  • Anyone who carries a balance month to month.
  • Anyone who has opened five or more personal cards in 24 months. Chase generally declines them for both cards.
  • Anyone who wants simple cash back with no tracking of credits.

Under Chase's current rule you can earn each Sapphire card's bonus once, and holding one does not block the other. So starting with the Preferred does not close the door on the Reserve later.

Quick answers

Is the Reserve worth $795 for three trips a year? Only if you use well over the $300 travel credit in other credits you would have paid for anyway. Many light travelers do not.

Does the Preferred get me into lounges? No. Lounge access comes with the Reserve only.

Can I have both cards? Yes. Since January 2026 Chase allows each card's bonus once per person, even if you hold the other.
