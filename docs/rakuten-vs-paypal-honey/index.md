# Rakuten or PayPal Honey: which one fits, and can you use both?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-vs-paypal-honey/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Rakuten pays cash back by check or PayPal, or as American Express points, on four set dates a year. PayPal Honey is mainly a coupon-code search that also pays PayPal Rewards points at some stores. You can hold both accounts, but both companies say only one rewards program is credited for a given purchase.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## Side by side

| | Rakuten | PayPal Honey |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free | Free |
| Main job | Cash back at stores | Tests coupon codes at checkout; points at participating stores |
| What you earn | Cash back, or Amex or Bilt points | PayPal Rewards points; 100 points = $1 at PayPal checkout |
| How you are paid | Check, PayPal, Samsung Cash or points | Points used at PayPal checkout, store e-gift cards, or a donation |
| Minimum | $5 on the help page; $5.01 in the terms | 1,000 points ($10) for a gift card; for PayPal checkout, any minimum is shown when you redeem |
| Wait | Typically 3 to 14 weeks to confirm, then the next of four payment dates | Pending in 2 to 14 days; stays pending about 60 to 90 days; then redeem on request |
| Sign-up | Email and password, or Facebook, Google or Apple | Google, PayPal or Apple |
| Do rewards expire | Idle 12 months: a $5 monthly fee may come out of the balance | Points expire unless you earn 10 points every 365 days, or meet PayPal's activity rule |

## How each one pays

**Rakuten.** Confirmed cash back is sent by February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15. Eligible American Express card members can take Membership Rewards points in place of cash. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/) and [Amex points instead of cash](/rakuten-amex-points-instead-of-cash/).

**Honey.** Honey's help center describes a free browser extension that tests coupon codes at checkout on more than 30,000 sites. Points are a second feature. Honey says they are not offered at every store it supports, and you turn them on with a button in the extension.

Points are redeemed on request, not on a schedule. Honey's and PayPal's help pages list three ways: toward a purchase at PayPal checkout, as a store e-gift card (emailed within 24 hours), or as a donation. Redeeming through PayPal needs a US personal PayPal account in good standing, linked to the Honey account by the same email address.

Sources disagree on one thing. PayPal's program terms, last updated March 17, 2025, also describe redeeming points as cash sent to a PayPal account. PayPal's current help page does not list that option, so whether cash redemption is still open is not confirmed.

## Can you use both on one purchase?

Not for rewards. Each company's own help page says so.

- Rakuten: clicking a link from another coupon or rewards site after activating Rakuten will disable its cash back. It asks members to disable or uninstall competing extensions.
- Honey: when two or more rewards programs are active, the credit goes to only one, and Honey says it has no way of knowing which. It suggests disabling other rewards programs and ad blockers.

Coupon codes are a second issue. Rakuten's help center says codes not listed on Rakuten "may void Cash Back." So a code that Honey finds can cost you the Rakuten cash back on that order. Neither company's pages say what happens when Honey only tests codes and no points are activated; that case is not confirmed.

To hold both, compare the two before you buy, activate only one for that order, and keep the other switched off until the order is placed.

## Which fits

Neither pays more at every store, and rates at both change. Rakuten suits someone who wants a check or a PayPal payment and can wait for a payment date. Honey suits someone who mostly wants a coupon code tried at checkout and already pays with PayPal. See also [Rakuten or Capital One Shopping](/rakuten-vs-capital-one-shopping/).

## Who should skip it

- Skip Honey's points if you do not use PayPal checkout and do not want store gift cards.
- Skip Rakuten if you want a small balance paid out right away. It pays on four fixed dates.
- Skip running both extensions at checkout. One order, one credit.
- Skip both if you do not want a browser extension active while you shop.

## Quick answers

**Does PayPal Honey pay cash like Rakuten?**
Honey pays PayPal Rewards points. Its help pages list PayPal checkout, gift cards and donations; cash redemption is not confirmed.

**Can one order earn from both Rakuten and Honey?**
No. Both companies say only one rewards program is credited for a purchase.

**Do you need a PayPal account to use Honey?**
Not to join; Google or Apple sign-in works. Redeeming points through PayPal needs a linked PayPal account.
