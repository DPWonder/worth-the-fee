# Can you bring guests into a Centurion Lounge with the Amex Platinum, and what does it cost?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/amex-platinum-centurion-lounge-guest-rules/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes. A Platinum cardholder can bring up to two guests into a US Centurion Lounge, but each adult guest costs $50 and each child costs $30. Guests are free only after you put $75,000 of eligible purchases on the card in a calendar year.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## Guest prices at US Centurion Lounges

| Guest | Price per visit |
|---|---|
| Adult, 18 or older | $50 |
| Child | $30 |
| Infant | Free, with proof of age |
| Additional Platinum cardholder on your account | Not a guest. Enters free on their own card |

Sources disagree on the child age band. The Points Guy says $30 applies from age 2 to 17 and under 2 is free. An October 2026 AwardWallet guide says 3 to 17, with under 3 free. If you travel with a two-year-old, ask at the desk.

The limit is two guests per visit. That means a family of four cannot all enter on one card: the cardholder plus two guests is three people.

No day pass is sold. A person without an eligible card can enter only as someone's guest.

## The same-flight rule

Since July 8, 2026, guests must be traveling on the same flight as the cardholder. AwardWallet quotes the Amex rule: "Card Member guests must be traveling on the same flight as the primary Card Member". It applies at US lounges and at the London, Tokyo Haneda and Hong Kong lounges. A friend flying another airline from the same terminal can no longer come in with you.

## Rules that apply to you and your guests

- You need the card, a government ID and a same-day confirmed boarding pass.
- Entry is allowed within 3 hours of your scheduled departure.
- On a connection, you may enter up to 5 hours before the onward flight.
- There is no entry on arrival, unless you are connecting.

Lounges can also be full. See [the occasional flyer page](/amex-platinum-worth-it-occasional-flyer/) for how the lounge network fits a light travel year.

## When guests are free

Spend $75,000 in eligible purchases on the card in a calendar year, and up to two guests per visit become free. The free access lasts for the rest of that year, all of the following year, and until January 31 of the year after that. Someone who reaches $75,000 during 2026 keeps it through January 31, 2028.

Two details are not confirmed by the sources checked: whether purchases on additional cards count toward the $75,000, and whether an additional cardholder also gets free guests once the account qualifies.

## The other route: an additional card

An additional Platinum card costs $195 a year. Its holder gets their own Centurion Lounge entry, with you or alone, and no guest fee. Compared with a $50 adult guest fee, the card costs less from the fourth visit in a year:

| Visits a year with the same adult | Guest fees | Additional card |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | $100 | $195 |
| 3 | $150 | $195 |
| 4 | $200 | $195 |
| 6 | $300 | $195 |

The additional card also brings other lounge access and hotel status, which the guest fee does not. The no-fee Companion Platinum Card does not include lounge access. Details are on [the authorized user page](/amex-platinum-authorized-user-worth-195/).

## Other lounges treat guests differently

The Centurion fee does not apply across the whole Platinum lounge list. Priority Pass lounges and Escape Lounges allow two free guests where the lounge takes guests. Delta Sky Club has its own guest price, and sources disagree on it: The Points Guy says $50, Upgraded Points says $29.

Some Centurion Lounges outside the US, such as Sydney, Mexico City and Delhi, have their own guest rules.

## Who should skip it

Do not count Centurion Lounge access as a family or couple benefit if:

- You usually travel with a partner or children and will not add them as cardholders. Each visit then costs $30 to $100 in guest fees.
- Your group is larger than three. One card cannot bring everyone in.
- Your home airport has no Centurion Lounge. There were 16 in the US in August 2026.
- Your travel companions are often on different flights.

## Quick answers

**How much is a Centurion Lounge guest with the Amex Platinum?**
$50 per adult and $30 per child at US lounges, for up to two guests per visit.

**How do you get free guests?**
Spend $75,000 in eligible purchases on the card in a calendar year. Then up to two guests per visit are free through January 31 two years later.

**Can a guest be on a different flight?**
No. Since July 8, 2026, guests must be on the same flight as the cardholder.
