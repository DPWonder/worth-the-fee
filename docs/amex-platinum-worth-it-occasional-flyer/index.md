# Is the Amex Platinum worth its annual fee if you only fly 4 to 6 times a year?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/amex-platinum-worth-it-occasional-flyer/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

For most people who fly 4 to 6 times a year, the Amex Platinum is worth its $895 annual fee only if they would already spend money on the things its credits pay for. The lounges and travel perks alone do not cover the fee at that number of flights.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## What the fee buys

The card costs $895 a year. In return it gives statement credits on set things (hotels, Resy restaurants, Uber, streaming and more), airport lounge access, and hotel status with Hilton and Marriott.

The credits add up to a large number on paper. The 11 main yearly credits total $2,994. But most are split into monthly or quarterly pieces, several need enrollment, and each one only counts if you would have bought that thing anyway.

## What 4 to 6 flights a year gets you in lounges

Lounge access depends on which airports and airlines you use.

| Lounge type | What you get | Limit that matters for an occasional flyer |
|---|---|---|
| Centurion Lounges | Entry within 3 hours of departure | Guests cost $50 each ($30 for ages 2 to 17) unless you spend $75,000 a year on the card |
| Delta Sky Club | 10 visits a year | Only on a same-day Delta flight; basic economy tickets are excluded |
| Priority Pass Select | Lounge entry, up to two guests free where allowed | Needs enrollment; Priority Pass restaurants are not included |

At 4 to 6 round trips you have at most a dozen or so chances a year to use a lounge, and only where one exists at your airports. Ten Sky Club visits are enough for a Delta flyer at that pace. If you travel with a partner or children, the $50 Centurion guest fee changes the math fast. An additional Platinum card for a second adult costs $195 a year and gives that person their own lounge access.

## The credits an occasional flyer can realistically use

These are the credits that fit a person who takes a few trips a year:

| Credit | Amount per year | Catch |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel credit | Up to $600 ($300 each half-year) | Prepaid stays at Fine Hotels + Resorts or The Hotel Collection, booked through Amex Travel; two-night minimum for The Hotel Collection now and for both programs from January 1, 2027 |
| Airline fee credit | Up to $200 | One airline you pick; fees like checked bags, not tickets |
| Uber Cash | Up to $200 | $15 a month, plus $20 extra in December |
| Digital entertainment | Up to $300 | $25 a month, enrollment required |
| Resy restaurants | Up to $400 | $100 a quarter, enrollment required |

A person who uses the full hotel credit, the airline credit, Uber Cash and the streaming credit gets $1,300 back. That covers the fee with room to spare. A person who uses only the airline credit and one $300 hotel credit gets $500 back and pays $395 net for lounge visits and status.

## Who should not get it

- You fly 4 to 6 times a year from an airport with no lounge the card opens.
- You would have to change your habits to use the credits. A credit you force yourself to spend is not savings.
- You do not want to track monthly and quarterly credits. Unused pieces do not roll over.
- You mostly want points on everyday spending. Outside flights and prepaid hotels, the card earns 1 point per dollar.
- You fly one airline and mainly want free checked bags. An airline card such as the Delta SkyMiles Gold ($0 the first year, then $150) gives a free first checked bag on Delta for far less.

## About the welcome offer

Amex advertises the welcome offer as "as high as" a number and shows each applicant their own offer before they accept the card. Offers vary by applicant, so leave it out of your math. People who have had the card before are generally not eligible.

## Quick answers

**How many flights a year make the Amex Platinum worth it?**
There is no set number. It depends on how many of the credits you would use anyway and whether your airports have lounges the card opens.

**Can I bring my family into the lounge?**
At Centurion Lounges, guests cost $50 each, or $30 for ages 2 to 17, unless you spend $75,000 a year on the card. Priority Pass and Escape lounges allow two guests free where space allows.

**Do the credits make up for the $895 fee?**
Only the ones you would have paid for anyway. Add up those and compare the total with $895.
