Do you need a credit card or bank account to join Rakuten?

Facts checked November 27, 20264 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

No. Rakuten's terms ask for an email address and a password to open an account, and no credit card, debit card or bank account. A card is asked for only if you choose to link one for in-store offers, and a mailing address is asked for before Rakuten will pay you.

The terms add that you may choose to give more information to use certain offers and services. That is a choice, not a condition of membership.

When a card does come up

The one place Rakuten asks for a card number is card linking. It is a separate, optional feature with its own terms: you enroll a card, add a store's offer, and pay in the store with that card. Rakuten's help center says most Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards and most American Express cards can be linked, so even this step does not need a credit card.

Rakuten's linked-cards article says it will never charge a linked card and will never ask for the card's CVV. The in-store terms say the card network may share the merchant name, location, date and amount of purchases at participating merchants, and that a card can be removed at any time. The steps are on linking a card for in-store cash back.

Shopping online through Rakuten does not need a linked card. You pay the store, with whatever payment method the store accepts.

What you need to get paid

The terms list four conditions for payment: an active account, a valid email address, a password and a physical address. Then you pick a method.

Method What Rakuten needs
Check The name and mailing address in Account Settings
PayPal A valid PayPal email that is not used on another Rakuten account
Bilt Points, Samsung Cash or Amex points An account with that program, linked to Rakuten

No listed method is a direct deposit, so Rakuten does not ask for bank account or routing numbers. A check is deposited at your own bank; a PayPal balance is handled under PayPal's own rules. The trade-offs are on check or PayPal.

The minimum is small but real. The terms say, "The minimum payout amount for Rewards is $5.01." The help page says $5 or more. A smaller balance carries over to the next payment date.

Does joining affect your credit

The terms and the privacy policy do not mention a credit check, a credit report or a Social Security number for membership, and the sign-up asks for nothing a credit check would need. Whether Rakuten ever asks for more to verify a member is not confirmed.

The Rakuten American Express Card is a different thing: a credit card with its own application. It is not needed for membership, and its application terms were not read for this page.

Who can join, and what is collected

Members must be at least 18 and live in the 50 United States or Washington D.C., and each person is limited to one account. Whether the join form also asks for a name or phone number is not confirmed; the help pages do not list its fields.

Not asking for a card does not mean collecting little. The privacy policy lists name, postal and email address, phone number, and payment account details among the information Rakuten may hold when you provide them, along with shopping activity. See the extension and your privacy.

Joining bonuses also need purchases, not a card on file; see how the sign-up and referral bonuses work.

Who should skip it

  • Anyone under 18 or living outside the 50 states and Washington D.C.
  • Anyone who will not give a mailing address. Rakuten's terms make it a condition of payment.
  • Anyone with neither a PayPal account nor a way to deposit a paper check, unless one of the points options fits.
  • Anyone who does not want card purchases shared with Rakuten: skip card linking and shop online only.

Quick answers

Do you need a credit card to join Rakuten? No. The terms ask for an email address and a password. A card is linked only for optional in-store offers.

Does Rakuten need your bank account? No. Payment goes by check, PayPal or a points program. No direct deposit option is listed.

Does joining Rakuten affect your credit score? Rakuten's terms and privacy policy mention no credit check for membership. Anything beyond that is not confirmed.
