Rakuten Target cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Facts checked November 24, 20264 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Target, but only on some departments. On November 24, 2026, Rakuten's Target page showed 1% cash back, limited to categories such as apparel, home, furniture, kitchen and beauty. Grocery, electronics, toys, gift cards and Drive Up orders are excluded.

Rakuten's terms for Target work as an allow list. Only the named categories earn.

The terms carry one limited-time exception: select Procter & Gamble products in otherwise excluded categories are eligible, including certain diaper, razor and floor-cleaning brands. The page showed 7.5% for these on an earlier check, November 21. On November 24 it showed no separate rate for them, so the rate is not confirmed.

Other terms on the page

  • Coupon codes. The terms say using a code not listed on Rakuten will void the cash back.
  • Free gift card promotions. If an order comes with a free Target gift card, the amount that earns is reduced by the value of that card.
  • Resale. Orders Target judges to be for reselling do not earn.
  • US site only. Orders placed through Target sites for other countries void the cash back.
  • Pickup. Drive Up orders are excluded. The terms do not mention in-store Order Pickup, so that is not confirmed.
  • App. The terms do not mention the Target app. Rakuten's general rule is that a purchase only earns if it starts from a Rakuten link, in the Rakuten app or with the extension.
  • Target Circle earnings. These appear on the excluded list. Whether that means the part of an order paid with them is not confirmed. Paying with the store's own card is a separate question; see Rakuten and the Target Circle Card.

Taxes and shipping never count, according to Rakuten. A mixed cart earns only on the eligible items.

A store coupon code worth more than 1% saves more than the cash back it voids. On a $200 order, a 5% code is $10 against $2.

How the rate moves

The page showed no usual-rate label for Target on November 24, 2026, which Rakuten adds when a rate has been raised. That suggests 1% was the standard rate that day. Rakuten's terms say rates can vary by member, product category and where the offer is shown. Whether Target's rate will be raised for Black Friday week is not confirmed.

Other big-box stores

Groceries and household goods are most of what many shoppers buy here, and they earn nothing. The other large chains are each handled differently on Rakuten: see Walmart and Costco.

In stores

Target did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 24, 2026. The list was cut off, so an in-store Target offer is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.

When you get the money

Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See when Rakuten pays and what to do when cash back did not track. Card rewards are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • Your Target orders are mostly groceries, household goods, toys or electronics. None of those earn.
  • You use Drive Up. Those orders are excluded.
  • You have a coupon code that is not on Rakuten and it is worth more than 1%.
  • You pay mainly with Target gift cards from promotions. The earning amount is reduced.
  • Your eligible spend is small. A Rakuten payment needs about $5 confirmed across all stores.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Target? 1% on listed categories when checked on November 24, 2026. Rates change daily.

Do Target groceries earn cash back? No. Grocery and household essentials are on the excluded list.

Do Drive Up orders count? No. Rakuten's terms for Target exclude Drive Up orders. In-store Order Pickup is not mentioned.
