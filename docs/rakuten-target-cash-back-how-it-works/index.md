# Rakuten Target cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-target-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-27
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Target, but only on some departments. On Black Friday, November 27, 2026, Rakuten's Target page showed 1% cash back, the same as on every check since November 24, limited to categories such as apparel, home, furniture, kitchen and beauty. Grocery, electronics, toys, gift cards and Drive Up orders are excluded.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate here and at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate, by date

| Date read | Rate on Rakuten's Target page |
|---|---|
| November 24, 2026 | 1% |
| November 25, 2026 | 1% |
| November 26, 2026 | 1% |
| November 27, 2026 (Black Friday) | 1% |

The rate was not raised for Black Friday. The page showed no "was" label, which Rakuten adds when a rate has been raised, so 1% looks like the standard rate. Rakuten's terms say rates can vary by member, product category and where the offer is shown. Whether the rate changes for Cyber Monday, November 30, is not confirmed; see [Rakuten on Black Friday](/rakuten-black-friday-cash-back-2026/) and [Rakuten on Cyber Monday](/rakuten-cyber-monday-cash-back-2026/).

## What earns and what does not

Rakuten's terms for Target work as an allow list. Only the named categories earn.

| Earns the 1% (as listed November 27, 2026) | Earns nothing |
|---|---|
| Apparel, jewelry and accessories | Grocery and household essentials |
| Baby gear | Diapers, wipes, baby food and formula |
| Furniture | Home electronics and Apple products |
| Kitchen and home decor | Small appliances |
| Beauty, personal care and bath | Toys, bicycles, sports and outdoors |
| Outdoor furniture and decor | Pets, music, books, movies, video games and consoles |
| | Pharmacy and over-the-counter items, optical, photo, cafe |
| | Prepaid cards and gift cards |
| | Target Circle 360, contract mobile |
| | Drive Up orders |

The terms carry one limited-time exception: select Procter & Gamble products in otherwise excluded categories are eligible, including certain diaper, razor and floor-cleaning brands. On November 27 the page showed a 7.5% offer on a handful of these products, such as two diaper brands and floor-cleaning kits. It showed the same 7.5% on November 21 and no separate rate on November 24, so the offer comes and goes. How long it lasts is not confirmed.

## Other terms on the page

- **Coupon codes.** The terms say using a code not listed on Rakuten will void the cash back. The deals listed on November 27 were sale links, not codes.
- **Free gift card promotions.** If an order comes with a free Target gift card, the amount that earns is reduced by the value of that card.
- **Resale.** Orders Target judges to be for reselling do not earn.
- **US site only.** Orders placed through Target sites for other countries void the cash back.
- **Pickup.** Drive Up orders are excluded. The terms do not mention in-store Order Pickup, so that is not confirmed.
- **App.** The terms do not mention the Target app. Rakuten's general rule is that a purchase only earns if it starts from a Rakuten link, in the Rakuten app or with the extension.
- **Target Circle earnings.** These appear on the excluded list. Whether that means the part of an order paid with them is not confirmed. Paying with the store's own card is a separate question; see [Rakuten and the Target Circle Card](/rakuten-target-circle-card-stack/).

## What it comes to

| Eligible spend | At 1% |
|---|---|
| $50 | $0.50 |
| $200 | $2.00 |
| $600 (a sofa) | $6.00 |

Taxes and shipping never count, according to Rakuten. A mixed cart earns only on the eligible items.

A store coupon code worth more than 1% saves more than the cash back it voids. On a $200 order, a 5% code is $10 against $2.

## Other big-box stores

Groceries and household goods are most of what many shoppers buy here, and they earn nothing. The other large chains are each handled differently on Rakuten: see [Walmart](/rakuten-walmart-cash-back-how-it-works/) and [Costco](/rakuten-costco-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## In stores

The store's page on Rakuten showed an online rate only on November 27, 2026, with no in-store offer beside it. Rakuten's full in-store offer list was not re-read that day, so an in-store Target offer is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## When you get the money

The listing says cash back is added to the Rakuten account the same day. Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/) and [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/). Card rewards are separate and come on top; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- Your Target orders are mostly groceries, household goods, toys or electronics. None of those earn.
- You use Drive Up. Those orders are excluded.
- You have a coupon code that is not on Rakuten and it is worth more than 1%.
- You pay mainly with Target gift cards from promotions. The earning amount is reduced.
- Your eligible spend is small. A Rakuten payment needs about $5 confirmed across all stores.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Target?**
1% on listed categories on Black Friday, November 27, 2026, the same as earlier in the week. Rates change daily.

**Do Target groceries earn cash back?**
No. Grocery and household essentials are on the excluded list.

**Do Drive Up orders count?**
No. Rakuten's terms for Target exclude Drive Up orders. In-store Order Pickup is not mentioned.
