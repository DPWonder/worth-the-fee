# Is the Marriott Bonvoy Brilliant Amex worth its annual fee for an occasional Marriott guest?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/marriott-bonvoy-brilliant-amex-worth-650/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

For an occasional Marriott guest, the Bonvoy Brilliant's $650 annual fee is worth it only if two things are true: you eat at restaurants every month, so the $300 dining credit is used in full, and you will use the yearly free night at a hotel that would cost you $350 or more. The elite status on its own will not earn back the fee on a few stays a year.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## What the $650 buys

| Benefit | What it is | Main limit |
|---|---|---|
| Dining credit | Up to $25 a month ($300 a year) at restaurants worldwide | Monthly; unused months are lost |
| Free night award | One a year, at a hotel costing up to 85,000 points | Issued after each renewal, so the first comes after year one |
| Platinum Elite status | Held while you have the card | Most perks depend on the hotel and on availability |
| 25 elite night credits | Added each year | Useful only if you are chasing a higher status |
| Priority Pass Select | Airport lounges, two guests | Enrollment required; restaurants are not covered |
| Property credit | Up to $100 on 2-night stays at Ritz-Carlton and St. Regis | Only on one specific paid rate |
| Global Entry or TSA PreCheck | Fee credit every four years | $120 or up to $85 |

The card earns 6 points per dollar at Marriott, 3 at restaurants and on flights booked with airlines, and 2 elsewhere.

## The sum for an occasional guest

Start with the fee and take off what you would actually use.

| Step | Amount |
|---|---|
| Annual fee | $650 |
| Dining credit, if used all 12 months | minus $300 |
| Left to cover | $350 |
| Dining credit, if used 6 months of 12 | minus $150 |
| Left to cover in that case | $500 |

The rest has to come from the free night. Sources value it differently:

- One Mile at a Time values the free night at about $350, and Marriott points at 0.7 cents each.
- The Points Guy values Marriott points at 0.75 cents (October 2026). At that rate 85,000 points come to about $637.

The value to you is simpler than either: it is the cash price of the room you would book with it. Used on a $400 night, the card comes out $50 ahead after a full dining credit. Used on a $200 night, it comes out $150 behind. You can add up to 25,000 of your own points to reach a hotel costing up to 110,000 points, and some properties charge a resort fee on a free night.

In the first year there is no free night yet. An occasional guest pays $650 in year one and gets the dining credit, status and lounge access, but not the night.

## What Platinum Elite status is worth on a few stays

One Mile at a Time lists the Platinum Elite benefits as a 50% points bonus on stays, 4 p.m. checkout (guaranteed at non-resort hotels), room upgrades when available, lounge access at most brands, and a welcome gift that includes breakfast at many brands.

These have real value per night, and they are worth more the more nights you stay. On three or four nights a year, the total is small: a few breakfasts where the brand offers them, and a later checkout. Upgrades depend on availability and are not guaranteed.

The 25 elite night credits do not change anything for a guest who stays a handful of nights, since Platinum is already included with the card.

## A quick test

The card tends to work for an occasional guest who can say yes to all three:

1. I pay for a restaurant meal of $25 or more every month.
2. Once a year I stay at a Marriott hotel that costs $350 or more a night, and I could book it with the free night.
3. I plan to keep the card past the first year, when the free night starts.

If the second answer is no, a card with a lower fee, or no hotel card, is likely to cost less.

## Who should skip it

- People who stay at Marriott once or twice a year at mid-priced hotels.
- People who do not eat out every month.
- People who plan to hold the card for one year only.
- People who already have lounge access and hotel status through another card, such as [the Platinum Card](/amex-platinum-credits-real-value/).
- People who do not want to pay $650 up front and earn it back in pieces.

Welcome offers on this card vary by applicant and change often. Sources checked for this page listed three different offers, so none is quoted here.

## Quick answers

**Does the free night come in the first year?**
No. One Mile at a Time reports it is issued each year after renewal, starting at the first anniversary.

**Can the dining credit be saved up?**
No. It is up to $25 each month. A month you do not use is gone.

**Is breakfast guaranteed with Platinum Elite status?**
No. It is offered as a welcome gift choice at many Marriott brands, not all.
