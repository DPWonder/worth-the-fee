# Can you get a Sapphire welcome bonus if you had a Sapphire card before? The 2026 rules

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/chase-sapphire-bonus-eligibility-2026/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

It depends on which card. Under the rules in force since January 2026, you can earn the welcome bonus on each Sapphire card once in your lifetime, so a past Sapphire Preferred does not block a first Sapphire Reserve bonus, and the reverse. A second bonus on the same card is not available, no matter how long ago the first one was.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The rule by situation

| Your history | Sapphire Preferred bonus | Sapphire Reserve bonus |
|---|---|---|
| Never had either card | Possible | Possible |
| Earned the Preferred bonus before | No | Possible |
| Earned the Reserve bonus before | Possible | No |
| Earned both before | No | No |
| Hold a Preferred now, never had a Reserve | No (you already have the card) | Possible |
| Held a card before but never earned its bonus | Unclear, see below | Unclear, see below |

"Possible" is not a promise. You still have to be approved, and Chase decides bonus eligibility at the time you apply. Welcome offers also change and vary by applicant.

## How the rules got here

| Period | Rule |
|---|---|
| Before June 2025 | You could earn a Sapphire bonus again 48 months after your last one |
| June 2025 to January 2026 | One bonus per card for life, and holding one Sapphire card blocked a first bonus on the other |
| From January 22, 2026 | One bonus per card for life; holding or having held the other card no longer blocks it |

The Points Guy dates the latest change to January 22, 2026. A CNBC Select summary gives January 25. The 48-month reset is gone under either date.

## The unclear case: you held the card but got no bonus

The Points Guy's January 2026 news piece frames the test as whether you ever earned the bonus on that card. Chase's own card pages word it more broadly:

> "The new cardmember bonus may not be available to you if you previously held this card." (Chase)

A later Points Guy article, from July 2026, also says former cardholders may be ineligible even if they never earned a bonus. So if you once held a Sapphire card through a product change, with no bonus, do not assume you qualify. The two readings conflict, and Chase's wording leaves it room to say no.

Chase's page adds that it may look at how many cards you have opened and closed.

## The pop-up that tells you

Chase now shows a notice during the application if you would not receive the bonus. As The Points Guy describes it, the message says you may be approved for the card but will not get the bonus. You then choose:

- Cancel the application. The Points Guy reports this has no effect on your credit score.
- Continue and take the card without the bonus.

Keep a record of the offer terms shown when you apply.

## Other things that can stop you

- **You already have that card open.** Chase's Reserve page says the card is not available to someone who currently holds it.
- **The 5/24 rule.** If you opened five or more personal cards at any bank in the past 24 months, Chase generally declines the application. See [how to count your cards](/chase-5-24-rule-explained/).
- **A past bonus still counts after you close or downgrade.** Closing the card does not reset anything.
- **Product changes carry no bonus.** Switching an existing Chase card to a Sapphire is not a new account and normally comes with no welcome offer.

How these rules treat the Sapphire Reserve for Business was not confirmed in the sources read.

## Who should skip it

- Anyone who already earned the bonus on the card they are looking at. The card may still suit you, but work out the sums without a bonus.
- Anyone at or over 5/24. An application is likely to be declined.
- Anyone who would have to spend more than usual to meet a spending requirement. A bonus does not make up for purchases you would not otherwise make, or for interest on a balance.
- Anyone waiting for the old 48-month clock. It no longer exists.

## Quick answers

**I got the Sapphire Preferred bonus in 2019. Can I get it again?**
No. Each card's bonus is now once per lifetime, and the 48-month reset ended in June 2025.

**I have a Sapphire Preferred. Can I get the Sapphire Reserve bonus?**
Yes, if you never earned the Reserve bonus and Chase approves you. Holding the Preferred has not blocked it since January 2026.

**Will Chase tell me before I apply whether I get the bonus?**
Chase shows a notice during the application if you will not receive it, and you can cancel at that point.
