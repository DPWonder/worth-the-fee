TopCashback Highest Cash Back Guarantee: how does the 110% match work?

The short answer

TopCashback says that if another cash back site shows a higher rate on the day you buy, it will pay 110% of that higher rate on a purchase made through TopCashback. The match is not automatic: it covers seven named sites, and you have to file a claim with proof within seven days of the cash back tracking.

The rule in one example

TopCashback's help page gives the sum itself: if another site offers $10 of cash back, it pays $11. So the claim is worth the gap between the two sites plus 10% of the higher amount.

The figures in the table are worked examples of the 110% rule, not rates seen at any store.

Which sites count

The help page names the US cash back sites it will match, as read on November 25, 2026:

  • BeFrugal
  • GoCashBack
  • Honey
  • Max Rebates
  • Mr. Rebates
  • Rakuten
  • Tada

It says it does not match closed group sites or non-paid platforms. Capital One Shopping is not on the list.

The six conditions

  1. Eligible sites only. One of the seven above.
  2. A direct purchase that tracked. The order has to show in your TopCashback account first.
  3. Same-day rate. The higher rate has to be live on the exact day of your purchase. A rate that appears later does not count.
  4. Proof. A working URL to the higher rate. The page adds that a screenshot showing date and time helps.
  5. Paid transactions only. The cash back has to be worth at least 1 cent. Free cash back offers and reward points, such as Discover Card and MyPoints, do not qualify.
  6. Seven days. The claim has to go in within seven days of the cash back tracking, with the amount shown in your account.

The page ends by saying that if you are not sure a claim qualifies, you can submit it anyway.

What this means in practice

Rakuten is on the list, so a short Rakuten rate rise can be claimed if you bought through TopCashback that day and saved proof. Two limits follow from the conditions:

  • The proof has to be taken on the day. Rates change daily, and Rakuten's terms say rates can differ between members. A screenshot with the date and time is the safer record.
  • Points do not count. A Rakuten member paid in Amex points is on a cash back rate that converts to points, so the cash rate shown on Rakuten is what you would cite. Whether TopCashback accepts that in every case is not confirmed.

How long TopCashback takes to decide a claim is not confirmed; the help page gives no figure.

Who should skip it

  • The gap is small. On a $40 order, 2 points of difference is 80 cents plus the 10%; a claim may not be worth the time.
  • You did not record the other site's rate on the day. Without proof the claim has little to stand on.
  • You would rather buy through the site with the higher rate and be done. That gets you the higher rate with no claim, less the extra 10%.
  • The other site is not one of the seven.

Quick answers

Does TopCashback match Rakuten's rates? Its help page says yes: Rakuten is one of seven sites it will beat by 10%, after a claim with proof.

How long do you have to claim? Seven days from when the cash back tracks in your TopCashback account.

Is the match automatic? No. You submit a claim with a link to the higher rate from the day you bought.
