If the Amex Platinum fee stops being worth it, what are the options and what happens to the points?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

There are three options: ask Amex for a retention offer and keep the card, downgrade to the Gold Card ($325) or the Green Card ($150), or cancel. Your Membership Rewards points stay if you keep any card that earns them. If the Platinum is your only one and you close it, the points are forfeited.

There is no downgrade to a card with no annual fee, per Upgraded Points.

Option 1: ask for a retention offer

Call the number on the card or use chat and ask whether any offer is available. Amex does not publish these offers. Reports vary:

  • The Points Guy cites one offer of 50,000 points for keeping the card another year.
  • Frequent Miler (2022) reports offers of 30,000 to 55,000 points, some with a spending condition, and statement credits. Some people got nothing.

Do not count on an offer.

Option 2: downgrade

Upgraded Points (October 2026) reports:

  • The account must have been open at least 12 months.
  • The choices are the Gold Card, $325, or the Classic Green Card, $150. The Green Card closed to new applicants on July 23, 2026, and is still available as a downgrade.
  • It is done by phone or chat, and no change is recorded on your credit report.

The welcome offer cost. Amex generally gives a welcome offer once per card. A downgrade counts as having had the new card, and having had a Platinum Card can already block the Gold offer. See the once-per-lifetime rule.

Is Gold a fit? See Platinum versus Gold and whether the Gold fee pays back.

Option 3: cancel

Before closing, the points question comes first.

If another Membership Rewards card stays open, the points stay in your account. View From the Wing reports that newer cards may not be pooled into one balance, so confirm both cards show the same balance first.

If the Platinum is your only such card, closing it forfeits unused points. Ways to avoid that:

  • Transfer the points to an airline or hotel program first. Transfers cannot be reversed. See what the points are worth.
  • Open a second Membership Rewards card before closing. Frequent Miler names the Blue Business Plus, a business card with no annual fee.

Sources disagree on timing. Doctor of Credit says closing your last such card forfeits the points. AwardWallet (April 2026) says you have 30 days after canceling to use them. Move or use the points before you close.

What happens to the annual fee

Situation Refund reported
Close within 30 days after the closing date of the statement that shows the fee Full refund
Close later than that None
Downgrade at any time Prorated: the unused part is refunded and the new card's fee is charged for the rest

These rules come from Doctor of Credit (2020) and Frequent Miler (2022). Upgraded Points says only that you "may qualify" for a prorated refund on a downgrade. None was confirmed on an Amex page, so ask the agent to state the refund before you agree.

Before you act

  • Use the credits you have already paid for.
  • Closing in the first 12 months is a risk. Search summaries say Amex's terms let it take back welcome points; this is not confirmed.

Who should skip each option

  • Skip relying on a retention offer if the card only works for you with one.
  • Skip the Gold downgrade if you would not use its credits.
  • Skip the Green downgrade if a no-fee card can hold your points.
  • Skip canceling until the points are moved, used or held by another card.

Quick answers

Do I lose my points if I downgrade the Platinum Card? No. The Gold and Green cards both earn Membership Rewards points, so the balance stays.

Do I lose my points if I cancel? Only if no other card that earns Membership Rewards points stays open. In that case unused points are forfeited.

Will Amex refund the $895 fee? A full refund is reported if you close within 30 days after the closing date of the statement showing the fee. After that, no refund on closing is reported.
