What is the most practical way to use Amex points for flights to Hawaii?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

The practical route is to move Membership Rewards points to an airline program that can book a seat to Hawaii: Delta itself, or a partner program such as Flying Blue or Avianca LifeMiles that books Delta or United flights for fewer miles. Paying with points through Amex Travel is simpler, but gives a flat 1 cent per point on flights.

What Amex points cannot do

Amex has no transfer partner based in Hawaii. Hawaiian Airlines and Alaska share a program, Atmos Rewards, and it is not on Amex's partner list. United, American and Southwest are not Amex partners either. Of the large US airlines that fly to Hawaii, only Delta takes Amex points directly.

So most of the options below book one airline's seat with another airline's miles.

Delta has no award chart. Its prices rise and fall with the cash fare, so the Seattle example is one day's price, not a rule. The 15% award discount some guides mention belongs to Delta's own credit cards, not to the Platinum Card.

The transfer fee to Delta

Amex adds an excise tax offset fee when points go to a US airline. It is $0.0006 per point, capped at $99 per transfer.

Transfer or Amex Travel?

Do one sum. Divide the cash fare, in cents, by the miles the award costs.

  • If the answer is clearly above 1 cent per point, the transfer is the better use.
  • If it is near or below 1 cent, pay with points through Amex Travel, or pay cash. The Platinum Card earns 5 points per dollar on flights bought directly from airlines or through Amex Travel.

Third-party sites value a transferred Amex point at about 1.7 to 2 cents. That is an average across many trips, and a Hawaii economy seat will not always reach it.

Three cautions:

  • A transfer cannot be undone. Find the award seat first.
  • Search for the seat in the program you plan to use, not only on the airline's own site. Each program sets its own price.
  • Transfer bonuses come and go. In November 2026 AwardWallet listed a bonus to Avianca LifeMiles running through December 16, 2026. Check the current list.

Protections when you book with miles

Amex's baggage insurance guide says tickets paid with airline frequent flyer miles do not qualify, while Pay with Points bookings do. That is one point in favor of Amex Travel for travelers checking costly gear. More in what card insurance covers on a golf trip.

Who should skip it

  • Travelers who want Hawaiian Airlines or Southwest flights. Amex points do not reach either program.
  • Anyone with fixed dates in a school holiday. Partner award seats are scarce then, and the flat 1 cent through Amex Travel may be all that is left.
  • People holding the card mainly for Hawaii. See what the Amex Platinum does for one Hawaii trip.

Quick answers

Can Amex points be moved to Hawaiian Airlines? No. Hawaiian and Alaska's Atmos Rewards program is not an Amex transfer partner.

What is an Amex point worth on a flight booked through Amex Travel? 1 cent per point on flights, according to The Points Guy in August 2026.

Is there a fee to move Amex points to Delta? Yes. $0.0006 per point, up to $99 per transfer.
