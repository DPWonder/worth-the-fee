Can you take the Rakuten sign-up bonus as Amex points instead of cash?

Facts checked November 23, 20264 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

Rakuten's own pages say yes for its standard welcome bonus: it is added to your next cash back payment or points transfer, and Rakuten's American Express page states the bonus in points. For the bonus that comes with a friend's referral link, Rakuten's pages are silent and travel sites disagree, so that part is not confirmed.

What Rakuten says

  • The welcome bonus terms say the bonus is added to the next payment "or points transfer", and that a qualifying purchase is one that earns cash back or points.
  • Rakuten's American Express page, read on November 23, 2026, showed a welcome bonus of 5,000 points for new members who spend $25 on purchases eligible for points within 90 days. That figure is what the page showed that day, not a fixed amount.
  • The Membership Rewards FAQ says confirmed points earned on purchases, bonuses and other rewards are transferred to Amex.
  • The referral page and the referral terms do not mention points at all.

The ratio

Rakuten pays 1 Membership Rewards point per cent of cash back. Its example is 10% cash back becoming 10 points per dollar. On that basis $1 of bonus is 100 points. Rakuten does not print a separate ratio for bonuses; the sites that say the bonus converts use the same 100 points per dollar.

Whether points are worth more to you than the cash is a separate sum. See whether the points are worth more than the cash.

When the switch has to be made

Rakuten gives no deadline. What its pages say:

  • A new member can link Membership Rewards while signing up through Rakuten's American Express page.
  • An existing member changes the payment method in Account Settings.
  • Confirmed cash back earned before the switch is converted to points and sent by the next transfer date.

Two travel sites say otherwise: the setting applies only to purchases made after the switch. Given that disagreement, the order with the fewest open questions is to link Membership Rewards at sign-up, or before the first purchase. Whether a bonus earned first and switched later still becomes points is not confirmed. The steps are in how to switch to Amex points.

Points move to Amex on February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15, once the store has confirmed your purchase and you have at least 501 confirmed points.

Who should skip it

  • You redeem Amex points for statement credits or gift cards. That is 1 cent a point or less, so the cash bonus is equal or more.
  • You have no Membership Rewards card open. The link cannot be made without one.
  • You want certainty about a friend's-link bonus. Cash is the form every source agrees on.
  • You use promotions tied to other card issuers on Rakuten. Its terms say those are not available while you are set to Amex points.

Quick answers

Does the Rakuten welcome bonus convert to Amex points? Rakuten's welcome bonus terms say it is added to your next cash back payment or points transfer.

How many points is the bonus worth? Rakuten's rate is 1 point per cent, so 100 points per dollar. Bonus amounts change.

Do I have to switch to points before my first purchase? Rakuten sets no deadline, but sources disagree on later switches. Linking at sign-up avoids the question.
