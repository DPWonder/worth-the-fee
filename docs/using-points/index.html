Using points: every short answer on one page

Each answer links to its full page, with sources and the date it was checked. This is information, not financial advice.

Are Amex points or Chase points worth more?

Published valuations put the two almost level: The Points Guy values an Amex point at 2.0 cents and a Chase point at 2.05 cents, and One Mile at a Time uses 1.7 cents for both. The real difference is at the bottom and at the edges: Chase points are worth 1 cent as cash against about 0.6 cents for Amex, while Amex has more airline partners and Chase has Hyatt.

Which Amex transfer partners are worth using, and which rarely are?

Among Amex's roughly 20 transfer partners, Avianca LifeMiles, Virgin Atlantic, Air Canada Aeroplan, Flying Blue, ANA and the Avios programs are valued above the 1 cent a point is worth on Amex Travel flights. Hilton, Marriott, Choice and Emirates come out below 1 cent per Amex point, and Cathay Pacific and JetBlue sit close to it.

Which Chase transfer partners are worth using, and which rarely are?

Of Chase's 16 transfer partners, Hyatt, Virgin Atlantic, Air Canada Aeroplan, Flying Blue and the Avios programs are the ones published valuations put clearly above the 1 cent a point is worth in Chase Travel. Marriott, IHG and Wyndham are valued below 1 cent, so a transfer to them usually loses value unless a bonus is running.

Do Amex and Chase points expire, and what happens if you close the card?

Amex Membership Rewards points and Chase points do not expire while the card account stays open and in good standing. They are lost when you close the last card tied to that points balance, so use them or move them before you close it; sources do not agree on whether you get any days of grace afterward.

How many Amex or Chase points does a round trip to Europe cost?

Through airline partners, a round trip from the US to Europe starts at about 12,000 to 55,000 Amex or Chase points in economy and about 81,000 to 120,000 in business class, at each program's lowest published price. Taxes and surcharges run from about $100 to more than $1,000 on top, and the low prices are not offered on every date.

How many Amex or Chase points does a round trip to Japan cost?

At the lowest partner prices, a round trip from the US to Japan costs about 40,000 to 80,000 points in economy and about 100,000 to 120,000 in business class, plus taxes and surcharges of a few hundred dollars. Those prices depend on finding an open award seat, and business-class seats on ANA are hard to find.

When should you pay with points, and when with cash?

Work out cents per point: take the cash price, subtract the taxes and fees you still pay on the award, and divide by the points needed. If the answer is below what your points fetch the simple way (1 cent for Chase, 0.6 to 1 cent for Amex), pay cash. If it is above the 1.7 to 2 cents that published valuations use, points are doing well.

What does Priority Pass include with the Amex Platinum versus the Sapphire Reserve?

Both cards come with a Priority Pass Select membership: unlimited lounge visits, two guests free, and no Priority Pass restaurant credits. The differences are small: Chase enrolls you automatically and puts a digital card in its app, and its version still covers some non-lounge stops such as Minute Suites, while Amex makes you enroll and covers lounges only.
