Amex Platinum or Amex Gold: which fits someone who travels a few times a year but eats out every week?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

For someone who eats out every week and flies a few times a year, the Gold Card is the closer fit: it earns 4 points per dollar at restaurants and costs $325 a year. The Platinum Card earns 1 point per dollar at restaurants and costs $895; it suits that person only if they would use its lounges and its travel credits.

The points for this reader

Take an example: $100 a week at restaurants ($5,200 a year) and $2,000 a year on flights.

Spending Gold Platinum
Restaurants, $5,200 20,800 points 5,200 points
Flights, $2,000 6,000 points 10,000 points
Total 26,800 points 15,200 points

The Gold earns 11,600 more points in this example. The Platinum only catches up through flights: it earns 2 more points per dollar there, while the Gold earns 3 more per dollar at restaurants.

The fee after credits

The fee gap is $570 a year. What matters is the credits you would use without changing your habits. For this reader:

Gold Platinum
Annual fee $325 $895
Resy, used fully by a weekly diner near eligible restaurants $100 $400
Uber Cash, used every month $120 $200
One two-night hotel stay in an Amex program $0 $300
Checked bag fees on one airline $0 Up to $200
A streaming service on the list $0 Up to $300
Net cost if all of the above are used $105 $505 ahead
Net cost if only Resy and Uber are used $105 $295

So the answer turns on the travel side, not the dining side. A weekly diner who also uses the Platinum's hotel, airline and streaming credits comes out ahead on paper with the Platinum, and gets lounges as well, but earns fewer points on meals. A weekly diner who would use only the dining and Uber credits pays about $190 more a year for the Platinum and earns fewer points.

Each credit has conditions. See the Resy credit, the Uber credit and what the Platinum credits are worth.

What a few trips a year gets from the Platinum

Lounge access on three or four trips is six to eight visits. Guests at Centurion Lounges cost $50 per adult. The trip delay cover is stronger on the Platinum: up to $500 after 6 hours, against up to $300 after 12 hours on the Gold, according to One Mile at a Time.

Who should skip each one

Skip the Gold if you rarely eat out or buy groceries on a card, or if you would not use Uber, Resy or the named dining partners. Then the $325 fee buys little.

Skip the Platinum if restaurants are your main spending and you would not use the hotel credit or the lounges. You would pay $895 for a card that earns 1 point per dollar on most of what you buy.

Quick answers

Which earns more points at restaurants, Amex Gold or Platinum? The Gold: 4 points per dollar on up to $50,000 a year. The Platinum earns 1 point per dollar.

Does the Amex Gold have airport lounge access? No. Lounge access is a Platinum benefit.

What are the annual fees? $325 for the Gold and $895 for the Platinum, as of November 2026.
