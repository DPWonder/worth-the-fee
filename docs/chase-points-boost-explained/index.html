What is Chase Points Boost, and how much are Chase points worth now?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

Points Boost is Chase's system for giving more than 1 cent per point on selected flights and hotels booked through Chase Travel. Without a Boost, a point is worth 1 cent there; with one, it is worth up to 2 cents on a Sapphire Reserve and up to 1.5 or 1.75 cents on a Sapphire Preferred.

How it works

Points Boost started showing in Chase Travel on June 23, 2025. It replaced the old setup, where every booking through Chase Travel got the same fixed bonus.

Now the value depends on the booking. Chase marks some flights and hotels with a Boost, and the price in points is lower for those. The set of boosted bookings rotates, and Chase does not publish a list. You see the value only when you search.

What sources report about where Boosts show up:

  • Hotels: most often at hotels in The Edit, Chase's own collection, and at other frequently booked hotels.
  • Flights: more often on long-haul premium cabins with selected airlines. Boosts on domestic economy are uncommon. Basic economy is excluded.
  • Activities and cruises: 1 cent per point, no Boost.

Sources do not fully agree on the Sapphire Preferred's top figure. The Points Guy, Upgraded Points and NerdWallet give 1.75 cents for some premium-cabin flights. Chase's own Reserve page describes Boosts as up to 1.5x or 2x depending on the card, and AwardWallet says the Preferred's maximum drops to 1.5 cents from December 2025. The safe reading: expect 1.5 cents at most on the Preferred, and treat 1.75 as an occasional result.

The Points Guy also reports that 2-cent Boosts on the Reserve have become harder to find than at launch.

What happened to the old fixed rates

Before Points Boost, Sapphire Reserve points were worth a flat 1.5 cents on any Chase Travel booking, and Sapphire Preferred points a flat 1.25 cents. Those flat rates are being phased out.

Two small points of disagreement: Upgraded Points applies the "approved before June 23, 2025" condition to the Preferred as well, and Doctor of Credit gives the end date as October 25, 2027. This wording was not found on a Chase page checked today, so confirm it in your own account.

So what is a point worth?

It depends on what you do with it.

Use Value per point
Cash or statement credit 1 cent or less
Chase Travel, no Boost 1 cent
Chase Travel with a Boost 1.1 to 2 cents, by card
Transfer to an airline or hotel partner Varies; The Points Guy's September 2026 estimate is 2.05 cents, One Mile at a Time uses 1.7 cents

The transfer figures are estimates that assume you find good award space. For simple economy flights through Chase Travel, plan on 1 cent.

One more change affects transfers: the Hyatt rate from a Sapphire Preferred is now 4 to 3, not 1 to 1.

Who should skip it

  • Anyone counting on a fixed bonus. A Boost may not exist for the flight or hotel you want, and then the points are worth 1 cent.
  • Anyone who would pay a higher price in Chase Travel than booking direct just to get a Boost. Compare the cash prices first.
  • Anyone who prefers plain cash back. At 1 cent a point, a cash back card with no annual fee may do as well.

Quick answers

Are Chase points still worth 1.25 or 1.5 cents? Only for points earned before October 26, 2025, and only until October 26, 2027. Newer points are worth 1 cent unless a Boost applies.

Can I see which bookings have a Boost before I search? No. Chase does not publish a list. The value appears in the search results.

Does the Sapphire Preferred get 2-cent Boosts? No. Two cents is the top value on the Reserve cards only.
