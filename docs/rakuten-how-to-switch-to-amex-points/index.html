How do you switch Rakuten from cash back to Amex points, and can you switch back?

Facts checked November 23, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

You switch in Rakuten's Account Settings: change the payment method to Membership Rewards points, then sign in to your American Express Membership Rewards account to link it. Whether you can switch back to cash is not settled; Rakuten's help pages do not say, one large travel site says no, and two others report that it works.

Before you start

You need a US American Express card enrolled in Membership Rewards. Rakuten names the Gold, Green and Platinum cards as examples. The card must be open and not past due. Corporate cards and the Rakuten American Express Card are not eligible.

On whether points suit you, see Amex points instead of cash and whether the points are worth more than the cash.

The steps

Rakuten's own FAQ gives three steps. Two travel sites that walked through the screens add detail, shown in the right-hand column. The screens may have changed since they wrote.

Step Rakuten's FAQ Detail reported by travel sites
1 Go to Account Settings On the website it is in the menu under your name
2 Update your payment method to Membership Rewards points Look in the section on how you are paid. One site says to select PayPal or check first, then use the link in the American Express section
3 Enter your Membership Rewards username and password to connect the accounts A confirmation pop-up may appear, and Rakuten may email a six-digit code

Store pages still say "cash back". The rate is 1 point per cent, so $1 of cash back becomes 100 points.

How to check that it worked

  • After a transfer date, your Amex account lists the points as "Rakuten Additional MR Points" under points activity.
  • Transfers happen on February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15, once you have at least 501 confirmed points. The next one is February 15, 2027.

What happens to cash back you already have

Sources disagree.

  • Rakuten's FAQ says confirmed cash back earned before the switch is converted to points and sent by the next transfer date. Its points terms say the same of cash back "accrued but not disbursed" before linking.
  • The Points Guy says an existing cash balance stays cash and is paid by check or PayPal.

If you want an existing balance as cash, the cautious order is to wait until a payment date has passed and the cash has been sent, then switch.

Can you switch back?

Source What it says Date

The Points Guy The choice is irreversible Article bylined August 2025
Miles to Memories Switching back and forth works May 2025
Doctor of Credit Members could change payout type and change back after a payment Updated May 2026

Miles to Memories quotes a warning Rakuten shows on screen when a member moves to cash: points earned before the switch are converted to cash back and sent by check on the next payment date. That suggests the route back existed when the article was written.

Rakuten does not confirm it in writing, so treat a switch back as likely possible but not guaranteed. Whatever balance is unpaid at the moment of any switch may be converted.

Keeping the link working

  • If you close or replace the linked Amex card, or change your Membership Rewards login, relink. Rakuten's path is Account Settings, then Edit below Membership Rewards points.
  • The card account must stay open and in good standing until points are transferred.
  • Rakuten's terms say promotions tied to other card issuers will not be available while you are set to Amex points.

Who should skip it

  • You redeem Amex points for statement credits or gift cards. That is 1 cent a point or less, so cash is equal or more.
  • You may close your only Membership Rewards card soon. The link needs an open, eligible card.
  • You need certainty that you can return to cash. Rakuten does not state it.

Quick answers

Where is the setting to switch Rakuten to Amex points? In Account Settings, under how you are paid. You then sign in to Membership Rewards to link.

Can I switch Rakuten back from points to cash? Not confirmed. Rakuten's pages are silent; one site says no, two report that it works.

Does my existing cash back turn into points? Rakuten's FAQ says confirmed cash back is converted. One travel site says it stays cash.
