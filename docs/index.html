Amex Platinum

  • What are the Amex Platinum credits really worth if you only count the ones you would use anyway?On paper the 11 main Amex Platinum credits total $2,994 a year. Your real number is only the ones you would have paid for anyway.
  • Does the Amex Platinum do anything for golfers?Very little directly. The US Amex Platinum has no free rounds, green fee credit or tee time perk, but its hotel credit and lounges can help a golf trip.
  • What does the Amex Platinum actually do for one Hawaii trip a year?One Hawaii trip gets up to $300 back on a prepaid hotel stay and lounge access in Honolulu and Maui. Kona and Lihue have no lounge the card opens.
  • Amex Platinum or Chase Sapphire Reserve for a once-a-year Hawaii trip?For one Hawaii trip a year the Sapphire Reserve fits better: lower fee, a simple $300 travel credit and 1:1 Hyatt transfers.
  • Is the Amex Platinum worth its annual fee if you only fly 4 to 6 times a year?Usually only if you would use about $900 of its credits anyway. At 4 to 6 flights a year, the lounges alone do not cover the $895 fee.
  • How do Amex referral offers work, and why does the page say "as high as" a number?An Amex referral link opens an application where Amex shows your own offer before you accept. "As high as" is the top figure, not a promise.

Chase Sapphire

  • Is the Chase Sapphire Preferred worth it if you take one big trip a year?Usually yes: one hotel booking through Chase Travel returns $100 against the $95 fee, but only if you would book that way anyway.
  • Sapphire Preferred or Sapphire Reserve for someone who travels two or three times a year?For two or three trips a year the $95 Preferred fits most people; the $795 Reserve pays off only if you use several of its credits.

Rakuten cash back

  • How does the Rakuten sign-up bonus through a friend's link work, and what makes it fail?Join through a friend's link, spend $50 on purchases that earn cash back within 90 days, and the bonus is paid on Rakuten's next payment date.
  • Can you get Amex points from Rakuten instead of cash, and is it worth switching?Yes: Rakuten pays 1 Amex Membership Rewards point per cent of cash back. It is worth it only if you use points for more than 1 cent.
