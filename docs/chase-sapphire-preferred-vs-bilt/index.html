Chase Sapphire Preferred or a Bilt card for a renter?

Facts checked November 21, 20265 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

A renter who would put most everyday spending on the card earns more with a Bilt card, because rent itself earns up to 1.25 points per dollar. A renter who spends little on the card, or who wants strong trip and rental car cover, is closer to the Sapphire Preferred, since Bilt's rent points shrink to 250 a month when other spending is low.

What a Bilt card is now

The Wells Fargo Bilt Mastercard ended on February 6, 2026. Three cards replaced it the next day, serviced by Cardless and issued by Column N.A.: Bilt Blue ($0), Bilt Obsidian ($95) and Bilt Palladium ($495). A person can be the primary holder of one Bilt card.

The $495 Palladium earns 2x on everyday purchases and adds Priority Pass, a $400 hotel credit and $200 of Bilt Cash a year. From January 15, 2027 Bilt replaces those two with $600 of Bilt Cash, and the Obsidian's hotel credit becomes $100 of Bilt Cash.

How rent earns points

Bilt gives two options, and a cardholder can switch between them month to month.

Housing-only. The rate on rent depends on other card spending that month, as a share of the rent:

Other spending as a share of rent Points per dollar of rent
Under 25% 250 points flat
25% 0.5
50% 0.75
75% 1
100% or more 1.25

Bilt Cash. Everyday purchases earn 4% in Bilt Cash on top of points. Each $30 of Bilt Cash turns into 1,000 points on rent, up to 1 point per dollar. Bilt Cash now expires 12 months after it is earned.

Sources disagree on a fee. AwardWallet's January 2026 guide says a cardholder short on Bilt Cash can pay a 3% fee to earn the full rate. The Points Guy's Obsidian review says neither option carries a transaction fee.

Cards opened from January 1, 2027 get the housing-only option alone. Anyone with a card opened by December 31, 2026 can keep the Bilt Cash option by opting out before the card anniversary.

The sum for a renter

Take rent of $2,000 a month and $1,500 a month of other spending, $500 of it on dining. That is 75% of rent, so rent earns 1x under housing-only.

Now cut the other spending to $400 a month, 20% of rent. Rent earns 250 points a month, 3,000 a year, and the Bilt advantage mostly disappears.

The Preferred figures leave out gas, streaming and Chase Travel bonuses, which would raise its total for a reader with that spending.

Which pattern favors which card

  • Card spending near or above the rent each month: a Bilt card. Rent earns 1 to 1.25 points per dollar, on a $0 card if wanted.
  • Card spending under a quarter of the rent: the Preferred, or no change. Bilt pays 250 points a month at that level.
  • Wants Hyatt at 1:1: Bilt, but only through December 31, 2026. From January 1, 2027 Bilt moves to 4:3, the same as the Preferred.
  • Wants Alaska or Hawaiian miles: Bilt. Chase has no Atmos Rewards transfer.
  • Wants trip cancellation cover of up to $10,000 per traveler and primary rental car cover: the Preferred. See its travel insurance. The Bilt pages read list trip delay cover on the Obsidian and rental cover on the Blue without amounts.
  • Does not want to track tiers, Bilt Cash and yearly rule changes: the Preferred. Its rates do not depend on a monthly ratio.

Who should skip it

  • Skip a Bilt card for rent points if you would not move your everyday spending to it.
  • Skip the Bilt Cash option if a 12-month expiry on credits would catch you out.
  • Skip the Preferred if rent is your largest bill and you want it to earn. The Preferred has no rent reward.
  • Skip both if you carry a balance. Interest costs more than either card returns.

Welcome offers vary by applicant and change, so they are left out of the sums.

Quick answers

Does the Sapphire Preferred earn points on rent? It has no rent reward. Whether a landlord takes cards, and at what fee, depends on the landlord.

Is there still a no-annual-fee Bilt card? Yes. The Bilt Blue has no annual fee and earns up to 1.25 points per dollar on rent, depending on other spending.

Do Bilt points transfer to Hyatt at 1:1? Yes until December 31, 2026. The Points Guy reports the rate drops to 4:3 on January 1, 2027.
