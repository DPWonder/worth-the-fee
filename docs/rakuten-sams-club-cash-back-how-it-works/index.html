Rakuten Sam's Club cash back: what earns 9%, what earns 3%, and what is excluded?

Facts checked November 24, 20265 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

Yes. On November 24, 2026, Rakuten's Sam's Club page showed up to 9% cash back: 9% on new memberships, raised from a usual 4%, and 3% on all other eligible items, raised from a usual 1%. Groceries, household essentials, membership renewals, Scan & Go purchases, cell phones and iPads earn nothing.

The two tiers seen on November 24, 2026

"Was" is Rakuten's own label for the store's usual rate. The "up to 9%" in the headline is the membership rate only. Merchandise earns 3%.

The page names 17 departments at 3%, among them appliances, electronics, mattresses, home, kitchen and dining, outdoor and patio, toys, jewelry, clothing, office, and savings and clearance. Pickup orders and Delivery from Club are listed at 3% too, each marked "eligible items only". How long the raised rates last is not confirmed.

What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Sam's Club list these as earning nothing:

  • Consumables: groceries, household essentials and select personal care
  • Membership renewals
  • Scan & Go purchases
  • Cell phones and iPads
  • Tires, fuel, pharmacy, travel and Sam's Services
  • Alcohol, tobacco and stamps
  • Gift cards
  • My Pillow products
  • Orders deemed for reselling purposes, and high volume orders
  • The portion of an order paid with a gift card or Sam's Cash

Three of these matter more than the rest. Groceries and household essentials are a large share of a typical club order, and they earn 0%. Electronics earn 3%, but cell phones and iPads are carved out. Scan & Go, the store's phone checkout inside the club, does not earn.

The terms do not say which personal care items are "select", or how large an order must be to count as high volume. Both are not confirmed. Whether tablets other than iPads earn is not stated.

The membership tier

Sam's Club's own membership page listed two plans on November 24, 2026:

Plan Price a year 9% cash back At the usual 4%
Club $60 $5.40 $2.40
Plus (now includes Walmart+) $120 $10.80 $4.80

Rakuten's terms say new memberships and do not name a plan. Whether both plans earn 9%, whether an upgrade from Club to Plus counts as new, and whether a discounted membership offer earns on the reduced price are not confirmed. Renewals are excluded by name.

A membership bought at the club counter is an in-person purchase. Rakuten's cash back here is for orders that start from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.

Sam's Cash is separate

Sam's Club's page lists up to 2% back in Sam's Cash as a Plus benefit. That is the store's own reward, not Rakuten's. Rakuten's terms exclude the part of an order paid with Sam's Cash, so spending Sam's Cash lowers the amount that earns.

Other terms

  • Posting. The page says cash back is added to the Rakuten account within one week. That is longer than the next-day posting on many store pages.
  • Missing cash back. The page gives no store deadline. Rakuten's general terms allow 90 days from the transaction. See what to do when cash back did not track.
  • Coupon codes. No code rule is listed for Sam's Club. Rakuten's general page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.
  • App. The terms do not mention the Sam's Club app, apart from Scan & Go. Not confirmed.
  • In stores. Sam's Club did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 24, 2026. The list did not load in full, so this is not confirmed.

Taxes and shipping do not count. On an order of $500 for a television and $200 in groceries, only the television earns: $15. Whether an excluded item in the cart affects the rest of the order is not stated in the terms.

Costco and Walmart

Rakuten's Costco page showed no cash back on the same day. See Rakuten at Costco. Walmart, which owns Sam's Club, has a different kind of offer: see Rakuten at Walmart.

When you get the money

Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See when Rakuten pays. Card rewards are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • Your order is mostly groceries and household essentials. Those are excluded.
  • You are renewing a membership. Only new memberships earn.
  • You check out in the club with Scan & Go.
  • You are buying a cell phone, an iPad or tires.
  • You pay mostly with Sam's Cash or a gift card.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Sam's Club? On November 24, 2026: 9% on new memberships and 3% on other eligible items. The usual rates shown were 4% and 1%.

Do groceries earn cash back at Sam's Club? No. Groceries, household essentials and select personal care items are excluded.

Does a membership renewal earn cash back? No. Renewals are excluded. New memberships earned 9% on November 24, 2026.
