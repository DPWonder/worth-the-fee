Chase Sapphire Preferred or Capital One Venture: which fits a few trips a year?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Both cards cost $95 a year, so the choice comes down to where the money goes. The Venture earns more for someone whose spending is mostly general purchases, and the Sapphire Preferred earns more for someone whose dining and similar spending is larger than everything else, and it has stronger trip protection.

The plain sum

Call dining and the other 3x categories D, direct travel T, and everything else O.

  • Preferred: 3 points on D, 2 on T, 1 on O.
  • Venture: 2 miles on all three.

Travel booked direct is a tie. The Preferred gains 1 point per dollar on D and loses 1 per dollar on O. So the Preferred earns more only when D is larger than O.

A reader whose general spending is larger than restaurants, gas, streaming and online grocery together earns more with the Venture. Add up last year's statements to see which side is bigger.

What the Preferred adds back

  • The hotel credit. Up to $100 a year on a hotel booked through Chase Travel. A reader who would book one hotel night that way gets the fee back.
  • Trip protection. Trip cancellation and interruption up to $10,000 per traveler, trip delay up to $500 after 12 hours, and primary rental car cover. The page on Sapphire Preferred travel insurance has the limits and exclusions. The Venture reviews read list travel accident insurance and lost luggage cover, and no trip cancellation or delay cover.
  • United, Southwest and Hyatt. Capital One miles cannot go to any of the three.

What the Venture adds back

  • Redemption without a portal. Buy any flight, hotel or rental anywhere, then use miles to wipe the charge at 1 cent each within 90 days.
  • One rate to remember. 2x on everything means no tracking of categories.
  • Hertz Five Star status, with enrollment.

Which pattern favors which card

  • Two or three trips, booked wherever is cheapest, most spending on general purchases: Venture.
  • Eats out often, drives a lot, or flies United or Southwest: Sapphire Preferred.
  • Rents cars or books costly nonrefundable trips: Sapphire Preferred, for the cover.
  • Wants the least effort: Venture.

Who should skip both

  • Anyone who carries a balance month to month.
  • Anyone who wants cash, not travel. Venture miles are worth about half a cent as cash, and Chase points 1 cent.
  • For the Preferred: anyone who has opened five or more personal cards in 24 months. See Chase's 5/24 rule.

Welcome offers on both cards vary and change. Both issuers also limit repeat bonuses, so read the offer terms.

Quick answers

Which card earns more on groceries bought in a store? The Venture, at 2x. The Preferred gives 3x only on online grocery orders, and 1x in the store.

Does either card include lounge access? No. Neither card has airport lounge access today.

Is the Venture's rental car cover primary? Not confirmed in the sources read. The Sapphire Preferred's cover is primary.
