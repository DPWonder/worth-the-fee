TopCashback app: how does it work on iPhone and Android?

The short answer

The TopCashback app is a free app for iPhone, iPad and Android that does on a phone what the website does on a computer: you pick a store in the app, buy as usual, and the cash back is recorded in the same account. TopCashback's app page says a purchase made this way can take up to 6 weeks to show in your account.

What the app does

TopCashback's app page, read on November 25, 2026, lists three things:

  • Shop. Find a store or service and start the purchase from the app. The page says more than 7,000 stores pay cash back, and that new ones are added weekly.
  • Check your account. See how much cash back you have earned and follow each transaction.
  • Cash out. Send available cash back by bank transfer, Venmo or PayPal, or take a gift card.

You log in with the same details as on the website. There is one account, not a separate one for the phone.

How long cash back takes on the app

The app page says "It can take up to 6 weeks for the cash back to show in your account."

TopCashback's Getting Started page, which describes the website, gives a shorter figure: up to seven days for a purchase to appear as "In Progress," then one to three months on average before the store confirms it and it becomes "Available." The two pages do not explain the difference. A fair reading is that a week is typical and 6 weeks is the outer limit for the app, but that reading is not confirmed by TopCashback.

Either way, showing in the account is only the first stage. You cannot cash out until the store has confirmed the sale. The stages are set out on how TopCashback pays.

If a purchase does not show, the app page says to send a support ticket from your account.

The browser extension does not work on phones, by TopCashback's own help page. On a phone, the app or the mobile website is the way in.

Whether the app pays cash back on purchases made inside a store's own app, as opposed to the store's mobile website, is not stated on the pages read for this page.

What can stop a phone purchase from tracking

TopCashback's help page on missing cash back lists reasons that apply on any device: a coupon code that TopCashback did not list, an ad blocker, finishing the purchase in a different tab or window from the one TopCashback opened, or clicking another cash back or coupon site afterward. On a phone, the third is the easy one to trip over: if the store's link opens in one place and you check out in another, the store may not know TopCashback sent you.

Who should skip it

  • You buy on a computer. The website or the extension does the same job there.
  • You do not want another shopping app sending alerts. Notifications are optional, but the app is still one more thing on the phone.
  • You do most phone shopping inside store apps. Whether those purchases track is not confirmed.
  • You want the money quickly. The wait for a store to confirm a sale is the same on every device.

If you are still deciding between sites, see Rakuten or TopCashback and, for the larger site's phone tools, Rakuten's app and extension.

Quick answers

Is the TopCashback app free? Yes. The app page says it is free to download and use; your carrier's data charges can still apply.

Does the TopCashback app work on iPad and Android tablets? Yes. The page lists iPhone and iPad, and Android phones and tablets with the Google Play Store.

How long does cash back take to show in the TopCashback app? Up to 6 weeks, by the app page. It then has to be confirmed by the store before you can cash out.
