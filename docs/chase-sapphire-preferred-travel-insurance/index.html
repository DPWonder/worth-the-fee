What travel insurance comes with the Sapphire Preferred, and what does it not cover?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

The Sapphire Preferred includes trip cancellation and interruption cover, trip delay, baggage delay, lost luggage and primary rental car damage cover when you pay for the trip with the card. It does not pay medical bills abroad, it does not cover pre-existing conditions, and it does not pay out if you simply decide not to go.

What is included

Protection Limit When it applies
Trip cancellation and interruption Up to $10,000 per traveler, $20,000 per trip, $40,000 per 12 months Prepaid, non-refundable costs lost for a listed reason
Trip delay Up to $500 per traveler Delay of more than 12 hours, or an overnight stay
Baggage delay Up to $100 a day for 5 days Bags more than 6 hours late
Lost luggage Up to $3,000 per traveler Lost or damaged by the carrier; pays after the carrier does
Rental car collision damage Up to $60,000 Theft or collision damage to the rental car
Travel accident Up to $500,000 on a common carrier Accidental death or dismemberment
Emergency evacuation Up to $100,000 Trips of 5 to 60 days, more than 100 miles from home, arranged in advance by the administrator

The first six limits are on Chase's card page. The 12-month cap, the evacuation conditions and the exclusions below come from Upgraded Points and AwardWallet summaries of Chase's Guide to Benefits, which governs and was not read directly for this page.

Some or all of the trip must be charged to the card or paid with Chase points. Cover extends to the cardholder and immediate family.

Trip cancellation: the listed reasons

This cover pays only for reasons on Chase's list. Reported examples:

  • Injury, illness or death of you, a traveling companion or an immediate family member
  • Severe weather or a named storm
  • Jury duty or a subpoena
  • Your home or lodging becoming uninhabitable
  • A quarantine ordered by a physician

What it does not cover

Not covered Detail
Change of plans Deciding not to go, money trouble, or work obligations are not listed reasons
Pre-existing conditions Any condition with advice, diagnosis or treatment in the 60 days before your first trip payment
Medical bills abroad No emergency medical expense benefit is listed for this card
Long trips Trips over 60 days
Late pregnancy Travel after 26 weeks
A travel company going out of business Excluded
Standalone tickets Theme park, museum or golf bookings that are not part of a travel package

The medical gap matters most. The evacuation benefit moves you to care; it does not pay for the care.

Trip delay details

The delay has to come from a covered cause: equipment failure, weather, a strike or a hijacking. It pays for meals, lodging, toiletries and medication that the carrier does not cover. Keep receipts.

Rental car cover

"Primary" means you claim on the card first, not on your own auto insurer. Pay for the whole rental with the card and decline the rental company's collision damage waiver.

Limits to know:

  • Rentals of 31 consecutive days or fewer.
  • Most vehicles with a list price of $125,000 or less. Excluded: antique cars, cargo vans, trucks other than pickups, motorcycles, limousines, RVs, and passenger vans that seat more than 12.
  • Car-sharing such as Turo is excluded, according to Upgraded Points.
  • Liability is not covered. Damage to other cars or property and injuries to anyone are outside this benefit. So are your belongings in the car.
  • For New York residents, cover inside the United States is secondary to personal auto insurance.

Who should skip relying on it

  • Anyone traveling abroad without other medical cover.
  • Anyone with a health condition treated in the past 60 days that could affect the trip.
  • Anyone who wants to be able to cancel for any reason.
  • Anyone on a trip longer than 60 days, or renting a car for more than 31 days.
  • Anyone whose trip costs well over $10,000 per person.

In these cases a separate travel insurance policy is the usual answer. The card's cover is a base for an ordinary one-trip-a-year traveler, not a full policy.

Quick answers

Does the Sapphire Preferred cover medical bills overseas? No emergency medical benefit is listed for this card. It includes emergency evacuation with conditions, not treatment costs.

Is the rental car cover primary? Yes, for theft and collision damage up to $60,000, except for New York residents renting inside the United States. Liability is not included.

Do I have to pay for the whole trip with the card? For trip cancellation, some or all of the trip must be on the card or paid with Chase points. For the rental car cover, pay for the whole rental with the card.
