Rakuten Macy's cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

The short answer

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Macy's. On November 24, 2026, Rakuten's Macy's page showed 10% cash back, raised from a usual 2%. Furniture, mattresses, electronics and marketplace seller items earn 1.5%, and gift cards and a list of brands earn nothing.

"Was" is Rakuten's own label for the store's usual rate. The gap between 2% and 10% is large, and a raised rate can end without notice. The raised rate has already moved once this week: the same page showed 13% on November 21. How long 10% lasts is not confirmed.

What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Macy's list these as earning nothing:

  • Gift cards, gift certificates and corporate gift cards
  • Brands: Apple, Birkenstock, BCBG, Motherhood Maternity, Hugo Boss, Mango, Pandora, Marc Jacobs
  • Shops inside the site: Sports Fan Shop by Lids, shoes by Finish Line, Sunglass Hut sunglasses, LensCrafters glasses
  • Books, health and fitness items, leased brand items
  • Orders through macyswineshop.com
  • Adjustments, reorders and orders by store employees

One more term: the part of an order paid with Star Dollars does not qualify.

Leased brand items are not listed by name on the page. Which other brands fall under that term is not confirmed.

Coupon codes, pickup and app

  • Coupon codes. The page's FAQ says one store promo code per order can be used with Rakuten cash back. Rakuten's general page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back, so the two statements pull in different directions. A code shown on Rakuten's own page is the safer choice.
  • Pickup. The page describes free in-store and curbside pickup as ways to shop, but the terms do not say whether a pickup order earns. Not confirmed.
  • App. The terms do not mention the store's app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
  • Adjustments. These are excluded. The page does not define the term.

Whether an item is sold by a marketplace seller moves the rate from 10% to 1.5% at the rates seen that day.

Other department stores

Each store is handled differently on Rakuten, and not every store pays a rate. See the pages for Kohl's, Nordstrom and Lululemon.

In stores, and one card note

This retailer did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 24, 2026. The list was cut off, so an in-store offer is not confirmed. Separately, Rakuten's in-store FAQ lists the Macy's American Express card as not eligible for linking to in-store offers at any store. See how in-store cash back works.

When you get the money

Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See when Rakuten pays and what to do when cash back did not track. Card rewards are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • You are buying a gift card, or one of the excluded brands.
  • You are buying furniture, a mattress or electronics and expect the headline rate. Those earn 1.5%.
  • You pay mostly with Star Dollars. That part earns nothing.
  • You have a code that is not on Rakuten and it is worth more than the cash back rate that day.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Macy's? 10% on most categories on November 24, 2026, against a usual 2%. It was 13% three days earlier. Rates change daily.

Does furniture earn the same rate? No. Furniture, mattresses, electronics and marketplace seller items showed 1.5%.

Do gift cards earn cash back? No. Gift cards, gift certificates and corporate gift cards are excluded.
