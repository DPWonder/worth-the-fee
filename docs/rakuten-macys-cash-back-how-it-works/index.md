# Rakuten Macy's cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-macys-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-26
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Macy's: 12% on November 26, 2026, the day before Black Friday, raised from a usual 2%. Furniture, mattresses, electronics and marketplace seller items earn 1.5%, and gift cards and a list of brands earn nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate here and at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

| Date read | Rate shown for most categories |
|---|---|
| November 21, 2026 | 13% |
| November 24, 2026 | 10% (was 2%) |
| November 25, 2026 | 10% (was 2%) |

"Was" is Rakuten's own label for the store's usual rate. On November 26 the page carried a banner tying the 12% rate to Black Friday. The raised rate has moved twice since November 21, and a raised rate can end without notice. How long 12% lasts, and what the rate will be on Black Friday itself or on Cyber Monday, is not confirmed. ## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Macy's list these as earning nothing:

- Gift cards, gift certificates and corporate gift cards
- Brands: Apple, Birkenstock, BCBG, Motherhood Maternity, Hugo Boss, Mango, Pandora, Marc Jacobs
- Shops inside the site: Sports Fan Shop by Lids, shoes by Finish Line, Sunglass Hut sunglasses, LensCrafters glasses
- Books, health and fitness items, leased brand items
- Orders through macyswineshop.com
- Adjustments, reorders and orders by store employees

One more term: the part of an order paid with Star Dollars does not qualify.

Leased brand items are not listed by name on the page. Which other brands fall under that term is not confirmed.

## Coupon codes, pickup and app

- **Coupon codes.** The page's FAQ says one store promo code per order can be used with Rakuten cash back. Rakuten's general page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back, so the two statements pull in different directions. A code shown on Rakuten's own page is the safer choice. The deals listed on November 26 were sale links, such as up to 70% off Black Friday deals and 50% off select Levi's denim, not codes.
- **Pickup.** The page describes free in-store and curbside pickup as ways to shop, but the terms do not say whether a pickup order earns. Not confirmed.
- **App.** The terms do not mention the store's app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
- **Adjustments.** These are excluded. The page does not define the term.

## What it comes to

| Purchase | Price | At 2% | At 12% | At 1.5% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coat | $200 | $4.00 | $24.00 | n/a |
| Cookware set | $350 | $7.00 | $42.00 | n/a |
| Mattress | $1,200 | n/a | n/a | $18.00 |
| Marketplace seller item | $100 | n/a | n/a | $1.50 |

Taxes and shipping do not count. Cash back is figured on what you pay for the goods after discounts.

Whether an item is sold by a marketplace seller moves the rate from 12% to 1.5% at the rates seen that day.

## In stores

On November 26, 2026 the page showed an in-store offer next to the online rate: $10 cash back on a purchase of at least $100, valid through December 13, 2026. The in-store terms on the page:

- US stores only, not US territories, and not for online purchases
- Cannot be combined with other offers, and not valid on gift cards
- A debit card must be run as credit, with no PIN
- Once added, the offer covers qualifying purchases for one hour, then has to be added again
- An added offer that is not used expires after 45 days, or when the offer ends if that is sooner

The offer is a flat $10, not a percentage, so a $100 purchase and a $400 purchase earn the same. Rakuten's in-store FAQ lists the Macy's American Express card as not eligible for linking. Whether the online exclusions, such as brands, also apply in a store is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## When you get the money

The listing says cash back is added to the Rakuten account the same day. Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/) and [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/). Card rewards are separate and come on top; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- You are buying a gift card, or one of the excluded brands.
- You are buying furniture, a mattress or electronics and expect the headline rate. Those earn 1.5%.
- You pay mostly with Star Dollars. That part earns nothing.
- You have a code that is not on Rakuten and it is worth more than the cash back rate that day.
- You are spending under $100 at a store register. The in-store offer has a $100 minimum.

## Quick answers

**What is the Rakuten Macy's cash back rate today?**
12% on most categories on November 26, 2026, against a usual 2%. It was 10% the day before. Rates change daily.

**Does furniture earn the same rate?**
No. Furniture, mattresses, electronics and marketplace seller items showed 1.5%.

**Do gift cards earn cash back?**
No. Gift cards, gift certificates and corporate gift cards are excluded, online and in stores.
