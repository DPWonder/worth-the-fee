Does the Amex Platinum do anything for golfers?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Very little directly. The US Amex Platinum has no free rounds, no green fee credit and no tee time priority; what it offers a golfer is travel help around the trip, mainly a hotel credit, airport lounges and some travel protections.

What the card does not have

The current benefit lists for the US personal Platinum Card do not mention golf.

Golf benefit On the US Amex Platinum?
Free or discounted rounds No
Statement credit for green fees No
Priority tee times No
Golf club membership or network access No
Help booking a tee time through the concierge Not listed as a card benefit

Platinum cards in some other countries, such as Singapore, have golf programs. Those do not apply to the US card.

The card costs $895 a year. Nobody should pay that for golf benefits, because there are none to pay for.

What does help on a golf trip

These benefits are not about golf, but a golf trip can use them.

The hotel credit

The card gives up to $300 back each half-year, up to $600 per calendar year, on prepaid stays booked through Amex Travel at Fine Hotels + Resorts or The Hotel Collection properties. The Hotel Collection needs a two-night minimum, and from January 1, 2027, Fine Hotels + Resorts does too.

Fine Hotels + Resorts stays also include a property credit of at least $100 per stay, daily breakfast for two and a guaranteed 4 p.m. late checkout. What the property credit can be spent on varies by hotel, so ask the resort whether golf counts before you book.

Airline fee credit

The card gives up to $200 a year for incidental fees, such as checked bags, on one airline you select in advance. If that airline charges a normal checked bag fee for your clubs, this is the kind of fee the credit is described as covering. Whether a separate oversize or sports equipment fee counts is not confirmed.

Lounges

On travel days the card gets you into Centurion Lounges, Priority Pass lounges after enrollment, and Delta Sky Clubs up to 10 times a year when you fly Delta that day. Centurion guests cost $50 each, which matters if you travel with a group.

Points

Green fees, pro shop purchases and lessons earn 1 point per dollar. The card's 5 points per dollar apply only to flights and to prepaid hotels booked through Amex Travel.

Is that enough for a golfer?

Add up only what you would use. One golf trip a year with a two-night prepaid stay at an eligible hotel and checked clubs might return $300 from the hotel credit and up to $200 in airline fees. That is $500 against an $895 fee. The rest has to come from credits that have nothing to do with golf.

Who should not get it

  • Golfers who want rewards on green fees and club spending. This card earns 1 point per dollar there.
  • Golfers who drive to their courses and rarely fly or stay in hotels.
  • Golfers who stay in rentals or mid-priced hotels. The hotel credit only works at hotels in Amex's two programs.
  • Anyone expecting course access or tee time help as a card benefit.

Quick answers

Does the Amex Platinum give free rounds of golf? No. The US card has no free rounds, green fee credits or tee time priority.

Can I use the Amex hotel credit at a golf resort? Yes, if the resort is in Fine Hotels + Resorts or The Hotel Collection and you prepay through Amex Travel. The credit is for the stay, not for golf.

Does the card's baggage insurance cover golf clubs? Not confirmed. Check the benefit terms before relying on it.
