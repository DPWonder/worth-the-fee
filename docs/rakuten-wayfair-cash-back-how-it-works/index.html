The short answer

Not on the day checked. On November 22, 2026, Rakuten's Wayfair page showed "No Cash Back", with no rate, no "was" rate and no terms or exclusions. Wayfair has a page on Rakuten, but a purchase started from it that day would earn nothing.

"Was" is Rakuten's own label for a store's usual rate. The page's title describes it as coupons, promo codes and no cash back for November 2026.

The page describes the store, its main categories and its sale pages for Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Those are the store's own sales, not Rakuten cash back. The page gives no reason for the missing cash back and no return date.

What "No Cash Back" means in practice

  • Every purchase earns nothing. With no offer, there are no exclusions to read. Furniture, rugs, sale items, gift cards and app orders are all in the same position.
  • Earlier rates. Whether Wayfair paid a rate through Rakuten earlier in 2026 is not confirmed. A customer review on the page mentions cash back, but it carries no date.
  • Black Friday. Black Friday is November 27, 2026. Rates often move that week, and a store with no offer can gain one. Nothing on the page promises that Wayfair will.

A rate at another store helps only if the item and the delivered price are the same. On a large piece of furniture, a delivery fee or a price gap can outweigh 2%.

For scale, 2% on a $1,200 sofa is $24.00, and on a $200 rug it is $4.00. Taxes and shipping do not count.

What still works at Wayfair

Rakuten cash back and card rewards are separate. A card that earns points or cash on the purchase still does, whatever the Rakuten page shows; see stacking with card rewards. Other portals set their own rates; none was checked for Wayfair today. See Rakuten and TopCashback.

In stores

Wayfair did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 22, 2026. The list was cut off after 21 stores, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.

If an offer returns

The general rules would apply. Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See when Rakuten pays and how Black Friday rates work.

Who should skip it

  • You are buying from Wayfair today and expect cash back. The page shows none.
  • You are delaying a purchase in the hope of a rate. None is promised, and sale prices can end.
  • You would pay more at another store only to earn 2%.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Wayfair? None on November 22, 2026. The page showed "No Cash Back". Offers change daily.

What is excluded at Wayfair? No terms or exclusions are listed, because there is no offer. Every purchase earns nothing.

Will Wayfair have cash back on Black Friday? Not confirmed. Rates often move that week, but the page promises nothing.
