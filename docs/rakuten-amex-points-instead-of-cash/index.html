Can you get Amex points from Rakuten instead of cash, and is it worth switching?

Facts checked November 20, 2026

This page has a referral link at the end. The person who runs this site earns points or cash if you sign up through it.

Yes. If you hold a US American Express card that earns Membership Rewards, Rakuten will pay you 1 point for every cent of cash back in place of the cash. It is worth switching only if you reliably get more than 1 cent of value from each point, which in practice means moving points to airline or hotel programs.

This page is information, not financial advice.

The rate

Rakuten offer at a store Paid as cash Paid as points
$1 of cash back $1 100 points
2% back 2 cents per dollar 2 points per dollar
10% back 10 cents per dollar 10 points per dollar

Stores on Rakuten keep showing "cash back" after you switch. The amount is turned into points behind the scenes.

Who qualifies

  • You need a US Amex card enrolled in Membership Rewards. Rakuten names the Gold, Green and Platinum cards as examples.
  • The card must be open and not past due.
  • Corporate cards do not qualify. The Rakuten American Express Card does not qualify by itself either.
  • You do not have to pay with the Amex card. Any card works at checkout; the Amex card only decides where the reward goes.

Amex cards that earn cash back or an airline's or hotel's own points are outside Membership Rewards, so they do not count.

How to switch, step by step

  1. Sign in to Rakuten and open Account Settings.
  2. Change your payment method to Membership Rewards points.
  3. Sign in to your Membership Rewards account when asked, to link the two.
  4. Shop through Rakuten as usual.

If you are new to Rakuten, you can pick points while signing up.

If you later replace or close the linked card, you have to link the new one in Account Settings or transfers stop.

When the points arrive

Rakuten sends points on the same four dates it pays cash: February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15. Only rewards the store has confirmed before the date are included. Confirmation typically takes 3 to 14 weeks for shopping and 6 to 17 weeks after a trip ends for travel.

You need at least 501 confirmed points. A balance of 500 or fewer carries over to the next date. In your Amex account the line reads "Rakuten Additional MR Points".

Is it worth it?

It comes down to one question: what do you do with Amex points?

How you use a point Rough value Switch?
Statement credit or similar 1 cent or less No. Take the cash.
Transfers to airline partners, used with care 1.7 to 2 cents, by two travel sites' estimates Yes, if you really do this

Those higher figures are other people's estimates. They assume you find award seats and book them. Points you never use are worth nothing, and programs can raise their prices at any time. Cash cannot lose value that way.

Reasons to stay on cash:

  • You like a check or PayPal payment four times a year and do not want to manage points.
  • You have no trip in mind that points would pay for.
  • You use offers on Rakuten from other card issuers. Rakuten's terms say those may not be available once you choose Amex points.

Two things that are not settled

Can you switch back? One large travel site reported that the choice cannot be undone, and in the same article said you can always change your preference. Rakuten's own help pages do not say either way. Treat the switch as possibly permanent until you see otherwise in your own account settings.

What happens to your existing balance? Rakuten says cash back confirmed before you switch is turned into points and sent on the next transfer date. One travel site says the opposite. If you want that balance as cash, wait until after a payment date before switching.

Whether a new-member bonus arrives as points or cash is also unclear. Rakuten's terms mention both payment types, while an older article says the bonus stays as cash.

Quick answers

How many Amex points is $1 of Rakuten cash back? 100 points. The rate is 1 point per cent.

Do I have to pay with my Amex card? No. You only need to hold a qualifying card and link it.

When do the points show up? On February 15, May 15, August 15 or November 15, once you have at least 501 confirmed points.
