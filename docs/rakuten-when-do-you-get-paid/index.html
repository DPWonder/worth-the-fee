When does Rakuten pay you, and when is the next payout?

Facts checked November 23, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Rakuten pays four times a year, on February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15, as long as you have about $5 in confirmed cash back. The next payout after November 15, 2026 is February 15, 2027. A purchase can take months to be paid because the store must confirm it first, and only then does it wait for the next date.

The four dates

Payment date Next one
February 15 February 15, 2027
May 15 May 15, 2027
August 15 August 15, 2027
November 15 November 15, 2027

Rakuten's help page says only that each payment includes the cash back confirmed before that date. Rakuten's blog ties each date to a calendar quarter: the February 15 payment is for cash back earned October 1 to December 31, and the other three dates follow the same pattern. Rakuten's terms say it may change the schedule.

No Rakuten page names the next date for you. Its account FAQ says your confirmed balance is the amount in your next payment, and the payment page links to a "Track My Big Fat Check" tool behind sign-in. What that tool shows is not confirmed.

The minimum

Rakuten's own pages disagree by one cent. The help page says you need $5 or more in confirmed cash back. The terms say the minimum payout is $5.01. Either way, a smaller balance is not lost. It carries over to the next date.

Three stages before money moves

A new order is "processing" for 24 to 48 hours while Rakuten waits for the store to report it (see how long a purchase takes to show up). It is then "pending" while the store's return period runs. Once "confirmed", it is included in the next payment.

Type of purchase Typical time from pending to confirmed
Online and in-store shopping 3 to 14 weeks
Dining 3 to 5 days after you dine
Travel 6 to 17 weeks after the trip ends
Travel booked at Expedia Within one week after the trip ends

Rakuten's payment page gives a different travel figure: 45 to 120 days after booking. Its travel article and its confirmation article both say 6 to 17 weeks after the trip is complete. More on this in cash back on hotels and flights.

Why a purchase can take months

Two waits stack. First the store confirms, then the calendar decides.

Example Confirmed Paid
Online order on November 20, 2026, confirmed in 3 weeks About December 11, 2026 February 15, 2027
Same order, confirmed in 14 weeks About February 26, 2027 May 15, 2027
Hotel booked today for a stay ending March 15, 2027, confirmed 6 weeks after About April 26, 2027 May 15, 2027
Same stay, confirmed 17 weeks after About July 12, 2027 August 15, 2027

These dates are worked out from Rakuten's typical ranges; a store can be faster or slower. An ordinary online order takes roughly 3 to 6 months from checkout to payment. A trip booked well ahead can take most of a year.

How you are paid

You can be paid by PayPal, check, Bilt points, Samsung Cash or American Express Membership Rewards points. PayPal and check need a mailing address on file; Bilt and Membership Rewards do not.

Rakuten says the address is used to verify identity. You add it in Account Settings.

How many days a check or PayPal transfer takes to arrive after the payment date is not confirmed; Rakuten's page does not say.

Rakuten also says a confirmed balance of at least $5 can be redeemed for a gift card that arrives within minutes, subject to limits.

If you hold an eligible Amex card you can take points in place of cash on the same four dates. See whether Amex points are worth more than the cash.

Things that reduce or delay a payment

  • A return, exchange or cancellation. Rakuten deducts the cash back for it.
  • A purchase that never tracked. Rakuten's terms give 90 days from the purchase to report it. See what to do when cash back did not track.
  • Long inactivity. After 12 months with no shopping trip, the terms allow a $5 monthly maintenance fee taken from the balance.

Who should skip it

  • You need the rebate now. A lower price or a coupon at checkout is money today; Rakuten is money in a few months.
  • You shop online once or twice a year for small amounts. You may sit under the minimum for a long time.
  • You do not want to give a mailing address or link a PayPal account.

Quick answers

What dates does Rakuten pay? February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15.

What is the minimum to be paid? About $5 confirmed. One Rakuten page says $5, its terms say $5.01. Less carries over.

Why is my cash back still pending? The store's return period is running. Shopping typically takes 3 to 14 weeks; travel takes longer.
