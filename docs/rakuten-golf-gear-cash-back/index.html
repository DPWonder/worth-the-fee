Cash back is figured on the price of the goods. Rakuten says taxes and shipping never count.

On clubs, a raised rate can be real money, but only if that store's price is as low as you would pay elsewhere. A 2% rebate does not make up for a club that is $50 cheaper at another shop.

Exclusions that matter for golf

  • Gift cards. Most golf stores on Rakuten exclude buying gift cards, redeeming them, or both. Paying for a driver with a store gift card can mean no cash back.
  • Coupon codes. Several listings say a code not shown on Rakuten may void the cash back. If a brand's email code saves more than the rebate, use the code and skip the rebate.
  • Services. Dick's listing excludes services and extended warranties. Whether lessons, fittings or club repair earn at any golf store is not confirmed.
  • Trade-in credit. How it is treated at golf stores is not confirmed.
  • Phone and chat orders. Rakuten says orders placed by phone, chat or customer service do not qualify. A custom club order taken by phone falls here.
  • Returns and exchanges. Returned items lose their cash back. Clubs are often exchanged after a few rounds, so wait for the order to show as confirmed before counting on it.

Online only, as far as could be seen

These rates are for online orders that start at Rakuten. No golf retailer appeared in the part of Rakuten's in-store list that loaded on November 20, 2026, so in-store golf offers are not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.

When you get the money

Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See when Rakuten pays and what to do when cash back did not track.

Who should skip it

  • You buy clubs in a shop after a fitting. The online rate does not apply to that purchase.
  • You already have a coupon code, a member discount or a price match that beats 2% to 3%.
  • You pay with store gift cards or trade-in credit.
  • You buy golf gear once every few years and shop nowhere else through Rakuten. A payment needs about $5 confirmed.

Quick answers

Which golf stores are on Rakuten? On November 20, 2026: PGA TOUR Superstore, Golf Galaxy, Callaway, TaylorMade, Titleist, Rock Bottom Golf and Dick's Sporting Goods, among others.

What rate do golf stores pay? Mostly 2% to 3% that day. PGA TOUR Superstore showed 10%, up from a usual 2%.

Do gift card purchases earn cash back? Usually not. Most golf listings exclude gift cards.
