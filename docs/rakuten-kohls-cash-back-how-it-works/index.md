# Rakuten Kohl's cash back: does Rakuten work at Kohl's and with Kohl's Cash?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-kohls-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-27
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Kohl's: 2% on Black Friday, November 27, 2026, the same rate as on every check since November 24. Gift cards, Apple and Google products and the part of an order you pay for with Kohl's Cash earn no Rakuten cash back, and whether a Rakuten-tracked order still earns new Kohl's Cash is not confirmed by either company.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check Kohl's page on Rakuten on the day you shop. For this morning's headline rate here and at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate

| Date read | Rate on Rakuten's Kohl's page |
|---|---|
| November 24, 2026 | 2% |
| November 25, 2026 | 2% |
| November 26, 2026 | 2% |
| November 27, 2026 (Black Friday) | 2% |

On Black Friday Kohl's showed a single rate of 2%, with no category rates and no "was" label, so the rate was not raised for the day. Rakuten raises some store rates during sales, and its Black Friday page names Kohl's in its list of stores; see [Black Friday on Rakuten](/rakuten-black-friday-cash-back-2026/). Whether the rate changes for Cyber Monday, November 30, is not confirmed; see [Cyber Monday on Rakuten](/rakuten-cyber-monday-cash-back-2026/). Another department store, [Macy's](/rakuten-macys-cash-back-how-it-works/), was at 15% in this site's Black Friday reading.

The listing says cash back is added to the Rakuten account the next day. No in-store offer for Kohl's was shown, so this is about online orders.

## Kohl's Cash: the two halves

"Stacking" can mean two different things here.

**Spending Kohl's Cash.** Rakuten's exclusions for Kohl's include: "Part of your order paid for with Kohl's Cash." So the two do work on the same order, but Rakuten pays only on what is left.

| $100 order | Paid with Kohl's Cash | Earns Rakuten at 2% on | Cash back |
|---|---|---|---|
| No Kohl's Cash used | $0 | $100 | $2.00 |
| $10 Kohl's Cash used | $10 | $90 | $1.80 |
| $30 Kohl's Cash used | $30 | $70 | $1.40 |

**Earning Kohl's Cash.** Rakuten's page for Kohl's says shoppers receive $10 in Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent, online or in store, during promotional periods, and that it is spent like store credit on set redemption dates. Kohl's own FAQ could not be opened on November 25, 2026, and was not re-read on November 27. Kohl's Cash is issued by Kohl's at checkout, so nothing in Rakuten's terms stops it. A third-party guide says an order tracked by Rakuten still earns it. No Kohl's or Rakuten page read for this article states that, so treat it as likely but not confirmed.

## Coupons and Kohl's Rewards

Rakuten's page for Kohl's says up to four promo codes can be used on one online order, describes using them together with cash back, and says to activate before checking out. Its exclusion list for Kohl's does not mention coupons. On November 27 the page itself listed Kohl's codes, including a Black Friday code for an extra 20% off; a code shown on Rakuten is the safe kind.

Rakuten's general terms are more careful: a coupon not provided by Rakuten can cause a purchase to be credited elsewhere. If a code came from another coupon site or extension, that is the risk. The general rules are in [stacking with coupons and card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

Whether redeeming Kohl's Rewards lowers the amount Rakuten pays on is not stated on the page.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Kohl's, unchanged on November 27, 2026, exclude:

- Gift cards
- Amazon products
- Beats, JLab and Apple products (what earns at Apple's own store is in [Rakuten at Apple](/rakuten-apple-cash-back-how-it-works/))
- Google products
- Video game systems and gaming products
- Crocs
- BISSELL
- Jewelry with an original price of $2,000 or more
- Kohl's Cares merchandise
- The part of an order paid with Kohl's Cash
- Purchases made with Masterpass, Visa Checkout or Apple Pay

The last line is easy to miss. Paying with Apple Pay at checkout puts the order on the excluded list, so enter the card number the usual way. If an eligible order still shows nothing, see [what to do about missing cash back](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

Sephora at Kohl's is not on the exclusion list, and a Sephora sale at Kohl's was listed on the page at 2% on November 27. Whether a Sephora purchase earns Kohl's Cash is a separate Kohl's rule and is not confirmed. Sephora's own site is a different listing; see [Rakuten at Sephora](/rakuten-sephora-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Kohl's gift cards

Buying a gift card at Kohl's earns nothing. Rakuten separately listed Kohl's gift cards sold by Giftcards.com at 3% (labeled "was 1%") and by CardCash at 1% on November 25; that page was not re-read on November 27. Whether paying with one affects the Kohl's cash back is not stated in Kohl's terms on Rakuten. More in [gift cards and Rakuten](/rakuten-gift-cards-cash-back/).

## Who should skip it

- You pay most of the order with Kohl's Cash. Little is left to earn on.
- You check out with Apple Pay and do not want to switch.
- Your cart is mostly excluded brands, such as Apple, Google, Crocs or game consoles.
- You are using a coupon code from another rewards site or extension.
- You shop in the store. No in-store Kohl's offer was listed.

## Quick answers

**Does Rakuten work at Kohl's?**
Yes. Kohl's showed 2% on Black Friday, November 27, 2026, the same as earlier in the week. Rates change daily.

**Can you use Kohl's Cash and still get Rakuten cash back?**
Yes, on the part not paid with Kohl's Cash. The Kohl's Cash portion is excluded.

**Does Sephora at Kohl's earn Rakuten cash back?**
It was not on the exclusion list, and a Sephora sale at Kohl's was listed at 2% on November 27, 2026.
