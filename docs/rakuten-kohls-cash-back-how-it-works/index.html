Rakuten Kohl's cash back: does it work, and does it stack with Kohl's Cash?

Facts checked November 24, 20264 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Kohl's. It was listed at 2% on November 24, 2026. The part of an order you pay for with Kohl's Cash earns no Rakuten cash back, and whether a Rakuten-tracked order still earns new Kohl's Cash is not confirmed by either company.

The rate

Kohl's showed a single rate of 2%, with no category rates and no "was" label, so it looked like an ordinary day's rate. Rakuten raises store rates during sales; see Black Friday on Rakuten. Another department store, Macy's, showed a raised rate the same day.

The listing says cash back is added to the Rakuten account the next day. No in-store offer for Kohl's was shown, so this is about online orders.

Kohl's Cash: the two halves

"Stacking" can mean two different things here.

Spending Kohl's Cash. Rakuten's exclusions for Kohl's include: "Part of your order paid for with Kohl's Cash." So the two do work on the same order, but Rakuten pays only on what is left.

Earning Kohl's Cash. Kohl's says members earn $10 of Kohl's Cash for every $50 spent during an earn period, and that it can be redeemed only on the dates printed on the coupon. Rakuten's page for Kohl's describes it the same way. Kohl's Cash is issued by Kohl's at checkout, so nothing in Rakuten's terms stops it. A third-party guide says an order tracked by Rakuten still earns it. No Kohl's or Rakuten page read for this article states that, so treat it as likely but not confirmed.

Coupons and Kohl's Rewards

Rakuten's page for Kohl's says up to four promo codes can be used on one online order, describes using them together with cash back, and says to activate before checking out. Its exclusion list for Kohl's does not mention coupons.

Rakuten's general terms are more careful: a coupon not provided by Rakuten can cause a purchase to be credited elsewhere. If a code came from another coupon site or extension, that is the risk.

Whether redeeming Kohl's Rewards lowers the amount Rakuten pays on is not stated on the page.

What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Kohl's, as read on November 24, 2026, exclude:

  • Gift cards
  • Amazon products
  • Beats, JLab and Apple products (what earns at Apple's own store is in Rakuten at Apple)
  • Google products
  • Video game systems and gaming products
  • Crocs
  • BISSELL
  • Jewelry with an original price of $2,000 or more
  • Kohl's Cares merchandise
  • The part of an order paid with Kohl's Cash
  • Purchases made with Masterpass, Visa Checkout or Apple Pay

The last line is easy to miss. Paying with Apple Pay at checkout puts the whole order on the excluded list, so enter the card number the usual way.

Sephora at Kohl's is not on the exclusion list, and a Sephora sale at Kohl's was listed on the page at 2% that day. Kohl's own Kohl's Cash offer excludes Sephora purchases, which is a separate rule. Sephora's own site is a different listing; see Rakuten at Sephora.

Kohl's gift cards

Buying a gift card at Kohl's earns nothing. Rakuten separately listed Kohl's gift cards sold by Giftcards.com at 3% (labeled "was 1%") and by CardCash at 1% that day. Whether paying with one affects the Kohl's cash back is not stated in Kohl's terms on Rakuten. More in gift cards and Rakuten.

Who should skip it

  • You pay most of the order with Kohl's Cash. Little is left to earn on.
  • You check out with Apple Pay and do not want to switch.
  • Your cart is mostly excluded brands, such as Apple, Google, Crocs or game consoles.
  • You are using a coupon code from another rewards site or extension.
  • You shop in the store. No in-store Kohl's offer was listed.

Quick answers

Does Rakuten work at Kohl's? Yes. It showed 2% on November 24, 2026. Rates change daily.

Can you use Kohl's Cash and still get Rakuten cash back? Yes, on the part not paid with Kohl's Cash. The Kohl's Cash portion is excluded.

Does Sephora at Kohl's earn Rakuten cash back? It was not on the exclusion list, and a Sephora sale at Kohl's was listed at 2% that day.
