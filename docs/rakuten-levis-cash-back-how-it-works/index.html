Rakuten Levi's cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

The short answer

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Levi's. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Levi's page showed 10% cash back, marked "was 2%". Gift cards, collaboration collections with other brands, bulk or reseller orders and the part of an order paid with rewards earn nothing.

A "was" label means the rate has been raised above the store's usual rate. Here the usual rate is 2%. The page does not say when 10% ends. Nothing on the page promises the rate for Black Friday or Cyber Monday.

What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Levi's list these as earning nothing:

Earns nothing Note
Gift cards Both buying them and paying with them
The part paid with rewards The terms do not name the rewards
Collaboration collections Products made with other brands
Bulk and reseller orders No quantity is given

The terms do not list which collaborations are covered, and they do not say how many items make an order "bulk". Both are not confirmed.

The gift card line is wider than at many stores. It covers the redemption of gift cards as well as the purchase, so the part of an order paid with a Levi's gift card earns nothing.

Coupon codes and the welcome offer

The terms say that using coupon codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back, and to keep to codes Rakuten provides.

Rakuten's page describes several store discounts that come as codes from outside Rakuten:

  • A welcome offer for new email subscribers, described as 20% off with free shipping
  • 15% off for teachers, military members, first responders and students, verified through SheerID
  • Member codes from Red Tab, the store's free loyalty program

Under the terms, each of these carries the risk of voiding cash back. Whether any of them is treated as safe is not confirmed.

Red Tab

The page describes Red Tab as free to join. The cash back terms exclude the part of an order paid with rewards. They do not name Red Tab. Whether Red Tab rewards are the ones meant is not confirmed, though no other rewards program is described on the page.

SecondHand, pickup and stores

  • Levi's SecondHand. The page describes the resale section of the store's website. The cash back terms do not mention it. Not confirmed.
  • Pickup. The page says some stores offer in-store pickup for online orders. The terms do not mention pickup. Not confirmed.
  • In stores. The page showed an online rate only. No in-store offer was shown on it. See how in-store cash back works.
  • Returns. Rakuten's general help page says returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back.

When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added within one week. Payment follows every three months; see when Rakuten pays. If nothing appears, see what to do when cash back did not track.

Who should skip it

  • You have a store code that is not on Rakuten and is worth more than the cash back.
  • You are paying with a gift card or with rewards.
  • You are buying from a collaboration collection.
  • You are placing a bulk order.
  • You are counting on 10% later. The usual rate shown is 2%.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Levi's? 10% on November 25, 2026, shown as raised from 2%. Rates change daily.

Can you use the Levi's welcome code and still get cash back? Not safely. The terms say codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.

Does paying with a Levi's gift card earn cash back? No. The terms exclude both the purchase and the redemption of gift cards.
