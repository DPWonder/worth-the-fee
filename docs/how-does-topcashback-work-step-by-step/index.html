The short answer

TopCashback works in three steps: find the store on TopCashback, click the pink "Get Cash Back" button, and buy on the store's site as usual. Its Getting Started page says the purchase shows in your account within about seven days, and takes one to three months on average to become money you can withdraw.

The three steps

From TopCashback's Getting Started page, read on November 25, 2026:

  1. Search. Log in and find the store or service. The page says cash back is offered at more than 7,000 stores.
  2. Select. Click the pink "Get Cash Back" button. The store's website opens in a new tab.
  3. Buy. Shop in that new tab as you normally would. The value of the purchase is recorded in your account.

Behind those steps, the help page explains, the click tells the store that TopCashback sent you. The store pays TopCashback a commission, and TopCashback passes it to you. Tracking is done with cookies and happens in the background; nothing about cash back appears on the store's own pages.

All of the figures are TopCashback's own. The one-to-three-month step is an average; each store sets its own period, and travel bookings wait until the trip is over.

Set up payment details early

One detail on the Getting Started page is easy to miss: after you add new payment details, TopCashback disables payments for 72 hours as a security measure. Its tip is to add your details before you are ready to cash out, so the hold has passed by the time you need it.

Payment methods listed are PayPal, Venmo, bank transfer and more than 30 gift cards. More on each is on how TopCashback pays.

Coupons: the rule that catches people

The Getting Started page says only coupon codes "listed and authorized by TopCashback" work together with cash back. They are in the Special Deals and Coupons section of each store's page on TopCashback. A code from another site can cancel the cash back for that order.

What can go wrong

TopCashback's help page lists the usual reasons a purchase does not track:

  • A coupon code TopCashback did not list.
  • An ad blocker.
  • Buying in a different tab from the one that opened after the click.
  • Clicking another cash back or coupon site afterward.

If a purchase has not shown after seven days, the Getting Started page says to contact customer service.

How much is realistic

TopCashback's help pages say members earn $450 a year on average, and that those members shop through the site at least twice a month. The figure is the company's own and how it is calculated is not stated. What one person earns depends on what they buy and each store's rate, and rates change. A person who shops online a few times a year should expect far less than the average.

Cash back is a share of money already spent. Buying something extra to earn it costs more than it returns.

Who should skip it

  • You want the money within days. The store's confirmation takes one to three months on average.
  • You rely on coupon codes from other sites. They can cancel the cash back.
  • You keep an ad blocker on for every site.
  • You shop online rarely. The amounts will be small.

Quick answers

How long does TopCashback take to show a purchase? Up to seven days to appear as "In Progress," by its Getting Started page.

How long until TopCashback cash back is available? One to three months on average after it shows, because the store has to confirm the sale and pay TopCashback first.

Can I use a coupon code with TopCashback? Only codes listed on TopCashback's own page for that store. Other codes can cancel the cash back.
