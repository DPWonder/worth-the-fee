How does TopCashback pay you, and when can you cash out?

The short answer

TopCashback pays on request, not on fixed dates: once cash back is marked "available" you can send it to a bank account, Venmo or PayPal, or take a gift card. How long a purchase takes to become available is set by each store, and travel is not available until the stay is finished.

The stages a purchase goes through

Stage What it means What TopCashback's help pages say
Not showing yet The store has not reported the sale Can take a week or longer
Tracked The purchase appears on your Earnings page An estimated date is shown; it is only an estimate
Available The store has validated the sale You can cash out at any time
Paid You asked for the money You get one email to confirm and another when the payment is available

The wait between tracked and available is the long part. The help page says every purchase goes through a validation period, and that each store has a different one. Returns and cancellations are the reason stores wait.

Ways to be paid

As listed on TopCashback's help page on November 25, 2026:

  • Transfer to a bank account
  • Venmo
  • PayPal
  • A gift card. The page names Amazon, Starbucks, Target and Sephora as examples.

A minimum to cash out is not stated on the help pages read for this page. The Points Guy reports there is none for cash methods. How many days a bank or PayPal transfer takes after you request it is not confirmed.

How to cash out

The help page gives these steps:

  1. Open the Cash Out page.
  2. Choose a payment method.
  3. On the Payment Breakdown screen, choose how much to cash out. You can give part of it to a charity here.
  4. Confirm by answering your security question.

When a purchase is missing

TopCashback lists five common reasons a purchase did not track:

  1. A coupon code that was not listed on TopCashback.
  2. An ad blocker.
  3. Buying in a different tab from the one TopCashback opened.
  4. Clicking another cash back or coupon site afterward, so TopCashback was not the last click.
  5. Not enough time has passed.

If none of those apply and enough time has gone by, the help page says to submit a Missing Cash Back Claim so it can investigate. If cash back has tracked but has stayed short of available for longer than the estimate, it says to open a support ticket. How long either takes is not confirmed.

How this differs from Rakuten

Rakuten sends payments on four set dates a year and needs about $5 confirmed first; see when Rakuten pays. TopCashback has no payment dates: the money moves when you ask, once the store has validated the sale. Neither site lets you skip the store's validation wait. The full comparison is on Rakuten or TopCashback.

Who should skip it

  • You want money quickly after a purchase. On any cash back site the store's validation period comes first, and for travel it starts after the trip.
  • You use coupon codes found elsewhere. A code not listed on TopCashback is the first reason on its list for cash back not tracking.
  • You will not turn off an ad blocker for a purchase.
  • You shop rarely and for small amounts, so the sums stay small.

Whether the site itself can be trusted is covered on is TopCashback legit.

Quick answers

Does TopCashback have a payout schedule? No. Once cash back is marked available you can cash out at any time.

Can TopCashback pay to PayPal or Venmo? Yes. Its help page lists bank transfer, Venmo, PayPal and gift cards.

Why is my TopCashback not available yet? Each store has its own validation period. Travel becomes available only after the stay is complete.
