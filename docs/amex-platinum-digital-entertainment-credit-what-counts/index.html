Which subscriptions count for the Amex Platinum digital entertainment credit?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

The credit covers Disney+ (and Disney+ bundles), Hulu, ESPN streaming, Paramount+, standalone Peacock, YouTube Premium, YouTube TV, The New York Times and The Wall Street Journal, when you pay the provider directly with an enrolled Platinum Card. Netflix, Spotify, Apple TV and Amazon Prime Video are not on the list.

How the credit works

  • Up to $25 back each month, which is up to $300 a year.
  • You must enroll once in your Amex account. Charges before enrollment do not count.
  • An unused month is lost. If your eligible subscriptions cost $12 a month, you get $12, not $25.
  • Statement credits can take up to 8 weeks, though AwardWallet reports they usually post sooner.
  • There is one credit per card account. An additional card does not add a second $25.

The list is fixed by Amex. A streaming service that is not named earns nothing, however similar it is.

The billing rule that catches people

The charge must come straight from the provider. These do not count, even for a service on the list:

  • A subscription billed through a third party, such as an app store, a cable company or another streaming device's billing.
  • A subscription that is part of a cable bundle.
  • Gift cards.
  • Third-party promotional offers.

If you already pay for Disney+ or Paramount+ through an app store, you would need to cancel there and sign up again on the provider's own website with the Platinum Card for the credit to apply.

The Peacock change

Until July 31, 2026, some cardholders used a Peacock bundle to get a second service, such as Apple TV, covered by the credit. From August 1, 2026, Peacock bundles, Peacock add-ons and Peacock bought through a third party no longer count. A standalone Peacock plan bought at peacocktv.com still does.

Annual plans

How an annual subscription is credited is not confirmed. The only limit the sources state is $25 per month, which suggests a single yearly charge would earn $25 once. If you want the full credit, a monthly plan is the safer assumption.

One overlap to know about: the card's separate Walmart+ credit pays for a Walmart+ membership, and AwardWallet notes that Walmart+ includes a choice of Paramount+ or Peacock. If you use that, spend the $25 on something else.

Who should skip it

Do not count this credit toward the $895 annual fee if:

  • Your subscriptions are Netflix, Spotify, Apple or Amazon. None qualify.
  • You get streaming through a cable or phone bundle and do not want to change it.
  • You would sign up for a service only because the credit pays for it.

Quick answers

Does Netflix count for the Amex Platinum digital entertainment credit? No. Netflix is not on the list of eligible services.

Does a subscription billed through an app store count? No. The charge must come directly from the provider, such as disneyplus.com or peacocktv.com.

Do Peacock bundles still count? No. Since August 1, 2026, only a standalone Peacock plan bought directly from Peacock counts.
