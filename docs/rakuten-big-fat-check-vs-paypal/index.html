Rakuten payout: check or PayPal, and what are the dates and minimum?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

A check and a PayPal payment are for the same amount, on the same four dates (February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15), with the same minimum of about $5 confirmed. The difference is handling: a paper check comes by mail and is good for 180 days, while PayPal needs a valid PayPal email address in your Rakuten account.

Rakuten's help pages do not say how many days a check takes in the mail or how fast a PayPal payment lands. They also do not give a deadline for changing your method before a payment date.

The dates and the minimum

Only cash back the store has confirmed before a payment date is included. Anything under the minimum carries over. Rakuten's help page says $5 or more; its terms say $5.01. The next date after today is February 15, 2027.

The waits before a purchase counts are covered in when Rakuten pays.

How a check works

  • It is made out to the name on the account and mailed to the address on file. A wrong or old address is the usual reason a check goes missing.
  • It is valid for 90 days. Rakuten adds a 90-day grace period, so it can be deposited for 180 days from the issue date.
  • Many banks accept it by mobile deposit. Rakuten's instructions are to detach the top part, endorse the check, and photograph the front and back in your bank's app.
  • If the check is lost, destroyed or expired, Rakuten says it will issue a replacement. You contact Member Services.

Rakuten's terms add a caution. If a check expires or is returned uncashed, the amount goes back into your Rakuten account. There it may be subject to the inactivity maintenance fee or to state unclaimed property law.

How PayPal works

  • Rakuten's terms say you must provide a valid PayPal email address.
  • You set or change it in Account Settings on the website. In the app it is under Account, then Choose How You Get Paid.
  • The payment goes to the PayPal account tied to that email, so the email in Rakuten should match one on your PayPal account.

How to choose or change

  1. Sign in to Rakuten and open Account Settings.
  2. Find the section on how you are paid.
  3. Pick check or PayPal, and add a mailing address if none is on file.

Other ways to be paid

Rakuten also offers Bilt points, Samsung Cash, a gift card redeemed on request from a $5 confirmed balance, and American Express Membership Rewards points. For the points option, see Amex points instead of cash.

Which fits whom

Who should skip it

  • Skip the check if your address on file may be out of date. Update it first.
  • Skip PayPal if the email on your Rakuten account is not one linked to your PayPal account.
  • Skip both if you want the money sooner than the next quarterly date. Rakuten's gift card option is the only one it describes as arriving within minutes.

Quick answers

How long is a Rakuten check valid? 90 days, plus a 90-day grace period: 180 days from the issue date.

What if my Rakuten check never arrives? Rakuten says it will issue a replacement. Contact Member Services and check the address on file.

Is the minimum different for check and PayPal? No. Both need about $5 in confirmed cash back.
