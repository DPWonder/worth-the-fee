How to score a credit

  • All of it: you already pay for this, in the pieces the credit comes in.
  • Half: you would use it sometimes, or would pay less for it yourself.
  • None: you would not buy this without the credit.

What it leaves out

Lounges, hotel status, travel insurance, points earned and any welcome offer are not counted, because their value depends on how you travel. If the result is below zero, the gap is what you would pay each year for those. This page is information, not financial advice.
