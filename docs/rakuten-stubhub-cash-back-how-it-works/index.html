Rakuten StubHub cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

The short answer

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at StubHub. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's StubHub page showed 7% cash back, marked "was 2%". Buying or redeeming gift cards and orders completed by ticket brokers earn nothing, and the terms say a coupon code not listed on Rakuten will void cash back.

A "was" label appears when a rate has been raised above the store's usual rate. Here it points to 2% as the usual figure. The page does not say when the 7% ends. No category table appears, so sports, concerts, theater and festivals are not given separate rates.

What earns and what does not

Purchase Earns cash back?
Tickets bought on StubHub after a Rakuten click Yes, 7% on the day checked
Buying a StubHub gift card No
An order paid by redeeming a gift card No
Orders completed by ticket brokers No
An order using a code not listed on Rakuten No; the terms say it will void cash back

The terms do not say how a buyer is judged to be a ticket broker. Not confirmed.

The terms also do not say whether cash back is figured on the ticket price alone or on the total including StubHub's fees. Not confirmed.

Coupon codes: the firm wording

Many store pages say outside codes "may void" cash back. StubHub's terms say use of codes not listed on Rakuten "will void" it. On November 25, 2026, the offers on Rakuten's StubHub page were links to ticket categories, such as music festivals, MLS and Formula 1. No code was listed.

You wait until after the event

This is the main difference from a store purchase. The special terms say cash back is added to the Rakuten account within a week after the event.

The posting line on the same page reads slightly differently: within a week after "travel" has been completed. StubHub sells tickets, not travel, so the two lines appear to mean the same thing: the clock starts at the event date, not the purchase date.

Ticket bought Event date Earliest cash back would show

After that, payment follows Rakuten's usual schedule; see when Rakuten pays.

Canceled events and resold tickets

Rakuten's page describes StubHub's FanProtect Guarantee: if an event is canceled and not rescheduled, StubHub gives a refund or a credit toward a later purchase. The cash back terms do not cover that case. Rakuten's help page says a canceled or returned order is no longer eligible. Whether an order paid with a StubHub credit earns is not confirmed.

Whether cash back stays in place when the buyer later resells the ticket on StubHub is not stated. Not confirmed.

App and stores

  • App. The terms do not mention the StubHub app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension. Whether an order finished in the StubHub app tracks is not confirmed.
  • In stores. StubHub is an online marketplace, and the page showed an online rate only.

Who should skip it

  • You have a StubHub code from outside Rakuten. Using it voids the cash back.
  • You are buying or paying with a gift card.
  • You buy tickets as a broker.
  • The event is many months away and you want the money soon.
  • You tend to finish the purchase in another tab or app. Tracking is tied to the tab Rakuten opened.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at StubHub? 7% on November 25, 2026, marked "was 2%". Rates change daily.

When does StubHub cash back show up? Within a week after the event, according to the terms. Not at purchase.

Can you use a StubHub promo code and still get cash back? Only a code listed on Rakuten. The terms say other codes will void cash back.
