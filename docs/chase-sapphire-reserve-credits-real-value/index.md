# What are the Sapphire Reserve credits really worth if you only count the ones you would use anyway?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/chase-sapphire-reserve-credits-real-value/
Facts checked: 2026-11-22
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

At face value the Sapphire Reserve's recurring credits add up to about $2,384 a year against a $795 fee, before counting the DashPass membership and the Global Entry credit. Count only what you would have paid for anyway and most people land far lower, often between $300 and $900, so the card pays for itself only for those who already spend in several of its categories.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## Every credit, with amount and cadence

| Credit | Amount | Cadence | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Travel credit | Up to $300 | Each account anniversary year | Automatic on travel purchases; those purchases earn no points |
| The Edit hotels | Up to $500 | Calendar year, $250 per booking | Prepaid stays of two nights or more at hotels in Chase's The Edit collection |
| Dining | Up to $300 | $150 January to June, $150 July to December | Only restaurants in Sapphire Reserve Exclusive Tables on OpenTable |
| StubHub and viagogo | Up to $300 | $150 per half-year, through 12/31/2027 | Activation needed; no gift cards |
| DoorDash promos | Up to $420 | $35 a month, through 12/31/2029 | $15 off one restaurant order, two $10 off non-restaurant orders; unused promos expire at month end |
| DashPass | Chase's stated value $120 | 12 months | Activate by 12/31/2029 |
| Lyft | Up to $120 | $10 a month, through 9/30/2027 | Rides only; not tips, taxes or fees |
| Peloton | Up to $120 | $10 a month, through 12/31/2027 | Eligible memberships; activation needed |
| Apple TV and Apple Music | Chase's stated value $324 | Yearly | Activate by 6/22/2027 |
| Global Entry, TSA PreCheck or NEXUS | Up to $120 | Once every four years | About $30 a year |

Several reports also describe a one-time 2026 credit of up to $250 for a prepaid two-night stay at selected hotel brands in Chase Travel. It is not on Chase's public card page today, so treat the details as not confirmed and check your account.

## Why face value is the wrong number

A credit is worth its full amount only if it replaces money you were going to spend anyway. Three things shrink it:

- **Narrow lists.** The dining credit works at a set list of restaurants, not at any restaurant. The Edit credit works at a set collection of hotels, and needs a prepaid stay of two nights.
- **Use it or lose it.** The half-year and monthly credits do not roll over. A missed month of Lyft or DoorDash is gone.
- **Spending you would not have done.** A hotel in The Edit can cost more than the hotel you would have picked.

## Add up your own number

Go down the list and write a dollar figure next to each line. Use last year's real spending, not what you hope to do.

1. **Travel credit.** If you spend $300 a year on any travel, write $300. Most cardholders can.
2. **The Edit.** Write $250 for each two-night prepaid stay you would book at one of these hotels at the price Chase shows. If you are not sure, write $0.
3. **Dining.** Check the Exclusive Tables list for your city. Write $150 per half-year only if you would eat at one of those restaurants anyway.
4. **StubHub.** Write what you spent on resale tickets last year, up to $150 per half.
5. **DoorDash.** Write $15 for each month you already order restaurant delivery, and $10 or $20 for each month you already order groceries or convenience items there.
6. **Lyft and Peloton.** Write $10 for each month you already pay for them.
7. **Apple.** Write what you pay now for Apple TV and Apple Music. If you pay for neither, write $0.
8. **Global Entry.** Write $30.

Add the lines up and subtract $795.

## Two sample totals

These are illustrations, not typical results.

| Reader | What they count | Total | Against $795 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Uses travel credit only | $300 | $300 | Short by $495 |
| Adds Apple subscriptions, Lyft ten months, one Edit stay | $300 + $324 + $100 + $250 | $974 | Ahead by $179 |

Points and [airport lounge access](/chase-sapphire-reserve-lounge-access/) are separate from this sum. Count them only if you would use them.

## Who should skip it

- Anyone whose honest total is under $795 and who does not value the lounges or the higher earning rates enough to cover the gap.
- Anyone who does not want to track monthly and half-year deadlines.
- Anyone who carries a balance. Interest will outweigh any credit.
- Light travelers. The [Sapphire Preferred at $95](/sapphire-preferred-vs-reserve-light-traveler/) asks for much less.

## Quick answers

**Does the $300 travel credit alone justify the fee?**
No. It brings the cost down to $495. The remaining credits, points and lounges have to cover that.

**Do the credits roll over if I miss one?**
No. Monthly and half-year credits expire at the end of each period.

**Are all the credits permanent?**
No. Lyft ends 9/30/2027, StubHub and Peloton end 12/31/2027, and the DoorDash benefits run through 12/31/2029 unless Chase extends them.
