Does credit card travel insurance cover a golf trip?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Partly. The Sapphire cards and the Amex Platinum can pay when the trip itself is canceled, cut short or delayed, and when checked clubs are lost or damaged, up to set limits. No source read shows any of them paying for a round that is rained out while the trip goes ahead.

Rained-out rounds

Both issuers' trip cancellation cover is about the trip, not one activity inside it. Weather counts when it stops you from traveling or forces you home. No page read lists green fees or tee times as a covered loss.

That is "not confirmed" in both directions, and the full Chase guide was not read. If prepaid golf is a large part of the cost, ask the benefits administrator first.

Lost or damaged clubs

Chase Sapphire. Chase's summary gives up to $3,000 per traveler for bags that are lost, damaged or stolen. The cover is secondary: you claim from the airline first. Sources disagree on any lower limit for clubs. AwardWallet lists $500 for valuables such as jewelry and electronics and does not mention sporting equipment. Another comparison shows $1,000 for jewelry, sporting equipment or electronics. Treat the limit for clubs as not confirmed.

Amex Platinum. Amex's Baggage Insurance Plan guide, revised April 19, 2024, lists sporting equipment as a high-risk item. The guide says: "Any High Risk Items are subject to $1,000 maximum per Covered Person." Total loss on one trip is capped at $3,000 per person. New York residents have different limits.

If a set of clubs is worth more than $1,000, the gap is yours on the Platinum Card.

Delta and Southwest carry a soft golf bag only under a limited release of liability. Whether a card pays after the airline refuses on those grounds is not confirmed.

Late clubs and canceled flights

Chase's baggage delay benefit repays essential purchases. Sources give clothing and toiletries as examples. Whether rental clubs count is not confirmed.

Trip delay cover repays meals and a hotel. No source shows it paying for a missed round.

Who should skip relying on it

  • Anyone whose clubs are worth far more than $1,000. Card limits may not cover them. Other insurance was not checked for this page.
  • Golfers with large prepaid, non-refundable golf packages. Card cover for the golf itself is not confirmed.
  • Travelers who booked with airline miles and hold only the Amex Platinum.

Fees for checking the clubs are in flying with golf clubs.

Quick answers

Does card insurance refund green fees if it rains? No source read shows that. The cover is for a canceled, interrupted or delayed trip.

Are golf clubs covered if the airline loses them? Yes, within limits. Amex caps sporting equipment at $1,000 per person. The Chase limit for clubs is not confirmed.

Which card covers clubs that arrive late? The Sapphire cards list baggage delay cover after 6 hours. None was found in Amex's baggage guide.
