# Does credit card travel insurance cover a golf trip?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/golf-trip-travel-insurance-credit-card/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Partly. The Sapphire cards and the Amex Platinum can pay when the trip itself is canceled, cut short or delayed, and when checked clubs are lost or damaged, up to set limits. No source read shows any of them paying for a round that is rained out while the trip goes ahead.

This page is information, not financial advice.

Each card's Guide to Benefits is the binding document. Read the current one before a trip.

## What each card covers

| Problem | Sapphire Preferred / Reserve | Amex Platinum |
|---|---|---|
| Rained-out round, lost green fees | Not confirmed as covered. Chase's summary covers a trip canceled or cut short by sickness, severe weather or other covered situations | Not confirmed as covered. Amex's guide covers a trip canceled or interrupted by weather that stops a prudent person from traveling |
| Clubs lost or damaged by the airline | Up to $3,000 per traveler. A lower limit for sporting equipment is not confirmed | Up to $2,000 per person for checked bags, but sporting equipment is capped at $1,000 per person |
| Clubs arrive late | Up to $100 a day for 5 days once bags are more than 6 hours late, for essential purchases | No baggage delay benefit found in Amex's baggage guide |
| Flight canceled or badly delayed | Up to $500 per traveler after more than 12 hours (Preferred) or 6 hours (Reserve), or an overnight stay | Up to $500 per trip after more than 6 hours; 2 claims per 12 months |
| Whole trip canceled for a covered reason | Up to $10,000 per traveler, $20,000 per trip | Up to $10,000 per trip, $20,000 per card per 12 months |

## Rained-out rounds

Both issuers' trip cancellation cover is about the trip, not one activity inside it. Weather counts when it stops you from traveling or forces you home. No page read lists green fees or tee times as a covered loss.

That is "not confirmed" in both directions, and the full Chase guide was not read. If prepaid golf is a large part of the cost, ask the benefits administrator first.

## Lost or damaged clubs

**Chase Sapphire.** Chase's summary gives up to $3,000 per traveler for bags that are lost, damaged or stolen. The cover is secondary: you claim from the airline first. Sources disagree on any lower limit for clubs. AwardWallet lists $500 for valuables such as jewelry and electronics and does not mention sporting equipment. Another comparison shows $1,000 for jewelry, sporting equipment or electronics. Treat the limit for clubs as not confirmed.

**Amex Platinum.** Amex's Baggage Insurance Plan guide, revised April 19, 2024, lists sporting equipment as a high-risk item. The guide says: "Any High Risk Items are subject to $1,000 maximum per Covered Person." Total loss on one trip is capped at $3,000 per person. New York residents have different limits.

If a set of clubs is worth more than $1,000, the gap is yours on the Platinum Card.

Delta and Southwest carry a soft golf bag only under a limited release of liability. Whether a card pays after the airline refuses on those grounds is not confirmed.

## How you paid matters

| Card | What must be charged to the card |
|---|---|
| Sapphire cards | The trip booked with the card, through Chase Travel, or with Chase points. One writer reports an award flight qualified when the taxes and fees were paid with the card |
| Amex Platinum, baggage | The entire fare. Pay with Points bookings count. Tickets paid with airline frequent flyer miles do not |
| Amex Platinum, trip cancellation | Round-trip travel with the full transportation cost on the card. Sources disagree on award tickets |

## Late clubs and canceled flights

Chase's baggage delay benefit repays essential purchases. Sources give clothing and toiletries as examples. Whether rental clubs count is not confirmed.

Trip delay cover repays meals and a hotel. No source shows it paying for a missed round.

## Who should skip relying on it

- Anyone whose clubs are worth far more than $1,000. Card limits may not cover them. Other insurance was not checked for this page.
- Golfers with large prepaid, non-refundable golf packages. Card cover for the golf itself is not confirmed.
- Travelers who booked with airline miles and hold only the Amex Platinum.

Fees for checking the clubs are in [flying with golf clubs](/flying-with-golf-clubs-airline-fees/).

## Quick answers

**Does card insurance refund green fees if it rains?**
No source read shows that. The cover is for a canceled, interrupted or delayed trip.

**Are golf clubs covered if the airline loses them?**
Yes, within limits. Amex caps sporting equipment at $1,000 per person. The Chase limit for clubs is not confirmed.

**Which card covers clubs that arrive late?**
The Sapphire cards list baggage delay cover after 6 hours. None was found in Amex's baggage guide.
