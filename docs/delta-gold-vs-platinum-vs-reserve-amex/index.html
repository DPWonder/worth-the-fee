Delta SkyMiles Gold, Platinum or Reserve Amex: which one fits someone who flies Delta 4 to 8 times a year?

Facts checked November 20, 20265 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

At 4 to 8 Delta flights a year, the Gold card ($150 after a free first year) fits most people who check a bag, because the bag benefit is the same on all three cards. The Platinum ($350) makes sense mainly if you will use its yearly companion certificate, and the Reserve ($650) mainly if you want Delta Sky Club visits.

All three give 15% off award flights on Delta and cover the cardmember plus up to eight companions on the same reservation for bags.

What 4 to 8 flights is worth in bags

Delta's first checked bag costs $45 each way on domestic and short-haul international routes for tickets bought on or after April 8, 2026.

That saving is identical on Gold, Platinum and Reserve. If bags are the reason for the card, the extra fee on the higher cards buys nothing more. The Gold bag sums are worked out here.

When the Platinum's extra $200 pays off

The Platinum costs $200 a year more than the Gold. What it adds:

  • A companion certificate each year after renewal. You buy one adult round trip in Delta Main and the second traveler pays only taxes and fees, which run $22 to $250. Basic fares are excluded, and The Points Guy reports it books only into certain fare classes, so not every flight qualifies.
  • Up to $120 a year at US Resy restaurants and up to $120 a year on rideshare, both paid monthly.
  • $50 more Delta Stays credit, and a Global Entry or TSA PreCheck fee credit every four years.

If you take one round trip a year with another person and the second ticket would cost more than about $200 plus the taxes, the certificate covers the fee gap. The certificate arrives after the first renewal, so the first year does not include one.

Sources do not agree on where the Platinum certificate works. The Points Guy says all 50 states plus the Caribbean, Central America and Mexico. One Mile at a Time says the lower 48 states plus select Caribbean and Central American routes. Delta's own June 2026 release says domestic, Caribbean and Central American flights. Check the certificate terms before planning a Hawaii or Alaska trip around it.

When the Reserve's $650 pays off

The Reserve adds 15 Delta Sky Club visits per Medallion Year when you fly Delta, Centurion Lounge access when the Delta flight is booked with the card, a companion certificate that also works in Delta First, Premium Select and Comfort, and up to $240 a year at Resy restaurants.

At 4 to 8 flights a year you would use at most 8 of the 15 lounge visits, fewer if a round trip has no time for a lounge. The fee is $300 more than the Platinum. Lounge visits, the wider certificate and the larger restaurant credit together would need to be worth that to you.

A plain way to choose

  • You fly alone or mostly check bags: Gold.
  • You take at least one paid round trip a year with a companion and would use restaurant or rideshare credits monthly: Platinum.
  • You want a lounge on most trips and will use a First or Comfort companion ticket: Reserve.

Who should skip all three

  • People who fly Delta once a year or less.
  • People who never check a bag and travel alone.
  • Medallion members and first class passengers, who already get free bags.
  • People who want points that work on many airlines. These cards earn Delta miles only.

Welcome offers vary and change. Having or having had a higher Delta card can make you ineligible for a lower card's offer. See the once per lifetime rule.

Quick answers

Is the free checked bag different on the three cards? No. All three give a free first checked bag on Delta flights and a free second bag on domestic Delta flights.

Does the Gold card include lounge access or a companion certificate? No. Sky Club visits come only with the Reserve, and the companion certificate only with the Platinum and Reserve.

Do I get a companion certificate in the first year? No. Delta says the certificate comes each year after the card renews.
