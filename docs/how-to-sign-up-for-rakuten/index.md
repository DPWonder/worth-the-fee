# How do you sign up for Rakuten, and what does it ask for?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/how-to-sign-up-for-rakuten/
Facts checked: 2026-11-26
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

To sign up for Rakuten you give an email address and a password, or you join with a Facebook, Google or Apple account. No payment card or bank account is needed to join; a mailing address and a payment choice are needed later, before Rakuten will pay you.

This page is information, not financial advice. It covers only the joining step, as read on Rakuten's own pages on November 26, 2026. What happens after that is on [how Rakuten works, step by step](/how-does-rakuten-work-step-by-step/), and the costs are on [is Rakuten free](/is-rakuten-free/).

## What the sign-up asks for

| Item | Needed to join? | What Rakuten's pages say |
|---|---|---|
| Email and password | Yes, unless you use a social sign-in | The terms name an email address and password as what creates the account |
| Facebook, Google or Apple account | Optional | The join article lists all three as other ways to join |
| Credit or debit card | No | No card is named as a condition of membership |
| Bank account | No | Not asked for; payment goes by PayPal, check or another method you pick later |
| Mailing address | Not to join; yes to be paid | The terms list a physical address as a condition of payment |
| Friend's email | Optional | Only if a member referred you and you did not use that member's link |
| Name or phone number on the join form | Not confirmed | The help pages read do not list these fields |

A card comes up only if you later choose to link one for in-store offers. That is a separate, optional step.

## Who can join

Rakuten's terms set three limits:

- **Age:** at least 18 years old.
- **Place:** the service is for residents of the 50 United States or Washington D.C. People living elsewhere are told not to use it. Rakuten runs separate sites for some other countries, such as Canada.
- **One account:** each person is limited to one account. The referral terms add that several accounts with the same name, address or email may be flagged.

## The page you join from matters

Rakuten gives a new member one joining bonus, tied to how the account was opened. Join from Rakuten's own pages and the welcome bonus shown there applies. Join through a member's link, or pick "Did someone refer you?" at the bottom of the join form and enter that member's Rakuten email, and the referral bonus applies instead. The join article describes the referral choice only on the join form; adding a referrer after the account exists is not confirmed.

Bonus amounts vary and change, and the two cannot be combined. The comparison is on [welcome bonus or referral bonus](/rakuten-welcome-bonus-vs-referral-bonus/).

## What has to happen after you join

Opening the account does not pay a bonus by itself. Rakuten's new member FAQ lists three steps:

1. Sign up for a new account.
2. Shop through Rakuten and make a qualifying purchase of at least $25 within 90 days of becoming a member.
3. Choose how you want to be paid in Account Settings, with a mailing address on file.

That $25 figure is the welcome bonus requirement as read on November 26, 2026. A referral offer sets its own spending requirement, and either can change. The rules on what counts as a qualifying purchase, and what makes a bonus fail, are on [how the sign-up and referral bonuses work](/rakuten-50-bonus-how-it-works/).

## Choosing cash or Amex points

Every account earns cash back unless you change it. Rakuten lists these ways to be paid: PayPal, check, Bilt Points, Samsung Cash, and Membership Rewards points for eligible American Express card members. The choice is made in Account Settings on the website, or under Account, then Choose How You Get Paid, in the app. Rakuten says it can be updated at any time.

| Choice | What you provide | Minimum to be paid |
|---|---|---|
| PayPal | A valid PayPal email, used on only one Rakuten account | $5 on the help page; $5.01 in the terms |
| Check | The name and mailing address in Account Settings | Same |
| Amex points | The username and password of a US Membership Rewards account | 501 confirmed points |

For points, Rakuten's FAQ says a new member is prompted during sign-up to connect a Membership Rewards account. The card must be enrolled in the US Membership Rewards program and not canceled or past due. Corporate cards and the Rakuten American Express Card are not eligible. Whether a switch to points can be undone is disputed among outside sources; see [how to switch to Amex points](/rakuten-how-to-switch-to-amex-points/) and [taking the sign-up bonus as points](/rakuten-sign-up-bonus-as-amex-points/).

## Who should skip it

- Anyone under 18, or living outside the 50 states and Washington D.C.
- Anyone who already has a Rakuten account, including an old Ebates one. The terms allow one account per person, and the joining bonuses are for new members.
- Anyone who does not want to give a mailing address. Rakuten says it needs one before it pays.
- Anyone who would shop only to reach a bonus requirement. That spending costs more than the bonus returns.

## Quick answers

**Do you need a credit card to sign up for Rakuten?**
No. An email and password, or a Facebook, Google or Apple sign-in, creates the account.

**How old do you have to be to join Rakuten?**
At least 18, and a resident of the 50 United States or Washington D.C., under Rakuten's terms.

**Can you sign up for Rakuten with Google or Apple?**
Yes. Rakuten's join article lists Facebook, Google and Apple as ways to join.
