Is Rakuten free? Yes, free to join and use, with one fee that can apply

Facts checked November 26, 20265 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

Yes, Rakuten is free to join and free to use: there is no membership fee and no charge for shopping through it. The catch is not a price but a trade, because Rakuten collects data on your shopping, pays only four times a year, and holds any balance under about $5.

What it costs in money

Item What Rakuten's pages say
Fee to join None. The join page calls it "simple and free."
Fee for the app or browser extension None. Both are described as free.
Payment card needed to join No. An account needs an email and password, or a Facebook, Google or Apple sign-in.
Minimum before a payment is sent $5 on the help page; $5.01 in the terms
Fee taken from a payment None is listed on Rakuten's payment page. Any fee charged by PayPal: not confirmed.
Inactive account fee $5.00 a month, after more than 12 months with no shopping trip
Rakuten+ A separate, optional annual membership that renews automatically. Price: not confirmed.

A card comes into it only if you choose to link one for in-store offers. Rakuten's home page says of linking a card, "You don't have to." Members must be at least 18 and live in the 50 states or Washington D.C., and each person is limited to one account.

The one fee in the terms is for an inactive account. If you have not shopped or started a shopping trip for more than 12 months in a row, Rakuten may take $5.00 a month from your balance. It comes out of cash back you have earned, not from your bank. The terms say the fee cannot make a balance negative or leave you owing money, so an account with nothing in it is not charged. Shopping or starting a shopping trip stops it. After more than 36 idle months with a zero balance, Rakuten may close the account.

The paid extras you can ignore

Two things with Rakuten's name are not part of the free membership, and neither is needed to earn cash back.

  • Rakuten+. Rakuten's help center describes an annual membership, offered by invitation, that pays at least 10% at a list of premium stores. It renews automatically on the date you joined, can be canceled up to 3 days before renewal, and gives no refund for a part-used year. The help pages mention a free trial but do not state the price, so it is not confirmed.
  • The Rakuten American Express Card. A credit card with its own terms. Its fees were not read for this page.

How Rakuten earns if you pay nothing

Stores pay Rakuten a commission when it sends them a shopper who buys. Rakuten keeps part and passes the rest to you as cash back. Its terms say the cash back is funded from that partner compensation and, for some offers, from its own funds. The longer version is on how Rakuten makes money.

The costs that are not money

Your data. Rakuten's terms say the browser extension collects information on the pages you visit and on search results pages, whether or not you are interacting with it. That is how it shows offers. What is collected and how to limit it is covered on the extension and your privacy. Rakuten's home page says it does not sell personal data to data brokers, may share limited information with advertising partners, and lets you opt out of that sharing. You can earn without the extension by starting at Rakuten.com each time.

The wait. Cash back starts as pending. Rakuten says online and in-store cash back typically takes 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, and confirmed cash back is sent by February 15, May 15, August 15 and November 15. A purchase can take months to become money; see when Rakuten pays. Rakuten also lets members redeem a confirmed balance of at least $5 for a gift card without waiting for a payment date.

The minimum. The help page says you need $5 or more in confirmed cash back to be paid. The terms put the minimum at $5.01. Either way, a smaller balance is not lost: it carries over to the next payment date.

Returns. Returned or canceled orders do not earn cash back. For a partial return, Rakuten says the cash back is adjusted.

Coupon codes. Rakuten's help page says codes not listed on Rakuten "may void Cash Back." If a code from elsewhere saves more than the cash back would pay, the code is the better deal for that order; you usually cannot count on both.

Tracking that does not happen. Ad blockers, VPNs, declined cookies, or finishing checkout in a different tab can stop a purchase from being linked to your account. The fix is on missing cash back.

Is the joining bonus free too

The bonus for new members costs nothing, but it is not paid for joining alone. As read on November 26, 2026, the welcome bonus terms ask for at least $25 of purchases that earn cash back within 90 days, and the referral offer asks for $50. Amounts vary and change; see how the sign-up and referral bonuses work and welcome bonus or referral bonus.

Who should skip it

  • You do not want a company recording which shopping pages you visit. Use the website only, or leave it.
  • You shop online once or twice a year for small amounts. The balance may stay under $5, and after 12 idle months the fee can reduce it.
  • You rely on coupon codes from other sites at checkout.
  • You would buy more than you planned in order to earn cash back. That spending is the real cost.

Quick answers

Is Rakuten free to use? Yes. Rakuten charges no fee to join, shop, or use its app and extension.

Do you need a credit card to join Rakuten? No. An email and password is enough. A card is linked only for in-store offers, by choice.

What is the minimum Rakuten will pay out? About $5 in confirmed cash back. The help page says $5; the terms say $5.01. Less carries over.
