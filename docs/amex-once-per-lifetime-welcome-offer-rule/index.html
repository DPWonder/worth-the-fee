What is the Amex "once per lifetime" welcome offer rule, and how do you know if you are still eligible?

Facts checked November 20, 20265 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

The "once per lifetime" rule means American Express generally gives the welcome offer on a card only once per person: if you have or have had that card, you may not get the offer again. You find out for certain during the application, where Amex shows a notice if no welcome offer is available to you and lets you withdraw before the application is processed.

What the terms say

The Points Guy reports that the current Platinum Card terms say "you may not be eligible to receive a welcome offer" if you have or have had the card.

Two things to know about that wording:

  • It is about one card at a time. Having had the Gold Card does not, by this sentence alone, affect a Delta or Hilton card.
  • It says "may not". AwardWallet reported in October 2025 that Amex had changed the sentence from a flat statement that the offer was not available to past and current cardholders to this softer form. Whether Amex applies the rule more loosely now is not confirmed.

What counts as "this Card"

Situation Does it count as having had the card?
You held the card and closed it years ago Yes
The card was renamed (for example, Premier Rewards Gold became the Gold Card) Yes
You got the card by upgrading from another Amex card Yes
You hold the business version and apply for the personal version No, they are separate cards
You are an authorized user on someone else's card No, you can still get the offer on your own account

Amex's terms also list other things it may weigh: your history with balance transfers, your history as a cardmember, and how many cards you have opened and closed.

Card-family rules

On some cards the terms go further and name other cards in the same family. Holding or having held a higher card can block the offer on a lower one.

Applying for Cards that can make you ineligible Source
Gold Card The Gold Card, and any version of the Platinum Card (including the Schwab and Morgan Stanley versions) Upgraded Points, The Points Guy
Green Card Green, Gold and Platinum cards and earlier versions (rule added in 2024; Upgraded Points reports the card is now closed to new applicants) AwardWallet
Delta SkyMiles Gold Delta Gold, Platinum and Reserve and earlier versions The Points Guy
Delta SkyMiles Platinum Delta Platinum and Reserve The Points Guy

The rule runs one way. The Points Guy notes that someone who wants both the Gold and Platinum offers would need the Gold first. For Delta, the family rule covers personal cards; business cards are outside it.

Family rules for the Hilton, Marriott and Blue Cash cards are not confirmed in the sources read for this page. Read the offer terms on the application for the exact list.

The pop-up notice

The confirmed part: Amex tells you before processing the application if you are not eligible for the welcome offer.

  • A notice can appear during the application saying you may be approved for the card but will not get the welcome offer. Upgraded Points gives the message as stating that no welcome offer is available to you at this time.
  • You then choose to withdraw or to go on without the offer.
  • Upgraded Points reports the notice comes before a hard credit inquiry, so withdrawing does not affect your credit score.
  • The Points Guy adds that a similar message can show while you browse Amex cards, and that Amex has a welcome offer eligibility tool.

How to check before you apply

  1. List every Amex card you have held, including closed ones and earlier names of the same card.
  2. Read the "offer terms" on the application page. Look for the eligibility sentence and the cards it names.
  3. Go through the application up to the offer screen. On cards with "as high as" offers, Amex shows your offer amount there. More on that in how Amex referral offers work.
  4. If a notice says there is no welcome offer for you, decide whether the card is worth holding without one.

What is not confirmed

  • A seven-year reset. A common belief is that "lifetime" ends about seven years after you close a card. One Mile at a Time says Amex has not confirmed this. Do not plan on it.
  • Targeted offers. Amex sometimes sends offers by mail, email or in your account without the lifetime sentence. These depend on being targeted and cannot be requested.

Who should skip a new application

  • People who had the same card before and are applying mainly for the welcome offer.
  • People who hold or held a higher card in the same family, where the terms name it.
  • People who see the no-offer notice and would not want the card on its benefits alone.

Welcome offer amounts vary by applicant and change. Nothing here promises one.

Quick answers

Can I get an Amex welcome offer twice on the same card? Generally no. The terms say you may not be eligible if you have or have had the card.

Will Amex tell me before a credit check? The terms say Amex notifies you before processing the application. Upgraded Points reports the notice appears before a hard inquiry.

Does having the Platinum Card block the Gold Card offer? It can. The Gold Card terms name the Platinum Card as a reason you may not be eligible.
