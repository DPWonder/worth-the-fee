How many Delta Sky Club visits does the Amex Platinum give, and what are the rules?

Facts checked November 21, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

The Platinum Card gives 10 Delta Sky Club visits per year, counted from February 1 to January 31, and only when you fly Delta that day on a ticket that is not basic economy. Guests cost $50 each, and so does each visit after the tenth.

The numbers

These figures are from Delta's own Sky Club access page. The rules have applied since February 1, 2025.

Rule Detail
Visits included 10 per Medallion Year (February 1 to January 31)
Unused visits Lost; no rollover, no transfer
Extra visits after 10 $50 per person
Guests Up to two guests, or immediate family, at $50 each per visit
Children under 2 Free
Grab and Go (food to take away, no lounge entry) $25 per person
Unlimited visits After $75,000 of eligible purchases on the card in a calendar year

The 10 visits are for the cardholder only. They cannot be used to bring a guest in.

What counts as one visit

A visit is not one door entry. Delta defines it as entries at one or more airports for up to 24 hours, starting from your first entry or Grab and Go use.

Trip Visits used
One Sky Club before a nonstop flight 1
Sky Clubs at the departure airport and at a connection, same day 1
Sky Club before departure and another after landing, within 24 hours 1
Outbound on Monday, return on Friday, a Sky Club each way 2

So 10 visits cover about five round trips for someone who uses a Sky Club in each direction.

Which flights qualify

You need a same-day boarding pass for one of these, departing from or arriving at the airport where the lounge is:

  • A Delta-operated flight, including Delta Connection.
  • A Delta-marketed flight operated by WestJet, with a ticket number starting 006.

Not accepted:

  • Delta Main Basic, the airline's basic economy fare. Guests on that fare are refused too.
  • Flights on other airlines, including other Delta partners.
  • Partner lounges. The benefit is for Delta Sky Clubs only.

When you can enter

  • Before departure: only within 3 hours of the scheduled departure time.
  • During a layover: at any time.
  • After arrival: Delta's page allows entry on a same-day flight arriving at that airport. Upgraded Points notes this works on domestic arrivals; after an international flight you leave through customs and are outside the secure area.

A phone boarding pass can disappear from the app after landing. Upgraded Points suggests keeping a screenshot.

Additional cardholders

Each additional Platinum cardholder gets their own 10 visits a year, according to Delta's page. An additional Platinum card costs $195 a year; see whether an additional card is worth $195. The no-fee Companion Platinum Card does not get Sky Club access.

For a couple, this can cost less than paying guest fees. Ten shared visits with one guest at $50 would be $500 in guest fees.

Unlimited visits

After $75,000 of eligible purchases on the account in a calendar year, visits become unlimited. Purchases by additional cardholders count toward the total. Delta says it usually takes about a week, and up to 12 weeks, to take effect.

Sources differ on how long it lasts. Delta's page, as read, describes the rest of that calendar year and through January 31 of the next calendar year. The Points Guy says it runs through the end of the next program year, which would be a year longer. Confirm with Amex before counting on the longer period.

Even with unlimited visits, guests still cost $50 each.

What it is worth

A paid visit costs $50, so 10 visits have a list value of $500. The real value depends on how often you fly Delta from an airport with a Sky Club, on a fare above basic economy.

Delta round trips a year Visits likely used Value at $50 each
0 0 $0
2 4 $200
5 or more 10 $500

Frequent Delta flyers may run out. The Delta SkyMiles Reserve card gives 15 visits; see Platinum vs. Delta SkyMiles Reserve. The Platinum Card's other lounges have separate rules; see Centurion Lounge guest rules.

Who should skip it

Count Sky Club access as $0 if:

  • You rarely fly Delta.
  • You usually buy Delta's basic economy fare.
  • Your home airport has no Sky Club and you seldom connect through a Delta hub.
  • You travel with family and would pay $50 a person each time.

Quick answers

How many Sky Club visits come with the Amex Platinum? Ten per year, counted from February 1 to January 31. Unused visits do not roll over.

Can a Platinum cardholder enter a Sky Club on a basic economy ticket? No. Delta Main Basic fares are excluded, for the cardholder and for guests.

How much is a Sky Club guest with the Amex Platinum? $50 per guest per visit, for up to two guests or immediate family. Children under 2 are free.
