How the readings are taken

Once a day a script opens each store's public page on rakuten.com and records the headline rate in the page title, such as "Up to 8%" or "2%". "None" means the page said the store offers no cash back that day. A store is missing for a day when its page could not be read. Category rates and exclusions are not in this file; each store's own page here lists them as read on its checked date.

Use the data

The full history is one plain file: rakuten-rates.csv (columns: date, store, rate). You may quote or chart it; please link to this page as the source. For today's rates with what changed since yesterday, see Rakuten rates today, by store.
