Amex Business Platinum vs personal Platinum: how do the fee and credits differ?

Facts checked November 20, 20265 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Both cards cost $895 a year and share the same lounge access, hotel status and $600 hotel credit. The difference is in the other credits: the personal Platinum's are for dining, streaming, Uber and retail, and the Business Platinum's are for business costs such as Dell, Indeed, Adobe and a phone bill.

The Business Platinum also has two credits for very large spenders: up to $1,200 in Amex Travel flight credits and up to $2,400 toward One AP, after $250,000 of spend in a calendar year. Upgraded Points lists a newer ZO membership credit as well; that one is not confirmed by a second source.

The plain sum

  • Shared credits: $600 + $200 + $219 = $1,019 on either card.
  • Personal only: $400 + $300 + $300 + $300 + $200 + $200 + $155 + $120 = $1,975.
  • Business only, no spending threshold: $150 + $360 + $300 + $200 + $120 = $1,130. Adobe adds $250 once $600 has been spent there.

Face value is the ceiling, not the result. The test is the same on both cards: count only what you, or the business, would have paid cash for.

A consumer who eats at Resy restaurants, pays for streaming and takes an Uber each month can use $900 of personal-only credits with no change in habits. That person would use little of the business list.

A business that already pays for Indeed job ads, an Adobe plan, a phone line and Dell equipment can use $880 of business-only credits the same way ($360 + $250 + $120 + $150). The same business gets nothing from lululemon or Equinox credits.

The 2x rate matters only for big-ticket buyers. A $20,000 equipment purchase earns 40,000 points on the Business Platinum and 20,000 on the personal card.

Which pattern favors which card

  • Personal travel, restaurants, streaming, rides: personal Platinum.
  • Books flights directly with airlines: personal Platinum, 5x against 1x.
  • Regular purchases of $5,000 or more, or software and hardware spend: Business Platinum.
  • Pays for flights with points on one airline: Business Platinum, for the 35% back.
  • Wants lounge access for a spouse or employee: personal Platinum; its additional card costs less. See whether an authorized user is worth $195.

Who should skip it

  • Skip the Business Platinum without real business spending. It is a business card, and its credits pay for business tools.
  • Skip the personal Platinum if the hotel credit and two or three of the brand credits do not match things already in your budget. The page on what the credits are worth helps with the count.
  • Skip both if lounge access is the only draw and trips are rare; see the occasional flyer page.
  • Anyone who carries a balance.

Amex treats the personal and business versions as separate cards for welcome offers. Offers on both vary by applicant and change.

Quick answers

Do the personal and Business Platinum cost the same? Yes, $895 a year each. Additional Platinum cards differ: $195 on the personal card and a reported $400 on the business card.

Does the Business Platinum have the Resy and Uber credits? No. Neither review read lists Resy, Uber, digital entertainment, lululemon, Equinox, Oura or Walmart+ credits on the business card.

Is lounge access the same on both? Largely yes: Centurion Lounges, Priority Pass, Escape, Plaza Premium and 10 Delta Sky Club visits a year on each.
