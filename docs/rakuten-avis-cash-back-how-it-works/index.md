# Rakuten Avis cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-avis-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Avis page showed 2.5% cash back, with no raised rate. Rentals picked up in Alaska, rentals outside the U.S. and Canada and rentals that use the Avis Worldwide Discount earn nothing, and cash back posts only after the travel is completed.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate

The page showed 2.5% with no "was" label and no rate by category or car class. With no "was" label, 2.5% is the plain rate. Nothing on the page promises a higher rate on Black Friday (November 27, 2026) or after.

The page does not say which part of a rental bill the 2.5% is counted on. Whether it covers only the base rate, or also extras such as added drivers, fuel or insurance, is not confirmed. Rakuten's general help page says taxes never earn cash back.

## What earns and what does not

| Rental | Earns cash back? |
|---|---|
| Picked up in the U.S., other than Alaska | Yes |
| Picked up in Canada | Yes |
| Picked up in Alaska | No |
| Picked up outside the U.S. and Canada | No |
| Booked with the Avis Worldwide Discount | No |
| Canceled before pickup | No, under Rakuten's general rules |

The terms do not define the "Avis Worldwide Discount". Which discount codes fall under it is not confirmed.

## Coupon codes

The terms say coupon codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back, and to stick to codes Rakuten provides. On November 25, 2026, the page listed one offer with a code, for 25% off, next to several sale links.

Under the terms, a membership or coupon code that is not listed on Rakuten puts the 2.5% at risk.

## Pay now or pay later

Rakuten's page describes lower rates for paying at booking. The cash back terms do not separate prepaid rentals from rentals paid at the counter. Whether both earn is not confirmed.

## When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account within six weeks after travel has been completed. Payment then follows Rakuten's schedule of four dates a year; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).

## What it comes to

Round examples, not prices from the page.

| Rental | Base cost | At 2.5% |
|---|---|---|
| Weekend, three days | $180 | $4.50 |
| One week | $450 | $11.25 |
| One week, picked up in Alaska | $450 | $0 |

## App, loyalty program and counter

- **App.** Rakuten's page says the Avis app can make, change and cancel reservations. The terms do not mention the app. A booking earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension. Whether a booking made or changed in the Avis app tracks is not confirmed.
- **Avis Preferred.** The terms do not mention the loyalty program. Whether a rental paid with points earns is not confirmed.
- **At the counter.** The page showed an online rate only. Avis did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See [how in-store cash back works](/rakuten-in-store-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## Who should skip it

- You rent in Alaska, or anywhere outside the U.S. and Canada.
- You have a membership, employer or coupon code that is not on Rakuten and it cuts the rate by more than 2.5%.
- You book with the Avis Worldwide Discount.
- You book by phone. Rakuten's general help page says phone orders do not qualify.
- You want the money soon. Nothing posts until up to six weeks after the trip.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Avis?**
2.5% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

**Do rentals outside the United States earn cash back?**
Rentals in Canada do. Rentals outside the U.S. and Canada, and pickups in Alaska, earn nothing.

**When does Avis cash back post?**
Within six weeks after the travel is completed, according to the posting line on Rakuten's page.
