Is the Chase Sapphire referral offer the same as the public or in-branch offer?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Often, but not always. On November 20, 2026 the Sapphire Preferred referral page and Chase's public page showed the same bonus, 75,000 points after $5,000 of purchases in three months. The two have differed for days or weeks in the past, and no current in-branch offer could be confirmed.

The three routes on November 20, 2026

Route Sapphire Preferred bonus Spending requirement Status
Chase public page 75,000 points $5,000 in 3 months Confirmed on Chase's page
Referral link 75,000 points $5,000 in 3 months Confirmed on that date
Chase branch Not confirmed Not confirmed No source read lists a branch offer this month

For the Sapphire Reserve, the public page and the referral page both showed 100,000 points, and the public page states $6,000 of purchases in three months.

Chase itself says offers are not uniform. Its card page states: "Offers may vary depending on where you apply," for example online or in a branch, and they can change over time.

Three patterns come out of this:

  • Referral links usually follow the public offer, sometimes a day late.
  • When an offer ends, referral links have lagged in either direction. In 2021 they were lower. In summer 2026 they stayed higher for about a week.
  • Branch offers have at times been higher than online.

Sources disagree by one day on when the 2026 referral offer ended: Frequent Miler lists August 5, Doctor of Credit August 6.

Frequent Miler also reported that in 2021 Chase would not match applicants who had used the lower referral offer to the higher public one. Do not count on a match after the fact.

About branch offers

A branch offer is one a Chase banker can submit for you in person. What sources say:

  • Past branch offers sometimes had a higher points figure, a longer spending window, or a second step that needed more spending.
  • A Frequent Miler post, seen in a search summary, says the annual fee has been waived for the first year more often on branch offers than online. Not confirmed for 2026.
  • No source read lists a Sapphire branch offer for November 2026. Frequent Miler's card page lists only the 75,000-point offer.

Asking a banker what the branch offer is does not involve a credit check. Applying does.

How to compare on the day

  1. Open Chase's public Sapphire page. Note the points, the spending requirement, the time allowed and the annual fee.
  2. Open the referral page. Note the same four things.
  3. If you bank with Chase, ask at a branch what its offer is before you apply anywhere.
  4. Compare whole offers, not only points, and keep a copy of the terms you apply under.

What is the same on every route

  • Bonus eligibility. One bonus per Sapphire card per lifetime. See the 2026 eligibility rules.
  • 5/24. See how to count your cards.
  • The credit check. Each application adds a hard inquiry.
  • What counts as spending. See the spending requirement.

No source read says a referral or branch application is exempt from these. That is not confirmed as a stated Chase rule.

Who should skip it

  • Anyone who already earned the bonus on that Sapphire card. No route brings it back.
  • Anyone at or over 5/24. The route does not change the likely denial.
  • Anyone waiting for a higher offer who needs the card's travel protections for a trip soon. Past higher offers were short and their dates were not announced far ahead.
  • Anyone who would have to stretch to meet a larger spending requirement.

No offer amount is promised here. Offers change.

Quick answers

Is the Sapphire referral bonus higher than the public bonus? Not as of November 20, 2026, when both showed 75,000 points on the Preferred. It has been higher and lower at times.

Are Chase branch offers higher than online offers? Sometimes in the past. No branch offer for November 2026 was confirmed in the sources read.

Will Chase match a higher offer after I apply? Do not plan on it. Frequent Miler reported Chase declining to match in 2021.
