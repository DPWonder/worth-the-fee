Rakuten Sonos cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

The short answer

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Sonos. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Sonos page showed 2% cash back, with no raised rate. Gift cards, dealer orders and orders deemed for reselling earn nothing, and a coupon code not listed on Rakuten voids the cash back.

A "was" label appears when a rate has been raised above the store's base rate. This page has none, so 2% is the plain rate. The page listed the store's Black Friday offer, up to 25% off, at the same 2%. Nothing on the page promises a higher rate on Black Friday (November 27, 2026).

What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Sonos are short. Cash back is not available on:

  • Dealer orders
  • Orders placed through the dealer portal
  • Orders deemed to be for reselling
  • Gift cards

No product is excluded. Speakers, soundbars, headphones and accessories are not named as earning nothing.

Coupon codes: the firm wording

The terms say that using coupon codes not listed on Rakuten "will void" cash back, and to keep to codes Rakuten provides. Many store pages say "may void"; this one is firmer. On November 25, 2026, the offers Rakuten listed for this store were a sale, free shipping and a bundle price, with no code shown.

Upgrade credits and status discounts

The page's question-and-answer section describes two of the store's own discounts:

  • An upgrade program, with a credit of up to 30% for owners of an existing product, activated in the store account.
  • Status discounts of 15% for students and frontline workers who qualify.

The cash back terms do not mention either one. Whether an upgrade credit or a status discount counts as a code "not listed on Rakuten" is not confirmed. Both are far larger than 2%, so the question matters: if one of them voids cash back, the discount is still the bigger number.

Buying the brand at another store

The 2% is for orders on the brand's own site. The same products sold by other retailers fall under those retailers' Rakuten terms and rates. For one example, with a rate list by brand, see Rakuten at Best Buy. Rates on both pages change daily.

App and stores

  • App. The terms do not mention the store's app. Whether an order placed in the app tracks is not confirmed.
  • In stores. The page showed an online rate only. The brand did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.

When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "within one week". That is slower than the "tomorrow" shown on many store pages. Payment follows on Rakuten's schedule; see when Rakuten pays. Card rewards are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • You qualify for the 15% status discount or an upgrade credit. Either is worth more than 2%, and whether they combine with cash back is not confirmed.
  • You have a code that is not on Rakuten. Using it voids the 2%.
  • You are buying a gift card.
  • You order as a dealer or through the dealer portal.
  • You are waiting for a raised rate. None was shown, and none is promised.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Sonos? 2% on November 25, 2026, with no raised rate shown. Rates change daily.

Are any Sonos products excluded? No product is named. The exclusions are gift cards, dealer orders and reselling.

Can you use a promo code and still get cash back? Only a code listed on Rakuten. The terms say other codes will void cash back.
