Does Rakuten give cash back on hotels and flights?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Yes for hotels: booking sites such as Expedia, Booking.com and Priceline paid up to 8% to 10% through Rakuten when checked on November 20, 2026. Flights earn very little, usually a flat $1 to $2 per booking, and travel cash back is confirmed only after the trip is over.

Several stores had raised rates that day. The "was" figures of 1% to 4% are closer to an ordinary day.

A few airlines had their own listings, at about 1% to 1.5%.

The Hilton and Marriott listings seen that day were for buying points, not booking rooms. No Hyatt listing was seen.

Priceline's terms say taxes, insurance and resort fees earn nothing. Expect the rate to apply to the room price, not the full bill: 8% on a $1,000 room is $80, and 4% on an ordinary day is $40.

Rakuten's pages do not fully agree here. Its travel article says 6 to 17 weeks after the trip is complete. Its payment page says 45 to 120 days after booking. Booking.com, Priceline and IHG each say cash back is added within 3 months after travel.

So a room booked in November for a March stay may be paid on May 15 or August 15. The full schedule is in when Rakuten pays.

What voids travel cash back

  • Changing the booking. Rakuten's travel article says: "Any changes made to your reservation or booking will void Cash Back." That covers dates, room type and flight times.
  • Canceling.
  • Using a coupon code that Rakuten did not list. On Priceline, any coupon code voids it.
  • Booking by phone or through a business version of the store.
  • Paying with points. IHG's listing excludes stays paid with points and corporate or group rates.

If plans change, Rakuten's advice is to cancel and rebook through Rakuten.

What you give up by not booking direct

For a chain hotel, booking through a third-party site usually means no hotel points and no credit toward elite status. The Points Guy's guide to Expedia puts it this way: in most cases you will not earn hotel points or elite credit, and the hotel does not have to honor your status, though some do. Its Marriott guide says third-party bookings are non-qualifying rates that earn no points or tier benefits.

Whether a third-party Marriott stay still counts as an elite night is not confirmed; sources disagree. Each chain's own terms are the final word.

A chain that lists its own site on Rakuten, as IHG did that day, is the middle path. Whether such a booking also earns the chain's points is not confirmed on Rakuten's page.

Who should skip it

  • You hold hotel elite status or are working toward it. The points and perks can be worth more than a few percent.
  • Your plans may change. An edited booking loses the cash back.
  • You mostly book flights. A dollar or two is not worth altering how you book.
  • You pay with hotel points or use a corporate rate.

Quick answers

Does Rakuten pay cash back on flights? Yes, but little. Expedia, Booking.com and Priceline paid a flat $1 to $2 per flight booking on November 20, 2026.

When is hotel cash back confirmed? Typically 6 to 17 weeks after the stay ends, then it goes out on the next payment date.

Do you still earn hotel points? Usually not when you book a chain hotel through a third-party site.
