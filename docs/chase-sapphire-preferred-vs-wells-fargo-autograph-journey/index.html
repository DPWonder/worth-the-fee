Chase Sapphire Preferred or Wells Fargo Autograph Journey: which $95 card fits?

Facts checked November 21, 20265 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Both cards cost $95 a year, and the Wells Fargo Autograph Journey earns more points on flights and hotels booked directly, at 4x and 5x against the Sapphire Preferred's 2x. The Preferred fits a reader who wants points that move to United, Southwest and Hyatt, or primary rental car cover in the United States.

Sources disagree on the Wells Fargo partner list. AwardWallet's May 2026 table shows nine airline programs and two hotel programs. The Points Guy's March 2025 review lists seven programs, without Cathay Pacific, JetBlue or Wyndham. Wells Fargo's card page names none, so check the list in a Wells Fargo account before counting on a partner.

The fee sum

  • Autograph Journey: $95 minus the $50 airline credit is $45. The credit posts after one airline purchase of $50 or more, booked anywhere.
  • Preferred: $95 minus the $100 hotel credit is minus $5. The credit works only on a hotel stay booked through Chase Travel.

A reader who buys one plane ticket a year uses the Wells Fargo credit without trying. A reader who would not book a hotel through Chase's portal should count the Preferred at the full $95.

The points sum

Take $4,000 a year of airfare and $3,000 of hotels, both booked direct, plus $4,000 of dining.

Sapphire Preferred Autograph Journey
Airfare, $4,000 8,000 16,000
Hotels, $3,000 6,000 15,000
Dining, $4,000 12,000 12,000
Total points 26,000 43,000

The Autograph Journey is 17,000 points ahead on that pattern. Two things narrow the gap.

First, booking the same travel through Chase Travel earns 5x on the Preferred: 35,000 points on the $7,000, for a total of 47,000. Portal bookings can cost hotel loyalty benefits, so this is a trade.

Second, the points are not equal. Both are worth 1 cent as a statement credit. Above that, value depends on partners. Chase points move to United, Southwest and Hyatt; none of those is in either Wells Fargo partner list read. The Points Guy valued Chase points at 2.05 cents in November 2026 and Wells Fargo points at 1.6 cents in March 2025.

Add $3,000 a year of gas and streaming and the Preferred earns 9,000 points on it; the Autograph Journey earns 3,000.

Which pattern favors which card

  • Books flights and hotels direct and redeems for statement credits or simple travel: Autograph Journey. It earns two to two and a half times as much on that spending.
  • Wants to fly on United or Southwest points, or stay at Hyatt: Preferred.
  • Rents cars in the US: Preferred. Its rental car cover is primary, so a claim does not go through personal auto insurance first.
  • Pays a phone bill and wants the phone covered: Autograph Journey.
  • Uses Flying Blue, Avios or Virgin Atlantic: either. Both issuers transfer to those programs.
  • Has opened five or more cards in two years: Chase generally declines under its 5/24 rule. Wells Fargo's approval rules were not checked for this page.

The Citi Strata Premier comparison and the Capital One Venture comparison cover two other cards at this fee.

Who should skip it

  • Skip the Autograph Journey if your plan depends on a specific US airline or hotel partner. Its list is shorter and sources do not agree on it.
  • Skip the Preferred if you book travel direct, redeem for cash value and never transfer points. It earns fewer points on that spending.
  • Skip both if you take one trip a year and spend little on dining. A no-fee card costs less.
  • Skip both if you carry a balance. Interest costs more than either card returns.

Welcome offers vary by applicant and change, so they are left out of the sums.

Quick answers

Which card earns more on hotels and flights? The Autograph Journey on direct bookings: 5x on hotels and 4x on airlines against 2x. The Preferred earns 5x only through Chase Travel.

Do Wells Fargo points transfer to United, Southwest or Hyatt? No, not in the partner lists read. Chase points transfer to all three.

Does the Autograph Journey include airport lounge access? No. Neither card includes lounge access.
