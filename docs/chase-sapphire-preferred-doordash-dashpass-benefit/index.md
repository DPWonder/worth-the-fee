# What DoorDash and DashPass benefits come with the Sapphire Preferred, and what are they worth?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/chase-sapphire-preferred-doordash-dashpass-benefit/
Facts checked: 2026-11-21
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

The Sapphire Preferred comes with a free DashPass membership, which runs until December 31, 2029 if you activate it before then, plus $10 off one non-restaurant DoorDash order each month. At face value that is up to $240 a year, but it is worth only what you already spend on DoorDash delivery fees and grocery or convenience orders.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## The two parts

| Part | What you get | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| DashPass | $0 delivery fee and reduced service fees on eligible orders | Order must meet the minimum subtotal shown in the app; activate by 12/31/2029 |
| Monthly promo | $10 off one non-restaurant order per calendar month | Grocery, convenience and similar orders; subtotal only; no rollover |

Chase puts the value of DashPass at $120 a year. DoorDash's help page, seen in a search summary, gives the current paid price as $9.99 a month.

## How long DashPass lasts

Chase's terms say the membership runs "from activation until December 31, 2029 or for 12 months, whichever is longer." Activate in 2026 and it lasts about three years. Activate in late 2029 and it lasts 12 months from that date.

Sources disagree on the activation deadline. Chase's terms, Chase's Preferred update page and DoorDash all say December 31, 2029. A search summary of an older Chase page gave December 31, 2027. The 2029 date is on the pages read in full today.

After the free period, DoorDash's help page says a membership renews at the monthly rate then in force. Set a reminder if you do not want to pay for it.

## How to turn it on

1. Add the Sapphire Preferred as a payment method in the DoorDash or Caviar app. A digital wallet such as Apple Pay does not qualify.
2. Tap the activation button.
3. Pay with that same card at checkout to get the monthly promo.

Authorized users can add their card and activate too. Chase's terms say everyone on the account shares one membership period, which starts when the first person activates.

If you already pay for DashPass, DoorDash says the Chase membership replaces it and the billing stops. It does not mention a refund of past charges.

## The $10 monthly promo

- It applies to one order a month from a non-restaurant store on DoorDash, such as a grocery or convenience store.
- It comes off the subtotal. Fees, taxes and tip are still charged.
- Unused value is gone at the end of the month.
- Gift cards are excluded, and alcohol orders may be restricted.
- Chase sets no separate minimum order for the promo, but DoorDash's own order minimums apply.

Restaurant orders get the DashPass fee savings but not this promo. The Sapphire Reserve has a larger set of DoorDash promos, covered in [the Reserve credits page](/chase-sapphire-reserve-credits-real-value/).

## What it is worth to you

Use last year's DoorDash history, not a guess.

| Reader | DashPass | Monthly promo | Yearly total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Never uses DoorDash | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Already pays for DashPass, no grocery orders | $120 | $0 | $120 |
| Orders restaurant delivery twice a month, no DashPass today | Fee savings on about 24 orders | $0 | Depends on the fees saved |
| Already pays for DashPass and orders groceries on DoorDash monthly | $120 | $120 | $240 |

Two cautions on the sums:

- Prices for items on delivery apps can be higher than in the store. A $10 promo on a grocery order that costs $8 more than a store trip, plus tip, saves little.
- A promo that leads to an order you would not have placed is spending, not saving.

This benefit sits beside the card's other perks in the [first-year plan](/chase-sapphire-preferred-first-year-plan/).

## Who should skip it

- Anyone who does not use DoorDash. The benefit has no cash value.
- Anyone outside DoorDash's delivery area, or whose local non-restaurant stores are not on the app.
- Anyone who would order more delivery only because of the promo.
- Anyone weighing the $95 fee mainly on this perk. It is a discount inside one app, not money back.

## Quick answers

**Does the $10 promo work on restaurant orders?**
No. On the Sapphire Preferred it applies to one non-restaurant order a month, such as grocery or convenience.

**Does the unused $10 roll over?**
No. Chase's terms say the monthly discount does not roll over into a later month.

**Do authorized users get DashPass too?**
Yes. Each adds the card in the app and activates; everyone on the account shares the same membership period.
