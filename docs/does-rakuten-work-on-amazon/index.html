Does Rakuten give cash back on Amazon?

Facts checked November 21, 20263 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Not in the usual way. On November 21, 2026 Rakuten listed Amazon with no percentage rate, only a flat $5 on each of the first three eligible Amazon orders for members in their first 60 days. Longer-standing members saw no ongoing Amazon rate on that page.

What was listed on November 21, 2026

Item What Rakuten's Amazon page said
Rate $5 per order, not a percentage
How many orders The first 3 eligible Amazon orders
Who Members within their first 60 days
Deadline Purchases within 60 days of signing up
How to shop Rakuten browser extension, Rakuten app or Samsung Wallet Perks
Counts toward the welcome or referral bonus No

The page also says the offer can be changed at any time.

One thing stands out in the terms: "Amazon is not a partner of Rakuten." Rakuten says the offer comes from Rakuten alone and that it is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Amazon. At most stores, the store pays Rakuten a commission and Rakuten shares it. Here the wording says the reward is Rakuten's own.

Where sources differ

  • Minimum order. A search summary of Rakuten's Amazon page says each order must be $25 or more, and that the 60-day rule applies to sign-ups on or after January 23, 2026. The page as read on November 21 showed neither. Treat the $25 minimum as possible and check the terms shown to your account.
  • The Rakuten website. The terms name the extension, the app and Samsung Wallet Perks. They do not name a click from rakuten.com. Whether that route counts is not confirmed.
  • Older statements. A search summary of an older Rakuten help article said cash back at Amazon ended in March 2020. That article no longer loads. Earlier Amazon offers reported by Frequent Miler were targeted and have expired.

What does not earn

The terms exclude:

  • Subscriptions and digital purchases
  • Gift cards and vouchers, both buying and redeeming them, and store credit
  • Orders using a voucher or promo code not featured on Rakuten
  • Orders placed through Add to Delivery, Auto Buy or Rufus AI
  • Amazon Pharmacy, Amazon Health urgent care and the Amazon FSA/HSA Store

The sums

Three orders at $5 each is $15 in total. That is the ceiling of the offer as listed.

Order size Reward As a share of the order
$25 $5 20%
$100 $5 5%
$500 $5 1%

So the offer is worth most on small orders. After the third order, or after day 60, the page showed nothing further for Amazon itself.

The Amazon orders do not count toward Rakuten's welcome bonus. That bonus needs spending at other stores; see how the Rakuten bonus works.

Amazon-brand devices sold by other stores may also be excluded there. Kohl's listing on Rakuten, for one, excludes Amazon products.

Who should skip it

  • You joined Rakuten more than 60 days ago. Nothing was listed for you at Amazon on the day checked.
  • Your Amazon spending is mostly subscriptions, digital items or gift cards. Those are excluded.
  • You use Auto Buy or add items to an existing Prime delivery. Those orders are excluded.
  • You plan to count Amazon orders toward the welcome bonus. They do not count.

Quick answers

Does Rakuten pay a percentage back at Amazon? No percentage was listed on November 21, 2026. The listing was a flat $5 on each of a new member's first three eligible orders.

Can a long-time member earn at Amazon? Not from the Amazon listing seen that day. It was limited to members in their first 60 days.

Do Amazon gift cards earn the $5? No. The terms exclude buying and redeeming gift cards and vouchers.
