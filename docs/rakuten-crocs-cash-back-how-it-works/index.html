Rakuten Crocs cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

The short answer

Yes, Rakuten pays cash back at Crocs. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's Crocs page showed 9% cash back, marked "was 3%". The offer covers the crocs.com US site only, and orders judged to be for reselling, along with anything paid for by redeeming a gift card, earn nothing.

A "was" label appears when a rate has been raised above the store's usual rate. Here it points to 3% as the usual figure. The page does not say when the 9% ends. No category table appears; every product and offer on the page carried the same 9%.

Two points stand out.

Gift cards. Many Rakuten store pages exclude gift cards outright. The Crocs terms say cash back is available on the purchase of gift cards, but not on their redemption. So the card earns when bought and the later order paid with it does not. For how this compares, see Rakuten and gift cards.

Reselling. The terms do not say how an order is deemed to be for reselling, or what quantity triggers it. Not confirmed.

Coupon codes

The Crocs terms say use of coupon or promotional codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. On November 25, 2026, Rakuten's page listed these Crocs offers alongside the 9% rate:

  • An extra 20% off sitewide
  • $20 off orders of $100
  • $15 off orders of $75
  • Up to 60% off plus doorbusters
  • 30% off the Pokemon collection
  • 25% off the NFL collection with free shipping

The page also describes a Crocs Club sign-up coupon and a student discount through Student Beans. Neither is named in the cash back terms. Whether using one of them voids cash back is not confirmed.

Where the page disagrees with itself

These are the store's own offers, not cash back rules, but the figures on Rakuten's page do not match each other:

  • Free shipping is given as orders over $44.99 in one place, $24.99 in another and $49.99 in a third.
  • The Crocs Club coupon is described as 20% off in one section and 15% off in another.

The Crocs site on the day of purchase is the place to look.

Pickup, app and stores

  • Pickup. Not mentioned on the page.
  • App. The terms do not mention a Crocs app. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
  • In stores. The Crocs page showed an online rate only. Crocs did not appear in the part of Rakuten's in-store offer list that loaded on November 25, 2026; the list does not load in full, so this is not confirmed. See how in-store cash back works.
  • Taxes and shipping. The Crocs terms do not mention them. Not confirmed for this store.

When it shows up

The posting line says cash back is added to the Rakuten account "today". Rakuten's help page says online cash back typically takes 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, and that returns reduce or void it. Payment follows on Rakuten's schedule; see when Rakuten pays. If a purchase never appears, see what to do when cash back did not track.

Who should skip it

  • You are ordering from a Crocs site outside the US.
  • You are buying in quantity to resell.
  • You are paying with a Crocs gift card.
  • You have a code from outside Rakuten that takes more off than the cash back gives.
  • You are counting on 9% later in the season. The page marks 3% as the earlier rate and promises nothing.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at Crocs? 9% on November 25, 2026, marked "was 3%". Rates change daily.

Do Crocs gift cards earn cash back? Buying one does, according to the terms. Paying with one does not.

Does Rakuten work on Crocs sites outside the US? No. The terms limit cash back to orders on the crocs.com US site.
