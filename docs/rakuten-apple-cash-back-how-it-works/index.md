# Rakuten Apple cash back: is Apple on Rakuten, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-apple-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-25
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes, Apple is on Rakuten, but cash back covers a narrow list. Apple was listed at 2% on November 25, 2026, and only for accessories, Apple Refurbished products, AppleCare+ and Beats. A new iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch or AirPods earns nothing.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check Apple's page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate here and at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## What earns and what does not

Rakuten's terms for Apple say: "Cash Back is only available on Accessories, Apple Refurbished products, AppleCare+ and Beats."

| Earned 2% on November 25, 2026 | Earned nothing |
|---|---|
| Apple Certified Refurbished | iPhone |
| AirTag | Mac and MacBooks |
| Cases and protection | iPad |
| Apple Watch bands | Apple Watch |
| Mice and keyboards | AirPods |
| Other accessories | HomePod, HomePod mini and Apple TV |
| Beats by Dre | Apple Vision Pro |
| AppleCare+ | Gift cards and gift wrap |

Also excluded: Apple displays (Pro Display XDR and Studio Display), Apple Vision Pro accessories, redeeming a gift card, Apple Developer Programs, AppleCare+ and AppleCare One paid as a monthly subscription, resale orders, and orders through Apple's employee purchase, government or veterans and military stores.

No "was" rate was shown, so 2% looked like the ordinary rate that day, not a raised one. That held two days before Black Friday, when many other stores were raised; see [Rakuten on Black Friday](/rakuten-black-friday-cash-back-2026/). Rakuten's Black Friday page features Apple, and the exclusions above still apply.

## The sums

| Purchase | At 2% |
|---|---|
| $50 case | $1.00 |
| $100 of accessories | $2.00 |
| $200 Beats headphones | $4.00 |
| $1,000 new Mac | $0 |

A mixed cart is where it gets less obvious. Buy a new Mac with a $100 keyboard and only the keyboard counts, so the order earns about $2.

## Refurbished products

Refurbished is the one route to cash back on a larger Apple purchase. The terms include Apple Refurbished products and show Apple Certified Refurbished at 2%. On a $900 refurbished item that is $18.

One caution. New Macs, iPads and iPhones are on the excluded list, and the terms as read do not spell out how the two rules meet for a refurbished Mac or iPad. The plain reading is that refurbished products qualify. It is not confirmed beyond that wording, so check the pending amount after the order.

## Limits and timing

- **Unit limits.** The terms set lifetime limits: 6 units per model for iPhone, Mac, iPad, Watch, Vision, Apple TV and HomePod; 32 each of the AirTag 1-pack and AirTag accessories; 8 AirTag 4-packs; and 10 each of AirPods and other eligible accessories. The limits count purchases on Apple's website and in its app, and no reward is paid past the limit for a product.
- **Posting.** Apple's listing says cash back is added to the Rakuten account the same day. It still has to move from pending to confirmed before it is paid; see [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/).
- **Gift cards.** Buying an Apple gift card earns nothing. More on that in [gift cards and Rakuten](/rakuten-gift-cards-cash-back/).
- **Other stores.** Apple products sold elsewhere follow that store's terms. The Rakuten listings for [Best Buy](/rakuten-best-buy-cash-back-how-it-works/), [Target](/rakuten-target-cash-back-how-it-works/) and [Kohl's](/rakuten-kohls-cash-back-how-it-works/) each exclude Apple products.
- **Other phone and laptop makers.** Their own stores have different rules; see [Rakuten at Samsung](/rakuten-samsung-cash-back-how-it-works/).

## How this fits with other savings

Rakuten cash back comes on top of whatever the credit card earns on the purchase; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/). Whether Apple's education pricing or a trade-in changes eligibility is not stated in the terms read, so it is not confirmed.

If an eligible order shows nothing after two days, see [what to do about missing cash back](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/).

## Who should skip it

- You are buying a new iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch or AirPods and nothing else. The order earns nothing.
- You pay for AppleCare+ monthly. The subscription form is excluded.
- You buy through an employee, government or veterans store. Those orders are excluded.
- You are buying a gift card.

## Quick answers

**Is Apple on Rakuten?**
Yes. Apple was listed at 2% on November 25, 2026, for accessories, Apple Refurbished products, AppleCare+ and Beats only.

**Does a new iPhone earn Rakuten cash back at Apple?**
No. iPhone was on the excluded list on November 25, 2026.

**When is Apple cash back added?**
The listing says the same day. It is paid on Rakuten's next quarterly date after it is confirmed.
