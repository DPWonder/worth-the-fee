Rakuten Apple cash back: is Apple on Rakuten, and what is excluded?

The short answer

Yes, Apple is on Rakuten: 2% cash back on November 26, 2026, the day before Black Friday, and only for accessories, Apple Refurbished products, AppleCare+ and Beats. A new iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch or AirPods earns nothing.

A mixed cart is where it gets less obvious. Buy a new Mac with a $100 keyboard and only the keyboard counts, so the order earns about $2.

Refurbished products

Refurbished is the one route to cash back on a larger Apple purchase. The terms include Apple Refurbished products and show Apple Certified Refurbished at 2%. On a $900 refurbished item that is $18.

One caution. New Macs, iPads and iPhones are on the excluded list, and the terms as read do not spell out how the two rules meet for a refurbished Mac or iPad. The plain reading is that refurbished products qualify. It is not confirmed beyond that wording, so check the pending amount after the order.

Limits and timing

  • Unit limits. The terms set lifetime limits: 6 units per model for iPhone, Mac, iPad, Watch, Vision, Apple TV and HomePod; 32 each of the AirTag 1-pack and AirTag accessories; 8 AirTag 4-packs; and 10 each of AirPods and other eligible accessories. The limits count purchases on Apple's website and in its app, and no reward is paid past the limit for a product.
  • Posting. Apple's listing says cash back is added to the Rakuten account the same day. It still has to move from pending to confirmed before it is paid; see when Rakuten pays.
  • Gift cards. Buying an Apple gift card earns nothing. More on that in gift cards and Rakuten.
  • Other stores. Apple products sold elsewhere follow that store's terms. The Rakuten listings for Best Buy, Target and Kohl's each exclude Apple products.
  • Other phone and laptop makers. Their own stores have different rules; see Rakuten at Samsung.

How this fits with other savings

Rakuten cash back comes on top of whatever the credit card earns on the purchase; see stacking with card rewards. Whether Apple's education pricing or a trade-in changes eligibility is not stated in the terms read, so it is not confirmed.

If an eligible order shows nothing after two days, see what to do about missing cash back.

Who should skip it

  • You are buying a new iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch or AirPods and nothing else. The order earns nothing.
  • You pay for AppleCare+ monthly. The subscription form is excluded.
  • You buy through an employee, government or veterans store. Those orders are excluded.
  • You are buying a gift card.
