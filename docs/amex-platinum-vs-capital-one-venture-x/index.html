Amex Platinum or Capital One Venture X: which fee is easier to earn back?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

The Venture X fee is easier to earn back. One $300 booking through Capital One Travel plus the 10,000 anniversary miles covers its $395 fee, while the $895 Platinum fee takes several separate credits, most of them tied to named brands.

The Venture X sum

  • Fee: $395.
  • Travel credit: $300, on flights, hotels or rental cars booked through Capital One Travel.
  • Anniversary miles: 10,000, worth $100 when used against a travel purchase at 1 cent each.

$395 minus $300 minus $100 is minus $5. A reader who books at least $300 of travel a year through Capital One's portal is square from the second year on. The miles post at the first anniversary, so year one comes out $95 short on this count.

Two limits matter. The credit works only on bookings made through Capital One Travel, not on travel bought direct. And whether the credit resets on the calendar year or the account anniversary is not confirmed in the sources read.

The Platinum sum

To cover $895 a reader has to add up credits they would have spent cash on anyway. The larger ones:

Credit Yearly amount What it takes
Hotel $600 Two prepaid stays at Fine Hotels + Resorts or The Hotel Collection, one in each half of the year
Resy $400 A meal at a US Resy restaurant each quarter
Digital entertainment $300 A listed subscription every month
lululemon $300 A purchase each quarter
Uber Cash $200 A ride or Uber Eats order every month
Airline fees $200 Bag or seat fees on one airline picked in advance

Used in full, the hotel credit plus any two of the others passes $895 on paper. Without the hotel credit it takes three or four of them, kept up every month or quarter. The page on what the credits are worth goes through each one.

Which pattern favors which card

  • A few trips a year, one traveler, mixed spending: Venture X. The fee is covered with one booking, and 2x applies to every purchase.
  • $30,000 a year of non-travel spending: Venture X earns 60,000 miles. The Platinum earns 30,000 points.
  • Frequent flyer near a Centurion Lounge or on Delta: Platinum. Venture X has its own lounges at Dallas Fort Worth, Denver, Dulles, Las Vegas, JFK, LaGuardia and Washington National only.
  • Travels with a partner or children: Since February 1, 2026 the Venture X charges for every lounge guest. The Platinum charges at Centurion Lounges but admits two guests free at Priority Pass lounges.
  • Stays at luxury hotels twice a year anyway: Platinum, for the $600 hotel credit.

Who should skip both

  • Anyone who carries a balance. Interest costs more than either card returns.
  • Anyone who takes one trip a year and never visits a lounge. A $95 card or a no-fee card costs less.
  • For the Platinum: anyone who does not want to track monthly and quarterly credits.
  • For the Venture X: anyone who books hotels direct for loyalty benefits and would not use the portal.

Welcome offers on both cards vary by applicant and change, so they are left out of the sums.

Quick answers

Does the Venture X pay for itself with no effort? Close. It takes $300 of travel booked through Capital One Travel each year, plus the 10,000 anniversary miles from year two.

Is Venture X lounge access as wide as the Platinum's? No. Both include Priority Pass, but the Platinum adds Centurion Lounges and 10 Delta Sky Club visits a year.

Can Venture X miles go to Delta, United or American? No. JetBlue is the only US airline partner. Amex points transfer to Delta.
