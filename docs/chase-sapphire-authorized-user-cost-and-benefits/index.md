# What does an authorized user cost on the Sapphire Preferred and the Reserve, and what do they get?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/chase-sapphire-authorized-user-cost-and-benefits/
Facts checked: 2026-11-21
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

An authorized user costs nothing on the Sapphire Preferred and $195 a year each on the Sapphire Reserve. On both cards they earn points at the card's rates into the primary cardholder's balance; on the Reserve they also get their own airport lounge access, but not a second set of the card's credits.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## Side by side

| | Sapphire Preferred | Sapphire Reserve |
|---|---|---|
| Fee per authorized user | $0 | $195 a year |
| Primary cardholder's fee | $95 | $795 |
| Earning rates | Same as the primary card | Same as the primary card |
| Where points go | Primary cardholder's account | Primary cardholder's account |
| Airport lounges | None | Own Priority Pass with two free guests; Sapphire Lounges; select Air Canada lounges |
| Travel and rental car cover | Yes, per One Mile at a Time | Yes, same as the primary cardholder |
| DashPass | Can activate on their own DoorDash account | Can activate, with the monthly promos |
| Extra statement credits | None | None |
| Welcome bonus of their own | No | No |

## What both cards share

Chase's terms say an authorized user has the same charging privileges as the primary cardmember but is not financially responsible. The primary cardmember owes every dollar an authorized user spends.

One Mile at a Time reports, for the Reserve:

- There is typically no credit check to add someone.
- The card usually appears on the authorized user's own credit report, which can help build a history.
- It also counts as one of their cards under [Chase's 5/24 rule](/chase-5-24-rule-explained/).
- Their spending counts toward the primary cardholder's welcome bonus requirement.
- The new card is mailed to the primary cardholder.

Being an authorized user does not stop someone from later opening the same card in their own name.

Chase's minimum age for an authorized user is not confirmed.

## What $195 buys on the Reserve

The main gain is lounge access when the authorized user travels without the primary cardholder. Chase's terms give each cardholder, primary or authorized, a Priority Pass membership with two free guests and $27 for each extra guest. Air Canada lounge access covers the cardholder and one guest. Details are on the [Reserve lounge access page](/chase-sapphire-reserve-lounge-access/).

## What $195 does not buy

No second set of credits. One Mile at a Time lists the travel credit, dining credit, The Edit credit and Global Entry credit as one per account. An authorized user's spending can use up those credits, but the totals do not grow. Whether an authorized user's card can trigger the StubHub, Lyft and Peloton credits is not confirmed.

Whether Chase refunds part of the $195 when an authorized user is removed mid-year is not confirmed.

## The lounge sum

The primary cardholder already brings two guests free. So an authorized user card adds lounge access only in two cases: the person flies without the primary cardholder, or the group is larger than three.

| Authorized user's solo lounge visits a year | Cost per visit at $195 |
|---|---|
| 2 | $97.50 |
| 5 | $39 |
| 10 | $19.50 |

For a couple who always fly together, the second card adds no lounge access the first card did not already give.

The other route is a second Reserve account at $795, with its own credits and its own shot at a welcome bonus. That is a separate decision with a hard credit check; an authorized user card is $600 less and carries no credits.

## On the Preferred

Because it is free, the question is only whether you want the person spending on your account. Reasons people add one: pooling a household's dining and travel spending into one points balance, or giving a family member a card with no foreign transaction fee for a trip. The Apple TV offer on the Preferred is for the primary cardholder, per The Points Guy.

An older snippet on one site says Preferred authorized users cost $195 and get Priority Pass. Chase's pages and that site's current article say free, with no lounges.

## Who should skip it

- Couples who always travel together on a Reserve. The primary card's two free guests cover the lounge.
- Anyone who would add a person they cannot trust with their credit line. The primary cardholder pays the bill.
- Anyone planning their own Chase applications soon. An authorized user card can count toward 5/24.
- Anyone hoping for a second $300 travel credit or dining credit. It does not come with the card.

## Quick answers

**Is an authorized user free on the Sapphire Preferred?**
Yes. Chase charges no fee for authorized users on the Preferred.

**Do Reserve authorized users get their own Priority Pass?**
Yes. Chase's terms give each authorized user a membership with two free guests per visit.

**Do authorized users get their own $300 travel credit?**
No. The credits are per account, and an authorized user's spending draws on the same totals.
