# Rakuten Etsy cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-etsy-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-24
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Yes. On November 24, 2026, Rakuten's Etsy page showed 1% cash back, with no raised rate and no category tiers. The only exclusions listed are gift card purchases and shipping labels bought by Etsy sellers.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the store page on Rakuten on the day you buy. For this morning's headline rate here and at other stores, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## The rate seen on November 24, 2026

| Point | What Rakuten's Etsy page showed |
|---|---|
| Rate | 1% |
| "Was" label (the usual rate) | None shown |
| Category tiers | None |
| Cap per order | None listed |
| Posting | Added to the Rakuten account "tomorrow" |

Rakuten's Etsy Black Friday page showed the same 1% on the same day. No "was" label means the page did not mark 1% as raised or lowered.

One point of disagreement: an undated search-engine copy of Rakuten's Etsy page described the store as coupons only, with no cash back. The live page on November 24, 2026 showed 1%. A store's listing can change, which is a reason to look at the page on the day.

## What is excluded

Rakuten's terms for Etsy are short. Cash back is not available on:

- The purchase of gift cards
- Shipping labels purchased by Etsy sellers for product fulfillment

The second line is aimed at people who sell on Etsy. A seller's own costs of running a shop are not a shopping purchase.

The terms list nothing else. Handmade items, vintage items, craft supplies, personalized orders and digital downloads are not named as excluded. The terms also do not say that an item must ship from the United States.

## What the terms leave open

- **Paying with an Etsy gift card or Etsy credit.** The terms exclude buying gift cards. They do not say whether the part of an order paid with one earns. Not confirmed.
- **Etsy app.** The terms do not mention it. Not confirmed. A purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension.
- **Coupon codes.** No code rule is listed for Etsy. Rakuten's general page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back. Whether a code sent by an individual seller counts as "not listed" is not confirmed.
- **Several sellers in one cart.** The terms do not address it. Not confirmed.
- **Returns.** Sellers on Etsy set their own return rules. Rakuten's general help page says returned and canceled items lose their cash back.
- **Missing cash back.** No store deadline is given. Rakuten's general terms allow 90 days from the transaction.

## Etsy's own rewards

Rakuten's page describes Etsy Insider, the store's buyer membership: 5% back in Etsy credit on purchases, not counting tax and shipping, and up to $6 off shipping on U.S. domestic orders over $15. That description is Rakuten's, read on November 24, 2026. The price of Etsy Insider and whether its credit combines with Rakuten cash back on the same order are not confirmed.

## What it comes to

| Purchase | Price | Rate | Cash back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Printable download | $8 | 1% | $0.08 |
| Personalized gift | $40 | 1% | $0.40 |
| Custom jewelry | $120 | 1% | $1.20 |
| Vintage furniture | $500 | 1% | $5.00 |
| Etsy gift card | $50 | 0% | $0 |

Taxes and shipping do not count. On Etsy, shipping can be a large part of the total. An item priced at $30 with $9 shipping earns on $30: $0.30.

Rakuten pays out once the balance reaches about $5. Its help page says $5 or more, and its terms say $5.01. At 1%, Etsy purchases alone would need about $500 to reach that. Cash back from other stores counts toward the same balance.

## When you get the money

Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See [when Rakuten pays](/rakuten-when-do-you-get-paid/) and [what to do when cash back did not track](/rakuten-missing-cash-back-what-to-do/). Card rewards are separate and come on top; see [stacking with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).

## Who should skip it

- You are buying an Etsy gift card. It is excluded.
- You sell on Etsy and want cash back on shipping labels. Those are excluded.
- Your orders are small and occasional. At 1%, a $20 order earns $0.20.
- You order in the Etsy app and do not want to start from Rakuten.
- A seller's own coupon would save more than 1%, and you would rather not test whether the code affects cash back.

## Quick answers

**What rate does Rakuten pay at Etsy?**
1% on November 24, 2026, with no category tiers and no raised-rate label. Rates change daily.

**What is excluded at Etsy?**
Gift card purchases, and shipping labels bought by Etsy sellers. Nothing else is listed.

**Do digital downloads and custom orders earn cash back?**
The terms do not exclude them. They are not named separately, so check the store page on the day.
