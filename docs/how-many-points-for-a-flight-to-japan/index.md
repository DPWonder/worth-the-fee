# How many Amex or Chase points does a round trip to Japan cost?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/how-many-points-for-a-flight-to-japan/
Facts checked: 2026-11-20
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

At the lowest partner prices, a round trip from the US to Japan costs about 40,000 to 80,000 points in economy and about 100,000 to 120,000 in business class, plus taxes and surcharges of a few hundred dollars. Those prices depend on finding an open award seat, and business-class seats on ANA are hard to find.

This page is information, not financial advice.

## Lowest published prices, round trip

One-way prices from the sources are doubled here. "Both" means Amex and Chase points transfer to the program.

| Program (who can transfer) | Flies on | Economy | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| ANA Mileage Club (Amex only) | ANA | 40,000 low season, 50,000 regular, 55,000 high | 100,000 low, 105,000 regular, 110,000 high |
| Virgin Atlantic Flying Club (both) | ANA | 60,000 from the West Coast, 65,000 from the Central and Eastern US | 105,000 from the West Coast, 120,000 from the Central and Eastern US |
| United MileagePlus (Chase only) | United, ANA | About 80,000 | About 176,000 to 220,000 |
| Air Canada Aeroplan (both) | Air Canada, United, ANA | 65,000 on routes under 5,000 miles each way; longer routes not confirmed | 170,000 on routes of 5,001 to 7,000 miles each way |
| Avianca LifeMiles (Amex only) | ANA, United | About 110,000 | 180,000 to 200,000 |

Sources and dates: Upgraded Points for the ANA chart and the Virgin Atlantic prices; AwardWallet (April 2026) for Virgin Atlantic; Frequent Miler for United and LifeMiles; Air Canada's chart notice for Aeroplan, effective June 1, 2026.

Los Angeles and San Francisco to Tokyo are longer than 5,000 miles, so the 65,000-point Aeroplan economy price applies to few US routes.

## The cash part

- **ANA Mileage Club.** Upgraded Points says ANA's fuel surcharges can pass $500 on a round trip.
- **Virgin Atlantic on ANA.** Surcharges are passed on and vary. AwardWallet priced Chicago to Tokyo at about $183 one way. Frequent Miler's figures from 2024 were about $360 out and $450 back.
- **Aeroplan and LifeMiles.** No fuel surcharges, per Frequent Miler.

## What makes these awards hard to book

- **Seats.** Upgraded Points says ANA premium-cabin award space is hard to find. AwardWallet calls ANA seats from the mainland US "hit or miss".
- **Virgin Atlantic is booked by phone.** Its website does not show ANA seats. AwardWallet's method: find the seat on United's or Air Canada's site, call Virgin Atlantic to confirm it, then transfer. Reports say Virgin Atlantic cannot book ANA flights leaving the US within 14 days.
- **ANA transfers take about two days** from Amex. The seat can go in that time, and the transfer cannot be reversed.
- **Chase points do not move to ANA Mileage Club.** With Chase points, the routes to an ANA seat are Virgin Atlantic, United and Aeroplan.

## Other routes

**Delta (Amex only).** SkyMiles prices follow the cash fare. Thrifty Traveler, in a page last changed in October 2025, reported sale fares to Tokyo from 30,000 miles round trip in economy, with 30,000 to 50,000 typical in a sale. Outside sales, prices are much higher.

**Japan Airlines.** Chase and Amex points reach Japan Airlines seats through British Airways Avios, and since November 18, 2026 Chase points also move to Cathay Pacific Asia Miles and Qantas, which partner with Japan Airlines. Current prices for those awards were not confirmed. The lowest published Japan Airlines prices are in programs that take neither Amex nor Chase points.

**The card's travel site.** Amex Travel gives 1 cent per point on flights. Chase Travel gives 1 cent, or up to 2 cents on a Sapphire Reserve with [a Points Boost](/chase-points-boost-explained/). As an illustration only: a $1,200 economy fare costs 120,000 points at 1 cent, and a $5,000 business fare costs 500,000 points at 1 cent or 250,000 at 2 cents. Those fares are made-up round numbers, not quotes.

## Where sources disagree

- **ANA's own chart.** Upgraded Points lists 40,000 to 55,000 in economy and 100,000 to 110,000 in business, round trip. Frequent Miler's one-way figures match for low and regular season but give 82,500 one way in business in high season. AwardWallet gives 50,000 and 110,000 round trip on partner airlines. ANA revised its chart on June 24, 2025; its own chart page did not load, so these figures were not confirmed there.
- **United.** Frequent Miler gives 40,000 one way in economy and 88,000 to 110,000 in business. Other 2026 pages in search results give 35,000 and 70,000 to 100,000. United has no fixed chart; the price on the day is the price.
- **Virgin Atlantic.** Sources agree on 52,500 and 60,000 one way in business class. Virgin Atlantic raised these prices in 2024, and older pages show lower figures.

## Who should skip a points ticket to Japan

- Travelers fixed on cherry blossom season or other peak weeks, when award seats are scarce.
- People who will not make a phone booking or wait for a transfer.
- Economy travelers when cash fares are low. At 60,000 to 80,000 points, an $800 fare (an illustration, not a quote) returns about 1 to 1.3 cents per point before fees; run [the cents-per-point check](/points-or-cash-when-to-pay-with-points/).
- Families needing four seats on one flight in business class.

Which programs take which points: [Amex partners](/amex-transfer-partners-which-are-worth-it/) and [Chase partners](/chase-transfer-partners-which-are-worth-it/).

## Quick answers

**What is the lowest points price for business class to Japan?**
About 100,000 ANA miles round trip in low season (Amex points only), or 105,000 Virgin Atlantic points from the West Coast, plus surcharges.

**Can Chase points book ANA?**
Not through ANA's own program. Chase points move to Virgin Atlantic, United and Aeroplan, which can each book ANA seats.

**Can you book one way?**
Yes with Virgin Atlantic, United, Aeroplan and LifeMiles. AwardWallet says ANA Mileage Club has allowed one-way awards since June 2025.
