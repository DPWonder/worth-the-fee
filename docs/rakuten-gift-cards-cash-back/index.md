# Do gift card purchases earn Rakuten cash back?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-gift-cards-cash-back/
Facts checked: 2026-11-21
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

Mostly no. Rakuten says most gift cards are excluded, and every store listing read for this page excluded them. The exception is a gift card seller with its own Rakuten listing: Giftcards.com showed 3% and CardCash 1% on November 21, 2026.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rates change daily, so check the seller's page on Rakuten on the day you buy.

## The general rule

Rakuten's help page puts it in one line: "Most gift cards are excluded from Cash Back." It then adds that cash back is offered on select gift cards.

In practice that splits into two cases.

| Where you buy the gift card | Earns cash back? |
|---|---|
| From the store itself (for example, an Apple card at Apple) | Usually no |
| From a gift card seller listed on Rakuten | Yes, at that seller's rate and terms |

## Stores that exclude gift cards

Each of these listings, read on November 21, 2026, excluded gift card purchases:

| Store | Gift card rule in its Rakuten terms |
|---|---|
| Apple | Gift cards excluded |
| Kohl's | Gift cards excluded |
| Ulta Beauty | Any gift card, including e-gift cards |
| HP | Gift card purchases excluded |
| Dell | Gift cards excluded |
| Amazon | Buying and redeeming gift cards or vouchers excluded |

Assume a store excludes gift cards unless its terms say otherwise.

## Gift card sellers that did pay

| Seller | Rate seen November 21, 2026 | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Giftcards.com | 3% (usual rate listed as 1%) | Store-brand cards and Visa gift cards from $20 to $100 |
| CardCash | 1% | Sells cards below face value; a $50 Kohl's card was listed at $48.50 |

Giftcards.com's terms on Rakuten exclude:

- Mastercard gift cards and Mastercard eGift cards
- Giant Eagle and Foot Locker cards
- The portion paid for with rewards
- Purchases over $2,000
- Orders using a coupon code not listed on Rakuten (may void cash back)

The terms as read also carry a line excluding the Giftcards.com eGift card. Its scope is unclear, because Visa eGift cards were shown at 3% on another Rakuten page the same day. That point is not confirmed. A physical card is the safer assumption until the pending amount shows.

Cash back at Giftcards.com is added the next day, per its listing.

## The sums

Rakuten's listing gave 1% as the usual rate, so 3% looked like a raised one.

| Purchase at Giftcards.com | At 3% | At 1% |
|---|---|---|
| $50 store gift card | $1.50 | $0.50 |
| $100 Visa gift card | $3.00 | $1.00 |

Prepaid Visa gift cards commonly carry a purchase fee. The fee is not shown on Rakuten's page, so it is not confirmed here. Compare it with the cash back before buying: a fee of a few dollars can exceed 3% of a $100 card.

## Paying with a gift card

Paying is a separate rule from buying. Rakuten says some stores exclude a purchase, or the part of it, paid for with a gift card. Its gift card FAQ names Best Buy, CVS, Lowe's and Old Navy as stores that do not pay on the portion bought with a gift card, and says in-store purchases paid with a gift card do not earn.

| $100 order at a store that excludes gift card payment | Earns on |
|---|---|
| Paid fully by card | $100 |
| $40 gift card plus $60 by card | $60 |
| Paid fully with a gift card | $0 |

Other stores are silent on it. Titleist's listing, read a day earlier, excludes purchases made with gift cards. Read the store's terms before planning a two-step purchase.

## Not the same thing: taking your cash back as a gift card

Rakuten also lets some members redeem confirmed cash back for a gift card. That is a way to be paid, not a way to earn.

- Select members only, based on shopping history and balance
- At least $5 in confirmed cash back
- Members who earn American Express points are not eligible
- A request cannot be canceled once made

The other ways to be paid are in [check versus PayPal](/rakuten-big-fat-check-vs-paypal/).

## Who should skip it

- You want cash back for buying a store's own gift card at that store. It is almost always excluded.
- You are buying a Mastercard gift card. Giftcards.com's terms exclude it.
- You plan to pay with the gift card at a store that excludes gift card payment.

## Quick answers

**Do gift cards earn Rakuten cash back?**
Usually not at the store itself. Gift card sellers listed on Rakuten paid 1% to 3% on November 21, 2026.

**Do Visa gift cards earn cash back?**
Giftcards.com showed Visa gift cards at 3% that day. Mastercard gift cards were excluded.

**Does paying with a gift card void cash back?**
At some stores, for the part paid with the card. Rakuten names Best Buy, CVS, Lowe's and Old Navy.
