How we check facts

Card terms change several times a year. These are the rules every page follows.

Sources

  • The provider's own page comes first: the card issuer, the program's terms, or its help center.
  • When the provider's page does not state something, we use established travel and finance publications and name them.
  • If two sources disagree, the page says so and does not pick one silently.
  • If we could not confirm something, the page says "not confirmed" or leaves it out.

Dates

Every page shows the date its facts were last checked. A page that has not been checked recently may be out of date, so always confirm on the provider's site before you apply or sign up.

What we will not do

  • Promise a welcome offer amount. Issuers set offers per applicant and change them often.
  • Tell you to apply. We lay out the sums and say who a product does not suit.
  • Hide a referral link. Every one is labeled where it appears.
  • Publish invented reviews, ratings or testimonials.

Corrections

If you spot an error, write to hello@itsworththefee.com. We fix confirmed errors and update the checked date.

Not advice

Pages are general information. They are not financial, tax or legal advice and do not take your circumstances into account.
