Is TopCashback legit and free, and how does it make money?

The short answer

TopCashback is a real cash back site that has run since 2005 and charges no fee to join or to be paid. It says it passes the whole store commission to the member and earns its own money from bonuses stores pay for sending many shoppers, plus advertising on its site.

How the money moves

TopCashback's help pages describe three steps:

  1. You log in, find a store and click through to the store's site from TopCashback.
  2. You buy as usual. The store pays TopCashback a commission for sending a customer.
  3. TopCashback passes that commission to you as cash back.

That is the same model as Rakuten. The difference TopCashback points to is the share: its help page says it passes "100% of the Cash Back we receive from retailers" to members. Rakuten's terms describe keeping part of the store's payment; see how Rakuten makes money.

What "free" leaves out

No fee does not mean no cost. The limits on TopCashback's own help pages are these:

  • A wait. Cash back shows as tracked first and can be cashed out only once it is "available." Each store sets its own period. For travel, the stay has to be finished first.
  • Tracking can fail. The help page lists the common reasons: a coupon code not listed on TopCashback, an ad blocker, buying in a different tab from the one TopCashback opened, or clicking another cash back or coupon site afterward.
  • It can take a week or longer for a purchase to be reported at all.
  • Your shopping is the product. The store pays because TopCashback sent you. Like any cash back site, it records which stores you click and what the purchase earned.

How to get paid, and what to do when a purchase is missing, is on how TopCashback pays.

Signs it is a real business

  • The site footer dates the company from 2005 and lists sister sites in the UK, Germany, France, Japan, Australia and other countries.
  • It publishes its terms, a privacy policy, an advertiser disclosure and a vulnerability disclosure page.
  • NerdWallet and The Points Guy both cover it as a working cash back site.

None of this promises that every purchase will track. It means the company exists, publishes its rules and pays through ordinary methods: bank transfer, Venmo, PayPal or gift cards.

Who should skip it

  • You do not want a company recording which stores you shop at.
  • You use coupon codes from other sites at checkout. A code TopCashback does not list can cancel the cash back.
  • You keep an ad blocker on everywhere and will not turn it off for a purchase.
  • You would spend more than you planned in order to earn cash back.

For how it compares with the larger US site, see Rakuten or TopCashback.

Quick answers

Is TopCashback free? Yes. Its help page says there are no fees and no deductions from cash back.

How does TopCashback make money if it passes on all the commission? It says stores pay it bonuses for sending many shoppers, and it sells advertising and sponsored content on its site.

Is TopCashback a scam? No. It is a cash back company operating since 2005 with published terms. Purchases can still fail to track, for the reasons its help page lists.
