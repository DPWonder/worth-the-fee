How do Amex Platinum purchase protection, return protection and extended warranty work?

Facts checked November 21, 20264 min readHow we check facts

This page contains a referral link. We earn points or cash if you sign up through it. How we earn.

The short answer

Purchase protection covers a new item that is stolen, damaged or lost within 90 days, up to $10,000 per item and $50,000 per year. Return protection refunds up to $300 per item, $1,000 per year, when a store refuses a return within 90 days. Extended warranty adds up to one year to a manufacturer's warranty of five years or less.

The limits at a glance

The terms come from Amex's benefit guides, which do not name each card. Upgraded Points (updated November 11, 2026) and AwardWallet tie the $10,000 level to the Platinum Card.

Benefit Per item Per calendar year Time window
Purchase protection $10,000 $50,000 per card 90 days from purchase
Purchase protection, natural disaster $500 per event Same cap Same
Return protection $300 $1,000 per account 90 days from purchase
Extended warranty $10,000 $50,000 per card Up to 1 extra year

A payout never exceeds the amount charged to the card. Shipping and handling are not repaid.

Purchase protection

The $10,000 guide covers damage, loss or theft of an item bought with the card, including a gift. It pays the lesser of the repair cost, the replacement cost or the amount charged. It pays only what other insurance or a retailer does not.

Not covered, in part:

  • Items left unguarded, such as in an unlocked car
  • Items in the care of an airline or delivery carrier
  • Normal wear and product defects
  • Damage to sports equipment
  • Used and one-of-a-kind items
  • Consumables, gift cards and tickets
  • Motorized devices and software
  • Items bought for resale or for professional use

Return protection

This applies when a store will not take back an item within 90 days of purchase. The item must be unused and working, bought in the United States or its territories, and charged in full to the card. The yearly cap counts by purchase date.

An item is not eligible if the store's own return policy matches or beats these terms. The guide lists 28 excluded categories, including jewelry, watches, books, software, tickets, formal wear, seasonal items, used items and gift cards.

Extended warranty

The benefit matches the manufacturer's written warranty for up to one more year. It starts when that warranty, or a paid service contract if longer, ends. The warranty must be valid in the United States or its territories.

It pays to repair or replace the item, or refunds the amount charged, whichever is less. Product recalls are excluded.

How to claim

Benefit First step Then
Purchase protection Notice within 30 days of the loss, at americanexpress.com/onlineclaim or 800-228-6855 Proof of loss within 60 days
Extended warranty Notice within 30 days of the loss Proof of loss within 60 days
Return protection Call 800-228-6855 within 90 days of purchase Papers within 30 days of the call

Proof for purchase protection can include the itemized receipt, a repair estimate and, for theft, a police report filed before the claim. For return protection, send the store receipt and the card charge record. If Amex asks for the item, it repays that shipping cost.

Where sources disagree, and what is not confirmed

  • The Points Guy's shopping guide lists $1,000 per item for a card it calls the Platinum Credit Card. AwardWallet's fine print also shows $1,000 beside $10,000 in its main text. Upgraded Points and a separate Points Guy article say $10,000.
  • The Points Guy lists return protection and extended warranty only for the Schwab, Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley Platinum cards. Upgraded Points says the standard Platinum Card has return protection. AwardWallet lists extended warranty and does not mention return protection.
  • Not confirmed: an Amex page that names the Platinum Card for each benefit after the 2025 refresh. The guides are revised 2023 and 2024. Check the guide in your account before relying on a limit.

The sums

The value is what you would lose without it: items you could not return, or that broke or were stolen in the first 90 days, less what a home or renter's policy would pay.

These benefits alone do not carry an $895 fee. They are one line in the full count of what the card returns. Phones have their own cover.

Who should skip relying on it

  • Anyone whose main purchases are excluded, such as jewelry returns or used goods.
  • Anyone who shops mostly at stores with long return windows.
  • Anyone who will not keep receipts.

Quick answers

How long does Amex Platinum purchase protection last? Up to 90 days from the purchase date.

What is the return protection limit? $300 per item and $1,000 per account per calendar year.

Does the extended warranty cover every item? No. The manufacturer's warranty must be five years or less. Motorized devices, software and used items are excluded.
