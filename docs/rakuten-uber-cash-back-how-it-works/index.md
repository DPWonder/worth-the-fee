# Rakuten Uber cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Source: https://itsworththefee.com/rakuten-uber-cash-back-how-it-works/
Facts checked: 2026-11-26
Publisher: Worth the Fee (independent; some pages end with a disclosed referral link)

No, not as of November 26, 2026. Rakuten's addresses for both Uber and Uber Eats lead to a notice that the store is no longer available on Rakuten, so rides and food orders earn $0 there. What does work is the Uber Cash that comes with some American Express cards, plus Rakuten's listing for Grubhub if the goal is food delivery.

This page is information, not financial advice. Rakuten's listings change, so check on the day you ride or order. For this morning's rates at stores that do pay, see [Rakuten rates today, by store](/rakuten-cash-back-rates-today-by-store/).

## What Rakuten shows

| Address on Rakuten | What loaded on November 26, 2026 |
|---|---|
| rakuten.com/uber.com | A page titled for Uber, with the notice that the store is no longer available |
| rakuten.com/ubereats.com | The same notice, titled for Uber Eats |
| /shop/uber, /shop/ubereats, /shop/uber-eats | Rakuten's general home page, not a store page |
| rakuten.com/lyft.com | The same notice; the page title adds "(Deactivated)" |
| /shop/postmates | A store page labeled "No Cash Back" |

There is no rate, no "was" label, no terms, no exclusion list and no posting time for Uber or Uber Eats. The rates that appear on the notice page sit under other stores' names.

The wording "no longer available" points to an earlier listing. When Uber left Rakuten, what it paid and whether it will return are not confirmed.

## What it comes to

| Purchase | Through Rakuten | Cash back |
|---|---|---|
| $25 Uber ride | No listing | $0 |
| $30 Uber Eats order | No listing | $0 |
| $30 Grubhub order, first purchase as a new customer | 4% | $1.20 |
| $30 Grubhub order, existing customer | 1% | $0.30 |

Grubhub's rates are from its Rakuten page on November 26, 2026. Its terms exclude gift cards and self-service corporate accounts. Menu prices and fees differ between apps, so $1.20 back does not make one app cheaper than the other. No ride service was found with a live Rakuten rate that day; a full search of Rakuten's store list was not possible, so this is not confirmed.

## What works instead: Uber Cash from a card

Two American Express cards put Uber Cash into the cardholder's Uber account. It can be spent on rides and Uber Eats orders in the US.

| Card | Uber Cash | How it arrives | Yearly total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amex Platinum | $15 a month, plus $20 more in December | Monthly, in the Uber app | $200 |
| Amex Gold | $10 a month | Monthly, in the Uber app | $120 |

The limits, from Uber's terms for the Platinum benefit:

- Unused Uber Cash expires at the end of each calendar month. Nothing rolls over.
- An American Express card must be the selected payment method on the ride or order.
- It works only in the United States.
- A card added through Apple Pay or PayPal does not qualify.

Used in 6 months of the year, not counting December, the Platinum benefit comes to $90, not $200. The full sums are in [the Amex Platinum Uber credit](/amex-platinum-uber-credit-worth-it/).

The Platinum Card also refunds up to $120 a calendar year of Uber One membership fees. Uber One is priced at $9.99 a month or $96 a year, according to The Points Guy.

## Other routes

- **Card rewards.** A card pays its usual travel, dining or everyday rate on an Uber charge with or without Rakuten. See [stacking Rakuten with card rewards](/rakuten-stack-with-credit-card-rewards/).
- **Gift cards.** Giftcards.com showed 3% on Rakuten on November 26, 2026, marked "was 1%". Its terms exclude the Giftcards.com eGift card, Mastercard gift cards and purchases over $2,000. Whether it sells Uber gift cards, and whether they earn the 3%, is not confirmed. More in [Rakuten and gift cards](/rakuten-gift-cards-cash-back/).
- **Rakuten Dining.** This card-linked program pays when a linked card is used at a participating restaurant. Whether an Uber Eats order from one of those restaurants counts is not confirmed.

## Who should skip it

- You are opening Rakuten or its extension before booking a ride. There is no Uber offer to activate.
- You hold neither Amex card. The Uber Cash is a card benefit and cannot be bought into through Rakuten.
- You use Uber only a few times a year. Monthly Uber Cash that expires is worth little in months with no ride or order.
- You are outside the US. The Amex Uber Cash works only in the United States.

## Quick answers

**Does Rakuten pay cash back on Uber rides?**
No. On November 26, 2026, Rakuten's Uber address said the store is no longer available.

**Does Rakuten pay cash back on Uber Eats?**
No. The Uber Eats address showed the same notice and no rate.

**What can lower an Uber bill instead?**
Card benefits. The Amex Platinum adds $15 of Uber Cash a month, which expires monthly.
