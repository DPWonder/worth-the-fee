Is the Amex Platinum Uber credit worth anything if you rarely take Uber?

Facts checked November 20, 20264 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

It can be, because the monthly Uber Cash also works on Uber Eats orders in the US, not only on rides. But it comes as $15 a month that expires at the end of each month, so someone who uses neither Uber nor Uber Eats most months should count it as close to $0, not $200.

What the card gives you

The US personal Platinum Card has two separate Uber benefits.

Benefit Amount How it arrives What it pays for
Uber Cash Up to $200 a year $15 in your Uber account each month, plus $20 extra in December ($35 that month) Rides and Uber Eats orders in the US
Uber One credit Up to $120 per calendar year A statement credit on your card bill Uber One membership fees only

How the Uber Cash works

  • You add the Platinum Card as a payment method in the Uber app.
  • At checkout, Uber Cash must be switched on and an American Express card must be the selected payment method. If the order costs more than your Uber Cash, that card pays the rest.
  • It works only in the United States.
  • It goes to the primary cardholder, on one Uber account. Additional cardholders do not get their own.
  • It cannot be used on a family profile. A card added through Apple Pay or PayPal does not qualify.

What expires

Everything, monthly. Uber's terms say unused Amex Uber Cash "expires at 11:59 PM Hawaii Standard Time on the last day of each calendar month." Nothing rolls over, so you cannot save three months for one airport ride.

That is the main limit for a light user. The yearly figure is $200, but it is really 12 small pieces:

If you use it in You get
All 12 months $200
6 months (not December) $90
Only a December trip $35
Never $0

Using it on Uber Eats

For people who rarely ride, food is the practical use. A $15 piece may not cover a delivery order once fees and a tip are added, so expect to pay part of the bill yourself. The honest value is $15 off a meal you would have ordered anyway. If you would not have ordered, the credit made you spend money, not save it.

Whether the Amex Uber Cash applies to pickup orders (where you collect the food and skip the delivery fee) is not confirmed. Uber's terms say only that it covers eligible rides and orders where Uber Cash is accepted. Whether it covers the tip is also not confirmed.

The Uber One credit

Uber One is Uber's paid membership. It is priced at $9.99 a month or $96 a year. Members get a $0 delivery fee on eligible Uber Eats orders, lower service fees, and 6% back on rides as Uber One credits, according to The Points Guy.

The Platinum Card refunds up to $120 per calendar year of Uber One fees. That covers the membership in full either way. The rules:

  • The Platinum Card must be the payment method for the membership, charged directly by Uber.
  • Uber Cash must be switched off for the membership fee. Any part paid with Uber Cash earns no credit.
  • Free trials and discounted portions earn no credit.
  • Uber One renews by itself. Removing the card does not cancel it. If you later close the card, cancel the membership in the Uber app or you will keep paying.

Sources differ on whether you must enroll for the Uber One credit. The Points Guy and Upgraded Points say enrollment is required. Uber's page describes only setting the card as the payment method.

A free membership to a service you do not use is worth $0.

Who should skip it

Leave the Uber benefits out of your sums if:

  • You live where Uber and Uber Eats barely operate.
  • You do not order delivery and take fewer than a handful of rides a year.
  • You spend most of the year outside the US.
  • You would only place an order to "use up" the $15.

Quick answers

Does unused Amex Uber Cash roll over to the next month? No. It expires on the last day of each calendar month, at 11:59 PM Hawaii time.

Can the Uber Cash be used on Uber Eats? Yes. It works on rides and Uber Eats orders in the US, with an Amex card selected as the payment method.

Do additional cardholders get their own Uber Cash? No. The Uber Cash goes only to the primary cardholder, on one Uber account.
