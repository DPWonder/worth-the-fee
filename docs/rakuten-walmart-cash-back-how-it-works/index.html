Rakuten Walmart cash back: does it work, and what is excluded?

Facts checked November 24, 20264 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

Yes, Rakuten works with Walmart, in a limited form. On November 24, 2026, Rakuten's Walmart page showed a flat $5 cash back on each of up to three eligible orders of $25 or more, not a percentage of the order. Groceries, pharmacy items and orders placed in the Walmart app are excluded.

How the Walmart offer differs from other stores

Most stores on Rakuten pay a percentage and fund it with a commission. Walmart is different. Rakuten's page says "Walmart is not a partner of Rakuten" and that the offer is provided by Rakuten only.

Point What Rakuten's Walmart page said on November 24, 2026
Amount $5 per eligible order
Number of orders 3 (so $15 at most)
Order minimum $25 or more
How to order Rakuten browser extension, Rakuten app, or Samsung Wallet Perks

The terms name the extension, the Rakuten app and Samsung Wallet Perks. A click from the Rakuten website is not named, so whether that route counts is not confirmed.

Rakuten says the offer is for eligible members and may be changed or ended at any time. The dated window for longer-standing members closes at 11:59 PM Pacific Time on November 25, 2026. Whether a Walmart offer continues after that is not confirmed.

What is excluded

Rakuten's page lists these as earning nothing:

  • Groceries
  • Pharmacy and prescriptions. A pharmacy item in the cart voids cash back for the whole order
  • Optical and vision
  • Financial services: Walmart MoneyCard, credit cards, money transfers, bill pay
  • Alcohol, firearms and ammunition, tobacco and vape products
  • Walmart+ memberships and renewals
  • Subscription and auto-reorder purchases
  • Express checkout, Reorder and Buy it again purchases
  • Purchases made directly in the Walmart app

The page does not address gift cards, pickup orders or marketplace seller items. Those are not confirmed either way. Rakuten's general rule is that most gift card purchases earn nothing.

On coupon codes, the page's FAQ says cash back generally stacks with Walmart promo codes and sales.

What $5 comes to

Because the amount is flat, the return shrinks as the order grows.

Order size Cash back As a share of the order
$25 $5 20%
$50 $5 10%
$100 $5 5%
$400 $5 1.25%

Three small eligible orders earn more than one large one: $15 against $5.

Other big-box and club stores

The flat-dollar setup is particular to this store. The warehouse clubs and the other big-box chain are each handled differently on Rakuten: see Sam's Club, Costco and Target.

What does not carry over

Walmart orders do not count toward other Rakuten bonuses. The page names the welcome bonus and the referral bonus. A new member cannot use a Walmart order to meet the spending for the sign-up bonus.

Where sources differ

  • The page's FAQ says $5 on the first three Walmart purchases and gives no minimum. The terms say the next three eligible orders of $25 or more through the named channels. The terms are more specific.
  • A search summary of an older copy of the same Rakuten page showed no cash back at Walmart. The page read on November 24, 2026 showed $5. The offer comes and goes.

When you get the money

Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See when Rakuten pays and what to do when cash back did not track. Card rewards are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • You mainly buy groceries or pharmacy items at Walmart. Neither earns.
  • You order in the Walmart app or use Buy it again. Both are excluded.
  • You have already used the three orders, or your account is outside the offer dates.
  • You are counting on a Walmart order to trigger a welcome or referral bonus. It will not.
  • You do not want the browser extension. See what the extension collects.

Quick answers

Does Rakuten pay a percentage at Walmart? No. On November 24, 2026 it was a flat $5 on each of up to three orders of $25 or more.

Do Walmart groceries earn Rakuten cash back? No. Groceries are on the excluded list, along with pharmacy, optical and Walmart+ memberships.

Can I order in the Walmart app? No. Purchases made directly in the Walmart app are excluded. The terms name the Rakuten extension, the Rakuten app and Samsung Wallet Perks.

Not a Rakuten member yet

Joining costs nothing; the one fee in the terms is covered in is Rakuten free. A new member gets one joining bonus, not two. Welcome bonus or a friend's referral bonus compares the two offers as read on November 24, 2026.
