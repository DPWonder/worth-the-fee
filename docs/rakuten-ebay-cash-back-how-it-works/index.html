Does Rakuten give cash back at eBay, and what is excluded?

Facts checked November 21, 20264 min readHow we check facts

The short answer

Yes, in select departments. On November 21, 2026, Rakuten's eBay page showed 1% cash back, capped at $50 on any single purchase. Electronics, phones, computers, video games, gift cards and purchases made by sending the seller an offer are excluded.

How you buy matters

The terms set rules on the type of sale, not only the item.

  • Auctions and Buy It Now. Cash back is paid on winning bids and Buy It Now purchases.
  • Offers to the seller. Rakuten's terms say cash back is not available on purchases made through the site's make-an-offer feature. If a seller accepts your offer, the purchase earns nothing.
  • Bidding services. Using a third-party bidding service voids cash back.
  • Unpaid items. Nothing is earned on an item that goes unpaid.
  • The cap. The terms say: "A maximum of $50 Cash Back may be earned on any single purchase."

How an auction is tracked when the bid is placed days before the auction ends is not explained on the page. Rakuten's general rule is that a purchase earns only if it starts from a Rakuten link, the Rakuten app or the extension. Whether the click must come before the bid or before payment is not confirmed.

At 1%, the cap is reached at $5,000. Taxes and shipping do not count.

On a negotiable listing, the choice is between making an offer and earning cash back. An accepted offer that takes $20 off a $300 item saves more than the $3.00 that Buy It Now would earn.

How the rate moves

The page showed no usual-rate label on November 21, 2026, which Rakuten adds when a rate has been raised. That suggests 1% was the standard rate that day. Rates change daily, and Rakuten's terms say they can vary by member and category.

The page also showed an offer for holders of Rakuten's own credit card: an extra 4% when paying with that card through PayPal. That is a card benefit, separate from the store rate, and its terms were not read for this page.

App, coupon codes and gift cards

  • App. The terms do not mention the marketplace's own app. An order started directly in that app is not expected to track.
  • Coupon codes. No store-specific rule appears. Rakuten's general page says codes not listed on Rakuten may void cash back.
  • Gift cards. The gift cards and coupons department is excluded. Whether paying with a gift card affects cash back is not confirmed.
  • Local pickup. Not mentioned; not confirmed either way.

When you get the money

Online purchases typically take 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, then are paid on the next of four dates a year. See when Rakuten pays and what to do when cash back did not track. Card rewards are separate and come on top; see stacking with card rewards.

Who should skip it

  • You buy electronics, phones, computers or video games. Those departments are excluded.
  • You negotiate by sending sellers an offer. Those purchases earn nothing.
  • You use a sniping or bidding service.
  • You buy mostly in the marketplace's own app.
  • Your purchases are small. At 1%, $500 of eligible spending is needed to reach the roughly $5 that a payment requires.

Quick answers

What rate does Rakuten pay at eBay? 1% in select departments on November 21, 2026. Rates change daily.

Do electronics earn cash back? No. Electronics, cell phones, computers, cameras and video games are on the excluded list.

Is there a limit per purchase? Yes. The terms cap cash back at $50 on any single purchase.
